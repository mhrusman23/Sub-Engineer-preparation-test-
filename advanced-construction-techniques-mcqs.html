Q1. The lower part of a building is called

  1. A. Foundation
  2. B. Sub-structure
  3. C. Super structure
  4. D. a and b
Answer: D. a and b

Q2. Building part above plinth level is called

  1. A. Foundation
  2. B. Sub-structure
  3. C. Super structure
  4. D. a and b
Answer: C. Super structure

Q3. Building part between ground level and ground floor level is called

  1. A. Plinth
  2. B. D.P.C
  3. C. Foundation
  4. D. Upper part of foundation
Answer: A. Plinth

Q4. Part of building containing walls, floors, roofs and parapet etc. is called

  1. A. Upper part of building
  2. B. Super structure
  3. C. Sub-structure
  4. D. All of these
Answer: B. Super structure

Q5. Renovation of building is such type of construction work

  1. A. Annual repair
  2. B. Special repair
  3. C. Quadrennial repair
  4. D. Original work

Q6. Extension in existing building is such type of construction work

  1. A. Original work
  2. B. Special repair
  3. C. Annual repair
  4. D. Quadrennial repair
Answer: A. Original work

Q7. Re-painting of doors and windows of existing building is the item of

  1. A. Quadrennial repair
