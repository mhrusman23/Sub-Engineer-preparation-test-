Q1. The lower part of a building is called

  1. A. Foundation
  2. B. Sub-structure
  3. C. Super structure
  4. D. a and b
Answer: D. a and b

Q2. Before starting any project, indication of the area of construction, storage of material, machinery and offices etc. is called

  1. A. Management
  2. B. Site layout
  3. C. Site clearance
  4. D. Alignment
Answer: B. Site layout

Q3. Minimum depth of deep foundation "d" in terms of its breadth "b"

  1. A. 3b

  4. D. 4b
Answer: B. 2b
