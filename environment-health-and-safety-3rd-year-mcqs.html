Q1. Surrounding or conditions in which a person or animal or plant lives or operates is called.

  1. A. Climate
  2. B. Environment
  3. C. Pollution
  4. D. Weather

Q2. A substance which has harmful effect is called.

  1. A. Climate
  2. B. Environment
  3. C. Weather
