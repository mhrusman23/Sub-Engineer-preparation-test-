Q1. Allowable absorption of water in 1st class brick is....

  1. A. 1/6
  2. B. 1/8
  3. C. 1/4
  4. D. 1/10
Answer: A. 1/6

Q2. Raw umber is used in the paint to get the color....

  1. A. Blue
  2. B. green
  3. C. grey
  4. D. red
Answer: C. grey

Q3. A view which shows depth and width of foundation......

  1. A. elevation
  2. B. detailed plan
  3. C. cross section
  4. D. Foundation
Answer: C. cross section

Q4. A short chain can be mended by....

  1. A. Replacing a link
  2. B. straightening a bent link
  3. C. Replacing the open rings
  4. D. all of these
Answer: B. straightening a bent link

Q5. How many types of bench mark are there in surveying....

  1. A. 2
  2. B. 3
  3. C. 4
  4. D. 1
Answer: C. 4

Q6. Unit of current in S.I.....

  1. A. Watt
  2. B. ampere
  3. C. volt
  4. D. ohm
Answer: B. ampere

Q7. It is used to control class A-fire....

  1. A. dry chemical
  2. B. water
  3. C. Sand
  4. D. foam
Answer: B. water

Q8. Life of a pipe can be enhanced by....

  1. A. glazing
  2. B. Galvanizing
  3. C. painting
  4. D. all of these
Answer: D. all of these

Q9. Vertical distance between dip and crown of a trap is called....

  1. A. intercepting trap
  2. B. floor trap
  3. C. break
  4. D. Seal
Answer: D. Seal

Q10. Jointing method for PPRC fitting....

  1. A. Threading
  2. B. solution
  3. C. heating
  4. D. welding
Answer: C. heating

Q11. Such valve is used in flushing cistern....

  1. A. check valve
  2. B. Sluice valve
  3. C. float valve
  4. D. safety valve
Answer: C. float valve

Q12. Non-return valve is also called....

  1. A. Check valve
  2. B. gate valve
  3. C. float valve
  4. D. sluice valve
Answer: A. Check valve

Q13. Safety Valve is also called....

  1. A. Gate Valve
  2. B. Pressure Relief Valve
  3. C. Float valve
  4. D. Sluice valve
Answer: B. Pressure Relief Valve

Q14. In one-liter sample of water salt is 100 mg. In PPM it will be....

  1. A. 10
  2. B. 10,000
  3. C. 100
  4. D. 1,000
Answer: C. 100

Q15. A time period for which a particle of water detains in settling tank is called....

  1. A. stop period
  2. B. Design period
  3. C. dew time
  4. D. detention period
Answer: D. detention period

Q16. A well in which suction pipes of pumps are installed is called....

  1. A. deep well
  2. B. sump well
  3. C. dry well
  4. D. Tube well
Answer: B. sump well

Q17. The ground water entering in the sewer is called....

  1. A. exfiltration
  2. B. springs
  3. C. water table
  4. D. Infiltration
Answer: D. Infiltration

Q18. Locating courses of sewer lines on the map of the area is called....

  1. A. Layout
  2. B. alignment
  3. C. outfall
  4. D. screading
Answer: A. Layout

Q19. On straight line spacing between two manholes for branch sewer line is kept in meters......

  1. A. 50-100
  2. B. 300-500
  3. C. 150-200
  4. D. 100-150
Answer: B. 300-500

Q20. An appurtenance to divert storm water from combined sewer is called....

  1. A. inverted siphon
  2. B. regulator
  3. C. trestle and pier
  4. D. outlet
Answer: B. regulator

Q21. Positive departure is also called....

  1. A. Southing
  2. B. Easting
  3. C. Northing
  4. D. Westing
Answer: B. Easting

Q22. Sum of internal angles of hexagonal traverse is.... (For internal angle formula (2n-4)90 and hexagonal=6 solution (2(6)-4)90= (12-4)90= (8)90=720°

  1. A. 540°
  2. B. 720°
  3. C. 900°
  4. D. 1200°
Answer: B. 720°

Q23. In case of road survey, which traverse is suitable....

  1. A. open
  2. B. close
  3. C. both a & b
  4. D. none of these
Answer: A. open

Q24. Function of optical plummets....

  1. A. over hanging
  2. B. Centering
  3. C. leveling
  4. D. none of these
Answer: B. Centering

Q25. The temporary structure constructed to support a safe working plate form for working is called....

  1. A. form work
  2. B. under pinning
  3. C. Scaffolding
  4. D. shoring
Answer: C. Scaffolding
