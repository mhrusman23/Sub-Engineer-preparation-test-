Q1. A survey in which curvature of earth is taken into account.

  1. A. Cadestral
  2. B. Geodetic Survey
  3. C. Plane Survey
  4. D. City Survey
Answer: B. Geodetic Survey

Q2. A survey in which curvature of earth is not taken into account.

  1. A. Cadestral
  2. B. Geodetic Survey
  3. C. City Survey
  4. D. Plane Survey
Answer: D. Plane Survey

Q3. A survey which is carried out to detect the mountains, river, water bodies, wooden area and other cultural details are:

  1. A. City Survey
  2. B. Cadastral Survey
  3. C. Topographical Survey
  4. D. Hydrographic survey
Answer: C. Topographical Survey

Q4. A survey in which lines are measured parallel to level line.

  1. A. Geodetic survey
  2. B. Plane Survey
  3. C. Topographic Survey
  4. D. City survey
Answer: A. Geodetic survey

Q5. The main principle of survey is to work from

  1. A. The whole to the part
  2. B. the centre to the boundary
  3. C. The part to the whole.
  4. D. All of these
Answer: A. The whole to the part

Q6. Surveys which show the natural features of a country are knows as

  1. A. Cadastral surveys
  2. B. Engineering surveys
  3. C. Topographic surveys.
  4. D. None of these
Answer: C. Topographic surveys.

Q7. In chain survey the area is divided into

  1. A. Replacing the open rings.
