Q1. A survey in which curvature of earth is taken into account.

  1. A. Geodetic Survey
  2. B. City Survey
  3. C. Cadestral
  4. D. Plane Survey
Answer: A. Geodetic Survey

Q2. The deflection between two lines is called:

  1. A. Bearing
  2. B. Dip
  3. C. Angle
  4. D. None of these
Answer: C. Angle

Q3. An instrument used in plane tabling for sighting.

  1. A. Telescope
  2. B. Trough compass
  3. C. U-Frame
  4. D. Alidade
Answer: D. Alidade

Q4. Process to determine the elevation of points of earth:

  1. A. Chaining
  2. B. Traversing
  3. C. Tachometry
  4. D. Leveling
Answer: D. Leveling

Q5. A line joining the points of equal RLs is called:

  1. A. Level line
  2. B. Contour line
  3. C. Horizontal line
  4. D. Curved line
Answer: B. Contour line

Q6. The Instrument having such a telescope with three horizontal cross hairs is called

  1. A. Tacheometer
  2. B. Theodolite
  3. C. Dumpy level
  4. D. Auto level
Answer: A. Tacheometer

Q7. Hydrographic survey is carried out in the following fields.

  1. A. Sea
  2. B. Space
  3. C. Hills
  4. D. Ground
Answer: A. Sea

Q8. Horizontal distance between centroids of the sections of cutting and filling is termed as

  1. A. Lead
  2. B. Haulage
  3. C. Lift
  4. D. Carriage
Answer: A. Lead

Q9. RL of BM + BS

  1. A. I.Z
  2. B. H.I
  3. C. F.S
  4. D. R.L
Answer: B. H.I

Q10. A survey in which curvature of earth is not taken into account.

  1. A. City Survey
  2. B. Plane Survey
  3. C. Cadestral
  4. D. Geodetic Survey
Answer: B. Plane Survey

Q11. Difference of back bearing and fore bearing of a line in WCB system.

  1. A. 270°
  2. B. 360°
  3. C. 180°
  4. D. 90°
Answer: C. 180°

Q12. Instrument used to indicate north direction on the sheet of plane table.

  1. A. Surveyor Compass
  2. B. Prismatic Compass
  3. C. Trough compass
  4. D. All of these
Answer: C. Trough compass

Q13. A surface parallel to mean spherical surface of earth is called.

  1. A. Level Surface
  2. B. Plane Surface
  3. C. Curved surface.
  4. D. None of these
Answer: A. Level Surface

Q14. The vertical distance between two consecutive contours;

  1. A. Contour interval
  2. B. Contour map
  3. C. Horizontal equivalent
  4. D. Horizontal distance
Answer: A. Contour interval

Q15. Horizontal distances can be measured with this levelling instrument

  1. A. Dumpy level
  2. B. Compass
  3. C. Theodolite
  4. D. Tacheometer
Answer: D. Tacheometer

Q16. Determining the depth of water is termed as

  1. A. Sighting
  2. B. Sounding
  3. C. Boating
  4. D. All of these
Answer: B. Sounding

Q17. Vertical distance between centroids of the sections of cutting and filling is termed as

  1. A. Carriage
  2. B. Haulage
  3. C. Lift
  4. D. Lead
Answer: C. Lift

Q18. for ordinary levelling work allowable closing error

  1. A. E = + 0.2
  2. B. E = + 0.4
  3. C. E = + 0.3
  4. D. E = + 0.1
Answer: D. E = + 0.1

Q19. A survey which is carried out to detect the mountains, river, water bodies, wooden area and other cultural details are:

  1. A. City Survey
  2. B. Hydrographic survey
  3. C. Topographical Survey
  4. D. Cadastral Survey
Answer: C. Topographical Survey

Q20. Third quadrant is illustrated as.

  1. A. SW
  2. B. NW
  3. C. SE
  4. D. NE
Answer: A. SW

Q21. Instrument used for orientation:

  1. A. Alidade
  2. B. Spirit level
  3. C. Plumb bob
  4. D. U-Frame
Answer: A. Alidade

Q22. A plane tangential to the level surface at a point is called.

  1. A. Vertical Plane
  2. B. Normal Plane
  3. C. Horizontal Plane
  4. D. None of these
Answer: A. Vertical Plane

Q23. Boundary line of still water in lake shows.

  1. A. Contour-line
  2. B. Level surface
  3. C. Vertical surface
  4. D. Horizontal surface
Answer: A. Contour-line

Q24. Difference of upper and lower staff readings taken by tacheometer is called.

  1. A. Horizontal Distance
  2. B. Vertical Distance
  3. C. Staff Intercept
  4. D. Constant
Answer: C. Staff Intercept

Q25. The process of finding depth of water at places in a channel is termed as.

  1. A. Depth of water
  2. B. Depthometry
  3. C. Tacheometry
  4. D. Sounding
Answer: D. Sounding
