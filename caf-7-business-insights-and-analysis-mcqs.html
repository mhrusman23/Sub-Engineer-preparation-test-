Q1. Current ratio is calculated as current assets divided by:

  1. A. Equity
  2. B. Total liabilities
  3. C. Current liabilities
  4. D. Sales
Answer: C. Current liabilities

Q2. Gross profit margin equals gross profit divided by:

  1. A. Equity
  2. B. Revenue
  3. C. Total assets
  4. D. Net profit
Answer: B. Revenue

Q3. Return on capital employed (ROCE) uses profit before interest and tax divided by:

  1. A. Sales
  2. B. Net assets less debt only
  3. C. Capital employed
  4. D. Revenue
Answer: C. Capital employed

Q4. SWOT stands for strengths, weaknesses, opportunities and:

  1. A. Targets
  2. B. Tasks
  3. C. Threats
  4. D. Trends
Answer: C. Threats

Q5. Horizontal analysis compares financial data:

  1. A. Within one line only
  2. B. Across periods
  3. C. Between countries only
  4. D. Across departments only
Answer: B. Across periods

Q6. A low inventory turnover can indicate:

  1. A. Low debt
  2. B. Slow-moving inventory
  3. C. Efficient selling
  4. D. High cash
Answer: B. Slow-moving inventory

Q7. Interest cover is calculated as profit before interest and tax divided by:

  1. A. Total debt
  2. B. Sales
  3. C. Interest expense
  4. D. Equity
Answer: C. Interest expense

Q8. A KPI is a:

  1. A. Known production index
  2. B. Key performance indicator
  3. C. Key profit income
  4. D. Key payment instruction
Answer: B. Key performance indicator

Q9. Dashboards are used to:

  1. A. Post to the ledger
  2. B. Record all journals
  3. C. Compute taxes
  4. D. Display key information visually
Answer: D. Display key information visually

Q10. Trend analysis helps to identify:

  1. A. One-off errors only
  2. B. Patterns over time
  3. C. Legal duties
  4. D. Contract terms
Answer: B. Patterns over time

Q11. Which financial metric evaluates a firm's capability to cover its short-term financial obligations using only its most liquid assets?

  1. A. Current ratio
  2. B. Quick ratio
  3. C. Cash ratio
  4. D. Working capital ratio
Answer: B. Quick ratio

Q12. What is the primary objective of performing a PESTEL analysis in business strategic planning?

  1. A. To evaluate internal strengths and weaknesses
  2. B. To assess external macro-environmental factors
  3. C. To measure daily employee performance
  4. D. To calculate operational profit margins
Answer: B. To assess external macro-environmental factors

Q13. In the context of business data visualization, what does a scatter plot primarily help analysts identify?

  1. A. Proportional breakdown of a single total
  2. B. Temporal trends over consecutive years
  3. C. Correlation or relationship between two numeric variables
  4. D. Cumulative frequency distribution
Answer: C. Correlation or relationship between two numeric variables

Q14. Which pricing strategy involves setting an initially high price for a novel product and lowering it over time as competition enters?

  1. A. Penetration pricing
  2. B. Price skimming
  3. C. Cost-plus pricing
  4. D. Value-based pricing
Answer: B. Price skimming

Q15. What does a high asset turnover ratio generally indicate about a company's operational efficiency?

  1. A. Ineffective utilization of assets to generate sales
  2. B. Excessive investment in fixed assets
  3. C. Efficient generation of revenue per rupee of assets
  4. D. High levels of idle inventory
Answer: C. Efficient generation of revenue per rupee of assets

Q16. Which of Porter's Generic Strategies focuses on targeting a narrow competitive scope with lower cost than industry rivals?

  1. A. Cost leadership
  2. B. Differentiation
  3. C. Cost focus
  4. D. Differentiation focus
Answer: C. Cost focus

Q17. Which financial ratio measures how effectively a company uses its total assets to generate sales revenue?

  1. A. Quick ratio
  2. B. Asset turnover ratio
  3. C. Debt-to-equity ratio
  4. D. Interest coverage ratio
Answer: B. Asset turnover ratio

Q18. What is the primary purpose of conducting a BCG growth-share matrix analysis in strategic planning?

  1. A. To assess external macro-environmental threats
  2. B. To measure short-term employee productivity
  3. C. To evaluate a company's product portfolio based on market growth and share
  4. D. To calculate the exact break-even point for new products
Answer: C. To evaluate a company's product portfolio based on market growth and share

Q19. Which analytical technique evaluates percentage changes in financial statement items over multiple successive accounting periods?

  1. A. Vertical analysis
  2. B. Horizontal analysis
  3. C. Ratio analysis
  4. D. Break-even analysis
Answer: B. Horizontal analysis

Q20. In the context of business performance management, what does the acronym KPI stand for?

  1. A. Key Profit Indicator
  2. B. Key Performance Indicator
  3. C. Known Production Input
  4. D. Key Portfolio Investment
Answer: B. Key Performance Indicator

Q21. Which strategic management framework examines political, economic, social, technological, environmental, and legal factors?

  1. A. SWOT analysis
  2. B. PESTEL analysis
  3. C. Porter's Five Forces
  4. D. BCG matrix
Answer: B. PESTEL analysis

Q22. What does a quick ratio of less than 1 typically indicate about a firm's liquidity position?

  1. A. The firm has excessive cash reserves
  2. B. The firm can easily pay all short-term liabilities immediately
  3. C. The firm may struggle to meet its immediate short-term obligations without selling inventory
  4. D. The firm is generating high operating profits
Answer: C. The firm may struggle to meet its immediate short-term obligations without selling inventory

Q23. In the PESTEL framework, regulations concerning employment protection and consumer rights belong to which category?

  1. A. Economic factors
  2. B. Legal factors
  3. C. Technological factors
  4. D. Environmental factors
Answer: B. Legal factors

Q24. In porter's competitive forces model, the bargaining power of buyers is likely to be high when:

  1. A. Switching costs for buyers are very low
  2. B. Products in the industry are highly differentiated
  3. C. There are very few buyers in the market
  4. D. Suppliers can easily integrate forward
Answer: A. Switching costs for buyers are very low

Q25. Which of the following Islamic finance modes represents a form of partnership where profits are shared according to an agreed ratio, but losses are borne strictly in proportion to capital contributions?

  1. A. Mudarabah
  2. B. Musharakah
  3. C. Murabaha
  4. D. Ijarah
Answer: B. Musharakah
