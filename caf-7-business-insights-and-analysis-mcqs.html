Q1. Current ratio is calculated as current assets divided by:

  1. A. Equity
  2. B. Total liabilities
  3. C. Current liabilities
  4. D. Sales
Answer: C. Current liabilities

Q2. Gross profit margin equals gross profit divided by:

  1. A. Equity
  2. B. Revenue
  3. C. Total assets
  4. D. Net profit
Answer: B. Revenue

Q3. Return on capital employed (ROCE) uses profit before interest and tax divided by:

  1. A. Sales
  2. B. Net assets less debt only
  3. C. Capital employed
  4. D. Revenue
Answer: C. Capital employed

Q4. SWOT stands for strengths, weaknesses, opportunities and:

  1. A. Targets
  2. B. Tasks
  3. C. Threats
  4. D. Trends
Answer: C. Threats

Q5. Horizontal analysis compares financial data:

  1. A. Within one line only
  2. B. Across periods
  3. C. Between countries only
  4. D. Across departments only
Answer: B. Across periods

Q6. A low inventory turnover can indicate:

  1. A. Low debt
  2. B. Slow-moving inventory
  3. C. Efficient selling
  4. D. High cash
Answer: B. Slow-moving inventory

Q7. Interest cover is calculated as profit before interest and tax divided by:

  1. A. Total debt
  2. B. Sales
  3. C. Interest expense
  4. D. Equity
Answer: C. Interest expense

Q8. A KPI is a:

  1. A. Known production index
  2. B. Key performance indicator
  3. C. Key profit income
  4. D. Key payment instruction
Answer: B. Key performance indicator

Q9. Dashboards are used to:

  1. A. Post to the ledger
  2. B. Record all journals
  3. C. Compute taxes
  4. D. Display key information visually
Answer: D. Display key information visually

Q10. Trend analysis helps to identify:

  1. A. One-off errors only
  2. B. Patterns over time
  3. C. Legal duties
  4. D. Contract terms
Answer: B. Patterns over time

Q11. Which financial metric evaluates a firm's capability to cover its short-term financial obligations using only its most liquid assets?

  1. A. Current ratio
  2. B. Quick ratio
  3. C. Cash ratio
  4. D. Working capital ratio
Answer: B. Quick ratio

Q12. What is the primary objective of performing a PESTEL analysis in business strategic planning?

  1. A. To evaluate internal strengths and weaknesses
  2. B. To assess external macro-environmental factors
  3. C. To measure daily employee performance
  4. D. To calculate operational profit margins
Answer: B. To assess external macro-environmental factors

Q13. In the context of business data visualization, what does a scatter plot primarily help analysts identify?

  1. A. Proportional breakdown of a single total
  2. B. Temporal trends over consecutive years
  3. C. Correlation or relationship between two numeric variables
  4. D. Cumulative frequency distribution
Answer: C. Correlation or relationship between two numeric variables

Q14. Which pricing strategy involves setting an initially high price for a novel product and lowering it over time as competition enters?

  1. A. Penetration pricing
  2. B. Price skimming
  3. C. Cost-plus pricing
  4. D. Value-based pricing
Answer: B. Price skimming

Q15. What does a high asset turnover ratio generally indicate about a company's operational efficiency?

  1. A. Ineffective utilization of assets to generate sales
  2. B. Excessive investment in fixed assets
  3. C. Efficient generation of revenue per rupee of assets
  4. D. High levels of idle inventory
Answer: C. Efficient generation of revenue per rupee of assets

Q16. Which of Porter's Generic Strategies focuses on targeting a narrow competitive scope with lower cost than industry rivals?

  1. A. Cost leadership
  2. B. Differentiation
  3. C. Cost focus
  4. D. Differentiation focus
Answer: C. Cost focus
