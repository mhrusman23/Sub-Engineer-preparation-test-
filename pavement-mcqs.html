Q1. What is the primary function of bitumen in flexible pavement construction?

  1. A. To act as a structural base
  2. B. To bind aggregates and provide a waterproof layer
  3. C. To increase subgrade bearing capacity
  4. D. To act as reinforcement
Answer: B. To bind aggregates and provide a waterproof layer

Q2. What is the designation of the lowermost structural layer in a highway pavement system?

  1. A. Wearing course
  2. B. Base course
  3. C. Sub-base course
  4. D. Subgrade
Answer: D. Subgrade

Q3. Which traffic parameter represents the total yearly traffic volume divided by the number of days in the year, commonly used for highway design?

  1. A. Annual average daily traffic
  2. B. Peak hourly traffic volume
  3. C. Design capacity volume
  4. D. Cubic mean traffic flow
Answer: A. Annual average daily traffic

Q4. In flexible pavement design, how are concentrated wheel loads primarily transferred and distributed downward?

  1. A. Directly to the subgrade without dispersion
  2. B. Through grain-to-grain contact spread across underlying layers
  3. C. Exclusively through rigid reinforcement mesh
  4. D. By hydrostatic fluid pressure absorption
Answer: B. Through grain-to-grain contact spread across underlying layers

Q5. What is the primary binding material utilized in the construction of flexible highway pavements?

  1. A. Portland cement
  2. B. Hydraulic lime
  3. C. Bitumen
  4. D. Epoxy resin
Answer: C. Bitumen

Q6. Which material serves as the standard binding agent used in the construction of flexible road pavements?

  1. A. Portland cement
  2. B. Hydraulic lime
  3. C. Bitumen
  4. D. Epoxy resin
Answer: C. Bitumen

Q7. Which specific structural component forms the bottom-most foundation layer of a highway pavement system?

  1. A. Wearing course
  2. B. Base course
  3. C. Sub-base course
  4. D. Subgrade
Answer: D. Subgrade

Q8. When designing highway and pavement facilities, what does the abbreviation AADT commonly stand for?

  1. A. Annual Average Daily Traffic
  2. B. Average Annual Design Time
  3. C. Aggregate Annual Daily Travel
  4. D. Annual Aggregate Distribution Total
Answer: A. Annual Average Daily Traffic

Q9. What category of pavement construction is predominantly selected for heavy-duty airport runways due to its high load-bearing rigidity?

  1. A. Flexible pavement
  2. B. Rigid pavement
  3. C. Gravel pavement
  4. D. Earth pavement
Answer: B. Rigid pavement

Q10. Which specific structural tier sits at the absolute bottom of a standard highway pavement section?

  1. A. Wearing course
  2. B. Base course
  3. C. Sub-base course
  4. D. Subgrade
Answer: D. Subgrade

Q11. What type of binder is traditionally incorporated in flexible pavement mixtures to hold aggregate particles together?

  1. A. Cement
  2. B. Lime
  3. C. Bitumen
  4. D. Clay
Answer: C. Bitumen

Q12. What primary traffic volume metric is conventionally utilized for structural and geometric highway design calculations?

  1. A. Annual average daily traffic
  2. B. Minimum hourly flow rate
  3. C. Seasonal weekend traffic average
  4. D. Instantaneous peak surge count
Answer: A. Annual average daily traffic

Q13. How do flexible pavements primarily transfer and spread vehicle wheel loads to the underlying subgrade foundation?

  1. A. By acting as a rigid concrete plate
  2. B. By grain-to-grain transfer through successive layers
  3. C. By concentrating loads at single central points
  4. D. By completely suspending the load through steel reinforcement
Answer: B. By grain-to-grain transfer through successive layers

Q14. Which type of pavement structure is predominantly selected for heavy-duty facilities such as airport runways?

  1. A. Flexible pavement
  2. B. Rigid pavement
  3. C. Gravel pavement
  4. D. Composite-only flexible pavement
Answer: B. Rigid pavement

Q15. How do flexible pavement layers typically transmit and distribute wheel loads down to the underlying subgrade?

  1. A. By concentrating stresses at single points
  2. B. By spreading loads outward through successive layers
  3. C. By transferring all weight via steel reinforcement bars
  4. D. By deflecting loads entirely horizontally without vertical reduction
Answer: B. By spreading loads outward through successive layers

Q16. Which test is commonly used in highway engineering to evaluate the aggregate impact value of road construction materials?

  1. A. Resistance to weathering and soundness
  2. B. Resistance to sudden shock or impact loading
  3. C. Bitumen penetration and ductility
  4. D. Subgrade soil shear strength
Answer: B. Resistance to sudden shock or impact loading

Q17. Which layer in a flexible pavement is placed directly above the subgrade to prevent the intrusion of fine subgrade soil into the base course?

  1. A. Surface course
  2. B. Subbase course
  3. C. Binder course
  4. D. Wearing course
Answer: B. Subbase course

Q18. What does ESAL stand for in pavement design and traffic analysis?

  1. A. Equivalent Single Axle Load
  2. B. Effective Structural Asphalt Layer
  3. C. Elastic Settlement Analysis Limit
  4. D. External Surface Area Loading
Answer: A. Equivalent Single Axle Load

Q19. In rigid pavement design, contraction joints are primarily provided to relieve stresses caused by which of the following?

  1. A. Heavy axle load repetitions
  2. B. Decrease in temperature and moisture loss
  3. C. Excessive subgrade settlement
  4. D. High tire contact pressure
Answer: B. Decrease in temperature and moisture loss

Q20. What is the primary purpose of applying a prime coat on a granular base course before laying the bituminous surface?

  1. A. To increase the skid resistance of the pavement surface
  2. B. To bond the asphalt layer to a smooth concrete surface
  3. C. To plug capillary voids and promote adhesion with the bituminous layer
  4. D. To cool down the underlying aggregate temperature rapidly
Answer: C. To plug capillary voids and promote adhesion with the bituminous layer

Q21. Which type of roller is most suitable for the final compaction and smoothing of asphalt concrete surface courses?

  1. A. Sheepfoot roller
  2. B. Pneumatic tired roller
  3. C. Tandem smooth-wheel roller
  4. D. Grid roller
Answer: C. Tandem smooth-wheel roller

Q22. Why are expansion and contraction joints necessary in rigid concrete pavements?

  1. A. A) To improve surface skid resistance
  2. B. B) To accommodate thermal expansion and contraction movements
  3. C. C) To allow rainwater drainage into the subgrade
  4. D. D) To facilitate easy installation of reinforcing steel
Answer: B. B) To accommodate thermal expansion and contraction movements

Q23. How are wheel loads primarily transferred to the underlying subgrade in a rigid pavement structure?

  1. A. A) Through grain-to-grain contact of aggregates
  2. B. B) Through slab action and flexural strength
  3. C. C) Through intense hydraulic pressure
  4. D. D) Through mechanical interlocking of base stones
Answer: B. B) Through slab action and flexural strength

Q24. Which Indian Roads Congress code guideline is designated for the structural design of flexible pavements?

  1. A. A) IRC 10
  2. B. B) IRC 37
  3. C. C) IRC 58
  4. D. D) IRC 90
Answer: B. B) IRC 37

Q25. What is the relative construction cost comparison between rigid pavements and flexible pavements during initial setup?

  1. A. A) Rigid pavement initial cost is lower
  2. B. B) Rigid pavement initial cost is higher
  3. C. C) Both costs are completely identical
  4. D. D) Flexible pavement initial cost is significantly higher
Answer: B. B) Rigid pavement initial cost is higher
