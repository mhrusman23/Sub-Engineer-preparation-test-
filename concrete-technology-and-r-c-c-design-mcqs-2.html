Q1. The minimum percentage of steel reinforcement in a RCC column as per standard practice is:

  1. A. 0.1%
  2. B. 0.8%
  3. C. 2.5%
  4. D. 4.0%
Answer: B. 0.8%

Q2. What is the primary purpose of adding gypsum during the manufacturing process of Portland cement?

  1. A. To accelerate the early strength development
  2. B. To control and retard the setting time of cement
  3. C. To improve the resistance against sulfate attacks
  4. D. To increase the ultimate compressive strength
Answer: B. To control and retard the setting time of cement

Q3. According to standard specifications, what is the initial setting time for ordinary Portland cement?

  1. A. Not less than 30 minutes
  2. B. Not more than 30 minutes
  3. C. Not less than 60 minutes
  4. D. Not more than 600 minutes
Answer: A. Not less than 30 minutes

Q4. In the working stress method of RCC design, what is the modular ratio primarily dependent upon?

  1. A. Grade of steel reinforcement
  2. B. Characteristic strength of concrete
  3. C. Permissible tensile stress in steel
  4. D. Modulus of elasticity of concrete
Answer: D. Modulus of elasticity of concrete

Q5. Which slump value range is typically recommended for mass concrete placements such as dams and thick foundations?

  1. A. 25 mm to 50 mm
  2. B. 75 mm to 100 mm
  3. C. 100 mm to 150 mm
  4. D. 150 mm to 200 mm
Answer: A. 25 mm to 50 mm

Q6. What is the maximum limit of water-cement ratio generally specified for durable reinforced concrete structures exposed to severe weather?

  1. A. 0.35
  2. B. 0.45
  3. C. 0.55
  4. D. 0.65
Answer: B. 0.45

Q7. In limit state design of beams, what is the maximum strain allowed in the outermost compression fiber of concrete at failure?

  1. A. 0.002
  2. B. 0.0035
  3. C. 0.0025
  4. D. 0.0040
Answer: B. 0.0035

Q8. What is the primary function of air-entraining admixtures in concrete mixes?

  1. A. To accelerate the setting time
  2. B. To improve resistance to freeze-thaw cycles
  3. C. To increase concrete unit weight
  4. D. To reduce the need for cement
Answer: B. To improve resistance to freeze-thaw cycles

Q9. Which test is most commonly used to measure the consistency and workability of freshly mixed concrete on site?

  1. A. Vicat needle test
  2. B. Slump test
  3. C. Le Chatelier test
  4. D. Los Angeles abrasion test
Answer: B. Slump test

Q10. In reinforced concrete design, what is the main purpose of providing stirrups or shear reinforcement in beams?

  1. A. To increase bending stiffness
  2. B. To resist diagonal tension stresses
  3. C. To prevent corrosion of main bars
  4. D. To improve concrete compressive strength
Answer: B. To resist diagonal tension stresses

Q11. What is the typical curing period required for ordinary Portland cement concrete to achieve nearly full design strength?

  1. A. 3 days
  2. B. 7 days
  3. C. 14 days
  4. D. 28 days
Answer: D. 28 days

Q12. According to typical building codes, what is the maximum area of tension reinforcement allowed in a reinforced concrete beam?

  1. A. 1 percent
  2. B. 2 percent
  3. C. 4 percent
  4. D. 8 percent
Answer: C. 4 percent

Q13. Segregation in fresh concrete refers to which of the following phenomena?

  1. A. Separation of coarse aggregates from the mortar matrix
  2. B. Rapid evaporation of mixing water from the surface
  3. C. Excessive expansion during the hardening phase
  4. D. Premature stiffening due to high ambient temperatures
Answer: A. Separation of coarse aggregates from the mortar matrix

Q14. In a singly reinforced rectangular section, the depth of the neutral axis for a balanced section is given by kd = 0.5d. What is the value of k?

  1. A. 0.5
  2. B. 0.6
  3. C. 0.7
  4. D. 0.8
Answer: A. 0.5

Q15. Which admixture is primarily used to reduce the heat of hydration in mass concrete?

  1. A. Accelerator
  2. B. Retarder
  3. C. Pozzolanic admixture
  4. D. Air entraining agent
Answer: C. Pozzolanic admixture

Q16. Which of the following is NOT a factor that significantly influences bleeding in fresh concrete?

  1. A. Water content
  2. B. Cement fineness
  3. C. Aggregate grading
  4. D. Concrete temperature
Answer: D. Concrete temperature

Q17. What is the typical water‑cement ratio for a concrete mix that must achieve a characteristic compressive strength of 30 MPa?

  1. A. 0.45
  2. B. 0.55
  3. C. 0.65
  4. D. 0.75
Answer: B. 0.55

Q18. What is the recommended minimum thickness for a load‑bearing RCC wall in residential construction?

  1. A. 50 mm
  2. B. 100 mm
  3. C. 150 mm
  4. D. 200 mm
Answer: B. 100 mm

Q19. What is the minimum required steel reinforcement ratio for a reinforced concrete beam as per Pakistan Standard PS 23?

  1. A. 0.2 %
  2. B. 0.5 %
  3. C. 1.0 %
  4. D. 1.5 %
Answer: B. 0.5 %
