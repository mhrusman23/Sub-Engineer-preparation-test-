Q1. The brick masonry work is usually measured in which unit?

  1. A. Square meters
  2. B. Linear meters
  3. C. Cubic meters
  4. D. Metric tons
Answer: C. Cubic meters

Q2. The unit of measurement of brickwork is generally:

  1. A. Square metre
  2. B. Kilogram
  3. C. Cubic metre
  4. D. Metre
Answer: C. Cubic metre

Q3. The unit of measurement of plastering is generally:

  1. A. Number
  2. B. Square metre
  3. C. Metre
  4. D. Cubic metre
Answer: B. Square metre

Q4. A bill of quantities (BOQ) lists:

  1. A. Items of work with quantities and rates
  2. B. Only labour names
  3. C. Only payments made
  4. D. Only drawings
Answer: A. Items of work with quantities and rates

Q5. Rate analysis determines the:

  1. A. Number of workers only
  2. B. Profit of contractor only
  3. C. Total project time
  4. D. Cost per unit of an item of work
Answer: D. Cost per unit of an item of work

Q6. The unit of measurement of excavation is generally:

  1. A. Square metre
  2. B. Number
  3. C. Cubic metre
  4. D. Metre
Answer: C. Cubic metre

Q7. The unit of measurement of steel reinforcement is generally:

  1. A. Metre
  2. B. Kilogram or tonne
  3. C. Cubic metre
  4. D. Square metre
Answer: B. Kilogram or tonne

Q8. Contingencies in an estimate are provided for:

  1. A. Profit only
  2. B. Unforeseen expenses
  3. C. Taxes only
  4. D. Land cost
Answer: B. Unforeseen expenses

Q9. Centre line method is used to estimate quantities of:

  1. A. Roofs only
  2. B. Doors only
  3. C. Roads only
  4. D. Walls and foundations
Answer: D. Walls and foundations

Q10. An abstract of cost summarises:

  1. A. Only quantities
  2. B. Quantities, rates and amounts
  3. C. Only rates
  4. D. Only drawings
Answer: B. Quantities, rates and amounts

Q11. Detailed estimate is prepared from:

  1. A. Previous bills only
  2. B. Memory only
  3. C. Drawings and specifications
  4. D. Guess only
Answer: C. Drawings and specifications

Q12. Which of the following describes a situation where a company is unable to pay its debts and legal proceedings for winding up begin?

  1. A. Merger
  2. B. Liquidation
  3. C. Restructuring
  4. D. Acquisition
Answer: B. Liquidation

Q13. When calculating diluted earnings per share (DEPS), how are potential ordinary shares treated if they are anti-dilutive?

  1. A. Included in the weighted average number of shares
  2. B. Ignored in the calculation
  3. C. Added to the net profit numerator
  4. D. Deducted from the basic EPS figure
Answer: B. Ignored in the calculation

Q14. Which of the following is NOT a typical component of a detailed estimate?

  1. A. Quantity takeoff
  2. B. Unit rates
  3. C. Contingency allowance
  4. D. Profit margin
Answer: D. Profit margin

Q15. During a site survey, a contractor finds that the actual depth of a foundation pit is 2 meters deeper than planned. This discrepancy should be accounted for by:

  1. A. Increasing the labor rate
  2. B. Adjusting the unit price of excavation
  3. C. Adding a contingency
  4. D. Reducing the material quantity
Answer: B. Adjusting the unit price of excavation

Q16. Which of the following best describes a contingency allowance in a cost estimate?

  1. A. A fixed amount added for profit
  2. B. An extra budget for unforeseen site conditions
  3. C. A discount applied to material prices
  4. D. A tax surcharge on labor costs
Answer: B. An extra budget for unforeseen site conditions

Q17. The cost of a steel reinforcement bar is Rs. 80 per kilogram. If 1,200 kg are required for a project, what is the total cost?

  1. A. Rs. 96,000
  2. B. Rs. 90,000
  3. C. Rs. 100,000
  4. D. Rs. 88,000
Answer: A. Rs. 96,000

Q18. If the unit price of concrete is Rs. 4,500 per cubic meter and the required volume is 120 cubic meters, what is the material cost?

  1. A. Rs. 540,000
  2. B. Rs. 480,000
  3. C. Rs. 600,000
  4. D. Rs. 520,000
Answer: A. Rs. 540,000

Q19. Which of the following factors is generally excluded when calculating the cost estimate of an R.C.C. Spun Pipe sluice structure?

  1. A. A) Pipe material and transport expenses
  2. B. B) Excavation and bedding labor
  3. C. C) Prevailing meteorological weather conditions
  4. D. D) Jointing materials and accessories
Answer: C. C) Prevailing meteorological weather conditions

Q20. What is the standard measurement unit typically adopted for evaluating formwork or centering for concrete slabs?

  1. A. A) Cubic metre
  2. B. B) Metre run
  3. C. C) Square metre
  4. D. D) Kilogram
Answer: C. C) Square metre

Q21. Which specific estimation method utilizes past cost metrics and historical data taken directly from completed similar engineering projects?

  1. A. A) Detailed estimating
  2. B. B) Analogous estimating
  3. C. C) Plinth area estimating
  4. D. D) Cube rate estimating
Answer: B. B) Analogous estimating

Q22. When applying the trapezoidal rule for calculating earthwork volumes from cross-sections, what is the core assumption regarding the intermediate areas?

  1. A. A) Areas vary parabolically between stations
  2. B. B) Areas vary linearly between adjacent cross-sections
  3. C. C) All intermediate areas are assumed to be zero
  4. D. D) Areas remain constant throughout the total length
Answer: B. B) Areas vary linearly between adjacent cross-sections

Q23. What is the primary objective of preparing an initial cost estimate before executing a civil engineering project?

  1. A. A) To forecast potential project expenses accurately
  2. B. B) To design the architectural floor layout
  3. C. C) To schedule the daily site labor shifts
  4. D. D) To finalize the contractor selection immediately
Answer: A. A) To forecast potential project expenses accurately

Q24. What is the primary function of preparing a cost estimate in civil engineering projects?

  1. A. To forecast potential project expenses
  2. B. To schedule daily labor attendance
  3. C. To design structural steel members
  4. D. To select architectural paint colors
Answer: A. To forecast potential project expenses

Q25. In a municipal or base drainage pipeline layout, at what specific locations are inspection chambers or manholes mandatorily installed?

  1. A. At every change of direction
  2. B. At every change of gradient
  3. C. At fixed interval distances
  4. D. All of these locations
Answer: D. All of these locations
