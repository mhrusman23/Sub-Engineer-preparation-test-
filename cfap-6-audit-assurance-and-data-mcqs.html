Q1. ISA 315 (Revised 2019) concerns identifying and assessing the risks of:

  1. A. Tax errors only
  2. B. Going concern only
  3. C. Fraud only
  4. D. Material misstatement
Answer: D. Material misstatement

Q2. ISA 701 concerns communicating:

  1. A. Confirmations
  2. B. Related parties
  3. C. Sampling
  4. D. Key audit matters
Answer: D. Key audit matters

Q3. ISA 570 deals with:

  1. A. Estimates
  2. B. Using experts
  3. C. Going concern
  4. D. Group audits
Answer: C. Going concern

Q4. Which ISA addresses the auditor's responsibilities regarding fraud in an audit of financial statements?

  1. A. ISA 570
  2. B. ISA 320
  3. C. ISA 580
  4. D. ISA 240
Answer: D. ISA 240

Q5. Data analytics in audit can be used to:

  1. A. Avoid professional judgement
  2. B. Prepare tax returns
  3. C. Replace the auditor entirely
  4. D. Test whole populations
Answer: D. Test whole populations

Q6. ISQM 1 deals with:

  1. A. Group audits
  2. B. Audit sampling
  3. C. Fraud only
  4. D. Firm-level quality management
Answer: D. Firm-level quality management

Q7. A disclaimer of opinion is issued when the auditor:

  1. A. Finds minor errors
  2. B. Agrees with management
  3. C. Cannot obtain sufficient appropriate evidence
  4. D. Has a long engagement
Answer: C. Cannot obtain sufficient appropriate evidence

Q8. ISA 600 relates to:

  1. A. Sampling
  2. B. Opinions
  3. C. Fraud
  4. D. Audits of group financial statements
Answer: D. Audits of group financial statements

Q9. IT general controls include access controls and:

  1. A. Change management
  2. B. Marketing plans
  3. C. Pricing plans
  4. D. Staff training
Answer: A. Change management

Q10. Reasonable assurance is a:

  1. A. High but not absolute level of assurance
  2. B. Absolute guarantee
  3. C. Very low level
  4. D. Zero level
Answer: A. High but not absolute level of assurance

Q11. Which standard outlines the requirements for an auditor's report when expressing an unmodified opinion on financial statements?

  1. A. ISA 700
  2. B. ISA 705
  3. C. ISA 706
  4. D. ISA 720
Answer: A. ISA 700

Q12. What is the primary objective of performing substantive analytical procedures during an audit?

  1. A. To test internal control operating effectiveness
  2. B. To obtain relevant and reliable audit evidence
  3. C. To document the overall audit strategy
  4. D. To assess inherent risk at the assertion level
Answer: B. To obtain relevant and reliable audit evidence

Q13. Under ISQM 2, what is the role of an Engagement Quality Reviewer?

  1. A. To execute daily audit fieldwork procedures
  2. B. To prepare the draft financial statements
  3. C. To objectively evaluate significant judgments made by the engagement team
  4. D. To negotiate audit fees with management
Answer: C. To objectively evaluate significant judgments made by the engagement team

Q14. Which of the following is considered an automated control within an information technology environment?

  1. A. Manual review of bank reconciliations
  2. B. Physical count of year-end inventory
  3. C. System-enforced password complexity rules
  4. D. Manager approval signature on purchase orders
Answer: C. System-enforced password complexity rules

Q15. When evaluating the work of an auditor's external expert, what must the principal auditor primarily assess?

  1. A. The expert's independence and competence
  2. B. The expert's personal tax records
  3. C. The profit margins of the expert's firm
  4. D. The exact software used by the expert
Answer: A. The expert's independence and competence

Q16. How should an auditor treat subsequent events that occur between the date of the financial statements and the date of the auditor's report?

  1. A. Ignore them completely
  2. B. Perform procedures designed to identify such events
  3. C. Wait for the next annual general meeting
  4. D. Issue a disclaimer of opinion immediately
Answer: B. Perform procedures designed to identify such events
