Q1. The accounting equation is:

  1. A. Assets = Capital - Liabilities
  2. B. Assets = Capital + Liabilities
  3. C. Liabilities = Assets + Capital
  4. D. Capital = Assets + Liabilities
Answer: B. Assets = Capital + Liabilities

Q2. Which statement shows an entity's financial position at a point in time?

  1. A. Statement of profit or loss
  2. B. Statement of cash flows
  3. C. Statement of changes in equity
  4. D. Statement of financial position
Answer: D. Statement of financial position

Q3. The going concern assumption means the business:

  1. A. Makes a profit every year
  2. B. Will continue operating for the foreseeable future
  3. C. Will be sold next year
  4. D. Has no liabilities
Answer: B. Will continue operating for the foreseeable future

Q4. Depreciation is the allocation of an asset's cost over its:

  1. A. Useful life
  2. B. Guarantee period only
  3. C. Market life
  4. D. Tax life only
Answer: A. Useful life

Q5. An increase in an expense is recorded on the:

  1. A. Credit side
  2. B. Either side
  3. C. Neither side
  4. D. Debit side
Answer: D. Debit side

Q6. The accruals concept requires income and expenses to be recognised when:

  1. A. Cash is received or paid
  2. B. Earned or incurred, not when cash moves
  3. C. The owner decides
  4. D. The bank statement arrives
Answer: B. Earned or incurred, not when cash moves

Q7. Inventory is normally valued at the lower of cost and:

  1. A. Replacement cost only
  2. B. Selling price
  3. C. List price
  4. D. Net realisable value
Answer: D. Net realisable value

Q8. A bank reconciliation compares the cash book with the:

  1. A. Payables ledger
  2. B. Sales ledger
  3. C. Bank statement
  4. D. Trial balance
Answer: C. Bank statement

Q9. A prepaid expense at the year end is shown as a:

  1. A. Current liability
  2. B. Non-current liability
  3. C. Equity item
  4. D. Current asset
Answer: D. Current asset

Q10. Which account normally has a credit balance?

  1. A. Cash in hand
  2. B. Motor vehicles
  3. C. Trade payables
  4. D. Trade receivables
Answer: C. Trade payables

Q11. What is the key difference between financial accounting and management accounting?

  1. A. Financial accounting is for internal users, while management accounting is for external regulators
  2. B. Management accounting follows strict IFRS guidelines, whereas financial accounting does not
  3. C. Financial accounting focuses on historical reporting for external users, while management accounting focuses on future planning for internal management
  4. D. Management accounting is legally mandated for all registered companies
Answer: C. Financial accounting focuses on historical reporting for external users, while management accounting focuses on future planning for internal management

Q12. What is the primary objective of financial statements according to the Conceptual Framework for Financial Reporting?

  1. A. To provide financial information about the reporting entity that is useful to existing and potential investors, lenders, and other creditors
  2. B. To calculate the exact market value of the reporting entity
  3. C. To ensure full compliance with local tax authorities
  4. D. To determine management performance bonuses
Answer: A. To provide financial information about the reporting entity that is useful to existing and potential investors, lenders, and other creditors

Q13. Which basic accounting assumption states that a business will continue its operations for the foreseeable future?

  1. A. Economic entity assumption
  2. B. Going concern assumption
  3. C. Time period assumption
  4. D. Monetary unit assumption
Answer: B. Going concern assumption

Q14. What is the normal balance type of a contra-asset account such as Accumulated Depreciation?

  1. A. Debit balance
  2. B. Credit balance
  3. C. Zero balance
  4. D. Fluctuating balance
Answer: B. Credit balance

Q15. Under the accrual basis of accounting, when should revenues be recognized?

  1. A. When cash is actually received
  2. B. When the budget is approved
  3. C. When earned, regardless of cash receipt
  4. D. When the invoice is printed
Answer: C. When earned, regardless of cash receipt

Q16. Which of the following is classified as a long-term liability on the statement of financial position?

  1. A. Accounts payable
  2. B. Salaries payable
  3. C. Bonds payable
  4. D. Prepaid insurance
Answer: C. Bonds payable

Q17. What does a trial balance primarily help an accountant verify?

  1. A. The accuracy of business strategy
  2. B. The equality of total debits and credits
  3. C. The complete absence of all accounting errors
  4. D. The exact market value of all assets
Answer: B. The equality of total debits and credits

Q18. Which inventory valuation method assigns the most recent purchase costs to ending inventory during a period of rising prices?

  1. A. FIFO
  2. B. LIFO
  3. C. Weighted average
  4. D. Specific identification
Answer: B. LIFO

Q19. Which document is issued by a buyer to a seller to formally request the return of defective goods and claim a refund or credit?

  1. A. Debit Note
  2. B. Credit Note
  3. C. Purchase Order
  4. D. Sales Invoice
Answer: A. Debit Note

Q20. What is the primary effect on the accounting equation when a business owner withdraws cash for personal use?

  1. A. Both assets and liabilities increase
  2. B. Both assets and owner's equity decrease
  3. C. Assets decrease and liabilities increase
  4. D. Owner's equity increases and assets decrease
Answer: B. Both assets and owner's equity decrease

Q21. Which of the following errors will cause a mismatch in the totals of the trial balance?

  1. A. Recording a transaction completely in the wrong account of the same category
  2. B. Completely omitting a transaction from the books
  3. C. Posting a correct debit amount as a lesser or greater value on the credit side
  4. D. Recording both a debit and a credit with the exact same incorrect amount
Answer: C. Posting a correct debit amount as a lesser or greater value on the credit side

Q22. How are unearned revenues classified on a company's classified balance sheet?

  1. A. Current Assets
  2. B. Long-term Assets
  3. C. Current Liabilities
  4. D. Owner's Equity
Answer: C. Current Liabilities

Q23. Which depreciation method results in a higher depreciation expense in the early years of an asset's useful life?

  1. A. Straight-line method
  2. B. Declining balance method
  3. C. Units of production method
  4. D. Depletion method
Answer: B. Declining balance method

Q24. In a bank reconciliation statement, which item is typically added to the balance as per the cash book to match the bank statement?

  1. A. Bank service charges recorded by the bank
  2. B. Direct deposit made by a customer into the bank account
  3. C. Outstanding or unpresented cheques
  4. D. Dishonoured customer cheques
Answer: B. Direct deposit made by a customer into the bank account

Q25. If a company records a purchase of office equipment for Rs. 50,000 and pays Rs. 10,000 in cash, what is the entry for the remaining amount?

  1. A. Debit Office equipment 40,000; Credit Accounts payable 40,000
  2. B. Debit Accounts payable 40,000; Credit Office equipment 40,000
  3. C. Debit Office equipment 50,000; Credit Cash 50,000
  4. D. Debit Cash 40,000; Credit Accounts payable 40,000
Answer: A. Debit Office equipment 40,000; Credit Accounts payable 40,000
