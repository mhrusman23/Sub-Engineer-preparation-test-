Strength of Materials MCQs (Mechanical Engineering)

Free MCQs with answers for Strength of Materials. 16 questions in 2 chapter tests are in the app; all are shown here.

Q1. Stress is defined as force per unit:

  1. A. Volume
  2. B. Area
  3. C. Length
  4. D. Time
Answer: B. Area

Q2. Hooke's law applies within the:

  1. A. Fracture zone
  2. B. Elastic limit
  3. C. Yield after necking
  4. D. Plastic range
Answer: B. Elastic limit

Q3. Young's modulus is the ratio of stress to:

  1. A. Load
  2. B. Length
  3. C. Strain
  4. D. Area
Answer: C. Strain

Q4. Poisson's ratio is the ratio of lateral strain to:

  1. A. Stress
  2. B. Shear strain
  3. C. Volume strain
  4. D. Longitudinal strain
Answer: D. Longitudinal strain

Q5. The maximum bending moment in a simply supported beam with a central point load W and span L is:

  1. A. WL/2
  2. B. WL/4
  3. C. WL/8
  4. D. WL
Answer: B. WL/4

Q6. The unit of stress in SI is:

  1. A. Joule
  2. B. Pascal
  3. C. Newton
  4. D. Watt
Answer: B. Pascal

Q7. Torsion in a shaft produces:

  1. A. No stress
  2. B. Only tensile stress
  3. C. Shear stress
  4. D. Only compressive stress
Answer: C. Shear stress

Q8. The point where a material begins to deform permanently is the:

  1. A. Yield point
  2. B. Proportional limit only
  3. C. Fracture point
  4. D. Endurance limit
Answer: A. Yield point

Q9. Slenderness ratio is used in the analysis of:

  1. A. Springs only
  2. B. Columns
  3. C. Plates
  4. D. Beams in torsion
Answer: B. Columns

Q10. The neutral axis in pure bending has:

  1. A. Maximum strain
  2. B. Zero bending stress
  3. C. Maximum stress
  4. D. Highest shear only
Answer: B. Zero bending stress

Q11. Which internal resistance is mobilized when a structural member is subjected to equal and opposite pulling forces?

  1. A. Compressive stress
  2. B. Tensile stress
  3. C. Shear stress
  4. D. Bearing stress
Answer: B. Tensile stress

Q12. What physical property represents a material's capability to undergo significant plastic deformation before rupture?

  1. A. Brittleness
  2. B. Hardness
  3. C. Ductility
  4. D. Stiffness
Answer: C. Ductility

Q13. In a cantilever beam subjected to a point load at its free end, where does the maximum bending moment occur?

  1. A. At the free end
  2. B. At the mid-span
  3. C. At the fixed support
  4. D. One-third from the support
Answer: C. At the fixed support

Q14. What term describes the maximum load a column can support before it begins to buckle?

  1. A. Proof load
  2. B. Ultimate load
  3. C. Critical load
  4. D. Working load
Answer: C. Critical load

Q15. What is the primary effect of transverse shear forces acting on a beam cross-section?

  1. A. Longitudinal stretching
  2. B. Sliding of parallel planes
  3. C. Uniform contraction
  4. D. Torsional twisting
Answer: B. Sliding of parallel planes

Q16. Which geometric property of a cross-section directly dictates its resistance to torsional deformation?

  1. A. Moment of inertia
  2. B. Polar moment of inertia
  3. C. Radius of gyration
  4. D. Section modulus
Answer: B. Polar moment of inertia
