Q1. Which of the following tests is used to determine the bearing capacity of soil in situ?

  1. A. Los Angeles Abrasion Test
  2. B. Plate Load Test
  3. C. Vicat Needle Test
  4. D. Slump Test
Answer: B. Plate Load Test

Q2. What is the specific gravity of ordinary Portland cement?

  1. A. 2.55
  2. B. 2.85
  3. C. 3.15
  4. D. 3.50
Answer: C. 3.15

Q3. The minimum curing period for concrete structures under normal weather conditions is usually:

  1. A. 3 days
  2. B. 7 days
  3. C. 14 days
  4. D. 28 days
Answer: B. 7 days

Q4. The process of removing topsoil containing organic matter before earthwork is called?

  1. A. Compaction
  2. B. Consolidation
  3. C. Grubbing
  4. D. Scraping
Answer: D. Scraping

Q5. Which type of stress is induced when a member is subjected to equal and opposite pulls?

  1. A. Compressive stress
  2. B. Tensile stress
  3. C. Shear stress
  4. D. Bending stress
Answer: B. Tensile stress

Q6. What is the angle between the two plane faces of an optical square?

  1. A. 45 degrees
  2. B. 60 degrees
  3. C. 90 degrees
  4. D. 180 degrees
Answer: A. 45 degrees

Q7. Which test is conducted to determine the workability of fresh concrete on site?

  1. A. Slump test
  2. B. Vicat test
  3. C. Los Angeles test
  4. D. Le Chatelier test
Answer: A. Slump test

Q8. In a brick masonry wall, the alternate headers and stretchers in each course are called:

  1. A. English bond
  2. B. Flemish bond
  3. C. Stretcher bond
  4. D. Header bond
Answer: B. Flemish bond

Q9. The property of soil due to which it loses its shear strength upon remolding is known as:

  1. A. Thixotropy
  2. B. Sensitivity
  3. C. Liquefaction
  4. D. Plasticity
Answer: B. Sensitivity

Q10. What is the standard size of a modular brick according to Pakistan Standards?

  1. A. 19 cm x 9 cm x 9 cm
  2. B. 22.5 cm x 10 cm x 7.5 cm
  3. C. 20 cm x 10 cm x 10 cm
  4. D. 23 cm x 11.5 cm x 7.5 cm
Answer: A. 19 cm x 9 cm x 9 cm

Q11. The process of adding water to quick lime to form slaked lime is called:

  1. A. Calcination
  2. B. Slaking
  3. C. Hydration
  4. D. Carbonation
Answer: B. Slaking

Q12. Which survey is conducted to determine the absolute locations of points on the earth's surface considering its curvature?

  1. A. Plane surveying
  2. B. Geodetic surveying
  3. C. Cadastral surveying
  4. D. Topographic surveying
Answer: B. Geodetic surveying

Q13. The ability of a material to absorb moisture from the air is known as:

  1. A. Porosity
  2. B. Hygroscopy
  3. C. Permeability
  4. D. Capillarity
Answer: B. Hygroscopy

Q14. In surveying, a benchmark refers to:

  1. A. A point of known elevation
  2. B. A permanent boundary line
  3. C. The starting point of a chain
  4. D. An angle measuring station
Answer: A. A point of known elevation

Q15. Which type of bond in brick masonry consists of alternate headers and stretchers in every course?

  1. A. English bond
  2. B. Flemish bond
  3. C. Stretcher bond
  4. D. Header bond
Answer: B. Flemish bond

Q16. The voids ratio of a soil sample is defined as the ratio of:

  1. A. Volume of voids to total volume
  2. B. Volume of voids to volume of solids
  3. C. Volume of water to volume of voids
  4. D. Weight of solids to total weight
Answer: B. Volume of voids to volume of solids

Q17. What is the primary purpose of providing a transition curve in highway alignment?

  1. A. To improve aesthetics
  2. B. To avoid sudden centrifugal acceleration
  3. C. To reduce earthwork quantity
  4. D. To increase maximum speed limit
Answer: B. To avoid sudden centrifugal acceleration

Q18. The slump test for concrete is primarily a measure of its:

  1. A. Compressive strength
  2. B. Workability
  3. C. Durability
  4. D. Specific gravity
Answer: B. Workability

Q19. Which of the following is a non-destructive test used to assess the strength of hardened concrete?

  1. A. Compression test
  2. B. Rebound hammer test
  3. C. Split tensile test
  4. D. Flexural test
Answer: B. Rebound hammer test

Q20. What is the minimum curing period recommended for ordinary Portland cement concrete structures?

  1. A. 3 days
  2. B. 7 days
  3. C. 14 days
  4. D. 28 days
Answer: B. 7 days

Q21. The line joining points of equal elevation on the earth's surface is known as:

  1. A. Contour line
  2. B. Ridge line
  3. C. Valley line
  4. D. Gradrient line
Answer: A. Contour line

Q22. Which type of retaining wall utilizes the weight of the structure itself to resist lateral earth pressure?

  1. A. Cantilever retaining wall
  2. B. Counterfort retaining wall
  3. C. Gravity retaining wall
  4. D. Anchored retaining wall
Answer: C. Gravity retaining wall

Q23. The coefficient of permeability of coarse-grained soil like gravel is generally determined using:

  1. A. Constant head permeability test
  2. B. Falling head permeability test
  3. C. Consolidation test
  4. D. Hydrometer analysis
Answer: A. Constant head permeability test

Q24. What is the standard width of a single-lane carriageway in Pakistan for rural roads according to standards?

  1. A. 3.05 m
  2. B. 3.65 m
  3. C. 5.50 m
  4. D. 7.00 m
Answer: B. 3.65 m

Q25. In structural analysis, a simply supported beam subjected to a uniformly distributed load has maximum bending moment at:

  1. A. Supports
  2. B. Quarter span
  3. C. Mid-span
  4. D. Points of contraflexure
Answer: C. Mid-span
