Q1. Which of the following tests is used to determine the bearing capacity of soil in situ?

  1. A. Los Angeles Abrasion Test
  2. B. Plate Load Test
  3. C. Vicat Needle Test
  4. D. Slump Test
Answer: B. Plate Load Test

Q2. What is the specific gravity of ordinary Portland cement?

  1. A. 2.55
  2. B. 2.85
  3. C. 3.15
  4. D. 3.50
Answer: C. 3.15

Q3. The minimum curing period for concrete structures under normal weather conditions is usually:

  1. A. 3 days
  2. B. 7 days
  3. C. 14 days
  4. D. 28 days
Answer: B. 7 days

Q4. What is the primary function of gypsum added during the manufacturing of Portland cement?

  1. A. To increase compressive strength
  2. B. To retard the setting time
  3. C. To impart dark grey color
  4. D. To accelerate initial hardening
Answer: B. To retard the setting time

Q5. Which type of soil structure is formed by flaky particles deposited in a marine environment with high void ratios?

  1. A. Single-grained structure
  2. B. Honeycomb structure
  3. C. Flocculated structure
  4. D. Dispersed structure
Answer: C. Flocculated structure

Q6. In surveying, what error is eliminated by taking the mean of both face observations (face left and face right)?

  1. A. Error of collimation
  2. B. Refraction error
  3. C. Parallax error
  4. D. Curvature error
Answer: A. Error of collimation

Q7. Which coefficient in open channel flow represents the roughness of the channel bed and sides?

  1. A. Chezy's coefficient
  2. B. Manning's roughness coefficient
  3. C. Darcy-Weisbach friction factor
  4. D. Discharge coefficient
Answer: B. Manning's roughness coefficient

Q8. What is the critical path in a project network diagram?

  1. A. The path with the maximum number of activities
  2. B. The shortest path through the network
  3. C. The longest continuous chain of activities determining project duration
  4. D. The path with the least total float
Answer: C. The longest continuous chain of activities determining project duration

Q9. What is the primary purpose of adding air-entraining agents to concrete mixes?

  1. A. To accelerate the initial setting time
  2. B. To improve resistance against freeze-thaw damage
  3. C. To increase the ultimate compressive strength
  4. D. To reduce the overall unit weight of dry concrete
Answer: B. To improve resistance against freeze-thaw damage

Q10. Which of the following traffic survey methods is most appropriate for measuring origin and destination patterns?

  1. A. License plate method
  2. B. Roadside interview method
  3. C. Moving vehicle method
  4. D. Manual volume count method
Answer: B. Roadside interview method

Q11. In structural analysis, what type of support reaction is provided by a fixed support in a two-dimensional plane?

  1. A. Vertical force only
  2. B. Horizontal force only
  3. C. Vertical force and horizontal force only
  4. D. Vertical force, horizontal force, and a rotational moment
Answer: D. Vertical force, horizontal force, and a rotational moment

Q12. What is the main function of bitumen prime coat applied on a granular base course before laying asphalt?

  1. A. To level out surface irregularities
  2. B. To bond the asphalt layer to the base and waterproof it
  3. C. To increase the skid resistance of the finished pavement
  4. D. To act as a structural load-bearing sub-base layer
Answer: B. To bond the asphalt layer to the base and waterproof it

Q13. Which index property of soil is defined as the moisture content at which a soil transitions from the plastic state to the liquid state?

  1. A. Plastic limit
  2. B. Shrinkage limit
  3. C. Liquid limit
  4. D. Liquidity index
Answer: C. Liquid limit

Q14. What is the fundamental objective of performing a Los Angeles abrasion test on coarse aggregates?

  1. A. To measure resistance to weathering and chemical attack
  2. B. To determine the specific gravity and water absorption
  3. C. To measure toughness and resistance to mechanical wear
  4. D. To evaluate particle shape and angularity characteristics
Answer: C. To measure toughness and resistance to mechanical wear

Q15. What is the standard compaction test commonly known as in soil mechanics?

  1. A. Modified Proctor Test
  2. B. Standard Proctor Test
  3. C. California Bearing Ratio Test
  4. D. Direct Shear Test
Answer: B. Standard Proctor Test

Q16. Which admixtures are primarily used to reduce the water content in concrete while maintaining workability?

  1. A. Accelerators
  2. B. Air-entraining agents
  3. C. Superplasticizers
  4. D. Retarders
Answer: C. Superplasticizers

Q17. What is the primary purpose of a slip form construction technique?

  2. B. To continuously pour and move forms upward for vertical structures
  3. C. To accelerate the setting time of mortar
