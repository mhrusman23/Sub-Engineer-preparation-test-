Q1. Choose the correct sentence:

  1. A. He don't know the answer.
  2. B. He doesn't know the answer.
  3. C. He not know the answer.
  4. D. He doesn't knows the answer.
Answer: B. He doesn't know the answer.

Q2. Choose the correct word: 'The lecture was very ___; I learned a lot.'

  1. A. informative
  2. B. information
  3. C. inform
  4. D. informing people
Answer: A. informative

Q3. 'Academic vocabulary' words like 'analyse' and 'significant' are:

  1. A. Slang
  2. B. Formal words common in study and exams
  3. C. Names
  4. D. Verbs only
Answer: B. Formal words common in study and exams

Q4. Choose the correct sentence:

  1. A. The data was collected last week.
  2. B. The data were be collected last week.
  3. C. The data is collect last week.
  4. D. The data collecting last week.
Answer: A. The data was collected last week.

Q5. Which word is a synonym of 'important'?

  1. A. Trivial
  2. B. Significant
  3. C. Minor
  4. D. Weak
Answer: B. Significant

Q6. Choose the correct form: 'Neither answer ___ correct.'

  1. A. are
  2. B. is
  3. C. were being
  4. D. be
Answer: B. is

Q7. 'In spite of the rain, the match continued.' — 'In spite of' shows:

  1. A. Cause
  2. B. Contrast
  3. C. Time
  4. D. Addition
Answer: B. Contrast

Q8. Choose the correct sentence:

  1. A. She suggested to study harder.
  2. B. She suggested studying harder.
  3. C. She suggested me study harder to.
  4. D. She suggested that studying harder me.
Answer: B. She suggested studying harder.

Q9. The word 'consequently' means:

  1. A. Before that
  2. B. As a result
  3. C. In contrast
  4. D. For example
Answer: B. As a result

Q10. Choose the correct article: '___ university in this city is famous.'

  1. A. An
  2. B. The
  3. C. A university of
  4. D. No article needed here at all
Answer: B. The

Q11. Which sentence uses the passive correctly?

  1. A. The results published yesterday.
  2. B. The results were published yesterday.
  3. C. The results was publish yesterday.
  4. D. The results publishing yesterday.
Answer: B. The results were published yesterday.

Q12. 'Approximately 60 students' means:

  1. A. Exactly 60
  2. B. About 60
  3. C. More than 600
  4. D. Less than 6
Answer: B. About 60

Q13. Choose the correct sentence:

  1. A. I have been working here since three years.
  2. B. I have been working here for three years.
  3. C. I am working here since three years ago.
  4. D. I work here since three years.
Answer: B. I have been working here for three years.

Q14. The phrase 'take into account' means:

  1. A. Ignore
  2. B. Consider
  3. C. Count money
  4. D. Remove
Answer: B. Consider

Q15. Choose the correct word: 'The new law will ___ thousands of workers.'

  1. A. affect
  2. B. effect
  3. C. affected by
  4. D. effects
Answer: A. affect

Q16. Which is a correct conditional?

  1. A. If I will see him, I tell him.
  2. B. If I see him, I will tell him.
  3. C. If I seen him, I will tell him.
  4. D. If I see him, I would told him.
Answer: B. If I see him, I will tell him.

Q17. 'Research' in academic English is usually:

  1. A. Countable with many researches always
  2. B. Uncountable — 'research' without 'a' or plural in most uses
  3. C. A verb only
  4. D. An adjective
Answer: B. Uncountable — 'research' without 'a' or plural in most uses

Q18. Choose the correct sentence:

  1. A. One of the students have a question.
  2. B. One of the students has a question.
  3. C. One of the students have questioning.
  4. D. One of the students are has a question.
Answer: B. One of the students has a question.

Q19. The word 'whereas' is used to:

  1. A. Add a point
  2. B. Show a contrast between two facts
  3. C. Give a result
  4. D. Give an example
Answer: B. Show a contrast between two facts

Q20. Choose the correct preposition: 'She graduated ___ university in 2022.'

  1. A. from
  2. B. of
  3. C. by
  4. D. with of
Answer: A. from
