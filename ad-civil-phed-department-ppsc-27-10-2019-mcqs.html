Q1. Which is true?

  1. A. Cut-off decreases river length
  2. B. Meander decreases river length
  3. C. Both
  4. D. None of these
Answer: A. Cut-off decreases river length

Q2. Syphon aqueduct is formed when ______ is passing over ______:

  1. A. Canal, drainage
  2. B. Drainage, canal
  3. C. Road, canal
  4. D. Canal, road
Answer: A. Canal, drainage

Q3. Ground water comes from which zone?

  1. A. Saturation zone
  2. B. Capillary zone
  3. C. Vadose zone
  4. D. Root zone
Answer: A. Saturation zone

Q4. Water logging occurs when:

  1. A. Water reaches near ground surface
  2. B. Water table goes very deep
  3. C. Rain stops completely
  4. D. Canal is closed
Answer: A. Water reaches near ground surface

Q5. Water logged soil can be treated by:

  1. A. Both (controlling seepage and extracting excess ground water)
  2. B. Controlling seepage only
  3. C. Extracting excess ground water only
  4. D. None of these
Answer: A. Both (controlling seepage and extracting excess ground water)

Q6. Purpose of diversion in canal is:

  1. A. To raise the water level
  2. B. To lower the water level
  3. C. To drain the canal
  4. D. To measure discharge
Answer: A. To raise the water level

Q7. Recession limb of hydrograph depends upon:

  1. A. Basin characteristics
  2. B. Storm characteristics
  3. C. Both
  4. D. None of these
Answer: A. Basin characteristics

Q8. Hygrograph measures:

  1. A. Humidity
  2. B. Rainfall
  3. C. Discharge
  4. D. Temperature
Answer: A. Humidity

Q9. Mass curve is the curve between:

  1. A. Cumulative flow vs time
  2. B. Flow vs stage
  3. C. Rainfall vs time
  4. D. Depth vs velocity
Answer: A. Cumulative flow vs time

Q10. Radius of influence of a well is:

  1. A. Distance from center of well to point of zero drawdown
  2. B. Diameter of the well
  3. C. Depth of the well
  4. D. Casing radius
Answer: A. Distance from center of well to point of zero drawdown

Q11. Infiltration depends upon:

  1. A. All (shape of basin, storm characteristics, weed growth)
  2. B. Shape of basin only
  3. C. Storm characteristics only
  4. D. Weed growth only
Answer: A. All (shape of basin, storm characteristics, weed growth)

Q12. For reconnaissance survey, the best source is:

  1. A. Aerial photographs
  2. B. Chain survey
  3. C. Compass survey
  4. D. Plane table
Answer: A. Aerial photographs

Q13. Bernoulli's equation gives conservation of:

  1. A. Energy
  2. B. Mass
  3. C. Momentum
  4. D. Force
Answer: A. Energy

Q14. Head of water is:

  1. A. Energy per unit weight
  2. B. Force per unit area
  3. C. Energy per unit volume
  4. D. Power per unit weight
Answer: A. Energy per unit weight

Q15. Piezometer gives which head?

  1. A. Pressure head
  2. B. Velocity head
  3. C. Total head
  4. D. Datum head
Answer: A. Pressure head

Q16. For sub-critical flow, specific energy increases by:

  1. A. Increasing flow depth
  2. B. Decreasing flow depth
  3. C. Decreasing discharge
  4. D. Increasing slope
Answer: A. Increasing flow depth

Q17. Most economical condition for a triangular channel is:

  1. A. Width = 2 x depth
  2. B. Width = depth
  3. C. Width = 3 x depth
  4. D. Width = 0.5 x depth
Answer: A. Width = 2 x depth

Q18. By increasing altitude, atmospheric pressure:

  1. A. Decreases
  2. B. Increases
  3. C. Remains same
  4. D. First increases then decreases
Answer: A. Decreases

Q19. Constant discharge through a pipe of varying diameter is:

  1. A. Steady non-uniform flow
  2. B. Steady uniform flow
  3. C. Unsteady uniform flow
  4. D. Unsteady non-uniform flow
Answer: A. Steady non-uniform flow

Q20. Upward force on a body submerged in water is determined by:

  1. A. Archimedes principle
  2. B. Newton's law
  3. C. Bernoulli's theorem
  4. D. Pascal's law
Answer: A. Archimedes principle

Q21. In a container of liquid, pressure is:

  1. A. Directly proportional to depth from top
  2. B. Inversely proportional to depth from top
  3. C. Directly proportional to area
  4. D. Independent of depth
Answer: A. Directly proportional to depth from top

Q22. By not providing sufficient spacing between rails at joints:

  1. A. Buckling occurs
  2. B. Rails become stronger
  3. C. Rails get lubricated
  4. D. Nothing happens
Answer: A. Buckling occurs

Q23. Fish plates are used for:

  1. A. Joining rails with rails
  2. B. Joining rails with sleepers
  3. C. Fixing sleepers to ballast
  4. D. Joining two sleepers
Answer: A. Joining rails with rails

Q24. Creep of rail is:

  1. A. Longitudinal movement of rails
  2. B. Lateral movement of rails
  3. C. Vertical settlement of rails
  4. D. Twisting of rails
Answer: A. Longitudinal movement of rails

Q25. Sleeper density in railways:

  1. A. Increases near the joints
  2. B. Decreases near the joints
  3. C. Remains uniform throughout
  4. D. Is maximum at middle of rail
Answer: A. Increases near the joints
