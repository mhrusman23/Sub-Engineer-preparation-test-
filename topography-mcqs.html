Q1. Which line represents the line of intersection of the water surface of a reservoir with the topography?

  1. A. Contour line
  2. B. Shoreline
  3. C. Ridge line
  4. D. Valley line
Answer: B. Shoreline

Q2. What is the term for the fixed vertical distance separating two consecutive contour lines on a standard map?

  1. A. Horizontal equivalent
  2. B. Contour interval
  3. C. Gradient span
  4. D. Datum height
Answer: B. Contour interval

Q3. Along a steep hillside, how are the contour lines typically spaced on a properly scaled topographical plan?

  1. A. Spaced very close together
  2. B. Placed far apart
  3. C. Forming perfectly straight parallel lines
  4. D. Distributed randomly without pattern
Answer: A. Spaced very close together

Q4. Between the direct and indirect methods of contouring, which statement holds true regarding their cost and fieldwork?

  1. A. Direct method is cheaper and faster than indirect method
  2. B. Indirect method is generally faster and more economical for large areas
  3. C. Both methods require the exact same amount of time and labor
  4. D. Direct method uses interpolations exclusively
Answer: B. Indirect method is generally faster and more economical for large areas

Q5. What type of specialized map specifically highlights relief features, natural water bodies, and man-made structures using contour lines?

  1. A. Cadastral map
  2. B. Topographic map
  3. C. Geological map
  4. D. Navigation chart
Answer: B. Topographic map

Q6. In a contour map, what does a closed contour with higher values inside indicate?

  1. A. A depression
  2. B. A hill
  3. C. A flat plain
  4. D. A water body
Answer: B. A hill

Q7. Which of the following describes a closed loop of contour lines where the lowest elevations are found strictly at the center?

  1. A. Hillock
  2. B. Depression
  3. C. Uniform slope
  4. D. Ridge line
Answer: B. Depression

Q8. Which map type is primarily used to show elevation changes, contour lines, and natural features for engineering design?

  1. A. Cadastral map
  2. B. Topographic map
  3. C. Thematic map
  4. D. Road map
Answer: B. Topographic map

Q9. What is the term for a map that primarily delineates natural features like relief, rivers, and forests alongside artificial details?

  1. A. Cadastral map
  2. B. Topographic map
  3. C. Engineering map
  4. D. Architectural map
Answer: B. Topographic map

Q10. What kind of geographical feature is typically represented by a series of closed contours having lower values towards the center?

  1. A. A ridge
  2. B. A plateau
  3. C. A depression
  4. D. A peak
Answer: C. A depression

Q11. Which of the following statements regarding contour intervals on a standard map is true?

  1. A. Contour interval on any given map is kept constant
  2. B. Contour interval varies randomly based on local slope
  3. C. Contour interval increases near the peaks of hills
  4. D. Contour interval decreases near water bodies
Answer: A. Contour interval on any given map is kept constant

Q12. What is the primary defining characteristic of a cadastral map compared to a topographic map?

  1. A. It shows elevation contours in great detail
  2. B. It shows property boundaries and land ownership
  3. C. It highlights vegetation and forest cover only
  4. D. It is exclusively used for underwater hydrographic surveys
Answer: B. It shows property boundaries and land ownership

Q13. Which instrument is specifically utilized for measuring horizontal distances indirectly using a subtense bar?

  1. A. Plane table
  2. B. Subtense method
  3. C. Clinometer
  4. D. Planimeter
Answer: B. Subtense method

Q14. What does a series of closed contour lines with increasing elevation values towards the center indicate?

  1. A. A depression
  2. B. A valley
  3. C. A hillock
  4. D. A saddle
Answer: C. A hillock

Q15. What is the imaginary line on the earth's surface that connects points of equal magnetic declination called?

  1. A. Isogonic line
  2. B. Agonic line
  3. C. Contour line
  4. D. Isochrone line
Answer: A. Isogonic line

Q16. Which surveying instrument combines a theodolite with an electronic distance measurement device?

  1. A. Plane table
  2. B. Dumpy level
  3. C. Total station
  4. D. Prismatic compass
Answer: C. Total station

Q17. In plane table surveying, what is the graphical method of locating the position of an instrument station by sighting three known points called?

  1. A. Resection
  2. B. Intersection
  3. C. Traversing
  4. D. Radiation
Answer: A. Resection

Q18. What is the primary purpose of adding a peg or benchmark during topographical leveling operations?

  1. A. To measure magnetic bearing
  2. B. To provide a reference point of known elevation
  3. C. To calculate chainage along a road
  4. D. To determine horizontal angles accurately
Answer: B. To provide a reference point of known elevation

Q19. When contour lines are spaced very closely together on a map, what physical terrain feature does this indicate?

  1. A. A flat plain
  2. B. A uniform gentle slope
  3. C. A steep slope
  4. D. A valley floor
Answer: C. A steep slope

Q20. What is the primary characteristic of the contour interval across a standard topographic map sheet?

  1. A. It varies depending on slope steepness
  2. B. It is kept constant throughout the map
  3. C. It decreases near water bodies
  4. D. It doubles at higher elevations
Answer: B. It is kept constant throughout the map

Q21. What landform or geological feature is indicated by a series of closed contour lines that feature higher elevation values towards their center?

  1. A. A depression
  2. B. A river valley
  3. C. A hillock
  4. D. A saddle point
Answer: C. A hillock

Q22. Between the two primary methods of contouring, which statement correctly applies to the direct method?

  1. A. It is cheaper and faster than the indirect method
  2. B. It is more time-consuming and generally more expensive than the indirect method
  3. C. It is exclusively performed using aerial photographs
  4. D. It completely eliminates field observations
Answer: B. It is more time-consuming and generally more expensive than the indirect method

Q23. When contour lines merge or unite into a single continuous line on a map, what physical topographical feature does this typically represent?

  1. A. A flat plateau
  2. B. A vertical cliff
  3. C. A gentle slope
  4. D. A uniform valley
Answer: B. A vertical cliff

Q24. Which specific type of survey is primarily conducted to collect comprehensive data required for planning water supply and sewage disposal systems?

  1. A. Cadastral survey
  2. B. Topographic survey
  3. C. Geodetic survey
  4. D. Archaeological survey
Answer: B. Topographic survey

Q25. What type of map depicts horizontal spatial positions of natural and artificial features without containing any vertical elevation data or contour lines?

  1. A. Planimetric map
  2. B. Topographic map
  3. C. Orthophoto map
  4. D. Geological map
Answer: A. Planimetric map
