Q1. A four-bar chain has how many links?

  1. A. 5
  2. B. 3
  3. C. 4
  4. D. 6
Answer: C. 4

Q2. The ratio of angular velocities of two meshing gears depends on the ratio of their:

  1. A. Face widths
  2. B. Number of teeth
  3. C. Materials
  4. D. Lengths
Answer: B. Number of teeth

Q3. A governor is used to:

  1. A. Cool the engine
  2. B. Control engine speed
  3. C. Reduce fuel quality
  4. D. Increase engine power
Answer: B. Control engine speed

Q4. A cam and follower converts:

  1. A. Heat to work
  2. B. Rotary motion into reciprocating motion
  3. C. Thermal energy to electricity
  4. D. Reciprocating into rotary only
Answer: B. Rotary motion into reciprocating motion

Q5. Balancing of rotating masses reduces:

  1. A. Vibration
  2. B. Speed
  3. C. Torque only
  4. D. Efficiency only
Answer: A. Vibration

Q6. A belt drive transmits power by:

  1. A. Friction between belt and pulley
  2. B. Gears meshing
  3. C. Chemical bonds
  4. D. Magnetic force
Answer: A. Friction between belt and pulley

Q7. The number of degrees of freedom of a rigid body in a plane is:

  1. A. 3
  2. B. 2
  3. C. 6
  4. D. 4
Answer: A. 3

Q8. A gear train is used to:

  1. A. Store energy
  2. B. Measure speed only
  3. C. Transmit power with a speed ratio
  4. D. Cool engines
Answer: C. Transmit power with a speed ratio

Q9. Coriolis acceleration arises when a body moves along a:

  1. A. Rotating link
  2. B. Straight fixed link
  3. C. Fixed point
  4. D. Stationary plane
Answer: A. Rotating link

Q10. Static friction is generally:

  1. A. Greater than kinetic friction
  2. B. Zero
  3. C. Equal always
  4. D. Less than kinetic friction
Answer: A. Greater than kinetic friction

Q11. In a simple lever, the mechanical advantage is calculated as the ratio of which two distances?

  1. A. Load arm to effort arm
  2. B. Effort arm to load arm
  3. C. Load arm to fulcrum
  4. D. Effort arm to fulcrum
Answer: A. Load arm to effort arm

Q12. A belt drive uses friction to transmit power. Which factor most directly affects the maximum torque that can be transmitted without slip?

  1. A. Belt material hardness
  2. B. Pulley diameter
  3. C. Coefficient of friction between belt and pulley
  4. D. Number of belt segments
Answer: C. Coefficient of friction between belt and pulley

Q13. A differential gear allows wheels on a vehicle to rotate at different speeds. What is the primary mechanical advantage of a differential?

  1. A. It increases engine torque
  2. B. It balances the vehicle’s weight
  3. C. It permits wheels to turn at different speeds while maintaining power delivery
  4. D. It reduces fuel consumption
Answer: C. It permits wheels to turn at different speeds while maintaining power delivery

Q14. A camshaft in an internal combustion engine converts rotational motion into what type of motion of the valve?

  1. A. Reciprocating
  2. B. Rotational
  3. C. Oscillatory
  4. D. Rotational with a fixed angle
Answer: A. Reciprocating

Q15. Which of the following is NOT a type of simple machine?

  1. A. Lever
  2. B. Pulley
  3. C. Screw
  4. D. Gearbox
Answer: D. Gearbox

Q16. In a compound gear train, the overall speed ratio is determined by the product of the individual gear ratios. If gear A meshes with gear B and gear C meshes with gear D, which statement is true regarding the overall speed ratio?

  1. A. It equals the sum of the individual gear ratios
  2. B. It equals the product of the individual gear ratios
  3. C. It equals the difference of the individual gear ratios
  4. D. It equals the average of the individual gear ratios
Answer: B. It equals the product of the individual gear ratios
