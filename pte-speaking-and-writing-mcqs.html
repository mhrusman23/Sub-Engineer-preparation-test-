Q1. In PTE 'Read Aloud', you are scored on:

  1. A. Pronunciation and fluency
  2. B. Handwriting
  3. C. Speed of reading silently
  4. D. Memory
Answer: A. Pronunciation and fluency

Q2. PTE 'Describe Image' gives you preparation time of:

  1. A. No time
  2. B. 25 seconds
  3. C. 2 minutes
  4. D. 5 minutes
Answer: B. 25 seconds

Q3. In 'Repeat Sentence', you must:

  1. A. Write the sentence
  2. B. Repeat exactly what you hear
  3. C. Translate the sentence
  4. D. Explain the sentence
Answer: B. Repeat exactly what you hear

Q4. PTE Summarize Written Text must be written in:

  1. A. Three sentences
  2. B. One single sentence
  3. C. A list
  4. D. Your own language
Answer: B. One single sentence

Q5. The PTE essay should be between:

  1. A. 50-100 words
  2. B. 200-300 words
  3. C. 500-600 words
  4. D. 800 words
Answer: B. 200-300 words

Q6. In 'Re-tell Lecture', you should:

  1. A. Repeat word for word
  2. B. Summarise the lecture in your own words
  3. C. Ask questions
  4. D. Stay silent
Answer: B. Summarise the lecture in your own words

Q7. For PTE speaking, speaking too slowly mainly hurts:

  1. A. Content score
  2. B. Fluency score
  3. C. Spelling score
  4. D. Grammar score only
Answer: B. Fluency score

Q8. PTE is scored by:

  1. A. Only human examiners
  2. B. An automated AI system
  3. C. Other students
  4. D. The test centre manager
Answer: B. An automated AI system

Q9. 'Write from Dictation' mainly tests:

  1. A. Speaking
  2. B. Listening and writing accuracy
  3. C. Reading speed
  4. D. Vocabulary size
Answer: B. Listening and writing accuracy

Q10. In the PTE essay, a clear structure means:

  1. A. One long paragraph
  2. B. Introduction, body paragraphs and conclusion
  3. C. Only a conclusion
  4. D. Bullet points only
Answer: B. Introduction, body paragraphs and conclusion

Q11. In Read Aloud, if you stumble on a word, you should:

  1. A. Stop and restart
  2. B. Keep going smoothly without long pauses
  3. C. Skip the item
  4. D. Speak louder
Answer: B. Keep going smoothly without long pauses

Q12. For Describe Image, key features to mention include:

  1. A. Only colours
  2. B. Title, axes/trends, highest/lowest points and a conclusion
  3. C. Your opinion of the topic
  4. D. Nothing specific
Answer: B. Title, axes/trends, highest/lowest points and a conclusion

Q13. Summarize Written Text: your one sentence must:

  1. A. Copy a full sentence from the text
  2. B. Capture the main idea in your own words
  3. C. Be 5 words
  4. D. Include every detail
Answer: B. Capture the main idea in your own words

Q14. In the essay, spelling consistency (one variety) matters because:

  1. A. It does not matter
  2. B. Mixing spellings can lose marks
  3. C. American is banned
  4. D. British is banned
Answer: B. Mixing spellings can lose marks

Q15. Repeat Sentence scoring depends on:

  1. A. Writing the sentence
  2. B. Repeating the exact words in the right order
  3. C. Speaking fast
  4. D. Adding your own ending
Answer: B. Repeating the exact words in the right order

Q16. Answer Short Question needs:

  1. A. A full sentence always
  2. B. A short, correct answer (often one or a few words)
  3. C. An essay
  4. D. Silence
Answer: B. A short, correct answer (often one or a few words)

Q17. Re-tell Lecture: notes should capture:

  1. A. Every word
  2. B. The main points and supporting details
  3. C. Only the first line
  4. D. Only names
Answer: B. The main points and supporting details

Q18. Fluency in PTE speaking is helped by:

  1. A. Speaking in short broken phrases
  2. B. Steady rhythm and natural chunking of words
  3. C. Shouting
  4. D. Whispering
Answer: B. Steady rhythm and natural chunking of words

Q19. In the essay introduction, you should:

  1. A. Copy the prompt word for word
  2. B. Paraphrase the topic and state your position/plan
  3. C. Write 'I don't know'
  4. D. Start with a joke
Answer: B. Paraphrase the topic and state your position/plan

Q20. Dictation sentences: if you miss a word:

  1. A. Leave the whole sentence
  2. B. Write what you heard — partial marks are given per correct word
  3. C. Invent a new sentence
  4. D. Skip
Answer: B. Write what you heard — partial marks are given per correct word
