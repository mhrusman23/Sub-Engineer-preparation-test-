Q1. In PTE 'Read Aloud', you are scored on:

  1. A. Pronunciation and fluency
  2. B. Handwriting
  3. C. Speed of reading silently
  4. D. Memory
Answer: A. Pronunciation and fluency

Q2. PTE 'Describe Image' gives you preparation time of:

  1. A. No time
  2. B. 25 seconds
  3. C. 2 minutes
  4. D. 5 minutes
Answer: B. 25 seconds

Q3. In 'Repeat Sentence', you must:

  1. A. Write the sentence
  2. B. Repeat exactly what you hear
  3. C. Translate the sentence
  4. D. Explain the sentence
Answer: B. Repeat exactly what you hear

Q4. PTE Summarize Written Text must be written in:

  1. A. Three sentences
  2. B. One single sentence
  3. C. A list
  4. D. Your own language
Answer: B. One single sentence

Q5. The PTE essay should be between:

  1. A. 50-100 words
  2. B. 200-300 words
  3. C. 500-600 words
  4. D. 800 words
Answer: B. 200-300 words

Q6. In 'Re-tell Lecture', you should:

  1. A. Repeat word for word
  2. B. Summarise the lecture in your own words
  3. C. Ask questions
  4. D. Stay silent
Answer: B. Summarise the lecture in your own words

Q7. For PTE speaking, speaking too slowly mainly hurts:

  1. A. Content score
  2. B. Fluency score
  3. C. Spelling score
  4. D. Grammar score only
Answer: B. Fluency score

Q8. PTE is scored by:

  1. A. Only human examiners
  2. B. An automated AI system
  3. C. Other students
  4. D. The test centre manager
Answer: B. An automated AI system

Q9. 'Write from Dictation' mainly tests:

  1. A. Speaking
  2. B. Listening and writing accuracy
  3. C. Reading speed
  4. D. Vocabulary size
Answer: B. Listening and writing accuracy

Q10. In the PTE essay, a clear structure means:

  1. A. One long paragraph
  2. B. Introduction, body paragraphs and conclusion
  3. C. Only a conclusion
  4. D. Bullet points only
Answer: B. Introduction, body paragraphs and conclusion

Q11. In the 'Repeat Sentence' task, what is the main requirement for the response?

  1. A. Paraphrase the sentence in your own words
  2. B. Repeat the sentence exactly as heard
  3. C. Translate the sentence into Urdu
  4. D. Explain the meaning of the sentence
Answer: B. Repeat the sentence exactly as heard

Q12. Which of the following is NOT a criterion used to score the 'Read Aloud' task in PTE?

  1. A. Pronunciation accuracy
  2. B. Fluency and pacing
  3. C. Handwriting legibility
  4. D. Intonation and stress
Answer: C. Handwriting legibility

Q13. Which skill is primarily assessed in the PTE Speaking task 'Answer Short Question'?

  1. A. Long-form essay writing
  2. B. Short, precise spoken answers
  3. C. Reading comprehension
  4. D. Grammar correction
Answer: B. Short, precise spoken answers

Q14. Which of the following tasks is classified specifically under the Writing component of the PTE Academic exam?

  1. A. Describe Image
  2. B. Summarize Written Text
  3. C. Retell Lecture
  4. D. Reorder Paragraphs
Answer: B. Summarize Written Text

Q15. Which task is always encountered first by a test-taker at the very beginning of the PTE Academic exam?

  1. A. Read Aloud
  2. B. Personal Introduction
  3. C. Summarize Spoken Text
  4. D. Repeat Sentence
Answer: B. Personal Introduction

Q16. Which of the following tasks belongs specifically under the Writing module of the PTE exam?

  1. A. Describe Image
  2. B. Retell Lecture
  3. C. Summarize Written Text
  4. D. Repeat Sentence
Answer: C. Summarize Written Text

Q17. What is the status of the Personal Introduction task at the very beginning of the PTE Academic exam?

  1. A. It is unscored
  2. B. It carries 10 percent weight
  3. C. It is graded by human examiners
  4. D. It evaluates listening comprehension
Answer: A. It is unscored

Q18. How many major parts or sections are there in the standard PTE Academic test structure?

  1. A. One single part
  2. B. Two parts
  3. C. Three parts
  4. D. Four parts
Answer: C. Three parts

Q19. Approximately how much total time does Part 1 (Speaking & Writing) of the PTE Academic test take?

  1. A. 15 to 30 minutes
  2. B. 50 to 65 minutes
  3. C. 80 to 95 minutes
  4. D. 110 to 120 minutes

Q20. Which specific skills are evaluated during the PTE Read Aloud task?

  1. A. Reading and Speaking
  2. B. Listening and Writing
  3. C. Writing and Reading
  4. D. Listening and Speaking

Q21. What is the typical time range allocated for completing Part 1 (Speaking & Writing) in the PTE exam?

  1. A. 20 to 30 minutes

  3. C. 80 to 95 minutes
  4. D. 110 to 125 minutes

Q22. Which of the following tasks is formally evaluated under the Writing section of the PTE exam?

  1. A. Read Aloud
  2. B. Summarise Written Text

  4. D. Repeat Sentence

Q23. Within Part 1 of the PTE Academic test, how many question types are categorized specifically under Speaking and Writing?

  1. A. Five
  2. B. Six
  3. C. Seven
  4. D. Nine
Answer: C. Seven

Q24. What type of response is typically expected in the Answer Short Question task?

  1. A. A detailed paragraph explaining a diagram
  2. B. A single word or a few brief words
  3. C. An essay of at least two hundred words
  4. D. A complete spoken summary of a long lecture
Answer: B. A single word or a few brief words

Q25. How long is the unscored Personal Introduction allowed to take at the beginning of the test?

  1. A. 30 seconds
  2. B. 1 minute
  3. C. 2 minutes
  4. D. 5 minutes
