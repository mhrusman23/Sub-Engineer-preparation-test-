Q1. What is the ratio of cement, sand, and coarse aggregate in M20 grade concrete?

  1. A. 1:3:6
  2. B. 1:2:4
  3. C. 1:1.5:3
  4. D. 1:1:2
Answer: C. 1:1.5:3

Q2. In the standard designation of concrete mixes such as M15, what does the letter 'M' stand for?

  1. A. Maximum
  2. B. Modular
  3. C. Mix
  4. D. Massive
Answer: C. Mix

Q3. What is the process of upward movement of free water to the surface of freshly placed concrete known as?

  1. A. Bleeding
  2. B. Curing
  3. C. Hydration
  4. D. Segregation
Answer: A. Bleeding

Q4. Which specific admixture is added to concrete to intentionally introduce tiny, distributed air bubbles to improve freeze-thaw resistance?

  1. A. Accelerating admixture
  2. B. Air-entraining admixture
  3. C. Retarding admixture
  4. D. Plasticizer
Answer: B. Air-entraining admixture

Q5. Which chemical compound is predominantly responsible for the early strength development of ordinary Portland cement?

  1. A. Dicalcium silicate
  2. B. Tricalcium silicate
  3. C. Tetracalcium aluminoferrite
  4. D. Calcium sulfate
Answer: B. Tricalcium silicate

Q6. What is the term used to describe the property of fresh concrete that allows it to be easily mixed, transported, placed, and compacted without segregation?

  1. A. Durability
  2. B. Workability
  3. C. Bleeding
  4. D. Segregation
Answer: B. Workability

Q7. The undesirable separation of coarse aggregate from the cement mortar matrix during the handling of fresh concrete is called...

  1. A. Segregation
  2. B. Bleeding
  3. C. Hydration
  4. D. Efflorescence
Answer: A. Segregation

Q8. What is the minimum recommended curing period in days for ordinary Portland cement concrete to attain proper strength development?

  1. A. 3 days
  2. B. 7 days
  3. C. 14 days
  4. D. 28 days
Answer: B. 7 days

Q9. For regular Reinforced Cement Concrete (RCC) work, what is the minimum recommended grade of concrete as per modern design standards?

  1. A. M10
  2. B. M15
  3. C. M20
  4. D. M25
Answer: C. M20

Q10. The relationship stating that the compressive strength of fully compacted concrete depends primarily on the water-cement ratio is known as:

  1. A. Abrams' Law
  2. B. Hooke's Law
  3. C. Darcy's Law
  4. D. Fineness Modulus Rule
Answer: A. Abrams' Law

Q11. What term is used to describe the property of fresh concrete that allows it to be easily mixed, transported, placed, and compacted without excessive bleeding or segregation?

  1. A. A) Durability
  2. B. B) Workability
  3. C. C) Porosity
  4. D. D) Permeability
Answer: B. B) Workability

Q12. Which specific admixture is primarily added to concrete to accelerate its early strength development?

  1. A. Calcium chloride
  2. B. Gypsum
  3. C. Fly ash
  4. D. Air-entraining agents
Answer: A. Calcium chloride

Q13. Which test is most commonly performed in the field to measure the workability of freshly mixed concrete?

  1. A. Vee-Bee test
  2. B. Slump test
  3. C. Compacting factor test
  4. D. Flow table test
Answer: B. Slump test

Q14. What is the nominal mix ratio of cement, fine aggregate, and coarse aggregate for M15 grade concrete?

  1. A. 1:3:6
  2. B. 1:2:4
  3. C. 1:1.5:3
  4. D. 1:1:2
Answer: B. 1:2:4

Q15. The property of fresh concrete that determines its ease of mixing, placing, and compacting without experiencing segregation is known as...

  1. A. Durability
  2. B. Workability
  3. C. Permeability
  4. D. Bleeding

Q19. Which Bogue compound in Portland cement hydrates most rapidly and generates the highest early heat of hydration?

  1. A. Tricalcium aluminate (C3A)
  2. B. Tricalcium silicate (C3S)
  3. C. Dicalcium silicate (C2S)
  4. D. Tetracalcium aluminoferrite (C4AF)
Answer: A. Tricalcium aluminate (C3A)

Q20. According to standard construction specifications, the pH value of water used for mixing concrete should not be less than:

  1. A. 4.0
  2. B. 6.0
  3. C. 8.5
  4. D. 9.0

Q21. Which of the following testing methods is most suitable for measuring the workability of concrete having very low consistency?

  1. A. Slump test
  2. B. Compacting factor test
  3. C. Vee-Bee consistometer test
  4. D. Flow table test
Answer: C. Vee-Bee consistometer test

Q22. What is the primary role of adding gypsum during the final manufacturing stages of ordinary Portland cement?

  1. A. To increase compressive strength
  2. B. To accelerate setting time
  3. C. To retard the initial setting time
  4. D. To improve workability
Answer: C. To retard the initial setting time

Q23. What is the term used to describe the undesirable separation of coarse aggregate from the mortar matrix in fresh concrete?

  1. A. Bleeding
  2. B. Segregation
  3. C. Creep
  4. D. Shrinkage
Answer: B. Segregation

Q24. What is the primary role of adding gypsum during the manufacturing process of Portland cement?

  1. A. A) Accelerating early strength
  2. B. B) Retarding setting time
  3. C. C) Improving final colour
  4. D. D) Increasing density
Answer: B. B) Retarding setting time
