Q1. The main purpose of a health and safety policy is to:

  1. A. Blame workers
  2. B. Show management's commitment and arrangements for safety
  3. C. Replace training
  4. D. Increase paperwork only
Answer: B. Show management's commitment and arrangements for safety

Q2. Under the Health and Safety at Work Act, the primary duty for workplace safety rests with:

  1. A. The government
  2. B. The employer
  3. C. The newest worker
  4. D. Visitors
Answer: B. The employer

Q3. A 'hazard' is best defined as:

  1. A. An accident
  2. B. Something with the potential to cause harm
  3. C. A broken machine only
  4. D. A safety rule
Answer: B. Something with the potential to cause harm

Q4. 'Risk' is a combination of:

  1. A. Cost and time
  2. B. Likelihood and severity of harm
  3. C. Age and experience
  4. D. Noise and light
Answer: B. Likelihood and severity of harm

Q5. Health and safety culture is mainly shown by:

  1. A. Posters on walls
  2. B. Shared attitudes, values and behaviour towards safety
  3. C. The size of the company
  4. D. Number of meetings
Answer: B. Shared attitudes, values and behaviour towards safety

Q6. The moral argument for managing safety is:

  1. A. To avoid fines
  2. B. To prevent human suffering
  3. C. To win contracts
  4. D. To reduce insurance
Answer: B. To prevent human suffering

Q7. A safety management system (such as ISO 45001) follows the cycle:

  1. A. Plan-Do-Check-Act
  2. B. Buy-Sell-Use-Throw
  3. C. Hire-Fire
  4. D. Open-Close
Answer: A. Plan-Do-Check-Act

Q8. Leading indicators of safety performance include:

  1. A. Number of accidents
  2. B. Near-miss reports and inspections completed
  3. C. Compensation paid
  4. D. Days lost
Answer: B. Near-miss reports and inspections completed

Q9. Employer must consult workers on health and safety because:

  1. A. It is optional
  2. B. Workers know the risks of their jobs and law requires it
  3. C. It saves money only
  4. D. Managers are too busy
Answer: B. Workers know the risks of their jobs and law requires it

Q10. The main legal consequence of a serious safety breach can be:

  1. A. A verbal warning only
  2. B. Fines and imprisonment
  3. C. Extra holidays
  4. D. A new policy
Answer: B. Fines and imprisonment

Q11. Which specific management system cycle is widely adopted in international safety standards like ISO 45001?

  1. A. Plan-Do-Check-Act
  2. B. Identify-Assess-Control-Review
  3. C. Design-Implement-Operate-Monitor
  4. D. Observe-Analyze-Improve-Sustain
Answer: A. Plan-Do-Check-Act

Q12. What type of safety performance metric is measured by the number of completed safety audits and toolbox talks?

  1. A. Lagging indicator
  2. B. Leading indicator
  3. C. Reactive measure
  4. D. Outcome indicator
Answer: B. Leading indicator

Q13. What is the primary definition of 'risk' in occupational health and safety management?

  1. A. Any physical object that can cause harm
  2. B. The exact financial loss resulting from an incident
  3. C. The likelihood that a hazard will cause harm combined with its severity
  4. D. The total number of injuries recorded in a workplace
Answer: C. The likelihood that a hazard will cause harm combined with its severity

Q14. Which category of motivation for managing workplace health and safety relates directly to compliance with statutory laws and avoiding prosecution?

  1. A. Moral argument
  2. B. Financial argument
  3. C. Legal argument
  4. D. Social argument
Answer: C. Legal argument

Q15. In the hierarchy of risk control, what is considered the least effective method for protecting workers?

  1. A. Elimination
  2. B. Engineering controls
  3. C. Administrative controls
  4. D. Personal Protective Equipment
Answer: D. Personal Protective Equipment

Q16. What does a reactive monitoring measure typically evaluate in a safety management system?

  1. A. Completed safety training sessions
  2. B. Workplace inspections and audits
  3. C. Past accidents, injuries, and ill-health data
  4. D. Near-miss reporting rates
Answer: C. Past accidents, injuries, and ill-health data

Q17. Under standard occupational health and safety laws, who is primarily responsible for providing and maintaining safe systems of work without cost to the worker?

  1. A. The employee
  2. B. The labor union
  3. C. The employer
  4. D. The equipment manufacturer
Answer: C. The employer

Q18. Which tool is commonly utilized during an incident investigation to identify root causes by repeatedly asking 'Why?'

  1. A. Fishbone diagram
  2. B. 5-Why technique
  3. C. Risk matrix
  4. D. Job safety analysis
Answer: B. 5-Why technique

Q19. What is a primary objective of conducting a formal workplace risk assessment?

  1. A. To systematically identify hazards and select appropriate control measures
  2. B. To eliminate the need for employee safety training entirely
  3. C. To calculate monthly production quotas for the plant
  4. D. To replace standard operating procedures with insurance documents
Answer: A. To systematically identify hazards and select appropriate control measures

Q20. When assessing workplace safety performance, which of the following represents a root-cause investigation method alongside the 5-Why technique?

  1. A. Cost-benefit analysis
  2. B. Fishbone diagram
  3. C. Gantt chart
  4. D. Critical path method
Answer: B. Fishbone diagram

Q21. What is the very first step in the recognized hazard control hierarchy?

  1. A. Substitution
  2. B. Elimination
  3. C. Administrative controls
  4. D. Personal protective equipment
Answer: B. Elimination

Q22. According to the hierarchy of risk controls, using a less hazardous chemical in place of a highly toxic solvent is known as:

  1. A. Isolation
  2. B. Elimination
  3. C. Substitution
  4. D. Engineering control
Answer: C. Substitution

Q23. Which organization co-developed the content outline for the NEBOSH National General Certificate alongside NEBOSH?

  1. A. International Labour Organization
  2. B. World Health Organization
  3. C. Health and Safety Executive
  4. D. Occupational Safety and Health Administration
Answer: C. Health and Safety Executive

Q24. In which English city is the headquarters of the National Examination Board in Occupational Safety and Health (NEBOSH) located?

  1. A. London
  2. B. Leicester
  3. C. Manchester
  4. D. Birmingham
Answer: B. Leicester

Q25. What term describes the legal requirement for employers to weigh the cost of safety measures against the reduction of risk?

  1. A. Absolute liability
  2. B. Reasonably practicable
  3. C. Zero tolerance
  4. D. Strict compliance
Answer: B. Reasonably practicable
