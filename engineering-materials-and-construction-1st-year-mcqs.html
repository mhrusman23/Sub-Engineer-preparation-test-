Q1. Clay is also called.

  1. A. Alumina
  2. B. Silica
  3. C. Silt
  4. D. Iron oxide
Answer: A. Alumina

Q2. Stone is a material of construction.

  1. A. Artificial
  2. B. Natural
  3. C. Handmade
  4. D. All of these
Answer: B. Natural

Q3. Lime has been used as a material since time immemorial.

  1. A. Coloring
  2. B. Cementing
  3. C. Special
  4. D. None of these
Answer: B. Cementing

Q4. Cement is firstly introduced in :

  1. A. 1824
  2. B. 900
  3. C. 1885
  4. D. 1920
Answer: A. 1824

Q5. Sand consists of small grains of

  1. A. Silica
  2. B. Alumina
  3. C. Carbon
  4. D. All of these
Answer: A. Silica

Q6. Mortar may be defined as a formed by mixing, water, aggregates and binding material.

  1. A. Paste
  2. B. Product
  3. C. Solid
  4. D. Liquid
Answer: A. Paste

Q7. Concrete in which steel bars are used, is called

  1. A. R.B.C
  2. B. R.C.C
  3. C. P.C.C
  4. D. All of these
Answer: B. R.C.C

Q8. Those metals in which iron is the main constituent are called.

  1. A. Ferrous metal
  2. B. Pig iron
  3. C. Non ferrous metal
  4. D. All of these
Answer: A. Ferrous metal

Q9. The ingredient which provide colour to the paint is called

  1. A. Vehicle
  2. B. Base
  3. C. Pigment
  4. D. All of these
Answer: C. Pigment

Q10. The raw material used in the manufacture of glass.

  1. A. Clay
  2. B. Zinc
  3. C. Silica
  4. D. All of these
Answer: C. Silica

Q11. A material composed of two or more materials performing batter while its constituents can be identified.

  1. A. Compatible
  2. B. Hi-profile
  3. C. Composite
  4. D. Hi-tech
Answer: C. Composite

Q12. Fire resistant material used with cement.

  1. A. Marble Powder
  2. B. Asbestos
  3. C. Granite
  4. D. All of these
Answer: B. Asbestos

Q13. The primary object of a is to provide shelter to the inmates.

  1. A. Sub-structure
  2. B. Building
  3. C. Super-structure
  4. D. All of these
Answer: B. Building

Q14. Foundation whose depth is equal to its width, will be called

  1. A. Shallow foundation
  2. B. Any one
  3. C. Well foundation
  4. D. Deep foundation
Answer: A. Shallow foundation

Q15. Number of bricks required for one cubic meter of brick masonry,

  1. A. 500
  2. B. 400
  3. C. 600
  4. D. 1000
Answer: A. 500

Q16. The penetration of water contents inside a building is known as,

  1. A. Dampness
  2. B. Salinity
  3. C. Increases
  4. D. All of these
Answer: A. Dampness

Q17. Walls are constructed to protect the inmates from wind and .

  1. A. Weather
  2. B. Sun
  3. C. Light
  4. D. None of these

Q18. The wedge-shaped blocks used to construct the arch are called,

  1. A. Crown
  2. B. Skew back
  3. C. Voussoirs

Q19. The vertical members of the frame of door are called,

  1. A. Posts
  2. B. Heads

Q20. A flat mud roof having two layers of tiles.

  1. A. R.C.C
  2. B. First class
  3. C. R.B

  1. A. Painting
  2. B. Plastering
  3. C. Pointing
  4. D. All of these

Q22. Crushing strength of first-class bricks should not be less than in kg/cm2

  1. A. 105
  2. B. 35
  3. C. 70

  1. A. Laminated rocks
