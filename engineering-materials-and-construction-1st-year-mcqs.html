Q1. Clay is also called.

  1. A. Iron oxide
  2. B. Silica
  3. C. Silt
  4. D. Alumina
Answer: D. Alumina

Q2. The burning of bricks in permanent kiln is completed within days.

  1. A. 10
  2. B. 9
  3. C. 8
  4. D. 7
Answer: D. 7

Q3. Which is over burnt bricks.

  1. A. Jhama
  2. B. Pilla
  3. C. Fire brick
  4. D. 3rd Class brick
Answer: A. Jhama

Q4. Most common material for masonry work in Pakistan is

  1. A. Stone
  2. B. Tiles
  3. C. Conic Blocks
  4. D. Bricks
Answer: D. Bricks

Q5. Which is main ingredient of brick making.

  1. A. Lime
  2. B. Silica
  3. C. Magnesia
  4. D. Clay
Answer: B. Silica

Q6. A good brick should not break when dropped from a height in meters.

  1. A. 1
  2. B. 2
  3. C. 3
  4. D. 4
Answer: A. 1

Q7. The percentage of sand in good earth for making bricks.

  1. A. 50-60
  2. B. 20-30
  3. C. 40-50
  4. D. 30-40
Answer: A. 50-60

Q8. The percentage of alumina in a good brick earth lies between.

  1. A. 5-10
  2. B. 10-15
  3. C. 20-30
  4. D. 15-20
Answer: C. 20-30

Q9. Red Colour of brick depends upon.

  1. A. Burning
  2. B. Alumina
  3. C. Iron oxide

Answer: C. Iron oxide

Q10. A first class brick should not absorb water more than, its weight

  1. A. 10%
  2. B. 20%
  3. C. 25%
  4. D. 15%

Q11. Allowable absorption of water in 1st class brick is

  2. B. 1/8

  2. B. 70

Q13. Crushing strength of second-class bricks should not be less than in kg/cm2

  1. A. 105
  2. B. 90
  3. C. 70
  4. D. 80

Q14. The main function of alumina in brick earth is to impart.

  1. A. Colour
  2. B. Strength

Q15. Size of mould is kept more than the size of fully burnt brick by
