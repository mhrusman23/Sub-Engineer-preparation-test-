Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Engrgroup23 (ppscengr.com, "we", "us") collects, how we use it and what choices you have. By using the website you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: when you sign in with Google, Email or Phone number, we receive your name, email address or phone number and profile picture (if you use Google) through Google Firebase Authentication. Guest users are given an anonymous ID.
  • Practice data: your test answers, scores, saved attempts, and the questions you report, so that you can resume and see your progress.
  • Technical data: basic device and browser information, and your approximate network address, which are used to keep the service secure and to prevent abuse of the free guest access.
  • Stored in your browser: settings, a saved copy of the question bank and the app files (local storage, IndexedDB and cache) so the website opens faster. You can clear these in your browser settings.

2. How we use information

  • To let you sign in, run tests and save your progress.
  • To keep the website secure, fix errors and improve it.
  • To answer your messages.
  • To show advertising that helps keep the website free (see section 4).

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Services we use

We use Google Firebase (login and database), Vercel (hosting) and Google Gemini (the optional AI helper: when you ask the AI about a question, that question text is sent to the AI service to produce an answer).

4. Advertising and cookies

We may show ads from third-party networks such as Google AdSense and Adsterra. These companies may use cookies or similar technologies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. Google uses advertising cookies to serve ads based on your visit to our site and other sites on the internet. You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, and learn more at policies.google.com/technologies/ads. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

5. Data sharing

We share information only with the service providers listed above, who process it for us, or when the law requires it. We do not share your practice data with advertisers.

6. Data retention and your rights

We keep your account and practice data while your account is active. You may ask us to access, correct or delete your data by emailing maherusman1947@gmail.com.

7. Children

The website is meant for students and job candidates. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you think a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will remove it.

8. Security

We use reputable services and access rules to protect your data, but no method of transmission or storage over the internet is completely secure.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest version.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy: maherusman1947@gmail.com.
