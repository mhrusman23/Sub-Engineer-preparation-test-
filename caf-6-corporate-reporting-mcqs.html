Q1. Consolidated financial statements present a parent and its subsidiaries as:

  1. A. A partnership
  2. B. A branch
  3. C. Separate entities only
  4. D. A single economic entity
Answer: D. A single economic entity

Q2. A parent controls a subsidiary when it has power, exposure to variable returns and the ability to:

  1. A. Use its power to affect returns
  2. B. Appoint auditors only
  3. C. File taxes
  4. D. Pay dividends only
Answer: A. Use its power to affect returns

Q3. Non-controlling interest represents the equity in a subsidiary not owned by the:

  1. A. Parent
  2. B. Auditor
  3. C. Bank
  4. D. Regulator
Answer: A. Parent

Q4. Goodwill on acquisition arises when the consideration exceeds the:

  1. A. Dividends paid
  2. B. Share capital
  3. C. Book value of cash
  4. D. Fair value of net assets acquired
Answer: D. Fair value of net assets acquired

Q5. Intra-group sales are:

  1. A. Eliminated on consolidation
  2. B. Added to tax
  3. C. Ignored in the parent only
  4. D. Doubled
Answer: A. Eliminated on consolidation

Q6. IAS 21 deals with the effects of changes in:

  1. A. Tax rates
  2. B. Foreign exchange rates
  3. C. Share prices
  4. D. Interest rates
Answer: B. Foreign exchange rates

Q7. IAS 36 deals with:

  1. A. Revenue
  2. B. Inventories
  3. C. Impairment of assets
  4. D. Leases
Answer: C. Impairment of assets

Q8. IFRS 15 is about:

  1. A. Employee benefits
  2. B. Insurance
  3. C. Revenue from contracts with customers
  4. D. Leases
Answer: C. Revenue from contracts with customers

Q9. IAS 12 relates to:

  1. A. Segment reporting
  2. B. Provisions
  3. C. Income taxes
  4. D. Borrowing costs
Answer: C. Income taxes

Q10. The equity method is used for:

  1. A. Subsidiaries
  2. B. Associates and joint ventures
  3. C. Inventories
  4. D. Leases
Answer: B. Associates and joint ventures

Q11. Which standard prescribes the accounting treatment for borrowing costs that are directly attributable to the acquisition, construction, or production of a qualifying asset?

  1. A. IAS 16
  2. B. IAS 23
  3. C. IAS 38
  4. D. IAS 40
Answer: B. IAS 23

Q12. Under IFRS 16, how should a lessee initially measure its lease liability?

  1. A. At fair value of the asset
  2. B. At historical cost
  3. C. At the present value of lease payments not yet paid
  4. D. At total future cash outflows
Answer: C. At the present value of lease payments not yet paid

Q13. What is the primary objective of IAS 37 regarding provisions, contingent liabilities, and contingent assets?

  1. A. To ensure appropriate recognition criteria and measurement bases are applied
  2. B. To determine the fair value of financial instruments
  3. C. To calculate depreciation schedules for heavy machinery
  4. D. To value inventory at the lower of cost and net realizable value
Answer: A. To ensure appropriate recognition criteria and measurement bases are applied

Q14. Under IFRS 9, how are financial assets classified for subsequent measurement?

  1. A. Amortized cost, FVOCI, and FVTPL
  2. B. Cost model and revaluation model
  3. C. Historical cost and current cost
  4. D. Fair value through equity only
Answer: A. Amortized cost, FVOCI, and FVTPL

Q15. According to IAS 12, temporary differences are differences between which two values?

  1. A. Carrying amount of an asset or liability and its tax base
  2. B. Market value and book value
  3. C. Historical cost and replacement cost
  4. D. Nominal value and present value
Answer: A. Carrying amount of an asset or liability and its tax base

Q16. How does IFRS 15 define the recognition of revenue from contracts with customers?

  1. A. When cash is collected from the customer
  2. B. When control of the promised goods or services is transferred to the customer
  3. C. When the sales contract is legally signed
  4. D. When the manufacturing process is completed
Answer: B. When control of the promised goods or services is transferred to the customer

Q17. Which accounting standard outlines the requirements for recognizing and measuring provisions, contingent liabilities and contingent assets?

  1. A. IAS 32
  2. B. IAS 37
  3. C. IAS 39
  4. D. IFRS 13
Answer: B. IAS 37

Q18. Under IFRS 16, a lessee recognizes which two items on the commencement date of a lease?

  1. A. A right-of-use asset and a lease liability
  2. B. An operating expense and cash
  3. C. An intangible asset and accounts payable
  4. D. A finance lease asset and interest payable
Answer: A. A right-of-use asset and a lease liability

Q19. According to IAS 38, how are internally generated goodwill and brands treated?

  1. A. Recognized as an intangible asset
  2. B. Recognized as other comprehensive income
  3. C. Recognized in profit or loss directly
  4. D. Not recognized as an asset
Answer: D. Not recognized as an asset

Q20. IAS 16 prescribes the accounting treatment for which of the following?

  1. A. Intangible assets
  2. B. Property, plant and equipment
  3. C. Investment property
  4. D. Inventories
Answer: B. Property, plant and equipment

Q21. Under IFRS 9, financial assets measured at amortized cost are subject to which impairment model?

  1. A. Incurred loss model
  2. B. Fair value through profit or loss model
  3. C. Expected credit loss model
  4. D. Historical cost recovery model
Answer: C. Expected credit loss model

Q22. IAS 23 requires borrowing costs that are directly attributable to the acquisition, construction or production of a qualifying asset to be:

  1. A. Expensed immediately in profit or loss
  2. B. Capitalized as part of the cost of that asset
  3. C. Deferred and amortized over five years
  4. D. Credited directly to equity reserves
Answer: B. Capitalized as part of the cost of that asset

Q23. Which specific standard governs the accounting treatment for government grants and the disclosure of government assistance?

  1. A. IAS 26
  2. B. IAS 20
  3. C. IAS 41
  4. D. IAS 10
Answer: B. IAS 20

Q24. Under IAS 21, the currency of the primary economic environment in which an entity operates is known as what?

  1. A. Reporting currency
  2. B. Functional currency
  3. C. Presentation currency
  4. D. Nominal currency
Answer: B. Functional currency

Q25. Which standard specifies the recognition and measurement criteria for biological assets and agricultural activity?

  1. A. IAS 40
  2. B. IAS 41
  3. C. IAS 2
  4. D. IAS 16
Answer: B. IAS 41
