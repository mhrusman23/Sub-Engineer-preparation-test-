Q1. The average duration of a normal pregnancy is:

  1. A. 36 weeks
  2. B. 40 weeks
  3. C. 44 weeks
  4. D. 30 weeks
Answer: B. 40 weeks

Q2. Ectopic pregnancy most commonly occurs in the:

  1. A. Ovary
  2. B. Fallopian tube
  3. C. Uterus
  4. D. Cervix
Answer: B. Fallopian tube

Q3. The hormone detected by a urine pregnancy test is:

  1. A. Estrogen
  2. B. hCG
  3. C. Progesterone
  4. D. Prolactin
Answer: B. hCG

Q4. Postpartum haemorrhage is most commonly caused by:

  1. A. Infection
  2. B. Uterine atony (failure of the uterus to contract)
  3. C. High blood pressure
  4. D. Anaemia
Answer: B. Uterine atony (failure of the uterus to contract)

Q5. The placenta is delivered in which stage of labour?

  1. A. First
  2. B. Second
  3. C. Third
  4. D. Fourth
Answer: C. Third

Q6. Gestational diabetes is screened for at around:

  1. A. 8 weeks
  2. B. 24-28 weeks
  3. C. 36 weeks
  4. D. After delivery
Answer: B. 24-28 weeks

Q7. A Pap smear screens for:

  1. A. Ovarian cancer
  2. B. Cervical cancer
  3. C. Breast cancer
  4. D. Infection only
Answer: B. Cervical cancer

Q8. The most common cause of maternal death worldwide is:

  1. A. Infection
  2. B. Severe bleeding (haemorrhage)
  3. C. Diabetes
  4. D. Heart disease
Answer: B. Severe bleeding (haemorrhage)

Q9. During pregnancy, lying flat on the back late in pregnancy can cause:

  1. A. Better circulation
  2. B. Supine hypotension from vena cava compression
  3. C. Faster labour
  4. D. Nothing
Answer: B. Supine hypotension from vena cava compression

Q10. The fetal presentation most favourable for vaginal delivery is:

  1. A. Breech
  2. B. Cephalic (head first)
  3. C. Shoulder
  4. D. Face
Answer: B. Cephalic (head first)

Q11. A 32-year-old primigravida at 34 weeks of gestation presents with severe headache, visual disturbances, and a blood pressure of 160/110 mmHg. Laboratory investigations reveal elevated liver enzymes and a platelet count of 80,000/uL. What is the most appropriate initial emergency management?

  1. A. Administer oral labetalol and observe for 24 hours
  2. B. Administer intravenous magnesium sulfate and an antihypertensive agent
  3. C. Perform an immediate emergency Cesarean section without stabilization
  4. D. Send blood for repeat tests and discharge the patient with oral antiplatelet therapy
Answer: B. Administer intravenous magnesium sulfate and an antihypertensive agent

Q12. What is the primary mechanism of action of combined oral contraceptive pills in preventing unwanted pregnancies?

  1. A. Destruction of mature spermatozoa in the uterine cavity
  2. B. Inhibition of ovulation via suppression of gonadotropin release
  3. C. Permanent blockage of the fallopian tubes
  4. D. Accelerated transport of the fertilized ovum causing failed implantation
Answer: B. Inhibition of ovulation via suppression of gonadotropin release

Q13. During the management of shoulder dystocia during vaginal delivery, which primary maneuver involves hyperflexion and abduction of the mother's hips against her abdomen?

  1. A. McRoberts maneuver
  2. B. Zavanelli maneuver
  3. C. Woods screw maneuver
  4. D. Rubin maneuver
Answer: A. McRoberts maneuver

Q14. In the physiological changes of normal pregnancy, which cardiovascular parameter typically decreases during the second trimester compared to the pre-pregnancy state?

  1. A. Total blood volume
  2. B. Cardiac output
  3. C. Systemic vascular resistance
  4. D. Heart rate
Answer: C. Systemic vascular resistance

Q15. Which of the following is the most sensitive and specific initial imaging modality for diagnosing a suspected ectopic pregnancy in a hemodynamically stable patient with a positive pregnancy test?

  1. A. Abdominal plain radiograph
  2. B. Transvaginal ultrasonography
  3. C. Magnetic resonance imaging of the pelvis
  4. D. Computed tomography scan of the abdomen
Answer: B. Transvaginal ultrasonography

Q16. Which of the following interventions is considered standard primary prophylaxis for preventing neural tube defects in women of childbearing age planning a pregnancy?

  1. A. Daily intake of Vitamin C supplementation
  2. B. Daily intake of Folic Acid supplementation
  3. C. Routine administration of Vitamin D injections
  4. D. Weekly iron chelation therapy
Answer: B. Daily intake of Folic Acid supplementation

Q17. Which specific pelvic ligament is primarily ligated during a total hysterectomy to secure the main uterine blood supply?

  1. A. Round ligament
  2. B. Cardinal ligament
  3. C. Broad ligament
  4. D. Ovarian suspensory ligament
Answer: B. Cardinal ligament

Q18. What is the primary hormonal abnormality associated with Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS)?

  1. A. Estrogen deficiency
  2. B. Hyperandrogenism
  3. C. Hypoprolactinemia
  4. D. Progesterone excess
Answer: B. Hyperandrogenism

Q19. What is the initial pharmacological treatment of choice for symptomatic prolactinoma?

  1. A. Clomiphene citrate
  2. B. Bromocriptine
  3. C. Levonorgestrel
  4. D. Oxytocin
Answer: B. Bromocriptine

Q20. Which microorganism is the most frequent cause of pelvic inflammatory disease (PID)?

  1. A. Escherichia coli
  2. B. Chlamydia trachomatis
  3. C. Candida albicans
  4. D. Treponema pallidum
Answer: B. Chlamydia trachomatis

Q21. Which diagnostic imaging modality is considered the gold standard for evaluating congenital uterine anomalies and endometrial cavity pathology?

  1. A. Abdominal ultrasound
  2. B. Hysteroscopy
  3. C. Plain pelvic X-ray
  4. D. Computed tomography
Answer: B. Hysteroscopy

Q22. What is the hallmark clinical presentation of adenomyosis?

  1. A. Painful menstruation and heavy menstrual bleeding
  2. B. Postmenopausal bleeding only
  3. C. Amenorrhea and galactorrhea
  4. D. Watery vaginal discharge
Answer: A. Painful menstruation and heavy menstrual bleeding

Q23. What is the most frequent chromosomal abnormality identified in cases of early spontaneous first-trimester miscarriage?

  1. A. Trisomy 21
  2. B. Monosomy X (Turner syndrome)
  3. C. Autosomal trisomy
  4. D. Triploidy
Answer: C. Autosomal trisomy

Q24. What is the primary pharmacological agent utilized for the initial stabilization and prevention of seizures in a patient with severe preeclampsia?

  1. A. Labetalol
  2. B. Magnesium sulfate
  3. C. Phenytoin
  4. D. Diazepam
Answer: B. Magnesium sulfate

Q25. In the management of an acute postpartum hemorrhage unresponsive to initial oxytocin infusion, which second-line uterotonic medication is generally contraindicated in patients with a history of chronic hypertension?

  1. A. Carboprost
  2. B. Methylergonovine
  3. C. Misoprostol
  4. D. Tranexamic acid
Answer: B. Methylergonovine
