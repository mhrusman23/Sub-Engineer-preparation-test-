Q1. The average duration of a normal pregnancy is:

  1. A. 36 weeks
  2. B. 40 weeks
  3. C. 44 weeks
  4. D. 30 weeks
Answer: B. 40 weeks

Q2. Ectopic pregnancy most commonly occurs in the:

  1. A. Ovary
  2. B. Fallopian tube
  3. C. Uterus
  4. D. Cervix
Answer: B. Fallopian tube

Q3. The hormone detected by a urine pregnancy test is:

  1. A. Estrogen
  2. B. hCG
  3. C. Progesterone
  4. D. Prolactin
Answer: B. hCG

Q4. Postpartum haemorrhage is most commonly caused by:

  1. A. Infection
  2. B. Uterine atony (failure of the uterus to contract)
  3. C. High blood pressure
  4. D. Anaemia
Answer: B. Uterine atony (failure of the uterus to contract)

Q5. The placenta is delivered in which stage of labour?

  1. A. First
  2. B. Second
  3. C. Third
  4. D. Fourth
Answer: C. Third

Q6. Gestational diabetes is screened for at around:

  1. A. 8 weeks
  2. B. 24-28 weeks
  3. C. 36 weeks
  4. D. After delivery
Answer: B. 24-28 weeks

Q7. A Pap smear screens for:

  1. A. Ovarian cancer
  2. B. Cervical cancer
  3. C. Breast cancer
  4. D. Infection only
Answer: B. Cervical cancer

Q8. The most common cause of maternal death worldwide is:

  1. A. Infection
  2. B. Severe bleeding (haemorrhage)
  3. C. Diabetes
  4. D. Heart disease
Answer: B. Severe bleeding (haemorrhage)

Q9. During pregnancy, lying flat on the back late in pregnancy can cause:

  1. A. Better circulation
  2. B. Supine hypotension from vena cava compression
  3. C. Faster labour
  4. D. Nothing
Answer: B. Supine hypotension from vena cava compression

Q10. The fetal presentation most favourable for vaginal delivery is:

  1. A. Breech
  2. B. Cephalic (head first)
  3. C. Shoulder
  4. D. Face
Answer: B. Cephalic (head first)
