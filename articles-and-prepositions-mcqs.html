Q1. She is ___ honest girl.

  1. A. a
  2. B. an
  3. C. the
  4. D. no article
Answer: B. an

Q2. ___ sun rises in the east.

  1. A. A
  2. B. An
  3. C. The
  4. D. No article
Answer: C. The

Q3. He plays ___ football every Sunday.

  1. A. a
  2. B. an
  3. C. the
  4. D. no article
Answer: D. no article

Q4. I saw ___ movie yesterday. ___ movie was very interesting.

  1. A. a / The
  2. B. the / A
  3. C. an / The
  4. D. a / A
Answer: A. a / The

Q5. She is ___ best student in the class.

  1. A. a
  2. B. an
  3. C. the
  4. D. no article
Answer: C. the

  1. A. in

  3. C. on
  4. D. by
Answer: B. at
