Q1. The magnetic flux is measured in which of the following units?

  1. A. Tesla
  2. B. Weber
  3. C. Henry
  4. D. Gauss
Answer: B. Weber

Q2. The unit of magnetic flux is the:

  1. A. Tesla
  2. B. Weber
  3. C. Henry
  4. D. Gauss only
Answer: B. Weber

Q3. The unit of magnetic flux density is the:

  1. A. Tesla
  2. B. Henry
  3. C. Ampere
  4. D. Weber
Answer: A. Tesla

Q4. Faraday's law relates induced EMF to the rate of change of:

  1. A. Current only
  2. B. Resistance
  3. C. Magnetic flux
  4. D. Voltage only
Answer: C. Magnetic flux

Q5. Lenz's law states that induced current:

  1. A. Is always zero
  2. B. Opposes the change producing it
  3. C. Aids the change producing it
  4. D. Is always constant
Answer: B. Opposes the change producing it

Q6. The speed of electromagnetic waves in free space is about:

  1. A. 3 x 10^6 m/s
  2. B. 3 x 10^10 m/s
  3. C. 3 x 10^5 m/s
  4. D. 3 x 10^8 m/s
Answer: D. 3 x 10^8 m/s

Q7. Coulomb's law describes the force between:

  1. A. Two currents only
  2. B. Two point charges
  3. C. Two magnets only
  4. D. Two masses
Answer: B. Two point charges

Q8. The SI unit of electric field strength is:

  1. A. Newton
  2. B. Volt per metre
  3. C. Coulomb
  4. D. Ampere
Answer: B. Volt per metre

Q9. A material with relative permeability much greater than 1 is:

  1. A. Insulating
  2. B. Diamagnetic
  3. C. Superconducting only
  4. D. Ferromagnetic
Answer: D. Ferromagnetic

Q10. The direction of force on a current-carrying conductor in a magnetic field follows:

  1. A. Ohm's law
  2. B. Lenz's law only
  3. C. Fleming's right-hand rule
  4. D. Fleming's left-hand rule
Answer: D. Fleming's left-hand rule

Q11. Skin effect causes high-frequency current to flow mainly:

  1. A. At the centre
  2. B. Equally throughout
  3. C. Outside the conductor
  4. D. Near the surface of a conductor
Answer: D. Near the surface of a conductor

Q12. Which quantity is measured in tesla (T) in the SI system?

  1. A. Electric field strength
  2. B. Magnetic flux density
  3. C. Electrical resistance
  4. D. Capacitance
Answer: B. Magnetic flux density

Q13. When a conductor moves through a uniform magnetic field, the direction of the induced current is determined by which rule?

  1. A. Fleming's left-hand rule
  2. B. Fleming's right-hand rule
  3. C. Lenz's law
  4. D. Biot–Savart law
Answer: A. Fleming's left-hand rule

Q14. Which law states that the induced electromotive force is proportional to the negative rate of change of magnetic flux?

  1. A. Gauss's law for magnetism
  2. B. Faraday's law of induction
  3. C. Ampère's circuital law
  4. D. Biot–Savart law
Answer: B. Faraday's law of induction

Q15. Which of the following best describes the skin depth in a good conductor at high frequency?

  1. A. It increases with frequency.
  2. B. It is independent of frequency.
  3. C. It decreases as frequency rises.
  4. D. It is maximum at the resonant frequency.
Answer: C. It decreases as frequency rises.

Q16. What is the primary factor that determines the inductance of a straight solenoid?

  1. A. Core material permeability
  2. B. Wire color
  3. C. Ambient temperature
  4. D. Source voltage frequency
Answer: A. Core material permeability

Q17. Which theorem relates a closed-loop line integral of a magnetic field to the total current passing through the surface?

  1. A. Gauss's Law for magnetism
  2. B. Ampere's Circuital Law
  3. C. Faraday's Law of Induction
  4. D. Biot-Savart Law
Answer: B. Ampere's Circuital Law

Q18. What happens to the capacitance of a parallel plate capacitor if the distance between its plates is doubled?

  1. A. It doubles
  2. B. It remains unchanged
  3. C. It halves
  4. D. It quadruples
Answer: C. It halves

Q19. What type of magnetic material exhibits a negative susceptibility and is slightly repelled by a magnetic field?

  1. A. Ferromagnetic
  2. B. Paramagnetic
  3. C. Diamagnetic
  4. D. Antiferromagnetic
Answer: C. Diamagnetic

Q20. Which mathematical operator is used in Maxwell's equations to describe the curl of an electric field?

  1. A. Time rate of change of magnetic flux density
  2. B. Gradient of electric potential
  3. C. Volume charge density
  4. D. Surface current density
Answer: A. Time rate of change of magnetic flux density

Q21. What is the SI unit of magnetic field intensity (H)?

  1. A. Weber
  2. B. Tesla
  3. C. Ampere-turn per meter
  4. D. Henry
Answer: C. Ampere-turn per meter
