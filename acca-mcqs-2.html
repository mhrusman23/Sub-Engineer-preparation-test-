Q1. When calculating the Weighted Average Cost of Capital (WACC), how should the cost of debt be included?

  1. A. Before-tax cost of debt
  2. B. After-tax cost of debt
  3. C. Coupon rate of the bonds
  4. D. Yield to maturity without adjustments
Answer: B. After-tax cost of debt

Q2. Which audit procedure is primarily designed to test the operating effectiveness of internal controls?

  1. A. Substantive analytical procedures
  2. B. Tests of controls
  3. C. Tests of details of balances
  4. D. Physical inventory observation
Answer: B. Tests of controls

Q3. What is the primary objective of transfer pricing within a multinational decentralized organization?

  1. A. To maximize group profit while encouraging divisional autonomy and goal congruence
  2. B. To completely eliminate corporate income tax liabilities worldwide
  3. C. To ensure all divisions report identical profit margins regardless of market conditions
  4. D. To bypass external regulatory compliance requirements
Answer: A. To maximize group profit while encouraging divisional autonomy and goal congruence

Q4. Under IFRS 9, how are financial assets classified and measured?

  1. A. Only at fair value through profit or loss
  2. B. Based on the entity's business model for managing financial assets and the contractual cash flow characteristics
  3. C. Strictly at historical cost less accumulated depreciation
  4. D. Always at fair value through other comprehensive income
Answer: B. Based on the entity's business model for managing financial assets and the contractual cash flow characteristics

Q5. In performance management, what does variance analysis primarily help management to achieve?

  1. A. Eliminating all operational costs completely
  2. B. Identifying deviations from standards to take corrective action and control performance
  3. C. Calculating exact tax liabilities for the upcoming financial year
  4. D. Determining the market share of competitors
Answer: B. Identifying deviations from standards to take corrective action and control performance

Q6. What is the key difference between a finance lease and an operating lease for a lessee under IFRS 16?

  1. A. Operating leases require the recognition of a right-of-use asset and lease liability, whereas finance leases do not
  2. B. Finance leases require the lessee to recognize a right-of-use asset and lease liability, whereas IFRS 16 eliminates the operating lease distinction for lessees
  3. C. Finance lease payments are always expensed immediately as incurred
  4. D. Operating leases involve transferring ownership by the end of the lease term
Answer: B. Finance leases require the lessee to recognize a right-of-use asset and lease liability, whereas IFRS 16 eliminates the operating lease distinction for lessees

Q7. Under IAS 37, how should an entity account for a present obligation arising from a past event where it is probable that an outflow of economic benefits will be required, but the amount cannot be measured reliably?

  1. A. Recognize a provision with a zero balance
  2. B. Recognize a liability based on management best estimate
  3. C. Disclose as a contingent liability only
  4. D. Ignore until the amount can be measured
Answer: C. Disclose as a contingent liability only

Q8. Which of the following describes the core objective of the Internal Audit function in corporate governance?

  1. A. Ensuring compliance with external tax laws only
  2. B. Providing independent and objective assurance on risk management and controls
  3. C. Preparing the statutory financial statements for shareholders
  4. D. Detecting all instances of external fraud within the entity
Answer: B. Providing independent and objective assurance on risk management and controls

Q9. An entity issues a 5-year convertible bond with an option for the holder to convert it into a fixed number of equity shares. How should this instrument be classified initially under IAS 32?

  1. A. Purely as a financial liability
  2. B. Purely as an equity instrument
  3. C. Split into separate liability and equity components
  4. D. As a derivative asset at fair value
Answer: C. Split into separate liability and equity components

Q10. In project appraisal, what is the primary limitation of the traditional Payback Period method?

  1. A. It is difficult to calculate for small projects
  2. B. It ignores cash flows occurring after the payback period
  3. C. It requires complex cost of capital estimations
  4. D. It focuses entirely on net profit rather than cash flows
Answer: B. It ignores cash flows occurring after the payback period

Q11. When performing substantive analytical procedures during an audit, what is the primary factor that influences the auditor's expectation?

  1. A. The client's budget for audit fees
  2. B. The plausibility and predictability of the relationship between data
  3. C. The physical count of inventory on the balance sheet date
  4. D. The number of journal entries posted at year-end
Answer: B. The plausibility and predictability of the relationship between data

Q12. Which of the following best defines relevant costs in short-term decision making?

  1. A. Historical costs incurred in the past financial year
  2. B. Fixed overheads allocated based on labor hours
  3. C. Future cash flows that differ between alternative courses of action
  4. D. Sunk costs that cannot be recovered
Answer: C. Future cash flows that differ between alternative courses of action

Q13. Under IFRS 10, what is the fundamental criterion for determining whether an investor has control over an investee?

  1. A. Owning more than 50% of the voting rights
  2. B. Power over the investee, exposure to variable returns, and ability to use power to affect returns
  3. C. Having representation on the board of directors
  4. D. Providing long-term debt financing to the investee
Answer: B. Power over the investee, exposure to variable returns, and ability to use power to affect returns

Q14. What is the primary purpose of establishing a transfer price between divisions in a multinational group?

  1. A. To completely eliminate group tax liabilities
  2. B. To evaluate divisional performance and encourage goal congruence
  3. C. To hide profits from external auditors
  4. D. To comply with international shipping regulations
Answer: B. To evaluate divisional performance and encourage goal congruence

Q15. In capital budgeting, how does the presence of inflation affect the net present value (NPV) calculation?

  1. A. It has no impact if cash flows are nominal
  2. B. Nominal cash flows must be discounted using a real discount rate
  3. C. Cash flows and discount rates must be treated consistently, using either real or nominal terms
  4. D. Inflation always increases the NPV of a project
Answer: C. Cash flows and discount rates must be treated consistently, using either real or nominal terms

Q16. Which of the following costs is treated as a period cost under throughput accounting?

  1. A. Direct material cost
  2. B. Direct labour cost
  3. C. Factory overhead cost
  4. D. All production costs except direct materials
Answer: D. All production costs except direct materials

Q17. An entity issues a 5% convertible bond at its par value of $100,000. Similar non-convertible bonds yield 8%. How should this instrument be initially recognized under IFRS 9?

  1. A. Split into a liability component and an equity component
  2. B. Entirely as a financial liability at $100,000
  3. C. Entirely as equity
  4. D. As a derivative financial asset
Answer: A. Split into a liability component and an equity component

Q18. When using activity-based costing (ABC), what is the primary basis for allocating batch-level overhead costs?

  1. A. Number of units produced
  2. B. Machine hours
  3. C. Number of setups or orders
  4. D. Direct labour hours
Answer: C. Number of setups or orders

Q19. Under IAS 16 Property, Plant and Equipment, what is the required accounting treatment for a revaluation surplus arising on a class of assets?

  1. A. Credited directly to profit or loss
  2. B. Credited to other comprehensive income and accumulated in equity
  3. C. Credited directly to retained earnings
  4. D. Ignored until the asset is sold
Answer: B. Credited to other comprehensive income and accumulated in equity

Q20. An entity operates a loyalty programme. Customers collect points when making purchases which can be redeemed for future goods. How should the entity account for these points under IFRS 15?

  1. A. Recognise all revenue immediately upon the initial sale
  2. B. Allocate a portion of the transaction price to the loyalty points as a separate performance obligation
  3. C. Ignore the points until they are actually redeemed by the customers
  4. D. Treat the total points issued as a contingent liability until expiry
Answer: B. Allocate a portion of the transaction price to the loyalty points as a separate performance obligation

Q21. When computing diluted earnings per share (DEPS), how are potential ordinary shares from out-of-the-money share options treated?

  1. A. Included in the weighted average number of shares using the treasury stock method
  2. B. Included with full dilution effect immediately
  3. C. Ignored because they are anti-dilutive
  4. D. Added directly to net profit
Answer: C. Ignored because they are anti-dilutive

Q22. In variance analysis, which of the following best describes a favourable material mix variance?

  1. A. Using a higher proportion of expensive materials to reduce total labour hours
  2. B. Using a higher proportion of cheaper materials compared to the standard mix
  3. C. Purchasing raw materials at a price lower than the standard budgeted price
  4. D. Consuming fewer total quantities of inputs than the standard allowed for actual output
Answer: B. Using a higher proportion of cheaper materials compared to the standard mix

Q23. In capital budgeting, which method incorporates the time value of money and provides an absolute dollar measure of shareholder wealth creation?

  1. A. Accounting rate of return
  2. B. Payback period
  3. C. Net present value
  4. D. Internal rate of return
Answer: C. Net present value

Q24. What is the primary objective of a company establishing an audit committee comprising non-executive directors?

  1. A. To handle the day-to-day operational management decisions of the company
  2. B. To determine the individual remuneration packages of all executive directors
  3. C. To enhance the credibility of financial reporting and strengthen internal control oversight
  4. D. To prepare the annual statutory financial statements independently of management
Answer: C. To enhance the credibility of financial reporting and strengthen internal control oversight

Q25. Which of the following is considered a preventative control within an enterprise's information systems environment?

  1. A. Running monthly exception reports on unusual system log-ins
  2. B. Performing periodic physical inventory counts reconciled to the general ledger
  3. C. Implementing strict user access passwords and multi-factor authentication
  4. D. Reviewing post-implementation audit reports on software deployment
Answer: C. Implementing strict user access passwords and multi-factor authentication
