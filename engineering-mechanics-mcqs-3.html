Q1. Force is measured in which SI unit?

  1. A. Newton
  2. B. Pascal
  3. C. Joule
  4. D. Watt
Answer: A. Newton

Q2. The resultant of two equal and opposite collinear forces is:

  1. A. Double
  2. B. Infinite
  3. C. Half
  4. D. Zero
Answer: D. Zero

Q3. The moment of a force about a point equals force times:

  1. A. Angle
  2. B. Time
  3. C. Perpendicular distance
  4. D. Mass
Answer: C. Perpendicular distance

Q4. The centre of gravity of a uniform rod is at its:

  1. A. Quarter point
  2. B. End
  3. C. Outside
  4. D. Midpoint
Answer: D. Midpoint

Q5. The condition for equilibrium of a coplanar force system is that the resultant force and moment are:

  1. A. Unity
  2. B. Equal
  3. C. Zero
  4. D. Maximum
Answer: C. Zero

Q6. The coefficient of friction equals limiting friction divided by:

  1. A. Speed
  2. B. Normal reaction
  3. C. Weight times area
  4. D. Mass
Answer: B. Normal reaction

Q7. Newton's first law is the law of:

  1. A. Gravity
  2. B. Force
  3. C. Inertia
  4. D. Energy
Answer: C. Inertia

Q8. Which unit is used for work or energy in the SI system?

  1. A. Joule
  2. B. Watt
  3. C. Pascal
  4. D. Newton
Answer: A. Joule

Q9. Lami's theorem is applied to forces that are:

  1. A. Parallel
  2. B. Non-coplanar only
  3. C. Concurrent and in equilibrium
  4. D. Moving
Answer: C. Concurrent and in equilibrium

Q10. Power equals work done divided by:

  1. A. Force
  2. B. Time
  3. C. Distance
  4. D. Mass
Answer: B. Time

Q11. A particle moves in a straight line with a constant acceleration of 3 m/s². After 4 s, what is its velocity if it started from rest?

  1. A. 12 m/s
  2. B. 8 m/s
  3. C. 9 m/s
  4. D. 15 m/s
Answer: A. 12 m/s

Q12. Which of the following is a condition for a body to be in static equilibrium?

  1. A. Net force is zero
  2. B. Net moment about any point is zero
  3. C. Both a and b
  4. D. Only a
Answer: C. Both a and b

Q13. A beam of length 4 m is supported at both ends. A load of 200 N is applied at the center. What is the reaction at each support?

  1. A. 50 N
  2. B. 100 N
  3. C. 150 N
  4. D. 200 N
Answer: B. 100 N

Q14. The resultant of two equal forces of magnitude 10 N acting at an angle of 60° to each other is?

  1. A. 10 N
  2. B. 13.7 N
  3. C. 17.3 N
  4. D. 20 N
Answer: C. 17.3 N

Q15. A couple produces a moment but no resultant force. What is the magnitude of the resultant force?

  1. A. Zero
  2. B. Equal to the moment
  3. C. Half the moment
  4. D. Twice the moment
Answer: A. Zero

Q16. The magnitude of the resultant of two equal forces P separated by an angle θ is:

  1. A. 2P cos(θ/2)
  2. B. 2P sin(θ/2)
  3. C. P
  4. D. P/2
Answer: A. 2P cos(θ/2)

Q17. In a coplanar force system, equilibrium is achieved when the sum of moments about any point is:

  1. A. Zero
  2. B. Equal to the resultant force
  3. C. Equal to the applied load
  4. D. Equal to the weight of the system
Answer: A. Zero

Q18. Which angle between two equal forces gives the maximum resultant magnitude?

  1. A. 0°
  2. B. 45°
  3. C. 90°
  4. D. 180°
Answer: A. 0°

Q19. What is the SI unit for measuring torque?

  1. A. Newton‑metre
  2. B. Pascal
  3. C. Joule
  4. D. Watt
Answer: A. Newton‑metre

Q20. For a body in static equilibrium, the sum of all forces acting on it must be equal to:

  1. A. zero
  2. B. its weight
  3. C. the applied load
  4. D. the normal reaction
Answer: A. zero
