IELTS Writing MCQs (IELTS & English Tests)

Free MCQs with answers for IELTS Writing. 30 questions in 2 chapter tests are in the app; the first 25 are shown here.

Q1. IELTS Academic Writing Task 1 asks you to describe:

  1. A. A story
  2. B. A graph, chart, table or process
  3. C. Your opinion
  4. D. A job application
Answer: B. A graph, chart, table or process

Q2. The minimum word count for Writing Task 1 is:

  1. A. 100 words
  2. B. 150 words
  3. C. 200 words
  4. D. 250 words
Answer: B. 150 words

Q3. Writing Task 2 is:

  1. A. A letter
  2. B. An essay
  3. C. A report
  4. D. A poem
Answer: B. An essay

Q4. Which task carries more weight in the Writing score?

  1. A. Task 1
  2. B. Task 2
  3. C. Both are equal
  4. D. Neither
Answer: B. Task 2

Q5. A good Task 1 answer begins with:

  1. A. Your personal opinion
  2. B. A paraphrase of the chart title
  3. C. A conclusion
  4. D. A question
Answer: B. A paraphrase of the chart title

Q6. Coherence and cohesion refers to:

  1. A. Handwriting
  2. B. How ideas are organised and linked
  3. C. Word count
  4. D. Spelling only
Answer: B. How ideas are organised and linked

Q7. The minimum word count for Writing Task 2 is:

  1. A. 150 words
  2. B. 200 words
  3. C. 250 words
  4. D. 300 words
Answer: C. 250 words

Q8. In Task 2, your position should be:

  1. A. Hidden
  2. B. Clear throughout the essay
  3. C. Changed in every paragraph
  4. D. Given only in the last line
Answer: B. Clear throughout the essay

Q9. Copying long phrases from the question is:

  1. A. Recommended
  2. B. Not counted and lowers the score
  3. C. Required
  4. D. Rewarded
Answer: B. Not counted and lowers the score

Q10. Total time for IELTS Writing is:

  1. A. 30 minutes
  2. B. 45 minutes
  3. C. 60 minutes
  4. D. 90 minutes
Answer: C. 60 minutes

Q11. General Training Task 1 is usually:

  1. A. A chart description
  2. B. A letter
  3. C. A story
  4. D. A poem
Answer: B. A letter

Q12. A formal letter should begin:

  1. A. 'Hey,'
  2. B. 'Dear Sir or Madam,'
  3. C. 'Hi there,'
  4. D. 'Yo,'
Answer: B. 'Dear Sir or Madam,'

Q13. And end a formal letter (unknown name) with:

  1. A. 'Love,'
  2. B. 'Yours faithfully,'
  3. C. 'Bye,'
  4. D. 'Cheers,'
Answer: B. 'Yours faithfully,'

Q14. In Task 2, 'to what extent do you agree' wants:

  1. A. A summary
  2. B. Your clear degree of agreement with reasons
  3. C. A letter
  4. D. A chart
Answer: B. Your clear degree of agreement with reasons

Q15. Using a range of sentence types mainly improves:

  1. A. Word count
  2. B. Grammatical range score
  3. C. Speed
  4. D. Handwriting
Answer: B. Grammatical range score

Q16. A paragraph should focus on:

  1. A. Many unrelated ideas
  2. B. One main idea, developed
  3. C. Only examples
  4. D. Only questions
Answer: B. One main idea, developed

Q17. Which linking word shows result?

  1. A. However
  2. B. Therefore
  3. C. Firstly
  4. D. For example
Answer: B. Therefore

Q18. Band scores for Writing go from:

  1. A. 1 to 10
  2. B. 0 to 9
  3. C. A to F
  4. D. 0 to 100
Answer: B. 0 to 9

Q19. The examiner counts words that are:

  1. A. Copied from the question — these may not count
  2. B. All equal
  3. C. Only long words
  4. D. Only in the introduction
Answer: A. Copied from the question — these may not count

Q20. Task 1 overview must NOT include:

  1. A. Main trends
  2. B. Your personal opinion
  3. C. Comparisons
  4. D. Big picture
Answer: B. Your personal opinion

Q21. For a bar chart, a good grouping is:

  1. A. By colour
  2. B. By highest/lowest values or similar patterns
  3. C. Alphabetical always
  4. D. Random
Answer: B. By highest/lowest values or similar patterns

Q22. 'Whereas' and 'while' are useful for:

  1. A. Listing
  2. B. Comparing and contrasting data
  3. C. Concluding
  4. D. Apologising
Answer: B. Comparing and contrasting data

Q23. An essay that answers only half the question will:

  1. A. Get full marks
  2. B. Lose marks for task response
  3. C. Be ignored
  4. D. Get bonus marks
Answer: B. Lose marks for task response

Q24. Informal phrases like 'a lot of' are better written as:

  1. A. 'loads of'
  2. B. 'a great deal of' / 'many'
  3. C. 'tons of'
  4. D. 'heaps of'
Answer: B. 'a great deal of' / 'many'

Q25. Planning before writing (5 minutes) helps you to:

  1. A. Waste time
  2. B. Organise ideas and score better
  3. C. Memorise essays
  4. D. Finish early only
Answer: B. Organise ideas and score better
