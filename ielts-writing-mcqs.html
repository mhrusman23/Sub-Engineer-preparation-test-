Q1. IELTS Academic Writing Task 1 asks you to describe:

  1. A. A story
  2. B. A graph, chart, table or process
  3. C. Your opinion
  4. D. A job application
Answer: B. A graph, chart, table or process

Q2. The minimum word count for Writing Task 1 is:

  1. A. 100 words
  2. B. 150 words
  3. C. 200 words
  4. D. 250 words
Answer: B. 150 words

Q3. Writing Task 2 is:

  1. A. A letter
  2. B. An essay
  3. C. A report
  4. D. A poem
Answer: B. An essay

Q4. Which task carries more weight in the Writing score?

  1. A. Task 1
  2. B. Task 2
  3. C. Both are equal
  4. D. Neither
Answer: B. Task 2

Q5. A good Task 1 answer begins with:

  1. A. Your personal opinion
  2. B. A paraphrase of the chart title
  3. C. A conclusion
  4. D. A question
Answer: B. A paraphrase of the chart title

Q6. Coherence and cohesion refers to:

  1. A. Handwriting
  2. B. How ideas are organised and linked
  3. C. Word count
  4. D. Spelling only
Answer: B. How ideas are organised and linked

Q7. The minimum word count for Writing Task 2 is:

  1. A. 150 words
  2. B. 200 words
  3. C. 250 words
  4. D. 300 words
Answer: C. 250 words

Q8. In Task 2, your position should be:

  1. A. Hidden
  2. B. Clear throughout the essay
  3. C. Changed in every paragraph
  4. D. Given only in the last line
Answer: B. Clear throughout the essay

Q9. Copying long phrases from the question is:

  1. A. Recommended
  2. B. Not counted and lowers the score
  3. C. Required
  4. D. Rewarded
Answer: B. Not counted and lowers the score

Q10. Total time for IELTS Writing is:

  1. A. 30 minutes
  2. B. 45 minutes
  3. C. 60 minutes
  4. D. 90 minutes
Answer: C. 60 minutes

Q11. How much of the total IELTS Writing score does Task 2 account for?

  1. A. One-third
  2. B. Half
  3. C. Two-thirds
  4. D. Three-quarters
Answer: C. Two-thirds

Q12. What kind of language register is strongly recommended for IELTS Academic Writing Task 2?

  1. A. Informal and conversational
  2. B. Formal and objective
  3. C. Poetic and metaphorical
  4. D. Slang-heavy and colloquial
Answer: B. Formal and objective

Q13. What is the recommended time allocation for IELTS Writing Task 1?

  1. A. 10 minutes
  2. B. 20 minutes
  3. C. 30 minutes
  4. D. 40 minutes
Answer: B. 20 minutes

Q14. Which of the following describes the format of IELTS Writing Task 1 in the Academic module?

  1. A. Writing a formal complaint letter
  2. B. Writing a personal email to a friend
  3. C. Summarizing visual information such as a chart or graph
  4. D. Writing an argumentative essay on a social topic
Answer: C. Summarizing visual information such as a chart or graph

Q15. What is the minimum required word count for IELTS Academic Writing Task 1?

  1. A. 100 words
  2. B. 150 words
  3. C. 200 words
  4. D. 250 words
Answer: B. 150 words

Q16. Which assessment criterion evaluates the logical organization and paragraphing of an essay?

  1. A. Task Achievement
  2. B. Coherence and Cohesion
  3. C. Lexical Resource
  4. D. Grammatical Range and Accuracy
Answer: B. Coherence and Cohesion

Q17. Which strategy helps achieve a high Lexical Resource score in IELTS Writing?

  1. A. Using complex words in every sentence
  2. B. Using precise vocabulary and less common lexical items naturally
  3. C. Repeating key prompt words multiple times
  4. D. Using slang and informal idioms
Answer: B. Using precise vocabulary and less common lexical items naturally

Q18. What is the primary function of the overview paragraph in an IELTS Academic Task 1 response?

  1. A. To provide personal opinions on the data
  2. B. To summarize the main trends and general patterns without minor details
  3. C. To list every single data point shown in the chart
  4. D. To introduce the writer's life story
Answer: B. To summarize the main trends and general patterns without minor details

Q19. How should an IELTS candidate approach a 'Discuss both views and give your opinion' prompt in Task 2?

  1. A. Only discuss the view they agree with
  2. B. Ignore their own opinion and remain entirely neutral
  3. C. Discuss both sides impartially and clearly state their own opinion throughout
  4. D. Write a conclusion without mentioning either view
Answer: C. Discuss both sides impartially and clearly state their own opinion throughout

Q20. Which of the following demonstrates good Grammatical Range and Accuracy?

  1. A. Using only simple sentence structures
  2. B. Using a mix of simple and complex sentence forms with few errors
  3. C. Writing very long sentences filled with grammatical mistakes
  4. D. Avoiding verbs entirely
Answer: B. Using a mix of simple and complex sentence forms with few errors

Q21. In IELTS Task 1, what error should candidates avoid regarding data representation?

  1. A. Reporting every minor fluctuation instead of main trends
  2. B. Grouping similar data together
  3. C. Using comparative language
  4. D. Writing more than 150 words
Answer: A. Reporting every minor fluctuation instead of main trends

Q22. What does Task Response evaluate in IELTS Writing Task 2?

  1. A. The neatness of handwriting
  2. B. How fully and accurately the prompt is addressed with relevant ideas and support
  3. C. The exact number of paragraphs used
  4. D. The use of colorful adjectives
Answer: B. How fully and accurately the prompt is addressed with relevant ideas and support

Q23. Which of the following is evaluated under the Lexical Resource criterion?

  1. A. Punctuation rules
  2. B. Paragraph indentation
  3. C. Vocabulary range and precision
  4. D. Logical paragraph linking
Answer: C. Vocabulary range and precision

Q24. How should candidates approach an essay prompt that asks 'To what extent do you agree or disagree?'

  1. A. Remain completely neutral throughout
  2. B. Support both sides equally without a clear stance
  3. C. State a clear position and maintain it consistently throughout the essay
  4. D. Avoid giving any personal opinion
Answer: C. State a clear position and maintain it consistently throughout the essay

Q25. What is the main purpose of an overview in an IELTS Academic Task 1 report?

  1. A. To state your personal opinion on the issue
  2. B. To summarize the main trends or general patterns without specific data
  3. C. To provide a detailed calculation of all numbers
  4. D. To conclude with a recommendation
Answer: B. To summarize the main trends or general patterns without specific data
