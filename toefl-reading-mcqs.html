Q1. TOEFL iBT Reading passages are mostly:

  1. A. Fiction stories
  2. B. Academic texts
  3. C. Advertisements
  4. D. Personal letters
Answer: B. Academic texts

Q2. A 'vocabulary question' in TOEFL Reading asks for:

  1. A. The main idea
  2. B. The closest meaning of a word
  3. C. The author's name
  4. D. A summary
Answer: B. The closest meaning of a word

Q3. An inference question asks you to:

  1. A. Find a stated fact
  2. B. Conclude something implied by the text
  3. C. Translate a sentence
  4. D. Count paragraphs
Answer: B. Conclude something implied by the text

Q4. 'The passage states that...' questions are answered by:

  1. A. Guessing
  2. B. Locating explicit information
  3. C. Outside knowledge
  4. D. The introduction only
Answer: B. Locating explicit information

Q5. A good strategy for TOEFL Reading is to:

  1. A. Read the whole passage three times first
  2. B. Read questions and search for answers
  3. C. Skip all long passages
  4. D. Memorise the passage
Answer: B. Read questions and search for answers

Q6. Reference questions ('The word 'it' refers to...') test:

  1. A. Grammar of pronouns
  2. B. Spelling
  3. C. Pronunciation
  4. D. Handwriting
Answer: A. Grammar of pronouns

Q7. The summary question is worth:

  1. A. No points
  2. B. More points than other questions
  3. C. Half a point
  4. D. Negative points
Answer: B. More points than other questions

Q8. In TOEFL, 'prose summary' choices that are wrong usually:

  1. A. Are too short
  2. B. Contain minor details or wrong information
  3. C. Use simple words
  4. D. Come first
Answer: B. Contain minor details or wrong information

Q9. TOEFL Reading is delivered:

  1. A. On paper
  2. B. On a computer
  3. C. By telephone
  4. D. In an interview
Answer: B. On a computer

Q10. To manage time in Reading, you should:

  1. A. Spend 20 minutes on one question
  2. B. Keep a steady pace and not get stuck
  3. C. Answer only easy sections
  4. D. Leave the last passage
Answer: B. Keep a steady pace and not get stuck

Q11. An inference question in TOEFL Reading requires you to:

  1. A. Guess randomly without reading
  2. B. Find a fact explicitly written in the first paragraph
  3. C. Deduce information that is strongly implied but not directly stated
  4. D. Copy a full sentence from the passage
Answer: C. Deduce information that is strongly implied but not directly stated

Q12. When encountering a 'Sentence Insertion' question in TOEFL Reading, you must:

  1. A. Rewrite the entire paragraph in your own words
  2. B. Choose the best of four square brackets where a new sentence fits logically
  3. C. Delete the longest sentence in the paragraph
  4. D. Translate the sentence into another language
Answer: B. Choose the best of four square brackets where a new sentence fits logically

Q13. A negative fact question in TOEFL Reading typically uses which of the following words?

  1. A. EXCEPT or NOT
  2. B. ALWAYS or EVERY
  3. C. MOST or LEAST
  4. D. FIRST or LAST
Answer: A. EXCEPT or NOT

Q14. What is the primary purpose of 'Rhetorical Purpose' questions in TOEFL Reading?

  1. A. To test your spelling of difficult academic terms
  2. B. To ask why the author included a specific detail, phrase, or paragraph
  3. C. To check your typing speed on the keyboard
  4. D. To determine the author's exact birth year
Answer: B. To ask why the author included a specific detail, phrase, or paragraph

Q15. Which of the following best describes the incorrect choices in a TOEFL Reading summary question?

  1. A. They are always grammatically incorrect
  2. B. They are minor details or unsupported statements not central to the passage
  3. C. They are exact quotes from the conclusion
  4. D. They are shorter than the correct answers
Answer: B. They are minor details or unsupported statements not central to the passage

Q16. What do 'Reference Questions' in the TOEFL Reading section typically test?

  1. A. The grammatical function of a relative clause
  2. B. What a pronoun like 'it' or 'they' refers to in the text
  3. C. The page number where a specific keyword appears
  4. D. The chronological order of historical events
Answer: B. What a pronoun like 'it' or 'they' refers to in the text

Q17. What is a key characteristic of wrong options in TOEFL Reading summary questions?

  1. A. They are written in a completely different language
  2. B. They contain minor details or are unmentioned ideas in the text
  3. C. They are always shorter than the correct answer choices
  4. D. They repeat the exact first sentence of the passage
Answer: B. They contain minor details or are unmentioned ideas in the text

Q18. A 'Rhetorical Purpose Question' in TOEFL Reading asks you to explain:

  1. A. Why the author included a specific detail, paragraph, or phrase
  2. B. The etymology of a difficult Latin or Greek root word
  3. C. How many syllables are in the title of the passage
  4. D. The exact financial cost of publishing the research
Answer: A. Why the author included a specific detail, paragraph, or phrase

Q19. How are passages in the TOEFL iBT Reading section generally classified?

  1. A. Excerpted chapters from undergraduate textbooks
  2. B. Short narrative poems and lyrical prose
  3. C. Daily newspaper editorials and opinion columns
  4. D. Informal travel blogs and personal diaries
Answer: A. Excerpted chapters from undergraduate textbooks

Q20. A 'Negative Fact Question' in TOEFL Reading requires test-takers to find:

  1. A. The single main idea of the whole passage
  2. B. Which option is NOT mentioned or is false according to the text
  3. C. The exact definition of a highlighted academic term
  4. D. The author's hidden emotional tone
Answer: B. Which option is NOT mentioned or is false according to the text

Q21. In a TOEFL Reading Sentence Insertion task, you must decide:

  1. A. Which paragraph should be completely deleted
  2. B. Where a new sentence best fits into the passage context
  3. C. How to rewrite the title for better SEO performance
  4. D. Which sentence contains a grammatical error
Answer: B. Where a new sentence best fits into the passage context

Q22. Which of the following best describes the subject matter of the short academic passages tested in TOEFL Reading?

  1. A. Strictly confidential corporate balance sheets
  2. B. Topics drawn from general academic fields like history or science
  3. C. Personal diary entries written by historical figures
  4. D. Fictional mystery novels with romantic subplots
Answer: B. Topics drawn from general academic fields like history or science

Q23. What type of skill is specifically evaluated by questions following short everyday messages in the reading module?

  1. A. Interpreting idiomatic or informal language usage
  2. B. Drafting complex computer programming code
  3. C. Translating ancient languages into modern English
  4. D. Performing advanced calculus computations
Answer: A. Interpreting idiomatic or informal language usage

Q24. How does skimming and scanning help a test-taker during practical reading tasks in the TOEFL exam?

  1. A. It allows you to memorize every single word verbatim
  2. B. It helps quickly locate relevant details and grasp the main purpose
  3. C. It lets you translate the passage into a second language
  4. D. It guarantees you can skip answering all comprehension questions
Answer: B. It helps quickly locate relevant details and grasp the main purpose

Q25. Which task format in the reading section involves filling in missing letters within words?

  1. A. Complete the Words
  2. B. Prose Summary Task
  3. C. Rhetorical Purpose Analysis
  4. D. Sentence Insertion Challenge
Answer: A. Complete the Words
