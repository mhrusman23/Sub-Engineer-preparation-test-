Q1. A safety sign with a red circle and line means:

  1. A. Mandatory
  2. B. Prohibition — do not do this
  3. C. Warning
  4. D. Safe condition
Answer: B. Prohibition — do not do this

Q2. Blue circular safety signs mean:

  1. A. Warning
  2. B. Mandatory action (must do)
  3. C. Fire equipment
  4. D. Emergency exit
Answer: B. Mandatory action (must do)

Q3. Yellow triangular signs give:

  1. A. Instructions
  2. B. Warnings of a hazard
  3. C. Directions to exits
  4. D. First aid points
Answer: B. Warnings of a hazard

Q4. Good housekeeping on site mainly prevents:

  1. A. Theft
  2. B. Slips, trips and falls
  3. C. Noise
  4. D. Glare
Answer: B. Slips, trips and falls

Q5. The first action when an accident happens is to:

  1. A. Blame someone
  2. B. Make the area safe and get help for the injured
  3. C. Finish the job
  4. D. Hide the evidence
Answer: B. Make the area safe and get help for the injured

Q6. Near misses should be reported because:

  1. A. They fill forms
  2. B. They warn of accidents waiting to happen
  3. C. Managers like them
  4. D. They earn bonuses
Answer: B. They warn of accidents waiting to happen

Q7. Safety inductions for new workers cover:

  1. A. Payroll
  2. B. Site rules, hazards and emergency procedures
  3. C. Holidays
  4. D. Uniform sizes
Answer: B. Site rules, hazards and emergency procedures

Q8. Fatigue at work increases risk because it:

  1. A. Improves focus
  2. B. Reduces alertness and reaction time
  3. C. Makes workers stronger
  4. D. Has no effect
Answer: B. Reduces alertness and reaction time

Q9. Alcohol and drugs policies at work exist to:

  1. A. Punish social life
  2. B. Keep impaired workers from endangering others
  3. C. Save money
  4. D. Fill paperwork
Answer: B. Keep impaired workers from endangering others

Q10. An emergency assembly point is where workers:

  1. A. Have lunch
  2. B. Gather after evacuation for a headcount
  3. C. Park cars
  4. D. Store tools
Answer: B. Gather after evacuation for a headcount
