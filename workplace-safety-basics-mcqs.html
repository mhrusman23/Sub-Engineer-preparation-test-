Q1. A safety sign with a red circle and line means:

  1. A. Mandatory
  2. B. Prohibition — do not do this
  3. C. Warning
  4. D. Safe condition
Answer: B. Prohibition — do not do this

Q2. Blue circular safety signs mean:

  1. A. Warning
  2. B. Mandatory action (must do)
  3. C. Fire equipment
  4. D. Emergency exit
Answer: B. Mandatory action (must do)

Q3. Yellow triangular signs give:

  1. A. Instructions
  2. B. Warnings of a hazard
  3. C. Directions to exits
  4. D. First aid points
Answer: B. Warnings of a hazard

Q4. Good housekeeping on site mainly prevents:

  1. A. Theft
  2. B. Slips, trips and falls
  3. C. Noise
  4. D. Glare
Answer: B. Slips, trips and falls

Q5. The first action when an accident happens is to:

  1. A. Blame someone
  2. B. Make the area safe and get help for the injured
  3. C. Finish the job
  4. D. Hide the evidence
Answer: B. Make the area safe and get help for the injured

Q6. Near misses should be reported because:

  1. A. They fill forms
  2. B. They warn of accidents waiting to happen
  3. C. Managers like them
  4. D. They earn bonuses
Answer: B. They warn of accidents waiting to happen

Q7. Safety inductions for new workers cover:

  1. A. Payroll
  2. B. Site rules, hazards and emergency procedures
  3. C. Holidays
  4. D. Uniform sizes
Answer: B. Site rules, hazards and emergency procedures

Q8. Fatigue at work increases risk because it:

  1. A. Improves focus
  2. B. Reduces alertness and reaction time
  3. C. Makes workers stronger
  4. D. Has no effect
Answer: B. Reduces alertness and reaction time

Q9. Alcohol and drugs policies at work exist to:

  1. A. Punish social life
  2. B. Keep impaired workers from endangering others
  3. C. Save money
  4. D. Fill paperwork
Answer: B. Keep impaired workers from endangering others

Q10. An emergency assembly point is where workers:

  1. A. Have lunch
  2. B. Gather after evacuation for a headcount
  3. C. Park cars
  4. D. Store tools
Answer: B. Gather after evacuation for a headcount

Q11. What is the most effective immediate action to take when discovering a small electrical fire?

  1. A. Throw water on the flames
  2. B. Cut off the power supply if safe and use the correct fire extinguisher
  3. C. Run out of the building without telling anyone
  4. D. Attempt to fan the smoke away with cardboard
Answer: B. Cut off the power supply if safe and use the correct fire extinguisher

Q12. Why do strict workplace policies regarding alcohol and drugs exist?

  1. A. To control what employees eat for lunch
  2. B. To ensure workers remain alert and do not endanger themselves or others
  3. C. To reduce the company's utility bills
  4. D. To lengthen the working hours
Answer: B. To ensure workers remain alert and do not endanger themselves or others

Q13. What is the main purpose of an emergency assembly point during an evacuation?

  1. A. To conduct a headcount and ensure everyone has escaped safely
  2. B. To hold a team meeting about production goals
  3. C. To store spare personal protective equipment
  4. D. To wait for delivery trucks to arrive
Answer: A. To conduct a headcount and ensure everyone has escaped safely

Q14. Why is maintaining good housekeeping practices essential on an industrial site?

  1. A. It reduces slips, trips, and falls by keeping walkways clear
  2. B. It makes workers finish their shifts earlier
  3. C. It eliminates the need for personal protective equipment
  4. D. It improves the wireless internet signal
Answer: A. It reduces slips, trips, and falls by keeping walkways clear

Q15. What is the primary purpose of yellow triangular safety signs in a workplace?

  1. A. Indicate mandatory actions
  2. B. Warn of potential hazards or dangers
  3. C. Show the location of fire equipment
  4. D. Mark safe escape routes
Answer: B. Warn of potential hazards or dangers

Q16. What key information should be covered during a safety induction for a new employee?

  1. A. The company's annual profit targets
  2. B. Emergency evacuation routes, fire exits, and assembly points
  3. C. The personal hobbies of the site manager
  4. D. Details of the company cafeteria menu
Answer: B. Emergency evacuation routes, fire exits, and assembly points

Q17. The main reason for enforcing a strict alcohol policy at work is to:

  1. A. Reduce workplace noise
  2. B. Prevent impaired workers from endangering others
  3. C. Increase productivity
  4. D. Ensure compliance with tax laws
Answer: B. Prevent impaired workers from endangering others

Q18. A red octagonal sign on a construction site indicates what?

  1. A. Mandatory action
  2. B. Warning of a hazard
  3. C. Information about a facility
  4. D. Instruction to use a tool
Answer: B. Warning of a hazard

Q19. Which of the following is a primary purpose of a first aid kit in the workplace?

  1. A. To store hazardous chemicals
  2. B. To provide emergency medical assistance
  3. C. To act as a fire extinguisher
  4. D. To serve as a storage container for tools
Answer: B. To provide emergency medical assistance

Q20. Which of the following best describes the function of a safety induction for new employees?

  1. A. Teaching them how to use company software
  2. B. Explaining the company’s financial policies
  3. C. Introducing them to site rules, hazards and emergency procedures
  4. D. Providing a tour of the cafeteria
Answer: C. Introducing them to site rules, hazards and emergency procedures

Q21. During a site induction, which of the following is NOT typically included in the safety briefing?

  1. A. Site layout and emergency exits
  2. B. Required personal protective equipment
  3. C. Company payroll procedures
  4. D. Hazard identification procedures
Answer: C. Company payroll procedures

Q22. When a worker notices a potential slip hazard, the correct immediate action is to:

  1. A. Ignore it and continue working
  2. B. Report it to a supervisor
  3. C. Move the hazard to a safer location
  4. D. Take a photo for evidence
Answer: B. Report it to a supervisor

Q23. Why must atmospheric conditions inside a confined space be continuously monitored even if initial testing was clear?

  1. A. To measure ambient room humidity
  2. B. The internal atmosphere can change unexpectedly during work
  3. C. To check battery levels of ventilation equipment
  4. D. To calculate overall worker productivity
Answer: B. The internal atmosphere can change unexpectedly during work

Q24. What is the primary reason for keeping a detailed record of significant findings after conducting a risk assessment?

  1. A. To comply with documentation requirements and ensure controls are implemented
  2. B. To calculate employee weekly wages
  3. C. To replace machinery user manuals
  4. D. To track daily cafeteria attendance
Answer: A. To comply with documentation requirements and ensure controls are implemented

Q25. When should an employer provide personal protective equipment (PPE) to their workers?

  1. A. Only after an injury occurs on site
  2. B. Whenever workers are exposed to health or safety risks that cannot be adequately controlled by other means
  3. C. Only during annual safety audits
  4. D. Whenever employees request a uniform change
Answer: B. Whenever workers are exposed to health or safety risks that cannot be adequately controlled by other means
