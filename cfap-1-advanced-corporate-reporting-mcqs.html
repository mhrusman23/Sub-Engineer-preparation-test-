Q1. IFRS 3 requires a business combination to be accounted for using the:

  1. A. Pooling method
  2. B. Proportionate method
  3. C. Cost method only
  4. D. Acquisition method
Answer: D. Acquisition method

Q2. Under IFRS 10, control requires power, exposure to variable returns and:

  1. A. A seat on the board only
  2. B. Majority of dividends
  3. C. Ability to use power to affect returns
  4. D. Common management only
Answer: C. Ability to use power to affect returns

Q3. IFRS 16 requires lessees to recognise a lease liability and a:

  1. A. Right-of-use asset
  2. B. Provision
  3. C. Contingent liability
  4. D. Deferred tax asset
Answer: A. Right-of-use asset

Q4. IFRS 9 impairment of financial assets is based on:

  1. A. Dividends declared
  2. B. Incurred losses only
  3. C. Cash received
  4. D. Expected credit losses
Answer: D. Expected credit losses

Q5. IFRS 13 defines fair value as an:

  1. A. Cost price
  2. B. Entry price
  3. C. Exit price
  4. D. Replacement price
Answer: C. Exit price

Q6. Deferred tax assets and liabilities arise from:

  1. A. Permanent differences
  2. B. Dividends
  3. C. Share splits
  4. D. Temporary differences
Answer: D. Temporary differences

Q7. Which IFRS covers accounting for share-based payment transactions?

  1. A. IFRS 9
  2. B. IFRS 2
  3. C. IFRS 16
  4. D. IFRS 3
Answer: B. IFRS 2

Q8. Under IAS 21, monetary items are translated at the:

  1. A. Budget rate
  2. B. Historical rate always
  3. C. Average of all years
  4. D. Closing rate
Answer: D. Closing rate

Q9. IAS 37 prohibits recognising a:

  1. A. Contingent asset
  2. B. Reliable estimate
  3. C. Provision with probable outflow
  4. D. Present obligation
Answer: A. Contingent asset

Q10. Under IFRS 8, segments are based on information reviewed by the:

  1. A. External auditor
  2. B. Chief operating decision maker
  3. C. Shareholders' meeting
  4. D. Tax authority
Answer: B. Chief operating decision maker

Q11. Under IFRS 9, how are equity investments not held for trading measured subsequently if the irrevocable election is made?

  1. A. At amortized cost
  2. B. At fair value through profit or loss
  3. C. At fair value through other comprehensive income
  4. D. At historical cost less impairment
Answer: C. At fair value through other comprehensive income

Q12. How should a parent account for a decrease in ownership interest in a subsidiary that does not result in a loss of control?

  1. A. As a gain or loss in profit or loss
  2. B. As an equity transaction with owners
  3. C. As an impairment of goodwill
  4. D. As a complete disposal of the subsidiary
Answer: B. As an equity transaction with owners

Q13. In the context of IAS 21, what is the functional currency of an entity?

  1. A. The currency of the country where the entity is registered
  2. B. The currency of the primary economic environment in which the entity operates
  3. C. Always the US Dollar
  4. D. The currency used to pay dividends
Answer: B. The currency of the primary economic environment in which the entity operates

Q14. What is the primary objective of preparing consolidated financial statements under IFRS 10?

  1. A. To replace the individual financial statements of the parent
  2. B. To present financial information of a group as a single economic entity
  3. C. To calculate the exact tax liability of the group
  4. D. To avoid auditing individual subsidiaries
Answer: B. To present financial information of a group as a single economic entity

Q15. Which of the following is classified as a defined benefit plan under IAS 19?

  1. A. A plan where the employer's obligation is fulfilled by paying fixed contributions
  2. B. A plan that defines a post-employment benefit amount to be received, usually dependent on factors like age and service
  3. C. A short-term employee benefit plan
  4. D. A profit-sharing bonus scheme
Answer: B. A plan that defines a post-employment benefit amount to be received, usually dependent on factors like age and service

Q16. Under IAS 37, what is the correct treatment for a contingent liability acquired in a business combination under IFRS 3?

  1. A. It is never recognized
  2. B. It is recognized at fair value if its fair value can be measured reliably
  3. C. It is recognized only when an outflow is probable
  4. D. It is disclosed in the notes without any measurement
Answer: B. It is recognized at fair value if its fair value can be measured reliably

Q17. According to IFRS 15, when is revenue from a performance obligation satisfied over time recognized?

  1. A. When the entity receives full cash payment
  2. B. As the customer simultaneously receives and consumes the benefits
  3. C. Only upon the final delivery of the asset
  4. D. At the discretion of management
Answer: B. As the customer simultaneously receives and consumes the benefits

Q18. How should an entity account for a change in accounting estimate under IAS 8?

  1. A. Retrospectively by restating prior period financial statements
  2. B. Prospectively by recognizing it in current and future periods
  3. C. Directly in retained earnings without affecting profit or loss
  4. D. By reversing the previous policy completely
Answer: B. Prospectively by recognizing it in current and future periods

Q19. Under IAS 12, what creates a deductible temporary difference?

  1. A. Carrying amount of an asset exceeds its tax base
  2. B. Tax base of a liability exceeds its carrying amount
  3. C. Revenue taxable in future periods
  4. D. Permanent differences between accounting and tax profits
Answer: B. Tax base of a liability exceeds its carrying amount

Q20. In accordance with IFRS 10, what triggers the loss of control of a subsidiary?

  1. A. A minor reduction in the voting share percentage
  2. B. The parent losing power over the investee due to regulatory changes or loss of shares
  3. C. A temporary absence of board members
  4. D. A decline in the subsidiary's annual net profit
Answer: B. The parent losing power over the investee due to regulatory changes or loss of shares

Q21. Under IFRS 16, how does a lessee initially measure a lease liability?

  1. A. At the total undiscounted lease payments
  2. B. At the present value of lease payments not yet paid, discounted using the interest rate implicit in the lease
  3. C. At the fair value of the underlying asset only
  4. D. At zero until the first payment is made
Answer: B. At the present value of lease payments not yet paid, discounted using the interest rate implicit in the lease

Q22. How are actuarial gains and losses for a defined benefit plan recognized under IAS 19?

  1. A. Deferred in profit or loss over the average working lives of employees
  2. B. Recognized immediately in other comprehensive income
  3. C. Charged directly to retained earnings in the period incurred
  4. D. Ignored until the plan is formally wound up
Answer: B. Recognized immediately in other comprehensive income

Q23. Under IAS 12, how should the effects of a change in tax rates on deferred tax balances generally be recognized?

  1. A. In profit or loss
  2. B. Directly in equity
  3. C. In other comprehensive income
  4. D. As a prior period error
Answer: A. In profit or loss

Q24. According to IFRS 2, equity-settled share-based payment transactions are measured at fair value at which date?

  1. A. Grant date
  2. B. Vesting date
  3. C. Exercise date
  4. D. Reporting date
Answer: A. Grant date

Q25. Under IAS 36, what is the recoverable amount of an asset defined as?

  1. A. Higher of fair value less costs to sell and value in use
  2. B. Lower of fair value less costs to sell and value in use
  3. C. Net realizable value
  4. D. Historical cost less accumulated depreciation
Answer: A. Higher of fair value less costs to sell and value in use
