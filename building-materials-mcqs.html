Q1. The minimum depth of foundation for ordinary residential buildings as per thumb rules should be:

  1. A. 0.5 meters
  2. B. 0.9 meters
  3. C. 1.5 meters
  4. D. 2.0 meters
Answer: B. 0.9 meters

Q2. The process of turning down the top of a brick wall to protect it from rain is called?

  1. A. Coping
  2. B. Cornice
  3. C. Frieze
  4. D. Plinth
Answer: A. Coping

Q3. The process of filling the hollow spaces of concrete with cement slurry is known as:

  1. A. Gouging
  2. B. Grouting
  3. C. Grinding
  4. D. Glazing
Answer: B. Grouting

Q4. Which ingredient in brick earth prevents shrinkage and warping during drying?

  1. A. Alumina
  2. B. Silica
  3. C. Lime
  4. D. Iron oxide
Answer: B. Silica

Q5. Which of the following ingredients in brick earth prevents warping and shrinking during drying?

  1. A. Alumina
  2. B. Silica
  3. C. Lime
  4. D. Iron oxide
Answer: B. Silica

Q6. The process of removing sharp irregularities from a freshly dressed stone is known as:

  1. A. Quarrying
  2. B. Seasoning
  3. C. Scabbling
  4. D. Pitching
Answer: C. Scabbling

Q7. What is the unit weight of plain cement concrete (PCC) approximately?

  1. A. 20 kN/m³
  2. B. 24 kN/m³
  3. C. 25 kN/m³
  4. D. 18 kN/m³
Answer: B. 24 kN/m³

Q8. Which test is conducted to determine the resistance of aggregates against abrasion?

  1. A. Los Angeles Abrasion Test
  2. B. Slump Test
  3. C. Vicat Needle Test
  4. D. Le Chatelier Test
Answer: A. Los Angeles Abrasion Test

Q9. Which of the following tests is used to determine the abrasion resistance of aggregates?

  1. A. Los Angeles abrasion test
  2. B. Aggregate impact test
  3. C. Soundness test
  4. D. Specific gravity test
Answer: A. Los Angeles abrasion test

Q10. Which type of bond in brick masonry consists entirely of headers in each course?

  1. A. English bond
  2. B. Flemish bond
  3. C. Header bond
  4. D. Stretcher bond
Answer: C. Header bond

Q11. What is the standard height of a damp proof course (DPC) above the plinth level in residential buildings?

  1. A. At floor finish level
  2. B. 15 cm
  3. C. 30 cm to 45 cm
  4. D. 1 meter
Answer: B. 15 cm

Q12. What is the purpose of providing a 'frog' in a brick?

  1. A. To reduce the weight of the brick
  2. B. To provide a key for the mortar joint
  3. C. To enhance the aesthetic appearance
  4. D. To increase the length of the brick
Answer: B. To provide a key for the mortar joint

Q13. Which brick masonry bond consists of alternating headers and stretchers in each course?

  1. A. English bond
  2. B. Flemish bond
  3. C. Stretcher bond
  4. D. Header bond
Answer: B. Flemish bond

Q14. The process of removing sharp corners and edges of stones is known as?

  1. A. Quarrying
  2. B. Seasoning
  3. C. Dressing
  4. D. Pitching
Answer: C. Dressing

Q15. The type of flooring suitable for dance halls and gymnasiums is:

  1. A. Terrazzo flooring
  2. B. Timber flooring
  3. C. Concrete flooring
  4. D. Mud flooring
Answer: B. Timber flooring

Q16. What is the standard size of a modular brick according to Pakistani standards including mortar thickness?

  1. A. 19 cm x 9 cm x 9 cm
  2. B. 20 cm x 10 cm x 10 cm
  3. C. 22 cm x 11 cm x 7 cm
  4. D. 23 cm x 11.4 cm x 7.6 cm
Answer: B. 20 cm x 10 cm x 10 cm

Q17. What is the maximum limit of water absorption for a first class brick after 24 hours of immersion in water?

  1. A. 10%
  2. B. 15%
  3. C. 20%
  4. D. 25%
Answer: C. 20%

Q18. The process of adding water to quick lime to convert it into hydrated lime is known as:

  1. A. Calcination
  2. B. Slaking
  3. C. Vitrification
  4. D. Carbonation
Answer: B. Slaking

Q19. Bricks are made by burning:

  1. A. Gypsum
  2. B. Cement
  3. C. Clay
  4. D. Sand only
Answer: C. Clay

Q20. The main constituent of cement is:

  1. A. Clay only
  2. B. Lime (calcium oxide)
  3. C. Gypsum
  4. D. Sand
Answer: B. Lime (calcium oxide)

Q21. White patches on brick walls caused by soluble salts are called:

  1. A. Efflorescence
  2. B. Bleeding
  3. C. Spalling
  4. D. Creep
Answer: A. Efflorescence

Q22. Sand is used in mortar to:

  1. A. Increase setting time
  2. B. Add colour
  3. C. Reduce shrinkage and add bulk
  4. D. Make mortar fluid only
Answer: C. Reduce shrinkage and add bulk

Q23. Which timber property is affected by moisture content?

  1. A. Weight only
  2. B. Strength
  3. C. Colour only
  4. D. Smell only
Answer: B. Strength

Q24. Mild steel contains carbon in the range of about:

  1. A. 2 to 4 percent
  2. B. 0.15 to 0.25 percent
  3. C. 5 to 8 percent
  4. D. 10 percent
Answer: B. 0.15 to 0.25 percent

Q25. The lime used for white-washing is:

  1. A. Gypsum only
  2. B. Slaked lime
  3. C. Clinker
  4. D. Cement
Answer: B. Slaked lime
