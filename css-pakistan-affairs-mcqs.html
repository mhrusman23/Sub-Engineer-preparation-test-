Q1. The Pakistan Movement's Lahore Resolution was passed in:

Q2. The first Constitution of Pakistan was adopted in:

  1. A. 1947

Q3. Pakistan became a republic and left the British Crown's rule formally in:

Q6. Separate electorates for Muslims were granted under:

  1. A. Indian Councils Act 1892

Answer: B. Morley-Minto Reforms 1909

Q7. The Lucknow Pact (1916) was between:

  1. A. Congress and the British
  2. B. Muslim League and Congress
  3. C. League and the Sikhs
  4. D. Congress and the princes
Answer: B. Muslim League and Congress

Q8. Pakistan's first Governor-General was:

Q9. The 1973 Constitution made Pakistan a:
