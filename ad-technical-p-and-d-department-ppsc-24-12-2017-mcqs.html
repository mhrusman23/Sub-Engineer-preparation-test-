AD Technical P&D Department PPSC (24.12.2017) – Past Paper Solved MCQs

Solved MCQs from the PPSC paper “AD Technical P&D Department PPSC (24.12.2017)”. Full paper has 60 questions; the first 25 are shown here with correct answers. Open the app to take the complete timed test.

Q1. Timber beam has width of 100 mm and depth of 150 mm. If the shear stress is 2 MPa, the value of force applied is:

  1. A. 30 kN
  2. B. 20 kN
  3. C. 40 kN
  4. D. 25 kN
Answer: A. 30 kN

Q2. Number of equations required in slope deflection method is equal to:

  1. A. Kinematic degree of indeterminacy
  2. B. Static degree of indeterminacy
  3. C. Number of supports
  4. D. Number of members
Answer: A. Kinematic degree of indeterminacy

Q3. Euler's crippling load for both ends fixed is ______ than both ends hinged.

  1. A. More
  2. B. Less
  3. C. Equal
  4. D. Twice
Answer: A. More

Q4. Effective length for both ends fixed is equal to:

  1. A. L/2
  2. B. L
  3. C. 2L
  4. D. L/sqrt(2)
Answer: A. L/2

Q5. If depth of beam is doubled, elastic strength becomes:

  1. A. Four times
  2. B. Two times
  3. C. Eight times
  4. D. Same
Answer: A. Four times

Q6. Torsion test is performed to determine:

  1. A. Shear stress
  2. B. Tensile strength
  3. C. Compressive strength
  4. D. Flexural strength
Answer: A. Shear stress

Q7. Shear stress is maximum where bending moment is:

  1. A. Zero
  2. B. Maximum
  3. C. Minimum
  4. D. Constant
Answer: A. Zero

Q8. ACI moment coefficient method is used in:

  1. A. Two way slab
  2. B. One way slab
  3. C. Beam
  4. D. Column
Answer: A. Two way slab

Q9. Point of contraflexure is where:

  1. A. B.M. changes sign
  2. B. S.F. changes sign
  3. C. B.M. is maximum
  4. D. S.F. is zero
Answer: B. S.F. changes sign

Q10. Construction joint is provided where:

  1. A. B.M. and S.F. is minimum
  2. B. B.M. is maximum
  3. C. S.F. is maximum
  4. D. Anywhere
Answer: B. B.M. is maximum

Q11. Difference between time available to do a job and time required to do a job is called:

  1. A. Float
  2. B. Duration
  3. C. Slack of project
  4. D. Activity
Answer: A. Float

Q12. Shape factor for rectangular section is 1.5 while for circular it is:

  1. A. 1.7
  2. B. 1.5
  3. C. 1.6
  4. D. 2.0
Answer: A. 1.7

Q13. Product of E and I is called:

  1. A. Flexural rigidity
  2. B. Torsional rigidity
  3. C. Axial rigidity
  4. D. Shear rigidity
Answer: A. Flexural rigidity

Q14. Radius of Mohr's circle is equal to:

  1. A. Half of difference of principal stresses
  2. B. Difference of principal stresses
  3. C. Half of sum of principal stresses
  4. D. Sum of principal stresses
Answer: A. Half of difference of principal stresses

Q15. Principal stresses are:

  1. A. Normal stresses only
  2. B. Shear stresses only
  3. C. Both normal and shear
  4. D. Zero stresses
Answer: A. Normal stresses only

Q16. If twisting (torsion) moment is applied on a member:

  1. A. Every cross section suffers shear stress
  2. B. Only end section suffers stress
  3. C. Only middle section suffers stress
  4. D. No stress is produced
Answer: A. Every cross section suffers shear stress

Q17. In bending, shear stress is maximum at the centroid while in torsion shear stress is maximum at:

  1. A. Face of the rod
  2. B. Centroid
  3. C. Quarter point
  4. D. Neutral axis
Answer: A. Face of the rod

Q18. Shear stirrups spacing increases toward the center of beam; maximum shear spacing is:

  1. A. 0.75 d
  2. B. d/2
  3. C. d
  4. D. 1.5 d
Answer: A. 0.75 d

Q19. If a curve on a hilly area is widened, it is widened on the inner side; in plain areas curve is widened:

  1. A. On both sides equally
  2. B. On inner side only
  3. C. On outer side only
  4. D. Not widened
Answer: A. On both sides equally

Q20. Cold working of steel:

  1. A. Enhances yield strength and decreases ductility
  2. B. Decreases yield strength and increases ductility
  3. C. Has no effect on strength
  4. D. Increases both strength and ductility
Answer: A. Enhances yield strength and decreases ductility

Q21. Hoop stresses are:

  1. A. Circumferential tensile stresses and are double of longitudinal stress
  2. B. Circumferential compressive stresses
  3. C. Longitudinal tensile stresses
  4. D. Radial stresses
Answer: A. Circumferential tensile stresses and are double of longitudinal stress

Q22. Critical section for punching shear is at a distance ______ from the face of the column:

  1. A. d/2
  2. B. d
  3. C. 2d
  4. D. d/4
Answer: D. d/4

Q23. If a/D is greater than 6, the member fails in:

  1. A. Bending
  2. B. Shear
  3. C. Torsion
  4. D. Crushing
Answer: A. Bending

Q24. If steel attains yield strength prior to concrete crushing, the member is:

  1. A. Under-reinforced
  2. B. Over-reinforced
  3. C. Balanced
  4. D. Doubly reinforced
Answer: A. Under-reinforced

Q25. Most common bond types in brick masonry are:

  1. A. Header, stretcher and English bonds
  2. B. Frog bond
  3. C. Herring bone bond only
  4. D. Raking bond only
Answer: A. Header, stretcher and English bonds
