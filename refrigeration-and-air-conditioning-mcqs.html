Q1. A common refrigeration cycle is the:

  1. A. Diesel cycle
  2. B. Rankine cycle
  3. C. Vapour compression cycle
  4. D. Otto cycle
Answer: C. Vapour compression cycle

Q2. The coefficient of performance (COP) of a refrigerator is the ratio of:

  1. A. Heat rejected to heat absorbed
  2. B. Work to heat rejected
  3. C. Refrigerating effect to work input
  4. D. Work input to refrigerating effect
Answer: C. Refrigerating effect to work input

Q3. Which component absorbs heat in a refrigeration system?

  1. A. Expansion valve
  2. B. Evaporator
  3. C. Compressor
  4. D. Condenser
Answer: B. Evaporator

Q4. The compressor in a vapour compression system raises the refrigerant:

  1. A. Heat capacity
  2. B. Humidity
  3. C. Pressure
  4. D. Mass
Answer: C. Pressure

Q5. Relative humidity is the ratio of vapour pressure to:

  1. A. Atmospheric pressure
  2. B. Saturation vapour pressure
  3. C. Partial vapour
  4. D. Dew point pressure
Answer: B. Saturation vapour pressure

Q6. A unit of refrigeration capacity is the:

  1. A. Ton of refrigeration
  2. B. Joule
  3. C. Kelvin
  4. D. Pascal
Answer: A. Ton of refrigeration

Q7. The expansion device in a refrigeration cycle reduces the refrigerant:

  1. A. Volume of evaporator
  2. B. Temperature of surroundings
  3. C. Mass
  4. D. Pressure
Answer: D. Pressure

Q8. Dew point temperature is the temperature at which air becomes:

  1. A. Dry
  2. B. Saturated
  3. C. Hot
  4. D. Pure
Answer: B. Saturated

Q9. In air conditioning, sensible cooling changes:

  1. A. Atmospheric pressure
  2. B. Moisture only
  3. C. Dew point only
  4. D. Dry bulb temperature
Answer: D. Dry bulb temperature

Q10. The condenser in a refrigeration system:

  1. A. Rejects heat
  2. B. Raises pressure
  3. C. Stores refrigerant only
  4. D. Absorbs heat
Answer: A. Rejects heat

Q11. What is the primary function of a liquid receiver located immediately after the condenser in a refrigeration plant?

  1. A. To superheat the refrigerant
  2. B. To store liquid refrigerant
  3. C. To drop the system pressure
  4. D. To separate oil from gas
Answer: B. To store liquid refrigerant

Q12. In a psychrometric chart, what type of line represents a constant wet-bulb temperature process?

  1. A. Horizontal line
  2. B. Vertical line
  3. C. Diagonal line sloping downwards to the right
  4. D. Curved line pointing upwards
Answer: C. Diagonal line sloping downwards to the right

Q13. Which specific thermodynamic property remains completely constant during an ideal adiabatic throttling process across an expansion valve?

  1. A. Entropy
  2. B. Enthalpy
  3. C. Temperature
  4. D. Pressure
Answer: B. Enthalpy

Q14. What undesirable phenomenon occurs when moisture enters and freezes inside a capillary tube of a small refrigeration unit?

  1. A. Liquid slugging
  2. B. Compressor surge
  3. C. Choked flow
  4. D. Moisture freeze-up or plugging
Answer: D. Moisture freeze-up or plugging

Q15. Which chemical family of refrigerants was globally phased out due to its severe ozone depletion potential?

  1. A. Hydrofluorocarbons (HFCs)
  2. B. Chlorofluorocarbons (CFCs)
  3. C. Hydrofluoroolefins (HFOs)
  4. D. Natural refrigerants
Answer: B. Chlorofluorocarbons (CFCs)

Q16. What is the primary purpose of introducing outside fresh air into a centralized air conditioning system?

  1. A. To maintain indoor oxygen levels and dilute indoor pollutants
  2. B. To reduce the cooling load on the refrigeration compressor
  3. C. To increase the overall relative humidity of the conditioned space
  4. D. To eliminate the need for an exhaust fan system
Answer: A. To maintain indoor oxygen levels and dilute indoor pollutants

Q17. What is the primary function of an accumulator placed on the suction line before a compressor?

  1. A. To increase refrigerant superheat
  2. B. To trap liquid refrigerant and prevent slugging
  3. C. To filter out solid metal particles
  4. D. To subcool the liquid refrigerant
Answer: B. To trap liquid refrigerant and prevent slugging

Q18. Which thermodynamic process describes sensible heating on a psychrometric chart?

  1. A. Moving horizontally to the right along constant wet-bulb temperature lines
  2. B. Moving vertically upward along constant moisture content lines
  3. C. Moving diagonally along constant enthalpy lines
  4. D. Moving downward along constant relative humidity lines
Answer: B. Moving vertically upward along constant moisture content lines

Q19. What happens to the volumetric efficiency of a reciprocating compressor when the compression ratio increases?

  1. A. It increases
  2. B. It decreases
  3. C. It remains completely constant
  4. D. It first increases then drops to zero
Answer: B. It decreases

Q20. Which specific device is typically utilized in large centrifugal chillers for capacity control?

  1. A. Thermostatic expansion valve
  2. B. Capillary tube
  3. C. Inlet guide vanes
  4. D. Solenoid bypass valve
Answer: C. Inlet guide vanes

Q21. What physical property characterizes azeotropic refrigerant mixtures?

  1. A. They evaporate and condense at constant temperatures like single substances
  2. B. They exhibit severe temperature glide during phase change
  3. C. They separate into component liquids inside the condenser
  4. D. They are extremely flammable under high pressure
Answer: A. They evaporate and condense at constant temperatures like single substances

Q22. During a standard defrost cycle in an air-source heat pump, how does the system typically operate?

  1. A. It shuts down all fans and activates electric resistance heaters in the duct
  2. B. It temporarily switches back to cooling mode to reject heat into the outdoor coil
  3. C. It closes the liquid line solenoid and pumps down the entire charge
  4. D. It reverses the compressor rotation direction to melt the frost
Answer: B. It temporarily switches back to cooling mode to reject heat into the outdoor coil

Q23. The latent heat absorbed by the refrigerant inside the evaporator chamber primarily causes:

  1. A. Temperature rise of the vapour
  2. B. Phase change from liquid to vapour
  3. C. Pressure drop across the coils
  4. D. Increase in dynamic viscosity
Answer: B. Phase change from liquid to vapour

Q24. In a psychrometric chart, wet-bulb depression is best defined as the difference between:

  1. A. Dry-bulb temperature and wet-bulb temperature
  2. B. Dew point temperature and wet-bulb temperature
  3. C. Dry-bulb temperature and dew point temperature
  4. D. Saturation temperature and ambient temperature
Answer: A. Dry-bulb temperature and wet-bulb temperature

Q25. A domestic refrigerator operating on the vapour compression cycle typically utilizes which type of expansion device?

  1. A. Thermostatic expansion valve
  2. B. Capillary tube
  3. C. Automatic expansion valve
  4. D. Float valve
Answer: B. Capillary tube
