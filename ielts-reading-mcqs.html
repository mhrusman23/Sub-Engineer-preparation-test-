Q1. In IELTS Academic Reading, how many passages are there?

  1. A. Two
  2. B. Three
  3. C. Four
  4. D. Five
Answer: B. Three

Q2. Skimming a passage means:

  1. A. Reading every word slowly
  2. B. Reading quickly for the general idea
  3. C. Memorising the text
  4. D. Translating each line
Answer: B. Reading quickly for the general idea

Q3. In a True / False / Not Given question, 'Not Given' means:

  1. A. The statement is false
  2. B. The information is not in the passage
  3. C. The statement is partly true
  4. D. The writer disagrees
Answer: B. The information is not in the passage

Q4. Scanning is used to find:

  1. A. The main idea
  2. B. A specific fact or number
  3. C. The writer's opinion
  4. D. Difficult words
Answer: B. A specific fact or number

Q5. How long does the IELTS Reading test last?

  1. A. 40 minutes
  2. B. 60 minutes
  3. C. 75 minutes
  4. D. 90 minutes
Answer: B. 60 minutes

Q6. A paraphrase is:

  1. A. A direct copy of the text
  2. B. The same idea written in different words
  3. C. A summary of the whole book
  4. D. A list of references
Answer: B. The same idea written in different words

Q7. In matching-headings questions, you match each paragraph with:

  1. A. A question
  2. B. Its main idea heading
  3. C. A vocabulary word
  4. D. A diagram
Answer: B. Its main idea heading

Q8. The best place to find the main idea of a paragraph is usually:

  1. A. The last word
  2. B. The first or second sentence
  3. C. The longest sentence
  4. D. The examples
Answer: B. The first or second sentence

Q9. If a word is unfamiliar in the passage, you should:

  1. A. Stop reading
  2. B. Guess its meaning from context
  3. C. Skip the whole passage
  4. D. Use a dictionary
Answer: B. Guess its meaning from context

Q10. IELTS Reading answers must be written on:

  1. A. The question paper only
  2. B. The answer sheet
  3. C. A separate notebook
  4. D. The back of the ID card
Answer: B. The answer sheet

Q11. How many separate sections are included in the standard IELTS Reading paper?

  1. A. A) Two
  2. B. B) Three
  3. C. C) Four
  4. D. D) Five
Answer: B. B) Three

Q12. Which of the following task types requires test-takers to determine if statements match the writer's opinions or claims?

  1. A. A) Identifying writer's views/claims
  2. B. B) Matching numbers and dates
  3. C. C) Diagram labeling
  4. D. D) Sentence structure analysis
Answer: A. A) Identifying writer's views/claims

Q13. Which task type involves pairing specific paragraph letters with appropriate descriptive titles?

  1. A. A) Table completion
  2. B. B) Matching headings
  3. C. C) Short-answer questions
  4. D. D) Flow-chart completion
Answer: B. B) Matching headings

Q14. For which common question type do the answers almost always follow the sequential order of information in the reading passage?

  1. A. A) Matching headings to paragraphs
  2. B. B) Identifying information (True/False/Not Given)
  3. C. C) Matching random list features
  4. D. D) Finding the best title for the whole text
Answer: B. B) Identifying information (True/False/Not Given)

Q15. What is the total number of questions that appear across the entire IELTS Reading test?

  1. A. A) 30
  2. B. B) 35
  3. C. C) 40
  4. D. D) 50
Answer: C. C) 40

Q16. What is the total time allotted for candidates to complete the IELTS Reading test?

  1. A. A) 45 minutes
  2. B. B) 60 minutes
  3. C. C) 75 minutes
  4. D. D) 90 minutes
Answer: B. B) 60 minutes

Q17. Which of the following techniques is most effective for locating a specific fact in a long passage?

  1. A. Skimming the whole passage
  2. B. Scanning for keywords
  3. C. Reading the introduction carefully
  4. D. Summarising each paragraph
Answer: B. Scanning for keywords

Q18. In a 'True/False/Not Given' question, the answer 'Not Given' indicates that:

  1. A. The statement is definitely false
  2. B. The statement is definitely true
  3. C. The passage does not provide enough information to decide
  4. D. The statement is irrelevant to the passage
Answer: C. The passage does not provide enough information to decide

Q19. If a word in the passage is unfamiliar, the best immediate action is to:

  1. A. Write down the word and move on
  2. B. Look it up in a dictionary right away
  3. C. Try to infer its meaning from the surrounding context
  4. D. Skip the sentence and continue reading
Answer: C. Try to infer its meaning from the surrounding context

Q20. Which strategy should a candidate use to quickly understand the overall theme of an IELTS reading passage without reading every word?

  1. A. Skimming
  2. B. Scanning
  3. C. Memorization
  4. D. Proofreading
Answer: A. Skimming

Q21. When a question in IELTS Reading asks to complete a summary using words from the text, what must candidates strictly follow?

  1. A. The exact word limit specified
  2. B. Their own personal opinions
  3. C. Grammatical rules of a different language
  4. D. Double the allowed number of words
Answer: A. The exact word limit specified

Q22. What is the primary purpose of using the scanning technique during the IELTS reading test?

  1. A. To locate specific facts, dates, or names quickly
  2. B. To analyze the author's psychological state
  3. C. To translate the entire passage into another language
  4. D. To check for spelling errors in the passage
Answer: A. To locate specific facts, dates, or names quickly

Q23. In IELTS sentence completion tasks, the completed sentence must maintain what relationship with the original text?

  1. A. Factual and grammatical accuracy
  2. B. Completely different meaning
  3. C. Rhyming structure
  4. D. Use of slang words
Answer: A. Factual and grammatical accuracy

Q24. What should a candidate do if they encounter an exceptionally difficult question in the IELTS Reading test?

  1. A. Mark an initial guess, move on, and return if time permits
  2. B. Spend the remaining test time on that single question
  3. C. Leave the entire test booklet blank
  4. D. Ask the invigilator for the correct answer
Answer: A. Mark an initial guess, move on, and return if time permits

Q25. Which of the following best describes 'distractors' commonly found in IELTS reading multiple-choice questions?

  1. A. Incorrect options designed to tempt test-takers who misread the text
  2. B. Extra blank pages provided for rough work
  3. C. Instructions printed at the bottom of the page
  4. D. Words highlighted in bold by the examiner
Answer: A. Incorrect options designed to tempt test-takers who misread the text
