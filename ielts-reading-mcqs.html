Q1. In IELTS Academic Reading, how many passages are there?

  1. A. Two
  2. B. Three
  3. C. Four
  4. D. Five
Answer: B. Three

Q2. Skimming a passage means:

  1. A. Reading every word slowly
  2. B. Reading quickly for the general idea
  3. C. Memorising the text
  4. D. Translating each line
Answer: B. Reading quickly for the general idea

Q3. In a True / False / Not Given question, 'Not Given' means:

  1. A. The statement is false
  2. B. The information is not in the passage
  3. C. The statement is partly true
  4. D. The writer disagrees
Answer: B. The information is not in the passage

Q4. Scanning is used to find:

  1. A. The main idea
  2. B. A specific fact or number
  3. C. The writer's opinion
  4. D. Difficult words
Answer: B. A specific fact or number

Q5. How long does the IELTS Reading test last?

  1. A. 40 minutes
  2. B. 60 minutes
  3. C. 75 minutes
  4. D. 90 minutes
Answer: B. 60 minutes

Q6. A paraphrase is:

  1. A. A direct copy of the text
  2. B. The same idea written in different words
  3. C. A summary of the whole book
  4. D. A list of references
Answer: B. The same idea written in different words

Q7. In matching-headings questions, you match each paragraph with:

  1. A. A question
  2. B. Its main idea heading
  3. C. A vocabulary word
  4. D. A diagram
Answer: B. Its main idea heading

Q8. The best place to find the main idea of a paragraph is usually:

  1. A. The last word
  2. B. The first or second sentence
  3. C. The longest sentence
  4. D. The examples
Answer: B. The first or second sentence

Q9. If a word is unfamiliar in the passage, you should:

  1. A. Stop reading
  2. B. Guess its meaning from context
  3. C. Skip the whole passage
  4. D. Use a dictionary
Answer: B. Guess its meaning from context

Q10. IELTS Reading answers must be written on:

  1. A. The question paper only
  2. B. The answer sheet
  3. C. A separate notebook
  4. D. The back of the ID card
Answer: B. The answer sheet

Q11. A passage has 5 paragraphs. A question asks about paragraph 3 only. You should:

  1. A. Read the whole passage again
  2. B. Focus on paragraph 3 and its topic sentence
  3. C. Skip it
  4. D. Read paragraph 1
Answer: B. Focus on paragraph 3 and its topic sentence

Q12. 'The writer's purpose' means:

  1. A. The writer's job
  2. B. Why the writer wrote the text
  3. C. The writer's age
  4. D. The text length
Answer: B. Why the writer wrote the text

Q13. In Yes / No / Not Given questions, answers follow:

  1. A. The writer's opinions, not general facts
  2. B. Your own knowledge
  3. C. The title only
  4. D. The last paragraph
Answer: A. The writer's opinions, not general facts

Q14. A heading that says 'The drawbacks of tourism' matches a paragraph about:

  1. A. Benefits of travel
  2. B. Negative effects of tourism
  3. C. Hotel prices
  4. D. Famous cities
Answer: B. Negative effects of tourism

Q15. When two answers look similar, you should:

  1. A. Choose the first
  2. B. Re-read the exact lines in the passage
  3. C. Guess
  4. D. Leave it blank
Answer: B. Re-read the exact lines in the passage

Q16. Time management: 60 minutes for 40 questions means roughly:

  1. A. 5 minutes per question
  2. B. 1.5 minutes per question
  3. C. 30 seconds per question
  4. D. No limit
Answer: B. 1.5 minutes per question

Q17. A 'summary completion' task is best done by:

  1. A. Reading the whole book
  2. B. Reading the summary, predicting word types, then scanning the passage
  3. C. Guessing words
  4. D. Writing your own summary
Answer: B. Reading the summary, predicting word types, then scanning the passage

Q18. Words like 'although' and 'despite' signal:

  1. A. Addition
  2. B. Contrast
  3. C. Cause
  4. D. Order
Answer: B. Contrast

Q19. If a statement says 'ALL students passed' but the passage says 'most students passed', the answer is:

  1. A. True
  2. B. False
  3. C. Not Given
  4. D. Yes
Answer: B. False

Q20. The best order to answer a passage is:

  1. A. Always question 1 first, never skip
  2. B. Answer easier questions first, return to hard ones
  3. C. Only the last questions
  4. D. Random
Answer: B. Answer easier questions first, return to hard ones

Q21. A diagram-labelling question needs answers that are:

  1. A. Long sentences
  2. B. Short labels taken from the passage
  3. C. Your opinions
  4. D. Definitions
Answer: B. Short labels taken from the passage

Q22. 'Infer' means to:

  1. A. Copy a line
  2. B. Work out something not directly stated
  3. C. Translate
  4. D. Count words
Answer: B. Work out something not directly stated

Q23. Skimming the first paragraph mainly tells you:

  1. A. All answers
  2. B. The topic and writer's direction
  3. C. The conclusion
  4. D. Nothing
Answer: B. The topic and writer's direction

Q24. In multiple choice, options that repeat passage words exactly are often:

  1. A. Always correct
  2. B. Traps — the correct answer is usually paraphrased
  3. C. Always wrong
  4. D. The longest
Answer: B. Traps — the correct answer is usually paraphrased

Q25. A reference word like 'this' or 'they' points to:

  1. A. The next paragraph
  2. B. Something mentioned just before
  3. C. The title
  4. D. A picture
Answer: B. Something mentioned just before
