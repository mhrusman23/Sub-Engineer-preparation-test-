Q1. Mendelow's matrix classifies stakeholders by power and:

  1. A. Interest
  2. B. Age
  3. C. Size of holding only
  4. D. Location
Answer: A. Interest

Q2. Corporate governance is the system by which companies are:

  1. A. Audited only
  2. B. Merged
  3. C. Directed and controlled
  4. D. Taxed
Answer: C. Directed and controlled

Q3. In Ansoff's matrix, selling existing products in existing markets is:

  1. A. Diversification
  2. B. Market development
  3. C. Market penetration
  4. D. Product development
Answer: C. Market penetration

Q4. Porter's generic strategies are cost leadership, differentiation and:

  1. A. Diversification
  2. B. Focus
  3. C. Integration
  4. D. Merger
Answer: B. Focus

Q5. The agency problem arises from a conflict between shareholders and:

  1. A. Suppliers
  2. B. Customers
  3. C. Regulators
  4. D. Managers
Answer: D. Managers

Q6. Which is one of the six capitals of integrated reporting?

  1. A. Legal capital
  2. B. Natural capital
  3. C. Tax capital
  4. D. Political capital
Answer: B. Natural capital

Q7. Porter's value chain divides activities into primary and:

  1. A. Support activities
  2. B. Optional activities
  3. C. External activities
  4. D. Financial activities
Answer: A. Support activities

Q8. Which is a fundamental principle of the IESBA/ACCA ethical code?

  1. A. Popularity
  2. B. Secrecy
  3. C. Profitability
  4. D. Objectivity
Answer: D. Objectivity

Q9. Core competences are resources and skills that give:

  1. A. Immediate profit
  2. B. Tax relief
  3. C. Lower wages
  4. D. Competitive advantage
Answer: D. Competitive advantage

Q10. The balanced scorecard was developed by:

  1. A. Porter
  2. B. Kaplan and Norton
  3. C. Mintzberg
  4. D. Ansoff
Answer: B. Kaplan and Norton

Q11. In strategic business analysis, which tool is best suited for evaluating the macro-environmental influences on an industry?

  1. A. Porter's Five Forces
  2. B. PESTEL analysis
  3. C. BCG matrix
  4. D. Value chain analysis
Answer: B. PESTEL analysis

Q12. In strategic management, which model analyzes an industry's competitive forces including threat of new entrants and bargaining power?

  1. A. BCG Matrix
  2. B. Ansoff Matrix
  3. C. Porter's Five Forces
  4. D. SWOT Analysis
Answer: C. Porter's Five Forces

Q13. Which analytical tool is specifically designed to evaluate the competitive forces within an industry?

  1. A. PESTEL analysis
  2. B. BCG matrix
  3. C. Porter’s Five Forces
  4. D. SWOT analysis
Answer: C. Porter’s Five Forces

Q14. Which of the following is NOT a component of the ACCA Strategic Business Leader (SBL) exam’s professional skills assessment?

  1. A. Ethics and professional values
  2. B. Stakeholder communication
  3. C. Technical accounting standards
  4. D. Business analysis and decision making
Answer: C. Technical accounting standards

Q15. In the context of strategic planning, the term ‘value chain’ refers to:

  1. A. The sequence of activities that create customer value
  2. B. A financial metric for measuring profitability
  3. C. The hierarchy of corporate governance bodies
  4. D. A set of marketing channels used by the firm
Answer: A. The sequence of activities that create customer value

Q16. During a reputational crisis, the most effective initial response is to:

  1. A. Ignore media coverage until the situation resolves
  2. B. Issue a formal apology and outline corrective actions
  3. C. Increase marketing spend to distract stakeholders
  4. D. Immediately replace the CEO
Answer: B. Issue a formal apology and outline corrective actions

Q17. Which of the following best describes the primary purpose of a corporate governance framework in a listed company?

  1. A. To ensure compliance with tax regulations
  2. B. To provide a structure for board decision‑making and accountability
  3. C. To increase short‑term shareholder profits
  4. D. To limit the role of external auditors
Answer: B. To provide a structure for board decision‑making and accountability

Q18. In a strategic risk assessment, which factor is most likely to be classified as a ‘macro‑environmental’ risk?

  1. A. A new product launch
  2. B. A change in consumer preferences
  3. C. A shift in government regulation
  4. D. A competitor’s pricing strategy
Answer: C. A shift in government regulation
