Power Systems MCQs (Electrical Engineering)

Free MCQs with answers for Power Systems. 16 questions in 3 chapter tests are in the app; all are shown here.

Q1. In a three-phase balanced system, the phase difference between any two phases is:

  1. A. 90 degrees
  2. B. 120 degrees
  3. C. 180 degrees
  4. D. 360 degrees
Answer: B. 120 degrees

Q2. The standard frequency of the power supply in Pakistan is:

  1. A. 25 Hz
  2. B. 100 Hz
  3. C. 50 Hz
  4. D. 60 Hz
Answer: C. 50 Hz

Q3. High voltage is used for long-distance transmission to reduce:

  1. A. Power losses
  2. B. Line length
  3. C. Insulation
  4. D. Frequency
Answer: A. Power losses

Q4. A circuit breaker is used to:

  1. A. Store energy
  2. B. Convert AC to DC
  3. C. Increase voltage
  4. D. Interrupt fault current
Answer: D. Interrupt fault current

Q5. The function of a fuse is to:

  1. A. Increase current
  2. B. Measure current
  3. C. Protect against overcurrent
  4. D. Raise voltage
Answer: C. Protect against overcurrent

Q6. Which of these is a renewable source of electricity?

  1. A. Diesel
  2. B. Coal
  3. C. Solar
  4. D. Natural gas
Answer: C. Solar

Q7. Power factor is the cosine of the angle between voltage and:

  1. A. Resistance
  2. B. Frequency
  3. C. Power
  4. D. Current
Answer: D. Current

Q8. A busbar is a:

  1. A. Conductor that collects and distributes power
  2. B. Relay
  3. C. Type of transformer
  4. D. Measuring device
Answer: A. Conductor that collects and distributes power

Q9. Insulators on transmission lines are used to:

  1. A. Store charge
  2. B. Isolate conductors from towers
  3. C. Increase voltage
  4. D. Carry current
Answer: B. Isolate conductors from towers

Q10. The three-phase system has phases separated by:

  1. A. 60 degrees
  2. B. 180 degrees
  3. C. 90 degrees
  4. D. 120 degrees
Answer: D. 120 degrees

Q11. A relay in power systems is used to:

  1. A. Detect faults and trip breakers
  2. B. Generate power
  3. C. Store energy
  4. D. Step up voltage
Answer: A. Detect faults and trip breakers

Q12. What is the main advantage of using a capacitor bank in a high‑voltage transmission line?

  1. A. Reduces line resistance
  2. B. Improves power factor
  3. C. Increases fault current
  4. D. Lowers system frequency
Answer: B. Improves power factor

Q13. What is the primary purpose of a voltage regulator in a transmission system?

  1. A. To increase line current
  2. B. To maintain constant voltage levels
  3. C. To detect short circuits
  4. D. To reduce transmission frequency
Answer: B. To maintain constant voltage levels

Q14. Which of the following is a common characteristic of a 33 kV distribution line in Pakistan?

  1. A. Used mainly for industrial plants
  2. B. Operates at 50 Hz
  3. C. Has a single-phase configuration
  4. D. Requires no insulation
Answer: B. Operates at 50 Hz

Q15. Which type of fault causes a sudden short between two phases and the ground?

  1. A. Three‑phase fault
  2. B. Phase‑to‑phase fault
  3. C. Phase‑to‑ground fault
  4. D. Open circuit fault
Answer: C. Phase‑to‑ground fault

Q16. In a power system, the bus that has its voltage magnitude fixed but its angle free is known as:

  1. A. Slack bus
  2. B. PV bus
  3. C. PQ bus
  4. D. Load bus
Answer: A. Slack bus
