Q1. In a three-phase balanced system, the phase difference between any two phases is:

  1. A. 90 degrees
  2. B. 120 degrees
  3. C. 180 degrees
  4. D. 360 degrees
Answer: B. 120 degrees

Q2. The standard frequency of the power supply in Pakistan is:

  1. A. 25 Hz
  2. B. 100 Hz
  3. C. 50 Hz
  4. D. 60 Hz
Answer: C. 50 Hz

Q3. High voltage is used for long-distance transmission to reduce:

  1. A. Power losses
  2. B. Line length
  3. C. Insulation
  4. D. Frequency
Answer: A. Power losses

Q4. A circuit breaker is used to:

  1. A. Store energy
  2. B. Convert AC to DC
  3. C. Increase voltage
  4. D. Interrupt fault current
Answer: D. Interrupt fault current

Q5. The function of a fuse is to:

  1. A. Increase current
  2. B. Measure current
  3. C. Protect against overcurrent
  4. D. Raise voltage
Answer: C. Protect against overcurrent

Q6. Which of these is a renewable source of electricity?

  1. A. Diesel
  2. B. Coal
  3. C. Solar
  4. D. Natural gas
Answer: C. Solar

Q7. Power factor is the cosine of the angle between voltage and:

  1. A. Resistance
  2. B. Frequency
  3. C. Power
  4. D. Current
Answer: D. Current

Q8. A busbar is a:

  1. A. Conductor that collects and distributes power
  2. B. Relay
  3. C. Type of transformer
  4. D. Measuring device
Answer: A. Conductor that collects and distributes power

Q9. Insulators on transmission lines are used to:

  1. A. Store charge
  2. B. Isolate conductors from towers
  3. C. Increase voltage
  4. D. Carry current
Answer: B. Isolate conductors from towers

Q10. The three-phase system has phases separated by:

  1. A. 60 degrees
  2. B. 180 degrees
  3. C. 90 degrees
  4. D. 120 degrees
Answer: D. 120 degrees

Q11. A relay in power systems is used to:

  1. A. Detect faults and trip breakers
  2. B. Generate power
  3. C. Store energy
  4. D. Step up voltage
Answer: A. Detect faults and trip breakers

Q12. What is the main advantage of using a capacitor bank in a high‑voltage transmission line?

  1. A. Reduces line resistance
  2. B. Improves power factor
  3. C. Increases fault current
  4. D. Lowers system frequency
Answer: B. Improves power factor

Q13. What is the primary purpose of a voltage regulator in a transmission system?

  1. A. To increase line current
  2. B. To maintain constant voltage levels
  3. C. To detect short circuits
  4. D. To reduce transmission frequency
Answer: B. To maintain constant voltage levels

Q14. Which of the following is a common characteristic of a 33 kV distribution line in Pakistan?

  1. A. Used mainly for industrial plants
  2. B. Operates at 50 Hz
  3. C. Has a single-phase configuration
  4. D. Requires no insulation
Answer: B. Operates at 50 Hz

Q15. Which type of fault causes a sudden short between two phases and the ground?

  1. A. Three‑phase fault
  2. B. Phase‑to‑phase fault
  3. C. Phase‑to‑ground fault
  4. D. Open circuit fault
Answer: C. Phase‑to‑ground fault

Q16. In a power system, the bus that has its voltage magnitude fixed but its angle free is known as:

  1. A. Slack bus
  2. B. PV bus
  3. C. PQ bus
  4. D. Load bus
Answer: A. Slack bus

Q17. Sag in overhead transmission lines is provided to ensure that:

  1. A. Conductors do not exceed their safe mechanical stress limit
  2. B. Line resistance is kept at a minimum
  3. C. Inductive reactance is completely eliminated
  4. D. Corona discharge is maximized
Answer: A. Conductors do not exceed their safe mechanical stress limit

Q18. Corona loss in overhead transmission lines is significantly reduced by:

  1. A. Using bundled conductors
  2. B. Increasing the operating frequency
  3. C. Decreasing the spacing between conductors
  4. D. Using solid conductors instead of stranded
Answer: A. Using bundled conductors

Q19. Skin effect in transmission lines causes the current to flow:

  1. A. Uniformly throughout the cross-section
  2. B. Mostly near the center of the conductor
  3. C. Only in the ground wire
  4. D. Mostly near the outer surface of the conductor
Answer: D. Mostly near the outer surface of the conductor

Q20. In a radial distribution system, the main disadvantage is:

  1. A. High installation cost
  2. B. Complex network analysis
  3. C. Poor reliability as a fault cuts off supply to downstream consumers
  4. D. High voltage drop at the sending end
Answer: C. Poor reliability as a fault cuts off supply to downstream consumers

Q21. Per unit (pu) impedance of a transformer is based on its own rating, but when referred to a new base, it varies inversely as the:

  1. A. Square of the voltage base
  2. B. Square of the current base
  3. C. Square of the MVA base
  4. D. Linear value of frequency
Answer: A. Square of the voltage base

Q22. The primary purpose of installing a surge arrester in a substation is to protect equipment against:

  1. A. Overload currents
  2. B. Transient overvoltages due to lightning or switching
  3. C. Low frequency oscillations
  4. D. Harmonic distortion
Answer: B. Transient overvoltages due to lightning or switching

Q23. What is the primary function of a lightning arrestor in an electrical substation?

  1. A. To step up the system voltage
  2. B. To divert high voltage surges safely to the ground
  3. C. To measure high currents accurately
  4. D. To disconnect the faulty circuit automatically
Answer: B. To divert high voltage surges safely to the ground

Q24. Which type of power plant has the highest running or operating cost?

  1. A. Hydroelectric power plant
  2. B. Nuclear power plant
  3. C. Diesel power plant
  4. D. Run-of-river hydro plant
Answer: C. Diesel power plant

Q25. Which of the following faults is classified as a symmetrical fault in a three-phase power system?

  1. A. Single line to ground fault
  2. B. Line to line fault
  3. C. Three-phase short circuit fault
  4. D. Double line to ground fault
Answer: C. Three-phase short circuit fault
