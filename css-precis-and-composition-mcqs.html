Q1. A precis is:

  1. A. A long summary
  2. B. A shortened version of a passage keeping its main points, about one-third in length
  3. C. A translation
  4. D. A paraphrase of one line
Answer: B. A shortened version of a passage keeping its main points, about one-third in length

Q2. While making a precis, you should:

  1. A. Add your own opinions
  2. B. Keep only the essential points in your own words
  3. C. Copy sentences directly
  4. D. Change the meaning
Answer: B. Keep only the essential points in your own words

Q3. Choose the correct sentence:

  1. A. He is senior than me.
  2. B. He is senior to me.
  3. C. He is more senior than me is.
  4. D. He senior to me.
Answer: B. He is senior to me.

Q4. The precis must have a suitable:

  1. A. Poem
  2. B. Title
  3. C. Diagram
  4. D. Reference list
Answer: B. Title

Q5. Choose the correct pair: 'Affect' and 'Effect' —

  1. A. Affect is a noun
  2. B. Affect is usually a verb; effect is usually a noun
  3. C. Both are verbs
  4. D. Both are adjectives
Answer: B. Affect is usually a verb; effect is usually a noun

Q6. Narration (direct/indirect speech) changes require changes in:

  1. A. Only spelling
  2. B. Pronouns, tense and time expressions
  3. C. Only punctuation
  4. D. Word order only
Answer: B. Pronouns, tense and time expressions

Q7. Choose the correct sentence:

  1. A. One of the boys have won.
  2. B. One of the boys has won.
  3. C. One of the boys have win.
  4. D. One of the boys are won.
Answer: B. One of the boys has won.

Q8. A precis should be written in:

  1. A. First person always
  2. B. Third person and indirect form
  3. C. Questions
  4. D. Dialogue
Answer: B. Third person and indirect form

Q9. Pair of words: 'Stationary' means:

  1. A. Office papers
  2. B. Not moving
  3. C. A railway shop
  4. D. Writing material
Answer: B. Not moving

Q10. 'Stationery' refers to:

  1. A. Standing still
  2. B. Writing materials such as paper and pens
  3. C. A station building
  4. D. A statue
Answer: B. Writing materials such as paper and pens
