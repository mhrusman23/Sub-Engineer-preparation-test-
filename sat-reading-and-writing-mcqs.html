Q1. Which choice uses correct punctuation?

  1. A. The meeting, which was long ended at noon.
  2. B. The meeting which was long, ended at noon.
  3. C. The meeting, which was long, ended at noon.
  4. D. The meeting which, was long ended at noon.
Answer: C. The meeting, which was long, ended at noon.

Q2. 'Their' is used to show:

  1. A. A place
  2. B. Possession (belonging to them)
  3. C. A contraction of they are
  4. D. Time
Answer: B. Possession (belonging to them)

Q3. Choose the sentence with correct subject-verb agreement:

  1. A. The list of items are on the desk.
  2. B. The list of items is on the desk.
  3. C. The list of items were on the desk.
  4. D. The list of items be on the desk.
Answer: B. The list of items is on the desk.

Q4. Which word best completes: 'The results were ___; every test gave the same answer.'

  1. A. varied
  2. B. consistent
  3. C. unclear
  4. D. random
Answer: B. consistent

Q5. A semicolon is used to:

  1. A. End every sentence
  2. B. Join two closely related independent clauses
  3. C. Start a list only
  4. D. Show possession
Answer: B. Join two closely related independent clauses

Q6. In a passage, the main idea is:

  1. A. A small detail
  2. B. The central point the author makes
  3. C. The first word
  4. D. The title only
Answer: B. The central point the author makes

Q7. Which revision is most concise?

  1. A. Due to the fact that it rained, the match was cancelled.
  2. B. Because it rained, the match was cancelled.
  3. C. The match was cancelled due to the fact that rain fell.
  4. D. In light of the fact that it rained, the match was cancelled.
Answer: B. Because it rained, the match was cancelled.

Q8. 'However' at the start of a sentence should be followed by:

  1. A. A semicolon
  2. B. A comma
  3. C. A colon
  4. D. Nothing
Answer: B. A comma

Q9. Which word best completes the sentence: 'Despite the harsh criticism, the artist remained ___ in her dedication to her unique style.'

  1. A. vacillating
  2. B. steadfast
  3. C. indifferent
  4. D. yielding
Answer: B. steadfast

Q10. Identify the option that corrects the comma splice: 'The experiment failed, we had to start over.'

  1. A. The experiment failed; we had to start over.
  2. B. The experiment failed we, had to start over.
  3. C. The experiment failed and, we had to start over.
  4. D. The experiment, failed we had to start over.
Answer: A. The experiment failed; we had to start over.

Q11. Which choice provides the most effective combination of sentences? 'The museum showcased ancient artifacts. These artifacts had been recently discovered in Egypt.'

  1. A. The museum showcased ancient artifacts, and they had been recently discovered in Egypt.
  2. B. The museum showcased ancient artifacts recently discovered in Egypt.
  3. C. Showcasing ancient artifacts, the museum displayed items discovered recently in Egypt.
  4. D. The museum showcased ancient artifacts, which these were recently discovered in Egypt.
Answer: B. The museum showcased ancient artifacts recently discovered in Egypt.

Q12. What is the primary function of the word 'specifically' in a paragraph?

  1. A. To present a broad counterargument
  2. B. To provide a detailed example or clarification
  3. C. To conclude the main discussion
  4. D. To indicate chronological order
Answer: B. To provide a detailed example or clarification

Q13. Which transition word best indicates a contrast between two ideas?

  1. A. Furthermore
  2. B. Consequently
  3. C. Nevertheless
  4. D. Similarly
Answer: C. Nevertheless

Q14. Choose the sentence that correctly uses a colon to introduce a list.

  1. A. She needs to buy: apples, bananas, and oranges.
  2. B. She needs: apples, bananas, and oranges.
  3. C. For her recipe she needs the following: apples, bananas, and oranges.
  4. D. She needs to buy apples, bananas: and oranges.
Answer: C. For her recipe she needs the following: apples, bananas, and oranges.

Q15. Which vocabulary choice best completes the sentence: 'The sudden shift in policy was ___ to the staff, leaving everyone confused about their roles.'

  1. A. transparent
  2. B. perplexing
  3. C. beneficial
  4. D. predictable
Answer: B. perplexing

Q16. Identify the option that corrects a run-on sentence by using a semicolon properly:

  1. A. The experiment failed, we tried again.
  2. B. The experiment failed; however, we tried again.
  3. C. The experiment failed however we tried again.
  4. D. The experiment failed,; we tried again.
Answer: B. The experiment failed; however, we tried again.

Q17. Which transition word best indicates a cause-and-effect relationship between two independent clauses?

  1. A. Therefore
  2. B. Meanwhile
  3. C. Furthermore
  4. D. Conversely
Answer: A. Therefore

Q18. In analyzing the overall structure of a text, what does a shift from a broad historical overview to a specific case study typically indicate?

  1. A. A contradiction of the initial premise
  2. B. An abandonment of the main topic
  3. C. A narrowing of focus to provide concrete evidence
  4. D. A concluding summary of the argument
Answer: C. A narrowing of focus to provide concrete evidence

Q19. What is the primary function of words like 'similarly' or 'likewise' in a paragraph?

  1. A. To show contrast
  2. B. To indicate sequence
  3. C. To demonstrate comparison or similarity
  4. D. To provide an exception
Answer: C. To demonstrate comparison or similarity

Q20. Choose the option with the correct pronoun-antecedent agreement:

  1. A. Each of the students submitted their report.
  2. B. Each of the students submitted his or her report.
  3. C. Each of the students submitted our report.
  4. D. Each of the students submitted these report.
Answer: B. Each of the students submitted his or her report.

Q21. Which word best completes the sentence: "The data were ___; all experiments yielded the same result."

  1. A. inconsistent
  2. B. consistent
  3. C. variable
  4. D. random
Answer: B. consistent

Q22. Which sentence contains the correct use of a semicolon?

  1. A. I wanted to go to the market; however, it was closed.
  2. B. I wanted to go to the market; and it was closed.
  3. C. I wanted to go to the market; but it was closed.
  4. D. I wanted to go to the market; because it was closed.
Answer: A. I wanted to go to the market; however, it was closed.

Q23. Choose the sentence with proper subject‑verb agreement.

  1. A. The group of students were excited about the trip.
  2. B. The group of students was excited about the trip.
  3. C. The group of students are excited about the trip.
  4. D. The group of students be excited about the trip.
Answer: B. The group of students was excited about the trip.

Q24. In the passage, the main idea is best described as:

  1. A. A minor detail mentioned in the first paragraph.
  2. B. The central point the author makes.
  3. C. The author's personal opinion.
  4. D. The title of the article.
Answer: B. The central point the author makes.

Q25. Which revision makes the sentence most concise?

  1. A. The committee, after a lengthy discussion, decided to postpone the meeting until next week.
  2. B. The committee decided to postpone the meeting until next week after a lengthy discussion.
  3. C. The committee decided to postpone the meeting until next week.
  4. D. The committee, after a discussion, decided to postpone the meeting until next week.
Answer: C. The committee decided to postpone the meeting until next week.
