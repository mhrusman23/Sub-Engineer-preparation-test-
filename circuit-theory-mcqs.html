Q1. In a three-phase balanced system, the phase difference between voltages is:

  1. A. 90 degrees
  2. B. 120 degrees
  3. C. 180 degrees
  4. D. 360 degrees
Answer: B. 120 degrees

Q2. In a three-phase system, the standard phase angle difference is:

  1. A. 90 degrees
  2. B. 180 degrees
  3. C. 120 degrees
  4. D. 360 degrees
Answer: C. 120 degrees

Q3. In a three-phase balanced system, the sum of all three phase voltages at any instant is:

  1. A. Zero
  2. B. Maximum
  3. C. RMS value
  4. D. Peak value
Answer: A. Zero

Q4. Power factor in a purely resistive AC circuit is always:

  1. A. Zero
  2. B. Unity
  3. C. Lagging
  4. D. Leading
Answer: B. Unity

Q5. The dielectric material used in a capacitor primarily serves to:

  1. A. Conduct current
  2. B. Increase capacitance and prevent short circuits
  3. C. Reduce voltage drop
  4. D. Store magnetic energy
Answer: B. Increase capacitance and prevent short circuits

Q6. What is the SI unit of electric potential?

  1. A. Volt
  2. B. Ampere
  3. C. Ohm
  4. D. Watt
Answer: A. Volt

Q7. What is the unit of electrical power?

  1. A. Joule
  2. B. Watt
  3. C. Coulomb
  4. D. Farad
Answer: B. Watt

Q8. What is the SI unit of electric potential difference?

  1. A. Ampere
  2. B. Volt
  3. C. Ohm
  4. D. Watt
Answer: B. Volt

Q9. Which of the following is an active element in an electrical circuit?

  1. A. Resistor
  2. B. Capacitor
  3. C. Inductor
  4. D. Voltage source
Answer: D. Voltage source

Q10. Which of the following is an active element in an electric circuit?

  1. A. Resistor
  2. B. Inductor
  3. C. Capacitor
  4. D. Voltage Source
Answer: D. Voltage Source

Q11. The power factor of an alternating current circuit is equal to:

  1. A. Cosine of the phase angle
  2. B. Sine of the phase angle
  3. C. Tangent of the phase angle
  4. D. Ratio of resistance to impedance
Answer: A. Cosine of the phase angle

Q12. The power factor of a purely inductive AC circuit is always:

  1. A. Unity
  2. B. Zero lagging
  3. C. Zero leading
  4. D. 0.5 lagging
Answer: B. Zero lagging

Q13. The specific resistance of a conductor depends upon its:

  1. A. Length
  2. B. Cross-sectional area
  3. C. Material
  4. D. Shape
Answer: C. Material

Q14. The reciprocal of resistance is called:

  1. A. Reactance
  2. B. Impedance
  3. C. Conductance
  4. D. Susceptance
Answer: C. Conductance

Q15. The reciprocal of resistance is known as:

  1. A. Reactance
  2. B. Impedance
  3. C. Conductance
  4. D. Susceptance
Answer: C. Conductance

Q16. According to Ohm's law, the current flowing through a conductor is proportional to its:

  1. A. Resistance
  2. B. Voltage
  3. C. Power
  4. D. Capacitance
Answer: B. Voltage

Q17. Which device is used to step up or step down alternating voltage?

  1. A. Generator
  2. B. Motor
  3. C. Transformer
  4. D. Rectifier
Answer: C. Transformer

Q18. The power factor of an AC circuit is defined as the cosine of the phase angle between voltage and:

  1. A. Power
  2. B. Resistance
  3. C. Current
  4. D. Frequency
Answer: C. Current

Q19. Kirchhoff's Current Law (KCL) is based on the conservation of:

  1. A. Energy
  2. B. Charge
  3. C. Power

Answer: B. Charge

Q22. Ohm's law relates current, voltage, and which other quantity?

Q23. According to Ohm's law, the current flowing through a conductor is directly proportional to:

  1. A. Resistance
  2. B. Voltage

Q24. What is the unit of electrical resistance?

  1. A. Henry
