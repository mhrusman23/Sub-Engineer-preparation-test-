Q1. Centre of plain geometrical figure is called

  1. A. Centre
  2. B. Centroid
  3. C. Centre of gravity
  4. D. Central axis
Answer: B. Centroid

Q2. Centre of a solid geometrical object is called

  1. A. Central axis
  2. B. Centroid
  3. C. Centre of gravity
  4. D. Centre
Answer: C. Centre of gravity

Q3. Centroid of a symmetrical figure lies at

  1. A. Outside
  2. B. Centre
  3. C. Right
  4. D. Left
Answer: B. Centre

Q4. I-section has ______ symmetrical axes

  1. A. Two
  2. B. One
  3. C. Three
  4. D. Four
Answer: A. Two

Q5. Measuring unit of mass in the British system is

  1. A. Kilogram
  2. B. Pound
  3. C. Newton
  4. D. Slug
Answer: D. Slug

Q6. Density of water in M.K.S system

  3. C. 1000 kg/m³
  4. D. 9.81 kN/m³
