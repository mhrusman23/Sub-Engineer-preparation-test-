Q1. Centre of plain geometrical figure is called

  1. A. Centre of gravity
  2. B. Centroid
  3. C. Centre
  4. D. Central axis
Answer: B. Centroid

Q2. Measuring unit of moment of inertia in M.K.S system

  1. A. cm³
  2. B. cm⁴
  3. C. m⁴
  4. D. in⁴
Answer: C. m⁴

Q3. Resistance of a material against deformation

  1. A. Toughness
  2. B. Resilience
  3. C. Modulus
  4. D. Hardness
Answer: D. Hardness

Q4. Positive bending moment shows

  1. A. Hogging
  2. B. Jogging
  3. C. Tearing
  4. D. Sagging
Answer: D. Sagging

Q5. The property of a material by virtue of which it disappears the deformation after removal of external load

  1. A. Toughness
  2. B. Rigidity
  3. C. Elasticity
  4. D. Plasticity
Answer: C. Elasticity

Q6. Bending stress at the neutral axis of a beam

  1. A. Maximum tensile
  2. B. Negative
  3. C. Zero
  4. D. Maximum compressive
Answer: C. Zero

Q7. Force applied per unit area is called

  1. A. Unit force
  2. B. Stress
  3. C. Strain
  4. D. None of these
Answer: B. Stress

Q8. Moment area method is used to determine

  1. A. Compressive stress
  2. B. S.F and B.M
  3. C. Slope and deflection
  4. D. Tensile stress
Answer: C. Slope and deflection

Q9. A column having slenderness ratio less than 80 is treated as

  1. A. Short column
  2. B. Weak column
  3. C. Medium column
  4. D. Long column
Answer: A. Short column

Q10. Maximum value of pitch in a riveted joint

  1. A. 3 d
  2. B. 2.5 d
  3. C. 2 d
  4. D. 1.5 d
Answer: A. 3 d

Q11. The weakest section in a fillet weld is

  1. A. Throat
  2. B. Small leg
  3. C. Side normal to force
  4. D. Side parallel to force
Answer: A. Throat

Q12. Shape of a structural steel section

  1. A. L-Section
  2. B. T-Section
  3. C. Z-Section
  4. D. All of these
Answer: D. All of these

Q13. A triangular shaped structure used in replacement of a beam for long spans is called

  1. A. T-Beam
  2. B. Truss
  3. C. Pre-stressed beam
  4. D. Triangular beam
Answer: B. Truss

Q14. A wall which retains material by its own weight is called

  1. A. Cantilever wall
  2. B. Gravity wall
  3. C. Buttressed wall
  4. D. Counterfort wall
Answer: B. Gravity wall

Q15. Twisting moment is also called

  1. A. Hardness
  2. B. Torque
  3. C. Momentum
  4. D. Flexural
Answer: B. Torque

Q16. Centre of a solid geometrical object is called

  1. A. Centre of gravity
  2. B. Centre
  3. C. Centroid
  4. D. Central axis
Answer: A. Centre of gravity

Q17. Moment of inertia of a hollow circle with external diameter 'D' and internal diameter 'd'

  1. A. π(D⁴ − d⁴)/64
  2. B. π(D⁴ − d⁴)/32
  3. C. π(D⁴ + d⁴)/64
  4. D. π(d⁴ − D⁴)/64
Answer: A. π(D⁴ − d⁴)/64

Q18. Resistance to plastic indentation is

  1. A. Stiffness
  2. B. Elasticity
  3. C. Toughness
  4. D. Hardness
Answer: D. Hardness

Q19. Shape of shear force diagram in case of a cantilever having point load at its free end

  1. A. Triangle
  2. B. Circular
  3. C. Rectangle
  4. D. Parabolic
Answer: C. Rectangle

Q20. The capacity to resist plastic deformation of a material

  1. A. Ductility
  2. B. Elasticity
  3. C. Plasticity
  4. D. Brittleness
Answer: D. Brittleness

Q21. Strength of a beam is directly proportional to

  1. A. Bulk modulus
  2. B. Shear modulus
  3. C. Section modulus
  4. D. All of these
Answer: D. All of these

Q22. Stress measured at a plane perpendicular to the direction of force

  1. A. Normal stress
  2. B. Principal stress
  3. C. Direct stress
  4. D. All of these
Answer: C. Direct stress

Q23. Deflection at the free end of a cantilever beam having a point load 'W' at free end

  1. A. Wl³/3EI
  2. B. Wl³/2EI
  3. C. Wl⁴/3EI
  4. D. Wl²/2EI
Answer: A. Wl³/3EI

Q24. A short column fails by this way

  1. A. Buckling
  2. B. Bending
  3. C. Slipping
  4. D. Crushing
Answer: D. Crushing

Q25. Minimum number of rivets required to connect gusset plates

  1. A. 2
  2. B. 1
  3. C. 3
  4. D. 4
Answer: D. 4
