Q1. IFRS S1 sets general requirements for disclosing:

  1. A. Revenue recognition
  2. B. Lease accounting
  3. C. Sustainability-related financial information
  4. D. Tax planning
Answer: C. Sustainability-related financial information

Q2. IFRS S2 deals with:

  1. A. Inventories
  2. B. Climate-related disclosures
  3. C. Employee benefits
  4. D. Insurance
Answer: B. Climate-related disclosures

Q3. The ISSB stands for the International Sustainability Standards:

  1. A. Board
  2. B. Bank
  3. C. Bond
  4. D. Bureau
Answer: A. Board

Q4. ESG stands for environmental, social and:

  1. A. Governance
  2. B. Gain
  3. C. Growth
  4. D. Government
Answer: A. Governance

Q5. Scope 1 emissions are:

  1. A. Value chain emissions
  2. B. Emissions from purchased electricity
  3. C. Emissions of customers
  4. D. Direct emissions from owned sources
Answer: D. Direct emissions from owned sources

Q6. Scope 2 emissions arise from:

  1. A. Customer travel
  2. B. Waste of suppliers
  3. C. Company vehicles owned
  4. D. Purchased electricity, steam, heating or cooling
Answer: D. Purchased electricity, steam, heating or cooling

Q7. Greenwashing means:

  1. A. Misleading claims about environmental performance
  2. B. Recycling paper
  3. C. Reducing energy use
  4. D. Planting trees
Answer: A. Misleading claims about environmental performance

Q8. Assurance on sustainability information increases:

  1. A. Users' confidence in the information
  2. B. Tax relief
  3. C. Company profit directly
  4. D. Share count
Answer: A. Users' confidence in the information

Q9. Limited assurance generally provides:

  1. A. A higher level than reasonable
  2. B. Absolute certainty
  3. C. No assurance
  4. D. A lower level of assurance than reasonable assurance
Answer: D. A lower level of assurance than reasonable assurance

Q10. The TCFD framework is concerned with:

  1. A. Climate-related financial disclosures
  2. B. Audit independence
  3. C. Inventory valuation
  4. D. Tax planning
Answer: A. Climate-related financial disclosures

Q11. Which body officially absorbed the Climate Disclosure Standards Board (CDSB) and the Value Reporting Foundation (VRF)?

  1. A. International Accounting Standards Board
  2. B. International Sustainability Standards Board
  3. C. Global Reporting Initiative
  4. D. Task Force on Climate-related Financial Disclosures
Answer: B. International Sustainability Standards Board

Q12. Under sustainability assurance engagements, what is the primary distinction regarding evidence gathering between limited and reasonable assurance?

  1. A. Reasonable assurance requires more extensive procedures and evidence collection
  2. B. Limited assurance requires physical inspection of all production facilities
  3. C. Reasonable assurance focuses exclusively on financial statement line items
  4. D. Limited assurance relies entirely on internal audit reports without testing
Answer: A. Reasonable assurance requires more extensive procedures and evidence collection

Q13. In the context of greenhouse gas inventories, what category do indirect emissions from the generation of purchased electricity consumed by a reporting entity belong to?

  1. A. Scope 1
  2. B. Scope 2
  3. C. Scope 3
  4. D. Scope 4
Answer: B. Scope 2

Q14. What is the primary objective of applying materiality assessments in sustainability reporting under current frameworks?

  1. A. To maximize the total volume of published non-financial data
  2. B. To identify sustainability risks and opportunities that could affect enterprise value
  3. C. To comply exclusively with local tax authority guidelines
  4. D. To eliminate the need for third-party independent assurance
Answer: B. To identify sustainability risks and opportunities that could affect enterprise value

Q15. Which term describes the practice of misleading consumers or investors regarding the environmental benefits of a company's products or practices?

  1. A. Greenhushing
  2. B. Greenwashing
  3. C. Carbon offsetting
  4. D. Integrated laundering
Answer: B. Greenwashing

Q16. What core element of the TCFD framework evaluates how resilient an organization's strategy is under different climate-related scenarios?

  1. A. Risk Management
  2. B. Metrics and Targets
  3. C. Scenario Analysis
  4. D. Governance Oversight
Answer: C. Scenario Analysis

Q17. What type of direct emissions are categorized strictly under Scope 1 greenhouse gas inventories?

  1. A. Emissions from purchased electricity used in administrative offices
  2. B. Emissions originating from owned or controlled sources within operations
  3. C. Emissions produced by third-party logistics and freight providers
  4. D. Emissions resulting from employee commuting via public transit
Answer: B. Emissions originating from owned or controlled sources within operations

Q18. Which standard released by the IAASB serves as the overarching framework for performing sustainability assurance engagements?

  1. A. ISA 200
  2. B. ISSA 5000
  3. C. ISRE 2400
  4. D. ISAE 3402
Answer: B. ISSA 5000

Q19. When designing internal control systems for non-financial metrics, what primary responsibility does the assurance team evaluate regarding data pipelines?

  1. A. Setting executive remuneration packages based on ESG targets
  2. B. Tracking raw data from source generation through to final reporting consolidation
  3. C. Filing corporate tax returns with relevant government authorities
  4. D. Negotiating procurement contracts with raw material suppliers
Answer: B. Tracking raw data from source generation through to final reporting consolidation

Q20. In a double-materiality assessment, what two dimensions are evaluated simultaneously?

  1. A. Financial enterprise value and broader societal/environmental ecosystems
  2. B. Historical cost and current market replacement value
  3. C. Direct operational expenses and indirect capital expenditures
  4. D. Short-term liquidity and long-term solvency ratios
Answer: A. Financial enterprise value and broader societal/environmental ecosystems

Q21. Which category of greenhouse gas emissions specifically accounts for indirect emissions associated with the generation of purchased electricity, steam, heating, and cooling?

  1. A. Scope 1
  2. B. Scope 2
  3. C. Scope 3
  4. D. Scope 4
Answer: B. Scope 2

Q22. Which classification of greenhouse gas emissions covers all indirect emissions generated across an organization's upstream and downstream value chain?

  1. A. Scope 1 emissions
  2. B. Scope 2 emissions
  3. C. Scope 3 emissions
  4. D. Scope 4 emissions
Answer: C. Scope 3 emissions

Q23. Which of the following best describes the primary focus of the International Sustainability Standards Board (ISSB) when issuing new disclosure standards?

  1. A. Setting tax incentives for green projects
  2. B. Developing a global baseline for sustainability reporting
  3. C. Regulating carbon trading markets
  4. D. Creating industry‑specific ESG metrics
Answer: B. Developing a global baseline for sustainability reporting

Q24. In the context of double‑materiality, what does the 'financial materiality' dimension assess?

  1. A. Impact of sustainability issues on the company’s financial performance
  2. B. Impact of the company’s activities on the environment
  3. C. Stakeholder perceptions of ESG performance
  4. D. Regulatory compliance status
Answer: A. Impact of sustainability issues on the company’s financial performance

Q25. Which of the following is NOT a typical component of a sustainability assurance report?

  1. A. Scope of engagement
  2. B. Management’s discussion and analysis
  3. C. Assurance provider’s independence statement
  4. D. Detailed financial ratios
Answer: D. Detailed financial ratios
