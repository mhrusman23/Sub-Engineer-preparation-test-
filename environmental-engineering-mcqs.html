Q1. In a water supply scheme, the self-cleansing velocity of flow in sewers should generally be at least:

  1. A. 0.15 m/s
  2. B. 0.45 m/s
  3. C. 0.75 m/s
  4. D. 1.50 m/s
Answer: C. 0.75 m/s

Q2. Which component of sewage treatment involves the removal of suspended heavy inorganic particles by settling?

  1. A. Screening
  2. B. Grit chamber
  3. C. Trickling filter
  4. D. Sludge digestion
Answer: B. Grit chamber

Q3. In a water supply scheme, the maximum daily demand is generally taken as how many times the average daily demand?

  1. A. 1.0 time
  2. B. 1.5 times
  3. C. 1.8 to 2.0 times
  4. D. 2.7 to 3.0 times
Answer: C. 1.8 to 2.0 times

Q4. In a water supply scheme, what is the maximum permissible limit of total dissolved solids (TDS) in drinking water as per standard guidelines?

  1. A. 500 mg/L
  2. B. 1000 mg/L
  3. C. 1500 mg/L
  4. D. 2000 mg/L
Answer: B. 1000 mg/L

Q5. What is the maximum permissible limit of sulphates in portable water used for concrete mixing?

  1. A. 200 mg/l
  2. B. 400 mg/l
  3. C. 600 mg/l
  4. D. 1000 mg/l
Answer: B. 400 mg/l

Q6. The process of removing dissolved minerals from water to make it soft is known as:

  1. A. Sedimentation
  2. B. Filtration
  3. C. Demineralization
  4. D. Chlorination
Answer: C. Demineralization

Q7. The unit used to express water hardness is commonly:

  1. A. NTU
  2. B. mg/L as CaCO3
  3. C. pH
  4. D. dB
Answer: B. mg/L as CaCO3

Q8. BOD stands for biochemical oxygen:

  1. A. Distribution
  2. B. Demand
  3. C. Dissolution
  4. D. Deficiency
Answer: B. Demand

Q9. The pH of neutral water is:

  1. A. 10
  2. B. 7
  3. C. 14
  4. D. 0
Answer: B. 7

Q10. Chlorine is added to drinking water mainly for:

  1. A. Softening
  2. B. Colouring
  3. C. Sedimentation
  4. D. Disinfection
Answer: D. Disinfection

Q11. The process of removing suspended particles by gravity is:

  1. A. Chlorination
  2. B. Filtration
  3. C. Aeration
  4. D. Sedimentation
Answer: D. Sedimentation

Q12. Turbidity is commonly measured in:

  1. A. pH
  2. B. mg/L
  3. C. Pa
  4. D. NTU
Answer: D. NTU

Q13. The primary treatment of sewage mainly removes:

  1. A. Dissolved gases
  2. B. Settleable solids
  3. C. Salts
  4. D. Metals only
Answer: B. Settleable solids

Q14. The activated sludge process is a:

  1. A. Physical treatment only
  2. B. Biological treatment
  3. C. Disinfection method
  4. D. Chemical treatment only
Answer: B. Biological treatment

Q15. Noise is measured in:

  1. A. Newtons
  2. B. Pascals
  3. C. Decibels
  4. D. Hertz only
Answer: C. Decibels

Q16. The ozone layer protects the Earth from:

  1. A. Infrared radiation
  2. B. Sound waves
  3. C. Cosmic dust
  4. D. Ultraviolet radiation
Answer: D. Ultraviolet radiation
