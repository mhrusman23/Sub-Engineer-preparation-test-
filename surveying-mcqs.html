Q1. What is the angle of the apex of a standard metric chain tally used in surveying?

  1. A. Not applicable, tallies are metal plates of specific shapes
  2. B. 30 degrees
  3. C. 45 degrees
  4. D. 90 degrees
Answer: A. Not applicable, tallies are metal plates of specific shapes

Q2. Which line on a compass survey indicates the direction of magnetic north at any place?

  1. A. Meridian
  2. B. Base line
  3. C. Tie line
  4. D. Check line
Answer: A. Meridian

Q3. In surveying, offsets taken at an angle other than 90 degrees to the chain line are known as:

  1. A. Perpendicular offsets
  2. B. Oblique offsets
  3. C. Tie offsets
  4. D. Check offsets
Answer: B. Oblique offsets

Q4. In surveying, plumbing fork with a plumb bob is used with which instrument?

  1. A. Dumpy level
  2. B. Plane table
  3. C. Theodolite
  4. D. Prismatic compass
Answer: B. Plane table

Q5. The surveying method in which field observations and plotting are done simultaneously is called:

  1. A. Compass surveying
  2. B. Plane table surveying
  3. C. Theodolite surveying
  4. D. Chain surveying
Answer: B. Plane table surveying

Q6. The angle between the true meridian and magnetic meridian at a place is called:

  1. A. Azimuth
  2. B. Declination
  3. C. Bearing
  4. D. Zenith
Answer: B. Declination

Q7. In a traverse survey, the error due to local attraction is detected by observing:

  1. A. Included angles
  2. B. Forward and backward bearings
  3. C. Perpendicular offsets
  4. D. Magnetic declinations
Answer: B. Forward and backward bearings

Q8. In surveying, the angle between the true meridian and magnetic meridian is called:

  1. A. Azimuth
  2. B. Declination
  3. C. Bearing
  4. D. Deviation
Answer: B. Declination

Q9. Under IAS 37 Provisions, Contingent Liabilities and Contingent Assets, when is a provision recognized?

  1. A. When there is a possible obligation resulting from past events
  2. B. When a present obligation exists from a past event and a reliable estimate can be made
  3. C. When management decides to set aside funds for future repairs
  4. D. When the cash outflow is probable and exceeds 50% of total revenue
Answer: B. When a present obligation exists from a past event and a reliable estimate can be made

Q10. In corporate governance, what is the main role of the audit committee?

  1. A. Managing the daily sales operations of the business
  2. B. Designing new marketing campaigns for product launches
  3. C. Providing oversight of financial reporting and internal control systems
  4. D. Setting the individual salaries of all factory workers
Answer: C. Providing oversight of financial reporting and internal control systems

Q11. Which of the following is an example of a detective control in an automated accounting information system?

  1. A. Access control matrix
  2. B. Bank reconciliation review
  3. C. Input data validation mask
  4. D. Segregation of duties
Answer: B. Bank reconciliation review

Q12. What is the primary objective of transfer pricing within a divisionalized multinational organization?

  1. A. To maximize global sales revenue
  2. B. To encourage goal congruence and evaluate divisional performance fairly
  3. C. To completely eliminate corporate tax liabilities worldwide
  4. D. To ensure all divisions report identical profit margins
Answer: B. To encourage goal congruence and evaluate divisional performance fairly

Q13. What is the term used to describe the moral principles and values that govern an individual's or group's behavior in a business environment?

  1. A. Corporate governance
  2. B. Business ethics
  3. C. Social responsibility
  4. D. Internal audit
Answer: B. Business ethics

Q14. The instrument used to measure horizontal and vertical angles is the:

  1. A. Chain
  2. B. Theodolite
  3. C. Dumpy level
  4. D. Plane table
Answer: B. Theodolite

Q15. A dumpy level is mainly used to determine:

  1. A. Magnetic bearing only
  2. B. Horizontal angles only
  3. C. Differences in elevation
  4. D. Areas
Answer: C. Differences in elevation

Q16. The length of a standard Gunter's chain is:

  1. A. 33 feet
  2. B. 66 feet
  3. C. 100 feet
  4. D. 50 feet
Answer: B. 66 feet

Q17. A bench mark is a point of:

  1. A. Known angle
  2. B. Known elevation
  3. C. Known area
  4. D. Known length
Answer: B. Known elevation

Q18. In levelling, a backsight is taken on a point of:

  1. A. Known reduced level
  2. B. Changed point
  3. C. Turning point only
  4. D. Unknown level
Answer: A. Known reduced level

Q19. A contour line connects points of equal:

  1. A. Bearing
  2. B. Slope
  3. C. Distance
  4. D. Elevation
Answer: D. Elevation

Q20. The whole circle bearing of a line ranges from:

  1. A. 0 to 90 degrees
  2. B. 0 to 360 degrees
  3. C. 0 to 270 degrees
  4. D. 0 to 180 degrees
Answer: B. 0 to 360 degrees

Q21. Closed traverse error is known as the:

  1. A. Closing error
  2. B. Systematic error
  3. C. Random error
  4. D. Index error
Answer: A. Closing error

Q22. The least count of a vernier is the:

  1. A. Zero error
  2. B. Largest reading
  3. C. Smallest reading it can measure
  4. D. Average reading
Answer: C. Smallest reading it can measure

Q23. Magnetic declination is the angle between:

  1. A. Two contours
  2. B. Magnetic meridian and true meridian
  3. C. Two bearings
  4. D. True meridian and grid north
Answer: B. Magnetic meridian and true meridian

Q24. The total number of links in a 20-meter metric chain is:

  1. A. 50
  2. B. 100
  3. C. 150
  4. D. 200
Answer: B. 100

Q25. In leveling, the staff reading taken on a point of known elevation is called:

  1. A. Back sight
  2. B. Fore sight
  3. C. Intermediate sight
  4. D. Change point
Answer: A. Back sight
