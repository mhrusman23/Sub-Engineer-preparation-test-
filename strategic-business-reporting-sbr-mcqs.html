Q1. IFRS 3 requires business combinations to be accounted for using the:

  1. A. Equity method
  2. B. Acquisition method
  3. C. Pooling method
  4. D. Cost method
Answer: B. Acquisition method

Q2. IFRS 13 defines fair value as the price that would be received to:

  1. A. Scrap an asset
  2. B. Insure an asset
  3. C. Sell an asset in an orderly transaction
  4. D. Buy an asset new
Answer: C. Sell an asset in an orderly transaction

Q3. Under IFRS 16 a lessee generally recognises a right-of-use asset and a:

  1. A. Lease liability
  2. B. Provision
  3. C. Reserve
  4. D. Contingent asset
Answer: A. Lease liability

Q4. Deferred tax under IAS 12 arises from:

  1. A. Dividends
  2. B. Temporary differences
  3. C. Permanent differences only
  4. D. Share issues only
Answer: B. Temporary differences

Q5. IFRS 2 deals with:

  1. A. Revenue
  2. B. Inventories
  3. C. Share-based payment
  4. D. Leases
Answer: C. Share-based payment

Q6. Functional currency is the currency of the:

  1. A. Primary economic environment of the entity
  2. B. Auditor's country
  3. C. Bank account
  4. D. Parent's head office only
Answer: A. Primary economic environment of the entity

Q7. Under IFRS 9 financial assets are measured at amortised cost, FVOCI or:

  1. A. FVTPL
  2. B. Historic cost only
  3. C. Replacement cost
  4. D. Net realisable value
Answer: A. FVTPL

Q8. IFRS 8 uses the management approach to identify:

  1. A. Provisions
  2. B. Leases
  3. C. Operating segments
  4. D. Associates
Answer: C. Operating segments

Q9. IAS 33 deals with:

  1. A. Borrowing costs
  2. B. Government grants
  3. C. Earnings per share
  4. D. Inventories
Answer: C. Earnings per share

Q10. IAS 24 requires disclosure of:

  1. A. Tax rates
  2. B. Related party transactions
  3. C. Lease terms
  4. D. Segment profits
Answer: B. Related party transactions

Q11. Which accounting standard governs the recognition and measurement of government grants and disclosure of government assistance?

  1. A. IAS 20
  2. B. IAS 37
  3. C. IAS 12
  4. D. IAS 40
Answer: A. IAS 20

Q12. Under IAS 38 Intangible Assets, how should internally generated brands and mastheads be treated?

  1. A. Capitalized at development cost
  2. B. Recognized at fair value
  3. C. Expensed immediately when incurred
  4. D. Amortized over 5 years
Answer: C. Expensed immediately when incurred

Q13. According to IAS 36 Impairment of Assets, what represents the recoverable amount of an asset or cash-generating unit?

  1. A. Carrying amount less residual value
  2. B. Higher of fair value less costs of disposal and value in use
  3. C. Lower of historical cost and net realizable value
  4. D. Discounted future cash inflows without outflows
Answer: B. Higher of fair value less costs of disposal and value in use

Q14. How does IAS 21 require foreign currency monetary items at the end of a reporting period to be translated?

  1. A. At the historical exchange rate at the transaction date
  2. B. At the average exchange rate for the period
  3. C. At the closing rate at the reporting date
  4. D. At the opening rate of the financial year
Answer: C. At the closing rate at the reporting date

Q15. Which standard specifies the requirements for recognizing and measuring provisions, contingent liabilities, and contingent assets?

  1. A. IAS 10
  2. B. IAS 37
  3. C. IFRS 15
  4. D. IFRS 9
Answer: B. IAS 37

Q16. Under IFRS 15 Revenue from Contracts with Customers, when is revenue typically recognized?

  1. A. When the cash is received from the customer
  2. B. When the purchase order is signed by both parties
  3. C. When control of the goods or services is transferred to the customer
  4. D. When the manufacturing process is completed
Answer: C. When control of the goods or services is transferred to the customer

Q17. What level of qualification does the Strategic Business Reporting exam belong to within the ACCA certification structure?

  1. A. Applied Knowledge
  2. B. Applied Skills
  3. C. Strategic Professional
  4. D. Foundation Diploma
Answer: C. Strategic Professional

Q18. Under the conceptual framework and reporting guidelines tested in SBR, perspective in financial reports is primarily considered from the viewpoint of which key stakeholder group?

  1. A. External tax authorities
  2. B. Primary users such as existing and potential investors
  3. C. Internal departmental managers
  4. D. Corporate environmental auditors
Answer: B. Primary users such as existing and potential investors

Q19. Effective debriefing of practice questions in the computer-based SBR environment primarily assists a candidate in identifying what?

  1. A. Software licensing errors
  2. B. Personal knowledge gaps and exam technique flaws
  3. C. Typing speed bottlenecks on a standard keyboard
  4. D. Changes in global interest rates
Answer: B. Personal knowledge gaps and exam technique flaws
