Q1. If 2(x − 3) = 10, then x =

  1. A. 5
  2. B. 6
  3. C. 8
  4. D. 10
Answer: C. 8

Q2. The slope of the line y = 3x + 2 is:

  1. A. 2
  2. B. 3
  3. C. 5
  4. D. x
Answer: B. 3

Q3. What is the value of 5² + 4³?

  1. A. 45
  2. B. 89
  3. C. 109
  4. D. 41
Answer: B. 89

Q4. A rectangle has length 8 and width 5. Its perimeter is:

  1. A. 13
  2. B. 26
  3. C. 40
  4. D. 80
Answer: B. 26

Q5. If f(x) = x² − 1, then f(4) =

  1. A. 7
  2. B. 15
  3. C. 17
  4. D. 8
Answer: B. 15

Q6. The solution of the inequality 3x > 12 is:

  1. A. x > 3
  2. B. x > 4
  3. C. x < 4
  4. D. x > 12
Answer: B. x > 4

Q7. In a right triangle, sides 6 and 8 — the hypotenuse is:

  1. A. 10
  2. B. 12
  3. C. 14
  4. D. 48
Answer: A. 10

Q8. If 20% of a number is 50, the number is:

  1. A. 100
  2. B. 200
  3. C. 250
  4. D. 500
Answer: C. 250

Q9. The median of 3, 7, 9, 15, 21 is:

  1. A. 7
  2. B. 9
  3. C. 11
  4. D. 15
Answer: B. 9

Q10. A line parallel to y = 2x + 1 has slope:

  1. A. −2
  2. B. 1/2
  3. C. 2
  4. D. −1/2
Answer: C. 2
