Q1. If 2(x − 3) = 10, then x =

  1. A. 5
  2. B. 6
  3. C. 8
  4. D. 10
Answer: C. 8

Q2. The slope of the line y = 3x + 2 is:

  1. A. 2
  2. B. 3
  3. C. 5
  4. D. x
