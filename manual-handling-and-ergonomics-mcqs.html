Q1. Manual handling means:

  1. A. Using machines only
  2. B. Lifting, carrying, pushing or pulling loads by hand or bodily force
  3. C. Writing reports
  4. D. Driving vehicles
Answer: B. Lifting, carrying, pushing or pulling loads by hand or bodily force

Q2. The TILE approach to manual handling assessment stands for:

  1. A. Task, Individual, Load, Environment
  2. B. Time, Injury, Law, Energy
  3. C. Team, Inspection, Load, Exit
  4. D. Training, Insurance, Labour, Equipment
Answer: A. Task, Individual, Load, Environment

Q3. The safest way to lift a load is to:

  1. A. Bend your back
  2. B. Bend your knees and keep the load close
  3. C. Twist while lifting
  4. D. Lift as fast as possible
Answer: B. Bend your knees and keep the load close

Q4. Mechanical aids such as trolleys reduce risk by:

  1. A. Making loads heavier
  2. B. Reducing the physical effort needed
  3. C. Removing training
  4. D. Increasing speed
Answer: B. Reducing the physical effort needed

Q5. Ergonomics is the study of:

  1. A. Chemicals
  2. B. Fitting the job to the person
  3. C. Fire behaviour
  4. D. Electricity
Answer: B. Fitting the job to the person

Q6. A common injury from poor manual handling is:

  1. A. Hearing loss
  2. B. Back and musculoskeletal disorders
  3. C. Burns
  4. D. Eye damage
Answer: B. Back and musculoskeletal disorders

Q7. Before lifting, the FIRST thing to check is:

  1. A. Your gloves
  2. B. Whether the load can be moved safely / its weight and route
  3. C. The time
  4. D. Your phone
Answer: B. Whether the load can be moved safely / its weight and route

Q8. Team lifting requires:

  1. A. No planning
  2. B. One person in charge giving coordinated instructions
  3. C. Everyone lifting at different times
  4. D. Running with the load
Answer: B. One person in charge giving coordinated instructions

Q9. Display screen equipment (DSE) risks include:

  1. A. Eye strain and posture problems
  2. B. Burns
  3. C. Falls from height
  4. D. Noise damage
Answer: A. Eye strain and posture problems

Q10. Employers should avoid hazardous manual handling:

  1. A. Never
  2. B. So far as reasonably practicable
  3. C. Only for new staff
  4. D. Only in summer
Answer: B. So far as reasonably practicable

Q11. What is the primary objective of implementing ergonomic principles in a workplace?

  1. A. To reduce fatigue and musculoskeletal disorders
  2. B. To increase the speed of machinery
  3. C. To decrease the number of fire hazards
  4. D. To reduce the cost of raw materials
Answer: A. To reduce fatigue and musculoskeletal disorders

Q12. During a manual lift, where should the heaviest part of the load ideally be kept relative to the body?

  1. A. As far away as possible
  2. B. At arm's length
  3. C. As close to the trunk as possible
  4. D. Above shoulder height
Answer: C. As close to the trunk as possible

Q13. Which of the following describes a proper lifting posture?

  1. A. Bending completely from the waist with straight knees
  2. B. Bending the knees while keeping the back straight
  3. C. Twisting the torso while lifting the load
  4. D. Holding the breath throughout the entire lift
Answer: B. Bending the knees while keeping the back straight

Q14. What does the 'L' in the LITE acronym for manual handling risk assessment stand for?

  1. A. Location
  2. B. Load
  3. C. Length
  4. D. Limit
Answer: B. Load

Q15. When is a team lift generally required or recommended?

  1. A. When the load is light but bulky
  2. B. When the load is too heavy, awkward, or unstable for one person safely
  3. C. Whenever workers want to finish work faster
  4. D. Only when handling hazardous chemicals
Answer: B. When the load is too heavy, awkward, or unstable for one person safely

Q16. What does a dynamic risk assessment primarily involve in a workplace setting?

  1. A. A formal written report submitted annually
  2. B. An ongoing, real-time evaluation of risks as conditions change
  3. C. A mechanical test of lifting strength capacity
  4. D. A scheduled manager’s inspection of office equipment
Answer: B. An ongoing, real-time evaluation of risks as conditions change

Q17. Which of the following represents the core ergonomic approach to manual handling tasks?

  1. A. Designing tasks and workstations to fit the physical capabilities of the worker
  2. B. Relying exclusively on heavy-duty mechanical hoists and cranes
  3. C. Ignoring individual differences to maintain uniform productivity
  4. D. Encouraging workers to lift maximum weight loads regardless of discomfort
Answer: A. Designing tasks and workstations to fit the physical capabilities of the worker

Q18. According to occupational health and safety standards, who holds the responsibility for ensuring safe manual handling practices?

  1. A. Only the business owner or employer
  2. B. Only the frontline employees performing the physical tasks
  3. C. Both the employer and the employees sharing compliance duties
  4. D. The external government safety inspectorate
Answer: C. Both the employer and the employees sharing compliance duties

Q19. What is considered the primary and most effective method for minimizing the hazards associated with manual handling operations?

  1. A. Increasing the frequency of stretching breaks
  2. B. The elimination of manual handling risk through the use of mechanical aids
  3. C. Relying solely on personal protective footwear
  4. D. Reducing the number of working hours per shift
Answer: B. The elimination of manual handling risk through the use of mechanical aids

Q20. Which of the following physical characteristics of a load can directly contribute to a manual handling hazard?

  1. A. The bright color paint finish of the object
  2. B. Its weight, size, shape, and unstable shifting contents
  3. C. The brand name label printed on the packaging
  4. D. The retail cost of the stored items inside
Answer: B. Its weight, size, shape, and unstable shifting contents

Q21. What are the three main fundamental reasons for managing health and safety risks like manual handling in an organization?

  1. A. Moral, legal, and financial reasons
  2. B. Political, operational, and technical reasons
  3. C. Global, regional, and local reasons
  4. D. Theoretical, academic, and practical reasons
Answer: A. Moral, legal, and financial reasons

Q22. Which of the following is the first step in a dynamic risk assessment before a manual lift?

  1. A. Check the worker's personal protective equipment
  2. B. Determine the weight and shape of the load
  3. C. Ask the supervisor for approval
  4. D. Record the time of the lift
Answer: B. Determine the weight and shape of the load

Q23. Which control measure is the most effective in eliminating manual handling hazards?

  1. A. Providing more training
  2. B. Using a higher quality lifting belt
  3. C. Eliminating the need to move the load
  4. D. Installing a warning sign
Answer: C. Eliminating the need to move the load

Q24. Which body part is most commonly injured by improper manual handling?

  1. A. The eyes
  2. B. The back
  3. C. The ears
  4. D. The fingers
Answer: B. The back

Q25. When should a team lift be considered mandatory?

  1. A. When the load is lighter than 20 kg
  2. B. When the load exceeds the worker’s single‑lift limit
  3. C. When the worker prefers to lift alone
  4. D. When the workplace has no mechanical aids
Answer: B. When the load exceeds the worker’s single‑lift limit
