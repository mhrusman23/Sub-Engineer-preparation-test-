Q1. Manual handling means:

  1. A. Using machines only
  2. B. Lifting, carrying, pushing or pulling loads by hand or bodily force
  3. C. Writing reports
  4. D. Driving vehicles
Answer: B. Lifting, carrying, pushing or pulling loads by hand or bodily force

Q2. The TILE approach to manual handling assessment stands for:

  1. A. Task, Individual, Load, Environment
  2. B. Time, Injury, Law, Energy
  3. C. Team, Inspection, Load, Exit
  4. D. Training, Insurance, Labour, Equipment
Answer: A. Task, Individual, Load, Environment

Q3. The safest way to lift a load is to:

  1. A. Bend your back
  2. B. Bend your knees and keep the load close
  3. C. Twist while lifting
  4. D. Lift as fast as possible
Answer: B. Bend your knees and keep the load close

Q4. Mechanical aids such as trolleys reduce risk by:

  1. A. Making loads heavier
  2. B. Reducing the physical effort needed
  3. C. Removing training
  4. D. Increasing speed
Answer: B. Reducing the physical effort needed

Q5. Ergonomics is the study of:

  1. A. Chemicals
  2. B. Fitting the job to the person
  3. C. Fire behaviour
  4. D. Electricity
Answer: B. Fitting the job to the person

Q6. A common injury from poor manual handling is:

  1. A. Hearing loss
  2. B. Back and musculoskeletal disorders
  3. C. Burns
  4. D. Eye damage
Answer: B. Back and musculoskeletal disorders

Q7. Before lifting, the FIRST thing to check is:

  1. A. Your gloves
  2. B. Whether the load can be moved safely / its weight and route
  3. C. The time
  4. D. Your phone
Answer: B. Whether the load can be moved safely / its weight and route

Q8. Team lifting requires:

  1. A. No planning
  2. B. One person in charge giving coordinated instructions
  3. C. Everyone lifting at different times
  4. D. Running with the load
Answer: B. One person in charge giving coordinated instructions

Q9. Display screen equipment (DSE) risks include:

  1. A. Eye strain and posture problems
  2. B. Burns
  3. C. Falls from height
  4. D. Noise damage
Answer: A. Eye strain and posture problems

Q10. Employers should avoid hazardous manual handling:

  1. A. Never
  2. B. So far as reasonably practicable
  3. C. Only for new staff
  4. D. Only in summer
Answer: B. So far as reasonably practicable
