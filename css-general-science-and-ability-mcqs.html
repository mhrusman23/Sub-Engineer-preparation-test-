Q1. The chemical formula of common salt is:

  1. A. NaOH
  2. B. NaCl
  3. C. KCl
  4. D. CaCO3
Answer: B. NaCl

Q2. Which organ of the human body produces insulin?

  1. A. Liver
  2. B. Pancreas
  3. C. Kidney
  4. D. Spleen
Answer: B. Pancreas

Q3. The speed of light in vacuum is approximately:

  1. A. 300,000 km/s
  2. B. 150,000 km/s
  3. C. 1,000 km/s
  4. D. 30,000 km/s
Answer: A. 300,000 km/s

Q4. Photosynthesis in plants produces:

  1. A. Carbon dioxide
  2. B. Oxygen and glucose
  3. C. Nitrogen
  4. D. Water only
Answer: B. Oxygen and glucose

Q5. If a number is increased by 20% and then decreased by 20%, the net change is:

  1. A. No change
  2. B. 4% decrease
  3. C. 4% increase
  4. D. 2% decrease
Answer: B. 4% decrease

Q6. The largest planet in our solar system is:

  1. A. Saturn
  2. B. Jupiter
  3. C. Neptune
  4. D. Earth
Answer: B. Jupiter

Q7. Water boils at 100°C at:

  1. A. High altitude
  2. B. Sea level (standard pressure)
  3. C. In vacuum
  4. D. Only in winter
Answer: B. Sea level (standard pressure)

Q8. A man walks 3 km east then 4 km north. His distance from the start (straight line) is:

  1. A. 5 km
  2. B. 6 km
  3. C. 7 km
  4. D. 12 km
Answer: A. 5 km

Q9. Which gas makes up most of Earth's atmosphere?

  1. A. Oxygen
  2. B. Nitrogen
  3. C. Carbon dioxide
  4. D. Argon
Answer: B. Nitrogen

Q10. If 6 men do a job in 12 days, 4 men will do it in:

  1. A. 8 days
  2. B. 18 days
  3. C. 16 days
  4. D. 10 days
Answer: B. 18 days
