Q1. In a four-stroke engine, one cycle is completed in how many crankshaft revolutions?

  1. A. 4
  2. B. 2
  3. C. 3
  4. D. 1
Answer: B. 2

Q2. The Otto cycle is the ideal cycle for:

  1. A. Diesel engines
  2. B. Petrol engines
  3. C. Gas turbines
  4. D. Steam engines
Answer: B. Petrol engines

Q3. The Diesel cycle is the ideal cycle for:

  1. A. Diesel engines
  2. B. Steam turbines
  3. C. Jet engines
  4. D. Petrol engines
Answer: A. Diesel engines

Q4. The compression ratio is the ratio of:

  1. A. Swept volume to total
  2. B. Bore to stroke
  3. C. Clearance volume to swept volume
  4. D. Total cylinder volume to clearance volume
Answer: D. Total cylinder volume to clearance volume

Q5. The spark plug is used in:

  1. A. Petrol engines
  2. B. Gas turbines
  3. C. Diesel engines
  4. D. Steam engines
Answer: A. Petrol engines

Q6. A carburettor mixes:

  1. A. Fuel and water
  2. B. Oil and air
  3. C. Oil and water
  4. D. Air and fuel
Answer: D. Air and fuel

Q7. Octane number measures a fuel's resistance to:

  1. A. Evaporation
  2. B. Burning
  3. C. Freezing
  4. D. Knocking
Answer: D. Knocking

Q8. Cetane number is used for:

  1. A. Lubricating oil
  2. B. Diesel fuel ignition quality
  3. C. Coolant quality
  4. D. Petrol knock
Answer: B. Diesel fuel ignition quality

Q9. The ideal air-standard cycle for a gas turbine is the:

  1. A. Brayton cycle
  2. B. Otto cycle
  3. C. Diesel cycle
  4. D. Rankine cycle
Answer: A. Brayton cycle

Q10. The function of the lubricating oil is to:

  1. A. Raise temperature
  2. B. Increase fuel use
  3. C. Reduce friction and wear
  4. D. Cool exhaust only
Answer: C. Reduce friction and wear

Q11. Which component in a diesel engine system directly injects fuel into the combustion chamber?

  1. A. Carburettor
  2. B. Fuel injector
  3. C. Spark plug
  4. D. Fuel pump
Answer: B. Fuel injector

Q12. What is the primary purpose of the piston rings in an internal combustion engine?

  1. A. To connect the piston to the crankshaft
  2. B. To cool the engine block directly
  3. C. To seal combustion gases and control oil consumption
  4. D. To regulate the intake valve timing
Answer: C. To seal combustion gases and control oil consumption

Q13. In a two-stroke petrol engine, scavenging refers to which of the following processes?

  1. A. Igniting the air-fuel mixture using a spark
  2. B. Expelling exhaust gases and filling fresh charge
  3. C. Lubricating the main crankshaft bearings
  4. D. Cooling the exterior engine fins
Answer: B. Expelling exhaust gases and filling fresh charge

Q14. Which engine mechanism is typically used to convert reciprocating motion of the piston into rotary motion?

  1. A. Camshaft and valve assembly
  2. B. Crankshaft and connecting rod
  3. C. Flywheel and clutch plate
  4. D. Timing belt and pulley system
Answer: B. Crankshaft and connecting rod

Q15. What does volumetric efficiency of an internal combustion engine measure?

  1. A. Total fuel consumed per unit of power produced
  2. B. Ratio of actual mass of air drawn into the cylinder to the theoretical mass
  3. C. Thermal energy converted into mechanical work
  4. D. Percentage of exhaust emissions released into the atmosphere
Answer: B. Ratio of actual mass of air drawn into the cylinder to the theoretical mass

Q16. Why is supercharging used in internal combustion engines?

  1. A. To decrease the overall engine weight
  2. B. To increase the density of air entering the cylinders for higher power output
  3. C. To reduce the compression ratio automatically
  4. D. To eliminate the need for engine cooling systems
Answer: B. To increase the density of air entering the cylinders for higher power output
