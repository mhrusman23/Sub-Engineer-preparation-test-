Q1. COSHH stands for:

  1. A. Control of Substances Hazardous to Health
  2. B. Care of Safety and Health at Home
  3. C. Control of Safety Hazards in Hospitals
  4. D. Chemical Office Safety Handbook
Answer: A. Control of Substances Hazardous to Health

Q2. A safety data sheet (SDS) provides information about:

  1. A. Prices
  2. B. The hazards and safe handling of a substance
  3. C. Delivery dates
  4. D. Company profits
Answer: B. The hazards and safe handling of a substance

Q3. The diamond-shaped pictogram with an exclamation mark means:

  1. A. Explosive
  2. B. Health hazard / irritant
  3. C. Oxidiser
  4. D. Gas under pressure
Answer: B. Health hazard / irritant

Q4. Workplace exposure limits (WELs) control:

  1. A. Working hours
  2. B. Concentration of airborne substances workers may breathe
  3. C. Noise levels
  4. D. Lighting
Answer: B. Concentration of airborne substances workers may breathe

Q5. The best control for a hazardous substance is to:

  1. A. Give gloves
  2. B. Eliminate or substitute it with a safer substance
  3. C. Warn workers
  4. D. Open a window
Answer: B. Eliminate or substitute it with a safer substance

Q6. Local exhaust ventilation (LEV) works by:

  1. A. Blowing dust around
  2. B. Capturing contaminants at source
  3. C. Cooling the room
  4. D. Making noise
Answer: B. Capturing contaminants at source

Q7. Health surveillance is needed when:

  1. A. Workers are new
  2. B. Exposure may still cause health effects, e.g. to skin or lungs
  3. C. The manager requests it
  4. D. Once every ten years
Answer: B. Exposure may still cause health effects, e.g. to skin or lungs

Q8. Substances can enter the body by:

  1. A. Breathing in, skin contact and swallowing
  2. B. Looking at them
  3. C. Standing near them
  4. D. Hearing about them
Answer: A. Breathing in, skin contact and swallowing

Q9. A corrosive substance can:

  1. A. Burn skin and eyes on contact
  2. B. Only smell bad
  3. C. Improve cleaning
  4. D. Do nothing
Answer: A. Burn skin and eyes on contact

Q10. COSHH assessments must be reviewed:

  1. A. Never
  2. B. Regularly and when work changes
  3. C. Only after prosecution
  4. D. Every 20 years
Answer: B. Regularly and when work changes

Q11. What primary operational detail should be analyzed first when starting stage one of a COSHH substance evaluation?

  1. A. A) Identify all hazardous substances used or produced by the process
  2. B. B) Measure the cafeteria noise levels
  3. C. C) Inspect the office air conditioning filters
  4. D. D) Review the company marketing strategy
Answer: A. A) Identify all hazardous substances used or produced by the process

Q12. What is the primary purpose of carrying out a COSHH risk assessment in a workplace?

  1. A. A) To calculate employee quarterly bonuses
  2. B. B) To establish hazards and implement proper controls to protect workers
  3. C. C) To schedule routine machine maintenance hours
  4. D. D) To determine the market price of raw materials
Answer: B. B) To establish hazards and implement proper controls to protect workers

Q13. Inhalation, ingestion, and absorption through the skin are the main pathways for what type of workplace hazard entry?

  1. A. A) Hazardous chemical substances
  2. B. B) High voltage electrical currents
  3. C. C) Excessive vibration forces
  4. D. D) Slips, trips, and falls
Answer: A. A) Hazardous chemical substances

Q14. When assessing the exposure risks of hazardous materials during a process, what must an employer specifically consider regarding vulnerable individuals?

  1. A. A) Their personal credit scores
  2. B. B) Young persons and new or expectant mothers
  3. C. C) Their commuting distance to work
  4. D. D) Their preferred annual leave dates
Answer: B. B) Young persons and new or expectant mothers

Q15. Which specific group of hazardous agents requires risk assessments to account for materials that can induce genetic defects or cancer?

  1. A. A) Carcinogens and mutagens
  2. B. B) Inert fillers and pigments
  3. C. C) Simple asphyxiants
  4. D. D) Non-flammable solvents
Answer: A. A) Carcinogens and mutagens

Q16. Which document must an employer obtain from a manufacturer to help identify the health risks associated with a chemical product?

  1. A. A) Safety Data Sheet
  2. B. B) Fire Exit Blueprint
  3. C. C) Equipment Warranty Certificate
  4. D. D) Payroll Tax Ledger
Answer: A. A) Safety Data Sheet

Q17. Which specific hierarchy tier is classified as an engineering control under COSHH regulations?

  1. A. Providing personal protective equipment
  2. B. Substituting the toxic material with a safe one
  3. C. Installing a local exhaust ventilation system
  4. D. Conducting safety induction training
Answer: C. Installing a local exhaust ventilation system

Q18. What is the legal purpose of establishing a Workplace Exposure Limit (WEL) for a chemical?

  1. A. To eliminate the use of the chemical completely
  2. B. To restrict the maximum airborne concentration over a specified reference period
  3. C. To measure the exact financial cost of chemical waste
  4. D. To determine the minimum quantity required for manufacturing
Answer: B. To restrict the maximum airborne concentration over a specified reference period

Q19. Under what circumstance is regular health surveillance legally required for workers exposed to hazardous substances?

  1. A. When workers request additional cash bonuses
  2. B. When there is an identifiable disease linked to the working conditions
  3. C. Whenever any cleaning chemical is used on site
  4. D. Only after a serious accident has already occurred
Answer: B. When there is an identifiable disease linked to the working conditions

Q20. What information does the acronym 'CAS' typically convey on a chemical safety document?

  1. A. Chemical Abstract Service unique identifying number
  2. B. Certified Accident Standard rating
  3. C. Controlled Atmospheric Storage requirement
  4. D. Corrosive Action Severity index
Answer: A. Chemical Abstract Service unique identifying number

Q21. Why is personal protective equipment (PPE) considered the last line of defense in exposure control?

  1. A. It is the most expensive control method available
  2. B. It relies entirely on human behavior and proper fit to remain effective
  3. C. It completely removes the hazard from the work environment
  4. D. It requires no maintenance or replacement
Answer: B. It relies entirely on human behavior and proper fit to remain effective

Q22. Which hazardous substance classification is specifically denoted by a pictogram showing a tree and dead fish?

  1. A. Acute toxicity
  2. B. Gases under pressure
  3. C. Hazardous to the aquatic environment
  4. D. Oxidizing properties
Answer: C. Hazardous to the aquatic environment

Q23. If a worker is exposed to a substance that can enter the body through skin contact, which route of exposure is relevant?

  1. A. Inhalation
  2. B. Ingestion
  3. C. Absorption through skin
  4. D. Radiation absorption
Answer: C. Absorption through skin

Q24. When a new process introduces a chemical that has not been used before, what is the first step in a COSHH risk assessment?

  1. A. Select a personal protective equipment set
  2. B. Identify all substances involved
  3. C. Check the supplier’s warranty
  4. D. Schedule a health check for workers
Answer: B. Identify all substances involved

Q25. A chemical that can cause cancer is classified as a:

  1. A. Flammable substance
  2. B. Carcinogen
  3. C. Oxidiser
  4. D. Corrosive agent
Answer: B. Carcinogen
