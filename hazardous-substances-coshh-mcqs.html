Q1. COSHH stands for:

  1. A. Control of Substances Hazardous to Health
  2. B. Care of Safety and Health at Home
  3. C. Control of Safety Hazards in Hospitals
  4. D. Chemical Office Safety Handbook
Answer: A. Control of Substances Hazardous to Health

Q2. A safety data sheet (SDS) provides information about:

  1. A. Prices
  2. B. The hazards and safe handling of a substance
  3. C. Delivery dates
  4. D. Company profits
Answer: B. The hazards and safe handling of a substance

Q3. The diamond-shaped pictogram with an exclamation mark means:

  1. A. Explosive
  2. B. Health hazard / irritant
  3. C. Oxidiser
  4. D. Gas under pressure
Answer: B. Health hazard / irritant

Q4. Workplace exposure limits (WELs) control:

  1. A. Working hours
  2. B. Concentration of airborne substances workers may breathe
  3. C. Noise levels
  4. D. Lighting
Answer: B. Concentration of airborne substances workers may breathe

Q5. The best control for a hazardous substance is to:

  1. A. Give gloves
  2. B. Eliminate or substitute it with a safer substance
  3. C. Warn workers
  4. D. Open a window
Answer: B. Eliminate or substitute it with a safer substance

Q6. Local exhaust ventilation (LEV) works by:

  1. A. Blowing dust around
  2. B. Capturing contaminants at source
  3. C. Cooling the room
  4. D. Making noise
Answer: B. Capturing contaminants at source

Q7. Health surveillance is needed when:

  1. A. Workers are new
  2. B. Exposure may still cause health effects, e.g. to skin or lungs
  3. C. The manager requests it
  4. D. Once every ten years
Answer: B. Exposure may still cause health effects, e.g. to skin or lungs

Q8. Substances can enter the body by:

  1. A. Breathing in, skin contact and swallowing
  2. B. Looking at them
  3. C. Standing near them
  4. D. Hearing about them
Answer: A. Breathing in, skin contact and swallowing

Q9. A corrosive substance can:

  1. A. Burn skin and eyes on contact
  2. B. Only smell bad
  3. C. Improve cleaning
  4. D. Do nothing
Answer: A. Burn skin and eyes on contact

Q10. COSHH assessments must be reviewed:

  1. A. Never
  2. B. Regularly and when work changes
  3. C. Only after prosecution
  4. D. Every 20 years
Answer: B. Regularly and when work changes
