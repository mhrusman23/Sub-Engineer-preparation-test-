Q1. Mass of half bag of ordinary portland cement is

  1. A. 40 Kg
  2. B. 25 Kg
  3. C. 30 Kg
  4. D. 50 Kg
Answer: B. 25 Kg

Q2. Initial setting time of ordinary portland cement should not be less than

  1. A. 60 mins
  2. B. 30 mins
  3. C. 10 HRC
  4. D. 15 mins
Answer: B. 30 mins

Q3. Final setting time of ordinary portland cement should not be more than

  1. A. 45 mins
  2. B. 60 mins
  3. C. 600 mins
  4. D. 30 mins
Answer: C. 600 mins

Q4. Percentage quantity of lime in manufacturing of ordinary portland cement

  1. A. 17-25%
  2. B. 3-8%
  3. C. 60-67%
  4. D. 1-2%
Answer: C. 60-67%

Q5. Percentage quantity of alumina in manufacturing of ordinary portland cement

  1. A. 17-25%
  2. B. 60-67%
  3. C. 1-2%
  4. D. 3-8%
Answer: D. 3-8%

Q6. Percentage quantity of silica in manufacturing of ordinary portland cement

  1. A. 17-25%
  2. B. 1-2%
  3. C. 3-8%
  4. D. 60-67%
Answer: A. 17-25%

Q7. For initial setting of cement responsible chemical compound is

  1. A. C3A
  2. B. C2S
  3. C. C3S

Q8. According to fineness of ordinary portland cement retained % should not be more than

  1. A. 20%
  2. B. 10%
  3. C. 15%
  4. D. 5%
Answer: B. 10%

Q9. Cement, sand ratio of mortar prepared for compression test of cement is

  1. A. 1:4
  2. B. 1:3
  3. C. 1:2
  4. D. 1:1
Answer: B. 1:3

Q10. Admixture used to increase the setting time of cement is

  1. A. accelerator
  2. B. retarder
  3. C. Pozzolanic admixture
  4. D. reducer
Answer: B. retarder

Q11. Aggregates in concrete play a role of

  1. A. binding
  2. B. catalyst
  3. C. inert
  4. D. all of these
Answer: C. inert

Q12. Lime present in cement causes

  1. A. soundness
  2. B. strength
  3. C. controls the klinkering temperature
  4. D. both a and b
Answer: D. both a and b

Q13. Setting time of cement is determined to

  1. A. mixing of concrete
  2. B. placing of concrete
  3. C. compacting of cement
  4. D. all of these

Q14. Vicat apparatus is used to perform

  1. A. fineness test
  2. B. Briquette test
  3. C. soundness test
  4. D. consistency test

Q15. Apparatus used to find setting time of cement is

  1. A. Le Chatelier apparatus
  2. B. Slump cone

Q16. Quantity of gypsum mixed in ordinary Portland cement at the time of manufacture is

  1. A. 0%
  2. B. 15%

  1. A. C2S
  2. B. C3S
  3. C. C4AF
  4. D. C3A

Q18. Maximum temperature of rotary kiln at the time of manufacturing of cement is

  1. A. 1850 °C
  2. B. 1450 °C
  3. C. 1250 °C

Answer: B. 1450 °C

Q19. Cause of bulking of sand is

  1. A. surface dampness
  2. B. air voids
  3. C. clay particles
  4. D. viscosity
Answer: A. surface dampness

Q20. Compressive strength of 1 N/mm² is nearly equivalent to

  1. A. 1 Kg/mm²
  2. B. 1000 Kg/mm²
  3. C. 0.1 Kg/mm²
  4. D. 10 Kg/mm²
Answer: C. 0.1 Kg/mm²

Q21. Under same conditions, strength of concrete depends upon

  1. A. shear strength
  2. B. water-cement ratio
  3. C. bond strength
  4. D. nature of bond
Answer: B. water-cement ratio

Q22. Without crushing the sample or structure, method of testing is

  1. A. Briquette test
  2. B. Le Chatelier test
  3. C. Smith rebound hammer test
  4. D. Flexural test
Answer: C. Smith rebound hammer test

Q23. Main cause of gaining the strength of concrete is

  1. A. workability
  2. B. time
  3. C. hydration of cement
  4. D. temperature

Q24. The best check of quality of concrete is

  1. A. impermeability
  2. B. crushing strength
  3. C. density
  4. D. workability
Answer: B. crushing strength

Q25. Tensile strength of concrete as compared to its compressive strength is

  1. A. 25%
