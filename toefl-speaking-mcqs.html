TOEFL Speaking MCQs (IELTS & English Tests)

Free MCQs with answers for TOEFL Speaking. 20 questions in 1 chapter test are in the app; all are shown here.

Q1. TOEFL Speaking includes tasks where you:

  1. A. Only read aloud
  2. B. Speak about familiar topics and summarise readings/lectures
  3. C. Write essays
  4. D. Listen only
Answer: B. Speak about familiar topics and summarise readings/lectures

Q2. In the integrated tasks, you should:

  1. A. Give only your opinion
  2. B. Summarise the reading and lecture accurately
  3. C. Ignore the reading
  4. D. Memorise the lecture
Answer: B. Summarise the reading and lecture accurately

Q3. Good TOEFL speaking notes should be:

  1. A. Full sentences
  2. B. Brief keywords and structure
  3. C. Drawings only
  4. D. In another language only
Answer: B. Brief keywords and structure

Q4. Delivery in speaking is judged on:

  1. A. Accent only
  2. B. Fluency, pronunciation and natural pace
  3. C. Volume
  4. D. Speed only
Answer: B. Fluency, pronunciation and natural pace

Q5. Preparation time before speaking is:

  1. A. None
  2. B. Short (about 15-30 seconds depending on task)
  3. C. 5 minutes
  4. D. Unlimited
Answer: B. Short (about 15-30 seconds depending on task)

Q6. For the independent task, a strong answer has:

  1. A. No structure
  2. B. A clear position, two reasons and examples
  3. C. Only questions
  4. D. One word
Answer: B. A clear position, two reasons and examples

Q7. If you run out of things to say, you should:

  1. A. Stop early in silence
  2. B. Conclude your point neatly
  3. C. Repeat the question
  4. D. Speak about a new topic
Answer: B. Conclude your point neatly

Q8. Transition words like 'firstly' and 'as a result':

  1. A. Hurt your score
  2. B. Help organise your answer
  3. C. Are forbidden
  4. D. Are only for writing
Answer: B. Help organise your answer

Q9. Speaking too fast usually:

  1. A. Helps fluency
  2. B. Hurts clarity and increases mistakes
  3. C. Raises your score
  4. D. Is required
Answer: B. Hurts clarity and increases mistakes

Q10. In integrated tasks, your own opinion should:

  1. A. Be the whole answer
  2. B. Not be added — report the source information
  3. C. Replace the lecture
  4. D. Be in the introduction only
Answer: B. Not be added — report the source information

Q11. A template/structure for answers helps you to:

  1. A. Cheat
  2. B. Organise ideas within the short time
  3. C. Memorise answers
  4. D. Avoid listening
Answer: B. Organise ideas within the short time

Q12. Pronunciation problems matter most when they:

  1. A. Exist at all
  2. B. Make you hard to understand
  3. C. Are British
  4. D. Are American
Answer: B. Make you hard to understand

Q13. The speaking section is recorded and scored by:

  1. A. Other students
  2. B. Trained raters (and automated scoring support)
  3. C. Your teacher
  4. D. Nobody
Answer: B. Trained raters (and automated scoring support)

Q14. Pausing naturally between ideas is:

  1. A. A failure
  2. B. Normal and better than rushing
  3. C. Punished heavily
  4. D. Not allowed
Answer: B. Normal and better than rushing

Q15. For campus-situation tasks, the man's/woman's opinion is usually:

  1. A. Unimportant
  2. B. The key content of your summary
  3. C. Always negative
  4. D. Never stated
Answer: B. The key content of your summary

Q16. Time management: answers are usually:

  1. A. 10 minutes
  2. B. 45-60 seconds per task
  3. C. 5 minutes
  4. D. Unlimited
Answer: B. 45-60 seconds per task

Q17. Using specific details from the lecture:

  1. A. Weakens the answer
  2. B. Strengthens the summary and your score
  3. C. Is copying and banned
  4. D. Is unnecessary
Answer: B. Strengthens the summary and your score

Q18. A good conclusion in a 45-second answer:

  1. A. Is impossible
  2. B. Can be one short sentence restating your point
  3. C. Must be long
  4. D. Is never needed
Answer: B. Can be one short sentence restating your point

Q19. Filler words like 'um' used constantly:

  1. A. Improve fluency
  2. B. Can reduce your delivery score if excessive
  3. C. Are required
  4. D. Are a strategy
Answer: B. Can reduce your delivery score if excessive

Q20. Practising with a timer mainly improves:

  1. A. Vocabulary only
  2. B. Pacing and completeness of answers
  3. C. Accent
  4. D. Memory of scripts
Answer: B. Pacing and completeness of answers
