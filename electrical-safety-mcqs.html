Q1. Electricity can kill at voltages as low as:

  1. A. 1000 V
  2. B. 50 V in wet conditions
  3. C. 10,000 V
  4. D. It cannot kill
Answer: B. 50 V in wet conditions

Q2. The main effects of electric shock include:

  1. A. Burns and stopping the heart
  2. B. Hearing loss
  3. C. Back pain
  4. D. Skin rash only
Answer: A. Burns and stopping the heart

Q3. An RCD (residual current device) protects by:

  1. A. Increasing voltage
  2. B. Cutting power quickly when current leaks to earth
  3. C. Making cables stronger
  4. D. Cooling equipment
Answer: B. Cutting power quickly when current leaks to earth

Q4. PAT testing refers to checking:

  1. A. Buildings
  2. B. Portable electrical appliances
  3. C. Vehicles
  4. D. Lifts
Answer: B. Portable electrical appliances

Q5. Before working on electrical equipment, you must:

  1. A. Speed up
  2. B. Isolate it and prove it is dead
  3. C. Wet your hands
  4. D. Remove the fuse box
Answer: B. Isolate it and prove it is dead

Q6. Overloading a socket can cause:

  1. A. Savings
  2. B. Overheating and fire
  3. C. Better performance
  4. D. Nothing
Answer: B. Overheating and fire

Q7. Only competent electricians should:

  1. A. Use a kettle
  2. B. Carry out electrical installation and repairs
  3. C. Switch lights on
  4. D. Plug in a charger
Answer: B. Carry out electrical installation and repairs

Q8. Damaged cables should be:

  1. A. Taped and used
  2. B. Removed from service immediately
  3. C. Painted
  4. D. Used outdoors only
Answer: B. Removed from service immediately

Q9. In wet conditions, electrical risk is:

  1. A. Lower
  2. B. Higher because water conducts electricity
  3. C. The same
  4. D. Zero
Answer: B. Higher because water conducts electricity

Q10. A fuse protects a circuit by:

  1. A. Melting and breaking the circuit when current is too high
  2. B. Increasing current
  3. C. Storing electricity
  4. D. Making light
Answer: A. Melting and breaking the circuit when current is too high
