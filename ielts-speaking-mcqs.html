Q1. How many parts are in the IELTS Speaking test?

  1. A. Two
  2. B. Three
  3. C. Four
  4. D. Five
Answer: B. Three

Q2. In Part 2 (the long turn), you speak for:

  1. A. 30 seconds
  2. B. 1 to 2 minutes
  3. C. 5 minutes
  4. D. 10 minutes
Answer: B. 1 to 2 minutes

Q3. Before the Part 2 long turn, you get preparation time of:

  1. A. No time
  2. B. 1 minute
  3. C. 5 minutes
  4. D. 10 minutes
Answer: B. 1 minute

Q4. Part 1 questions are about:

  1. A. Advanced science
  2. B. Familiar topics like home and family
  3. C. Politics only
  4. D. Business law
Answer: B. Familiar topics like home and family

Q5. Fluency means:

  1. A. Speaking very fast
  2. B. Speaking smoothly without long pauses
  3. C. Using only long words
  4. D. Speaking loudly
Answer: B. Speaking smoothly without long pauses

Q6. In Speaking, grammatical range means:

  1. A. Using one tense only
  2. B. Using a variety of sentence structures
  3. C. Avoiding all errors
  4. D. Speaking in questions
Answer: B. Using a variety of sentence structures

Q7. The Speaking test lasts about:

  1. A. 5 minutes
  2. B. 11 to 14 minutes
  3. C. 30 minutes
  4. D. 45 minutes
Answer: B. 11 to 14 minutes

Q8. If you do not understand a question, you should:

  1. A. Stay silent
  2. B. Politely ask the examiner to repeat it
  3. C. Answer a different question
  4. D. Leave the room
Answer: B. Politely ask the examiner to repeat it

Q9. Part 3 questions are:

  1. A. Yes/no only
  2. B. More abstract discussion linked to Part 2
  3. C. Spelling tests
  4. D. Reading aloud
Answer: B. More abstract discussion linked to Part 2

Q10. Your accent in IELTS Speaking:

  1. A. Must be British
  2. B. Must be American
  3. C. Does not matter if you are clear
  4. D. Gives extra marks
Answer: C. Does not matter if you are clear

Q11. Part 1 lasts about:

  1. A. 1 minute
  2. B. 4-5 minutes
  3. C. 15 minutes
  4. D. 30 minutes
Answer: B. 4-5 minutes

Q12. A good answer to 'Where are you from?' includes:

  1. A. Only the city name
  2. B. The city plus a short interesting detail
  3. C. Your address
  4. D. A map
Answer: B. The city plus a short interesting detail

Q13. Using linking words like 'because', 'so' and 'actually':

  1. A. Is wrong
  2. B. Makes answers more natural and developed
  3. C. Is only for writing
  4. D. Confuses examiners
Answer: B. Makes answers more natural and developed

Q14. If asked about a topic you know nothing about, you should:

  1. A. Say nothing
  2. B. Be honest and still try to say something about it
  3. C. Leave
  4. D. Cry
Answer: B. Be honest and still try to say something about it

Q15. Part 2 topics are given on:

  1. A. A screen only
  2. B. A card with the topic and points to include
  3. C. The examiner's phone
  4. D. A book
Answer: B. A card with the topic and points to include

Q16. Speaking for the full 2 minutes in Part 2:

  1. A. Is impossible
  2. B. Shows good fluency if content stays relevant
  3. C. Is punished
  4. D. Is not allowed
Answer: B. Shows good fluency if content stays relevant

Q17. In Part 3, giving an example from society or news:

  1. A. Is off-topic
  2. B. Strengthens your answer
  3. C. Is forbidden
  4. D. Wastes time
Answer: B. Strengthens your answer

Q18. Your grammar score improves when you use:

  1. A. Only present simple
  2. B. A mix of tenses and sentence types accurately
  3. C. No verbs
  4. D. Only questions
Answer: B. A mix of tenses and sentence types accurately

Q19. A natural way to buy thinking time is:

  1. A. Silence
  2. B. 'That's an interesting question...'
  3. C. Repeating the question loudly
  4. D. Asking for another question
Answer: B. 'That's an interesting question...'

Q20. Describing your job or studies is a common:

  1. A. Part 3 topic
  2. B. Part 1 topic
  3. C. Writing topic
  4. D. Reading topic
Answer: B. Part 1 topic

Q21. Pronunciation features assessed include:

  1. A. Only accent
  2. B. Individual sounds, word stress and intonation
  3. C. Volume only
  4. D. Speed only
Answer: B. Individual sounds, word stress and intonation

Q22. If you make a small mistake while speaking:

  1. A. Stop the test
  2. B. Correct yourself briefly or continue — do not panic
  3. C. Start the answer again
  4. D. Apologise many times
Answer: B. Correct yourself briefly or continue — do not panic

Q23. Part 3 questions may ask you to:

  1. A. Spell words
  2. B. Predict the future and compare with the past
  3. C. Read aloud
  4. D. Write an essay
Answer: B. Predict the future and compare with the past

Q24. A strong vocabulary answer uses topic words, for example for environment:

  1. A. 'Thing' and 'stuff'
  2. B. 'Pollution', 'emissions' and 'sustainable'
  3. C. Only slang
  4. D. Only single words
Answer: B. 'Pollution', 'emissions' and 'sustainable'

Q25. Eye contact and a friendly manner:

  1. A. Give extra marks directly
  2. B. Help create a natural conversation atmosphere
  3. C. Are scored as grammar
  4. D. Are forbidden
Answer: B. Help create a natural conversation atmosphere
