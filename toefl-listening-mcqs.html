Q1. TOEFL Listening includes conversations and:

  1. A. Songs
  2. B. Academic lectures
  3. C. Advertisements
  4. D. Interviews with actors
Answer: B. Academic lectures

Q2. During TOEFL Listening you may:

  1. A. Replay the audio
  2. B. Take notes
  3. C. Use a dictionary
  4. D. Ask the teacher
Answer: B. Take notes

Q3. Questions are answered:

  1. A. While listening only
  2. B. After the audio, using your notes
  3. C. Before the audio
  4. D. The next day
Answer: B. After the audio, using your notes

Q4. A 'function question' asks:

  1. A. For a date
  2. B. Why the speaker says something
  3. C. For spelling
  4. D. For a summary
Answer: B. Why the speaker says something

Q5. Attitude questions test your understanding of:

  1. A. Facts only
  2. B. The speaker's opinion or feeling
  3. C. Grammar rules
  4. D. Vocabulary lists
Answer: B. The speaker's opinion or feeling

Q6. In lectures, the professor's examples usually:

  1. A. Are unimportant
  2. B. Support the main point
  3. C. Change the topic
  4. D. End the lecture
Answer: B. Support the main point

Q7. You should listen mainly for:

  1. A. Every single word
  2. B. Main ideas and how they connect
  3. C. Only numbers
  4. D. Only the first sentence
Answer: B. Main ideas and how they connect

Q8. If you miss a word while listening, you should:

  1. A. Stop and panic
  2. B. Keep listening for the overall meaning
  3. C. Go back
  4. D. Skip all questions
Answer: B. Keep listening for the overall meaning

Q9. TOEFL Listening answers are chosen:

  1. A. By writing sentences
  2. B. From multiple-choice options
  3. C. By speaking
  4. D. By drawing
Answer: B. From multiple-choice options

Q10. Note-taking in TOEFL Listening should be:

  1. A. Full sentences
  2. B. Short symbols and key words
  3. C. In your first language only
  4. D. Avoided completely
Answer: B. Short symbols and key words

Q11. Which strategy is recommended for organizing your notepaper during the TOEFL Listening section?

  1. A. Writing everything in one continuous block of text
  2. B. Dividing your page into halves for each speaker
  3. C. Only taking notes during the last ten seconds
  4. D. Using a foreign language for shorthand
Answer: B. Dividing your page into halves for each speaker

Q12. What does a function question typically ask you to identify?

  1. A. The grammatical tense used in a sentence
  2. B. Why a speaker mentions or discusses a specific point
  3. C. The spelling of a technical term
  4. D. How many paragraphs are in the transcript
Answer: B. Why a speaker mentions or discusses a specific point

Q13. What is a key purpose of a gist-purpose question during a campus conversation?

  1. A. To find out why a student visited a professor or staff member
  2. B. To evaluate the campus architecture
  3. C. To review the history of the university
  4. D. To determine the student's home address
Answer: A. To find out why a student visited a professor or staff member

Q14. What do inference questions in TOEFL Listening measure your ability to do?

  1. A. Read out loud with correct intonation
  2. B. Draw conclusions from information that is implied rather than directly stated
  3. C. Memorize the exact dialogue word for word
  4. D. Identify the dictionary definition of difficult nouns
Answer: B. Draw conclusions from information that is implied rather than directly stated

Q15. How should you handle detail questions regarding facts mentioned in the audio?

  1. A. Focus only on minor, passing comments
  2. B. Look for explicitly stated and important ideas
  3. C. Guess based on your personal opinions
  4. D. Ignore all facts and focus solely on tone
Answer: B. Look for explicitly stated and important ideas

Q16. What is the primary focus of a gist-content question in TOEFL Listening?

  1. A. To identify the main topic of the discussion
  2. B. To check specific dates and numbers
  3. C. To translate vocabulary words
  4. D. To grade the speaker's pronunciation
Answer: A. To identify the main topic of the discussion

Q17. What is the primary objective of a pragmatic understanding question in TOEFL Listening?

  1. A. To identify the literal definition of an idiom
  2. B. To determine the speaker's true intent or implied meaning
  3. C. To recall specific statistical data mentioned
  4. D. To outline the main topic of the entire lecture
Answer: B. To determine the speaker's true intent or implied meaning

Q18. Which note-taking technique is most effective for tracking shifting perspectives in a multi-speaker TOEFL academic discussion?

  1. A. Writing down every single word verbatim
  2. B. Using distinct columns or symbols for each speaker's viewpoint
  3. C. Ignoring the dialogue and focusing solely on the conclusion
  4. D. Translating the spoken English into your native language
Answer: B. Using distinct columns or symbols for each speaker's viewpoint

Q19. How are re-listening questions typically formatted in the TOEFL Listening section?

  1. A. You must read a new passage from a textbook
  2. B. A short segment of the audio is replayed for analysis
  3. C. You listen to a completely different lecture track
  4. D. The proctor reads a sentence aloud to you
Answer: B. A short segment of the audio is replayed for analysis

Q20. What indicates that a professor is introducing a major conceptual shift during a TOEFL lecture?

  1. A. A sudden drop in the speaker's speaking volume
  2. B. Transition markers like 'however', 'conversely', or 'let's look at it differently'
  3. C. The immediate termination of the lecture recording
  4. D. An increase in background classroom noise
Answer: B. Transition markers like 'however', 'conversely', or 'let's look at it differently'

Q21. What should you focus on when a TOEFL listening passage includes an extended biographical anecdote?

  1. A. Memorizing the exact birth and death dates of the historical figure
  2. B. Understanding how the anecdote illustrates the overarching academic theory
  3. C. Transcribing every proper noun spelled out phonetically
  4. D. Discarding the story entirely as non-testable information
Answer: B. Understanding how the anecdote illustrates the overarching academic theory

Q22. Which factor is evaluated by organization questions in TOEFL Listening lectures?

  1. A. Your physical posture while taking the exam
  2. B. How the lecture's main points and sub-topics are structured and related
  3. C. The alphabetical ordering of vocabulary words
  4. D. Your ability to arrange scrambled sentence strips on screen
Answer: B. How the lecture's main points and sub-topics are structured and related

Q23. What do inference questions primarily measure in TOEFL listening tasks?

  1. A. The exact memorization of spoken vocabulary
  2. B. The ability to draw logical conclusions from implied meanings
  3. C. The mathematical calculation of lecture duration
  4. D. The physical speed of the speaker's voice
Answer: B. The ability to draw logical conclusions from implied meanings

Q24. When addressing detail-type questions in the listening section, what level of information is required?

  1. A. Minor and trivial facts
  2. B. Explicitly stated major facts or ideas
  3. C. Unspoken personal opinions
  4. D. Historical background of the author
Answer: B. Explicitly stated major facts or ideas

Q25. What is evaluated by organization questions in the TOEFL listening test?

  1. A. How the speaker structures and relates information throughout the passage
  2. B. The neatness of the student's personal handwriting
  3. C. The physical layout of the testing room
  4. D. How quickly the student finishes the entire test
Answer: A. How the speaker structures and relates information throughout the passage
