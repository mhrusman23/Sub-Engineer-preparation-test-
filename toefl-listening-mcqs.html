TOEFL Listening MCQs (IELTS & English Tests)

Free MCQs with answers for TOEFL Listening. 20 questions in 2 chapter tests are in the app; all are shown here.

Open full test in the app

Q1. TOEFL Listening includes conversations and:

  1. A. Songs
  2. B. Academic lectures
  3. C. Advertisements
  4. D. Interviews with actors
Answer: B. Academic lectures

Q2. During TOEFL Listening you may:

  1. A. Replay the audio
  2. B. Take notes
  3. C. Use a dictionary
  4. D. Ask the teacher
Answer: B. Take notes

Q3. Questions are answered:

  1. A. While listening only
  2. B. After the audio, using your notes
  3. C. Before the audio
  4. D. The next day
Answer: B. After the audio, using your notes

Q4. A 'function question' asks:

  1. A. For a date
  2. B. Why the speaker says something
  3. C. For spelling
  4. D. For a summary
Answer: B. Why the speaker says something

Q5. Attitude questions test your understanding of:

  1. A. Facts only
  2. B. The speaker's opinion or feeling
  3. C. Grammar rules
  4. D. Vocabulary lists
Answer: B. The speaker's opinion or feeling

Q6. In lectures, the professor's examples usually:

  1. A. Are unimportant
  2. B. Support the main point
  3. C. Change the topic
  4. D. End the lecture
Answer: B. Support the main point

Q7. You should listen mainly for:

  1. A. Every single word
  2. B. Main ideas and how they connect
  3. C. Only numbers
  4. D. Only the first sentence
Answer: B. Main ideas and how they connect

Q8. If you miss a word while listening, you should:

  1. A. Stop and panic
  2. B. Keep listening for the overall meaning
  3. C. Go back
  4. D. Skip all questions
Answer: B. Keep listening for the overall meaning

Q9. TOEFL Listening answers are chosen:

  1. A. By writing sentences
  2. B. From multiple-choice options
  3. C. By speaking
  4. D. By drawing
Answer: B. From multiple-choice options

Q10. Note-taking in TOEFL Listening should be:

  1. A. Full sentences
  2. B. Short symbols and key words
  3. C. In your first language only
  4. D. Avoided completely
Answer: B. Short symbols and key words

Q11. Lecture questions often follow:

  1. A. Random order
  2. B. The order ideas are presented
  3. C. Alphabetical order
  4. D. The options order
Answer: B. The order ideas are presented

Q12. When a professor says 'Now, this is important', you should:

  1. A. Relax
  2. B. Pay special attention — a question often follows
  3. C. Stop taking notes
  4. D. Skip ahead
Answer: B. Pay special attention — a question often follows

Q13. Conversation questions often test:

  1. A. Dates
  2. B. The student's problem and the solution offered
  3. C. Spelling
  4. D. The professor's name
Answer: B. The student's problem and the solution offered

Q14. Replay questions ('What does the professor mean when he says...?') test:

  1. A. Memory of facts
  2. B. Meaning in context, including tone and idiom
  3. C. Grammar
  4. D. Numbers
Answer: B. Meaning in context, including tone and idiom

Q15. Idioms like 'miss the boat' mean:

  1. A. Take a ship
  2. B. Miss an opportunity
  3. C. Be late for class
  4. D. Go fishing
Answer: B. Miss an opportunity

Q16. Taking notes on examples is useful because:

  1. A. Examples are never tested
  2. B. Questions often ask why an example was given
  3. C. Notes are collected
  4. D. It wastes time
Answer: B. Questions often ask why an example was given

Q17. If a speaker changes their mind, the final position is:

  1. A. The first thing said
  2. B. What they finally decide/say
  3. C. Unimportant
  4. D. Always negative
Answer: B. What they finally decide/say

Q18. Organisational phrases ('There are two reasons...') help you:

  1. A. Sleep
  2. B. Structure your notes around main points
  3. C. Skip the lecture
  4. D. Choose option A
Answer: B. Structure your notes around main points

Q19. A question about attitude ('How does the student feel?') is answered from:

  1. A. Facts
  2. B. Tone of voice and word choice
  3. C. The transcript title
  4. D. The date
Answer: B. Tone of voice and word choice

Q20. You cannot replay the whole lecture, so during listening you must:

  1. A. Write everything
  2. B. Capture main points and key details in notes
  3. C. Memorise
  4. D. Read questions only
Answer: B. Capture main points and key details in notes
