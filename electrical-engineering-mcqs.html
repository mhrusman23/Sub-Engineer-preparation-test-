Q1. Which instrument is primarily used for the measurement of high resistance such as insulation resistance?

  1. A. Kelvin Bridge
  2. B. Megger
  3. C. Wheatstone Bridge
  4. D. Potentiometer
Answer: B. Megger

Q2. What type of damping force is commonly employed in permanent magnet moving coil (PMMC) instruments?

  1. A. Fluid friction damping
  2. B. Air friction damping
  3. C. Eddy current damping
  4. D. Gravity control damping
Answer: C. Eddy current damping

Q3. Which bridge circuit is most suitable for the precise measurement of medium resistance?

  1. A. Wheatstone Bridge
  2. B. Kelvin Double Bridge
  3. C. Schering Bridge
  4. D. Hay's Bridge
Answer: A. Wheatstone Bridge

Q4. What is the primary function of a Current Transformer (CT) in an AC measurement system?

  1. A. Step up high current for meters
  2. B. Step down high current for safe meter operation
  3. C. Measure high voltage directly
  4. D. Convert AC to DC
Answer: B. Step down high current for safe meter operation

Q5. Which instrument can be used to measure both AC and DC quantities with the same accuracy?

  1. A. PMMC instrument
  2. B. Moving Iron instrument
  3. C. Induction type instrument
  4. D. Electrostatic voltmeter
Answer: B. Moving Iron instrument

Q6. In an energy meter, what causes the creeping error, which is the slow continuous rotation of the disc with no load?

  1. A. Overload current
  2. B. Friction compensation and stray magnetic fields
  3. C. Low frequency
  4. D. High ambient temperature
Answer: B. Friction compensation and stray magnetic fields

Q7. Which instrument uses electrostatic forces for the measurement of voltage?

  1. A. Dynamometer type instrument
  2. B. Electrostatic voltmeter
  3. C. Hot wire instrument
  4. D. Moving iron instrument
Answer: B. Electrostatic voltmeter

Q8. What is the main purpose of shunts used with ammeters?

  1. A. To increase the voltage range
  2. B. To bypass the major portion of current around the meter movement
  3. C. To provide temperature compensation
  4. D. To convert AC to DC
Answer: B. To bypass the major portion of current around the meter movement

Q9. Which bridge is specifically used for the measurement of unknown inductance?

  1. A. Wien Bridge
  2. B. Maxwell's Inductance Bridge
  3. C. Schering Bridge
  4. D. De Sauty Bridge
Answer: B. Maxwell's Inductance Bridge

Q10. What type of control torque is usually provided in gravity-controlled measuring instruments?

  1. A. Hairspring mechanism
  2. B. Small adjustable weight attached to the moving system
  3. C. Torsion fiber
  4. D. Permanent magnet
Answer: B. Small adjustable weight attached to the moving system

Q11. Which instrument is universally classified as a secondary instrument?

  1. A. Tangent galvanometer
  2. B. PMMC ammeter
  3. C. Rayleigh current balance
  4. D. Standard current balance
Answer: B. PMMC ammeter

Q12. What is the primary function of series multipliers used with voltmeters?

  1. A. To decrease the range
  2. B. To increase the voltage range
  3. C. To measure alternating current
  4. D. To eliminate temperature errors
Answer: B. To increase the voltage range

Q13. Which type of damping is most effective in reducing oscillations of a moving‑coil galvanometer when it is used as a secondary instrument?

  1. A. Mechanical friction damping
  2. B. Air resistance damping
  3. C. Eddy current damping
  4. D. Gravity damping
Answer: C. Eddy current damping

Q14. Which instrument is best suited for measuring small AC currents without affecting the circuit being tested?

  1. A. Shunt resistor
  2. B. Current transformer (CT)
  3. C. Moving‑coil ammeter
  4. D. Voltmeter
Answer: B. Current transformer (CT)

Q15. In a standard moving‑coil ammeter, the coil is suspended by a magnetic field. What is the primary purpose of the magnetic field?

  1. A. To provide a reference voltage
  2. B. To generate a torque proportional to current
  3. C. To cool the coil
  4. D. To measure temperature
Answer: B. To generate a torque proportional to current

Q16. What is the main source of the creeping error observed in an electromechanical energy meter?

  1. A. High ambient temperature
  2. B. Stray magnetic fields and friction compensation
  3. C. Overload current
  4. D. Low frequency operation
Answer: B. Stray magnetic fields and friction compensation

Q17. Which bridge circuit is standard for the precise measurement of unknown capacitance and dielectric loss angle?

  1. A. Hay's Bridge
  2. B. Anderson Bridge
  3. C. Schering Bridge
  4. D. Wien's Bridge
Answer: C. Schering Bridge

Q18. What is the primary function of a potential transformer (PT) in electrical power systems?

  1. A. To step up high voltages for transmission line efficiency
  2. B. To step down high voltages to safe, measurable values for instruments
  3. C. To isolate heavy current loads from electronic protection relays
  4. D. To convert alternating voltage directly into direct voltage
Answer: B. To step down high voltages to safe, measurable values for instruments

Q19. Which motor is most commonly employed for driving domestic ceiling fans?

  1. A. Three-phase induction motor
  2. B. Permanent split capacitor motor
  3. C. Universal motor
  4. D. Repulsion motor
Answer: B. Permanent split capacitor motor

Q20. What is the primary purpose of laminating the core of a power transformer?

  1. A. To increase the magnetic flux density
  2. B. To reduce hysteresis losses
  3. C. To reduce eddy current losses
  4. D. To improve mechanical strength
Answer: C. To reduce eddy current losses

Q21. What is the main function of the Buchholz relay in a power transformer protection scheme?

  1. A. Protection against external lightning surges
  2. B. Protection against internal faults using gas accumulation
  3. C. Protection against overvoltage conditions
  4. D. Protection against external short circuits
Answer: B. Protection against internal faults using gas accumulation

Q22. In a three-phase balanced star-connected system, what is the mathematical relationship between line current and phase current?

  1. A. Line current is equal to phase current
  2. B. Line current is sqrt(3) times phase current
  3. C. Phase current is sqrt(3) times line current
  4. D. Line current is three times phase current
Answer: A. Line current is equal to phase current

Q23. What is the primary function of a conservator tank in a large oil-immersed power transformer?

  1. A. To cool the transformer oil during heavy loads
  2. B. To provide space for the expansion and contraction of transformer oil
  3. C. To house the tap changer mechanism safely
  4. D. To store spare insulating oil for emergencies
Answer: B. To provide space for the expansion and contraction of transformer oil

Q24. Which type of DC generator is characterized by having its field winding connected in parallel with the armature?

  1. A. Series generator
  2. B. Shunt generator
  3. C. Compound generator
  4. D. Separately excited generator
Answer: B. Shunt generator

Q25. What type of control torque arrangement is typically utilized in portable laboratory electrical measuring instruments?

  1. A. Gravity control mechanism
  2. B. Spring control mechanism using phosphor-bronze springs
  3. C. Fluid friction control system
  4. D. Hydraulic damping control
Answer: B. Spring control mechanism using phosphor-bronze springs
