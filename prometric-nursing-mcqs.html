Q1. The nurse's first priority in an emergency is to assess:

  1. A. The bill
  2. B. Airway, breathing and circulation (ABC)
  3. C. The family history
  4. D. The medication list
Answer: B. Airway, breathing and circulation (ABC)

Q2. A blood pressure of 180/110 mmHg with headache and blurred vision suggests:

  1. A. Normal variation
  2. B. Hypertensive crisis
  3. C. Low blood sugar
  4. D. Dehydration
Answer: B. Hypertensive crisis

Q3. When giving an IM injection to an adult, a common site is the:

  1. A. Forearm
  2. B. Deltoid or ventrogluteal muscle
  3. C. Abdomen
  4. D. Calf
Answer: B. Deltoid or ventrogluteal muscle

Q4. The correct angle for a subcutaneous injection is:

  1. A. 90 degrees always
  2. B. 45 degrees (or 90 with a short needle)
  3. C. 10 degrees
  4. D. 180 degrees
Answer: B. 45 degrees (or 90 with a short needle)

Q5. A patient complains of chest pain. The nurse's first action is to:

  1. A. Give food
  2. B. Stop activity, sit the patient down and assess — treat as an emergency
  3. C. Ignore it
  4. D. Give a sedative
Answer: B. Stop activity, sit the patient down and assess — treat as an emergency

Q6. Insulin syringes are measured in:

  1. A. Millilitres only
  2. B. Units
  3. C. Milligrams
  4. D. Drops
Answer: B. Units

Q7. The purpose of the surgical safety checklist is to:

  1. A. Save paper
  2. B. Reduce errors and improve team communication
  3. C. Speed up discharge
  4. D. Train students
Answer: B. Reduce errors and improve team communication

Q8. A patient with a nasogastric tube — before feeding, the nurse must:

  1. A. Nothing
  2. B. Confirm tube placement
  3. C. Remove the tube
  4. D. Give a test meal
Answer: B. Confirm tube placement

Q9. Falls prevention in hospital includes:

  1. A. Keeping the bed high
  2. B. Call bell within reach and non-slip footwear
  3. C. Dim lights at all times
  4. D. Locking the room
Answer: B. Call bell within reach and non-slip footwear

Q10. The most accurate way to confirm a patient's identity before a procedure is to:

  1. A. Ask their room number
  2. B. Check the wristband and ask name/date of birth
  3. C. Ask the visitor
  4. D. Look at the bed chart only
Answer: B. Check the wristband and ask name/date of birth
