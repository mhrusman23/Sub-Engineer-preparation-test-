Q1. The nurse's first priority in an emergency is to assess:

  1. A. The bill
  2. B. Airway, breathing and circulation (ABC)
  3. C. The family history
  4. D. The medication list
Answer: B. Airway, breathing and circulation (ABC)

Q2. A blood pressure of 180/110 mmHg with headache and blurred vision suggests:

  1. A. Normal variation
  2. B. Hypertensive crisis
  3. C. Low blood sugar
  4. D. Dehydration
Answer: B. Hypertensive crisis

Q3. When giving an IM injection to an adult, a common site is the:

  1. A. Forearm
  2. B. Deltoid or ventrogluteal muscle
  3. C. Abdomen
  4. D. Calf
Answer: B. Deltoid or ventrogluteal muscle

Q4. The correct angle for a subcutaneous injection is:

  1. A. 90 degrees always
  2. B. 45 degrees (or 90 with a short needle)
  3. C. 10 degrees
  4. D. 180 degrees
Answer: B. 45 degrees (or 90 with a short needle)

Q5. A patient complains of chest pain. The nurse's first action is to:

  1. A. Give food
  2. B. Stop activity, sit the patient down and assess — treat as an emergency
  3. C. Ignore it
  4. D. Give a sedative
Answer: B. Stop activity, sit the patient down and assess — treat as an emergency

Q6. Insulin syringes are measured in:

  1. A. Millilitres only
  2. B. Units
  3. C. Milligrams
  4. D. Drops
Answer: B. Units

Q7. The purpose of the surgical safety checklist is to:

  1. A. Save paper
  2. B. Reduce errors and improve team communication
  3. C. Speed up discharge
  4. D. Train students
Answer: B. Reduce errors and improve team communication

Q8. A patient with a nasogastric tube — before feeding, the nurse must:

  1. A. Nothing
  2. B. Confirm tube placement
  3. C. Remove the tube
  4. D. Give a test meal
Answer: B. Confirm tube placement

Q9. Falls prevention in hospital includes:

  1. A. Keeping the bed high
  2. B. Call bell within reach and non-slip footwear
  3. C. Dim lights at all times
  4. D. Locking the room
Answer: B. Call bell within reach and non-slip footwear

Q10. The most accurate way to confirm a patient's identity before a procedure is to:

  1. A. Ask their room number
  2. B. Check the wristband and ask name/date of birth
  3. C. Ask the visitor
  4. D. Look at the bed chart only
Answer: B. Check the wristband and ask name/date of birth

Q11. When a client arrives at the emergency unit presenting with a severely elevated blood pressure of 180/110 mmHg, accompanied by a severe headache and blurred vision, what should be the nurse's immediate priority?

  1. A. Discharge the patient with routine lifestyle advice
  2. B. Conduct a rapid comprehensive baseline assessment and report findings urgently
  3. C. Administer oral vitamins immediately
  4. D. Wait for routine morning lab results
Answer: B. Conduct a rapid comprehensive baseline assessment and report findings urgently

Q12. What is the primary objective of utilizing standardized clinical skills checklists during nursing practice evaluations?

  1. A. To replace clinical judgment entirely with written rules
  2. B. To ensure consistent assessment and identify specific areas requiring further review or training
  3. C. To accelerate the hospital discharge process for surgical patients
  4. D. To reduce administrative paperwork for hospital management
Answer: B. To ensure consistent assessment and identify specific areas requiring further review or training

Q13. Which anatomical site is most frequently selected for performing an intramuscular injection in an adult client?

  1. A. Ventrogluteal site
  2. B. Inner forearm
  3. C. Dorsal surface of the hand
  4. D. Abdominal periumbilical area
Answer: A. Ventrogluteal site

Q14. Which action represents the most effective immediate nursing response for a post-operative client experiencing intense pain despite recent analgesic administration?

  1. A. Instantly double the prescribed medication dosage
  2. B. Re-evaluate the patient's pain score and notify the attending physician
  3. C. Document that the prescribed analgesics are entirely ineffective
  4. D. Discontinue all current pain management protocols
Answer: B. Re-evaluate the patient's pain score and notify the attending physician

Q15. During the preparation for a subcutaneous medication injection in an adult patient, what is the standard recommended needle insertion angle?

  1. A. 15 degrees
  2. B. 45 or 90 degrees
  3. C. 180 degrees
  4. D. 30 degrees
Answer: B. 45 or 90 degrees

Q16. When assessing a client who has sustained a severe burn on the anterior trunk and both arms, which immediate physiological complication is the highest priority for the nurse?

  1. A. Hypovolemic shock due to fluid shift
  2. B. Hyperglycemia from acute stress response
  3. C. Neurological deficit from localized pain
  4. D. Impaired joint mobility from scar tissue
Answer: A. Hypovolemic shock due to fluid shift

Q17. A nurse is caring for an adult patient receiving continuous enteral nutrition via a gastrostomy tube. To significantly reduce the risk of aspiration, what position should the patient's bed be maintained in?

  1. A. Flat supine position
  2. B. Trendelenburg position
  3. C. Head of bed elevated 30 to 45 degrees
  4. D. Left lateral recumbent position
Answer: C. Head of bed elevated 30 to 45 degrees

Q18. A patient diagnosed with severe dehydration is prescribed an intravenous infusion of normal saline. Which clinical indicator should the nurse monitor most closely to evaluate the effectiveness of this fluid therapy?

  1. A. Hourly urine output
  2. B. Peripheral body temperature
  3. C. Skin pigmentation changes
  4. D. Bowel sound frequency
Answer: A. Hourly urine output

Q19. A client is prescribed standard droplet precautions in addition to routine hospital protocols. Which medical condition requires this specific level of infection control?

  1. A. Pulmonary tuberculosis
  2. B. Influenza virus infection
  3. C. Acquired immunodeficiency syndrome
  4. D. Methicillin-resistant Staphylococcus aureus
Answer: B. Influenza virus infection

Q20. Before administering a scheduled dose of digoxin to a patient in a cardiac care unit, which assessment must the nurse perform?

  1. A. Measure the patient's abdominal girth
  2. B. Auscultate the apical pulse for one full minute
  3. C. Check the range of motion in the upper extremities
  4. D. Assess the patient's visual acuity
Answer: B. Auscultate the apical pulse for one full minute

Q21. When performing routine wound care for a patient with a draining surgical incision, which technique ensures a proper sterile field setup?

  1. A. Opening sterile packages by pulling the wrapper toward the body
  2. B. Reaching across the established sterile field to adjust supplies
  3. C. Adding sterile items while holding them above and away from the field
  4. D. Touching the outer one-inch border of the sterile drape with bare hands
Answer: C. Adding sterile items while holding them above and away from the field

Q22. A patient with a nasogastric tube is about to receive a feeding. Before starting the feed, the nurse should:

  1. A. Check the tube’s placement by aspirating gastric contents
  2. B. Flush the tube with 100 mL of water
  3. C. Ask the patient to swallow before feeding
  4. D. Administer a pro‑kinetic agent
Answer: A. Check the tube’s placement by aspirating gastric contents

Q23. A 65‑year‑old patient with chronic kidney disease is prescribed a 10‑mg dose of furosemide. What is the most appropriate nursing action?

  1. A. Give the medication with a full glass of water
  2. B. Administer it before meals to avoid nausea
  3. C. Check serum potassium before each dose
  4. D. Hold the dose if the patient has a fever
Answer: C. Check serum potassium before each dose

Q24. During a routine assessment, a nurse notes a patient’s temperature is 38.9°C (102°F). What is the most appropriate immediate action?

  1. A. Administer a non‑steroidal anti‑inflammatory drug
  2. B. Notify the physician and prepare for a fever work‑up
  3. C. Give the patient a cold compress
  4. D. Order a blood culture
Answer: B. Notify the physician and prepare for a fever work‑up

Q25. Which action should a nurse take first when a patient reports sudden shortness of breath and wheezing?

  1. A. Administer a bronchodilator via nebulizer
  2. B. Ask the patient to sit upright and assess breathing
  3. C. Give the patient a glucose drink
  4. D. Start a routine vital sign check
Answer: B. Ask the patient to sit upright and assess breathing
