Q1. Thickness of road adopted by Romans.

  1. A. 30-60 cm
  2. B. 15-20 cm
  3. C. 70-120 cm
  4. D. 50-80 cm
Answer: C. 70-120 cm

Q2. Establishing the centreline of the proposed road in plan is called.

  1. A. Road survey
  2. B. Road centring
  3. C. Road alignment
  4. D. All of these
Answer: D. All of these

Q3. Transverse slope given to the road surface is termed as,

  1. A. Side slope
  2. B. Country slope
  3. C. Camber
  4. D. Gradient
Answer: C. Camber

Q4. An important and special road of superior type is termed as:

  1. A. Super highway
  2. B. Highway
  3. C. Express highway
  4. D. All of these
Answer: D. All of these

Q5. A low cost road in which the road metal is consolidated with the help of water and earthen material is termed as:

  1. A. Dressing
  2. B. Macadam
  3. C. Sheet asphalt
  4. D. Water bound macadam
Answer: D. Water bound macadam

Q6. It helps to drain off the rain water from road surface.

  1. A. Sub grade
  2. B. Base
  3. C. Side drain
  4. D. Camber
Answer: D. Camber

Q7. An accident in which persons are injured.

  1. A. human accident
  2. B. carriage accident
  3. C. Plant accident
  4. D. fire accident
Answer: A. human accident

Q8. Central island is also called

  1. A. Traffic island
  2. B. Refuge island
  3. C. Guide island
  4. D. Pedestrians island
Answer: A. Traffic island

Q9. Metro Bus system is suitable for such regions.

  1. A. Villages
  2. B. Urban
  3. C. Towns
  4. D. Rural
Answer: B. Urban

Q10. Broken surface of road roughly circular in plan is termed as:

  1. A. Crazing
  2. B. Pot hole
  3. C. Rut
  4. D. Creep
Answer: B. Pot hole

Q11. A road roller having two plane wheels is called as:

  1. A. Tandem roller
  2. B. Sheep foot roller
  3. C. Plane roller
  4. D. Vibrating roller
Answer: A. Tandem roller

Q12. A large shed at an airport that is used to house and repair of aircrafts is termed as:

  1. A. Aeroplane shed
  2. B. Garage
  3. C. Hangar
  4. D. Apron
Answer: C. Hangar

Q13. As a public transport railway was used first time in the world in,

  1. A. 1935
  2. B. 1765
  3. C. 1906
  4. D. 1825
Answer: D. 1825

Q14. Flat footed rails are fixed over sleepers placing ________ in between.

  1. A. Chairs
  2. B. Fish plates
  3. C. Tongue rail
  4. D. Bearing plates
Answer: D. Bearing plates

Q15. Longitudinal extension of rails is known as,

  1. A. Adzing
  2. B. Cantt
  3. C. Wave action
  4. D. Creep
Answer: D. Creep

Q16. Pair of switches is called as,

  1. A. stub
  2. B. stock rail
  3. C. crossing
  4. D. points
Answer: D. points

Q17. The mechanical relation between levers operating the signals and points is called as

  1. A. signalling
  2. B. controlling
  3. C. shunting
  4. D. interlocking
Answer: D. interlocking

Q18. An underground passage which is constructed without disturbing the ground surface.

  1. A. Causeway
  2. B. Tunnel
  3. C. Drill
  4. D. Bridge
Answer: B. Tunnel

Q19. A partly enclosed protected water area to provide safe and suitable accommodation for vessels is called.

  1. A. wharf
  2. B. break water
  3. C. wet dock
  4. D. harbour
Answer: D. harbour

Q20. The technique and operation of removing material like earth or garbage from the sea bed is termed as

  1. A. dredging
  2. B. maintenance
  3. C. improvement
  4. D. sundry
Answer: A. dredging

Q21. For safe and guided travel of ships such facilities are necessary.

  1. A. captain
  2. B. navigation aids
  3. C. air
  4. D. Fuel
Answer: B. navigation aids

Q22. In 3500 B.C paved roads are found at:

  1. A. D.I. Khan
  2. B. Harappa
  3. C. Lahore
  4. D. Taxila
Answer: B. Harappa

Q23. Sharp curves are proposed on the roads for such type of traffic.

  1. A. Slow moving
  2. B. Fast moving
  3. C. Heavy
  4. D. All of these
Answer: A. Slow moving

Q24. Edging of stone or concrete blocks between carriageway & shoulder or foot path.

  1. A. Kerb
  2. B. Berm
  3. C. Crown
  4. D. Edging
Answer: A. Kerb

Q25. A road connecting one town with another town is called.

  1. A. Arterial road
  2. B. District road
  3. C. Country road
  4. D. Urban road
Answer: C. Country road
