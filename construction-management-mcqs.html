Q1. CPM stands for critical path:

  1. A. Measure
  2. B. Method
  3. C. Model
  4. D. Management
Answer: B. Method

Q2. The critical path is the:

  1. A. Easiest path
  2. B. Longest path through the network
  3. C. Shortest path
  4. D. Cheapest path
Answer: B. Longest path through the network

Q3. A Gantt chart shows project activities against:

  1. A. Cost only
  2. B. Quality only
  3. C. Labour age
  4. D. Time
Answer: D. Time

Q4. Float or slack is the time an activity can be delayed without delaying:

  1. A. The bill
  2. B. The weather
  3. C. Only labour
  4. D. The project
Answer: D. The project

Q5. Which contract type fixes the price for the whole work?

  1. A. Lump sum contract
  2. B. Cost plus contract
  3. C. Time and material only
  4. D. Unit rate only
Answer: A. Lump sum contract

Q6. The tender is a:

  1. A. Formal offer to do work at a price
  2. B. Drawing
  3. C. Site visit
  4. D. Final payment
Answer: A. Formal offer to do work at a price

Q7. A site safety plan mainly aims to prevent:

  1. A. Delays in payment
  2. B. Weather changes
  3. C. Accidents and injuries
  4. D. Cost increases
Answer: C. Accidents and injuries

Q8. Quality control during construction ensures:

  1. A. More labour
  2. B. Work meets specifications
  3. C. Slower progress
  4. D. Higher cost
Answer: B. Work meets specifications

Q9. Resource levelling is used to:

  1. A. Hide delays
  2. B. Increase cost
  3. C. Smooth resource demand over time
  4. D. Reduce quality
Answer: C. Smooth resource demand over time

Q10. PERT uses three time estimates: optimistic, most likely and:

  1. A. Actual
  2. B. Standard
  3. C. Pessimistic
  4. D. Average
Answer: C. Pessimistic

Q11. Which construction document officially invites contractors to submit their pricing and project execution proposals?

  1. A. Work order
  2. B. Notice inviting tender
  3. C. Completion certificate
  4. D. Measurement book
Answer: B. Notice inviting tender

Q12. What is the primary purpose of maintaining a Hindrance Register on a construction site?

  1. A. Recording daily weather updates
  2. B. Tracking material delivery weights
  3. C. Documenting delays beyond the contractor's control
  4. D. Calculating weekly worker wages
Answer: C. Documenting delays beyond the contractor's control

Q13. Under which contract format does the owner pay the contractor for actual measured quantities at pre-agreed unit rates?

  1. A. Lump sum contract
  2. B. Cost plus fee contract
  3. C. Item rate contract
  4. D. Turnkey contract
Answer: C. Item rate contract

Q14. Which specific chart illustrates the daily availability, allocation, and requirement of construction equipment over a project timeline?

  1. A. Resource histogram
  2. B. Org chart
  3. C. Network diagram
  4. D. Control chart
Answer: A. Resource histogram

Q15. What does crashing a construction network schedule primarily involve?

  1. A. Extending project duration to save money
  2. B. Reducing duration by adding extra resources
  3. C. Stopping work temporarily due to safety risks
  4. D. Lowering material quality to cut expenses
Answer: B. Reducing duration by adding extra resources

Q16. Which official site document records all day-to-day progress, workforce count, and material receipts?

  1. A. Site order book
  2. B. Measurement book
  3. C. Site daily diary
  4. D. Indentation form
Answer: C. Site daily diary

Q17. In project network analysis, what does the process of crashing a schedule involve?

  1. A. Halting all site work due to weather emergencies
  2. B. Reducing project duration by adding extra resources to critical path activities
  3. C. Lowering the overall project budget by reducing labor wages
  4. D. Extending deadlines to ensure higher construction quality
Answer: B. Reducing project duration by adding extra resources to critical path activities

Q18. What is the primary purpose of analyzing rates during construction project estimation?

  1. A. To determine the actual cost of items on location and optimize resources
  2. B. To schedule the exact retirement dates of field workers
  3. C. To design the architectural floor plans of the structure
  4. D. To secure permanent land ownership documents
Answer: A. To determine the actual cost of items on location and optimize resources

Q19. Which of the following is considered a core managerial function within construction management?

  1. A. Communicating
  2. B. Drafting
  3. C. Surveying
  4. D. Excavating
Answer: A. Communicating

Q20. Which type of organizational structure is most popularly adopted for civil engineering construction projects?

  1. A. Line organization
  2. B. Matrix organization only
  3. C. Virtual organization
  4. D. Circular organization
Answer: A. Line organization

Q21. Which of the following entities is generally NOT classified as a primary stakeholder in a construction project?

  1. A. Project Owner
  2. B. Contractor
  3. C. Consulting Engineer
  4. D. Random Bystander Public
Answer: D. Random Bystander Public

Q22. What is the primary function of a site order book maintained at a construction project location?

  1. A. Recording daily financial expenditures
  2. B. Recording instructions issued by visiting or executive engineers
  3. C. Tracking inventory levels of cement and steel
  4. D. Logging labor attendance and working hours
Answer: B. Recording instructions issued by visiting or executive engineers

Q23. Which official site document is maintained primarily to record instructions issued by visiting supervisory engineers?

  1. A. Muster roll
  2. B. Measurement book
  3. C. Site order book
  4. D. Stock register
Answer: C. Site order book

Q24. What is the key characteristic of a 'line organization' structure widely used in civil engineering projects?

  1. A. Direct vertical authority flowing from top management down to workers
  2. B. Complete autonomy given to individual laborers
  3. C. Multiple supervisors giving orders to a single worker
  4. D. Absence of any formal chain of command
Answer: A. Direct vertical authority flowing from top management down to workers

Q25. What is the primary purpose of conducting an analysis of construction rates before project execution?

  1. A. To calculate material quantities and labor strength required
  2. B. To increase the profit margin of subcontractors
  3. C. To delay the procurement of heavy machinery
  4. D. To eliminate the need for project scheduling
Answer: A. To calculate material quantities and labor strength required
