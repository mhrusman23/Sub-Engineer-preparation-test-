Q1. In thinner multi-span slabs, the reinforcement provided to accommodate both negative and positive moments is:

  1. A. Middle-depth reinforcement
  2. B. Top reinforcement
  3. C. Bottom reinforcement
  4. D. Shear reinforcement
Answer: A. Middle-depth reinforcement

Q2. Positive slope downstream is called:

  1. A. Adverse slope
  2. B. Mild slope
  3. C. Critical slope
  4. D. Steep slope
Answer: A. Adverse slope

Q3. Depths corresponding to two specific energies are called:

  1. A. Alternate depths
  2. B. Conjugate depths
  3. C. Sequent depths
  4. D. Normal depths
Answer: A. Alternate depths

Q4. Depths before and after hydraulic jump are called:

  1. A. Conjugate depths
  2. B. Alternate depths
  3. C. Critical depths
  4. D. Normal depths
Answer: A. Conjugate depths

Q5. Graph between discharge and stage is called:

  1. A. Rating curve
  2. B. Hydrograph
  3. C. Hyetograph
  4. D. Mass curve
Answer: A. Rating curve

Q6. Relation between Manning and Chezy coefficient is:

  1. A. C = (1/n) R^(1/6)
  2. B. C = n R^(1/6)
  3. C. C = R/n
  4. D. C = n/R
Answer: C. C = R/n

Q7. Plants are grown on ridges/sides of water; the irrigation method used is:

  1. A. Furrow method
  2. B. Flood method
  3. C. Sprinkler method
  4. D. Drip method
Answer: A. Furrow method

Q8. Less carbon steel means:

  1. A. More ductile
  2. B. More brittle
  3. C. Harder
  4. D. Stronger
Answer: A. More ductile

Q9. Minimum cover in flat slab is:

  1. A. 15 mm
  2. B. 20 mm
  3. C. 25 mm
  4. D. 40 mm
Answer: A. 15 mm

Q10. Thickened part of a flat slab is called:

  1. A. Drop panel
  2. B. Column capital
  3. C. Fillet
  4. D. Haunch only
Answer: A. Drop panel

Q11. Drop panel is resistant against:

  1. A. Shear
  2. B. Bending
  3. C. Torsion
  4. D. Axial load
Answer: A. Shear

Q12. Unit hydrograph is for:

  1. A. Unit duration and unit rainfall
  2. B. Unit duration only
  3. C. Unit rainfall only
  4. D. Any duration
Answer: A. Unit duration and unit rainfall

Q13. Analysis of flood wave in reservoir is called:

  1. A. Flood routing
  2. B. Flood forecasting
  3. C. Flood frequency
  4. D. Flood control
Answer: A. Flood routing

Q14. Dickens formula: Q is directly proportional to:

  1. A. A^(3/4)
  2. B. A^(1/2)
  3. C. A
  4. D. A^2
Answer: A. A^(3/4)

Q15. Which one of the following is NOT a dimensionless factor?

  1. A. Manning's coefficient
  2. B. Froude number
  3. C. Reynold's number
  4. D. Weber number
Answer: A. Manning's coefficient

Q16. Froude number is expressed as:

  1. A. V / sqrt(gD)
  2. B. V x sqrt(gD)
  3. C. V / (gD)
  4. D. sqrt(V/gD)
Answer: A. V / sqrt(gD)

Q17. Froude number at critical section is:

  1. A. 1
  2. B. Less than 1
  3. C. More than 1
  4. D. 0
Answer: B. Less than 1

Q18. Divide wall is provided upstream of:

  1. A. Under sluice
  2. B. Weir
  3. C. Canal head
  4. D. Main canal
Answer: A. Under sluice

Q19. If it rains between 3pm and 4pm and pond starts to fill at 4pm, time of concentration is:

  1. A. 60 minutes
  2. B. 30 minutes
  3. C. 45 minutes
  4. D. 120 minutes
Answer: A. 60 minutes

Q20. Flood frequency of 10-year return period flood in next 4 years is:

  1. A. 35%
  2. B. 10%
  3. C. 40%
  4. D. 25%
Answer: A. 35%

Q21. Froude number is the ratio of:

  1. A. Inertia to gravitational forces
  2. B. Inertia to viscous forces
  3. C. Inertia to elastic forces
  4. D. Gravitational to viscous forces
Answer: A. Inertia to gravitational forces

Q22. Manning's formula does not involve:

  1. A. Viscosity of fluid
  2. B. Hydraulic radius
  3. C. Slope
  4. D. Roughness
Answer: A. Viscosity of fluid

Q23. Flow in a tapered pipe at constant rate is:

  1. A. Steady non-uniform
  2. B. Steady uniform
  3. C. Unsteady uniform
  4. D. Unsteady non-uniform
Answer: A. Steady non-uniform

Q24. If velocity of water is increased due to roughness then for constant flow, flow area:

  1. A. Decreases
  2. B. Increases
  3. C. Remains same
  4. D. Becomes zero
Answer: A. Decreases

Q25. Reynold's number is the ratio of:

  1. A. Inertial to viscous forces
  2. B. Inertial to gravitational forces
  3. C. Viscous to elastic forces
  4. D. Inertial to surface tension forces
Answer: A. Inertial to viscous forces
