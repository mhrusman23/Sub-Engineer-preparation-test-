Q1. Cantilever beam will be designed based on:

  1. A. Fixed end moment
  2. B. Mid span moment
  3. C. Fixed and mid span both
  4. D. None
Answer: A. Fixed end moment

Q2. Sheep foot roller is used for:

  1. A. Clays
  2. B. Sands
  3. C. Gravels
  4. D. Rock fills
Answer: A. Clays

Q3. One load moves from left to right; the load is UDL of length more than the length of beam. The max moment is induced when:

  1. A. Whole span is loaded
  2. B. Half span is loaded
  3. C. Load is at support
  4. D. Load is at free end
Answer: A. Whole span is loaded

Q4. In case of beams, reinforcement is necessarily provided for:

  1. A. Tension
  2. B. Bending only
  3. C. Compression
  4. D. Shear
Answer: A. Tension

Q5. Which method is preferred for hilly areas in angular measurement?

  1. A. Tacheometry
  2. B. Trilateration
  3. C. Chain surveying
  4. D. Plane tabling
Answer: A. Tacheometry

Q6. Bowditch rule is used for:

  1. A. Making corrections in angular measurements
  2. B. Making corrections in linear measurements only
  3. C. Calculating areas
  4. D. Levelling
Answer: A. Making corrections in angular measurements

Q7. When load is applied on a body and then removed and it recovers its shape, this is because of:

  1. A. Elasticity
  2. B. Plasticity
  3. C. Ductility
  4. D. Creep
Answer: A. Elasticity
