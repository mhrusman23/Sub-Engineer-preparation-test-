Q1. 'Climate change' refers to:

  1. A. Daily weather
  2. B. Long-term changes in Earth's temperature and weather patterns
  3. C. Seasons
  4. D. Rain only
Answer: B. Long-term changes in Earth's temperature and weather patterns

Q2. 'Carbon emissions' mainly come from:

  1. A. Trees
  2. B. Burning fossil fuels like coal, oil and gas
  3. C. Rain
  4. D. Oceans
Answer: B. Burning fossil fuels like coal, oil and gas

Q3. 'Renewable energy' comes from sources like:

  1. A. Coal
  2. B. Sun, wind and water
  3. C. Oil
  4. D. Gas
Answer: B. Sun, wind and water

Q4. To 'recycle' means to:

  1. A. Throw away
  2. B. Process used materials to make new products
  3. C. Burn waste
  4. D. Bury waste
Answer: B. Process used materials to make new products

Q5. 'Deforestation' is:

  1. A. Planting forests
  2. B. Cutting down large areas of forest
  3. C. Protecting trees
  4. D. Forest fires only
Answer: B. Cutting down large areas of forest

Q6. A species that is 'endangered' is:

  1. A. Very common
  2. B. At risk of disappearing completely
  3. C. Dangerous
  4. D. Newly discovered
Answer: B. At risk of disappearing completely

Q7. 'Pollution' can affect:

  1. A. Only air
  2. B. Air, water and land
  3. C. Only cities
  4. D. Nothing
Answer: B. Air, water and land

Q8. The 'greenhouse effect' involves gases that:

  1. A. Cool the Earth
  2. B. Trap heat in the atmosphere
  3. C. Make rain
  4. D. Clean the air
Answer: B. Trap heat in the atmosphere

Q9. 'Sustainable' practices are those that:

  1. A. Use up all resources
  2. B. Meet today's needs without harming future generations
  3. C. Are expensive
  4. D. Are illegal
Answer: B. Meet today's needs without harming future generations

Q10. A 'drought' is:

  1. A. Heavy rain
  2. B. A long period with very little rain
  3. C. A flood
  4. D. A storm
Answer: B. A long period with very little rain

Q11. 'Habitat' means:

  1. A. A habit
  2. B. The natural home of a plant or animal
  3. C. A zoo
  4. D. A farm
Answer: B. The natural home of a plant or animal

Q12. To 'conserve' water means to:

  1. A. Waste it
  2. B. Use it carefully and avoid wasting it
  3. C. Sell it
  4. D. Boil it
Answer: B. Use it carefully and avoid wasting it

Q13. 'Global warming' is mainly caused by:

  1. A. Volcanoes only
  2. B. Increased greenhouse gases from human activity
  3. C. The sun getting bigger
  4. D. Ocean tides
Answer: B. Increased greenhouse gases from human activity

Q14. A 'landfill' is a place where:

  1. A. Food is grown
  2. B. Waste is buried in the ground
  3. C. Cars are parked
  4. D. Water is cleaned
Answer: B. Waste is buried in the ground

Q15. 'Biodiversity' means:

  1. A. One species
  2. B. The variety of living things in an area
  3. C. A type of forest
  4. D. Clean air
Answer: B. The variety of living things in an area

Q16. An 'eco-friendly' product is one that:

  1. A. Is cheap
  2. B. Does not harm the environment
  3. C. Is electronic
  4. D. Is green in colour
Answer: B. Does not harm the environment

Q17. To 'emit' carbon dioxide means to:

  1. A. Absorb it
  2. B. Release it into the air
  3. C. Freeze it
  4. D. Measure it
Answer: B. Release it into the air

Q18. 'Fossil fuels' include:

  1. A. Solar and wind
  2. B. Coal, oil and natural gas
  3. C. Wood only
  4. D. Water
Answer: B. Coal, oil and natural gas

Q19. A 'flood' is often made worse by:

  1. A. Planting trees
  2. B. Deforestation and poor drainage
  3. C. Recycling
  4. D. Clean rivers
Answer: B. Deforestation and poor drainage

Q20. 'Organic' food is grown:

  1. A. In factories
  2. B. Without artificial chemicals
  3. C. Very quickly
  4. D. In water only
Answer: B. Without artificial chemicals
