Q1. What is the fineness modulus range for fine aggregates (sand) used in concrete?

  1. A. 1.0 to 2.0
  2. B. 2.0 to 3.5
  3. C. 5.5 to 8.0
  4. D. 3.5 to 5.0
Answer: B. 2.0 to 3.5

Q2. Which apparatus is used for determining the standard consistency of cement paste?

  1. A. Le Chatelier apparatus
  2. B. Vicat apparatus
  3. C. Blair's apparatus
  4. D. Autoclave apparatus
Answer: B. Vicat apparatus

Q3. The phenomenon of decrease in volume of concrete due to loss of moisture is called:

  1. A. Creep
  2. B. Shrinkage
  3. C. Segregation
  4. D. Bleeding
Answer: B. Shrinkage

Q4. Which type of cement is best suited for mass concrete works like dams to minimize heat of hydration?

  1. A. Rapid hardening cement
  2. B. Low heat Portland cement
  3. C. High alumina cement
  4. D. Quick setting cement
Answer: B. Low heat Portland cement

Q5. What is the ratio of ingredients in M20 grade concrete?

  1. A. 1:3:6
  2. B. 1:2:4
  3. C. 1:1.5:3
  4. D. 1:1:2
Answer: C. 1:1.5:3

Q6. What is the unit weight of plain cement concrete (PCC) as per standard civil engineering practices?

  1. A. 20 kN/m³
  2. B. 22 kN/m³
  3. C. 24 kN/m³
  4. D. 25 kN/m³
Answer: C. 24 kN/m³

Q7. The separation of water from freshly mixed concrete is known as:

  1. A. Segregation
  2. B. Bleeding
  3. C. Creep
  4. D. Shrinkage
Answer: B. Bleeding

Q8. In strategic business analysis, what is the primary purpose of conducting a Porter's Five Forces analysis?

  1. A. To evaluate the internal strengths and weaknesses of an organization
  2. B. To assess the industry attractiveness and competitive intensity
  3. C. To forecast future cash flows for capital budgeting decisions
  4. D. To measure individual employee key performance indicators
Answer: B. To assess the industry attractiveness and competitive intensity

Q9. What is the specific gravity of ordinary Portland cement generally taken as?

  1. A. 2.50
  2. B. 2.75
  3. C. 3.15
  4. D. 3.45
Answer: C. 3.15

Q10. Which compound of cement is responsible for the early strength development?

  1. A. Tricalcium silicate (C3S)
  2. B. Dicalcium silicate (C2S)
  3. C. Tricalcium aluminate (C3A)
  4. D. Tetracalcium aluminoferrite (C4AF)
Answer: A. Tricalcium silicate (C3S)

Q11. The process of removing air bubbles from fresh concrete to ensure proper compaction is known as:

  1. A. Curing
  2. B. Bleeding
  3. C. Segregation
  4. D. Vibration / Compaction
Answer: D. Vibration / Compaction

Q12. Which type of cement is recommended for massive concrete structures like dams to minimize heat of hydration?

  1. A. Rapid hardening cement
  2. B. Low heat Portland cement
  3. C. High alumina cement
  4. D. Quick setting cement
Answer: B. Low heat Portland cement

Q13. Which type of cement is recommended for mass concreting projects like dams to reduce heat of hydration?

  1. A. Rapid hardening cement
  2. B. Low heat Portland cement
  3. C. Quick setting cement
  4. D. High alumina cement
Answer: B. Low heat Portland cement

Q14. What is the maximum permissible limit of sulfate content in water for concrete mixing?

  1. A. 200 mg/l
  2. B. 400 mg/l
  3. C. 600 mg/l
  4. D. 300 mg/l
Answer: B. 400 mg/l

Q15. The slump test for concrete is used to measure its?

  1. A. Strength
  2. B. Workability
  3. C. Durability
  4. D. Density
Answer: B. Workability

Q16. What is the primary purpose of adding gypsum to clinker during cement manufacture?

  1. A. To increase compressive strength
  2. B. To control the initial setting time
  3. C. To impart dark color
  4. D. To reduce the weight
Answer: B. To control the initial setting time

Q17. The main ingredients of concrete are cement, water, fine aggregate and:

  1. A. Gypsum only
  2. B. Coarse aggregate
  3. C. Lime
  4. D. Clay
Answer: B. Coarse aggregate

Q18. Plain concrete is weak in:

  1. A. Durability
  2. B. Tension
  3. C. Fire resistance
  4. D. Compression
Answer: B. Tension

Q19. The strength of concrete is usually specified at:

  1. A. 3 days only
  2. B. 90 days only
  3. C. 7 days only
  4. D. 28 days
Answer: D. 28 days

Q20. The workability of concrete is commonly measured by the:

  1. A. Soundness test
  2. B. Slump test
  3. C. Vicat test
  4. D. Los Angeles test
Answer: B. Slump test

Q21. A higher water-cement ratio generally gives:

  1. A. Lower strength
  2. B. Higher durability
  3. C. Same strength
  4. D. Higher strength
Answer: A. Lower strength

Q22. Curing of concrete is done to:

  1. A. Dry it quickly
  2. B. Add colour
  3. C. Retain moisture for hydration
  4. D. Cool the aggregate only
Answer: C. Retain moisture for hydration

Q23. The main compound of cement that gives early strength is:

  1. A. Dicalcium silicate (C2S) only
  2. B. Tricalcium silicate (C3S)
  3. C. Gypsum
  4. D. Tricalcium aluminate only
Answer: B. Tricalcium silicate (C3S)

Q24. Gypsum is added to cement to:

  1. A. Add colour
  2. B. Reduce cost only
  3. C. Control the setting time
  4. D. Increase strength directly
Answer: C. Control the setting time

Q25. Segregation in concrete is the:

  1. A. Setting of water
  2. B. Separation of its ingredients
  3. C. Mixing of ingredients
  4. D. Hardening of cement
Answer: B. Separation of its ingredients
