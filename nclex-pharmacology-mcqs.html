Q1. The 'five rights' of medication administration include right patient, drug, dose, route and:

  1. A. Room
  2. B. Time
  3. C. Doctor
  4. D. Pharmacy
Answer: B. Time

Q2. Insulin is given to patients with:

  1. A. Hypertension
  2. B. Diabetes mellitus
  3. C. Asthma
  4. D. Arthritis
Answer: B. Diabetes mellitus

Q3. A common side effect of opioids such as morphine is:

  1. A. Diarrhoea
  2. B. Respiratory depression and constipation
  3. C. Insomnia
  4. D. Weight gain
Answer: B. Respiratory depression and constipation

Q4. Warfarin therapy is monitored using:

  1. A. Blood sugar
  2. B. INR (prothrombin time)
  3. C. Blood pressure
  4. D. Temperature
Answer: B. INR (prothrombin time)

Q5. Before giving digoxin, the nurse should check the:

  1. A. Blood pressure
  2. B. Apical pulse for one full minute
  3. C. Temperature
  4. D. Weight
Answer: B. Apical pulse for one full minute

Q6. Antibiotics should be given:

  1. A. Only when the patient asks
  2. B. At evenly spaced times to keep blood levels steady
  3. C. All at once
  4. D. With alcohol
Answer: B. At evenly spaced times to keep blood levels steady

Q7. A patient on ACE inhibitors (e.g. enalapril) commonly develops:

  1. A. A persistent dry cough
  2. B. Hair loss
  3. C. Blue skin
  4. D. Hearing loss
Answer: A. A persistent dry cough

Q8. The antidote for opioid overdose is:

  1. A. Flumazenil
  2. B. Naloxone
  3. C. Vitamin K
  4. D. Protamine
Answer: B. Naloxone

Q9. Metformin is used for:

  1. A. Type 2 diabetes
  2. B. Pain relief
  3. C. Infection
  4. D. High cholesterol
Answer: A. Type 2 diabetes

Q10. IV potassium must always be:

  1. A. Given as a fast IV push
  2. B. Diluted and given slowly — never IV push
  3. C. Given with insulin always
  4. D. Given orally only
Answer: B. Diluted and given slowly — never IV push

Q11. A client receiving heparin therapy requires monitoring of which specific laboratory test to evaluate the effectiveness of the treatment?

  1. A. International Normalized Ratio (INR)
  2. B. Activated Partial Thromboplastin Time (aPTT)
  3. C. Bleeding time
  4. D. Red blood cell count
Answer: B. Activated Partial Thromboplastin Time (aPTT)

Q12. A client is prescribed levothyroxine for hypothyroidism. The nurse should instruct the client to take this medication at which specific time of day?

  1. A. At bedtime with a high-fat snack
  2. B. In the evening with dinner
  3. C. In the morning on an empty stomach with a full glass of water
  4. D. Anytime during the day regardless of meals
Answer: C. In the morning on an empty stomach with a full glass of water

Q13. Which of the following laboratory values should be monitored closely for a client receiving intravenous furosemide therapy?

  1. A. Serum potassium
  2. B. Serum glucose
  3. C. Platelet count
  4. D. Blood urea nitrogen only
Answer: A. Serum potassium

Q14. Which medication is classified as a potassium-sparing diuretic often used in combination therapy for hypertension and heart failure?

  1. A. Furosemide
  2. B. Hydrochlorothiazide
  3. C. Spironolactone
  4. D. Bumetanide
Answer: C. Spironolactone

Q15. What is the primary pharmacological action of beta-blockers such as metoprolol in cardiac clients?

  1. A. Increasing heart rate and myocardial contractility
  2. B. Decreasing heart rate and reducing myocardial oxygen demand
  3. C. Causing direct vasoconstriction of peripheral vessels
  4. D. Dilating the bronchioles in the lungs
Answer: B. Decreasing heart rate and reducing myocardial oxygen demand

Q16. A nurse is preparing to administer digoxin to a client with heart failure. Which assessment finding requires immediate withholding of the medication?

  1. A. Blood pressure of 128/82 mmHg
  2. B. Apical pulse rate of 52 beats per minute
  3. C. Respiratory rate of 18 breaths per minute
  4. D. Temperature of 98.6 °F
Answer: B. Apical pulse rate of 52 beats per minute

Q17. Which drug class is most commonly used to treat hyperthyroidism by inhibiting thyroid hormone synthesis?

  1. A. Thyroidstimulants
  2. B. Beta‑blockers
  3. C. Antithyroid agents
  4. D. Entry inhibitors
Answer: C. Antithyroid agents

Q18. The antidiarrheal action of loperamide is mediated through which mechanism?

  1. A. Inhibition of mucosal absorption
  2. B. Stimulation of intestinal motility
  3. C. Activation of opioid receptors in gut
  4. D. Reduction of intestinal fluid secretion
Answer: C. Activation of opioid receptors in gut

Q19. In treating a patient with septic shock, which vasopressor is first line to raise mean arterial pressure?

  1. A. Norepinephrine
  2. B. Phenylephrine
  3. C. Dopamine
  4. D. Epinephrine
Answer: A. Norepinephrine

Q20. What is the primary pharmacological action of beta-blockers such as metoprolol?

  1. A. A) Increasing heart rate and contractility
  2. B. B) Blocking sympathetic nervous system stimulation to lower heart rate and blood pressure
  3. C. C) Directly dilating peripheral blood vessels
  4. D. D) Increasing renal excretion of sodium and water
Answer: B. B) Blocking sympathetic nervous system stimulation to lower heart rate and blood pressure

Q21. Which assessment finding requires immediate nursing action before administering digoxin?

  1. A. A) Apical pulse rate of 52 beats per minute
  2. B. B) Blood pressure of 120/80 mmHg
  3. C. C) Respiratory rate of 16 breaths per minute
  4. D. D) Mild peripheral edema
Answer: A. A) Apical pulse rate of 52 beats per minute

Q22. A client is prescribed levothyroxine for hypothyroidism. When is the best time for the client to take this medication?

  1. A. A) At bedtime with a high-fat snack
  2. B. B) In the morning on an empty stomach with a full glass of water
  3. C. C) With breakfast to prevent gastric upset
  4. D. D) Immediately after lunch
Answer: B. B) In the morning on an empty stomach with a full glass of water

Q23. Which laboratory test is essential to monitor for a client receiving long-term heparin therapy?

  1. A. A) Prothrombin Time (PT)
  2. B. B) Activated Partial Thromboplastin Time (aPTT)
  3. C. C) Serum creatinine
  4. D. D) Platelet count only
Answer: B. B) Activated Partial Thromboplastin Time (aPTT)

Q24. Which medication is classified as a potassium-sparing diuretic?

  1. A. A) Furosemide
  2. B. B) Hydrochlorothiazide
  3. C. C) Spironolactone
  4. D. D) Bumetanide
Answer: C. C) Spironolactone

Q25. What is the specific reversal agent or antidote for a severe heparin overdose?

  1. A. A) Vitamin K
  2. B. B) Protamine sulfate
  3. C. C) Naloxone
  4. D. D) Flumazenil
Answer: B. B) Protamine sulfate
