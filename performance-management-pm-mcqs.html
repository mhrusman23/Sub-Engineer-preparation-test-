Q1. The four perspectives of the balanced scorecard are financial, customer, internal business process and:

  1. A. Supplier
  2. B. Learning and growth
  3. C. Marketing
  4. D. Taxation
Answer: B. Learning and growth

Q2. Return on investment (ROI) is calculated as:

  1. A. Capital employed / profit
  2. B. Profit / sales x 100
  3. C. Sales / profit
  4. D. Profit / capital employed x 100
Answer: D. Profit / capital employed x 100

Q3. Residual income equals divisional profit less:

  1. A. Depreciation only
  2. B. Corporate tax
  3. C. An imputed interest charge on capital employed
  4. D. Total fixed costs
Answer: C. An imputed interest charge on capital employed

Q4. The price variance in standard costing equals (standard price - actual price) multiplied by:

  1. A. Budgeted quantity
  2. B. Opening inventory
  3. C. Standard quantity
  4. D. Actual quantity
Answer: D. Actual quantity

Q5. In throughput accounting, throughput equals sales less:

  1. A. Labour cost
  2. B. Direct material cost
  3. C. All variable costs
  4. D. Total cost
Answer: B. Direct material cost

Q6. Which cost is irrelevant for a decision?

  1. A. Incremental cost
  2. B. Sunk cost
  3. C. Opportunity cost
  4. D. Avoidable cost
Answer: B. Sunk cost

Q7. Opportunity cost is the:

  1. A. Average cost
  2. B. Benefit lost from the best alternative not chosen
  3. C. Cost of capital
  4. D. Cost already paid
Answer: B. Benefit lost from the best alternative not chosen

Q8. Target cost is found as target selling price less:

  1. A. Target profit
  2. B. Actual cost
  3. C. Fixed cost
  4. D. Overheads
Answer: A. Target profit

Q9. The three Es of value for money are economy, efficiency and:

  1. A. Earnings
  2. B. Effectiveness
  3. C. Equity
  4. D. Expansion
Answer: B. Effectiveness

Q10. A favourable variance means actual results are:

  1. A. Unrelated to budget
  2. B. Equal to zero
  3. C. Better than standard or budget
  4. D. Worse than standard
Answer: C. Better than standard or budget

Q11. Which of the following costs is classified as a relevant cost when making a short-term decision to accept a special order?

  1. A. Sunk cost
  2. B. Historical cost
  3. C. Avoidable incremental cost
  4. D. Allocated fixed overhead
Answer: C. Avoidable incremental cost

Q12. Which of the following costs is classified as a relevant cost for decision-making purposes?

  1. A. Sunk cost
  2. B. Historical cost
  3. C. Avoidable cost
  4. D. Apportioned overhead cost
Answer: C. Avoidable cost

Q13. What is the primary purpose of conducting variance analysis in standard costing systems?

  1. A. To punish employees who exceed their budget
  2. B. To identify deviations from standards and take corrective management action
  3. C. To eliminate the need for future budgeting
  4. D. To calculate the exact tax liability of the firm
Answer: B. To identify deviations from standards and take corrective management action

Q14. In a standard costing system, an adverse material usage variance and a favorable material price variance occurred. What does this combination typically suggest?

  1. A. Cheaper, lower-quality materials were purchased and used in excess
  2. B. Higher-quality materials were purchased at a discount and used efficiently
  3. C. More material was used because production volume decreased
  4. D. Prices increased unexpectedly while efficiency improved
Answer: A. Cheaper, lower-quality materials were purchased and used in excess

Q15. In variance analysis, which of the following causes a favorable direct material price variance?

  1. A. Paying a higher price per kg than standard
  2. B. Using less material than standard
  3. C. Paying a lower price per kg than standard
  4. D. Using more material than standard
Answer: C. Paying a lower price per kg than standard

Q16. How does transfer pricing based on opportunity cost affect divisional performance measurement when a supplying division has excess capacity?

  1. A. It should be set equal to the full cost plus a markup
  2. B. It should be set equal to the variable cost of production since opportunity cost is zero
  3. C. It must always equal the external market price
  4. D. It is calculated as the net present value of future cash flows
Answer: B. It should be set equal to the variable cost of production since opportunity cost is zero

Q17. What is the primary purpose of introducing a balanced scorecard in performance management?

  1. A. To focus exclusively on financial metrics
  2. B. To link operational activities to strategic goals
  3. C. To replace annual performance appraisals
  4. D. To calculate employee bonuses and wages
Answer: B. To link operational activities to strategic goals

Q18. Which appraisal error occurs when a supervisor evaluates an employee based entirely on their most recent actions?

  1. A. Halo effect
  2. B. Leniency error
  3. C. Recency effect
  4. D. Central tendency
Answer: C. Recency effect

Q19. What does a Key Performance Indicator (KPI) primarily measure in an engineering or construction project?

  1. A. Daily worker attendance only
  2. B. Progress toward specific strategic objectives
  3. C. Total fixed overhead costs
  4. D. Employee personality traits
Answer: B. Progress toward specific strategic objectives

Q20. Which performance management tool focuses specifically on continuous feedback and developmental coaching rather than just grading?

  1. A. Traditional merit rating
  2. B. Rank and yank system
  3. C. Agile performance review
  4. D. Strict quota assignment
Answer: C. Agile performance review

Q21. What is a major risk of implementing a forced distribution rating system in performance evaluations?

  1. A. Eliminating all appraisal biases
  2. B. Creating unnecessary workplace rivalry and lowered morale
  3. C. Ensuring every employee receives an excellent score
  4. D. Simplifying payroll calculations entirely
Answer: B. Creating unnecessary workplace rivalry and lowered morale

Q22. In 360-degree feedback, who typically rates the performance of the target employee?

  1. A. Only the direct department manager
  2. B. Peers, subordinates, supervisors, and customers
  3. C. External government auditors exclusively
  4. D. Human resource payroll officers only
Answer: B. Peers, subordinates, supervisors, and customers

Q23. When a division has excess capacity, which transfer price best reflects the true opportunity cost for performance measurement?

  1. A. Full cost plus a standard markup
  2. B. Variable cost of production
  3. C. External market price
  4. D. Net present value of future cash flows
Answer: B. Variable cost of production

Q24. A manufacturing division incurs a fixed cost of PKR 200,000 and variable cost of PKR 50 per unit. If it has 1,000 units of unused capacity, what is the appropriate transfer price to another division?

  1. A. PKR 250 per unit
  2. B. PKR 50 per unit
  3. C. PKR 200 per unit
  4. D. PKR 0 per unit
Answer: B. PKR 50 per unit

Q25. When calculating transfer price based on opportunity cost, which cost element should be excluded if the division has idle capacity?

  1. A. Variable cost
  2. B. Fixed cost
  3. C. Opportunity cost
  4. D. Direct labor cost
Answer: B. Fixed cost
