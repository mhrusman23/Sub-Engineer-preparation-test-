Q1. What is the standard height of a wash basin from the floor level in plumbing installations?

  1. A. 750 mm
  2. B. 800 mm
  3. C. 850 mm
  4. D. 900 mm
Answer: B. 800 mm

Q2. In building drainage engineering, what is the main consequence of omitting required cleanouts on a vertical drainage riser diagram during plan review?

  1. A. The layout fails building code compliance and cannot be approved as drawn
  2. B. The wastewater flow velocity will exceed maximum allowable limits
  3. C. The pipe material specification automatically defaults to cast iron
  4. D. The horizontal slope of the branch drains becomes instantly invalid
Answer: A. The layout fails building code compliance and cannot be approved as drawn

Q3. What is the primary function of installing a secondary roof drain system in conjunction with a primary drain on a roof surrounded by parapet walls?

  1. A. To increase the overall water discharge velocity
  2. B. To prevent structural overload if the primary drain becomes blocked
  3. C. To aerate the stormwater before it enters the municipal sewer
  4. D. To filter out large debris like leaves and twigs
Answer: B. To prevent structural overload if the primary drain becomes blocked

Q4. What is the primary function of a trap installed beneath sanitary plumbing fixtures like wash basins and sinks?

  1. A. To increase water flow velocity
  2. B. To maintain a water seal preventing sewer gases from entering the building
  3. C. To trap heavy solid debris from clogging the main sewer line
  4. D. To regulate water temperature fluctuations
Answer: B. To maintain a water seal preventing sewer gases from entering the building

Q5. Which of the following materials is commonly used for manufacturing corrosion-resistant soil and vent drainage pipes in modern plumbing systems?

  1. A. Cast Iron
  2. B. Unplasticized Polyvinyl Chloride (uPVC)
  3. C. Mild Steel
  4. D. Wrought Iron
Answer: B. Unplasticized Polyvinyl Chloride (uPVC)

Q6. What is the primary purpose of installing vent pipes in a building's sanitary drainage system?

  1. A. To supply fresh water to upper floor fixtures
  2. B. To circulate air and prevent siphonage of trap water seals
  3. C. To increase the wastewater discharge speed
  4. D. To filter out grease and oil from kitchen waste
Answer: B. To circulate air and prevent siphonage of trap water seals

Q7. Which standardized code document is most widely adopted by jurisdictions and local plumbing authorities for regulatory enforcement?

  1. A. National Electrical Code (NEC)
  2. B. International Plumbing Code (IPC)
  3. C. American Society of Civil Engineers (ASCE) Standard 7
  4. D. International Building Fire Code (IBFC)
Answer: B. International Plumbing Code (IPC)

Q8. According to standard plumbing codes for roof drainage, what type of drainage system is strictly mandatory when parapet walls completely block rainwater runoff?

  1. A. Gravity drainage only
  2. B. A combined primary and secondary overflow drainage system
  3. C. Single scupper drainage without overflow
  4. D. Open channel gutter system
Answer: B. A combined primary and secondary overflow drainage system

Q9. Before water supply pipes are concealed within floors or walls, which of the following mandatory tests must be performed to check for leaks?

  1. A. Hammer Test
  2. B. Pressure Test
  3. C. Slump Test
  4. D. Cube Test
Answer: B. Pressure Test

Q10. When performing maintenance or replacing a damaged section of a rigid plumbing pipe, what initial safety step must always be executed?

  1. A. Turning off the water supply and completely draining the pipeline
  2. B. Applying a thick coat of solvent cement to the exterior joints
  3. C. Increasing the ambient temperature of the room above 25°C
  4. D. Replacing all upstream fittings simultaneously
Answer: A. Turning off the water supply and completely draining the pipeline

Q11. Which specific device or assembly is utilized in plumbing systems to prevent the dangerous backflow of contaminated water into the safe potable water supply?

  1. A. Gate valve
  2. B. Backflow preventer
  3. C. Pressure reducing valve
  4. D. Water hammer arrestor
Answer: B. Backflow preventer

Q12. Which method is commonly recommended to protect exposed water supply pipes from freezing during extreme cold weather conditions?

  1. A. Wrapping pipes tightly with copper wire
  2. B. Insulating pipes using foam sleeves or heat tape
  3. C. Coating the exterior pipe surface with asphalt paint
  4. D. Increasing the internal water pressure of the system

Q21. What is the primary purpose of incorporating an air chamber or water hammer arrestor in a pressurized water supply system?

Q22. In drainage system design, what does the term 'invert level' refer to?
