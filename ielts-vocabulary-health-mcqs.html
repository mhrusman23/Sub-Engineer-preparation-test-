Q1. 'Obesity' means:

  1. A. Being underweight
  2. B. Being extremely overweight
  3. C. Being tall
  4. D. Being strong
Answer: B. Being extremely overweight

Q2. A 'balanced diet' includes:

  1. A. Only meat
  2. B. A variety of foods in the right amounts
  3. C. Only fruit
  4. D. No water
Answer: B. A variety of foods in the right amounts

Q3. 'Sedentary lifestyle' describes people who:

  1. A. Exercise daily
  2. B. Sit a lot and move very little
  3. C. Work outdoors
  4. D. Play sports
Answer: B. Sit a lot and move very little

Q4. To 'prevent' a disease means to:

  1. A. Cure it
  2. B. Stop it from happening
  3. C. Spread it
  4. D. Ignore it
Answer: B. Stop it from happening

Q5. 'Symptoms' are:

  1. A. Medicines
  2. B. Signs that you are ill
  3. C. Doctors
  4. D. Hospitals
Answer: B. Signs that you are ill

Q6. A 'vaccination' helps the body to:

  1. A. Gain weight
  2. B. Build protection against a disease
  3. C. Sleep better
  4. D. Grow taller
Answer: B. Build protection against a disease

Q7. 'Mental health' refers to:

  1. A. Physical strength
  2. B. Emotional and psychological well-being
  3. C. Memory only
  4. D. Intelligence
Answer: B. Emotional and psychological well-being

Q8. To 'recover' from an illness means to:

  1. A. Get worse
  2. B. Get well again
  3. C. Catch it again
  4. D. Spread it
Answer: B. Get well again

Q9. 'Chronic' disease is one that:

  1. A. Lasts a short time
  2. B. Lasts a long time or keeps returning
  3. C. Is always fatal
  4. D. Spreads fast
Answer: B. Lasts a long time or keeps returning

Q10. 'Stress' can negatively affect:

  1. A. Only the body
  2. B. Both mind and body
  3. C. Only sleep
  4. D. Nothing
Answer: B. Both mind and body

Q11. A 'nutrient' is:

  1. A. A disease
  2. B. A substance in food that the body needs
  3. C. A vitamin pill brand
  4. D. An exercise
Answer: B. A substance in food that the body needs

Q12. 'Life expectancy' means:

  1. A. Quality of life
  2. B. The average length of time a person is expected to live
  3. C. Retirement age
  4. D. Birth rate
Answer: B. The average length of time a person is expected to live

Q13. To 'diagnose' means to:

  1. A. Treat a patient
  2. B. Identify what illness someone has
  3. C. Operate
  4. D. Prescribe medicine
Answer: B. Identify what illness someone has

Q14. 'Side effects' of medicine are:

  1. A. Main benefits
  2. B. Unwanted reactions caused by the medicine
  3. C. Instructions
  4. D. Prices
Answer: B. Unwanted reactions caused by the medicine

Q15. 'Regular exercise' helps to reduce the risk of:

  1. A. Heart disease and diabetes
  2. B. All accidents
  3. C. Bad weather
  4. D. Exams
Answer: A. Heart disease and diabetes

Q16. A 'GP' (general practitioner) is:

  1. A. A surgeon
  2. B. A doctor who treats general illnesses
  3. C. A nurse
  4. D. A pharmacist
Answer: B. A doctor who treats general illnesses

Q17. 'Allergy' is:

  1. A. A vitamin
  2. B. A bad reaction to a substance like pollen or certain foods
  3. C. An infection
  4. D. A type of exercise
Answer: B. A bad reaction to a substance like pollen or certain foods

Q18. 'Hygiene' means:

  1. A. Cooking
  2. B. Keeping yourself and surroundings clean to stay healthy
  3. C. Sleeping
  4. D. Eating less
Answer: B. Keeping yourself and surroundings clean to stay healthy

Q19. To 'cut down on' sugar means to:

  1. A. Stop sugar completely
  2. B. Reduce the amount of sugar you eat
  3. C. Buy more sugar
  4. D. Cook sugar
Answer: B. Reduce the amount of sugar you eat

Q20. 'Treatment' is:

  1. A. The illness itself
  2. B. Medical care given to a patient to cure or manage an illness
  3. C. A hospital bill
  4. D. A symptom
Answer: B. Medical care given to a patient to cure or manage an illness
