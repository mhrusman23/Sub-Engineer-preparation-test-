Q1. The Constitution of Pakistan currently in force is the Constitution of:

  1. A. 1956
  2. B. 1962
  3. C. 1973
  4. D. 1985
Answer: C. 1973

Q2. A 'writ' of habeas corpus is issued to:

  1. A. Stop a trial
  2. B. Produce a person before the court and justify their detention
  3. C. Collect a debt
  4. D. Register a company
Answer: B. Produce a person before the court and justify their detention

Q3. In contract law, 'consideration' means:

  1. A. Politeness
  2. B. Something of value exchanged between the parties
  3. C. A written document
  4. D. A witness
Answer: B. Something of value exchanged between the parties

Q4. Mens rea in criminal law refers to:

  1. A. The act
  2. B. The guilty mind / intention
  3. C. The punishment
  4. D. The evidence
Answer: B. The guilty mind / intention

Q5. The highest court in Pakistan is:

  1. A. Lahore High Court
  2. B. The Supreme Court of Pakistan
  3. C. Federal Shariat Court
  4. D. District Court
Answer: B. The Supreme Court of Pakistan

Q6. A tort of negligence requires duty of care, breach, causation and:

  1. A. A contract
  2. B. Actual damage/loss
  3. C. A witness
  4. D. Registration
Answer: B. Actual damage/loss

Q7. Under the Pakistan Penal Code, 'qatl' relates to:

  1. A. Theft
  2. B. Culpable homicide / murder
  3. C. Fraud
  4. D. Defamation
Answer: B. Culpable homicide / murder

Q8. The principle 'audi alteram partem' means:

  1. A. Decide quickly
  2. B. Hear the other side (fair hearing)
  3. C. No appeal
  4. D. Written law only
Answer: B. Hear the other side (fair hearing)

Q9. A valid contract requires all EXCEPT:

  1. A. Offer and acceptance
  2. B. Free consent
  3. C. Lawful object
  4. D. Registration in every case
Answer: D. Registration in every case

Q10. Fundamental rights in Pakistan's Constitution are given in:

  1. A. The preamble only
  2. B. Chapter 1 of Part II (Articles 8-28)
  3. C. The schedules
  4. D. Provincial laws
Answer: B. Chapter 1 of Part II (Articles 8-28)

Q11. The primary objective of the Limitation Act is to:

  1. A. Fix maximum penalties for criminal offenses
  2. B. Bar legal remedies for stale claims after a specified statutory period
  3. C. Regulate the taxation of inherited properties
  4. D. Determine the jurisdiction of civil courts over revenue matters
Answer: B. Bar legal remedies for stale claims after a specified statutory period

Q12. According to the Law of Evidence in Pakistan, a fact is said to be 'proved' when:

  1. A. It is mentioned in a police report without contradiction
  2. B. The court considers its existence so probable that a prudent man would act upon that supposition
  3. C. Both parties agree to its validity in an informal meeting
  4. D. It is published in an official gazette
Answer: B. The court considers its existence so probable that a prudent man would act upon that supposition

Q13. In administrative law, the legal doctrine of 'ultra vires' means an act done:

  1. A. In good faith and with full authority
  2. B. Beyond the legal powers or jurisdiction of the authority
  3. C. With the unanimous consent of all parties involved
  4. D. During an emergency session of the legislature
Answer: B. Beyond the legal powers or jurisdiction of the authority

Q14. In civil procedure, an 'interim injunction' is granted primarily to:

  1. A. Permanently dismiss a frivolous lawsuit at the preliminary stage
  2. B. Maintain the status quo and prevent irreparable harm before the final judgment
  3. C. Execute a money decree against a judgment debtor
  4. D. Transfer a case from a high court to a lower district court
Answer: B. Maintain the status quo and prevent irreparable harm before the final judgment

Q15. The power of judicial review in Pakistan's constitutional framework allows the superior courts to:

  1. A. Examine acts and orders to determine their conformity with the Constitution
  2. B. Draft new legislation for the provincial assemblies
  3. C. Appoint the members of the federal cabinet
  4. D. Manage the operational budget of local governments
Answer: A. Examine acts and orders to determine their conformity with the Constitution

Q16. Under the concept of delegated legislation, rule-making authority is exercised by:

  1. A. The parliament directly during plenary sessions
  2. B. Subordinate bodies and executive agencies authorized by an enabling act
  3. C. The Supreme Court bench during constitutional petitions
  4. D. Private legal practitioners and bar councils
Answer: B. Subordinate bodies and executive agencies authorized by an enabling act

Q17. Max Weber characterizes modern bureaucratic administrative systems as resting primarily on which type of authority?

  1. A. Traditional authority
  2. B. Charismatic authority
  3. C. Legal-rational authority
  4. D. Hereditary authority
Answer: C. Legal-rational authority

Q18. In criminal jurisprudence, the legal phrase 'mens rea' denotes:

  1. A. The physical act of a crime
  2. B. A guilty mind or criminal intent
  3. C. The burden of proof
  4. D. An accomplice testimony
Answer: B. A guilty mind or criminal intent

Q19. According to maxims of equity and jurisprudence, the Latin term 'audi alteram partem' translates to:

  1. A. Let the buyer beware
  2. B. Hear the other side
  3. C. No one can be a judge in their own cause
  4. D. Justice delayed is justice denied
Answer: B. Hear the other side

Q20. In civil procedure, the prerogative writ of 'habeas corpus' is primarily issued to secure:

  1. A. Protection of property rights
  2. B. Production of an unlawfully detained person before a court

Q23. In the jurisprudence of equity, which legal doctrine prevents a party from going back on a representation upon which another party has relied to their detriment?

  1. A. Res judicata
  2. B. Estoppel
  3. C. Lis pendens
  4. D. Subrogation

Q24. Under criminal law principles in Pakistan, instigating another person to commit an offense is legally defined as:

  1. A. Conspiracy
  2. B. Abetment
  3. C. Attempt
  4. D. Facilitation
Answer: B. Abetment

Q25. Under Pakistani criminal jurisprudence, what does a sentence of imprisonment for life legally signify?

  2. B. Imprisonment till natural death
  3. C. 25 years subject to remission
  4. D. Imprisonment for the remainder of active working life
Answer: B. Imprisonment till natural death
