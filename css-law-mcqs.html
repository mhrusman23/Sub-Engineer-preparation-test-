Q1. The Constitution of Pakistan currently in force is the Constitution of:

  1. A. 1956
  2. B. 1962
  3. C. 1973
  4. D. 1985
Answer: C. 1973

Q2. A 'writ' of habeas corpus is issued to:

  1. A. Stop a trial
  2. B. Produce a person before the court and justify their detention
  3. C. Collect a debt
  4. D. Register a company
Answer: B. Produce a person before the court and justify their detention

Q3. In contract law, 'consideration' means:

  1. A. Politeness
  2. B. Something of value exchanged between the parties
  3. C. A written document
  4. D. A witness
Answer: B. Something of value exchanged between the parties

Q4. Mens rea in criminal law refers to:

  1. A. The act
  2. B. The guilty mind / intention
  3. C. The punishment
  4. D. The evidence
Answer: B. The guilty mind / intention

Q5. The highest court in Pakistan is:

  1. A. Lahore High Court
  2. B. The Supreme Court of Pakistan
  3. C. Federal Shariat Court
  4. D. District Court
Answer: B. The Supreme Court of Pakistan

Q6. A tort of negligence requires duty of care, breach, causation and:

  1. A. A contract
  2. B. Actual damage/loss
  3. C. A witness
  4. D. Registration
Answer: B. Actual damage/loss

Q7. Under the Pakistan Penal Code, 'qatl' relates to:

  1. A. Theft
  2. B. Culpable homicide / murder
  3. C. Fraud
  4. D. Defamation
Answer: B. Culpable homicide / murder

Q8. The principle 'audi alteram partem' means:

  1. A. Decide quickly
  2. B. Hear the other side (fair hearing)
  3. C. No appeal
  4. D. Written law only
Answer: B. Hear the other side (fair hearing)

Q9. A valid contract requires all EXCEPT:

  1. A. Offer and acceptance
  2. B. Free consent
  3. C. Lawful object
  4. D. Registration in every case
Answer: D. Registration in every case

Q10. Fundamental rights in Pakistan's Constitution are given in:

  1. A. The preamble only
  2. B. Chapter 1 of Part II (Articles 8-28)
  3. C. The schedules
  4. D. Provincial laws
Answer: B. Chapter 1 of Part II (Articles 8-28)
