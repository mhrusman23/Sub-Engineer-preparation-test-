Q1. The Constitution of Pakistan currently in force is the Constitution of:

  1. A. 1956
  2. B. 1962
  3. C. 1973
  4. D. 1985
Answer: C. 1973

Q2. A 'writ' of habeas corpus is issued to:

  1. A. Stop a trial
  2. B. Produce a person before the court and justify their detention
  3. C. Collect a debt
  4. D. Register a company
Answer: B. Produce a person before the court and justify their detention

Q3. In contract law, 'consideration' means:

  1. A. Politeness
  2. B. Something of value exchanged between the parties
  3. C. A written document
  4. D. A witness
Answer: B. Something of value exchanged between the parties

Q4. Mens rea in criminal law refers to:

  1. A. The act
  2. B. The guilty mind / intention
  3. C. The punishment
  4. D. The evidence
Answer: B. The guilty mind / intention

Q5. The highest court in Pakistan is:

  1. A. Lahore High Court
  2. B. The Supreme Court of Pakistan
  3. C. Federal Shariat Court
  4. D. District Court
Answer: B. The Supreme Court of Pakistan

Q6. A tort of negligence requires duty of care, breach, causation and:

  1. A. A contract
  2. B. Actual damage/loss
  3. C. A witness
  4. D. Registration
Answer: B. Actual damage/loss

Q7. Under the Pakistan Penal Code, 'qatl' relates to:

  1. A. Theft
  2. B. Culpable homicide / murder
  3. C. Fraud
  4. D. Defamation
Answer: B. Culpable homicide / murder

Q8. The principle 'audi alteram partem' means:

  1. A. Decide quickly
  2. B. Hear the other side (fair hearing)
  3. C. No appeal
  4. D. Written law only
Answer: B. Hear the other side (fair hearing)

Q9. A valid contract requires all EXCEPT:

  1. A. Offer and acceptance
  2. B. Free consent
  3. C. Lawful object
  4. D. Registration in every case
Answer: D. Registration in every case

Q10. Fundamental rights in Pakistan's Constitution are given in:

  1. A. The preamble only
  2. B. Chapter 1 of Part II (Articles 8-28)
  3. C. The schedules
  4. D. Provincial laws
Answer: B. Chapter 1 of Part II (Articles 8-28)

Q11. The first Constitution of Pakistan was enforced in:

  1. A. 1947
  2. B. 1962
  3. C. 1973
  4. D. 1956
Answer: D. 1956

Q12. The Constitution of 1962 was promulgated during the rule of:

  1. A. Ayub Khan
  2. B. Liaquat Ali Khan
  3. C. Yahya Khan
  4. D. Zulfikar Ali Bhutto
Answer: A. Ayub Khan

Q13. The Constitution of 1973 contains a total of ___ Articles:

  1. A. 240
  2. B. 280
  3. C. 260
  4. D. 300
Answer: B. 280

Q14. Article 2 of the Constitution of Pakistan declares:

  1. A. Urdu as the national language
  2. B. Pakistan a unitary state
  3. C. English as the official language
  4. D. Islam as the state religion
Answer: D. Islam as the state religion

Q15. Article 6 of the Constitution of Pakistan deals with:

  1. A. High treason
  2. B. Freedom of speech
  3. C. Right to education
  4. D. Dissolution of assemblies
Answer: A. High treason

Q16. Article 25 of the Constitution of Pakistan guarantees:

  1. A. Equality of citizens before law
  2. B. Freedom of trade
  3. C. Freedom of religion
  4. D. Right to information
Answer: A. Equality of citizens before law

Q17. Freedom of speech and expression is guaranteed by Article:

  1. A. 16
  2. B. 17
  3. C. 19
  4. D. 20
Answer: C. 19

Q18. Under Article 45, the President of Pakistan has the power to:

  1. A. Grant pardon and remit sentences
  2. B. Dissolve the Senate
  3. C. Appoint judges directly
  4. D. Impose taxes
Answer: A. Grant pardon and remit sentences

Q19. An Ordinance promulgated by the President under Article 89 remains in force for a maximum of:

  1. A. 30 days
  2. B. 60 days
  3. C. 120 days
  4. D. 90 days
Answer: C. 120 days

Q20. Article 13 of the Constitution protects a person against:

  1. A. Preventive detention
  2. B. Taxation without law
  3. C. Expropriation of property
  4. D. Double jeopardy (being tried twice for the same offence)
Answer: D. Double jeopardy (being tried twice for the same offence)

Q21. Under Article 10 of the Constitution, an arrested person must be produced before a magistrate within:

  1. A. 12 hours
  2. B. 48 hours
  3. C. 24 hours
  4. D. 72 hours
Answer: C. 24 hours

Q22. A Money Bill can be introduced only in the:

  1. A. Senate
  2. B. Provincial Assembly
  3. C. National Assembly
  4. D. Joint sitting of Parliament

Q23. Members of the Senate of Pakistan are elected for a term of:
