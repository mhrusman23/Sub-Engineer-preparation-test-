Q1. The primary goal of financial management is to:

  1. A. Maximise sales
  2. B. Maximise shareholder wealth
  3. C. Minimise all risk
  4. D. Maximise staff numbers
Answer: B. Maximise shareholder wealth

Q2. The cost of capital (WACC) is used as:

  1. A. A tax rate
  2. B. The discount rate for evaluating investments
  3. C. A salary scale
  4. D. An exchange rate
Answer: B. The discount rate for evaluating investments

Q3. NPV of a project is positive when:

  1. A. It earns exactly the required return
  2. B. It earns more than the required rate of return
  3. C. Cash flows are negative
  4. D. The payback is long
Answer: B. It earns more than the required rate of return

Q4. A company's leverage increases when it uses more:

  1. A. Equity
  2. B. Debt financing
  3. C. Cash
  4. D. Inventory
Answer: B. Debt financing

Q5. The current ratio measures:

  1. A. Profitability
  2. B. Short-term liquidity (current assets / current liabilities)
  3. C. Debt level
  4. D. Growth
Answer: B. Short-term liquidity (current assets / current liabilities)

Q6. Hedging with derivatives is used to:

  1. A. Gamble on prices
  2. B. Reduce exposure to risks such as currency or interest movements
  3. C. Avoid taxes
  4. D. Increase debt
Answer: B. Reduce exposure to risks such as currency or interest movements

Q7. Working capital management focuses on:

  1. A. Long-term assets
  2. B. Current assets and current liabilities
  3. C. Share issues
  4. D. Mergers
Answer: B. Current assets and current liabilities

Q8. If a project's IRR is greater than the cost of capital, the project should generally be:

  1. A. Rejected
  2. B. Accepted
  3. C. Ignored
  4. D. Delayed forever
Answer: B. Accepted

Q9. Dividend policy decides:

  1. A. Product prices
  2. B. How much profit is paid to shareholders vs retained
  3. C. Staff bonuses
  4. D. Loan amounts
Answer: B. How much profit is paid to shareholders vs retained

Q10. Ethics in finance requires managers to:

  1. A. Hide losses
  2. B. Act honestly and in stakeholders' interests
  3. C. Maximise bonuses
  4. D. Avoid disclosure
Answer: B. Act honestly and in stakeholders' interests

Q11. Which CMA exam section is primarily dedicated to evaluating basic financial statement ratios, profitability analysis, and special reporting issues?

  1. A. Financial Statement Analysis
  2. B. Cost Management
  3. C. Internal Controls
  4. D. Enterprise Risk Management
Answer: A. Financial Statement Analysis

Q12. Under cost-volume-profit analysis, the sales volume required to cover all fixed and variable costs with zero profit is known as the:

  1. A. Margin of safety point
  2. B. Operating leverage threshold
  3. C. Break-even point
  4. D. Contribution margin limit
Answer: C. Break-even point

Q13. Which financial metric represents the proportion of total assets financed through creditors rather than shareholders' equity?

  1. A. Asset turnover ratio
  2. B. Debt-to-assets ratio
  3. C. Quick ratio
  4. D. Gross profit margin
Answer: B. Debt-to-assets ratio

Q14. In capital budgeting, which evaluation method calculates the exact discount rate that equates the present value of cash inflows with the initial cash outflow?

  1. A. Net Present Value
  2. B. Internal Rate of Return
  3. C. Payback Period
  4. D. Accounting Rate of Return
Answer: B. Internal Rate of Return

Q15. Enterprise risk management (ERM) primarily focuses on:

  1. A. Calculating daily employee payroll taxes
  2. B. Managing risks across the entire organization to achieve strategic objectives
  3. C. Determining short-term inventory reorder quantities
  4. D. Preparing monthly external financial statement disclosures
Answer: B. Managing risks across the entire organization to achieve strategic objectives

Q16. In working capital management, the primary goal of optimizing inventory levels is to:

  1. A. Maximize long-term bond yields
  2. B. Minimize holding and shortage costs while maintaining operational efficiency
  3. C. Increase corporate tax liabilities
  4. D. Eliminate the need for fixed asset depreciation
Answer: B. Minimize holding and shortage costs while maintaining operational efficiency

Q17. Which of the following corporate finance metrics assesses the variability of returns associated with a specific asset relative to the entire market?

  1. A. Beta coefficient
  2. B. Current ratio
  3. C. Degree of operating leverage
  4. D. Times interest earned
Answer: A. Beta coefficient

Q18. In a multi‑product break‑even analysis, the contribution margin ratio for Product A is 40% and for Product B is 30%. If the sales mix is 60% A and 40% B, what is the overall contribution margin ratio?

  1. A. 32%
  2. B. 34%
  3. C. 36%
  4. D. 38%
Answer: C. 36%

Q19. A company’s operating leverage is calculated using which of the following ratios?

  1. A. Contribution margin divided by operating income
  2. B. Fixed operating costs divided by total sales
  3. C. Variable costs divided by total assets
  4. D. Operating income divided by net sales
Answer: A. Contribution margin divided by operating income

Q20. Which of the following best describes the primary objective of a cash budgeting exercise for a multinational corporation?

  1. A. To determine the optimal capital structure
  2. B. To forecast future cash inflows and outflows for liquidity management
  3. C. To calculate the weighted average cost of capital
  4. D. To evaluate long‑term investment projects
Answer: B. To forecast future cash inflows and outflows for liquidity management

Q21. The primary purpose of implementing a balanced scorecard in strategic management is to:

  1. A. Solely monitor short-term financial budget variances
  2. B. Link organizational strategy to a comprehensive set of financial and non-financial performance measures
  3. C. Determine the optimal capital structure for tax efficiency
  4. D. Calculate precise product line contribution margins
Answer: B. Link organizational strategy to a comprehensive set of financial and non-financial performance measures

Q22. In capital budgeting, the profitability index (PI) of a project is calculated as:

  1. A. Initial investment divided by annual cash inflows
  2. B. Present value of future cash inflows divided by initial investment
  3. C. Net present value divided by the cost of capital
  4. D. Internal rate of return minus the hurdle rate
Answer: B. Present value of future cash inflows divided by initial investment

Q23. Economic Value Added (EVA) is best defined as:

  1. A. Net operating profit after taxes minus the total cost of capital
  2. B. Total revenue minus variable operating costs
  3. C. Gross profit divided by average operating assets
  4. D. Cash flow from operations minus capital expenditures
Answer: A. Net operating profit after taxes minus the total cost of capital

Q24. Residual income is calculated as:

  1. A. Operating income minus (Target rate of return multiplied by invested capital)
  2. B. Net income minus total dividends paid to common shareholders
  3. C. Gross margin minus administrative and selling expenses
  4. D. Cash flow from operations minus depreciation expense
Answer: A. Operating income minus (Target rate of return multiplied by invested capital)

Q25. The degree of operating leverage (DOL) measures the sensitivity of a firm's:

  1. A. Earnings per share to changes in sales
  2. B. Operating income to changes in sales volume
  3. C. Sales revenue to changes in advertising expense
  4. D. Interest expense to changes in total debt
Answer: B. Operating income to changes in sales volume
