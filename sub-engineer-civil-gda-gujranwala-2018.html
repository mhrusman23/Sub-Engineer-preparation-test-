Q1. Difference of back bearing and fore bearing of a line in WCB system......

  1. A. 180°
  2. B. 360°
  3. C. 90°
  4. D. 270°
Answer: A. 180°

Q2. The instrument used to measure horizontal and vertical distances in plane table......

  1. A. Plane alidade
  2. B. u-frame
  3. C. telescopic alidade
  4. D. clinometers
Answer: C. telescopic alidade

Q3. The first reading of staff at a point or bench mark is called......

  1. A. Back sight
  2. B. fore sight
  3. C. any one
  4. D. inter sight
Answer: A. Back sight

Q4. Focusing is done to remove.....

  1. A. parallax
  2. B. Shade
  3. C. magnification
  4. D. all of these
Answer: A. parallax

Q5. Leveling operation always starts from a.....

  1. A. T.L
  2. B. C.P
  3. C. B.M
  4. D. datum line
Answer: C. B.M

Q6. Red color of brick depends upon......

  1. A. alumina
  2. B. Sand
  3. C. burning
  4. D. iron oxide
Answer: D. iron oxide

Q7. Gypsum is added to cement at the time of manufacturing for the purpose of....

  1. A. increase the weight
  2. B. increase the setting time
  3. C. Increase strength
  4. D. Decrease the setting time
Answer: B. increase the setting time

Q8. Sand is suitable for concrete work......

  1. A. medium sand
  2. B. Fine sand
  3. C. graded sand
  4. D. coarse sand
Answer: C. graded sand

Q9. To gain better strength such sand is considered suitable for preparing mortar......

  1. A. course
  2. B. Fine
  3. C. graded
  4. D. medium
Answer: C. graded

Q10. Ratio between ultimate and safe bearing capacities of soil is termed as....

  1. A. Bearing ratio
  2. B. safe ratio
  3. C. factor of safety
  4. D. safe ultimate ratio
Answer: C. factor of safety

Q11. Water bearing strata....

  1. A. deep well
  2. B. lake
  3. C. Sea
  4. D. aquifer
Answer: D. aquifer

Q12. The curve which is introduced at the point of change of gradient is called....

  1. A. transition curve
  2. B. compound curve
  3. C. Simple curve
  4. D. vertical curve
Answer: D. vertical curve

Q13. Curve of varying radius is called....

  1. A. vertical curve
  2. B. transition curve
  3. C. compound curve
  4. D. Simple curve
Answer: B. transition curve

Q14. Small temporary wall or fixing board in front of walls for guidance during setting out for any structure is called....

  1. A. Sign board
  2. B. toe-wall
  3. C. dwarf wall
  4. D. profile
Answer: D. profile

Q15. The transverse slope of the road is called....

  1. A. side slope
  2. B. super elevation
  3. C. camber
  4. D. Gradient
Answer: C. camber

Q16. Volume of one bag of cement is....

  1. A. 3 cft
  2. B. 2.5 cft
  3. C. 1.25 cft
  4. D. 1.5 cft
Answer: C. 1.25 cft

Q17. Number of bricks taken in 100 cft brick work....

  1. A. 1350
  2. B. 1400
  3. C. 500
  4. D. 1000
Answer: A. 1350

Q18. The vertical members of the frame of door are called....

  1. A. posts
  2. B. sills
  3. C. styles
  4. D. Heads
Answer: A. posts

Q19. The concrete roads are also called.....

  1. A. flexible road
  2. B. country road
  3. C. urban road
  4. D. Rigid road
Answer: D. Rigid road

Q20. Measuring unit of moment of inertia in MKS system....

  1. A. cm3
  2. B. cm4
  3. C. m4
  4. D. in4
Answer: C. m4

Q21. MOI of a square is....

  1. A. D4/36
  2. B. D4/12
  3. C. D4/6
  4. D. D4/24
Answer: B. D4/12

Q22. Negative bending moment shows....

  1. A. jogging
  2. B. Hogging
  3. C. sagging
  4. D. tearing
Answer: B. Hogging

Q23. When shear force at a point of beam is zero, the B.M is....

  1. A. average
  2. B. Maximum
  3. C. zero
  4. D. minimum
Answer: B. Maximum

Q24. The property of material by virtue of which it disappears the deformation after removal of external load....

  1. A. elasticity
  2. B. Plasticity
  3. C. toughness
  4. D. rigidity
Answer: A. elasticity

Q25. Ratio of stress to strain with in elastic limit......

  1. A. young's modulus
  2. B. modulus of rigidity
  3. C. section modulus
  4. D. bulk modulus
Answer: A. young's modulus
