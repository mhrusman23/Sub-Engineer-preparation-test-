Q1. The length of one link of Gunter chain is....

  1. A. 2 cm
  2. B. 2 dm
  3. C. 0.66'
  4. D. 1'
Answer: C. 0.66'

Q2. The principle of plane table is....

  1. A. Triangulation
  2. B. Traversing
  3. C. Parallelism
  4. D. Chaining
Answer: C. Parallelism

Q3. The device which used to measure the depth of deep water....

  1. A. current meter
  2. B. Sounding pole
  3. C. Sounding rod
  4. D. echo sounder
Answer: D. echo sounder

Q4. The lower most line used for lettering....

  1. A. Cap line
  2. B. drop line
  3. C. base line
  4. D. waist line
Answer: B. drop line

Q5. A section in which details are shown quarterly....

  1. A. outline section
  2. B. auxiliary section
  3. C. broken section
  4. D. Half section

  1. A. auxiliary section
  2. B. off-set section
  3. C. Revolved section
  4. D. phantom section
Answer: D. phantom section

Q7. The roof which slopes in four directions......

  1. A. gable
  2. B. hipped
  3. C. gambrel

Q8. The lower edge of sloping roof is termed....
