Q1. At standard temperature and pressure the volume per mass is called

  1. A. Specific volume
  2. B. Specific weight
  3. C. Density
  4. D. Specific gravity
Answer: A. Specific volume

Q2. Weight per unit volume of a liquid is called

  1. A. Density
  2. B. Specific weight
  3. C. Specific gravity
  4. D. Specific volume
Answer: B. Specific weight

Q3. Mass per unit volume of a liquid is called

  1. A. Specific volume
  2. B. Specific weight
  3. C. Specific gravity
  4. D. Density
Answer: D. Density

Q4. The ratio of the specific weight of a liquid to the specific weight of water is called

  1. A. Density
  2. B. Gravity
  3. C. Specific weight
  4. D. Specific gravity
Answer: D. Specific gravity

Q5. Specific gravity of water is taken as

  1. A. Unity
  2. B. Less than unity
  3. C. More than unity
  4. D. Not equal to unity
Answer: A. Unity

Q6. Specific gravity is expressed in

  1. A. kg/m³
  2. B. N/m³
  3. C. gram/cm³
  4. D. Nil
Answer: D. Nil

Q7. All liquids keep their surface

  1. A. Level
  2. B. Concave
  3. C. Uneven
  4. D. Convex
Answer: A. Level

Q8. The specific weight of water is taken as

  1. A. One ml/cm³
  2. B. 1000 kg/cm³
  3. C. 9810 N/m³
  4. D. Unity
Answer: C. 9810 N/m³

Q9. The surface tension of liquids is due to

  1. A. Pressure of atmosphere
  2. B. Weight of liquid
  3. C. Cohesion of molecules
  4. D. Adhesion of molecules
Answer: C. Cohesion of molecules

Q10. Thick liquids have ___ value of viscosity

  1. A. Equal to water
  2. B. More
  3. C. Less
  4. D. No viscosity
Answer: B. More

Q11. The viscosity of water at 20°C is

  1. A. 1.0 centi poise
  2. B. 1.65 centi poise
  3. C. 0.80 centi poise
  4. D. 2.25 centi poise
Answer: A. 1.0 centi poise

Q12. The property of a liquid which controls its rate of flow is

  1. A. Viscosity
  2. B. Sp. gravity
  3. C. Surface tension
  4. D. Cohesion
Answer: A. Viscosity

Q13. Atmospheric pressure is measured by

  1. A. Barometer
  2. B. Piezometer
  3. C. Manometer
  4. D. All of these
Answer: A. Barometer

Q14. Value of standard atmospheric pressure is taken as a column of water of height equal to

  1. A. 0.76 m
  2. B. 10.3 m
  3. C. 14.7 m
  4. D. 101.3 m
Answer: B. 10.3 m

Q15. Pressure of one Pascal (Pa) is equal to

  1. A. One Kg/m²
  2. B. 100 N/m²
  3. C. 100 Lb/in²
  4. D. One N/m²
Answer: D. One N/m²

Q16. Piezometer tube is used for

  1. A. To find velocity
  2. B. To find pressure
  3. C. To find Sp. gr.
  4. D. To find area
Answer: B. To find pressure

Q17. Pressure measured by manometer is called

  1. A. Atmospheric pressure
  2. B. Gauge pressure
  3. C. Absolute pressure
  4. D. All of these
Answer: B. Gauge pressure

Q18. Heavy liquid used in piezometer is

  1. A. Bromide
  2. B. Mercury
  3. C. Water
  4. D. None of these
Answer: D. None of these

Q19. Heavy liquid used in manometer is

  1. A. Water
  2. B. Bromide
  3. C. Mercury
  4. D. None of these
Answer: C. Mercury

Q20. Specific gravity of mercury is taken as

  1. A. 13.6
  2. B. 12.5
  3. C. 1.59
  4. D. 1
Answer: A. 13.6

Q21. One metre height of mercury is equal to the column height of water of

  1. A. 10 m
  2. B. 13.6 m
  3. C. 3.6 m
  4. D. 1 m
Answer: B. 13.6 m

Q22. Pressure below the atmospheric pressure is called

  1. A. Absolute pressure
  2. B. Positive pressure
  3. C. Vacuum
  4. D. Both a and b
Answer: C. Vacuum

Q23. The statement "All liquids exert pressure in all directions equally" is called

  1. A. Archimedes' law
  2. B. Pascal's law
  3. C. Newton's law
  4. D. Engineering law
Answer: B. Pascal's law

Q24. The study of pressure exerted by liquids at rest is termed as

  1. A. Hydrostation
  2. B. Hydrostatics
  3. C. Hydrology
  4. D. All of these
Answer: B. Hydrostatics

Q25. A square plate is immersed vertically in water. If it is turned horizontally at the same centroidal depth, the pressure on it will be

  1. A. Decreased
  2. B. Increased
  3. C. Same
  4. D. None of these
Answer: C. Same
