Q1. In frame structure building walls are used as ________ type.

  1. A. Central
  2. B. Shear walls
  3. C. Load bearing
  4. D. Non load bearing
Answer: D. Non load bearing

Q2. Load of building is transmitted through beams and ________ in frame structure building.

  1. A. Deck
  2. B. Columns
  3. C. Walls
  4. D. Piles
Answer: B. Columns

Q3. Maximum limit of percentage of steel in R.C.C structures is ________ by volume.

  1. A. 8
  2. B. 15
  3. C. 10
  4. D. 4
Answer: A. 8

Q4. Minimum ratio of concrete used with steel reinforcement is normally:

  1. A. (1:2:4)
  2. B. (1:1.5:3)
  3. C. (1:1:2)
  4. D. (1:3:6)
Answer: A. (1:2:4)

Q5. Volume of sloping portion of column base is mostly determined by ________ formula.

  1. A. Any one
  2. B. Quadratic
  3. C. Prismoidal
  4. D. Ordinate
Answer: C. Prismoidal

Q6. Vertical and horizontal sides of column base are equal in length in case of angle of inclination of ________

  1. A. 45°
  2. B. 75°
  3. C. 60°
  4. D. 30°
Answer: A. 45°

Q7. Vertical side of sloping side of column base is one meter and angle of inclination is 45°, then horizontal side will be ________

  1. A. 1.5 m
  2. B. 1.707 m
  3. C. 1 m
  4. D. 2 m
Answer: C. 1 m

Q8. Vertical side of sloping side of column base is one meter and angle of inclination is 60°, then horizontal side will be ________

  1. A. 0.707
  2. B. 0.58 m
  3. C. 1 m
  4. D. 1.5 m
Answer: B. 0.58 m

Q9. Extra length of steel bar is taken as ________ due to hook.

  1. A. 4D
  2. B. 9D
  3. C. 7.5D
  4. D. 6D
Answer: B. 9D

Q10. No extra length due to hook is provided to the steel bar in case of ________

  1. A. Plain steel
  2. B. Deformed
  3. C. Tar steel
  4. D. b and c
Answer: D. b and c

Q11. Truss is suitable to support the load of roofs of ________ span.

  1. A. Middle
  2. B. Longer
  3. C. Shorter
  4. D. All of these
Answer: B. Longer

Q12. For longer span of sloping roofs the most suitable supporting member is a:

  1. A. T-beam
  2. B. L-beam
  3. C. Steel beam
  4. D. Truss
Answer: D. Truss

Q13. Highest line at meeting of two slopes of truss is called:

  1. A. Valley
  2. B. Key
  3. C. Crown
  4. D. Ridge
Answer: D. Ridge

Q14. Lowest edge of a sloping roof is called:

  1. A. Toe
  2. B. Springer
  3. C. Eave
  4. D. Base
Answer: C. Eave

Q15. Outer support which receives the load of sloping roof is called ________ rafter.

  1. A. Common
  2. B. Principal
  3. C. Hip
  4. D. Jack
Answer: B. Principal

Q16. A rafter of a truss which directly receives the load of the roof is called:

  1. A. Jack
  2. B. Common
  3. C. Hip
  4. D. Principal
Answer: B. Common

Q17. Triangular shaped side wall of a sloping roof is called:

  1. A. Gable
  2. B. Truss
  3. C. Gambrel
  4. D. Toe
Answer: A. Gable

Q18. Tables prepared in which different properties of steel sections are shown are called:

  1. A. Steel table
  2. B. Properties table
  3. C. Section table
  4. D. Data table
Answer: A. Steel table

Q19. Density of mild steel is taken as ________ kg/m³.

  1. A. 7680
  2. B. 7850
  3. C. 7200
  4. D. 8910
Answer: B. 7850

Q20. Weight for rivets is generally added in the estimate of trusses as ________ % of total weight.

  1. A. 4
  2. B. 5
  3. C. 2
  4. D. 3
Answer: B. 5

Q21. A bridge having a span less than 6 meter is called:

  1. A. Causeway
  2. B. Syphon
  3. C. Culvert
  4. D. Bridge
Answer: C. Culvert

Q22. End supports of bridge which are constructed parallel to flow of water are called:

  1. A. Abutment
  2. B. Wing wall
  3. C. Toe wall
  4. D. Pier
Answer: A. Abutment

Q23. Middle supports of bridge constructed parallel to flow of water are called:

  1. A. Toe wall
  2. B. Abutment
  3. C. Pier
  4. D. Wing wall
Answer: C. Pier

Q24. A bridge which is not at right angle to the direction of flow of water in plan is called:

  1. A. Skew
  2. B. High level
  3. C. Low level
  4. D. Deck
Answer: A. Skew

Q25. Abutment and piers are considered as ________ parts of a bridge.

  1. A. Sub-structure
  2. B. Base
  3. C. Super structure
  4. D. Compulsory
Answer: A. Sub-structure
