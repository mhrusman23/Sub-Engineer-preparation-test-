Q1. In frame structure building walls are used as ________ type.

  1. A. Load bearing
  2. B. Shear walls
  3. C. Non load bearing
  4. D. Central
Answer: C. Non load bearing

Q2. A bridge having a span less than 6 meter is called:

  1. A. Syphon
  2. B. Bridge
  3. C. Culvert
  4. D. Causeway
Answer: C. Culvert

Q3. Overall cost of any item of work is determined per unit quantity of work by:

  1. A. Analysis of rates
  2. B. Quotation
  3. C. Tender
  4. D. Estimate
Answer: A. Analysis of rates

Q4. Which of the following is included in building services?

  1. A. Water supply
  2. B. Gas fitting
  3. C. Sanitary fittings
  4. D. All of these
Answer: D. All of these
