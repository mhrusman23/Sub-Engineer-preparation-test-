Q1. Which of the following tests is used to determine the toughness of road aggregates?

  1. A. Los Angeles abrasion test
  2. B. Impact test
  3. C. Soundness test
  4. D. Crushing test
Answer: B. Impact test

Q2. Which type of roller is most suitable for the compaction of cohesive soils like clay?

  1. A. Smooth wheel roller
  2. B. Pneumatic tired roller
  3. C. Sheepfoot roller
  4. D. Vibratory roller
Answer: C. Sheepfoot roller

Q3. The layer of a flexible pavement directly below the surface course is the:

  1. A. Subgrade
  2. B. Base course
  3. C. Shoulder
  4. D. Sub-base
Answer: B. Base course

Q4. The camber of a road is provided for:

  1. A. Braking
  2. B. Reducing cost
  3. C. Increasing speed
  4. D. Drainage of rainwater
Answer: D. Drainage of rainwater

Q5. The CBR test is used to evaluate:

  1. A. Cement fineness
  2. B. Aggregate shape
  3. C. Strength of subgrade soil
  4. D. Bitumen quality
Answer: C. Strength of subgrade soil

Q6. Superelevation is provided on:

  1. A. Horizontal curves
  2. B. Straight sections
  3. C. Footpaths
  4. D. Intersections only
Answer: A. Horizontal curves

Q7. The binder used in flexible pavement is:

  1. A. Cement
  2. B. Bitumen
  3. C. Lime
  4. D. Gypsum
Answer: B. Bitumen

Q8. A rigid pavement is constructed with:

  1. A. Gravel
  2. B. Cement concrete
  3. C. Bituminous mix
  4. D. Sand
Answer: B. Cement concrete

Q9. Stopping sight distance is the distance needed to:

  1. A. Overtake a vehicle
  2. B. Stop a vehicle after seeing an obstruction
  3. C. Park a vehicle
  4. D. Turn around
Answer: B. Stop a vehicle after seeing an obstruction

Q10. The Los Angeles abrasion test is for:

  1. A. Soil
  2. B. Cement
  3. C. Aggregates
  4. D. Bitumen
Answer: C. Aggregates

Q11. The right of way of a road is the:

  1. A. Width of shoulder
  2. B. Width of carriageway only
  3. C. Total land width acquired for the road
  4. D. Width of footpath
Answer: C. Total land width acquired for the road

Q12. The gradient of a road is expressed as:

  1. A. Width per length
  2. B. Time per length
  3. C. Rise per unit length
  4. D. Area per length
Answer: C. Rise per unit length

Q13. Which of the following is a typical design speed for a rural two-lane highway in Pakistan?

  1. A. 60 km/h
  2. B. 75 km/h
  3. C. 90 km/h
  4. D. 105 km/h
Answer: B. 75 km/h

Q14. Which test measures the resistance of a pavement surface to abrasion?

  1. A. California Bearing Ratio test
  2. B. Los Angeles abrasion test
  3. C. Proctor compaction test
  4. D. Skid resistance test
Answer: B. Los Angeles abrasion test

Q15. What is the primary purpose of a drainage ditch alongside a highway?

  1. A. To increase the number of lanes
  2. B. To provide a place for maintenance vehicles
  3. C. To remove excess water from the road surface
  4. D. To serve as a safety buffer for pedestrians
Answer: C. To remove excess water from the road surface

Q16. What is the main function of a shoulder on a highway?

  1. A. To provide a lane for emergency vehicles only
  2. B. To allow vehicles to overtake safely
  3. C. To serve as a buffer zone for pedestrians and cyclists
  4. D. To support the structural integrity of the pavement
Answer: C. To serve as a buffer zone for pedestrians and cyclists

Q17. Which parameter is used to determine the required thickness of a flexible pavement base layer?

  1. A. Cohesion of subgrade soil
  2. B. Group Index of the subgrade
  3. C. Skid resistance of the surface course
  4. D. Type of aggregate used
Answer: B. Group Index of the subgrade

Q18. In a multilane highway, what does the term 'median' refer to?

  1. A. The central lane for high-speed traffic
  2. B. A raised strip separating opposing traffic flows
  3. C. The width of the shoulder on each side
  4. D. The elevation difference between the two sides of the road
Answer: B. A raised strip separating opposing traffic flows
