Q1. A rectifier converts:

  1. A. AC to DC
  2. B. DC to AC
  3. C. DC to DC
  4. D. AC to AC
Answer: A. AC to DC

Q2. An inverter converts:

  1. A. AC to AC
  2. B. DC to AC
  3. C. AC to DC
  4. D. DC to DC
Answer: B. DC to AC

Q3. A thyristor is also known as a:

  1. A. Transistor only
  2. B. LED
  3. C. Silicon controlled rectifier
  4. D. Zener diode
Answer: C. Silicon controlled rectifier

Q4. A chopper converts:

  1. A. AC to AC
  2. B. Fixed DC to variable DC
  3. C. DC to AC
  4. D. AC to DC
Answer: B. Fixed DC to variable DC

Q5. The device that conducts in one direction only is a:

  1. A. Diode
  2. B. Resistor
  3. C. Capacitor
  4. D. Inductor
Answer: A. Diode

Q6. Which device is a fully controlled switch?

  1. A. Zener
  2. B. Diode
  3. C. Rectifier bridge
  4. D. MOSFET
Answer: D. MOSFET

Q7. A cycloconverter converts:

  1. A. DC to AC
  2. B. AC at one frequency to AC at another
  3. C. DC to DC
  4. D. AC to DC
Answer: B. AC at one frequency to AC at another

Q8. A full-wave bridge rectifier uses how many diodes?

  1. A. 4
  2. B. 3
  3. C. 1
  4. D. 2
Answer: A. 4

Q9. The main advantage of switching regulators is high:

  1. A. Noise
  2. B. Efficiency
  3. C. Loss
  4. D. Heat
Answer: B. Efficiency

Q10. PWM stands for pulse width:

  1. A. Movement
  2. B. Management
  3. C. Measurement
  4. D. Modulation
Answer: D. Modulation

Q11. Which power semiconductor device possesses current-controlled latching characteristics and requires a gate pulse for turn-on?

  1. A. Power MOSFET
  2. B. Silicon Controlled Rectifier
  3. C. IGBT
  4. D. Schottky Diode
Answer: B. Silicon Controlled Rectifier

Q12. What is the primary function of a freewheeling diode connected across an inductive DC motor load in a converter circuit?

  1. A. To increase the supply voltage
  2. B. To provide a discharge path for stored inductive energy
  3. C. To convert AC into DC
  4. D. To block reverse current from the source
Answer: B. To provide a discharge path for stored inductive energy

Q13. In a single-phase half-wave controlled rectifier with a purely resistive load, the maximum firing angle delay is:

  1. A. 90 degrees
  2. B. 180 degrees
  3. C. 270 degrees
  4. D. 360 degrees
Answer: B. 180 degrees

Q14. Which electronic switch combines the high input impedance of a MOSFET and the low on-state conduction losses of a BJT?

  1. A. DIAC
  2. B. TRIAC
  3. C. IGBT
  4. D. UJT
Answer: C. IGBT

Q15. What type of converter is utilized to transform a fixed DC voltage directly into a variable DC voltage level?

  1. A. Rectifier
  2. B. Inverter
  3. C. Chopper
  4. D. Cycloconverter
Answer: C. Chopper

Q16. Which power semiconductor device is fundamentally bidirectional and capable of conducting current in both polarities?

  1. A. SCR
  2. B. TRIAC
  3. C. GTO
  4. D. Power Diode
Answer: B. TRIAC
