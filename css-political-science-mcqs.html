Q1. Who wrote 'The Republic'?

  1. A. Aristotle
  2. B. Plato
  3. C. Machiavelli
  4. D. Locke
Answer: B. Plato

Q2. The concept of the 'social contract' is associated with:

  1. A. Marx only
  2. B. Hobbes, Locke and Rousseau
  3. C. Plato only
  4. D. Weber only
Answer: B. Hobbes, Locke and Rousseau

Q3. Democracy literally means:

  1. A. Rule by the rich
  2. B. Rule by the people
  3. C. Rule by one
  4. D. Rule by the army
Answer: B. Rule by the people

Q4. Montesquieu is famous for the theory of:

  1. A. Class struggle
  2. B. Separation of powers
  3. C. Divine right
  4. D. Social contract
Answer: B. Separation of powers

Q5. A unitary state is one where:

  1. A. Power is divided with provinces
  2. B. All governing power is concentrated in the central government
  3. C. There are two presidents
  4. D. States are independent
Answer: B. All governing power is concentrated in the central government

Q6. The book 'The Prince' was written by:

  1. A. Machiavelli
  2. B. Hobbes
  3. C. Mill
  4. D. Bentham
Answer: B. Hobbes

Q7. Political culture refers to:

  1. A. Election results
  2. B. The attitudes, beliefs and values people hold about politics
  3. C. Party funds
  4. D. Voting machines
Answer: B. The attitudes, beliefs and values people hold about politics

Q8. An oligarchy is rule by:

  1. A. The majority
  2. B. A small group of people
  3. C. One person
  4. D. The military only
Answer: B. A small group of people

Q9. John Locke argued that government is legitimate only with:

  1. A. Divine right
  2. B. The consent of the governed
  3. C. Military power
  4. D. Wealth
Answer: B. The consent of the governed

Q10. A pressure group differs from a political party because it:

  1. A. Contests elections
  2. B. Seeks to influence policy without seeking to govern
  3. C. Has no members
  4. D. Is illegal
Answer: B. Seeks to influence policy without seeking to govern

Q11. The theory of the separation of powers into executive, legislative, and judicial branches was prominently advanced by:

  1. A. Montesquieu
  2. B. Voltaire
  3. C. Aristotle
  4. D. Plato
Answer: A. Montesquieu

Q12. A political system where ultimate power rests with the people and is exercised directly or through elected representatives is known as:

  1. A. Monarchy
  2. B. Oligarchy
  3. C. Democracy
  4. D. Theocracy
Answer: C. Democracy

Q13. John Locke argued that legitimate government is fundamentally based upon:

  1. A. Divine right of kings
  2. B. The consent of the governed
  3. C. Absolute military power
  4. D. Hereditary succession
Answer: B. The consent of the governed

Q14. The term 'political culture' is best described as:

  1. A. The legal framework of the constitution
  2. B. The set of attitudes, beliefs, and sentiments that give order and meaning to a political process
  3. C. The administrative budget of a state
  4. D. The electoral map of a country
Answer: B. The set of attitudes, beliefs, and sentiments that give order and meaning to a political process

Q15. The book 'Leviathan', establishing the foundation of social contract theory through an absolute sovereign, was authored by:

  1. A. John Locke
  2. B. Thomas Hobbes
  3. C. Jean-Jacques Rousseau
  4. D. Karl Marx
Answer: B. Thomas Hobbes

Q16. The primary objective of a pressure group, unlike a political party, is to:

  1. A. Win elections and form government
  2. B. Influence public policy and government decisions
  3. C. Appoint judges to the supreme court
  4. D. Collect taxes from citizens
Answer: B. Influence public policy and government decisions

Q17. What is the main function of the National Assembly in Pakistan?

  1. A. To interpret laws
  2. B. To execute laws
  3. C. To legislate and approve the national budget
  4. D. To adjudicate disputes between provinces
Answer: C. To legislate and approve the national budget

Q18. Which of the following is NOT a feature of a unitary state?

  1. A. Centralized authority
  2. B. Uniform laws across all regions
  3. C. Decentralized autonomous provinces
  4. D. Direct control over local governments
Answer: C. Decentralized autonomous provinces

Q19. The principle of separation of powers divides government into how many branches?

  1. A. Two
  2. B. Three
  3. C. Four
  4. D. Five
Answer: B. Three

Q20. In a parliamentary system, the head of government is usually chosen by which body?

  1. A. The President
  2. B. The Supreme Court
  3. C. The elected parliament
  4. D. The provincial assemblies
Answer: C. The elected parliament

Q21. Which document is considered the supreme law of Pakistan?

  1. A. The Constitution of 1973
  2. B. The National Development Plan
  3. C. The Basic Principles of Governance
  4. D. The Civil Service Regulations
Answer: A. The Constitution of 1973

Q22. Who authored the notable philosophical book titled 'The Reconstruction of Religious Thought in Islam'?

  1. A. Sir Syed Ahmed Khan
  2. B. Allama Iqbal
  3. C. Maulana Abul Kalam Azad
  4. D. Shah Waliullah
Answer: B. Allama Iqbal

Q23. What term is used to describe the imperialistic policy of extending a nation's dominance and influence politically, economically, or culturally over other territories?

  1. A. Isolationism
  2. B. Imperialism
  3. C. Federalism
  4. D. Bilateralism
Answer: B. Imperialism

Q24. In the study of comparative politics, which framework emphasizes the distribution of administrative and legislative authority between central and provincial governments?

  1. A. Unitary System
  2. B. Federal System
  3. C. Dictatorial Regime
  4. D. Absolute Monarchy
Answer: B. Federal System

Q25. Which classic political philosopher is best recognized for articulating the concept of the absolute and indivisible sovereignty of the state in his work 'Six Books of the Commonwealth'?

  1. A. Jean Bodin
  2. B. Niccolò Machiavelli
  3. C. Jeremy Bentham
  4. D. Karl Marx
Answer: A. Jean Bodin
