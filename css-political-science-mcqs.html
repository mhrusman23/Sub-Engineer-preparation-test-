Q1. Who wrote 'The Republic'?

  1. A. Aristotle
  2. B. Plato
  3. C. Machiavelli
  4. D. Locke
Answer: B. Plato

Q2. The concept of the 'social contract' is associated with:

  1. A. Marx only
  2. B. Hobbes, Locke and Rousseau
  3. C. Plato only
  4. D. Weber only
Answer: B. Hobbes, Locke and Rousseau

Q3. Democracy literally means:

  1. A. Rule by the rich
  2. B. Rule by the people
  3. C. Rule by one
  4. D. Rule by the army
Answer: B. Rule by the people

Q4. Montesquieu is famous for the theory of:

  1. A. Class struggle
  2. B. Separation of powers
  3. C. Divine right
  4. D. Social contract
Answer: B. Separation of powers

Q5. A unitary state is one where:

  1. A. Power is divided with provinces
  2. B. All governing power is concentrated in the central government
  3. C. There are two presidents
  4. D. States are independent
Answer: B. All governing power is concentrated in the central government

Q6. The book 'The Prince' was written by:

  1. A. Machiavelli
  2. B. Hobbes
  3. C. Mill
  4. D. Bentham
Answer: B. Hobbes

Q7. Political culture refers to:

  1. A. Election results
  2. B. The attitudes, beliefs and values people hold about politics
  3. C. Party funds
  4. D. Voting machines
Answer: B. The attitudes, beliefs and values people hold about politics

Q8. An oligarchy is rule by:

  1. A. The majority
  2. B. A small group of people
  3. C. One person
  4. D. The military only
Answer: B. A small group of people

Q9. John Locke argued that government is legitimate only with:

  1. A. Divine right
  2. B. The consent of the governed
  3. C. Military power
  4. D. Wealth
Answer: B. The consent of the governed

Q10. A pressure group differs from a political party because it:

  1. A. Contests elections
  2. B. Seeks to influence policy without seeking to govern
  3. C. Has no members
  4. D. Is illegal
Answer: B. Seeks to influence policy without seeking to govern

Q11. Plato's ideal state was to be ruled by:

  1. A. Philosopher-kings
  2. B. Elected statesmen
  3. C. Military generals
  4. D. Wealthy merchants
Answer: A. Philosopher-kings

Q12. In Plato's ideal state, society was divided into:

  1. A. Three classes
  2. B. Two classes
  3. C. Four classes
  4. D. No classes at all
Answer: A. Three classes

Q13. Plato was a pupil of:

  1. A. Socrates
  2. B. Aristotle
  3. C. Pythagoras
  4. D. Heraclitus
Answer: A. Socrates

Q14. Plato founded his school, the Academy, in:

  1. A. Rome
  2. B. Sparta
  3. C. Athens
  4. D. Alexandria
Answer: C. Athens

Q15. Aristotle described man as a:

  1. A. Political animal
  2. B. Social animal only
  3. C. Economic animal
  4. D. Religious animal
Answer: A. Political animal

Q16. Aristotle's celebrated work on politics is titled:

  1. A. Politics
  2. B. The Prince
  3. C. Leviathan
  4. D. Utopia
Answer: A. Politics

Q17. Aristotle classified constitutions mainly according to:

  1. A. The number of rulers and the aim of rule
  2. B. The size of territory
  3. C. The wealth of citizens
  4. D. The strength of the army
Answer: A. The number of rulers and the aim of rule

Q18. Aristotle considered the best practicable state to be dominated by:

  1. A. The very rich
  2. B. The very poor
  3. C. The middle class
  4. D. The priestly class
Answer: C. The middle class

Q19. Aristotle was the teacher of:

  1. A. Alexander the Great
  2. B. Julius Caesar
  3. C. Pericles
  4. D. Augustus
Answer: A. Alexander the Great

Q20. According to Aristotle, 'polity' is the good form of rule by:

  1. A. One man
  2. B. The few
  3. C. The many
  4. D. The army
Answer: C. The many

Q21. Aristotle held that the chief cause of revolutions is:

  1. A. Inequality
  2. B. Foreign invasion
  3. C. Religion
  4. D. Climate
Answer: A. Inequality

Q22. Machiavelli is known for separating politics from:

  1. A. Morality and religion
  2. B. Economics
  3. C. Law
  4. D. History
Answer: A. Morality and religion

Q23. Machiavelli advised the prince to combine in himself the qualities of:

  1. A. The lion and the fox
  2. B. The wolf and the lamb
  3. C. The eagle and the dove
  4. D. The tiger and the deer
Answer: A. The lion and the fox

Q24. Machiavelli held that, if a ruler cannot be both, it is safer for him to be:

  1. A. Feared than loved
  2. B. Loved than feared
  3. C. Neither loved nor feared
  4. D. Loved by all equally
Answer: A. Feared than loved

Q25. Machiavelli's teaching is popularly summarised as:

  1. A. The end justifies the means
  2. B. Might is right
  3. C. Knowledge is power
  4. D. Unity is strength
Answer: A. The end justifies the means
