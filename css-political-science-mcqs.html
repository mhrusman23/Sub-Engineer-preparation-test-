Q1. Who wrote 'The Republic'?

  1. A. Aristotle
  2. B. Plato
  3. C. Machiavelli
  4. D. Locke
Answer: B. Plato

Q2. The concept of the 'social contract' is associated with:

  1. A. Marx only
  2. B. Hobbes, Locke and Rousseau
  3. C. Plato only
  4. D. Weber only
Answer: B. Hobbes, Locke and Rousseau

Q3. Democracy literally means:

  1. A. Rule by the rich
  2. B. Rule by the people
  3. C. Rule by one
  4. D. Rule by the army
Answer: B. Rule by the people

Q4. Montesquieu is famous for the theory of:

  1. A. Class struggle
  2. B. Separation of powers
  3. C. Divine right
  4. D. Social contract
Answer: B. Separation of powers

Q5. A unitary state is one where:

  1. A. Power is divided with provinces
  2. B. All governing power is concentrated in the central government
  3. C. There are two presidents
  4. D. States are independent
Answer: B. All governing power is concentrated in the central government

Q6. The book 'The Prince' was written by:

  1. A. Machiavelli
  2. B. Hobbes
  3. C. Mill
  4. D. Bentham
Answer: B. Hobbes

Q7. Political culture refers to:

  1. A. Election results
  2. B. The attitudes, beliefs and values people hold about politics
  3. C. Party funds
  4. D. Voting machines
Answer: B. The attitudes, beliefs and values people hold about politics

Q8. An oligarchy is rule by:

  1. A. The majority
  2. B. A small group of people
  3. C. One person
  4. D. The military only
Answer: B. A small group of people

Q9. John Locke argued that government is legitimate only with:

  1. A. Divine right
  2. B. The consent of the governed
  3. C. Military power
  4. D. Wealth
Answer: B. The consent of the governed

Q10. A pressure group differs from a political party because it:

  1. A. Contests elections
  2. B. Seeks to influence policy without seeking to govern
  3. C. Has no members
  4. D. Is illegal
Answer: B. Seeks to influence policy without seeking to govern
