Q1. The father of modern Public Administration as a discipline is considered:

  1. A. Max Weber
  2. B. Woodrow Wilson
  3. C. Herbert Simon
  4. D. F.W. Taylor
Answer: B. Woodrow Wilson

Q2. Bureaucracy, according to Weber, is characterised by:

  1. A. Personal favours
  2. B. Rules, hierarchy and written records
  3. C. No structure
  4. D. Hereditary posts
Answer: B. Rules, hierarchy and written records

Q3. POSDCORB — the functions of management — was given by:

  1. A. Fayol
  2. B. Luther Gulick
  3. C. Maslow
  4. D. Herzberg
Answer: B. Luther Gulick

Q4. New Public Management (NPM) emphasises:

  1. A. More rules
  2. B. Market-like practices, efficiency and results in government
  3. C. Bigger offices
  4. D. Lifetime jobs only
Answer: B. Market-like practices, efficiency and results in government

Q5. Accountability in administration means officials must:

  1. A. Hide decisions
  2. B. Answer for their actions and use of public resources
  3. C. Avoid audits
  4. D. Work independently of law
Answer: B. Answer for their actions and use of public resources

Q6. Decentralisation means:

  1. A. All power at the centre
  2. B. Transfer of authority to lower/local levels
  3. C. Closing local offices
  4. D. Privatisation
Answer: B. Transfer of authority to lower/local levels

Q7. Maslow's hierarchy of needs starts with:

  1. A. Self-actualisation
  2. B. Physiological needs
  3. C. Esteem
  4. D. Safety
Answer: B. Physiological needs

Q8. An ombudsman is an official who:

  1. A. Collects taxes
  2. B. Investigates citizens' complaints against administration
  3. C. Commands the police
  4. D. Writes laws
Answer: B. Investigates citizens' complaints against administration

Q9. E-governance uses technology to:

  1. A. Reduce services
  2. B. Deliver public services more efficiently and transparently
  3. C. Replace all officials
  4. D. Increase paperwork
Answer: B. Deliver public services more efficiently and transparently

Q10. Span of control refers to:

  1. A. Office size
  2. B. The number of subordinates one manager can effectively supervise
  3. C. Budget size
  4. D. Working hours
Answer: B. The number of subordinates one manager can effectively supervise
