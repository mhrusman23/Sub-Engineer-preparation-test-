Q1. Which element is essential for a valid simple contract?

  1. A. Registration
  2. B. A written document always
  3. C. Consideration
  4. D. A witness
Answer: C. Consideration

Q2. The case Salomon v Salomon established that a company has:

  1. A. Separate legal personality
  2. B. Only one shareholder
  3. C. Unlimited members
  4. D. No liability
Answer: A. Separate legal personality

Q3. Limited liability means a shareholder's liability is limited to:

  1. A. The unpaid amount on their shares
  2. B. Nothing at all
  3. C. Their total personal wealth
  4. D. The company's total debts
Answer: A. The unpaid amount on their shares

Q4. A false statement of fact that induces a person to enter a contract is a:

  1. A. Misrepresentation
  2. B. Consideration
  3. C. Counter-offer
  4. D. Acceptance
Answer: A. Misrepresentation

Q5. Which is NOT an element of the tort of negligence?

  1. A. Breach of duty
  2. B. Duty of care
  3. C. Resulting damage
  4. D. Intention to harm
Answer: D. Intention to harm

Q6. The ratio decidendi of a case is the:

  1. A. Judge's passing comment
  2. B. Name of the parties
  3. C. Legal principle on which the decision is based
  4. D. Amount of damages
Answer: C. Legal principle on which the decision is based

Q7. Statute law is made by the:

  1. A. Local police
  2. B. Arbitrators
  3. C. Legislature
  4. D. Company directors
Answer: C. Legislature

Q8. The usual remedy for breach of contract is:

  1. A. Damages
  2. B. Forfeiture of property
  3. C. Fine only
  4. D. Imprisonment
Answer: A. Damages

Q9. In a general partnership, partners usually have:

  1. A. Liability only for their own acts
  2. B. Limited liability
  3. C. No liability
  4. D. Unlimited personal liability
Answer: D. Unlimited personal liability

Q10. An offer can be ended by:

  1. A. Delay in posting
  2. B. A friend's comment
  3. C. Revocation before acceptance
  4. D. Silence of the offeree
Answer: C. Revocation before acceptance

Q11. What is the primary source of English common law?

  1. A. Acts of Parliament
  2. B. Judicial precedent
  3. C. European Union directives
  4. D. Customary international law
Answer: B. Judicial precedent

Q12. Which of the following is an essential element for a valid contract?

  1. A. Written documentation
  2. B. Notarization by a lawyer
  3. C. Consideration
  4. D. Approval by a government agency
Answer: C. Consideration

Q13. What type of authority does an appointed company director inherently possess to bind the company in business transactions?

  1. A. Actual authority only
  2. B. Apparent or ostensible authority
  3. C. No authority without shareholder vote
  4. D. Delegated authority only
Answer: B. Apparent or ostensible authority

Q14. Which type of agent's authority is created when a principal leads a third party to believe the agent has authority they do not actually possess?

  1. A. Actual express authority
  2. B. Actual implied authority
  3. C. Apparent or ostensible authority
  4. D. Ratified authority
Answer: C. Apparent or ostensible authority

Q15. Under company law, what document defines the internal management and administrative rules of a registered corporation?

  1. A. Memorandum of Association
  2. B. Articles of Association
  3. C. Prospectus
  4. D. Certificate of Incorporation
Answer: B. Articles of Association

Q16. What legal principle establishes that a registered company is a legal entity distinct from its shareholders?

  1. A. Ultra vires doctrine
  2. B. Separate legal personality
  3. C. Constructive notice
  4. D. Indoor management rule
Answer: B. Separate legal personality

Q17. When a company enters into a transaction that falls completely outside the objects clause of its constitutional documents, what is that act called?

  1. A. Ultra vires
  2. B. Intra vires
  3. C. Voidable contract
  4. D. Breach of fiduciary duty
Answer: A. Ultra vires

Q18. What is the minimum number of directors required for a public limited company under standard corporate governance frameworks?

  1. A. One
  2. B. Two
  3. C. Three
  4. D. Seven
Answer: B. Two

Q19. What legal document outlines the external powers and objectives of a registered company?

  1. A. Articles of Association
  2. B. Memorandum of Association
  3. C. Prospectus
  4. D. Shareholders Agreement
Answer: B. Memorandum of Association

Q20. What is the primary legal consequence of the 'veil of incorporation' in company law?

  1. A. Unlimited liability for all shareholders
  2. B. Separation of the company's legal personality from its members
  3. C. Automatic dissolution after five years
  4. D. Tax exemption on all corporate profits
Answer: B. Separation of the company's legal personality from its members

Q21. What resolution threshold is strictly required to alter a company's Articles of Association?

  1. A. Simple majority (>50%)
  2. B. Special resolution (not less than 75%)
  3. C. Unanimous consent (100%)
  4. D. Board of directors approval only
Answer: B. Special resolution (not less than 75%)

Q22. Which type of resolution requires a 75% majority of votes cast by eligible shareholders to be passed?

  1. A. Ordinary resolution
  2. B. Special resolution
  3. C. Board resolution
  4. D. Unanimous resolution
Answer: B. Special resolution

Q23. What term describes a false statement of fact made by one contracting party that induces the other party to enter into the contract?

  1. A. Mistake
  2. B. Misrepresentation
  3. C. Frustration
  4. D. Duress
Answer: B. Misrepresentation

Q24. What is the primary remedy available for a breach of contract under common law?

  1. A. Specific performance
  2. B. Damages
  3. C. Injunction
  4. D. Rescission
Answer: B. Damages

Q25. Which of the following describes consideration in contract law?

  1. A. A moral obligation
  2. B. Something of value given in exchange for a promise
  3. C. A written formal document
  4. D. An intention to create legal relations
Answer: B. Something of value given in exchange for a promise
