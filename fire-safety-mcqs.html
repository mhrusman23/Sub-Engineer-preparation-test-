Q1. The three elements of the fire triangle are:

  1. A. Heat, fuel and oxygen
  2. B. Water, sand and foam
  3. C. Smoke, flame and ash
  4. D. Wood, paper and plastic
Answer: A. Heat, fuel and oxygen

Q2. Removing which element stops a fire?

  1. A. Any one of heat, fuel or oxygen
  2. B. Only water
  3. C. Only smoke
  4. D. None
Answer: A. Any one of heat, fuel or oxygen

Q3. A Class A fire involves:

  1. A. Flammable liquids
  2. B. Solid materials such as wood and paper
  3. C. Electrical equipment
  4. D. Cooking oils
Answer: B. Solid materials such as wood and paper

Q4. A CO2 extinguisher is most suitable for:

  1. A. Wood fires
  2. B. Electrical fires
  3. C. Metal fires
  4. D. Deep fat fryers
Answer: B. Electrical fires

Q5. A fire risk assessment should identify:

  1. A. Only exit routes
  2. B. People at risk, hazards and controls
  3. C. The cost of alarms
  4. D. Insurance details
Answer: B. People at risk, hazards and controls

Q6. Emergency lighting is provided so that:

  1. A. Rooms look brighter
  2. B. Escape routes can be used if power fails
  3. C. CCTV works better
  4. D. Alarms are louder
Answer: B. Escape routes can be used if power fails

Q7. The correct action on discovering a fire is to:

  1. A. Hide
  2. B. Raise the alarm and evacuate
  3. C. Collect belongings first
  4. D. Open all windows and doors
Answer: B. Raise the alarm and evacuate

Q8. Fire drills should be held:

  1. A. Never
  2. B. At regular planned intervals
  3. C. Only after a real fire
  4. D. Once in the company's life
Answer: B. At regular planned intervals

Q9. A 'means of escape' must be:

  1. A. Locked
  2. B. Kept clear and available at all times
  3. C. Used for storage
  4. D. Painted red
Answer: B. Kept clear and available at all times

Q10. Foam extinguishers are mainly used on:

  1. A. Flammable liquid fires
  2. B. Electrical fires
  3. C. Gas leaks
  4. D. Metal fires
Answer: A. Flammable liquid fires
