Q1. The three elements of the fire triangle are:

  1. A. Heat, fuel and oxygen
  2. B. Water, sand and foam
  3. C. Smoke, flame and ash
  4. D. Wood, paper and plastic
Answer: A. Heat, fuel and oxygen

Q2. Removing which element stops a fire?

  1. A. Any one of heat, fuel or oxygen
  2. B. Only water
  3. C. Only smoke
  4. D. None
Answer: A. Any one of heat, fuel or oxygen

Q3. A Class A fire involves:

  1. A. Flammable liquids
  2. B. Solid materials such as wood and paper
  3. C. Electrical equipment
  4. D. Cooking oils
Answer: B. Solid materials such as wood and paper

Q4. A CO2 extinguisher is most suitable for:

  1. A. Wood fires
  2. B. Electrical fires
  3. C. Metal fires
  4. D. Deep fat fryers
Answer: B. Electrical fires

Q5. A fire risk assessment should identify:

  1. A. Only exit routes
  2. B. People at risk, hazards and controls
  3. C. The cost of alarms
  4. D. Insurance details
Answer: B. People at risk, hazards and controls

Q6. Emergency lighting is provided so that:

  1. A. Rooms look brighter
  2. B. Escape routes can be used if power fails
  3. C. CCTV works better
  4. D. Alarms are louder
Answer: B. Escape routes can be used if power fails

Q7. The correct action on discovering a fire is to:

  1. A. Hide
  2. B. Raise the alarm and evacuate
  3. C. Collect belongings first
  4. D. Open all windows and doors
Answer: B. Raise the alarm and evacuate

Q8. Fire drills should be held:

  1. A. Never
  2. B. At regular planned intervals
  3. C. Only after a real fire
  4. D. Once in the company's life
Answer: B. At regular planned intervals

Q9. A 'means of escape' must be:

  1. A. Locked
  2. B. Kept clear and available at all times
  3. C. Used for storage
  4. D. Painted red
Answer: B. Kept clear and available at all times

Q10. Foam extinguishers are mainly used on:

  1. A. Flammable liquid fires
  2. B. Electrical fires
  3. C. Gas leaks
  4. D. Metal fires
Answer: A. Flammable liquid fires

Q11. Which hazard category presents a significant risk of fatalities and injuries specifically during the actual evacuation phase of a workplace fire?

  1. A. Smoke inhalation and stampede injuries
  2. B. Long-term industrial hearing loss
  3. C. Chemical corrosion of equipment
  4. D. Ergonomic strain from lifting boxes
Answer: A. Smoke inhalation and stampede injuries

Q12. Which color combination is internationally standardized for the emergency sign indicating the location of a fire alarm call point?

  1. A. Blue and white
  2. B. Green and white
  3. C. Yellow and black
  4. D. Red and white
Answer: D. Red and white

Q13. In addition to a functional fire alarm system, which safety device is installed along escape routes to maintain visibility during a mains power failure?

  1. A. Emergency lighting
  2. B. Automated roof ventilation hatches
  3. C. High-output halogen spotlights
  4. D. Directional floor carpeting
Answer: A. Emergency lighting

Q14. Which specific fire extinguisher type is restricted strictly to Class A fires and must never be applied to electrical or liquid fires?

  1. A. Water extinguisher
  2. B. Carbon dioxide extinguisher
  3. C. Dry powder extinguisher
  4. D. Foam extinguisher
Answer: A. Water extinguisher

Q15. Which specific mode of action describes how water extinguishes a solid material fire?

  1. A. Smothering oxygen
  2. B. Cooling the burning fuel
  3. C. Inhibiting chemical chain reactions
  4. D. Displacing ambient air
Answer: B. Cooling the burning fuel

Q16. What is the primary operational advantage of using a multi-purpose Dry Powder extinguisher?

  1. A. It leaves no residue after discharge
  2. B. It is completely safe for confined computer rooms
  3. C. It interrupts the chemical chain reaction across multiple fire classes
  4. D. It actively cools hot metallic surfaces
Answer: C. It interrupts the chemical chain reaction across multiple fire classes

Q17. What is the primary purpose of compartmentation in building fire safety design?

  1. A. To completely eliminate smoke production
  2. B. To contain fire and smoke within a specific zone for a limited time
  3. C. To provide direct access for exterior firefighting units
  4. D. To structurally support heavy HVAC ducting systems
Answer: B. To contain fire and smoke within a specific zone for a limited time

Q18. Which action is strictly prohibited when utilizing a Carbon Dioxide (CO2) fire extinguisher?

  1. A. Directing the horn at electrical switchboards
  2. B. Holding the metallic horn or discharge discharge nozzle directly
  3. C. Using it inside a poorly ventilated office space
  4. D. Sweeping the nozzle from side to side at the base
Answer: B. Holding the metallic horn or discharge discharge nozzle directly

Q19. What is the primary function of a thermal fusible link in a commercial kitchen fire suppression system?

  1. A. To sound an early warning alarm buzzer
  2. B. To automatically release the mechanical valve when ambient temperature rises sharply
  3. C. To manually isolate the electrical grid supply
  4. D. To monitor the pressure gauge of wet chemical cylinders
Answer: B. To automatically release the mechanical valve when ambient temperature rises sharply

Q20. What defines the maximum travel distance along an evacuation route to reach a place of safety?

  1. A. The total distance an occupant travels from any point in a building to an exterior exit
  2. B. The height of the building divided by stairwell width
  3. C. The total horizontal length of the corridor network only
  4. D. The distance between two consecutive fire extinguishers
Answer: A. The total distance an occupant travels from any point in a building to an exterior exit

Q21. Which of the following is NOT a method of fire suppression?

  1. A. Cooling
  2. B. Starving the fire
  3. C. Smothering
  4. D. Igniting the fire
Answer: D. Igniting the fire

Q22. If a CO₂ extinguisher is used in a poorly ventilated room, what secondary risk may arise?

  1. A. Water damage
  2. B. Oxygen displacement leading to suffocation
  3. C. Electrical shock
  4. D. Fire spread
Answer: B. Oxygen displacement leading to suffocation

Q23. During a fire drill in a high-rise office, the evacuation should begin when:

  1. A. The fire alarm sounds
  2. B. The sprinkler system activates
  3. C. The fire department arrives
  4. D. The building manager gives a signal
Answer: A. The fire alarm sounds

Q24. Which type of extinguisher is most effective for a fire involving flammable liquids?

  1. A. Water extinguisher
  2. B. Foam extinguisher
  3. C. Carbon dioxide extinguisher
  4. D. Dry powder extinguisher
Answer: B. Foam extinguisher

Q25. Which fire class is associated with combustible metals such as magnesium or titanium?

  1. A. Class A
  2. B. Class B
  3. C. Class C
  4. D. Class D
Answer: D. Class D
