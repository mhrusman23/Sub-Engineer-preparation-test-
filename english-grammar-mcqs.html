Q1. 'I have lived here ___ 2015.'

  1. A. for
  2. B. since
  3. C. from
  4. D. by
Answer: B. since

Q2. Which is a correct question?

  1. A. Where you live?
  2. B. Where do you live?
  3. C. Where does you live?
  4. D. Where you do live?
Answer: B. Where do you live?

Q3. 'The report ___ by the manager yesterday.'

  1. A. writes
  2. B. was written
  3. C. is write
  4. D. has write
Answer: B. was written

Q4. Choose the correct article: '___ honest man'

  1. A. a
  2. B. an
  3. C. the
  4. D. no article
Answer: B. an

Q5. 'If it rains, we ___ at home.'

  1. A. would stay
  2. B. will stay
  3. C. stayed
  4. D. staying
Answer: B. will stay

Q6. The comparative of 'good' is:

  1. A. Gooder
  2. B. More good
  3. C. Better
  4. D. Best
Answer: C. Better

Q7. 'Neither of the answers ___ correct.'

  1. A. are
  2. B. is
  3. C. were
  4. D. be
Answer: B. is

Q8. 'He is interested ___ learning English.'

  1. A. on
  2. B. at
  3. C. in
  4. D. for
Answer: C. in

Q9. Choose the correct passive voice form: 'They are building a new bridge.'

  1. A. A new bridge is built by them.
  2. B. A new bridge is being built by them.
  3. C. A new bridge was being built by them.
  4. D. A new bridge has been built by them.
Answer: B. A new bridge is being built by them.

Q10. Identify the sentence with the correct subject-verb agreement.

  1. A. A bouquet of yellow roses lend color to the room.
  2. B. Each of the students have submitted their assignment.
  3. C. The flock of birds flies south for the winter.
  4. D. Mathematics are my favorite subject in college.
Answer: C. The flock of birds flies south for the winter.

Q11. Fill in the blank with the appropriate modal verb: 'You ___ submit your report before Friday if you want it approved.'

  1. A. might
  2. B. must
  3. C. could
  4. D. would
Answer: B. must

Q12. Select the correct indirect speech for: He said, 'I am going to Lahore tomorrow.'

  1. A. He said that he is going to Lahore tomorrow.
  2. B. He said that he was going to Lahore the next day.
  3. C. He said that he went to Lahore the following day.
  4. D. He said that he has been going to Lahore tomorrow.
Answer: B. He said that he was going to Lahore the next day.

Q13. Which of the following sentences contains a correct conditional clause?

  1. A. If I had the money, I will buy a new car.
  2. B. If she studies hard, she would pass the exam.
  3. C. If they had arrived early, they would have caught the train.
  4. D. If water reaches 100 degrees, it will boiled.
Answer: C. If they had arrived early, they would have caught the train.
