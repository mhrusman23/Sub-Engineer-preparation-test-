Q1. Choose the correct sentence:

  1. A. She don't like tea.
  2. B. She doesn't likes tea.
  3. C. She doesn't like tea.
  4. D. She not like tea.
Answer: C. She doesn't like tea.

Q2. 'I have lived here ___ 2015.'

  1. A. for
  2. B. since
  3. C. from
  4. D. by
Answer: B. since

Q3. The past tense of 'go' is:

  1. A. Goed
  2. B. Went
  3. C. Gone away
  4. D. Going
Answer: B. Went

Q4. Which is a correct question?

  1. A. Where you live?
  2. B. Where do you live?
  3. C. Where does you live?
  4. D. Where you do live?
Answer: B. Where do you live?

Q5. 'The report ___ by the manager yesterday.'

  1. A. writes
  2. B. was written
  3. C. is write
  4. D. has write
Answer: B. was written

Q6. Choose the correct article: '___ honest man'

  1. A. a
  2. B. an
  3. C. the

Q7. 'If it rains, we ___ at home.'

  3. C. stayed

Q8. The comparative of 'good' is:

  1. A. Gooder
  2. B. More good
  3. C. Better

Q9. 'Neither of the answers ___ correct.'

  1. A. Ali wrote a letter.
