Q1. An art to collect data about material and cost required for a project is called

  1. A. Quantity survey
  2. B. Estimation
  3. C. S.D.O
  4. D. a and b
Answer: A. Quantity survey

Q2. A person whose job is to calculate quantities for a project is called

  1. A. Quantity surveyor
  2. B. Estimator
  3. C. S.D.O
  4. D. a and b
Answer: D. a and b

Q3. Necessary data for preparing an estimate of a project is

  1. A. Rates
  2. B. Specifications
  3. C. Drawings
  4. D. All of these
Answer: D. All of these

Q4. Design of project is presented by

  1. A. Drawings
  2. B. M.B
  3. C. Rates
  4. D. B.O.Q
Answer: A. Drawings

Q5. Details not shown in the drawing is presented by

  1. A. Rates
  2. B. B.O.Q
  3. C. Specifications
  4. D. Drawings
Answer: C. Specifications

Q6. Main classes of specifications are

  1. A. General
  2. B. Detailed
  3. C. Standard
  4. D. a and b
Answer: D. a and b

Q7. Type of specifications that is a part of contract is

  1. A. General
  2. B. Detailed
  3. C. Standard
  4. D. a and b
Answer: C. Standard

Q8. Rough cost estimate is carried out to get

  1. A. Administrative approval
  2. B. Project report
  3. C. Technical sanction
  4. D. Budget
Answer: A. Administrative approval

Q9. Detailed estimate is carried out to get

  1. A. Technical sanction
  2. B. Budget
  3. C. Project report
  4. D. Administrative approval
Answer: A. Technical sanction

Q10. Estimate is prepared if the cost of project is deviated more than 5% of its detailed estimate.

  1. A. Revised
  2. B. Rough cost
  3. C. Repair estimate
  4. D. Detailed
Answer: A. Revised

Q11. Cost of water supply and sanitary added in estimate of building.

  1. A. 8%
  2. B. 6%
  3. C. 2%
  4. D. 4%

Q12. Cost of electricity added in estimate of building.

  1. A. 4%
  2. B. 2%
  3. C. 6%
  4. D. 8%

Q13. Cost of gas supply added in estimate of building.

  1. A. 2%
  2. B. 8%
  3. C. 4%

Q14. Detailed estimate is also called ................. estimate.

  1. A. Rough cost estimate
  2. B. Premium estimate
  3. C. Revised estimate

Q15. ......... estimate is prepared to get administrative approval.

  1. A. Item rate estimate
  2. B. Revised estimate
  3. C. Rough cost estimate
  4. D. Detailed estimate

Q16. Administrative approval is taken for this estimate;

  1. A. Revised
  2. B. Repair
  3. C. Detailed
  4. D. Rough cost

Q19. Unit of volume is;

Q20. Area of triangle of base 2 ft and height 3 ft is;

  1. A. 12 sft
  2. B. 2 sft
  3. C. 3 sft
  4. D. 6 sft

Q21. The formula for the total perimeter of semi-circle is;

  1. A. R(π+1)
  2. B. (π/2+1)D

Q22. Quality of any material and construction work is described by .....

  1. A. Specifications
  2. B. Cost
  3. C. Drawing

Q23. Specifications are essential part of

  1. A. Estimate

Q24. Type of specifications is

  1. A. General

  3. C. Detailed

Q25. Name, nature and class of work is described in specifications .....
