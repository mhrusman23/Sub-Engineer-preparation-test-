Q1. The Mughal Empire in India was founded by Babur after the Battle of Panipat in:

  1. A. 1526
  2. B. 1556
  3. C. 1605
  4. D. 1707
Answer: A. 1526

Q2. The British East India Company gained control of Bengal after:

  1. A. Battle of Plassey (1757)
  2. B. Battle of Panipat (1761)
  3. C. First World War
  4. D. Battle of Buxar only
Answer: A. Battle of Plassey (1757)

Q3. The War of Independence (Mutiny) against the British took place in:

  1. A. 1805
  2. B. 1857
  3. C. 1905
  4. D. 1919
Answer: B. 1857

Q4. The Indian National Congress was founded in:

  1. A. 1857
  2. B. 1885
  3. C. 1905
  4. D. 1920
Answer: B. 1885

Q5. Muhammad bin Qasim conquered Sindh in:

  1. A. 612 AD
  2. B. 712 AD
  3. C. 912 AD
  4. D. 1012 AD
Answer: B. 712 AD

Q6. The Delhi Sultanate was established by:

  1. A. Babur
  2. B. Qutb-ud-din Aibak
  3. C. Akbar
  4. D. Sher Shah
Answer: B. Qutb-ud-din Aibak

Q7. Akbar's policy of 'Sulh-i-Kul' meant:

  1. A. War on all
  2. B. Peace with all (religious tolerance)
  3. C. High taxes
  4. D. Isolation
Answer: B. Peace with all (religious tolerance)

Q8. The Partition of Bengal (1905) was carried out under:

  2. B. Lord Mountbatten
  3. C. Lord Wavell
  4. D. Lord Linlithgow
