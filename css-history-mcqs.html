Q1. The Mughal Empire in India was founded by Babur after the Battle of Panipat in:

  1. A. 1526
  2. B. 1556
  3. C. 1605
  4. D. 1707
Answer: A. 1526

Q2. The British East India Company gained control of Bengal after:

  1. A. Battle of Plassey (1757)
  2. B. Battle of Panipat (1761)
  3. C. First World War
  4. D. Battle of Buxar only
Answer: A. Battle of Plassey (1757)
