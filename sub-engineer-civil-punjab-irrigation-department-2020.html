Q1. A transparent solution containing resin and oil which is used for finishing purposes....

  1. A. emulsion
  2. B. Enamel
  3. C. plastic
  4. D. varnish
Answer: D. varnish

Q2. Value of deflection at the ends of a simply supported beam....

  1. A. minimum
  2. B. zero
  3. C. Maximum
  4. D. average
Answer: B. zero

Q3. Vertical side of a sloping side of column base is 1 meter and angle of inclination is 60° then horizontal side will be....

  1. A. 0.58 m
  2. B. 0.707 m
  3. C. 1.58 m
  4. D. 1.5 m
Answer: A. 0.58 m

Q4. The measuring unit for refilling of trench for sewer line will be....

  1. A. per rft
  2. B. rft
  3. C. cft
  4. D. sft
Answer: C. cft

Q5. To prepare concrete of one bag of cement for 0.60 cement (w/c) ratio, water required will be......

  1. A. 35-liter
  2. B. 30-liter
  3. C. 25-liter
  4. D. 21-liter
Answer: B. 30-liter

Q6. Low level signal used for shunting is called....

  1. A. Warner signal
  2. B. Route signal
  3. C. disc signal
  4. D. light signal
Answer: C. disc signal

Q7. Watershed canal is also called.......canals.

  1. A. Side slope
  2. B. ridge
  3. C. contour
  4. D. basin
Answer: B. ridge

Q8. Close contour lines on a contour map represent......

  1. A. steep ground
  2. B. flat ground
  3. C. Cliff
  4. D. uniform slope
Answer: A. steep ground

Q9. Circumference of a circle of 1-meter diameter.......

  1. A. 1.57 m
  2. B. 1 m
  3. C. 3.14 m
  4. D. 6.28 m
Answer: C. 3.14 m

Q10. In a well-conditioned triangle, no angle should be less than......

  1. A. 30°
  2. B. 75°
  3. C. 40°
  4. D. 60°
Answer: A. 30°

Q11. The loosening of a wearing surface of road under traffic or weather resulting

  1. A. Mapping
  2. B. fretting
  3. C. abrasion
  4. D. crazing
Answer: B. fretting

Q12. The operation of leveling from any BM to the starting point of any project is called......leveling.

  1. A. Reciprocal
  2. B. x-sectional
  3. C. differential
  4. D. fly

Q13. The value of MOI of a section is minimum at....

  1. A. centroid
  2. B. top
  3. C. Base
  4. D. bottom

Q14. Last lines drawn in free hand sketching......

  1. A. horizontal
  2. B. Inclined
  3. C. vertical

Q15. Hexagon can be divided into six triangles....

  1. A. Scalene
  2. B. equilateral
  3. C. right angle

Q16. At equator, the amount of dip is......

  1. A. 60°
