Q1. The balance of power theory is central to which school of IR?

  1. A. Liberalism
  2. B. Realism
  3. C. Marxism
  4. D. Constructivism
Answer: B. Realism

Q2. The UN Security Council has how many permanent members?

  1. A. 3
  2. B. 5
  3. C. 7
  4. D. 10
Answer: B. 5

Q3. NATO is primarily a:

  1. A. Trade bloc
  2. B. Military alliance
  3. C. Cultural body
  4. D. Central bank
Answer: B. Military alliance

Q4. Soft power, a concept by Joseph Nye, means:

  1. A. Military strength
  2. B. Getting others to want what you want through attraction and culture
  3. C. Economic sanctions
  4. D. Nuclear capability
Answer: B. Getting others to want what you want through attraction and culture

Q5. The Cold War was mainly between:

  1. A. USA and China
  2. B. USA and USSR
  3. C. UK and France
  4. D. India and Pakistan
Answer: B. USA and USSR

Q6. Diplomacy is best defined as:

  1. A. War by other means
  2. B. Managing international relations through negotiation and dialogue
  3. C. Trade only
  4. D. Espionage
Answer: B. Managing international relations through negotiation and dialogue

Q7. The concept of 'collective security' is the basis of:

  1. A. NATO only
  2. B. The United Nations system
  3. C. The WTO
  4. D. OPEC
Answer: B. The United Nations system

Q8. Globalisation refers to:

  1. A. Isolation of states
  2. B. Growing interconnection of economies, cultures and politics worldwide
  3. C. Only trade
  4. D. Only migration
Answer: B. Growing interconnection of economies, cultures and politics worldwide

Q9. A 'failed state' is one that:

  1. A. Loses an election
  2. B. Cannot provide basic governance and security to its people
  3. C. Has a small army
  4. D. Is landlocked
Answer: B. Cannot provide basic governance and security to its people

Q10. The foreign policy of a state is mainly shaped by:

  1. A. Only geography
  2. B. National interests, capabilities and the international environment
  3. C. Only history
  4. D. Only public opinion
Answer: B. National interests, capabilities and the international environment
