Q1. The balance of power theory is central to which school of IR?

  1. A. Liberalism
  2. B. Realism
  3. C. Marxism
  4. D. Constructivism
Answer: B. Realism

Q2. The UN Security Council has how many permanent members?

  1. A. 3
  2. B. 5
  3. C. 7
  4. D. 10
Answer: B. 5

Q3. NATO is primarily a:

  1. A. Trade bloc
  2. B. Military alliance
  3. C. Cultural body
  4. D. Central bank
Answer: B. Military alliance

Q4. Soft power, a concept by Joseph Nye, means:

  1. A. Military strength
  2. B. Getting others to want what you want through attraction and culture
  3. C. Economic sanctions
  4. D. Nuclear capability
Answer: B. Getting others to want what you want through attraction and culture

Q5. The Cold War was mainly between:

  1. A. USA and China
  2. B. USA and USSR
  3. C. UK and France
  4. D. India and Pakistan
Answer: B. USA and USSR

Q6. Diplomacy is best defined as:

  1. A. War by other means
  2. B. Managing international relations through negotiation and dialogue
  3. C. Trade only
  4. D. Espionage
Answer: B. Managing international relations through negotiation and dialogue

Q7. The concept of 'collective security' is the basis of:

  1. A. NATO only
  2. B. The United Nations system
  3. C. The WTO
  4. D. OPEC
Answer: B. The United Nations system

Q8. Globalisation refers to:

  1. A. Isolation of states
  2. B. Growing interconnection of economies, cultures and politics worldwide
  3. C. Only trade
  4. D. Only migration
Answer: B. Growing interconnection of economies, cultures and politics worldwide

Q9. A 'failed state' is one that:

  1. A. Loses an election
  2. B. Cannot provide basic governance and security to its people
  3. C. Has a small army
  4. D. Is landlocked
Answer: B. Cannot provide basic governance and security to its people

Q10. The foreign policy of a state is mainly shaped by:

  1. A. Only geography
  2. B. National interests, capabilities and the international environment
  3. C. Only history
  4. D. Only public opinion
Answer: B. National interests, capabilities and the international environment

Q11. The book 'Politics Among Nations' (1948), a founding text of modern realism, was written by:

  1. A. E. H. Carr
  2. B. Raymond Aron
  3. C. Kenneth Waltz
  4. D. Hans Morgenthau
Answer: D. Hans Morgenthau

Q12. Hans Morgenthau is regarded as the leading figure of:

  1. A. Marxism
  2. B. Constructivism
  3. C. Classical realism
  4. D. Liberalism
Answer: C. Classical realism

Q13. Kenneth Waltz's 'Theory of International Politics' (1979) is the founding text of:

  1. A. Critical theory
  2. B. Poststructuralism
  3. C. Neorealism
  4. D. Neoliberalism
Answer: C. Neorealism

Q14. According to Waltz, the feature of international structure that varies and matters most is the:

  1. A. Regime type of states
  2. B. Culture of states
  3. C. Ideology of the units
  4. D. Distribution of capabilities
Answer: D. Distribution of capabilities

Q15. John Mearsheimer is the leading proponent of:

  1. A. Liberal peace theory
  2. B. World-systems theory
  3. C. Defensive realism
  4. D. Offensive realism
Answer: D. Offensive realism

Q16. The term 'security dilemma' was originally coined by:

  1. A. Kenneth Waltz
  2. B. Hans Morgenthau
  3. C. John Herz
  4. D. Robert Jervis
Answer: C. John Herz

Q17. 'Anarchy is what states make of it' is the famous argument of:

  1. A. Alexander Wendt
  2. B. John Mearsheimer
  3. C. Stephen Walt
  4. D. Robert Gilpin
Answer: A. Alexander Wendt

Q18. Constructivism holds that the identities and interests of states are:

  1. A. Determined by geography
  2. B. Given by human nature
  3. C. Fixed by material power
  4. D. Socially constructed
Answer: D. Socially constructed

Q19. The term 'constructivism' in International Relations was coined by:

  1. A. Nicholas Onuf
  2. B. Alexander Wendt
  3. C. Martha Finnemore
  4. D. John Ruggie
Answer: A. Nicholas Onuf

Q20. Immanuel Kant's 1795 essay that laid the foundations of liberal thinking on peace is:

  1. A. Perpetual Peace
  2. B. The Prince
  3. C. Leviathan
  4. D. The Social Contract
Answer: A. Perpetual Peace

Q21. The modern democratic peace theory was popularized by:

  1. A. Michael Doyle
  2. B. Francis Fukuyama
  3. C. Samuel Huntington
  4. D. Immanuel Wallerstein
Answer: A. Michael Doyle

Q22. The concept of 'complex interdependence' was developed by:

  1. A. Keohane and Nye
  2. B. Waltz and Mearsheimer
  3. C. Wendt and Onuf
  4. D. Bull and Watson
Answer: A. Keohane and Nye

Q23. Robert Keohane's book 'After Hegemony' (1984) is a key work of:

  1. A. Neoliberal institutionalism
  2. B. Classical realism
  3. C. Dependency theory
  4. D. Feminism
Answer: A. Neoliberal institutionalism

Q24. David Mitrany is associated with which approach to international cooperation?

  1. A. Functionalism
  2. B. Federalism
  3. C. Realism
  4. D. Geopolitics
Answer: A. Functionalism

Q25. Neofunctionalism, the theory of regional integration, was developed by:

  1. A. Ernst Haas
  2. B. David Mitrany
  3. C. Karl Deutsch
  4. D. Andrew Moravcsik
Answer: A. Ernst Haas
