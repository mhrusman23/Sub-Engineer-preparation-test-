Q1. The balance of power theory is central to which school of IR?

  1. A. Liberalism
  2. B. Realism
  3. C. Marxism
  4. D. Constructivism
Answer: B. Realism

Q2. The UN Security Council has how many permanent members?

  1. A. 3
  2. B. 5
  3. C. 7
  4. D. 10
Answer: B. 5

Q3. NATO is primarily a:

  1. A. Trade bloc
  2. B. Military alliance
  3. C. Cultural body
  4. D. Central bank
Answer: B. Military alliance

Q4. Soft power, a concept by Joseph Nye, means:

  1. A. Military strength
  2. B. Getting others to want what you want through attraction and culture
  3. C. Economic sanctions
  4. D. Nuclear capability
Answer: B. Getting others to want what you want through attraction and culture

Q5. The Cold War was mainly between:

  1. A. USA and China
  2. B. USA and USSR
  3. C. UK and France
  4. D. India and Pakistan
Answer: B. USA and USSR

Q6. Diplomacy is best defined as:

  1. A. War by other means
  2. B. Managing international relations through negotiation and dialogue
  3. C. Trade only
  4. D. Espionage
Answer: B. Managing international relations through negotiation and dialogue

Q7. The concept of 'collective security' is the basis of:

  1. A. NATO only
  2. B. The United Nations system
  3. C. The WTO
  4. D. OPEC
Answer: B. The United Nations system

Q8. Globalisation refers to:

  1. A. Isolation of states
  2. B. Growing interconnection of economies, cultures and politics worldwide
  3. C. Only trade
  4. D. Only migration
Answer: B. Growing interconnection of economies, cultures and politics worldwide

Q9. A 'failed state' is one that:

  1. A. Loses an election
  2. B. Cannot provide basic governance and security to its people
  3. C. Has a small army
  4. D. Is landlocked
Answer: B. Cannot provide basic governance and security to its people

Q10. The foreign policy of a state is mainly shaped by:

  1. A. Only geography
  2. B. National interests, capabilities and the international environment
  3. C. Only history
  4. D. Only public opinion
Answer: B. National interests, capabilities and the international environment

Q11. In International Relations terminology, what does the principle of 'Westphalian Sovereignty' primarily refer to?

  1. A. The supremacy of international courts over domestic laws
  2. B. The right of states to non-interference and absolute authority within their territorial borders
  3. C. The mandatory disarmament of defeated nations after a war
  4. D. The economic integration of European states
Answer: B. The right of states to non-interference and absolute authority within their territorial borders

Q12. Which theoretical paradigm emphasizes international institutions and economic interdependence as pathways to peace?

  1. A. Realism
  2. B. Liberalism
  3. C. Marxism
  4. D. Realpolitik
Answer: B. Liberalism

Q13. Which of the following bodies is the principal judicial organ of the United Nations?

  1. A. International Criminal Court
  2. B. International Court of Justice
  3. C. European Court of Human Rights
  4. D. Permanent Court of Arbitration
Answer: B. International Court of Justice

Q14. What is the primary objective of the Nuclear Non-Proliferation Treaty (NPT)?

  1. A. To completely eliminate all conventional weapons globally
  2. B. To prevent the spread of nuclear weapons and weapon technology while promoting peaceful uses of atomic energy
  3. C. To establish a single global monopoly on uranium mining
  4. D. To ban all nuclear energy generation worldwide
Answer: B. To prevent the spread of nuclear weapons and weapon technology while promoting peaceful uses of atomic energy

Q15. Which term describes a geopolitical situation where power is distributed among three or more major global centers?

  1. A. Unipolarity
  2. B. Bipolarity
  3. C. Multipolarity
  4. D. Hegemony
Answer: C. Multipolarity

Q16. The Bretton Woods Conference of 1944 resulted in the establishment of which two major international financial institutions?

  1. A. World Trade Organization and Bank for International Settlements
  2. B. International Monetary Fund and World Bank
  3. C. Asian Development Bank and OPEC
  4. D. European Central Bank and Federal Reserve
Answer: B. International Monetary Fund and World Bank

Q17. Which theoretical perspective in International Relations argues that states operate in an anarchic environment and must rely on self-help for survival?

  1. A. Liberalism
  2. B. Realism
  3. C. Constructivism
  4. D. Marxism
Answer: B. Realism

Q18. What primary concept does the 1648 Peace of Westphalia introduce to the modern international system?

  1. A. Supranational governance
  2. B. State sovereignty
  3. C. Collective security
  4. D. Economic protectionism
Answer: B. State sovereignty

Q19. Which international agreement, signed in 2015, aims to limit global temperature rise well below 2 degrees Celsius above pre-industrial levels?

  1. A. Kyoto Protocol
  2. B. Paris Agreement
  3. C. Montreal Protocol
  4. D. Copenhagen Accord
Answer: B. Paris Agreement

Q20. In the context of deterrence theory, what term refers to a state's credible capability to retaliate and inflict unacceptable damage on an aggressor?

  1. A. Second-strike capability
  2. B. Preventive diplomacy
  3. C. Collective defense
  4. D. Forward deployment
Answer: A. Second-strike capability

Q21. Which organization acts as the primary international lender of last resort for countries facing severe balance-of-payments crises?

  1. A. World Bank
  2. B. International Monetary Fund
  3. C. World Trade Organization
  4. D. Asian Development Bank
Answer: B. International Monetary Fund

Q22. What term describes a foreign policy approach where a state deliberately avoids alliances and international political entanglements?

  1. A. Imperialism
  2. B. Isolationism
  3. C. Multilateralism
  4. D. Appeasement
Answer: B. Isolationism

Q23. The principle that a single dominant power can provide global stability is known as:

  1. A. balance of power
  2. B. hegemony
  3. C. collective security
  4. D. universal governance
Answer: B. hegemony

Q24. Which concept best describes a state aligning with a stronger power to avoid conflict?

  1. A. balancing
  2. B. bandwagoning
  3. C. deterrence
  4. D. realpolitik
Answer: B. bandwagoning

Q25. According to the balance‑of‑power theory, peace is most likely when:

  1. A. a single state dominates all others
  2. B. all states are equally weak
  3. C. power is distributed among several states
  4. D. there is a global alliance against all states
Answer: C. power is distributed among several states
