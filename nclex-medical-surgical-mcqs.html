Q1. The classic signs of a myocardial infarction (heart attack) include:

  1. A. Leg pain
  2. B. Crushing chest pain radiating to the arm or jaw
  3. C. Headache only
  4. D. Rash
Answer: B. Crushing chest pain radiating to the arm or jaw

Q2. A patient with heart failure should be monitored daily for:

  1. A. Weight gain and fluid overload
  2. B. Hair growth
  3. C. Appetite only
  4. D. Vision changes
Answer: A. Weight gain and fluid overload

Q3. Post-operative patients are encouraged to walk early to prevent:

  1. A. Hunger
  2. B. Deep vein thrombosis and lung complications
  3. C. Boredom
  4. D. Insomnia
Answer: B. Deep vein thrombosis and lung complications

Q4. Diabetes insipidus is characterised by:

  1. A. Low urine output
  2. B. Very large amounts of dilute urine and thirst
  3. C. Weight gain
  4. D. Low blood sugar
Answer: B. Very large amounts of dilute urine and thirst

Q5. The priority action for a patient having a seizure is to:

  1. A. Put something in the mouth
  2. B. Protect from injury and keep the airway clear
  3. C. Hold them down
  4. D. Give water
Answer: B. Protect from injury and keep the airway clear

Q6. Signs of hypoglycemia include:

  1. A. Dry skin and thirst
  2. B. Sweating, shaking and confusion
  3. C. Slow pulse
  4. D. Weight gain
Answer: B. Sweating, shaking and confusion

Q7. A patient with COPD should receive oxygen:

  1. A. As much as possible
  2. B. Controlled, as prescribed — high flow can be harmful
  3. C. Never
  4. D. Only at night
Answer: B. Controlled, as prescribed — high flow can be harmful

Q8. Appendicitis typically causes pain in the:

  1. A. Left upper abdomen
  2. B. Right lower abdomen
  3. C. Back
  4. D. Chest
Answer: B. Right lower abdomen

Q9. After thyroid surgery, the nurse watches for:

  1. A. Hypocalcaemia (tingling, tetany)
  2. B. High fever only
  3. C. Hair loss
  4. D. Constipation
Answer: A. Hypocalcaemia (tingling, tetany)

Q10. The best position for a patient in shock is:

  1. A. Sitting upright
  2. B. Lying flat with legs elevated (if not contraindicated)
  3. C. Standing
  4. D. Prone
Answer: B. Lying flat with legs elevated (if not contraindicated)

Q11. Which clinical finding is most indicative of a pulmonary embolism in a postoperative patient?

  1. A. Sudden onset shortness of breath and pleuritic chest pain
  2. B. Gradual lowering of blood pressure and warm extremities
  3. C. Localized pain and erythema at the surgical incision site
  4. D. Decreased bowel sounds and mild abdominal distention
Answer: A. Sudden onset shortness of breath and pleuritic chest pain

Q12. What is the priority preoperative nursing responsibility regarding informed consent?

  1. A. Explaining the complex surgical risks to the patient in detail
  2. B. Deciding if the surgical intervention is medically necessary
  3. C. Verifying that the patient signed voluntarily and understands the procedure
  4. D. Convincing a reluctant patient to proceed with the operation
Answer: C. Verifying that the patient signed voluntarily and understands the procedure

Q13. Which intervention is essential during the management of active tuberculosis treatment protocols?

  1. A. Administering a single anti-tubercular drug for two weeks
  2. B. Ensuring long-term adherence to a multi-drug regimen
  3. C. Encouraging a low-protein diet to decrease kidney workload
  4. D. Isolating the patient permanently from all family members
Answer: B. Ensuring long-term adherence to a multi-drug regimen

Q14. When preparing a patient for peritoneal dialysis, which condition serves as a primary contraindication?

  1. A. Controlled systemic hypertension
  2. B. History of extensive abdominal adhesions or peritonitis
  3. C. Mild peripheral edema in the lower extremities
  4. D. Well-managed type 2 diabetes mellitus
Answer: B. History of extensive abdominal adhesions or peritonitis

Q15. What is a major clinical indicator monitored in a patient experiencing autonomic dysreflexia?

  1. A. Severe sudden hypertension accompanied by a pounding headache
  2. B. Profound continuous hypotension and warm flushed skin
  3. C. Rapidly dropping heart rate with loss of consciousness
  4. D. Generalized muscle twitching in the lower extremities
Answer: A. Severe sudden hypertension accompanied by a pounding headache

Q16. A nurse is caring for a client experiencing an acute Addisonian crisis. Which clinical manifestation requires immediate intervention?

  1. A. Severe hypotension and vascular collapse
  2. B. Marked hypertension and tachycardia
  3. C. Hyperglycemia and sudden weight gain
  4. D. Flushed skin and excessive sweating
Answer: A. Severe hypotension and vascular collapse

Q17. When assessing a client admitted with diabetic ketoacidosis (DKA), which finding is characteristic of this metabolic emergency?

  1. A. Kussmaul respirations and fruity breath odor
  2. B. Profound hypoglycemia and cold skin
  3. C. Extreme bradypnea and peripheral edema
  4. D. Elevated blood pH and normal bicarbonate
Answer: A. Kussmaul respirations and fruity breath odor

Q18. While monitoring a client with cirrhosis who has developed portal hypertension, the nurse remains vigilant for which life-threatening complication?

  1. A. Esophageal variceal bleeding
  2. B. Renal artery stenosis
  3. C. Acute pulmonary embolism
  4. D. Spontaneous pneumothorax
Answer: A. Esophageal variceal bleeding

Q19. A patient undergoing active chemotherapy develops a fever accompanied by neutropenia. What is the nurse's immediate priority nursing intervention?

  1. A. Administering prescribed antipyretics immediately
  2. B. Initiating broad-spectrum antibiotics promptly
  3. C. Placing the client on strict protective isolation
  4. D. Drawing a complete blood count laboratory panel
Answer: B. Initiating broad-spectrum antibiotics promptly

Q20. During the management of a client with severe fluid volume overload and acute heart failure, the nurse should closely monitor which critical parameter daily?

  1. A. Total daily caloric intake
  2. B. Accurate daily body weight
  3. C. Circumference of both arms
  4. D. Fasting blood glucose levels
Answer: B. Accurate daily body weight

Q21. A client with chronic kidney disease (CKD) undergoing regular hemodialysis requires careful assessment of which vascular access site complication?

  1. A. Palpable thrill and audible bruit
  2. B. Complete absence of blood flow noise
  3. C. Elevated local skin temperature only
  4. D. Continuous localized bruising
Answer: A. Palpable thrill and audible bruit

Q22. A patient with acute pulmonary edema is placed in a position that improves oxygenation and reduces preload. Which position is most appropriate?

  1. A. Supine with pillows under knees
  2. B. High‑fowler (head of bed 45°)
  3. C. Prone position
  4. D. Flat on the back
Answer: B. High‑fowler (head of bed 45°)

Q23. A 52‑year‑old man presents with right lower quadrant abdominal pain that started 12 hours ago. On examination, the pain is localized to the right iliac fossa. Which organ is most likely inflamed?

  1. A. Stomach
  2. B. Appendix
  3. C. Gallbladder
  4. D. Pancreas
Answer: B. Appendix

Q24. A patient in the ICU is experiencing a generalized tonic–clonic seizure. The nurse should first:

  1. A. Place a tongue depressor in the mouth
  2. B. Administer IV benzodiazepine
  3. C. Move the patient to the floor
  4. D. Clear the area around the patient
Answer: D. Clear the area around the patient

Q25. A 45‑year‑old woman with type 2 diabetes reports shaking, sweating, and confusion. Her finger‑stick glucose is 48 mg/dL. Which symptom is most characteristic of hypoglycemia?

  1. A. Dry skin and thirst
  2. B. Weight gain
  3. C. Slow pulse
  4. D. Sweating and shaking
Answer: D. Sweating and shaking
