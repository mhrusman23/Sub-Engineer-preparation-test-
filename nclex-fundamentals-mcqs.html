Q1. The first step of the nursing process is:

  1. A. Planning
  2. B. Assessment
  3. C. Evaluation
  4. D. Implementation
Answer: B. Assessment

Q2. Hand hygiene is most important because it:

  1. A. Saves time
  2. B. Prevents the spread of infection
  3. C. Is required by law only
  4. D. Keeps hands soft
Answer: B. Prevents the spread of infection

Q3. Normal adult oral body temperature is about:

  1. A. 35.0°C
  2. B. 37.0°C
  3. C. 38.5°C
  4. D. 40.0°C
Answer: B. 37.0°C

Q4. A patient's blood pressure of 120/80 mmHg is considered:

  1. A. Low
  2. B. Normal
  3. C. Stage 2 hypertension
  4. D. An emergency
Answer: B. Normal

Q5. When measuring respirations, the nurse should:

  1. A. Tell the patient to breathe deeply
  2. B. Count without making the patient aware
  3. C. Count for 5 seconds
  4. D. Ask the patient to count
Answer: B. Count without making the patient aware

Q6. The best site to check the pulse of an unconscious adult is:

  1. A. Radial
  2. B. Carotid
  3. C. Brachial
  4. D. Dorsal pedis
Answer: B. Carotid

Q7. Standard precautions apply to:

  1. A. Only infected patients
  2. B. All patients, all the time
  3. C. Only surgical patients
  4. D. Only children
Answer: B. All patients, all the time

Q8. A patient on bed rest is at risk of:

  1. A. Pressure injuries and blood clots
  2. B. Hearing loss
  3. C. Vision problems
  4. D. Allergies
Answer: A. Pressure injuries and blood clots

Q9. The correct order for donning PPE is:

  1. A. Gloves, gown, mask
  2. B. Gown, mask, goggles, gloves
  3. C. Mask, gloves, gown
  4. D. Any order is fine
Answer: B. Gown, mask, goggles, gloves

Q10. Documenting care should be done:

  1. A. At the end of the week
  2. B. As soon as possible after the care is given
  3. C. Only if there is a problem
  4. D. By the doctor
Answer: B. As soon as possible after the care is given

Q11. Which documentation practice best protects the nurse legally and ensures accurate clinical communication?

  1. A. Recording care before performing the interventions to save time
  2. B. Documenting objective findings immediately after providing care
  3. C. Using generalized personal opinions to describe client behavior
  4. D. Erasing incorrect entries so the record remains spotless
Answer: B. Documenting objective findings immediately after providing care

Q12. During the physical assessment of a client, which technique should the nurse perform first in the standard sequence of examination?

  1. A. Palpation
  2. B. Percussion
  3. C. Inspection
  4. D. Auscultation
Answer: C. Inspection

Q13. When donning full personal protective equipment (PPE), which item is typically put on last right before entering the room?

  1. A. Gown
  2. B. Mask or respirator
  3. C. Goggles or face shield
  4. D. Gloves
Answer: D. Gloves

Q14. To prevent the formation of pressure injuries in a bedridden client, how frequently should the nurse reposition the individual?

  1. A. Every 30 minutes
  2. B. Every 2 hours
  3. C. Every 4 hours
  4. D. Once per shift
Answer: B. Every 2 hours

Q15. Which nursing action is essential before administering any medication via an enteral feeding tube?

  1. A. Verifying tube placement in the stomach or intestine
  2. B. Heating the formula to body temperature
  3. C. Mixing all prescribed crushed medications together
  4. D. Flushing the tube with sterile water after medication
Answer: A. Verifying tube placement in the stomach or intestine

Q16. What is the primary purpose of applying standard precautions in a healthcare setting?

  1. A. To eliminate the need for hand hygiene
  2. B. To protect healthcare workers and clients from transmitted pathogens
  3. C. To isolate clients diagnosed with airborne infections only
  4. D. To reduce the cost of personal protective equipment
Answer: B. To protect healthcare workers and clients from transmitted pathogens

Q17. Which angle is strictly recommended for administering an intramuscular injection into the deltoid muscle of an adult?

  1. A. 15 degrees
  2. B. 45 degrees
  3. C. 90 degrees
  4. D. 30 degrees
Answer: C. 90 degrees

Q18. What is the primary objective of utilizing the nursing process in clinical practice?

  1. A. To provide organized, individualized patient-centered care
  2. B. To reduce hospital administration paperwork
  3. C. To replace physician medical diagnoses
  4. D. To calculate medication costs for patients
Answer: A. To provide organized, individualized patient-centered care

Q19. When removing personal protective equipment (PPE), which item should a nurse take off first?

  1. A. Mask
  2. B. Gown
  3. C. Gloves
  4. D. Eye protection
Answer: C. Gloves

Q20. Which type of acquired immunity develops after an individual recovers from a natural infection?

  1. A. Natural active immunity
  2. B. Natural passive immunity
  3. C. Artificial active immunity
  4. D. Artificial passive immunity
Answer: A. Natural active immunity

Q21. Which Korotkoff sound corresponds to the systolic blood pressure reading?

  1. A. Disappearance of all sounds
  2. B. First faint tapping sound
  3. C. Muffled blowing sound
  4. D. A sharp distinct snapping sound
Answer: B. First faint tapping sound

Q22. What is the primary reason for performing an Allen test prior to arterial blood gas sampling?

  1. A. To assess ulnar artery collateral circulation
  2. B. To measure local skin temperature
  3. C. To determine blood coagulation time
  4. D. To check for localized infection
Answer: A. To assess ulnar artery collateral circulation

Q23. Which specific surgical position is typically expected and utilized for a client scheduled for a thyroidectomy?

  1. A. Trendelenburg position
  2. B. Semi-Fowler's position with neck extension
  3. C. Prone position
  4. D. Lateral recumbent position
Answer: B. Semi-Fowler's position with neck extension

Q24. Which clinical finding in an adult client is most indicative of chronic hypoxia?

  1. A. Digital clubbing
  2. B. Sudden peripheral edema
  3. C. Acute chest pain
  4. D. Intermittent tremors
Answer: A. Digital clubbing

Q25. An older adult client is admitted with facial drooping, a weak cough, and an absent gag reflex. Which initial action should the nurse implement?

  1. A. Offer small sips of clear fluids to test swallowing
  2. B. Withhold oral intake and request a speech therapy swallow evaluation
  3. C. Administer a full oral diet with thickened liquids
  4. D. Provide crushed medications mixed in water orally
Answer: B. Withhold oral intake and request a speech therapy swallow evaluation
