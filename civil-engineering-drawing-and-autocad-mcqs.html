Q1. Minimum depth of deep foundation "d" in terms of its breadth "b"

  1. A. 4b
  2. B. b
  3. C. 3b
  4. D. 2b
Answer: D. 2b

Q2. According to thumb rule, breadth of a wall 9" thick.

  1. A. 36"
  2. B. 30"
  3. C. 18"
  4. D. 24"
Answer: B. 30"

Q3. According to thumb rule breadth of a wall 30 cm thick.

  1. A. 60 cm
  2. B. 90 cm
  3. C. 70 cm
  4. D. 80 cm
Answer: B. 90 cm

Q4. Raft foundation is a type of

  1. A. Wall foundation
  2. B. Caisson foundation
  3. C. Shallow foundation
  4. D. Deep foundation

Q5. Foundation suitable for single storey building.

  1. A. Spread

  3. C. Raft

  1. A. Spread

  3. C. Pile
  4. D. Raft

Q7. Foundation suitable for series of closed spaced columns.

  1. A. Spread
  2. B. Raft
  3. C. Strap
  4. D. Pile

Q8. Suitable foundation for high rising buildings;

  1. A. Well

  3. C. Stepped

Q9. This is not a deep foundation;

Q11. A pile that transferes load at its lower end.

  2. B. Bearing cum friction pile
  3. C. Friction pile
  4. D. All of these
