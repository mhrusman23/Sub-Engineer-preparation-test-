Q1. Sociology is the scientific study of:

  1. A. Rocks
  2. B. Human society and social behaviour
  3. C. Plants
  4. D. Markets only
Answer: B. Human society and social behaviour

Q2. Who is known as the father of sociology?

  1. A. Karl Marx
  2. B. Auguste Comte
  3. C. Max Weber
  4. D. Emile Durkheim
Answer: B. Auguste Comte

Q3. A 'norm' in society is:

  1. A. A law only
  2. B. A shared rule or expectation of behaviour
  3. C. A building
  4. D. A ritual object
Answer: B. A shared rule or expectation of behaviour

Q4. Socialisation is the process by which individuals:

  1. A. Earn money
  2. B. Learn the culture and norms of their society
  3. C. Move cities
  4. D. Form governments
Answer: B. Learn the culture and norms of their society

Q5. Durkheim's famous study 'Suicide' showed that suicide rates are influenced by:

  1. A. Weather only
  2. B. Social integration and regulation
  3. C. Income only
  4. D. Genetics only
Answer: B. Social integration and regulation

Q6. A nuclear family consists of:

  1. A. Grandparents and cousins
  2. B. Parents and their children
  3. C. Only siblings
  4. D. A whole clan
Answer: B. Parents and their children

Q7. Culture includes:

  1. A. Only language
  2. B. Beliefs, values, customs and material objects of a group
  3. C. Only food
  4. D. Only religion
Answer: B. Beliefs, values, customs and material objects of a group

Q8. Urbanisation refers to:

  1. A. Building roads
  2. B. The growth of cities and city living
  3. C. Farming
  4. D. Migration to villages
Answer: B. The growth of cities and city living

Q9. Deviance in sociology means behaviour that:

  1. A. Is always criminal
  2. B. Violates social norms
  3. C. Is always punished
  4. D. Is rare
Answer: B. Violates social norms

Q10. Primary groups (like family) are characterised by:

  1. A. Formal rules
  2. B. Close, personal and long-lasting relationships
  3. C. Large size
  4. D. Written contracts
Answer: B. Close, personal and long-lasting relationships
