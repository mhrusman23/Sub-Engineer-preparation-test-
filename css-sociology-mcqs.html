Q1. Sociology is the scientific study of:

  1. A. Rocks
  2. B. Human society and social behaviour
  3. C. Plants
  4. D. Markets only
Answer: B. Human society and social behaviour

Q2. Who is known as the father of sociology?

  1. A. Karl Marx
  2. B. Auguste Comte
  3. C. Max Weber
  4. D. Emile Durkheim
Answer: B. Auguste Comte

Q3. A 'norm' in society is:

  1. A. A law only
  2. B. A shared rule or expectation of behaviour
  3. C. A building
  4. D. A ritual object
Answer: B. A shared rule or expectation of behaviour

Q4. Socialisation is the process by which individuals:

  1. A. Earn money
  2. B. Learn the culture and norms of their society
  3. C. Move cities
  4. D. Form governments
Answer: B. Learn the culture and norms of their society

Q5. Durkheim's famous study 'Suicide' showed that suicide rates are influenced by:

  1. A. Weather only
  2. B. Social integration and regulation
  3. C. Income only
  4. D. Genetics only
Answer: B. Social integration and regulation

Q6. A nuclear family consists of:

  1. A. Grandparents and cousins
  2. B. Parents and their children
  3. C. Only siblings
  4. D. A whole clan
Answer: B. Parents and their children

Q7. Culture includes:

  1. A. Only language
  2. B. Beliefs, values, customs and material objects of a group
  3. C. Only food
  4. D. Only religion
Answer: B. Beliefs, values, customs and material objects of a group

Q8. Urbanisation refers to:

  1. A. Building roads
  2. B. The growth of cities and city living
  3. C. Farming
  4. D. Migration to villages
Answer: B. The growth of cities and city living

Q9. Deviance in sociology means behaviour that:

  1. A. Is always criminal
  2. B. Violates social norms
  3. C. Is always punished
  4. D. Is rare
Answer: B. Violates social norms

Q10. Primary groups (like family) are characterised by:

  1. A. Formal rules
  2. B. Close, personal and long-lasting relationships
  3. C. Large size
  4. D. Written contracts
Answer: B. Close, personal and long-lasting relationships

Q11. According to Auguste Comte, the highest and final stage of human thought in his 'law of three stages' is the:

  1. A. Theological stage
  2. B. Metaphysical stage
  3. C. Positive stage
  4. D. Feudal stage
Answer: C. Positive stage

Q12. Durkheim's concept of 'anomie' refers to a condition of:

  1. A. Strong social integration
  2. B. Cultural conflict
  3. C. Social mobility
  4. D. Normlessness
Answer: D. Normlessness

Q13. According to Durkheim, altruistic suicide results from:

  1. A. Sudden social change
  2. B. Low social integration
  3. C. Excessive social integration
  4. D. Loss of status
Answer: C. Excessive social integration

Q14. Max Weber's concept of 'Verstehen' means:

  1. A. Statistical analysis of society
  2. B. Observation from a distance
  3. C. Economic determinism
  4. D. Understanding social action from the actor's own point of view
Answer: D. Understanding social action from the actor's own point of view

Q15. Weber's charismatic authority is based on:

  1. A. Written rules and laws
  2. B. Tradition and custom
  3. C. The exceptional personal qualities of a leader
  4. D. Hereditary succession
Answer: C. The exceptional personal qualities of a leader

Q16. In 'The Protestant Ethic and the Spirit of Capitalism', Weber linked Protestant beliefs with the:

  1. A. Rise of feudalism
  2. B. Development of capitalism
  3. C. Spread of socialism
  4. D. Decline of the Church
Answer: B. Development of capitalism

Q17. Karl Marx's theory of historical development is known as:

  1. A. Historical materialism
  2. B. Idealism
  3. C. Positivism
  4. D. Evolutionism
Answer: A. Historical materialism

Q18. According to Marx, the class that owns the means of production is called the:

  1. A. Proletariat
  2. B. Bourgeoisie
  3. C. Peasantry
  4. D. Clergy
Answer: B. Bourgeoisie

Q19. In Marx's theory, alienation means that workers become estranged from:

  1. A. The state
  2. B. Their families
  3. C. Religion
  4. D. The product of their labour
Answer: D. The product of their labour

Q20. Herbert Spencer compared society to:

  1. A. A living organism
  2. B. A machine
  3. C. A contract
  4. D. A battlefield
Answer: A. A living organism

Q21. The phrase 'survival of the fittest' was coined by:

  1. A. Karl Marx
  2. B. Charles Darwin
  3. C. Herbert Spencer
  4. D. Auguste Comte
Answer: C. Herbert Spencer

Q22. The book 'The Division of Labour in Society' (1893) was written by:

  1. A. Max Weber
  2. B. Karl Marx
  3. C. Émile Durkheim
  4. D. Georg Simmel
Answer: C. Émile Durkheim

Q23. Weber defined sociology as the interpretive understanding of:

  1. A. Social facts
  2. B. Class conflict
  3. C. Social evolution
  4. D. Social action
Answer: D. Social action

Q24. 'The Communist Manifesto' (1848) was co-authored by:

  1. A. Marx and Engels
  2. B. Marx and Weber
  3. C. Engels and Durkheim
  4. D. Weber and Spencer
Answer: A. Marx and Engels

Q25. Durkheim's 'collective conscience' refers to:

  1. A. Individual beliefs
  2. B. Laws of the state
  3. C. The shared beliefs and moral attitudes common to a society
  4. D. Religious rituals only
Answer: C. The shared beliefs and moral attitudes common to a society
