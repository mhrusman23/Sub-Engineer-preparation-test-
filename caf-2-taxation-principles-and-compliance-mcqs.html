Q1. Income tax in Pakistan is governed by the:

  1. A. Income Tax Ordinance, 2001
  2. B. Companies Act, 2017
  3. C. Contract Act, 1872
  4. D. Sales Tax Act, 1990
Answer: A. Income Tax Ordinance, 2001

Q2. The federal body responsible for collecting federal taxes in Pakistan is the:

  1. A. SBP
  2. B. PSX
  3. C. FBR
  4. D. SECP
Answer: C. FBR

Q3. Sales tax in Pakistan is governed by the:

  1. A. Federal Excise Act, 2005
  2. B. Sales Tax Act, 1990
  3. C. Customs Act only
  4. D. Income Tax Ordinance, 2001
Answer: B. Sales Tax Act, 1990

Q4. Income tax is an example of a:

  1. A. Direct tax
  2. B. Excise duty
  3. C. Indirect tax
  4. D. Customs duty
Answer: A. Direct tax

Q5. Deducting tax at the time of payment is known as:

  1. A. Withholding
  2. B. Refunding
  3. C. Splitting
  4. D. Rebating
Answer: A. Withholding

Q6. The normal tax year for most taxpayers in Pakistan is the twelve months ending on:

  1. A. 31 December
  2. B. 31 March
  3. C. 30 June
  4. D. 30 September
Answer: C. 30 June

Q7. Deliberately understating income to pay less tax is:

  1. A. Evasion
  2. B. Avoidance
  3. C. Planning
  4. D. Deferral
Answer: A. Evasion

Q8. A tax levied on goods and services at each stage of supply is a:

  1. A. Property tax
  2. B. Wealth tax
  3. C. Sales tax
  4. D. Gift tax
Answer: C. Sales tax

Q9. Input tax is the tax a registered person pays on its:

  1. A. Salaries
  2. B. Sales
  3. C. Dividends
  4. D. Purchases
Answer: D. Purchases

Q10. Filing a tax return means submitting a declaration of income and tax to the:

  1. A. Bank
  2. B. Tax authority
  3. C. Stock exchange
  4. D. Court
Answer: B. Tax authority

Q11. What is the primary legal statute governing sales tax registration, collection, and administration in Pakistan?

  1. A. Income Tax Ordinance, 2001
  2. B. Sales Tax Act, 1990
  3. C. Federal Excise Act, 2005
  4. D. Companies Act, 2017
Answer: B. Sales Tax Act, 1990

Q12. Which of the following describes a tax where the rate increases as the taxable income increases?

  1. A. Regressive tax
  2. B. Proportional tax
  3. C. Progressive tax
  4. D. Flat tax
Answer: C. Progressive tax

Q13. Under the Income Tax Ordinance, 2001, what type of tax year is twelve-month period ending on June 30th?

  1. A. Special tax year
  2. B. Transition tax year
  3. C. Normal tax year
  4. D. Calendar tax year
Answer: C. Normal tax year

Q14. What is the term used when a taxpayer legally exploits loopholes in tax laws to minimize their tax liability?

  1. A. Tax evasion
  2. B. Tax avoidance
  3. C. Tax fraud
  4. D. Tax delinquency
Answer: B. Tax avoidance

Q15. Which revenue authority is primarily mandated to administer and collect federal taxes across Pakistan?

  1. A. Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan
  2. B. Federal Board of Revenue
  3. C. State Bank of Pakistan
  4. D. Board of Investment
Answer: B. Federal Board of Revenue

Q16. What is the tax called that is levied on the manufacture, production, or importation of specific select goods within Pakistan?

  1. A. Capital value tax
  2. B. Federal excise duty
  3. C. Income tax
  4. D. Customs duty
Answer: B. Federal excise duty
