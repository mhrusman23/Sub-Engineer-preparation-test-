Q1. In the US federal system, individual income tax is administered by:

  1. A. The FBI
  2. B. The IRS (Internal Revenue Service)
  3. C. The Federal Reserve
  4. D. State police
Answer: B. The IRS (Internal Revenue Service)

Q2. A C corporation is taxed:

  1. A. Only at shareholder level
  2. B. At the corporate level, and dividends again at shareholder level (double taxation)
  3. C. Never
  4. D. Only by states
Answer: B. At the corporate level, and dividends again at shareholder level (double taxation)

Q3. An S corporation's income generally:

  1. A. Is taxed twice
  2. B. Passes through to shareholders' personal returns
  3. C. Is tax-free
  4. D. Is taxed at 50%
Answer: B. Passes through to shareholders' personal returns

Q4. A contract requires offer, acceptance and:

  1. A. A lawyer
  2. B. Consideration
  3. C. Registration
  4. D. A witness always
Answer: B. Consideration

Q5. Under the UCC, a 'merchant' is someone who:

  1. A. Sells anything once
  2. B. Deals in goods of the kind or has special knowledge of them
  3. C. Owns a shop building
  4. D. Is over 21
Answer: B. Deals in goods of the kind or has special knowledge of them

Q6. Bankruptcy Chapter 7 involves:

  1. A. Reorganisation
  2. B. Liquidation of assets to pay creditors
  3. C. No debts
  4. D. Only farms
Answer: B. Liquidation of assets to pay creditors

Q7. A tort is:

  1. A. A tax form
  2. B. A civil wrong that causes harm, such as negligence
  3. C. A contract
  4. D. A type of company
Answer: B. A civil wrong that causes harm, such as negligence

Q8. An agent acting within actual authority binds:

  1. A. Only himself
  2. B. The principal
  3. C. The customer
  4. D. Nobody
Answer: B. The principal

Q9. Estimated tax payments are made by taxpayers:

  1. A. Only once a year
  2. B. Quarterly, when tax is not withheld from income
  3. C. Monthly always
  4. D. Never
Answer: B. Quarterly, when tax is not withheld from income

Q10. A gift between spouses (US citizens) is generally:

  1. A. Fully taxable
  2. B. Unlimited and tax-free under the marital deduction
  3. C. Taxed at 40%
  4. D. Illegal
Answer: B. Unlimited and tax-free under the marital deduction

Q11. Under IRS Circular 230, a practitioner who knows that a client has made an error in a previously filed tax return must:

  1. A. Immediately report the taxpayer to the Internal Revenue Service criminal division
  2. B. Promptly advise the client of the error and the consequences under the law
  3. C. Amend the return unilaterally without informing the client
  4. D. Terminate the professional relationship immediately without notification
Answer: B. Promptly advise the client of the error and the consequences under the law

Q12. Which business entity type is generally subject to a centralized entity-level tax while its shareholders also pay personal income tax on distributed earnings, creating double taxation?

  1. A. General Partnership
  2. B. S Corporation
  3. C. C Corporation
  4. D. Limited Liability Company taxed as a partnership
Answer: C. C Corporation

Q13. Which of the following professional standards governs a CPA's responsibilities when preparing federal tax returns and signing them as a preparer?

  1. A. Statements on Standards for Accounting and Review Services (SSARS)
  2. B. Statements on Standards for Tax Services (SSTS)
  3. C. Statements on Auditing Standards (SAS)
  4. D. Generally Accepted Accounting Principles (GAAP)
Answer: B. Statements on Standards for Tax Services (SSTS)

Q14. A corporation's penalty for underpaying federal estimated tax installments is treated as:

  1. A. A fully deductible business expense in the year paid
  2. B. An addition to tax and is not tax-deductible
  3. C. A partially deductible interest item subject to limitations
  4. D. A fully amortizable intangible asset over 60 months
Answer: B. An addition to tax and is not tax-deductible

Q15. When a CPA prepares a client tax return, under what circumstance may they disclose tax return information without formal taxpayer consent?

  1. A. To satisfy a local bank's credit evaluation of the taxpayer
  2. B. To assist a commercial lender in determining loan eligibility
  3. C. To comply with an enforceable administrative summons or court order
  4. D. To help a supplier evaluate the financial stability of the client
Answer: C. To comply with an enforceable administrative summons or court order

Q16. In the context of federal taxation, how are the standard business tax credits generally treated regarding a taxpayer's tax liability?

  1. A. They are treated as deductions from gross income
  2. B. They provide a dollar-for-dollar reduction of actual tax liability
  3. C. They reduce adjusted gross income directly before taking deductions
  4. D. They are treated as taxable income in the following tax year
Answer: B. They provide a dollar-for-dollar reduction of actual tax liability

Q17. What is the primary tax classification of a newly formed domestic limited liability company (LLC) with a single owner if no election is made with the IRS?

  1. A. Association taxable as a C corporation
  2. B. Partnership
  3. C. Disregarded entity
  4. D. S corporation
Answer: C. Disregarded entity

Q18. Under the modified accelerated cost recovery system (MACRS) for tax depreciation, which property class generally uses the 200% declining balance method for nonfarm residential rental property?

  1. A. 3-year property
  2. B. 5-year property
  3. C. 7-year property
  4. D. Residential rental property uses straight-line
Answer: D. Residential rental property uses straight-line

Q19. Which of the following describes a 'like-kind exchange' qualifying for nonrecognition of gain under Internal Revenue Code Section 1031?

  1. A. Exchange of inventory for machinery
  2. B. Exchange of domestic real property for foreign real property
  3. C. Exchange of apartment building held for investment for commercial land held for investment
  4. D. Exchange of corporate stocks and bonds
Answer: C. Exchange of apartment building held for investment for commercial land held for investment

Q20. In the context of federal gift tax, which of the following transfers is completely exempt from gift tax regardless of the annual exclusion amount?

  1. A. Direct payment of college tuition to an educational institution for a grandchild
  2. B. Transfer of cash to a sibling for birthday celebrations
  3. C. Gift of a vacation home to an adult child
  4. D. Transfer of shares of stock to a close friend
Answer: A. Direct payment of college tuition to an educational institution for a grandchild

Q21. When a partner contributes property with a built-in gain to a partnership in exchange for a partnership interest, how is the partner's initial basis in that partnership interest determined?

  1. A. Fair market value of the contributed property
  2. B. Adjusted basis of the contributed property plus any recognized gain
  3. C. Zero basis until the property is sold
  4. D. Proportionate share of partnership liabilities only
Answer: B. Adjusted basis of the contributed property plus any recognized gain

Q22. For a corporation under federal tax law, how is the penalty for underpaying estimated taxes treated?

  1. A. Fully deductible as ordinary business interest
  2. B. Treated as an addition to tax and is not deductible
  3. C. Partially deductible based on the prime rate
  4. D. Deductible only if reasonable cause is established
Answer: B. Treated as an addition to tax and is not deductible

Q23. How does an S corporation compute its general taxable income according to the Internal Revenue Code?

  1. A. Using complex corporate-specific depreciation methods only
  2. B. In the same general manner as an individual, with specified exceptions
  3. C. Excluding all capital gains from the calculation
  4. D. Using identical tax brackets to a standard C corporation
Answer: B. In the same general manner as an individual, with specified exceptions

Q24. Which of the following items represents a deductible business expense for a sole proprietorship?

  1. A. Federal income tax payments
  2. B. Ordinary and necessary marketing and advertising expenses
  3. C. Personal living and family medical costs
  4. D. Penalties paid for municipal code violations
Answer: B. Ordinary and necessary marketing and advertising expenses

Q25. Under what circumstance is a tax debt generally considered nondischargeable in a bankruptcy liquidation?

  1. A. If the tax return was filed electronically
  2. B. If the underlying liability was incurred through fraud or willful evasion
  3. C. If the tax relates to a period within the last three years
  4. D. If the taxpayer utilized a professional tax preparer
Answer: B. If the underlying liability was incurred through fraud or willful evasion
