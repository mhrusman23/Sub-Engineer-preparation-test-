Q1. In the US federal system, individual income tax is administered by:

  1. A. The FBI
  2. B. The IRS (Internal Revenue Service)
  3. C. The Federal Reserve
  4. D. State police
Answer: B. The IRS (Internal Revenue Service)

Q2. A C corporation is taxed:

  1. A. Only at shareholder level
  2. B. At the corporate level, and dividends again at shareholder level (double taxation)
  3. C. Never
  4. D. Only by states
Answer: B. At the corporate level, and dividends again at shareholder level (double taxation)

Q3. An S corporation's income generally:

  1. A. Is taxed twice
  2. B. Passes through to shareholders' personal returns
  3. C. Is tax-free
  4. D. Is taxed at 50%
Answer: B. Passes through to shareholders' personal returns

Q4. A contract requires offer, acceptance and:

  1. A. A lawyer
  2. B. Consideration
  3. C. Registration
  4. D. A witness always
Answer: B. Consideration

Q5. Under the UCC, a 'merchant' is someone who:

  1. A. Sells anything once
  2. B. Deals in goods of the kind or has special knowledge of them
  3. C. Owns a shop building
  4. D. Is over 21
Answer: B. Deals in goods of the kind or has special knowledge of them

Q6. Bankruptcy Chapter 7 involves:

  1. A. Reorganisation
  2. B. Liquidation of assets to pay creditors
  3. C. No debts
  4. D. Only farms
Answer: B. Liquidation of assets to pay creditors

Q7. A tort is:

  1. A. A tax form
  2. B. A civil wrong that causes harm, such as negligence
  3. C. A contract
  4. D. A type of company
Answer: B. A civil wrong that causes harm, such as negligence

Q8. An agent acting within actual authority binds:

  1. A. Only himself
  2. B. The principal
  3. C. The customer
  4. D. Nobody
Answer: B. The principal

Q9. Estimated tax payments are made by taxpayers:

  1. A. Only once a year
  2. B. Quarterly, when tax is not withheld from income
  3. C. Monthly always
  4. D. Never
Answer: B. Quarterly, when tax is not withheld from income

Q10. A gift between spouses (US citizens) is generally:

  1. A. Fully taxable
  2. B. Unlimited and tax-free under the marital deduction
  3. C. Taxed at 40%
  4. D. Illegal
Answer: B. Unlimited and tax-free under the marital deduction
