Q1. The plural of 'child' is:

  1. A. Childrens
  2. B. Children
  3. C. Childes
  4. D. Childs
Answer: B. Children

Q2. The past tense of 'go' is:

  1. A. Gone
  2. B. Going
  3. C. Goed
  4. D. Went
Answer: D. Went

Q3. The synonym of 'abundant' is:

  1. A. Scarce
  2. B. Plentiful
  3. C. Rare
  4. D. Tiny
Answer: B. Plentiful

Q4. The antonym of 'ancient' is:

  1. A. Aged
  2. B. Historic
  3. C. Old
  4. D. Modern
Answer: D. Modern

Q5. Choose the correct sentence:

  1. A. She has been working here since 2020
  2. B. She is been working here since 2020
  3. C. She has been work here since 2020
  4. D. She have been working here since 2020
Answer: A. She has been working here since 2020

Q6. Neither of the boys ___ present.

  2. B. be
  3. C. were
  4. D. is
Answer: D. is

Q7. He is senior ___ me.

  1. A. over
  2. B. to
  3. C. than

Q8. The idiom 'a piece of cake' means:

  1. A. Very sweet
  2. B. Very easy

Q9. A person who loves books is called a:

  1. A. Bibliophile
  2. B. Pedant
  3. C. Philanthropist

Q10. The article used before a vowel sound is:

  2. B. A

Q11. Which word best completes the sentence: "The scientist's discovery was a _______ breakthrough in medicine."

  1. A. minor
  2. B. significant
  3. C. trivial
  4. D. ordinary
Answer: B. significant

Q12. Choose the sentence that uses the past perfect correctly.

  1. A. She had finished the report before the meeting started.
  2. B. She has finished the report before the meeting started.
  3. C. She finished the report before the meeting started.
  4. D. She had finished the report after the meeting started.
Answer: A. She had finished the report before the meeting started.

Q13. What does the idiom "break the ice" mean?

  1. A. To start a conversation in a friendly way
  2. B. To literally break ice
  3. C. To make a mistake
  4. D. To stop a conversation
Answer: A. To start a conversation in a friendly way

Q14. Which sentence correctly uses the comparative form of the adjective "good"?

  2. B. She is better than her brother.
  3. C. She is gooder than her brother.
  4. D. She is good than her brother.
Answer: B. She is better than her brother.
