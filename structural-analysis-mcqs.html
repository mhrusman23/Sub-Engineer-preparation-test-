Q1. The minimum number of main longitudinal bars in a circular column as per ACI or standard practice is:

  1. A. 4
  2. B. 6
  3. C. 8
  4. D. 12
Answer: B. 6

Q2. What is the bending moment at simply supported ends of a simply supported beam carrying a uniformly distributed load?

  1. A. Maximum
  2. B. Zero
  3. C. Minimum positive
  4. D. Infinite
Answer: B. Zero

Q3. A beam supported by a hinge at one end and a roller at the other is:

  1. A. Continuous
  2. B. Simply supported
  3. C. Fixed
  4. D. Cantilever
Answer: B. Simply supported

Q4. A cantilever beam is fixed at:

  1. A. No end
  2. B. Both ends
  3. C. Middle only
  4. D. One end and free at the other
Answer: D. One end and free at the other

Q5. The degree of static indeterminacy of a simply supported beam is:

  1. A. 2
  2. B. 1
  3. C. 0
  4. D. 3
Answer: C. 0

Q6. The bending moment at the free end of a cantilever is:

  1. A. Maximum
  2. B. Zero
  3. C. Infinite
  4. D. Equal to the load
Answer: B. Zero

Q7. The shear force diagram for a simply supported beam with a central point load is:

  1. A. Rectangular with a sign change at the centre
  2. B. Parabolic
  3. C. Triangular only
  4. D. Zero
Answer: A. Rectangular with a sign change at the centre

Q8. Maximum bending moment in a simply supported beam with a UDL w and span L is:

  1. A. wL squared / 4
  2. B. wL squared / 2
  3. C. wL squared / 8
  4. D. wL / 2
Answer: C. wL squared / 8

Q9. A truss member carries only:

  1. A. Torsion
  2. B. Shear only
  3. C. Axial force
  4. D. Bending moment
Answer: C. Axial force

Q10. The method of joints is used to analyse:

  1. A. Trusses
  2. B. Foundations
  3. C. Beams only
  4. D. Slabs
Answer: A. Trusses

Q11. The moment distribution method was developed by:

  1. A. Hardy Cross
  2. B. Mohr
  3. C. Maxwell
  4. D. Castigliano
Answer: A. Hardy Cross

Q12. A point of contraflexure is a point where the bending moment:

  1. A. Is maximum
  2. B. Is infinite
  3. C. Is zero always
  4. D. Changes sign
Answer: D. Changes sign

Q13. The triangular space enclosed between the extrados and the line joining the springings is called:

  1. A. Spandril
  2. B. Soffit
  3. C. Haunch
  4. D. Impost
Answer: A. Spandril

Q14. Which type of stress is induced in a tie member of a roof truss?

  1. A. Compressive stress
  2. B. Tensile stress
  3. C. Shear stress
  4. D. Torsional stress
Answer: B. Tensile stress

Q15. Which of the following structures has zero static indeterminacy?

  1. A. Fixed-fixed beam
  2. B. Continuous beam
  3. C. Simply supported beam
  4. D. Propped cantilever beam
Answer: C. Simply supported beam

Q16. What is the Muller-Breslau principle primarily used to determine?

  1. A. Deflections
  2. B. Influence lines
  3. C. Bending moments
  4. D. Shear forces
Answer: B. Influence lines

Q17. In a three-hinged parabolic arch subjected to a uniformly distributed load over the entire span, the bending moment at any section is:

  1. A. Maximum
  2. B. Zero
  3. C. Constant
  4. D. Minimum
Answer: B. Zero

Q18. What does the area of the shear force diagram between two points on a beam represent?

  1. A. Slope at that section
  2. B. Deflection at that section
  3. C. Change in bending moment between the points
  4. D. Total load between the points
Answer: C. Change in bending moment between the points

Q19. For a plane truss containing 'm' members and 'j' joints to be statically determinate internally, which relationship must hold true?

  1. A. m = 2j - 3
  2. B. m = 3j - 6
  3. C. m = j - 2
  4. D. m = 2j + 3
Answer: A. m = 2j - 3
