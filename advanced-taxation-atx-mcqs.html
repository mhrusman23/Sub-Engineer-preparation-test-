Q1. The main purpose of a double tax treaty is to:

  1. A. Increase tax rates
  2. B. Remove all taxes
  3. C. Tax only residents
  4. D. Relieve double taxation
Answer: D. Relieve double taxation

Q2. The arm's length principle applies to:

  1. A. Payroll
  2. B. Dividends only
  3. C. VAT returns
  4. D. Transfer pricing
Answer: D. Transfer pricing

Q3. Thin capitalisation rules restrict:

  1. A. Interest deductions on excessive debt
  2. B. Capital allowances
  3. C. Share issues
  4. D. Dividends
Answer: A. Interest deductions on excessive debt

Q4. BEPS stands for base erosion and:

  1. A. Profit shifting
  2. B. Property sharing
  3. C. Pricing standards
  4. D. Payment scheduling
Answer: A. Profit shifting

Q5. A permanent establishment is generally a:

  1. A. Fixed place of business in another country
  2. B. Temporary holiday
  3. C. Bank account only
  4. D. Website domain only
Answer: A. Fixed place of business in another country

Q6. Controlled foreign company rules target:

  1. A. Employee bonuses
  2. B. Diversion of profits to low-tax jurisdictions
  3. C. Local sales
  4. D. Charity donations
Answer: B. Diversion of profits to low-tax jurisdictions

Q7. Under the credit method of double tax relief, foreign tax paid is:

  1. A. Added to profit
  2. B. Doubled
  3. C. Credited against domestic tax
  4. D. Ignored
Answer: C. Credited against domestic tax

Q8. A general anti-abuse rule (GAAR) is designed to counter:

  1. A. All planning
  2. B. Salary payments
  3. C. Audits
  4. D. Abusive tax arrangements
Answer: D. Abusive tax arrangements

Q9. Withholding tax commonly applies to cross-border payments of royalties, interest and:

  1. A. Salaries only
  2. B. Rent of home
  3. C. Loans of goods
  4. D. Dividends
Answer: D. Dividends

Q10. Tax evasion is:

  1. A. Encouraged
  2. B. Illegal
  3. C. Always legal
  4. D. A deduction
Answer: B. Illegal

Q11. Which specific relief mechanism completely exempts foreign branch profits from domestic taxation in certain jurisdictions?

  1. A. Tax credit method
  2. B. Exemption method
  3. C. Deduction method
  4. D. Roll-over relief
Answer: B. Exemption method

Q12. What is the primary objective of introducing a Patent Box regime in corporate tax planning?

  1. A. To increase customs duties on imported technology
  2. B. To penalize companies for outsourcing research
  3. C. To offer a lower corporate tax rate on income derived from intellectual property
  4. D. To eliminate all withholding taxes on royalties
Answer: C. To offer a lower corporate tax rate on income derived from intellectual property

Q13. Under transfer pricing regulations, which method compares the gross margin of a tested party with the gross margins of independent comparable firms?

  1. A. Comparable uncontrolled price method
  2. B. Resale price method
  3. C. Transactional net margin method
  4. D. Profit split method
Answer: B. Resale price method

Q14. What does the 'nexus approach' for intellectual property regimes under BEPS Action 5 primarily require?

  1. A. Immediate write-off of all intangible assets
  2. B. A direct link between tax benefits and substantial R&D expenditures
  3. C. Mandatory registration of patents in tax havens
  4. D. Equal taxation of all foreign and domestic dividends
Answer: B. A direct link between tax benefits and substantial R&D expenditures

Q15. In the context of corporate reorganizations, what does 'group relief' typically allow a company to do?

  1. A. Avoid filing annual tax returns
  2. B. Surrender trading losses to another group company to offset its profits
  3. C. Exempt all capital gains from group transactions
  4. D. Delay the payment of employee income tax
Answer: B. Surrender trading losses to another group company to offset its profits

Q16. What is the main function of an Advance Pricing Agreement (APA) between a taxpayer and tax authorities?

  1. A. To determine the exact tax penalty for past evasion
  2. B. To settle ongoing tax litigation in court
  3. C. To agree on an appropriate transfer pricing methodology for future transactions in advance
  4. D. To provide an automatic waiver of all withholding taxes
Answer: C. To agree on an appropriate transfer pricing methodology for future transactions in advance

Q17. Approximately how many professional skills marks are embedded within the 50-mark Section A question of the ATX exam?

  1. A. 5 professional skills marks
  2. B. 10 professional skills marks
  3. C. 15 professional skills marks
  4. D. 25 professional skills marks
Answer: B. 10 professional skills marks

Q18. Which of the following best describes the core educational shift from the foundational Taxation (TX) paper to Advanced Taxation (ATX)?

  1. A. Moving from strategic advisory to basic manual bookkeeping
  2. B. Shifting emphasis from pure computations to complex advice, tax interactions, and options analysis
  3. C. Focusing entirely on multi-national audit procedures and risk assessment
  4. D. Replacing UK tax laws exclusively with international accounting standards
Answer: B. Shifting emphasis from pure computations to complex advice, tax interactions, and options analysis

Q19. Which of the following topics is specifically examined as part of the ethical component in Section A of the ATX exam?

  1. A. Plant and machinery depreciation rates
  2. B. Money laundering regulations and disclosures
  3. C. Optimal capital structure calculations
  4. D. Inventory valuation methods under FIFO
Answer: B. Money laundering regulations and disclosures

Q20. During an audit, the accountant discovers that a client has been claiming a tax deduction for a loan interest that was actually paid to a related party. What is the most appropriate action?

  1. A. Approve the deduction as it is a legitimate expense
  2. B. Reject the deduction and advise the client to repay the loan
  3. C. Adjust the deduction and disclose the related‑party nature to HMRC
  4. D. Ignore the issue as it does not affect the final tax return
Answer: C. Adjust the deduction and disclose the related‑party nature to HMRC

Q21. A UK taxpayer receives a royalty income of £12,000 from a foreign source. The foreign country imposes a 10% withholding tax. What is the maximum amount of UK tax that can be credited against the UK tax due on this royalty?

  1. A. £1,200
  2. B. £1,500
  3. C. £12,000
  4. D. £0
Answer: A. £1,200

Q22. A UK resident company receives a dividend from a foreign subsidiary. The dividend is subject to a 15% withholding tax in the source country. Under the double tax relief system, how is this foreign tax treated in the UK?

  1. A. It is added to the company's UK tax liability
  2. B. It is ignored for UK tax purposes
  3. C. It is credited against the UK tax payable on the dividend
  4. D. It is treated as a deductible expense
Answer: C. It is credited against the UK tax payable on the dividend

Q23. A UK corporation is considering a transfer of a fixed asset to a subsidiary in a low‑tax jurisdiction. Which tax regime is most likely to apply to the transfer?

  1. A. Capital Gains Tax
  2. B. Corporation Tax on disposal
  3. C. Value Added Tax
  4. D. No tax as it is an intra‑group transfer
Answer: B. Corporation Tax on disposal

Q24. A UK taxpayer claims a loss on a self‑employment activity that was carried out for 3 months in 2023. The loss is £4,000. Which of the following is the correct treatment for the loss?

  1. A. The loss can be carried forward to offset future profits
  2. B. The loss can be set against other income in the same year
  3. C. The loss must be written off permanently
  4. D. The loss is only deductible if the activity is a trade
Answer: B. The loss can be set against other income in the same year

Q25. A General Anti-Abuse Rule (GAAR) is typically enacted to target:

  1. A. Unintentional computational errors in tax returns
  2. B. Artificial arrangements entered into primarily for tax avoidance
  3. C. Deliberate concealment of business income
  4. D. Failure to submit corporate tax documents on time
Answer: B. Artificial arrangements entered into primarily for tax avoidance
