Q1. Angle between both lenses of optical square......

  1. A. 120°
  2. B. 45°
  3. C. 90°
  4. D. 30°
Answer: B. 45°

Q2. Gypsum is added to cement at the time of manufacturing for the purpose of....

  1. A. increase the setting time
  2. B. increase the strength
  3. C. decrease the setting time
  4. D. increase the weight
Answer: A. increase the setting time

Q3. The roof which slopes in two directions....

  1. A. hipped
  2. B. gambrel
  3. C. Mansard
  4. D. gable
Answer: B. gambrel

Q4. Water bearing strata....

  1. A. aquifer
  2. B. lake
  3. C. Sea
  4. D. deep well
Answer: A. aquifer

Q5. A tank in which sedimentation take place is usually termed as....

  1. A. Settling tank
  2. B. clarifier
  3. C. grit chamber
  4. D. both a & b
Answer: D. both a & b

Q6. On straight line spacing between two manholes for branch sewer line is kept in meters....

  1. A. 100 - 150
  2. B. 300 - 500
  3. C. 150 - 200
  4. D. 50 - 100
Answer: B. 300 - 500

Q7. In well-conditioned triangle no angle should be less than....

  1. A. 60°
  2. B. 45°
  3. C. 70°
  4. D. 30°
Answer: D. 30°

Q8. The nature of sag correction is always....

  1. A. Positive
  2. B. negative
  3. C. both a & b
  4. D. none of these
Answer: B. negative

Q9. Rise of step of stair x tread or (R x T) =..........

  1. A. 430 - 500 cm
  2. B. 330 - 400 cm
  3. C. 400 - 430 cm
  4. D. 300 - 330 cm
Answer: C. 400 - 430 cm

Q10. The minimum slope of stair should be......

  1. A. gloves
