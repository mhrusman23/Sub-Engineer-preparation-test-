Jr Engineer Civil WAPDA PTS (19.08.2018) – Past Paper Solved MCQs

Solved MCQs from the PPSC paper “Jr Engineer Civil WAPDA PTS (19.08.2018)”. Full paper has 65 questions; the first 25 are shown here with correct answers. Open the app to take the complete timed test.

Q1. Lacquer paints:

  1. A. All the above (applied on structural steel, less durable than enamel, consist of resin and nitro-cellulose, contain alcohol as thinner)
  2. B. Are generally applied on structural steel only
  3. C. Are less durable as compared to enamel paints only
  4. D. Consist of resin and nitro-cellulose only
Answer: A. All the above (applied on structural steel, less durable than enamel, consist of resin and nitro-cellulose, contain alcohol as thinner)

Q2. Pick up the polymineralic rock from the following:

  1. A. Granite
  2. B. Quartz sand
  3. C. Pure gypsum
  4. D. Magnesite
Answer: A. Granite

Q3. For the manufacture of Portland cement, the proportions of raw materials used are:

  1. A. Lime 63%, silica 22%, other ingredients 15%
  2. B. Lime 22%, silica 63%, other ingredients 15%
  3. C. Silica 40%, lime 40%, other ingredients 20%
  4. D. Silica 70%, lime 20%, other ingredients 10%
Answer: A. Lime 63%, silica 22%, other ingredients 15%

Q4. Pick up the correct statement:

  1. A. All the above (soft stones for carving, light stones for arches, hard stones to stand high pressure)
  2. B. Soft stones are required for carving only
  3. C. Light stones are required for arches only
  4. D. Hard stones are required to stand high pressure only
Answer: A. All the above (soft stones for carving, light stones for arches, hard stones to stand high pressure)

Q5. The minimum water content at which the soil retains its liquid state and also possesses a small shearing strength against flowing is known as:

  1. A. Liquid limit
  2. B. Plastic limit
  3. C. Shrinkage limit
  4. D. Permeability limit
Answer: A. Liquid limit

Q6. Dado is usually provided in:

  1. A. Bath rooms
  2. B. Dinning halls
  3. C. Living rooms
  4. D. Verandahs
Answer: A. Bath rooms

Q7. The 9 cm x 9 cm side of a brick as seen in the wall face is generally known as:

  1. A. Header
  2. B. Stretcher
  3. C. Front
  4. D. Side
Answer: A. Header

Q8. The under surface of an arch is called:

  1. A. Soffit
  2. B. Intrados
  3. C. Haunch
  4. D. Back
Answer: A. Soffit

Q9. The process of making the background rough before plastering is:

  1. A. Hacking
  2. B. Dubbing
  3. C. Blistering
  4. D. Peeling
Answer: A. Hacking

Q10. The angular steps used for changing direction of the stairs are called:

  1. A. Winders
  2. B. Round steps
  3. C. Angular steps
  4. D. Radial steps
Answer: A. Winders

Q11. The line of intersection of the surfaces of a sloping roof forming an external angle exceeding 180 degrees is:

  1. A. Hip
  2. B. Ridge
  3. C. Valley
  4. D. None of these
Answer: A. Hip

Q12. A wooden block hinged on a post outside a door is known as:

  1. A. Cleat
  2. B. Stop
  3. C. Horn
  4. D. None of these
Answer: A. Cleat

Q13. If 1500 g of water is required to have 1875 g of cement paste of normal consistency, the percentage of water is:

  1. A. 25%
  2. B. 20%
  3. C. 30%
  4. D. 35%
Answer: A. 25%

Q14. For given water content, workability decreases if the concrete aggregates contain an excess of:

  1. A. All the above (thin, flat, elongated and flaky particles)
  2. B. Thin particles only
  3. C. Flat particles only
  4. D. Elongated particles only
Answer: A. All the above (thin, flat, elongated and flaky particles)

Q15. After casting, an ordinary cement concrete on drying:

  1. A. Shrinks
  2. B. Expands
  3. C. Mixes
  4. D. None of these
Answer: A. Shrinks

Q16. Hydration of cement is due to chemical action of water with:

  1. A. All the above (tricalcium silicate, dicalcium silicate and tricalcium aluminate)
  2. B. Tricalcium silicate and dicalcium silicate only
  3. C. Dicalcium silicate and tricalcium aluminate only
  4. D. Tricalcium aluminate only
Answer: A. All the above (tricalcium silicate, dicalcium silicate and tricalcium aluminate)

Q17. Proper proportioning of concrete ensures:

  1. A. All the above (desired strength and workability, durability, water tightness, resistance to water)
  2. B. Desired strength and workability only
  3. C. Desired durability only
  4. D. Water tightness only
Answer: A. All the above (desired strength and workability, durability, water tightness, resistance to water)

Q18. The bulk density of aggregates does NOT depend upon:

  1. A. Size and shape of the container
  2. B. Size and shape of aggregates
  3. C. Specific gravity of aggregates
  4. D. Grading of aggregates
Answer: A. Size and shape of the container

Q19. Curing:

  1. A. All of the above (reduces shrinkage, preserves properties, prevents loss of water by evaporation)
  2. B. Reduces the shrinkage of concrete only
  3. C. Preserves the properties of concrete only
  4. D. Prevents the loss of water by evaporation only
Answer: A. All of the above (reduces shrinkage, preserves properties, prevents loss of water by evaporation)

Q20. Pick up the correct proportions of chemical ingredients of cement:

  1. A. Lime : Silica : Alumina : Iron oxide = 63 : 22 : 6 : 3
  2. B. Silica : Lime : Alumina : Iron oxide = 63 : 22 : 6 : 3
  3. C. Alumina : Silica : Lime : Iron oxide = 63 : 22 : 6 : 3
  4. D. Iron oxide : Alumina : Silica : Lime = 63 : 22 : 6 : 3
Answer: A. Lime : Silica : Alumina : Iron oxide = 63 : 22 : 6 : 3

Q21. Slump test is measure of:

  1. A. All (mobility, consistency, homogeneity)
  2. B. Mobility only
  3. C. Consistency only
  4. D. Homogeneity only
Answer: A. All (mobility, consistency, homogeneity)

Q22. The strength and quality of concrete depend upon:

  1. A. All the above (grading, surface area, shape and surface texture of aggregates)
  2. B. Grading of aggregates only
  3. C. Surface area of aggregates only
  4. D. Shape of aggregates only
Answer: A. All the above (grading, surface area, shape and surface texture of aggregates)

Q23. The process of mixing, transporting, placing and compacting concrete using Ordinary Portland Cement should not take more than:

  1. A. 30 minutes
  2. B. 40 minutes
  3. C. 60 minutes
  4. D. 90 minutes
Answer: A. 30 minutes

Q24. If 50 kg of fine aggregates and 100 kg of coarse aggregates are mixed in a concrete whose water-cement ratio is 0.6, the weight of water required for harsh mix is:

  1. A. 12 kg
  2. B. 8 kg
  3. C. 10 kg
  4. D. 15 kg
Answer: A. 12 kg

Q25. The bulk density of aggregates depends upon:

  1. A. All the above (shape, grading, compaction)
  2. B. Shape only
  3. C. Grading only
  4. D. Compaction only
Answer: A. All the above (shape, grading, compaction)
