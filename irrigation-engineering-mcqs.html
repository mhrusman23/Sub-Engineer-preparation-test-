Q1. Irrigation is the artificial application of:

  1. A. Water to crops
  2. B. Pesticide
  3. C. Fertiliser
  4. D. Seeds
Answer: A. Water to crops

Q2. A canal that takes off from a river is called a:

  1. A. Watercourse
  2. B. Distributary
  3. C. Main canal
  4. D. Minor
Answer: C. Main canal

Q3. A barrage differs from a weir in that it has:

  1. A. Gates to regulate water levels
  2. B. Only a crest
  3. C. No pier
  4. D. No structure
Answer: A. Gates to regulate water levels

Q4. Duty of water is the area irrigated by:

  1. A. One litre
  2. B. One hectare of water
  3. C. One cumec of water
  4. D. One tonne
Answer: C. One cumec of water

Q5. A cross regulator is used to control water levels in the:

  1. A. River bed
  2. B. Drain only
  3. C. Parent canal
  4. D. Watercourse only
Answer: C. Parent canal

Q6. Delta of a crop is the total depth of water required:

  1. A. In one hour
  2. B. During the crop period
  3. C. In one day
  4. D. In one season only
Answer: B. During the crop period

Q7. Canal lining is done to reduce:

  1. A. Seepage losses
  2. B. Slope
  3. C. Evaporation only
  4. D. Flow
Answer: A. Seepage losses

Q8. An aqueduct carries a:

  1. A. Road over a canal
  2. B. Canal under a road
  3. C. Drain over a canal
  4. D. Canal over a drain
Answer: D. Canal over a drain

Q9. The Indus Basin Irrigation System is one of the largest:

  1. A. Hydropower systems
  2. B. Dry ports
  3. C. Contiguous irrigation systems in the world
  4. D. Rail systems
Answer: C. Contiguous irrigation systems in the world

Q10. Sprinkler irrigation delivers water in the form of:

  1. A. Flooding
  2. B. Basins
  3. C. Spray like rainfall
  4. D. Furrows
Answer: C. Spray like rainfall

Q11. The depth of cut in a canal is defined as the vertical distance from the top of the berm to the bottom of the channel. What is the typical formula for the berm height in a rectangular canal?

  1. A. Berm height = 0.5 × depth of cut
  2. B. Berm height = 1.5 × depth of cut
  3. C. Berm height = 2 × depth of cut
  4. D. Berm height = 3 × depth of cut
Answer: B. Berm height = 1.5 × depth of cut

Q12. A canal constructed to drain excess water from a water‑logged field is commonly known as a:

  1. A. Drainage canal
  2. B. Flood control canal
  3. C. Water‑logging canal
  4. D. Ridge canal
Answer: A. Drainage canal

Q13. Which of the following is NOT a primary purpose of lining a canal?

  1. A. Reduce seepage losses
  2. B. Decrease evaporation
  3. C. Improve water quality
  4. D. Lower construction cost
Answer: D. Lower construction cost

Q14. A canal that runs along the side of a valley, following the slope of the land, is called a:

  1. A. Contour canal
  2. B. Ridge canal
  3. C. Side‑slope canal
  4. D. Valley canal
Answer: C. Side‑slope canal

Q15. In a gravity canal, the free surface level is maintained at what type of line to ensure uniform flow?

  1. A. Elevation line
  2. B. Contour line
  3. C. Slope line
  4. D. Gradient line
Answer: B. Contour line

Q16. The sodium adsorption ratio (SAR) of irrigation water is calculated using the concentrations of Na⁺, Ca²⁺, and Mg²⁺. If SAR is found to be 12, the water is classified as:

  1. A. Low sodium water
  2. B. Medium sodium water
  3. C. High sodium water
  4. D. Very high sodium water
Answer: B. Medium sodium water

Q17. What is the primary function of a dividing wall in a diversion headwork?

  1. A. To separate the under-sluices from the main weir body
  2. B. To stop all flood water from entering the canal
  3. C. To increase the storage capacity of the reservoir
  4. D. To generate hydroelectric power
Answer: A. To separate the under-sluices from the main weir body

Q18. Which type of canal alignment is generally preferred when it runs roughly parallel to the natural drainage of the country?

  1. A. Contour canal
  2. B. Ridge canal
  3. C. Side-slope canal
  4. D. Inundation canal
Answer: B. Ridge canal

Q19. According to Lacey's silt theory, what is the regime velocity of a channel proportional to?

  1. A. Square root of the silt factor
  2. B. Cube root of the discharge and silt factor
  3. C. Directly to the bed slope
  4. D. Inverse of the wetted perimeter
Answer: B. Cube root of the discharge and silt factor

Q20. Which hydraulic structure is constructed across a drainage channel to pass canal water safely over it?

  1. A. Superpassage
  2. B. Aqueduct
  3. C. Level crossing
  4. D. Inlet and outlet
Answer: B. Aqueduct

Q21. What does the term 'kor period' signify in irrigation engineering?

  1. A. The entire duration from sowing to harvesting
  2. B. The brief initial period when a crop requires maximum watering
  3. C. The time taken to fill a reservoir completely
  4. D. The period during which canals remain closed for maintenance
Answer: B. The brief initial period when a crop requires maximum watering

Q22. What is the primary cause of waterlogging in irrigated agricultural lands?

  1. A. Excessive chemical fertilizers
  2. B. Over-irrigation and poor natural drainage
  3. C. 种植 deep-rooted crops
  4. D. High wind velocity during summer
Answer: B. Over-irrigation and poor natural drainage

Q23. A watershed canal aligned along the ridge of a command area has the primary advantage of:

  1. A. Requiring heavy cross-drainage works
  2. B. Irrigating both sides of the canal by gravity
  3. C. Eliminating the need for canal lining
  4. D. Maximizing seepage and percolation losses
Answer: B. Irrigating both sides of the canal by gravity

Q24. The total depth of water required to bring a crop to maturity from sowing to harvesting, expressed in centimeters or meters, is called the:

  1. A. Duty
  2. B. Delta
  3. C. Base period
  4. D. Consumptive use
Answer: B. Delta

Q25. In plain topography, major irrigation distribution canals are predominantly aligned along the:

  1. A. Valley line
  2. B. Contour line
  3. C. Ridge line
  4. D. Transverse line
Answer: C. Ridge line
