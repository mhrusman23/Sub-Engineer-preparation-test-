Q1. 'Curriculum' means:

  1. A. School holidays
  2. B. The subjects and content taught in a course
  3. C. School uniform
  4. D. Exam results
Answer: B. The subjects and content taught in a course

Q2. A 'scholarship' is:

  1. A. A school building
  2. B. Money given to a student to help pay for education
  3. C. A type of exam
  4. D. A teacher's salary
Answer: B. Money given to a student to help pay for education

Q3. 'Literacy' means the ability to:

  1. A. Count money
  2. B. Read and write
  3. C. Drive
  4. D. Speak many languages
Answer: B. Read and write

Q4. A 'graduate' is a person who:

  1. A. Just started school
  2. B. Has completed a university degree
  3. C. Failed an exam
  4. D. Teaches children
Answer: B. Has completed a university degree

Q5. 'Tuition fees' are:

  1. A. Free books
  2. B. Money paid for teaching/instruction
  3. C. Exam fines
  4. D. Bus fares
Answer: B. Money paid for teaching/instruction

Q6. To 'enrol' in a course means to:

  1. A. Leave it
  2. B. Register and join it officially
  3. C. Teach it
  4. D. Cancel it
Answer: B. Register and join it officially

Q7. 'Distance learning' means:

  1. A. Studying in a far school
  2. B. Studying remotely, usually online
  3. C. Running to school
  4. D. Evening classes only
Answer: B. Studying remotely, usually online

Q8. A 'lecture' is:

  1. A. A sports event
  2. B. A formal talk given to students on a subject
  3. C. A written exam
  4. D. A school trip
Answer: B. A formal talk given to students on a subject

Q9. 'Assignment' in education means:

  1. A. A classroom
  2. B. A piece of work given to students to complete
  3. C. A holiday
  4. D. A certificate
Answer: B. A piece of work given to students to complete

Q10. To 'drop out' of school means to:

  1. A. Enter school
  2. B. Leave school before finishing
  3. C. Fail one test
  4. D. Change schools
Answer: B. Leave school before finishing

Q11. 'Higher education' refers to:

  1. A. Primary school
  2. B. University and college education
  3. C. Home schooling
  4. D. Nursery
Answer: B. University and college education

Q12. A 'syllabus' is:

  1. A. An exam paper
  2. B. An outline of the topics covered in a course
  3. C. A mark sheet
  4. D. A library card
Answer: B. An outline of the topics covered in a course

Q13. 'Vocational training' prepares people for:

  1. A. University research
  2. B. Practical jobs and specific trades
  3. C. Sports
  4. D. Music
Answer: B. Practical jobs and specific trades

Q14. To 'cram' before an exam means to:

  1. A. Sleep well
  2. B. Study a lot in a short time at the last moment
  3. C. Cheat
  4. D. Skip the exam
Answer: B. Study a lot in a short time at the last moment

Q15. 'Feedback' from a teacher means:

  1. A. A prize
  2. B. Comments about how well you did and how to improve
  3. C. A new book
  4. D. An absence note
Answer: B. Comments about how well you did and how to improve

Q16. A 'semester' is:

  1. A. A full degree
  2. B. One half of an academic year
  3. C. A single lesson
  4. D. An exam week
Answer: B. One half of an academic year

Q17. 'Plagiarism' means:

  1. A. Good writing
  2. B. Copying someone else's work and claiming it as your own
  3. C. Fast reading
  4. D. Group study
Answer: B. Copying someone else's work and claiming it as your own

Q18. A 'thesis' is:

  1. A. A short note
  2. B. A long research paper for a degree
  3. C. A timetable
  4. D. A quiz
Answer: B. A long research paper for a degree

Q19. 'Compulsory' subjects are those that:

  1. A. Are optional
  2. B. Must be taken by everyone
  3. C. Are free
  4. D. Are online
Answer: B. Must be taken by everyone

Q20. To 'major in' a subject means to:

  1. A. Fail it
  2. B. Study it as your main subject at university
  3. C. Teach it
  4. D. Drop it
Answer: B. Study it as your main subject at university
