Q1. NPV equals the present value of cash inflows minus the:

  1. A. Interest earned
  2. B. Tax paid
  3. C. Initial investment
  4. D. Future inflows
Answer: C. Initial investment

Q2. WACC is used as a discount rate for projects with:

  1. A. Zero risk
  2. B. Negative cash flows only
  3. C. Average business risk
  4. D. Unknown risk only
Answer: C. Average business risk

Q3. The CAPM links expected return to:

  1. A. Total risk only
  2. B. Inflation only
  3. C. Dividend policy
  4. D. Systematic risk
Answer: D. Systematic risk

Q4. An option that gives the right to buy an asset at a set price is a:

  1. A. Forward
  2. B. Put option
  3. C. Call option
  4. D. Swap
Answer: C. Call option

Q5. A put option gives the holder the right to:

  1. A. Buy at the exercise price
  2. B. Receive dividends
  3. C. Vote at meetings
  4. D. Sell at the exercise price
Answer: D. Sell at the exercise price

Q6. An interest rate swap commonly exchanges:

  1. A. Shares
  2. B. Principals
  3. C. Currencies permanently
  4. D. Fixed and floating payments
Answer: D. Fixed and floating payments

Q7. The price-earnings ratio multiplied by earnings per share gives:

  1. A. Share price
  2. B. Total equity
  3. C. Dividend
  4. D. Net assets
Answer: A. Share price

Q8. Free cash flow to the firm is cash flow available to:

  1. A. Lenders only
  2. B. Government only
  3. C. Shareholders only
  4. D. All capital providers
Answer: D. All capital providers

Q9. Weighted average cost of capital weights each source by its:

  1. A. Proportion of total capital
  2. B. Rank
  3. C. Age
  4. D. Risk-free rate
Answer: A. Proportion of total capital

Q10. A forward contract fixes the:

  1. A. Price at the end only
  2. B. Tax rate
  3. C. Dividend
  4. D. Exchange rate for a future date
Answer: D. Exchange rate for a future date

Q11. In the context of capital budgeting, the discount rate that reflects the weighted average cost of all sources of capital is known as:

  1. A. Cost of Debt
  2. B. Weighted Average Cost of Capital
  3. C. Internal Rate of Return
  4. D. Marginal Cost of Capital
Answer: B. Weighted Average Cost of Capital

Q12. The Modigliani‑Miller theorem without taxes states that a firm’s value is:

  1. A. Independent of its capital structure
  2. B. Dependent on the debt‑to‑equity ratio
  3. C. Determined solely by its assets
  4. D. Maximised when debt equals equity
Answer: A. Independent of its capital structure

Q13. A firm’s free cash flow to the firm (FCFF) is best described as:

  1. A. Cash available to equity holders only
  2. B. Cash available to all capital providers after operating expenses and taxes
  3. C. Cash generated from sales before interest and taxes
  4. D. Cash used for dividend payments
Answer: B. Cash available to all capital providers after operating expenses and taxes

Q14. Which financial metric measures the incremental value created by a project after covering its cost of capital?

  1. A. Economic Value Added
  2. B. Return on Equity
  3. C. Operating Profit
  4. D. Cash Flow from Operations
Answer: A. Economic Value Added

Q15. Which of the following is a primary objective of strategic financial management?

  1. A. Maximising short‑term liquidity
  2. B. Minimising tax liabilities only
  3. C. Optimising the firm’s capital structure
  4. D. Increasing inventory turnover
Answer: C. Optimising the firm’s capital structure

Q16. Which ratio indicates the proportion of a company's capital structure financed by debt?

  1. A. Debt to Equity Ratio
  2. B. Current Ratio
  3. C. Return on Assets
  4. D. Gross Profit Margin
Answer: A. Debt to Equity Ratio

Q17. A company issues a 5‑year bond with a face value of PKR 1,000,000 and a coupon rate of 6% payable annually. If the market yield at issuance is 7%, what is the present value of the bond?

  1. A. PKR 917,000
  2. B. PKR 930,000
  3. C. PKR 945,000
  4. D. PKR 960,000
Answer: A. PKR 917,000

Q18. A company is evaluating a project that requires an initial outlay of PKR 10,000,000 and is expected to generate annual cash inflows of PKR 3,000,000 for 5 years. If the appropriate discount rate is 12%, what is the Net Present Value (NPV) of the project?

  1. A. PKR 1,200,000
  2. B. PKR 2,500,000
  3. C. PKR 3,800,000
  4. D. PKR 5,100,000
Answer: C. PKR 3,800,000

Q19. During a merger, the acquiring company pays a premium of 20% over the target's market price. Which financial metric is most appropriate to assess whether the deal creates value for shareholders?

  1. A. Return on Equity (ROE)
  2. B. Earnings Per Share (EPS)
  3. C. Economic Value Added (EVA)
  4. D. Debt‑to‑Equity Ratio (D/E)
Answer: C. Economic Value Added (EVA)

Q20. A firm has the following capital structure: 60% equity at a cost of 18% and 40% debt at a cost of 8% after a tax rate of 35%. What is the firm's weighted average cost of capital (WACC)?

  1. A. 12.2%
  2. B. 13.5%
  3. C. 14.8%
  4. D. 16.1%
Answer: B. 13.5%

Q21. Which of the following best describes the purpose of a forward rate agreement (FRA) in interest rate risk management?

  1. A. To lock in a future exchange rate for currency transactions
  2. B. To hedge against future changes in commodity prices
  3. C. To fix the interest rate on a loan that will commence in the future
  4. D. To guarantee a fixed dividend payment to shareholders
Answer: C. To fix the interest rate on a loan that will commence in the future

Q22. A firm uses a money‑market hedge to protect against a future foreign currency payment of USD 500,000 due in 90 days. If the current spot rate is PKR 280 per USD and the 90‑day forward rate is PKR 282 per USD, what is the effective hedged rate?

  1. A. PKR 278
  2. B. PKR 280
  3. C. PKR 282
  4. D. PKR 284
Answer: C. PKR 282

Q23. The pecking order framework in capital structure decisions suggests that firms prefer to finance new investments using:

  1. A. Internal retained earnings first
  2. B. New equity issuances first
  3. C. Subordinated convertible bonds first
  4. D. Venture capital funding first
Answer: A. Internal retained earnings first

Q24. In the context of corporate restructuring and strategic finance, asset unbundling typically involves:

  1. A. Separating distinct business segments into independent units
  2. B. Merging two competing firms into a single conglomerate
  3. C. Issuing new equity to finance inventory purchases
  4. D. Converting short-term debt into long-term bonds
Answer: A. Separating distinct business segments into independent units

Q25. When applying Free Cash Flow to Equity (FCFE) models for enterprise and equity valuation, the cash flow available belongs exclusively to:

  1. A. Common shareholders after all obligations
  2. B. Senior debt holders before interest payments
  3. C. Governments through corporate tax collections
  4. D. Preferred stockholders ahead of all others
Answer: A. Common shareholders after all obligations
