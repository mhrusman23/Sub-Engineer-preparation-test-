Q1. The unit of pressure in SI is the:

  1. A. Newton
  2. B. Pascal
  3. C. Joule
  4. D. Watt
Answer: B. Pascal

Q2. The continuity equation for incompressible flow is:

  1. A. A1P1 = A2P2
  2. B. A1V1 = A2V2
  3. C. V1 = V2 only
  4. D. P1V1 = P2V2
Answer: B. A1V1 = A2V2

Q3. A weir is used to measure:

  1. A. Flow in open channels
  2. B. Viscosity
  3. C. Pressure in pipes
  4. D. Temperature
Answer: A. Flow in open channels

Q4. The Froude number is the ratio of inertial force to:

  1. A. Gravity force
  2. B. Viscous force
  3. C. Pressure force
  4. D. Surface tension
Answer: A. Gravity force

Q5. Head loss in pipes due to friction is calculated by the:

  1. A. Chezy only
  2. B. Darcy-Weisbach equation
  3. C. Manning only
  4. D. Bernoulli equation only
Answer: B. Darcy-Weisbach equation

Q6. Manning's equation is used for flow in:

  1. A. Turbines
  2. B. Pumps
  3. C. Closed pipes only
  4. D. Open channels
Answer: D. Open channels

Q7. The pressure at a point in a liquid acts equally in:

  1. A. Upward only
  2. B. One direction
  3. C. All directions
  4. D. Downward only
Answer: C. All directions

Q8. Hydraulic jump occurs when flow changes from:

  1. A. Laminar to turbulent
  2. B. Subcritical to supercritical
  3. C. Steady to unsteady
  4. D. Supercritical to subcritical
Answer: D. Supercritical to subcritical

Q9. The hydraulic radius is the ratio of area to:

  1. A. Slope
  2. B. Depth
  3. C. Wetted perimeter
  4. D. Width
Answer: C. Wetted perimeter

Q10. A pitot tube measures:

  1. A. Surface tension
  2. B. Temperature
  3. C. Velocity of flow
  4. D. Density
Answer: C. Velocity of flow

Q11. In a rectangular open channel, the flow transitions from supercritical to subcritical after a sudden contraction. This phenomenon is known as:

  1. A. Hydraulic jump
  2. B. Froude transition
  3. C. Critical point
  4. D. Flow separation
Answer: A. Hydraulic jump

Q12. The ratio of the channel cross‑sectional area to the wetted perimeter is called the:

  1. A. Hydraulic radius
  2. B. Specific energy
  3. C. Hydraulic head
  4. D. Flow coefficient
Answer: A. Hydraulic radius

Q13. For laminar flow in a circular pipe, the Darcy–Weisbach friction factor f is given by:

  1. A. 16 / Re
  2. B. 32 / Re
  3. C. 64 / Re
  4. D. 1 / Re
Answer: C. 64 / Re

Q14. The pressure exerted by a fluid at rest on a submerged surface depends on:

  1. A. Depth only
  2. B. Depth and fluid density
  3. C. Depth, density, and temperature
  4. D. Depth, density, and viscosity
Answer: B. Depth and fluid density

Q15. Which of the following best describes a flow where the velocity profile is uniform across the cross‑section?

  1. A. Uniform flow
  2. B. Steady flow
  3. C. Laminar flow
  4. D. Turbulent flow
Answer: A. Uniform flow

Q16. The specific speed of a hydraulic turbine is used to characterize its:

  1. A. Maximum physical size
  2. B. Type and performance
  3. C. Total frictional losses
  4. D. Maximum rotational friction
Answer: B. Type and performance

Q17. In a pipe network, the total head loss between two junctions depends primarily on:

  1. A. The square of the discharge
  2. B. The absolute pressure
  3. C. The velocity head only
  4. D. The temperature of the fluid
Answer: A. The square of the discharge

Q18. Chezy's formula for the velocity of flow in an open channel is given by:

  1. A. V = C * sqrt(R * S)
  2. B. V = (1/n) * R^(2/3) * S^(1/2)
  3. C. V = sqrt(2 * g * H)
  4. D. V = C * R * S
Answer: A. V = C * sqrt(R * S)

Q19. The Reynolds number is defined as the ratio of inertial forces to:

  1. A. Surface tension forces
  2. B. Viscous forces
  3. C. Gravity forces
  4. D. Elastic forces
Answer: B. Viscous forces

Q20. The point through which the resultant hydrostatic force acts on a submerged plane surface is known as the:

  1. A. Centroid
  2. B. Center of buoyancy
  3. C. Center of pressure
  4. D. Metacenter
Answer: C. Center of pressure

Q21. A fluid in which shear stress is directly proportional to the rate of shear strain is called a:

  1. A. Dilatant fluid
  2. B. Newtonian fluid
  3. C. Bingham plastic
  4. D. Pseudoplastic fluid
Answer: B. Newtonian fluid

Q22. Which dimensionless parameter determines whether open channel flow is subcritical, critical, or supercritical?

  1. A. Reynolds number
  2. B. Froude number
  3. C. Mach number
  4. D. Weber number
Answer: B. Froude number

Q23. What is the primary function of a surge tank in a penstock system of a hydroelectric power plant?

  1. A. To increase the turbine speed
  2. B. To filter solid debris from water
  3. C. To relieve water hammer pressure
  4. D. To measure total discharge rate
Answer: C. To relieve water hammer pressure

Q24. In a centrifugal pump, cavitation typically occurs at which location?

  1. A. At the discharge tip of the impeller
  2. B. Inside the volute casing outlet
  3. C. At the eye of the pump impeller
  4. D. Along the outer pipe wall
Answer: C. At the eye of the pump impeller

Q25. What does the hydraulic grade line (HGL) represent graphically along a pipeline?

  1. A. Velocity head plus elevation head
  2. B. Sum of pressure head and elevation head
  3. C. Total energy head above a datum
  4. D. Friction head loss per unit length
Answer: B. Sum of pressure head and elevation head
