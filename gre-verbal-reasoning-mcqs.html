Q1. Choose the word closest in meaning to 'CANDID':

  1. A. Dishonest
  2. B. Frank and honest
  3. C. Shy
  4. D. Angry
Answer: B. Frank and honest

Q2. 'Ephemeral' means:

  1. A. Lasting forever
  2. B. Lasting a very short time
  3. C. Very large
  4. D. Very old
Answer: B. Lasting a very short time

Q3. Choose the word closest in meaning to 'PRAGMATIC':

  1. A. Idealistic
  2. B. Practical
  3. C. Emotional
  4. D. Careless
Answer: B. Practical

Q4. The author's main purpose in a passage is found by asking:

  1. A. How long is it?
  2. B. Why did the author write it?
  3. C. How many paragraphs?
  4. D. Who published it?
Answer: B. Why did the author write it?

Q5. 'Ambiguous' means:

  1. A. Clear
  2. B. Open to more than one interpretation
  3. C. False
  4. D. Simple
Answer: B. Open to more than one interpretation

Q6. Choose the pair that completes the sentence: 'The scientist's theory was ___ by later experiments, which proved it correct.'

  1. A. refuted
  2. B. confirmed
  3. C. ignored
  4. D. forgotten
Answer: B. confirmed

Q7. 'Verbose' describes someone who:

  1. A. Speaks very little
  2. B. Uses more words than necessary
  3. C. Writes poetry
  4. D. Speaks loudly
Answer: B. Uses more words than necessary

Q8. In a reading passage, an 'assumption' is:

  1. A. A proven fact
  2. B. Something taken for granted without proof
  3. C. The conclusion
  4. D. An example
Answer: B. Something taken for granted without proof

Q9. Choose the word closest in meaning to 'MITIGATE':

  1. A. Make worse
  2. B. Make less severe
  3. C. Hide
  4. D. Measure
Answer: B. Make less severe

Q10. Text completion questions are best solved by:

  1. A. Choosing the longest word
  2. B. Predicting your own word for the blank before reading options
  3. C. Guessing randomly
  4. D. Reading the last line only
Answer: B. Predicting your own word for the blank before reading options

Q11. 'Mollify' means to:

  1. A. Make something worse
  2. B. Increase in volume
  3. C. Soften the anger or anxiety
  4. D. Complicate a matter
Answer: C. Soften the anger or anxiety

Q12. 'Gregarious' means:

  1. A. Fond of company
  2. B. Easily frightened
  3. C. Very strict
  4. D. Prone to anger
Answer: A. Fond of company

Q13. Choose the word closest in meaning to 'GULLIBLE':

  1. A. Easily persuaded to believe something
  2. B. Extremely wise and cautious
  3. C. Suspicious of motives
  4. D. Aggressive in argument
Answer: A. Easily persuaded to believe something

Q14. 'Laconic' describes a style of speech that is:

  1. A. Very emotional and loud
  2. B. Using very few words
  3. C. Extremely detailed and long
  4. D. Unclear and confusing
Answer: B. Using very few words

Q15. 'Alacrity' means:

  1. A. Slow reluctance
  2. B. Brisk and cheerful readiness
  3. C. Deep sadness
  4. D. Extreme confusion
Answer: B. Brisk and cheerful readiness

Q16. Choose the word closest in meaning to 'OBDURATE':

  1. A. Flexible
  2. B. Stubborn
  3. C. Transparent
  4. D. Obedient
Answer: B. Stubborn

Q17. Choose the word closest in meaning to 'ENIGMA':

  1. A. Mystery
  2. B. Clarity
  3. C. Certainty
  4. D. Harmony
Answer: A. Mystery

Q18. What is the primary antonym of 'COPIOUS'?

  1. A. Abundant
  2. B. Plentiful
  3. C. Scanty
  4. D. Generous
Answer: C. Scanty

Q19. Choose the word closest in meaning to 'LUCID':

  1. A. Obscure
  2. B. Clear
  3. C. Gloomy
  4. D. Complex
Answer: B. Clear

Q20. The term 'MITIGATE' is best defined as:

  1. A. To intensify
  2. B. To lessen the severity
  3. C. To prolong
  4. D. To ignore completely
Answer: B. To lessen the severity

Q21. Choose the word closest in meaning to 'AUSPICIOUS':

  1. A. Unfavorable
  2. B. Promising
  3. C. Uncertain
  4. D. Hostile
Answer: B. Promising

Q22. What does the word 'SPORADIC' mean?

  1. A. Constant
  2. B. Regular
  3. C. Occasional
  4. D. Continuous
Answer: C. Occasional

Q23. Which strategy is most effective for solving a Text Completion question with a single blank?

  1. A. A) Select the longest word among the choices
  2. B. B) Predict the missing word before reviewing the options
  3. C. C) Guess based on the word’s length
  4. D. D) Read only the last sentence of the passage
Answer: B. B) Predict the missing word before reviewing the options

Q24. The scientist’s hypothesis was ___ by subsequent experiments that proved it accurate.

  1. A. A) refuted
  2. B. B) confirmed
  3. C. C) ignored
  4. D. D) forgotten
Answer: B. B) confirmed

Q25. Which of the following is a common technique for answering Sentence Equivalence questions?

  1. A. A) Choose the option that appears first alphabetically
  2. B. B) Find two words that produce the same meaning in the context
  3. C. C) Pick the longest word in the list
  4. D. D) Ignore the surrounding sentences
Answer: B. B) Find two words that produce the same meaning in the context
