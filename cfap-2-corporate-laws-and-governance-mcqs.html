Q1. The principal company law in Pakistan is the:

  1. A. Contract Act, 1872
  2. B. Partnership Act, 1932
  3. C. Companies Act, 2017
  4. D. Companies Ordinance, 1984 only
Answer: C. Companies Act, 2017

Q2. The securities and corporate regulator in Pakistan is the:

  1. A. SBP
  2. B. FBR
  3. C. NAB
  4. D. SECP
Answer: D. SECP

Q3. The board of directors is responsible to the company's:

  1. A. Employees only
  2. B. Members (shareholders)
  3. C. Auditors only
  4. D. Customers only
Answer: B. Members (shareholders)

Q4. A company's constitutional documents are the memorandum and:

  1. A. Annual return
  2. B. Articles of association
  3. C. Prospectus
  4. D. Audit report
Answer: B. Articles of association

Q5. An independent director is one who:

  1. A. Has no material relationship with the company
  2. B. Owns the majority of shares
  3. C. Is a full-time executive
  4. D. Is the chief executive's relative
Answer: A. Has no material relationship with the company

Q6. The audit committee mainly oversees:

  1. A. Marketing campaigns
  2. B. Plant operations
  3. C. Staff recruitment only
  4. D. Financial reporting and audit
Answer: D. Financial reporting and audit

Q7. An annual general meeting (AGM) is a meeting of:

  1. A. Creditors only
  2. B. Auditors only
  3. C. Members
  4. D. Employees only
Answer: C. Members

Q8. Winding up of a company means:

  1. A. Issuing new shares
  2. B. Starting a new branch
  3. C. Ending its legal existence in an orderly process
  4. D. Changing its auditors
Answer: C. Ending its legal existence in an orderly process

Q9. Corporate governance aims to balance the interests of:

  1. A. Directors only
  2. B. Regulators only
  3. C. Stakeholders
  4. D. Auditors only
Answer: C. Stakeholders

Q10. A director's duty of care means acting with:

  1. A. Maximum speed
  2. B. Secrecy only
  3. C. Reasonable skill and diligence
  4. D. Personal profit
Answer: C. Reasonable skill and diligence

Q11. Which specific corporate officer is legally responsible for authenticating the annual financial statements alongside the chief executive officer in Pakistan?

  1. A. Chief Internal Auditor
  2. B. Company Secretary
  3. C. Chief Financial Officer
  4. D. Head of Legal Affairs
Answer: C. Chief Financial Officer

Q12. What is the minimum number of directors required for a single member private limited company in Pakistan?

  1. A. One director
  2. B. Two directors
  3. C. Three directors
  4. D. Five directors
Answer: A. One director

Q13. Which corporate body in Pakistan is legally mandated to recommend the appointment and remuneration of external auditors to the general meeting?

  1. A. Board of Directors
  2. B. Audit Committee
  3. C. Chief Executive Officer
  4. D. Company Secretary
Answer: B. Audit Committee

Q14. What is the statutory timeframe within which a public unlisted company must hold its first annual general meeting from the date of its incorporation?

  1. A. Within 12 months
  2. B. Within 16 months
  3. C. Within 18 months
  4. D. Within 24 months
Answer: B. Within 16 months

Q15. Which resolution is legally required to alter the authorized share capital clause of a company's memorandum of association?

  1. A. Ordinary resolution
  2. B. Special resolution
  3. C. Unanimous resolution
  4. D. Board resolution
Answer: A. Ordinary resolution

Q16. What is the minimum percentage of voting shares required by members to requisition an extraordinary general meeting of a company?

  1. A. Five percent
  2. B. Ten percent
  3. C. Fifteen percent
  4. D. Twenty percent
Answer: B. Ten percent

Q17. In the event of a voluntary winding up, who possesses the primary authority to appoint the liquidator and determine their remuneration?

  1. A. The Creditors
  2. B. The Court
  3. C. The Company in General Meeting
  4. D. The Securities and Exchange Commission
Answer: C. The Company in General Meeting

Q18. What specific duration must a listed company maintain for the retention of its books of account and financial records?

  1. A. Five financial years
  2. B. Seven financial years
  3. C. Ten financial years
  4. D. Twelve financial years
Answer: B. Seven financial years

Q19. Under the provisions of the Companies Act 2017, what is the maximum time limit within which a company is required to issue and dispatch certificates of shares after the allotment of any shares?

  1. A. Within 30 days of allotment
  2. B. Within 45 days of allotment
  3. C. Within 60 days of allotment
  4. D. Within 90 days of allotment
Answer: B. Within 45 days of allotment

Q20. When a company decides to declare a final cash dividend, the statutory closure of share transfer books must be notified to which regulatory entity before the commencement of book closure?

  1. A. Federal Board of Revenue (FBR)
  2. B. State Bank of Pakistan (SBP)
  3. C. Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan (SECP) and the Stock Exchange
  4. D. Competition Commission of Pakistan (CCP)
Answer: C. Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan (SECP) and the Stock Exchange

Q21. Under the Companies Act 2017, what specific type of resolution is required by members in a general meeting to empower the chief executive officer to approve arm's length related party transactions on their behalf for the upcoming year?

  1. A. Ordinary resolution
  2. B. Special resolution
  3. C. Unanimous resolution
  4. D. Board resolution
Answer: A. Ordinary resolution

Q22. Under the regulatory framework governing public sector companies in Pakistan, what mandatory corporate body is primarily tasked with reviewing financial statements and internal controls before they are approved by the board?

  1. A. Human Resource and Remuneration Committee
  2. B. Procurement Oversight Committee
  3. C. Audit Committee
  4. D. Nomination Committee
Answer: C. Audit Committee

Q23. In the context of share capital management under the Companies Act 2017, when a listed company issues further shares other than by way of rights, what type of shareholder approval mechanism must be followed?

  1. A. Approval through a special resolution passed by members
  2. B. Written consent from the registrar of companies only
  3. C. Direct authorization via the corporate audit committee
  4. D. Sole discretion of the chief financial officer
Answer: A. Approval through a special resolution passed by members

Q24. According to the corporate governance framework for listed companies, what is the primary regulatory objective concerning the separation of roles between the chairman of the board and the chief executive officer?

  1. A. To ensure operational efficiency and maximize daily output
  2. B. To prevent concentration of power and maintain appropriate checks and balances
  3. C. To reduce the remuneration expenses of top-tier executives
  4. D. To simplify the statutory external audit procedure
Answer: B. To prevent concentration of power and maintain appropriate checks and balances

Q25. Under the Listed Companies (Code of Corporate Governance) Regulations, who is eligible to be appointed as the chairman of the audit committee?

  1. A. The Chief Executive Officer
  2. B. An independent director
  3. C. The Chief Financial Officer
  4. D. An executive director
Answer: B. An independent director
