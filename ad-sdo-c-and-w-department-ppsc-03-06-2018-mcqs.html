AD SDO C&W Department PPSC (03.06.2018) – Past Paper Solved MCQs

Solved MCQs from the PPSC paper “AD SDO C&W Department PPSC (03.06.2018)”. Full paper has 73 questions; the first 25 are shown here with correct answers. Open the app to take the complete timed test.

Q1. When cracks appear in tension in concrete, concrete stress in tension is considered as:

  1. A. Zero
  2. B. Half
  3. C. Maximum
  4. D. Full tensile strength
Answer: A. Zero

Q2. Terzaghi's equation of bearing capacity assumes:

  1. A. General shear failure
  2. B. Local shear failure only
  3. C. Punching shear failure only
  4. D. Bearing failure
Answer: A. General shear failure

Q3. Pile depth in sandy soil: diameter of pile is ______ pile depth.

  1. A. Inversely proportional to
  2. B. Directly proportional to
  3. C. Equal to
  4. D. Double of
Answer: A. Inversely proportional to

Q4. Negative skin friction:

  1. A. Reduces bearing capacity
  2. B. Increases bearing capacity
  3. C. Has no effect
  4. D. Doubles capacity
Answer: A. Reduces bearing capacity

Q5. Location of plane table is done by:

  1. A. Resection / intersection method
  2. B. Radiation method only
  3. C. Traversing
  4. D. Levelling
Answer: A. Resection / intersection method

Q6. First reading at a point is:

  1. A. Back sight
  2. B. Fore sight
  3. C. Intermediate sight
  4. D. Change point
Answer: A. Back sight

Q7. Mohr's circle has minimum size at which state?

  1. A. Active earth pressure
  2. B. Passive earth pressure
  3. C. At rest condition
  4. D. Wet condition
Answer: A. Active earth pressure

Q8. Top of the weir is called:

  1. A. Crest
  2. B. Apron
  3. C. Toe
  4. D. Heel
Answer: A. Crest

Q9. Sluice is:

  1. A. Outlet for water
  2. B. Inlet gate only
  3. C. Dam type
  4. D. Canal lining
Answer: A. Outlet for water

Q10. Chute is:

  1. A. An inclined fall
  2. B. A vertical fall
  3. C. A horizontal fall
  4. D. A weir
Answer: A. An inclined fall

Q11. Modified Proctor test gives ______ value of OMC and ______ value of dry density as compared to standard Proctor.

  1. A. Less, greater
  2. B. Greater, less
  3. C. Same, same
  4. D. Less, less
Answer: A. Less, greater

Q12. In split cylinder test, material fails in:

  1. A. Tension
  2. B. Compression
  3. C. Shear
  4. D. Flexure
Answer: A. Tension

Q13. Co-efficient of linear expansion of concrete and steel is:

  1. A. Same
  2. B. Different
  3. C. Double
  4. D. Half
Answer: A. Same

Q14. Slip circle has ______ factor of safety (Swedish circle method for determination of critical plane).

  1. A. Minimum
  2. B. Maximum
  3. C. Zero
  4. D. Infinite
Answer: A. Minimum

Q15. Cantilever is a:

  1. A. One way slab
  2. B. Two way slab
  3. C. Flat slab
  4. D. Waffle slab
Answer: A. One way slab

Q16. Shorter to longer span ratio greater than 0.5 is:

  1. A. Two way slab
  2. B. One way slab
  3. C. Flat slab
  4. D. Ribbed slab
Answer: A. Two way slab

Q17. In PERT, the performance of the specific task is called:

  1. A. Activity
  2. B. Event
  3. C. Duration
  4. D. Float
Answer: A. Activity

Q18. In PERT, time to do a job is called:

  1. A. Duration
  2. B. Event
  3. C. Activity
  4. D. Slack
Answer: A. Duration

Q19. In PERT, start and finish of an activity is represented by:

  1. A. Event
  2. B. Arrow
  3. C. Node only
  4. D. Duration
Answer: A. Event

Q20. Bar charts are:

  1. A. Horizontal lines
  2. B. Vertical lines
  3. C. Curves
  4. D. Diagonal lines
Answer: A. Horizontal lines

Q21. Bar chart is used for:

  1. A. Minor works
  2. B. Major works
  3. C. Dam works
  4. D. All projects
Answer: A. Minor works

Q22. Difference between canal FSL and embankment is called:

  1. A. Freeboard
  2. B. Duty
  3. C. Delta
  4. D. Draft
Answer: A. Freeboard

Q23. For full daylight, windows should be on ______ side:

  1. A. Southern
  2. B. Northern
  3. C. Eastern
  4. D. Western
Answer: A. Southern

Q24. Pre-stressing in which wires are stressed before pouring is called:

  1. A. Pre-tensioning
  2. B. Post-tensioning
  3. C. Bond stressing
  4. D. External stressing
Answer: A. Pre-tensioning

Q25. Horizontal cross wires of stadia diaphragm are:

  1. A. 3
  2. B. 2
  3. C. 4
  4. D. 5
Answer: D. 5
