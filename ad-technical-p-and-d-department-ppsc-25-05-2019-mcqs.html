Q1. CBR can be used as a ______ test:

  1. A. Both (field and lab)
  2. B. Field only
  3. C. Lab only
  4. D. None
Answer: A. Both (field and lab)

Q2. A granular soil is lying under water table; permeability of it can be checked using:

  1. A. Both (constant head permeability and pumping water from it)
  2. B. Constant head permeability only
  3. C. Pumping water from it only
  4. D. None
Answer: A. Both (constant head permeability and pumping water from it)

Q3. In statistics, the difference between maximum and minimum value is known as:

  1. A. Range
  2. B. Variant
  3. C. Standard deviation
  4. D. Mean
Answer: A. Range

Q4. Tarbela dam is a biggest:

  1. A. Rock and earth fill dam
  2. B. Earth fill dam
  3. C. Rockfill dam
  4. D. Gravity dam
Answer: A. Rock and earth fill dam

Q5. Main issue in operation and maintenance at upstream of Mangla dam is:

  1. A. Sediments
  2. B. Abutment
  3. C. Upstream floor
  4. D. None
Answer: A. Sediments

Q6. Marshall test for subbase material is used for:

  1. A. Stability
  2. B. Flow
  3. C. Void ratio
  4. D. None
Answer: A. Stability

Q7. Cross slope of highways in plain area is:

  1. A. 0-5%
  2. B. 5-10%
  3. C. 11-15%
  4. D. 15-20%
Answer: A. 0-5%

Q8. After brakes are applied, the total distance to stop the vehicle is called:

  1. A. Stopping sight distance
  2. B. Breaking distance
  3. C. Brake reaction distance
  4. D. None
Answer: A. Stopping sight distance

Q9. For bearing capacity of soils, which test is used?

  1. A. Both (SPT and CPT)
  2. B. SPT only
  3. C. CPT only
  4. D. None
Answer: A. Both (SPT and CPT)

Q10. CPT measures:

  1. A. All (pore pressure, sleeve friction, tip resistance)
  2. B. Pore pressure only
  3. C. Sleeve friction only
  4. D. Tip resistance only
Answer: A. All (pore pressure, sleeve friction, tip resistance)

Q11. Deformation under constant load is known as:

  1. A. Creep
  2. B. Shrinkage
  3. C. Strain
  4. D. Elongation
Answer: A. Creep

Q12. The concrete C25/30 means it has:

  1. A. Both (25 MPa cylinder strength and 30 MPa cube strength)
  2. B. 25 MPa cube strength only
  3. C. 30 MPa cylinder strength only
  4. D. None
Answer: A. Both (25 MPa cylinder strength and 30 MPa cube strength)

Q13. Which of the following concrete can be best suited for pre-stressing?

  1. A. C25/35
  2. B. C10/12
  3. C. C18/22
  4. D. C25/30
Answer: A. C25/35

Q14. Truss members cannot be made by:

  1. A. Concrete
  2. B. Wood
  3. C. Metal
  4. D. Steel
Answer: A. Concrete

Q15. Flexibility coefficients are used in:

  1. A. Force method
  2. B. Displacement method
  3. C. Both
  4. D. Virtual work method only
Answer: A. Force method

Q16. Stiffness coefficients are used for:

  1. A. Displacement method
  2. B. Force method
  3. C. Both
  4. D. Virtual work method only
Answer: A. Displacement method

Q17. Castigliano's theorem is used for:

  1. A. Yielding of support and elastic
  2. B. Non-yielding of support and elastic
  3. C. Yielding of support and non-elastic
  4. D. Non-yielding of support and non-elastic
Answer: A. Yielding of support and elastic

Q18. Most important resource is:

  1. A. Manpower
  2. B. Money
  3. C. Machinery
  4. D. Material
Answer: A. Manpower

Q19. Escalation in engineering economy can be regarded as:

  1. A. Inflation
  2. B. Deflation
  3. C. Both
  4. D. None
Answer: A. Inflation

Q20. If a building is resting on clayey soil then most important settlement is:

  1. A. Consolidation settlement
  2. B. Immediate settlement
  3. C. Secondary settlement
  4. D. Elastic settlement
Answer: A. Consolidation settlement

Q21. Retarders used in concrete:

  1. A. Sucrose
  2. B. Bagasse
  3. C. Fly ash
  4. D. Lime
Answer: A. Sucrose

Q22. Supplementary cementitious material is:

  1. A. All (fly ash, silica fume, calcined clay)
  2. B. Fly ash only
  3. C. Silica fume only
  4. D. Calcined clay only
Answer: A. All (fly ash, silica fume, calcined clay)

Q23. Organic soils can be stabilized for making embankments using:

  1. A. Lime
  2. B. Fly ash
  3. C. Cement
  4. D. Bagasse
Answer: A. Lime

Q24. Corrosion inhibition can be done by:

  1. A. Calcium nitrate
  2. B. Sodium nitrate
  3. C. Both
  4. D. None
Answer: A. Calcium nitrate

Q25. Man made material is:

  1. A. Plastic
  2. B. Copper
  3. C. Gold
  4. D. Metal
Answer: A. Plastic
