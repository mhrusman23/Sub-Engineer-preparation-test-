Q1. She ___ to school every day.

  1. A. go
  2. B. goes
  3. C. going
  4. D. gone
Answer: B. goes

Q2. They ___ football yesterday.

  1. A. play
  2. B. plays
  3. C. played
  4. D. playing
Answer: C. played

Q3. I ___ my homework already.

  1. A. finish
  2. B. have finished
  3. C. finishes
  4. D. finishing
Answer: B. have finished

Q4. He ___ TV when I called him.

  1. A. watches
  2. B. is watching
  3. C. was watching
  4. D. watch
Answer: C. was watching

Q5. We ___ in this city since 2010.

  1. A. live
  2. B. have been living
  3. C. lives
  4. D. lived yesterday
Answer: B. have been living

Q6. By next year, she ___ her degree.

  1. A. completes
  2. B. will have completed
  3. C. complete
  4. D. completing
Answer: B. will have completed

Q7. The train ___ at 6 pm every evening.

  1. A. leave
  2. B. leaves
  3. C. leaving
  4. D. left tomorrow
Answer: B. leaves

Q8. I ___ him for five years.

  1. A. know
  2. B. have known
  3. C. knows
  4. D. knowing
Answer: B. have known

Q9. While I was cooking, he ___ the table.

  1. A. sets
  2. B. set
  3. C. setting
  4. D. is set
Answer: B. set

Q10. She ___ her keys two days ago.

  1. A. loses
  2. B. lost
  3. C. has lose
  4. D. losing
Answer: B. lost

Q11. They ___ to London next month. (planned)

  1. A. are travelling
  2. B. travels
  3. C. travel yesterday
  4. D. travelling last week
Answer: A. are travelling

Q12. He usually ___ tea, but today he is drinking coffee.

  1. A. drinks
  2. B. drink
  3. C. drank
  4. D. drinking yesterday
Answer: A. drinks

Q13. The sun ___ in the east.

  1. A. rise
  2. B. rises
  3. C. rose today
  4. D. rising
Answer: B. rises

Q14. I ___ a letter when the light went out.

  1. A. write
  2. B. was writing
  3. C. writes
  4. D. am write
Answer: B. was writing

Q15. She ___ three books so far.

  1. A. writes
  2. B. has written
  3. C. write
  4. D. writing
Answer: B. has written

Q16. If it ___ tomorrow, we will stay home.

  1. A. rains
  2. B. rain
  3. C. rained yesterday
  4. D. raining
Answer: A. rains

Q17. They ___ the bridge by 2025. (future perfect)

  1. A. will have built
  2. B. builds
  3. C. built now
  4. D. building
Answer: A. will have built

Q18. He ___ his car last week.

  1. A. sells
  2. B. sold
  3. C. has sell
  4. D. selling
Answer: B. sold

Q19. Look! The baby ___.

  1. A. cries
  2. B. is crying
  3. C. cry
  4. D. cried yesterday
Answer: B. is crying

Q20. We ___ dinner at 8 pm tonight. (arrangement)

  1. A. are having
  2. B. has
  3. C. have yesterday
  4. D. having last night
Answer: A. are having
