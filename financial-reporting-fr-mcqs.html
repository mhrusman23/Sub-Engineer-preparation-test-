Q1. Which IAS deals with the presentation of financial statements?

  1. A. IAS 37
  2. B. IAS 2
  3. C. IAS 1
  4. D. IAS 16
Answer: C. IAS 1

Q2. Under IAS 16, after initial recognition property, plant and equipment may use the cost model or the:

  1. A. Amortised model
  2. B. Fair value through profit model
  3. C. Equity model
  4. D. Revaluation model
Answer: D. Revaluation model

Q3. IAS 2 requires inventory to be measured at the lower of cost and:

  1. A. Replacement cost
  2. B. Market price
  3. C. Selling price
  4. D. Net realisable value
Answer: D. Net realisable value

Q4. Under IFRS 15, revenue is recognised when:

  1. A. Cash is received
  2. B. A performance obligation is satisfied
  3. C. The contract is signed
  4. D. The invoice is printed
Answer: B. A performance obligation is satisfied

Q5. A provision is recognised under IAS 37 when there is a present obligation, a probable outflow and:

  1. A. A bank guarantee
  2. B. A shareholder vote
  3. C. A reliable estimate
  4. D. An auditor's note
Answer: C. A reliable estimate

Q6. An associate is an entity over which the investor has:

  1. A. Joint control only
  2. B. Significant influence
  3. C. Full control
  4. D. No influence
Answer: B. Significant influence

Q7. Investments in associates are accounted for using the:

  1. A. Equity method
  2. B. Fair value method only
  3. C. Cost method only
  4. D. Proportionate method
Answer: A. Equity method

Q8. IAS 7 classifies cash flows into operating, investing and:

  1. A. Reserving
  2. B. Taxation
  3. C. Financing
  4. D. Trading
Answer: C. Financing

Q9. An adjusting event after the reporting period provides evidence of conditions that existed at:

  1. A. The reporting date
  2. B. Next year end
  3. C. The date of the AGM
  4. D. The date of incorporation
Answer: A. The reporting date

Q10. An asset is impaired when its carrying amount exceeds its:

  1. A. Residual value
  2. B. Original cost
  3. C. Insured value
  4. D. Recoverable amount
Answer: D. Recoverable amount

Q11. Under IFRS 15 Revenue from Contracts with Customers, when should a performance obligation satisfied over time be recognized?

  1. A. Only upon full completion of the project
  2. B. As the entity transfers control of the goods or services over time
  3. C. When the cash is received from the customer
  4. D. At the signing date of the contract
Answer: B. As the entity transfers control of the goods or services over time

Q12. Under IFRS 15, when is revenue recognized?

  1. A. When cash is received
  2. B. When control of the goods or services is transferred to the customer
  3. C. When the contract is signed
  4. D. When the production process is complete
Answer: B. When control of the goods or services is transferred to the customer

Q13. Which of the following is a key requirement for recognizing revenue under IFRS 15 Revenue from Contracts with Customers?

  1. A. Cash has been received in full
  2. B. The contract has been approved by management only
  3. C. It is probable that the entity will collect the consideration to which it is entitled
  4. D. All performance obligations must be performed over time
Answer: C. It is probable that the entity will collect the consideration to which it is entitled

Q14. In financial accounting under IFRS 15, when should revenue from a contract with a customer be recognized?

  1. A. When cash is received
  2. B. When the purchase order is signed
  3. C. When control of the goods or services is transferred
  4. D. At the end of the financial year
Answer: C. When control of the goods or services is transferred

Q15. Which of the following is treated as an adjusting event after the reporting period under IAS 10?

  1. A. Announcing a plan to discontinue an operation
  2. B. Decline in market value of investments between the end of the reporting period and date of authorization
  3. C. Resolution of a court case that confirms the entity had a present obligation at the reporting date
  4. D. Major business combination after the reporting period
Answer: C. Resolution of a court case that confirms the entity had a present obligation at the reporting date

Q16. What is the primary objective of financial reporting according to the Conceptual Framework for Financial Reporting?

  1. A. To provide information useful to existing and potential investors, lenders, and other creditors
  2. B. To calculate the exact market value of the entity
  3. C. To determine the amount of tax payable to the government
  4. D. To assist management in daily operational decision making
Answer: A. To provide information useful to existing and potential investors, lenders, and other creditors

Q17. How should a contingent liability be treated in the financial statements according to IAS 37?

  1. A. Recognized as a liability and disclosed in the notes
  2. B. Recognized as an asset if recovery is virtually certain
  3. C. Disclosed in the notes only, unless the possibility of outflow is remote
  4. D. Ignored completely until cash actually changes hands
Answer: C. Disclosed in the notes only, unless the possibility of outflow is remote

Q18. Under IAS 37 Provisions, Contingent Liabilities and Contingent Assets, when should a provision be recognized?

  1. A. When there is a possible obligation whose existence will be confirmed by future events
  2. B. When an entity has a present obligation from a past event and a reliable estimate can be made
  3. C. Whenever management anticipates a future loss
  4. D. When a contingent asset is virtually certain to be realized
Answer: B. When an entity has a present obligation from a past event and a reliable estimate can be made

Q19. What is the primary objective of IAS 37 (Provisions, Contingent Liabilities and Contingent Assets)?

  1. A. To ensure appropriate recognition criteria and measurement bases are applied to provisions and contingent items
  2. B. To account for financial instruments
  3. C. To determine the depreciation of property, plant and equipment
  4. D. To recognize revenue from contracts with customers
Answer: A. To ensure appropriate recognition criteria and measurement bases are applied to provisions and contingent items

Q20. How should development costs that meet the capitalization criteria under IAS 38 be initially recognized?

  1. A. Expensed immediately in profit or loss
  2. B. Recognized as an intangible asset at cost
  3. C. Recognized as part of retained earnings
  4. D. Deferred and amortized over revenue
Answer: B. Recognized as an intangible asset at cost

Q21. When calculating earnings per share (EPS) under IAS 33, how should a bonus issue during the year be treated?

  1. A. Weighted from the exact date of issue
  2. B. Ignored for current year EPS calculations
  3. C. Treated as if it occurred at the beginning of the earliest period presented
  4. D. Included only for cash dividend purposes
Answer: C. Treated as if it occurred at the beginning of the earliest period presented

Q22. Under IAS 16 Property, Plant and Equipment, how is the depreciation method reviewed?

  1. A. Only when the asset is sold
  2. B. At least at each financial year-end
  3. C. Every five years
  4. D. Only when requested by auditors
Answer: B. At least at each financial year-end

Q23. Under IAS 16 Property, Plant and Equipment, how should subsequent expenditure on an asset be treated?

  1. A. Always expensed immediately in profit or loss
  2. B. Capitalized only if it enhances the future economic benefits of the asset beyond its originally assessed standard of performance
  3. C. Added to the statement of cash flows as an operating expense
  4. D. Ignored unless approved by external auditors
Answer: B. Capitalized only if it enhances the future economic benefits of the asset beyond its originally assessed standard of performance

Q24. Which of the following costs should be included in the initial carrying amount of an item of property, plant and equipment according to IAS 16?

  1. A. Initial operating losses incurred before the asset achieves planned performance
  2. B. Costs of conducting business in a new location
  3. C. Costs of site preparation directly attributable to bringing the asset to location
  4. D. Administration and other general overhead costs
Answer: C. Costs of site preparation directly attributable to bringing the asset to location

Q25. What does the current ratio measure in financial statement analysis?

  1. A. Long-term solvency
  2. B. Short-term liquidity
  3. C. Asset turnover efficiency
  4. D. Profitability margins
Answer: B. Short-term liquidity
