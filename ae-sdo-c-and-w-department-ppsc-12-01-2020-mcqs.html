Q1. Which shape of aggregate gives maximum strength?

  1. A. Cubical
  2. B. Flaky
  3. C. Rounded
  4. D. Elongated
Answer: A. Cubical

Q2. Coming out of cement slurry from concrete is called:

  1. A. Bleeding
  2. B. Creep
  3. C. Segregation
  4. D. Shrinkage
Answer: A. Bleeding

Q3. Cement will be sound if it does not contain:

  1. A. Iron oxide
  2. B. Free lime
  3. C. Silica
  4. D. Alumina
Answer: B. Free lime

Q4. Suitable stone for construction of pier is:

  1. A. Chalk
  2. B. Laterite
  3. C. Limestone
  4. D. Granite
Answer: D. Granite

Q5. Stones are placed in their natural plane so that load acts:

  1. A. Tangential
  2. B. Oblique
  3. C. Parallel
  4. D. Normal
Answer: D. Normal

Q6. Which component of pozzolana acts with free lime?

  1. A. Silica
  2. B. Iron oxide
  3. C. Magnesia
  4. D. Alumina
Answer: A. Silica

Q7. Vicat apparatus is used for:

  1. A. Abrasion
  2. B. Soundness
  3. C. Consistency
  4. D. Tensile strength
Answer: C. Consistency

Q8. Fire proof paints have:

  1. A. Glass fibers
  2. B. Asbestos fibers
  3. C. PVC fibers
  4. D. Cotton fibers
Answer: B. Asbestos fibers

Q9. Seasoning is not helpful in:

  1. A. Increasing durability
  2. B. Removing natural defects
  3. C. Reducing weight
  4. D. Increasing strength
Answer: B. Removing natural defects

Q10. Weir is narrow crested when width of crest is:

  1. A. Twice the depth of water
  2. B. Half of the depth of water
  3. C. More than twice the depth
  4. D. Equal to depth of water
Answer: B. Half of the depth of water

Q11. In applying trapezoidal rule, prismoidal correction is:

  1. A. Subtracted
  2. B. Multiplied
  3. C. Added
  4. D. Ignored
Answer: A. Subtracted

Q12. In plane table survey, method used to locate point from one station is:

  1. A. Resection
  2. B. Intersection
  3. C. Traversing
  4. D. Radiation
Answer: D. Radiation

Q13. Earth is taken flat for area up to:

  1. A. 1000 km2
  2. B. 100 km2
  3. C. 250 km2
  4. D. 500 km2
Answer: C. 250 km2

Q14. Vertical distance between contour lines is called:

  1. A. Horizontal equivalent
  2. B. Contour interval
  3. C. Gradient
  4. D. Equivalent
Answer: B. Contour interval

Q15. Sum of latitudes and departures for a closed traverse is:

  1. A. 1
  2. B. Depends on area
  3. C. Depends on scale
  4. D. 0
Answer: D. 0

Q16. When d is the distance between staff and instrument, the error due to earth's curvature is proportional to:

  1. A. d2
  2. B. d
  3. C. sqrt(d)
  4. D. d3
Answer: A. d2

Q17. The line on which the setup of survey is built is called:

  1. A. Contour line
  2. B. Tie line
  3. C. Base line
  4. D. Check line
Answer: C. Base line

Q18. In chain surveying, area is divided into:

  1. A. Circles
  2. B. Squares
  3. C. Trapeziums
  4. D. Triangles
Answer: D. Triangles

Q19. Shift of curve is given by:

  1. A. L/24R
  2. B. L2/12R
  3. C. L2/24R
  4. D. L2/6R
Answer: C. L2/24R

Q20. Lines joining points of same declination are called:

  1. A. Isobars
  2. B. Isogonic lines
  3. C. Agonic lines
  4. D. Contour lines
Answer: B. Isogonic lines

Q21. Closely spaced contour lines represent:

  1. A. Steep slope
  2. B. Flat slope
  3. C. Depression only
  4. D. Gentle slope
Answer: A. Steep slope

Q22. Design of vertical curve is based on:

  1. A. Speed only
  2. B. Gradient
  3. C. Radius only
  4. D. Superelevation
Answer: B. Gradient

Q23. Nozzles are provided for:

  1. A. Increasing pressure
  2. B. Decreasing discharge
  3. C. Increasing velocity
  4. D. Decreasing velocity
Answer: C. Increasing velocity

Q24. Fluid having zero viscosity is called:

  1. A. Plastic fluid
  2. B. Real fluid
  3. C. Newtonian fluid
  4. D. Ideal fluid
Answer: D. Ideal fluid

Q25. When a floating body is given small displacement, it starts oscillating about a point called:

  1. A. Center of buoyancy
  2. B. Metacenter
  3. C. Centroid
  4. D. Center of pressure
Answer: B. Metacenter
