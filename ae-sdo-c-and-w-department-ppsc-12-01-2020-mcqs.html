Q1. Which shape of aggregate gives maximum strength?

  1. A. Cubical
  2. B. Elongated
  3. C. Flaky
  4. D. Rounded
Answer: A. Cubical

Q2. Coming out of cement slurry from concrete is called:

  1. A. Bleeding
  2. B. Segregation
  3. C. Creep
  4. D. Shrinkage
Answer: A. Bleeding

Q3. Cement will be sound if it does not contain:

  1. A. Free lime
  2. B. Silica
  3. C. Alumina
  4. D. Iron oxide
Answer: A. Free lime

Q4. Suitable stone for construction of pier is:

  1. A. Granite
  2. B. Limestone
  3. C. Chalk
  4. D. Laterite
Answer: A. Granite

Q5. Stones are placed in their natural plane so that load acts:

  1. A. Normal
  2. B. Parallel
  3. C. Tangential
  4. D. Oblique
Answer: A. Normal

Q6. Which component of pozzolana acts with free lime?

  1. A. Silica
  2. B. Alumina
  3. C. Iron oxide
  4. D. Magnesia
Answer: A. Silica

Q7. Vicat apparatus is used for:

  1. A. Consistency
  2. B. Soundness
  3. C. Abrasion
  4. D. Tensile strength
Answer: A. Consistency

Q8. Fire proof paints have:

  1. A. Asbestos fibers
  2. B. PVC fibers
  3. C. Glass fibers
  4. D. Cotton fibers
Answer: A. Asbestos fibers

Q9. Seasoning is not helpful in:

  1. A. Removing natural defects
  2. B. Reducing weight
  3. C. Increasing strength
  4. D. Increasing durability
Answer: A. Removing natural defects

Q10. Weir is narrow crested when width of crest is:

  1. A. Half of the depth of water
  2. B. Equal to depth of water
  3. C. Twice the depth of water
  4. D. More than twice the depth
Answer: A. Half of the depth of water

Q11. In applying trapezoidal rule, prismoidal correction is:

  1. A. Subtracted
  2. B. Added
  3. C. Multiplied
  4. D. Ignored
Answer: A. Subtracted

Q12. In plane table survey, method used to locate point from one station is:

  1. A. Radiation
  2. B. Intersection
  3. C. Resection
  4. D. Traversing
Answer: A. Radiation

Q13. Earth is taken flat for area up to:

  1. A. 250 km2
  2. B. 100 km2
  3. C. 500 km2
  4. D. 1000 km2
Answer: A. 250 km2

Q14. Vertical distance between contour lines is called:

  1. A. Contour interval
  2. B. Horizontal equivalent
  3. C. Gradient
  4. D. Equivalent
Answer: A. Contour interval

Q15. Sum of latitudes and departures for a closed traverse is:

  1. A. 0
  2. B. 1
  3. C. Depends on area
  4. D. Depends on scale
Answer: A. 0

Q16. When d is the distance between staff and instrument, the error due to earth's curvature is proportional to:

  1. A. d2
  2. B. d
  3. C. sqrt(d)
  4. D. d3
Answer: A. d2

Q17. The line on which the setup of survey is built is called:

  1. A. Base line
  2. B. Check line
  3. C. Tie line
  4. D. Contour line
Answer: A. Base line

Q18. In chain surveying, area is divided into:

  1. A. Triangles
  2. B. Squares
  3. C. Trapeziums
  4. D. Circles
Answer: A. Triangles

Q19. Shift of curve is given by:

  1. A. L2/24R
  2. B. L/24R
  3. C. L2/12R
  4. D. L2/6R
Answer: A. L2/24R

Q20. Lines joining points of same declination are called:

  1. A. Isogonic lines
  2. B. Agonic lines
  3. C. Contour lines
  4. D. Isobars
Answer: A. Isogonic lines

Q21. Closely spaced contour lines represent:

  1. A. Steep slope
  2. B. Flat slope
  3. C. Depression only
  4. D. Gentle slope
Answer: A. Steep slope

Q22. Design of vertical curve is based on:

  1. A. Gradient
  2. B. Radius only
  3. C. Speed only
  4. D. Superelevation
Answer: A. Gradient

Q23. Nozzles are provided for:

  1. A. Increasing velocity
  2. B. Decreasing velocity
  3. C. Increasing pressure
  4. D. Decreasing discharge
Answer: A. Increasing velocity

Q24. Fluid having zero viscosity is called:

  1. A. Ideal fluid
  2. B. Real fluid
  3. C. Newtonian fluid
  4. D. Plastic fluid
Answer: A. Ideal fluid

Q25. When a floating body is given small displacement, it starts oscillating about a point called:

  1. A. Metacenter
  2. B. Centroid
  3. C. Center of buoyancy
  4. D. Center of pressure
Answer: A. Metacenter
