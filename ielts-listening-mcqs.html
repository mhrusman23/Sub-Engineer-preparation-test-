Q1. How many sections are in the IELTS Listening test?

  1. A. Two
  2. B. Three
  3. C. Four
  4. D. Five
Answer: C. Four

Q2. You hear each recording:

  1. A. Twice
  2. B. Three times
  3. C. Only once
  4. D. As often as you like
Answer: C. Only once

Q3. Section 1 of IELTS Listening is usually:

  1. A. An academic lecture
  2. B. An everyday conversation
  3. C. A news report
  4. D. A poem
Answer: B. An everyday conversation

Q4. At the end of the Listening test you get extra time to:

  1. A. Re-listen to the recording
  2. B. Transfer answers to the answer sheet
  3. C. Ask questions
  4. D. Take a break
Answer: B. Transfer answers to the answer sheet

Q5. A signpost word like 'however' shows:

  1. A. Addition
  2. B. Contrast
  3. C. Time order
  4. D. An example
Answer: B. Contrast

Q6. In a map-labelling task, you should first:

  1. A. Write full sentences
  2. B. Study the map and key places
  3. C. Close your eyes
  4. D. Read Section 4
Answer: B. Study the map and key places

Q7. Numbers and dates in Listening must be written:

  1. A. Only in words
  2. B. Exactly as required by the instructions
  3. C. In your own language
  4. D. With extra zeros
Answer: B. Exactly as required by the instructions

Q8. Distractors in Listening are:

  1. A. Loud noises
  2. B. Wrong options designed to mislead
  3. C. Breaks in the audio
  4. D. Extra questions
Answer: B. Wrong options designed to mislead

Q9. The Listening test lasts about:

  1. A. 10 minutes
  2. B. 20 minutes
  3. C. 30 minutes
  4. D. 60 minutes
Answer: C. 30 minutes

Q10. Before each section starts, you should:

  1. A. Sleep
  2. B. Read the questions quickly
  3. C. Write an essay
  4. D. Check your phone
Answer: B. Read the questions quickly

Q11. In Section 2, the talk is usually:

  1. A. A conversation between students
  2. B. A monologue about a place, tour or service
  3. C. A lecture on science
  4. D. A debate
Answer: B. A monologue about a place, tour or service

Q12. In Section 3, you usually hear:

  1. A. One speaker only
  2. B. Two or three people discussing an academic topic
  3. C. A weather report
  4. D. A story
Answer: B. Two or three people discussing an academic topic

Q13. Section 4 is:

  1. A. A song
  2. B. An academic lecture by one speaker
  3. C. An interview
  4. D. A phone call
Answer: B. An academic lecture by one speaker

Q14. When a speaker says 'Let me spell that', you should:

  1. A. Ignore it
  2. B. Write the exact letters — a name or word will be spelled
  3. C. Relax
  4. D. Skip the question
Answer: B. Write the exact letters — a name or word will be spelled

Q15. If the speaker corrects themselves ('Tuesday — sorry, Wednesday'), the answer is:

  1. A. Tuesday
  2. B. Wednesday
  3. C. Both
  4. D. Neither
Answer: B. Wednesday

Q16. Plural or singular mistakes in answers:

  1. A. Never matter
  2. B. Can make the answer wrong
  3. C. Are rewarded
  4. D. Are ignored
Answer: B. Can make the answer wrong

Q17. For form completion, answers are usually:

  1. A. Long sentences
  2. B. Names, dates, numbers and short details
  3. C. Opinions
  4. D. Paragraphs
Answer: B. Names, dates, numbers and short details

Q18. A speaker's phrase 'The main advantage is...' signals:

  1. A. The end
  2. B. An important point likely tested
  3. C. A joke
  4. D. A break
Answer: B. An important point likely tested

Q19. If you miss one answer, you should:

  1. A. Stop the test
  2. B. Move on quickly to the next question
  3. C. Go back and think
  4. D. Guess all remaining
Answer: B. Move on quickly to the next question

Q20. Word limit 'NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS' means:

  1. A. Exactly two words
  2. B. One or two words maximum
  3. C. Any number
  4. D. Two sentences
Answer: B. One or two words maximum

Q21. Dates like 'the fifteenth of May' should be written:

  1. A. Only as 15/5
  2. B. As instructed — usually 15 May or May 15
  3. C. In Roman numerals
  4. D. Never
Answer: B. As instructed — usually 15 May or May 15

Q22. In multiple choice listening, you should:

  1. A. Read options during the talk only
  2. B. Read options before the talk and listen for paraphrases
  3. C. Choose the longest
  4. D. Choose A always
Answer: B. Read options before the talk and listen for paraphrases

Q23. A talk about a library tour in Section 2 often includes:

  1. A. Exam dates
  2. B. A map or plan to label
  3. C. Formulas
  4. D. Poems
Answer: B. A map or plan to label

Q24. Synonyms in options mean you should listen for:

  1. A. Exact same words only
  2. B. Same meaning in different words
  3. C. Numbers
  4. D. Names
Answer: B. Same meaning in different words

Q25. The voice says 'roughly fifty'. The best written answer is:

  1. A. 50 exactly
  2. B. 'about 50' or as instructed by the word limit
  3. C. 15
  4. D. 500
Answer: B. 'about 50' or as instructed by the word limit
