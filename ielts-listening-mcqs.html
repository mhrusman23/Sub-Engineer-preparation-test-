Q1. How many sections are in the IELTS Listening test?

  1. A. Two
  2. B. Three
  3. C. Four
  4. D. Five
Answer: C. Four

Q2. You hear each recording:

  1. A. Twice
  2. B. Three times
  3. C. Only once
  4. D. As often as you like
Answer: C. Only once

Q3. Section 1 of IELTS Listening is usually:

  1. A. An academic lecture
  2. B. An everyday conversation
  3. C. A news report
  4. D. A poem
Answer: B. An everyday conversation

Q4. At the end of the Listening test you get extra time to:

  1. A. Re-listen to the recording
  2. B. Transfer answers to the answer sheet
  3. C. Ask questions
  4. D. Take a break
Answer: B. Transfer answers to the answer sheet

Q5. A signpost word like 'however' shows:

  1. A. Addition
  2. B. Contrast
  3. C. Time order
  4. D. An example
Answer: B. Contrast

Q6. In a map-labelling task, you should first:

  1. A. Write full sentences
  2. B. Study the map and key places
  3. C. Close your eyes
  4. D. Read Section 4
Answer: B. Study the map and key places

Q7. Numbers and dates in Listening must be written:

  1. A. Only in words
  2. B. Exactly as required by the instructions
  3. C. In your own language
  4. D. With extra zeros
Answer: B. Exactly as required by the instructions

Q8. Distractors in Listening are:

  1. A. Loud noises
  2. B. Wrong options designed to mislead
  3. C. Breaks in the audio
  4. D. Extra questions
Answer: B. Wrong options designed to mislead

Q9. The Listening test lasts about:

  1. A. 10 minutes
  2. B. 20 minutes
  3. C. 30 minutes
  4. D. 60 minutes
Answer: C. 30 minutes

Q10. Before each section starts, you should:

  1. A. Sleep
  2. B. Read the questions quickly
  3. C. Write an essay
  4. D. Check your phone
Answer: B. Read the questions quickly

Q11. When tackling map or diagram labelling tasks in IELTS Listening, what is the best initial approach?

  1. A. Guess all answers before listening
  2. B. Ignore the visual elements completely
  3. C. Study the given visual layout and orientation clues
  4. D. Translate every label into your native language
Answer: C. Study the given visual layout and orientation clues

Q12. What should a candidate ideally do during the short preparation pause before each listening section begins?

  1. A. Review the upcoming questions and predict potential answers
  2. B. Review answers already written for the previous section
  3. C. Close your eyes and rest until the audio starts
  4. D. Check your phone for time remaining
Answer: A. Review the upcoming questions and predict potential answers

Q13. How many sections make up the entire IELTS Listening exam?

  1. A. Two sections
  2. B. Three sections
  3. C. Four sections
  4. D. Five sections
Answer: C. Four sections

Q14. How are incorrect details or changing opinions by speakers commonly referred to in IELTS Listening?

  1. A. Distractors
  2. B. Transcripts
  3. C. Headings
  4. D. Prefixes
Answer: A. Distractors

Q15. What kind of scenario is typically featured in Part 1 of the IELTS Listening test?

  1. A. A university lecture on science
  2. B. A conversation set in an everyday social context
  3. C. A complex academic debate between experts
  4. D. A solo radio broadcast about history
Answer: B. A conversation set in an everyday social context

Q16. What is the primary function of signpost words like 'however' or 'on the other hand' in listening audio?

  1. A. To signal a change in direction or contrast in ideas
  2. B. To indicate the end of the entire test
  3. C. To repeat the exact number previously mentioned
  4. D. To spell out a proper noun letter by letter
Answer: A. To signal a change in direction or contrast in ideas

Q17. What type of linguistic clues do words like 'most important' and 'greatest influence' typically introduce in an IELTS listening passage?

  1. A. Superlatives indicating top ranking items
  2. B. Contrasting ideas and counter-arguments
  3. C. Chronological dates and historical events
  4. D. Geographical directions on a map
Answer: A. Superlatives indicating top ranking items

Q18. If multiple options in a multiple-choice question are mentioned in the audio recording, how should you determine the correct answer?

  1. A. Pick the very first option you hear
  2. B. Choose the option that actually answers the question stem completely
  3. C. Select whichever option has the longest name
  4. D. Guess the option that is repeated most frequently
Answer: B. Choose the option that actually answers the question stem completely

Q19. When an audio speaker mentions a name or a term and then spells it out letter by letter, what should you do?

  1. A. Ignore the spelling as unnecessary detail
  2. B. Carefully write down the spelling to ensure accuracy
  3. C. Translate the spelled word into your native language
  4. D. Count the total number of letters being spelled
Answer: B. Carefully write down the spelling to ensure accuracy

Q20. In diagram or map labelling tasks, why is it crucial to study the central axis or surrounding features before the audio starts?

  1. A. To memorize the exact transcript word-for-word
  2. B. To orient yourself spatially and know what components to listen for
  3. C. To estimate the exact word count of the entire test
  4. D. To predict the gender of the speakers
Answer: B. To orient yourself spatially and know what components to listen for

Q21. When a listening question contains phrases like 'due to' or 'main reason', what specific relationship are you primarily expected to identify?

  1. A. Cause and effect
  2. B. Sequence of events
  3. C. Spatial orientation
  4. D. Numerical comparison
Answer: A. Cause and effect

Q22. What is the primary danger of hearing an initial piece of information, such as an incorrect street name, before the speaker corrects themselves?

  1. A. It represents a distractor intended to test your vigilance
  2. B. It means the test recording has skipped a section
  3. C. It proves that all subsequent answers will be false
  4. D. It indicates the audio track is malfunctioning
Answer: A. It represents a distractor intended to test your vigilance

Q23. Approximately how long does the entire IELTS Listening audio recording playback last?

  1. A. A) 15 minutes
  2. B. B) 30 minutes
  3. C. C) 45 minutes
  4. D. D) 60 minutes
Answer: B. B) 30 minutes

Q24. How many total questions are there across all parts of the IELTS Listening test?

  1. A. A) 20 questions
  2. B. B) 30 questions
  3. C. C) 40 questions
  4. D. D) 50 questions
Answer: C. C) 40 questions

Q25. Which module of the full IELTS examination is administered first to candidates?

  1. A. A) Listening
  2. B. B) Reading
  3. C. C) Writing
  4. D. D) Speaking
Answer: A. A) Listening
