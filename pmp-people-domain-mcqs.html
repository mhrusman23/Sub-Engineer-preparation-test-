Q1. The People domain of the PMP exam mainly covers:

  1. A. Budgets
  2. B. Leading and managing the project team
  3. C. Contracts
  4. D. Software
Answer: B. Leading and managing the project team

Q2. A servant leader in Agile focuses on:

  1. A. Giving orders
  2. B. Removing obstacles and supporting the team
  3. C. Doing all the work
  4. D. Avoiding the team
Answer: B. Removing obstacles and supporting the team

Q3. The best way to resolve a conflict between two team members is usually:

  1. A. Ignore it
  2. B. Collaborative problem-solving (confront the issue together)
  3. C. Fire one of them
  4. D. Escalate immediately to the sponsor
Answer: B. Collaborative problem-solving (confront the issue together)

Q4. Emotional intelligence helps a project manager to:

  1. A. Calculate budgets
  2. B. Understand and manage emotions of self and others
  3. C. Write code
  4. D. Design networks
Answer: B. Understand and manage emotions of self and others

Q5. In the Tuckman model, team development stages are:

  1. A. Forming, Storming, Norming, Performing, Adjourning
  2. B. Plan, Do, Check, Act
  3. C. Start, Middle, End
  4. D. Hire, Train, Fire
Answer: A. Forming, Storming, Norming, Performing, Adjourning

Q6. A project manager builds a high-performing team mainly by:

  1. A. Fear
  2. B. Clear roles, trust and recognition
  3. C. Long hours
  4. D. Competition only
Answer: B. Clear roles, trust and recognition

Q7. When a stakeholder is disengaged, the project manager should first:

  1. A. Remove them
  2. B. Analyse their interest/influence and plan engagement
  3. C. Ignore them
  4. D. Complain to the sponsor
Answer: B. Analyse their interest/influence and plan engagement

Q8. Active listening means:

  1. A. Waiting to speak
  2. B. Fully concentrating, paraphrasing and confirming understanding
  3. C. Taking notes only
  4. D. Interrupting politely
Answer: B. Fully concentrating, paraphrasing and confirming understanding

Q9. Virtual teams need extra attention to:

  1. A. Office furniture
  2. B. Communication and trust-building
  3. C. Travel budgets
  4. D. Time zones only
Answer: B. Communication and trust-building

Q10. Coaching and mentoring team members helps to:

  1. A. Increase paperwork
  2. B. Develop their skills and performance
  3. C. Replace training
  4. D. Reduce salaries
Answer: B. Develop their skills and performance

Q11. When managing a globally distributed virtual team, the project manager should primarily emphasize:

  1. A. Relying solely on asynchronous formal progress reports
  2. B. Establishing clear communication protocols and building trust across cultures
  3. C. Requiring all members to log into webcams for eight hours daily
  4. D. Discouraging informal chats to maintain strict corporate focus
Answer: B. Establishing clear communication protocols and building trust across cultures

Q12. A project manager notices that a key team member's productivity has suddenly dropped. According to best practices for the People domain, the project manager should first:

  1. A. Have a private discussion to understand underlying blockers
  2. B. Issue an official warning letter regarding performance
  3. C. Immediately redistribute their tasks to other colleagues
  4. D. Complain to the functional manager during the review
Answer: A. Have a private discussion to understand underlying blockers

Q13. During stakeholder engagement activities, a project manager discovers that a powerful stakeholder holds unrealistic expectations about project delivery speed. The best approach is to:

  1. A. Ignore the stakeholder's feedback until project closure
  2. B. Engage the stakeholder early to understand concerns and align expectations
  3. C. Limit the stakeholder's access to regular project status meetings
  4. D. Agree to all demands regardless of feasibility constraints
Answer: B. Engage the stakeholder early to understand concerns and align expectations

Q14. An essential element of emotional intelligence that enables a project manager to sense others' feelings and perspectives is:

  1. A. Technical proficiency
  2. B. Empathy
  3. C. Financial acumen
  4. D. Risk modeling
Answer: B. Empathy

Q15. When a newly formed project team is experiencing friction and disagreements regarding priorities, the best immediate action for the project manager is to:

  1. A. Escalate the issue directly to the project sponsor
  2. B. Facilitate direct dialogue to address root causes constructively
  3. C. Reassign the conflicting members to separate workstreams
  4. D. Impose a strict project management plan to restrict autonomy
Answer: B. Facilitate direct dialogue to address root causes constructively

Q16. Which of the following best describes the core mindset of a servant leader within an agile team context?

  1. A. Directing every daily task with absolute authority
  2. B. Focusing on removing impediments and supporting team growth
  3. C. Controlling all communications sent to external clients
  4. D. Enforcing strict adherence to predictive administrative controls
Answer: B. Focusing on removing impediments and supporting team growth

Q17. What is the primary benefit of empowering project team members to make local decisions regarding their assigned work packages?

  1. A. It eliminates the need for any project documentation
  2. B. It increases accountability, motivation, and ownership of outcomes
  3. C. It shifts all project risks away from the project manager
  4. D. It guarantees the project will finish under budget
Answer: B. It increases accountability, motivation, and ownership of outcomes

Q18. An experienced stakeholder is frequently missing important project status meetings and showing resistance to project deliverables. The best approach for the project manager is to:

  1. A. Stop sending them any further project documentation
  2. B. Meet with the stakeholder one-on-one to understand their concerns and expectations
  3. C. Complain to the project sponsor about their lack of cooperation
  4. D. Proceed with the project without considering their input
Answer: B. Meet with the stakeholder one-on-one to understand their concerns and expectations

Q19. During a virtual team meeting, a project manager notices that several remote members remain silent and appear disengaged. What action should the project manager take?

  1. A. Remove them from the project distribution list
  2. B. Directly invite their input using inclusive facilitation techniques
  3. C. Assume they have nothing valuable to contribute
  4. D. Complain about their lack of enthusiasm to the functional manager
Answer: B. Directly invite their input using inclusive facilitation techniques

Q20. Which of the following activities best demonstrates a project manager applying servant leadership principles to support their agile team?

  1. A. Assigning detailed daily tasks to each individual
  2. B. Shielding the team from external interruptions and removing impediments
  3. C. Enforcing strict adherence to traditional bureaucratic processes
  4. D. Setting mandatory overtime without team consultation
Answer: B. Shielding the team from external interruptions and removing impediments

Q21. To maintain high team morale and ensure alignment during a major organizational change, a project manager should primarily focus on:

  1. A. Increasing reporting frequency to executive management
  2. B. Transparent and consistent communication with all team members
  3. C. Reducing the project scope to lower stress
  4. D. Replacing resistant team members without notice
Answer: B. Transparent and consistent communication with all team members

Q22. When handling a disagreement between two key technical experts regarding software architecture, what is the most effective initial approach for the project manager?

  1. A. Escalate the issue to the sponsor immediately
  2. B. Encourage the parties to collaborate and find a win-win solution
  3. C. Force your own technical decision on them
  4. D. Ignore the disagreement until it resolves itself
Answer: B. Encourage the parties to collaborate and find a win-win solution

Q23. An agile project manager empowers the team by:

  1. A. Making all technical decisions without consulting them
  2. B. Allowing them self-organization and autonomy in estimating and completing work
  3. C. Removing all accountability from the project schedule
  4. D. Requiring daily approval for every task
Answer: B. Allowing them self-organization and autonomy in estimating and completing work

Q24. When managing a multicultural virtual team, a project manager should pay special attention to:

  1. A. Enforcing a single time zone for all meetings
  2. B. Ignoring cultural differences to maintain uniformity
  3. C. Establishing clear communication norms and acknowledging time zone differences
  4. D. Mandating video participation for every single chat
Answer: C. Establishing clear communication norms and acknowledging time zone differences

Q25. During the storming stage of team development, a project manager should primarily:

  1. A. Exercise strict authoritative control
  2. B. Facilitate open communication and conflict resolution
  3. C. Disband the team immediately
  4. D. Assign all tasks personally to avoid arguments
Answer: B. Facilitate open communication and conflict resolution
