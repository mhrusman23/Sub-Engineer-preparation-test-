PPSC Test MCQs (17.03.2018) – Past Paper Solved MCQs

Q1. Inclination between true meridian and survey line is called:

  1. A. Azimuth
  2. B. Declination
  3. C. Bearing error
  4. D. Isogonic line
Answer: A. Azimuth

Q2. Application of Castigliano's first theorem is valid when:

  1. A. Principle of superposition is applicable
  2. B. Material is plastic
  3. C. Structure is unstable
  4. D. Loads are dynamic
Answer: A. Principle of superposition is applicable

Q3. In moment distribution method, sum of distribution factors for a joint is:

  1. A. 1
  2. B. 0
  3. C. 0.5
  4. D. 2
Answer: B. 0

Q4. Number of independent equations to be satisfied for static equilibrium of a plane structure is:

  1. A. 3
  2. B. 2
  3. C. 4
  4. D. 6
Answer: D. 6

Q5. Degree of static indeterminacy of a pin jointed plane frame is:

  1. A. m + r - 2j
  2. B. m + r - 3j
  3. C. 2m + r - 2j
  4. D. m - r + 2j
Answer: A. m + r - 2j

Q6. In a pin jointed frame, if m + r > 2j, the frame is:

  1. A. Stable and statically indeterminate
  2. B. Unstable
  3. C. Stable and determinate
  4. D. Critical
Answer: A. Stable and statically indeterminate

Q7. A pin jointed frame is unstable when:

  1. A. m + r < 2j
  2. B. m + r > 2j
  3. C. m + r = 2j
  4. D. m = 2j
Answer: A. m + r < 2j

Q8. At principal axis of a section, the product of inertia is:

  1. A. Zero
  2. B. Maximum
  3. C. Minimum
  4. D. Negative
Answer: A. Zero

Q9. Torque applied on a member means each cross section of it undergoes:

  1. A. Shear stress
  2. B. Normal stress
  3. C. Bending stress
  4. D. No stress
Answer: A. Shear stress

Q10. If neutral axis depth is greater than critical neutral axis depth, the section is:

  1. A. Over-reinforced
  2. B. Under-reinforced
  3. C. Balanced
  4. D. Doubly reinforced
Answer: A. Over-reinforced

Q11. While doing treatment of water, sedimentation is done:

  1. A. Before filtration
  2. B. After filtration
  3. C. Simultaneously with filtration
  4. D. Never
Answer: A. Before filtration

Q12. Pitot tube is used for measuring:

  1. A. Flow velocity
  2. B. Discharge directly
  3. C. Pressure only
  4. D. Turbidity
Answer: A. Flow velocity

Q13. Small quantity of water can be moved up to large height with small hydraulic effort; the machine used is:

  1. A. Hydraulic ram
  2. B. Centrifugal pump
  3. C. Reciprocating pump
  4. D. Jet pump
Answer: A. Hydraulic ram

Q14. In 3-moment equation, a fixed end is replaced by:

  1. A. Zero length span
  2. B. Infinite length span
  3. C. Simple support
  4. D. Roller support
Answer: A. Zero length span

Q15. Fixed support in a conjugate beam is replaced by:

  1. A. Free end
  2. B. Roller support
  3. C. Hinge
  4. D. No change
Answer: A. Free end

Q16. Carry over factor for a member whose far end is fixed is:

  1. A. 0.5
  2. B. 1.0
  3. C. 0.75
  4. D. 0.25
Answer: A. 0.5

Q17. For a fixed joint, there is/are:

  1. A. 2 displacements and one rotation
  2. B. Only 1 rotation
  3. C. Only 2 displacements
  4. D. 3 rotations
Answer: A. 2 displacements and one rotation

Q18. For a retaining wall, factor of safety for sliding is 1.5; factor of safety for overturning is:

  1. A. 2
  2. B. 1.5
  3. C. 1
  4. D. 3
Answer: C. 1

Q19. Dry density as compared to bulk density is:

  1. A. Less
  2. B. More
  3. C. Equal
  4. D. Double
Answer: A. Less

Q20. Viscosity is a function of:

  1. A. Temperature
  2. B. Density only
  3. C. Volume
  4. D. Pressure only
Answer: A. Temperature

Q21. Estimated time to complete an activity is called:

  1. A. Duration
  2. B. Float
  3. C. Event
  4. D. Milestone
Answer: A. Duration

Q22. In hilly areas, cutting of road is done on:

  1. A. Inner side
  2. B. Outer side
  3. C. Both sides
  4. D. Center
Answer: A. Inner side

Q23. Force considered for virtual work method is/are:

  1. A. External + internal
  2. B. Only external
  3. C. Only internal
  4. D. Only reactive
Answer: A. External + internal

Q24. Ideal fluid is:

  1. A. Frictionless & incompressible
  2. B. Frictional & compressible
  3. C. Viscous & compressible
  4. D. Turbulent
Answer: A. Frictionless & incompressible

Q25. Which rule is used when angular measurements are more accurate than linear measurements?

  1. A. Transit rule
  2. B. Bowditch rule
  3. C. Compass rule
  4. D. Empirical rule
Answer: A. Transit rule
