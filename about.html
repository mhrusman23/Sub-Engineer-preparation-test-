About Engrgroup23

Who we are

Engrgroup23 (ppscengr.com) is a free online practice website for Civil Engineering job tests in Punjab, Pakistan. It was built by Engr Usman Amjad and Engr Umar Sajjid to help students prepare for exams such as PPSC Sub Engineer (Civil) and Building Inspector, and for Civil Diploma subjects.

What you will find here

  • Past papers with solved MCQs and the correct answers.
  • Civil Diploma 1st, 2nd and 3rd year subject-wise MCQs.
  • Online mock tests with scoring, timer and Urdu translation help.
  • An AI helper that explains a question when you ask for it.

Our aim

We want every student to practise real past-paper style questions for free, on a phone, with a clear explanation. Content is checked by engineers, but mistakes can still happen. If you find a wrong answer or a wrong question, please tell us and we will correct it.

Please note

This website is an independent educational project. It is not connected with the Punjab Public Service Commission (PPSC) or any government department.
