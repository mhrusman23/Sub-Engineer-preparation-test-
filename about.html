About Engrgroup23

Who we are

Engrgroup23 (ppscengr.com) is a free online practice website for job tests and exams in Pakistan. It was built by Engr Usman Amjad and Engr Umar Sajjid to help students prepare for PPSC past papers and subject MCQs in Civil, Electrical, Mechanical, IT, ACCA, CA and General Knowledge.

Our vision

Our vision is international in standard and simple in promise: free, quality exam practice for every student, on any phone, anywhere. We started with PPSC Sub Engineer (Civil) and Building Inspector papers, and we keep launching new subjects so that no student has to buy expensive books or join costly academies just to practise.

Subjects we cover

  • PPSC past papers — Sub Engineer, Building Inspector and other solved papers.
  • Civil — Civil Diploma 1st, 2nd and 3rd year subjects and engineering MCQs.
  • Electrical — Circuit Theory, Machines, Power Systems and more.
  • Mechanical — Thermodynamics, Machine Design, Fluid Mechanics and more.
  • IT — Programming, Databases, Operating Systems, Computer Networks and more.
  • ACCA — BT, MA, FA, LW, PM, TX, FR, AA, FM and the Strategic Professional papers.
  • CA — CAF 1 to 8 and CFAP 1 to 6.
  • General Knowledge — Pakistan GK, Pakistan Studies, Islamiat, English, Urdu, Mathematics, General Science and Current Affairs.

What you will find here

  • Past papers with solved MCQs and the correct answers.
  • Subject-wise MCQs for every subject above, with new questions added regularly.
  • Unlimited AI practice — fresh MCQs generated for you as you practise.
  • Online mock tests with scoring and a timer.
  • A Real Exam Test mode that feels like the real exam hall.
  • An AI tutor that explains a question when you ask for it, with Urdu translation help.

Our aim

We want every student to practise real past-paper style questions for free, on a phone, with a clear explanation. Content is checked by engineers, but mistakes can still happen. If you find a wrong answer or a wrong question, please tell us and we will correct it.

Please note

This website is an independent educational project. It is not connected with the Punjab Public Service Commission (PPSC) or any government department.
