Q1. A survey in which curvature of earth is taken into account.

  1. A. Geodetic Survey
  2. B. Cadastral
  3. C. Plane Survey
  4. D. City Survey
Answer: A. Geodetic Survey

Q2. A survey which is carried out to detect the mountains, river, water bodies, wooden area and other cultural details are:

  1. A. Topographical Survey
  2. B. Cadastral Survey
  3. C. City Survey
  4. D. Hydrographic survey
Answer: A. Topographical Survey

Q3. The main principle of survey is to work from

  1. A. The whole to the part
  2. B. the centre to the boundary
  3. C. The part to the whole.
  4. D. All of these
Answer: A. The whole to the part

Q4. The length of Engineer's chain

  1. A. 150ft
  2. B. 66ft.
  3. C. 33ft.
  4. D. 100ft
Answer: D. 100ft

Q5. The length of revenue chain.

  1. A. 150 ft
  2. B. 33ft.
  3. C. 100ft.
  4. D. 66ft.
Answer: B. 33ft.

Q6. Length of one link of 30-meter chain.

  1. A. 30cm
  2. B. 5cm
  3. C. 10cm
  4. D. 20cm
Answer: D. 20cm

Q7. Correction for slope:

  1. A. 2L/h
  2. B. h/L²
  3. C. h²/2L
  4. D. h/L
Answer: C. h²/2L

Q8. An invar tape is made of an alloy of

  1. A. Copper and steel
  2. B. Nickel and steel
  3. C. Brass and steel
  4. D. copper and nickle
Answer: B. Nickel and steel

Q9. Difference of back bearing and fore bearing of a line in WCB system.

  1. A. 270°
  2. B. 180°
  3. C. 360°
  4. D. 90°
Answer: B. 180°

Q10. Angle between true meridian and magnetic meridian:

  1. A. Bearing
  2. B. Compass
  3. C. Declination
  4. D. Dip
Answer: C. Declination

Q11. Maximum value of bearing measured by surveyor compass.

  1. A. 90°
  2. B. 270°
  3. C. 360°
  4. D. 180°
Answer: A. 90°

Q12. If the bearing of line AB is 45 NE and AC is 35 SE then angle BAC will be.

  1. A. 60°
  2. B. 75°
  3. C. 90°
  4. D. 100°
Answer: D. 100°

Q13. If the reduced bearing of line AB = 80° SE, its W.C.B

  1. A. 100°
  2. B. 240°
  3. C. 200°
  4. D. 270°
Answer: A. 100°

Q14. W.C.B of a line is 120°, its R.B will be

  1. A. SE 60°
  2. B. SW 20°
  3. C. SW 60°
  4. D. SE 20°
Answer: A. SE 60°

Q15. If n is the number of sides of a closed traverse then sum of its internal angles will be

  1. A. (al+4)90°
  2. B. (2n+4)90°
  3. C. (2n-4)90°
  4. D. (n+2) 90°
Answer: C. (2n-4)90°

Q16. R.B of a line is N 87 W, its W.C.B will be

  1. A. 273°
  2. B. 187°
  3. C. 87°
  4. D. 93°
Answer: A. 273°

Q17. At the magnetic poles, magnitude of dip is

  1. A. 0
  2. B. 45°
  3. C. 90°
  4. D. 180°
Answer: C. 90°

Q18. An instrument used in plane tabling for sighting.

  1. A. Telescope
  2. B. Alidade
  3. C. U-Frame
  4. D. Trough compass
Answer: B. Alidade

Q19. Process to determine the elevation of points of earth:

  1. A. Traversing
  2. B. Tachometry
  3. C. Chaining
  4. D. Levelling
Answer: D. Levelling

Q20. The selected Datum for Pakistan is situated at:

  1. A. Mean sea level at Karachi
  2. B. M.S.Lat Thatta.
  3. C. M.S.Lat Gwadar
  4. D. All of these
Answer: A. Mean sea level at Karachi

Q21. The first reading of staff at a point or bench mark is called.

  1. A. Inter Sight
  2. B. Fore Sight
  3. C. Any one
  4. D. Back Sight
Answer: D. Back Sight

Q22. A point upon which B.S and F.S both readings are taken is called.

  1. A. Bench Mark
  2. B. Datum
  3. C. Double points
  4. D. Change Point
Answer: D. Change Point

Q23. BS on a BM is 6.50 ft and RL of BM is 100 ft. The H.I. will be

  1. A. 6.50'
  2. B. 106.50'
  3. C. 93.50'
  4. D. 100'
Answer: B. 106.50'

Q24. Back sight on a B.M having R.L 100 m is 2.685 m. At any point F.S is 1.345 m, R.L of this point will be

  1. A. 100.685
  2. B. 101.34
  3. C. 100.345
  4. D. 102.685
Answer: B. 101.34

Q25. The operation of leveling across any river is called.

  1. A. Differential leveling
  2. B. Reciprocal leveling
  3. C. Fly leveling
  4. D. X-sectioning
Answer: B. Reciprocal leveling
