Q1. The primary financial objective of a company is usually to:

  1. A. Maximise shareholder wealth
  2. B. Minimise all costs
  3. C. Maximise turnover only
  4. D. Avoid all risk
Answer: A. Maximise shareholder wealth

Q2. An investment should normally be accepted if its NPV is:

  1. A. Positive
  2. B. Negative
  3. C. Less than the payback
  4. D. Zero only
Answer: A. Positive

Q3. The IRR is the discount rate at which NPV equals:

  1. A. One
  2. B. Zero
  3. C. The profit
  4. D. The cost of capital
Answer: B. Zero

Q4. Simple payback period ignores the:

  1. A. Time value of money
  2. B. Project length
  3. C. Cash inflows
  4. D. Initial cost
Answer: A. Time value of money

Q5. WACC is a weighted average of the cost of equity and the cost of:

  1. A. Inventory
  2. B. Tax
  3. C. Debt
  4. D. Labour
Answer: C. Debt

Q6. In CAPM, the cost of equity equals Rf plus beta multiplied by:

  1. A. (Rm + Rf)
  2. B. Rf
  3. C. Rm
  4. D. (Rm - Rf)
Answer: D. (Rm - Rf)

Q7. Working capital is calculated as current assets minus:

  1. A. Total assets
  2. B. Current liabilities
  3. C. Equity
  4. D. Non-current liabilities
Answer: B. Current liabilities

Q8. Higher gearing generally means higher:

  1. A. Liquidity
  2. B. Financial risk
  3. C. Dividend certainty
  4. D. Inventory
Answer: B. Financial risk

Q9. A forward contract is mainly used to hedge:

  1. A. Inventory risk
  2. B. Audit risk
  3. C. Staff turnover
  4. D. Exchange rate risk
Answer: D. Exchange rate risk

Q10. Which source of finance is a form of equity?

  1. A. Retained earnings
  2. B. Bank loan
  3. C. Overdraft
  4. D. Debenture
Answer: A. Retained earnings

Q11. When computing the weighted average cost of capital (WACC), how should the cost of debt be incorporated?

  1. A. Before-tax cost of debt
  2. B. After-tax cost of debt
  3. C. Nominal coupon rate
  4. D. Current dividend yield
Answer: B. After-tax cost of debt

Q12. How does an increase in the corporate tax rate affect a company's Weighted Average Cost of Capital (WACC)?

  1. A. Increases WACC
  2. B. Decreases WACC
  3. C. Has no impact on WACC
  4. D. Eliminates the cost of equity
Answer: B. Decreases WACC

Q13. In financial management, what does a high Price-to-Earnings (P/E) ratio typically indicate about a company's market perception?

  1. A. The company is in financial distress
  2. B. Investors expect high future earnings growth
  3. C. The stock is significantly undervalued
  4. D. The company pays very high dividend yields
Answer: B. Investors expect high future earnings growth

Q14. What does a current ratio of 1.5:1 indicate about an entity's liquidity?

  1. A. The entity has $1.50 of liquid cash for every $1 of non-current liabilities
  2. B. The entity has $1.50 of current assets for every $1 of current liabilities
  3. C. The entity has $1.50 of current liabilities for every $1 of current assets
  4. D. The entity is experiencing severe bankruptcy risk
Answer: B. The entity has $1.50 of current assets for every $1 of current liabilities

Q15. Which of the following ratios measures a company's ability to cover its short-term obligations with its most liquid assets?

  1. A. Current ratio
  2. B. Quick ratio
  3. C. Gearing ratio
  4. D. Return on capital employed
Answer: B. Quick ratio

Q16. Which investment appraisal method takes into account the time value of money and uses the cost of capital as the discount rate?

  1. A. Accounting Rate of Return (ARR)
  2. B. Payback Period
  3. C. Net Present Value (NPV)
  4. D. Return on Capital Employed (ROCE)
Answer: C. Net Present Value (NPV)

Q17. In investment appraisal, what does a Net Present Value (NPV) of zero indicate?

  1. A. The project earns a return exactly equal to the cost of capital
  2. B. The project is generating a net loss
  3. C. The project earns a return higher than the cost of capital
  4. D. The initial investment was not recovered
Answer: A. The project earns a return exactly equal to the cost of capital

Q18. When applying shadow pricing in environmental cost-benefit analysis, what does the shadow price represent?

  1. A. The market price regulated by the government
  2. B. The opportunity cost of using a resource when no active market price exists
  3. C. The future discounted value of a capital asset
  4. D. The incremental tax shield on debt financing
Answer: B. The opportunity cost of using a resource when no active market price exists

Q19. Which of the following is considered a relevant cash flow when evaluating a capital investment project?

  1. A. Sunk costs already incurred on preliminary market research
  2. B. Allocated general corporate overheads
  3. C. Future incremental cash inflows directly generated by the project
  4. D. Past interest payments on existing bank loans
Answer: C. Future incremental cash inflows directly generated by the project

Q20. What does a gearing ratio measure in corporate finance?

  1. A. The short-term liquidity of a company
  2. B. The proportion of capital funded by debt compared to equity
  3. C. The speed at which inventory is sold
  4. D. The overall profitability of operational activities
Answer: B. The proportion of capital funded by debt compared to equity

Q21. What is the key implication of the Modigliani-Miller theorem without taxes regarding capital structure?

  1. A. The value of a firm is maximized at a 50% debt-to-equity ratio
  2. B. The market value of a firm is independent of its capital structure
  3. C. Debt financing always increases the overall weighted average cost of capital
  4. D. Equity financing is always cheaper than debt financing
Answer: B. The market value of a firm is independent of its capital structure

Q22. What is the impact on operating gearing if a company replaces manual labour with automated machinery financed by debt?

  1. A. Operating gearing will decrease
  2. B. Operating gearing will increase
  3. C. Operating gearing will remain completely unchanged
  4. D. Financial gearing will decrease while operating gearing remains unaffected
Answer: B. Operating gearing will increase

Q23. In the context of investment appraisal, which cash flows are considered relevant for a capital budgeting decision?

  1. A. Sunk costs already incurred
  2. B. Future incremental cash flows
  3. C. Allocated general overhead costs
  4. D. Historical book values of existing equipment
Answer: B. Future incremental cash flows

Q24. An entity uses an EOQ model for inventory management. If the ordering cost doubles while all other variables remain constant, what will be the effect on the Economic Order Quantity?

  1. A. It will increase by a factor of the square root of 2
  2. B. It will double
  3. C. It will remain unchanged
  4. D. It will decrease by half
Answer: A. It will increase by a factor of the square root of 2

Q25. What is the primary financial goal of a corporate firm in financial management?

  1. A. Maximization of total revenue
  2. B. Maximization of shareholder wealth
  3. C. Minimization of operational taxes
  4. D. Maximization of market share
Answer: B. Maximization of shareholder wealth
