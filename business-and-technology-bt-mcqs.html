Q1. Which of these is a primary objective of a not-for-profit organisation?

  1. A. Increasing owner wealth
  2. B. Maximising dividends to owners
  3. C. Delivering its service or cause within available funds
  4. D. Maximising its share price
Answer: C. Delivering its service or cause within available funds

Q2. In Mintzberg's managerial roles, representing the organisation at ceremonial events is the role of the:

  1. A. Monitor
  2. B. Disseminator
  3. C. Negotiator
  4. D. Figurehead
Answer: D. Figurehead

Q3. Which of Porter's five forces looks at how easily new firms can enter an industry?

  1. A. Threat of substitutes
  2. B. Rivalry among existing competitors
  3. C. Threat of new entrants
  4. D. Bargaining power of buyers
Answer: C. Threat of new entrants

Q4. PESTEL analysis is mainly used to examine the:

  1. A. Financial ratios only
  2. B. External macro-environment
  3. C. Internal resources only
  4. D. Production schedule
Answer: B. External macro-environment

Q5. Which document sets out the rules for the internal management of a company?

  1. A. Articles of association
  2. B. Audit report
  3. C. Prospectus
  4. D. Sales invoice
Answer: A. Articles of association

Q6. Reconciling a bank statement to the cash book is an example of a:

  1. A. Detective control
  2. B. Dividend policy
  3. C. Physical security control
  4. D. Strategic plan
Answer: A. Detective control

Q7. The CIA triad of information security stands for confidentiality, integrity and:

  1. A. Auditability
  2. B. Availability
  3. C. Accuracy
  4. D. Authority
Answer: B. Availability

Q8. Separating the roles of chairman and chief executive mainly strengthens:

  1. A. Credit control
  2. B. Corporate governance
  3. C. Production speed
  4. D. Product pricing
Answer: B. Corporate governance

Q9. In a SWOT analysis, a new competitor entering the market is classed as a:

  1. A. Weakness
  2. B. Opportunity
  3. C. Strength
  4. D. Threat
Answer: D. Threat

Q10. A sales invoice produced by an accounting system is typically an output of a:

  1. A. Expert system
  2. B. Transaction processing system
  3. C. Executive information system
  4. D. Decision support model
Answer: B. Transaction processing system

Q11. In strategic management, which matrix helps an organization analyze its business portfolio based on market growth and relative market share?

  1. A. Ansoff Matrix
  2. B. Boston Consulting Group (BCG) Matrix
  3. C. SWOT Analysis
  4. D. Value Chain Analysis
Answer: B. Boston Consulting Group (BCG) Matrix

Q12. Which of the following governance principles emphasizes the separation of the roles of the Chairman and the Chief Executive Officer (CEO)?

  1. A. Shareholder primacy principle
  2. B. Board independence and balance of power
  3. C. Short-term profitability focus
  4. D. Strict cost-cutting policy
Answer: B. Board independence and balance of power

Q13. According to the ACCA Code of Ethics and Conduct, which fundamental principle requires an accountant to be straightforward and honest in all professional relationships?

  1. A. Objectivity
  2. B. Professional Competence and Due Care
  3. C. Integrity
  4. D. Confidentiality
Answer: C. Integrity

Q14. Which of the following describes the fundamental principle of 'Professional Competence and Due Care' under the ACCA Code of Ethics?

  1. A. To be straightforward and honest in all professional relationships
  2. B. To maintain professional knowledge and skill at the level required to ensure competent service
  3. C. To not allow bias or conflict of interest to override professional judgments
  4. D. To respect the confidentiality of information acquired as a result of professional relationships
Answer: B. To maintain professional knowledge and skill at the level required to ensure competent service

Q15. According to the ACCA Code of Ethics and Conduct, which fundamental principle requires a professional accountant to be straightforward and honest?

  1. A. Objectivity
  2. B. Integrity
  3. C. Professional competence
  4. D. Confidentiality
Answer: B. Integrity

Q16. Which analytical framework is primarily utilized by an organization to evaluate external macro-environmental influences such as political, economic, social, and technological factors?

  1. A. PESTEL analysis
  2. B. Value chain analysis
  3. C. Liquidity analysis
  4. D. Variance analysis
Answer: A. PESTEL analysis

Q17. Which control category does the periodic physical counting of inventory against warehouse ledger records typically represent?

  1. A. Preventative control
  2. B. Strategic control
  3. C. Detective control
  4. D. Feedback control
Answer: C. Detective control

Q18. Which tool is commonly applied by professionals to categorize daily tasks into four quadrants based on their urgency and importance?

  1. A. Eisenhower matrix
  2. B. Ansoff growth matrix
  3. C. Boston Consulting Group matrix
  4. D. PESTEL framework
Answer: A. Eisenhower matrix

Q19. Which foundational corporate document formally establishes a company's existence and outlines its external operating boundaries with shareholders and the public?

  1. A. Articles of association
  2. B. Memorandum of association
  3. C. Internal audit charter
  4. D. Board diversity policy
Answer: B. Memorandum of association

Q20. When a multinational corporation sets different prices for the exact same product in the United States compared to European markets, this pricing strategy is known as:

  1. A. Penetration pricing
  2. B. Price discrimination
  3. C. Skimming pricing
  4. D. Cost-plus pricing
Answer: B. Price discrimination

Q21. Within information security principles, what do the three letters of the CIA triad stand for?

  1. A. Control, Integration, Access
  2. B. Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability
  3. C. Compliance, Innovation, Auditability
  4. D. Communication, Information, Accuracy
Answer: B. Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability

Q22. According to Charles Handy's typology of organizational cultures, which culture is characterized by power emanating from a central figure or group, typical of small entrepreneurial start-ups?

  1. A. Power culture
  2. B. Role culture
  3. C. Task culture
  4. D. Person culture
Answer: A. Power culture

Q23. Which of the following is classified as a preventive internal control within an accounting information system?

  1. A. Reconciling the bank statement
  2. B. Performing a monthly physical inventory count
  3. C. Requiring dual authorization for large payments
  4. D. Investigating budget variances after month-end
Answer: C. Requiring dual authorization for large payments

Q24. According to the ACCA Code of Ethics and Conduct, which fundamental principle obliges a professional accountant not to compromise professional or business judgment because of bias, conflict of interest, or undue influence of others?

  1. A. Integrity
  2. B. Objectivity
  3. C. Confidentiality
  4. D. Professional Behaviour
Answer: B. Objectivity

Q25. In the context of macro-environmental analysis, demographic shifts such as an aging population belong to which component of the PESTEL framework?

  1. A. Political
  2. B. Economic
  3. C. Social
  4. D. Legal
Answer: C. Social
