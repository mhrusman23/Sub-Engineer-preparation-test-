Q1. A cost that stays constant in total as activity changes within the relevant range is a:

  1. A. Semi-variable cost
  2. B. Stepped variable cost
  3. C. Variable cost
  4. D. Fixed cost
Answer: D. Fixed cost

Q2. Contribution per unit equals selling price per unit minus:

  1. A. Overhead per unit
  2. B. Variable cost per unit
  3. C. Total cost per unit
  4. D. Fixed cost per unit
Answer: B. Variable cost per unit

Q3. Break-even point in units is found by dividing fixed costs by:

  1. A. Profit per unit
  2. B. Contribution per unit
  3. C. Selling price per unit
  4. D. Variable cost per unit
Answer: B. Contribution per unit

Q4. Under absorption costing, fixed production overheads are:

  1. A. Ignored in inventory valuation
  2. B. Treated as selling costs
  3. C. Included in the cost of units produced
  4. D. Charged in full to the period
Answer: C. Included in the cost of units produced

Q5. Which inventory issue method assumes the oldest stock is used first?

  1. A. LIFO
  2. B. Standard cost
  3. C. FIFO
  4. D. Weighted average
Answer: C. FIFO

Q6. The economic order quantity (EOQ) balances ordering costs against:

  1. A. Holding costs
  2. B. Labour costs
  3. C. Depreciation
  4. D. Selling costs
Answer: A. Holding costs

Q7. A budget that starts from zero each period and justifies every item is a:

  1. A. Zero-based budget
  2. B. Rolling budget
  3. C. Flexed budget
  4. D. Incremental budget
Answer: A. Zero-based budget

Q8. The principal budget factor is the:

  1. A. Largest cost in the budget
  2. B. Factor that limits the organisation's activity
  3. C. Total of fixed overheads
  4. D. Cash balance at year end
Answer: B. Factor that limits the organisation's activity

Q9. Idle time cost represents:

  1. A. Direct material wastage
  2. B. Overtime premium
  3. C. Labour paid for time not spent producing
  4. D. Machine depreciation
Answer: C. Labour paid for time not spent producing

Q10. Prime cost consists of direct materials, direct labour and:

  1. A. Factory rent
  2. B. Direct expenses
  3. C. Indirect labour
  4. D. Administration overheads
Answer: B. Direct expenses

Q11. Which pricing strategy involves setting a high initial price for a new innovative product to skim maximum profit from early adopters?

  1. A. Penetration pricing
  2. B. Price skimming
  3. C. Target costing
  4. D. Marginal cost pricing
Answer: B. Price skimming

Q12. In variance analysis, what does a favorable material yield variance indicate?

  1. A. Less material was used than standard for the actual output achieved
  2. B. More material was purchased at a lower price than standard
  3. C. Actual output was lower than the budgeted output
  4. D. Material prices increased unexpectedly during the period
Answer: A. Less material was used than standard for the actual output achieved

Q13. In variance analysis, what causes a favorable direct material price variance?

  1. A. Actual material price paid is higher than standard price
  2. B. Actual material price paid is lower than standard price
  3. C. More material used than standard allowed
  4. D. Less material used than standard allowed
Answer: B. Actual material price paid is lower than standard price

Q14. What is the key characteristic of relevant costs in decision-making?

  1. A. They are historical costs
  2. B. They are future costs that differ between alternatives
  3. C. They are sunk costs already incurred
  4. D. They are allocated fixed overheads
Answer: B. They are future costs that differ between alternatives

Q15. When calculating Economic Order Quantity (EOQ), which of the following costs is assumed to remain constant?

  1. A. Total holding cost
  2. B. Annual demand
  3. C. Total ordering cost
  4. D. Purchase price per unit
Answer: B. Annual demand

Q16. In an ABC (Activity-Based Costing) system, what characterizes batch-level activities?

  1. A. Costs that are incurred regardless of how many units or batches are produced
  2. B. Costs related to activities performed each time a batch is handled or processed
  3. C. Activities required to support specific individual units of product
  4. D. Activities related to maintaining the overall factory building
Answer: B. Costs related to activities performed each time a batch is handled or processed

Q17. Which of the following is classified as a direct cost in a standard manufacturing environment?

  1. A. Factory supervisor salary
  2. B. Raw materials used in production
  3. C. Factory rent and rates
  4. D. Depreciation of plant and machinery
Answer: B. Raw materials used in production

Q18. Which of the following costs is typically classified as a stepped fixed cost in a manufacturing environment?

  1. A. Direct material cost
  2. B. Supervisor salaries when an extra supervisor is hired for every 20 workers
  3. C. Factory rent paid under a long-term lease
  4. D. Depreciation calculated on a straight-line basis
Answer: B. Supervisor salaries when an extra supervisor is hired for every 20 workers

Q19. In a standard costing system, an adverse material price variance combined with a favorable material usage variance might indicate what?

  1. A. Purchase of higher quality raw materials at a higher price, leading to less waste
  2. B. Purchase of lower quality raw materials, leading to excessive waste
  3. C. Inefficient factory workers taking longer to process materials
  4. D. An increase in standard usage quantities by management
Answer: A. Purchase of higher quality raw materials at a higher price, leading to less waste

Q20. Which pricing strategy involves setting a very low initial price to rapidly capture a large market share?

  1. A. Market skimming pricing
  2. B. Penetration pricing
  3. C. Value-based pricing
  4. D. Cost-plus pricing
Answer: B. Penetration pricing

Q21. What is the primary objective of management accounting?

  1. A. Filing annual tax returns
  2. B. Providing internal reports for decision making
  3. C. Auditing historical financial records
  4. D. Preparing statements for external shareholders
Answer: B. Providing internal reports for decision making

Q22. Which term describes a cost that does not change in total within a relevant range of production volume?

  1. A. Variable cost
  2. B. Fixed cost
  3. C. Mixed cost
  4. D. Semi-variable cost
Answer: B. Fixed cost

Q23. In cost-volume-profit analysis, what does the contribution margin represent?

  1. A. Sales revenue minus fixed costs
  2. B. Net income plus operating expenses
  3. C. Sales revenue minus variable costs
  4. D. Gross profit minus administrative expenses
Answer: C. Sales revenue minus variable costs

Q24. What does a favorable labor efficiency variance indicate?

  1. A. Actual hours worked were less than standard hours allowed
  2. B. Actual wage rate was lower than the standard rate
  3. C. More workers were hired than planned
  4. D. Production took longer than expected
Answer: A. Actual hours worked were less than standard hours allowed

Q25. Which budget is always prepared first in the master budgeting process?

  1. A. Production budget
  2. B. Cash budget
  3. C. Sales budget
  4. D. Direct materials budget
Answer: C. Sales budget
