Q1. ISA 315 relates to identifying and assessing risks of:

  1. A. Material misstatement
  2. B. Tax saving
  3. C. Cash flow
  4. D. Dividends
Answer: A. Material misstatement

Q2. ISA 570 deals with the auditor's responsibilities on:

  1. A. Related parties
  2. B. Inventory counts
  3. C. Sampling
  4. D. Going concern
Answer: D. Going concern

Q3. ISA 330 covers the auditor's responses to:

  1. A. Directors' pay
  2. B. Client budgets
  3. C. Assessed risks
  4. D. Tax rules
Answer: C. Assessed risks

Q4. ISQM 1 requires firms to design a system of:

  1. A. Marketing
  2. B. Quality management
  3. C. Cost accounting
  4. D. Tax planning
Answer: B. Quality management

Q5. Key audit matters (ISA 701) are communicated in the auditor's report of:

  1. A. Sole traders only
  2. B. Listed entities
  3. C. Dormant companies only
  4. D. Charities only
Answer: B. Listed entities

Q6. ISA 580 deals with:

  1. A. Using experts
  2. B. Sampling
  3. C. Written representations
  4. D. Fraud
Answer: C. Written representations

Q7. An emphasis of matter paragraph:

  1. A. Does not modify the opinion
  2. B. Always modifies the opinion
  3. C. Replaces the opinion
  4. D. Is optional for adverse opinions only
Answer: A. Does not modify the opinion

Q8. ISA 240 deals with the auditor's responsibilities relating to:

  1. A. Going concern
  2. B. Group audits
  3. C. Leases
  4. D. Fraud
Answer: D. Fraud

Q9. ISA 720 deals with:

  1. A. Other information in documents with audited statements
  2. B. Materiality
  3. C. Confirmations
  4. D. Sampling
Answer: A. Other information in documents with audited statements

Q10. An audit committee mainly helps to oversee:

  1. A. Machine maintenance
  2. B. Financial reporting and audit independence
  3. C. Daily sales
  4. D. Staff holidays
Answer: B. Financial reporting and audit independence

Q11. When an auditor relies on the work of a management's expert, which specific evaluation must be documented under ISA 500?

  1. A. The expert's personal tax records
  2. B. The competence, capabilities, and objectivity of the expert
  3. C. The expert's political affiliations
  4. D. The market share of the expert's firm
Answer: B. The competence, capabilities, and objectivity of the expert

Q12. Which standard guides the auditor on performing review engagements of historical financial statements other than audits?

  1. A. ISRE 2400
  2. B. ISAE 3000
  3. C. ISA 260
  4. D. ISQC 1
Answer: A. ISRE 2400

Q13. What is the primary objective of performing analytical procedures near the end of the audit under ISA 520?

  1. A. To calculate the total audit fee
  2. B. To assist the auditor in forming an overall conclusion on the financial statements
  3. C. To replace all substantive tests of details
  4. D. To test the physical inventory counts
Answer: B. To assist the auditor in forming an overall conclusion on the financial statements

Q14. Under ISA 600, what is the principal auditor referred to as when involving another auditor in the audit of group financial statements?

  1. A. Component auditor
  2. B. Engagement partner
  3. C. Group auditor
  4. D. Independent reviewer
Answer: C. Group auditor

Q15. According to ISAE 3402, what type of report provides assurance on controls at a service organization relevant to user entities' internal control over financial reporting?

  1. A. Type A report
  2. B. Type 1 or Type 2 report
  3. C. Compilation report
  4. D. Agreed-upon procedures report
Answer: B. Type 1 or Type 2 report

Q16. Which safeguard is most critical when an audit firm provides non-audit services, such as internal audit, to a public interest entity audit client?

  1. A. Increasing the audit fee
  2. B. Rotating the junior audit staff annually
  3. C. Applying strict self-review safeguards and ensuring management takes full responsibility
  4. D. Delaying the audit report release date
Answer: C. Applying strict self-review safeguards and ensuring management takes full responsibility

Q17. In accordance with ISA 620, what is the most critical factor an auditor must evaluate regarding an auditor's expert?

  1. A. The expert's fee structure
  2. B. The competence, capabilities, and objectivity of the expert
  3. C. The geographical location of the expert
  4. D. The formatting style of the expert's report
Answer: B. The competence, capabilities, and objectivity of the expert

Q18. Which of the following procedures is most appropriate for an auditor to perform when evaluating management's assessment of going concern?

  1. A. Reviewing subsequent events for items affecting liquidity
  2. B. Recalculating the depreciation charge of all assets
  3. C. Confirming accounts receivable balances directly with debtors
  4. D. Performing physical inventory counts at year-end
Answer: A. Reviewing subsequent events for items affecting liquidity

Q19. When an auditor decides to rely on an internal audit function, which evaluation is primarily required by ISA 610?

  1. A. Assessing internal auditors' qualifications and objectivity
  2. B. Calculating the financial savings of the internal department
  3. C. Reviewing management's personal performance appraisals
  4. D. Determining the direct reporting line to the external auditor
Answer: A. Assessing internal auditors' qualifications and objectivity

Q20. Which of the following represents a primary objective of performing a review engagement on prospective financial information?

  1. A. Providing absolute assurance on future outcomes
  2. B. Expressing an opinion on whether the assumptions provide a reasonable basis
  3. C. Guaranteeing the entity will meet its projected targets
  4. D. Replacing management's responsibility for forecasting
Answer: B. Expressing an opinion on whether the assumptions provide a reasonable basis

Q21. What is the primary purpose of an engagement quality control review (EQCR) listed in ISQM 1?

  1. A. To perform the detailed substantive testing on behalf of the engagement partner
  2. B. To provide an objective evaluation of significant judgments made by the engagement team
  3. C. To handle direct negotiations with the client regarding audit fees
  4. D. To prepare the final audit report independently of the audit firm
Answer: B. To provide an objective evaluation of significant judgments made by the engagement team

Q22. Under ISA 250, what is the auditor's primary responsibility regarding non-compliance with laws and regulations (NOCLAR)?

  1. A. To prevent all instances of illegal acts by the client
  2. B. To report every minor infraction directly to tax authorities
  3. C. To consider the legal and regulatory framework and its compliance
  4. D. To act as legal counsel for the audited entity
Answer: C. To consider the legal and regulatory framework and its compliance

Q23. In the context of group audits, what must the group engagement team do regarding component auditors?

  1. A. Accept all component work without review
  2. B. Determine the type of work to be performed on financial information of components
  3. C. Retake all inventory counts conducted by component auditors
  4. D. Set the individual salaries for component audit staff
Answer: B. Determine the type of work to be performed on financial information of components

Q24. When assessing the risk of material misstatement due to fraud, what specific presumption must an auditor make regarding revenue recognition?

  1. A. Revenue is always correctly stated without exception
  2. B. There is a risk of fraud in revenue recognition
  3. C. Revenue transactions require no substantive testing
  4. D. Management override is impossible in sales cycles
Answer: B. There is a risk of fraud in revenue recognition

Q25. When evaluating the appropriateness of using the going concern basis of accounting, which factor should the auditor primarily consider?

  1. A. The historical profitability over the past ten years
  2. B. Management's plans for future actions and their feasibility
  3. C. The total market capitalization of the entity
  4. D. The amount of dividends paid in the prior period
Answer: B. Management's plans for future actions and their feasibility
