Q1. The most common cause of death in construction is:

  1. A. Electricity
  2. B. Falls from height
  3. C. Noise
  4. D. Dust
Answer: B. Falls from height

Q2. Scaffolds should be erected and altered by:

  1. A. Anyone
  2. B. Trained, competent scaffolders
  3. C. Visitors
  4. D. The client
Answer: B. Trained, competent scaffolders

Q3. Before digging, underground services should be:

  1. A. Ignored
  2. B. Located and marked using plans and detectors
  3. C. Guessed
  4. D. Cut quickly
Answer: B. Located and marked using plans and detectors

Q4. A hard hat protects mainly against:

  1. A. Noise
  2. B. Falling objects
  3. C. Dust
  4. D. Cold
Answer: B. Falling objects

Q5. Excavations can kill workers by:

  1. A. Collapse of the sides burying them
  2. B. Sunlight
  3. C. Rain
  4. D. Wind
Answer: A. Collapse of the sides burying them

Q6. Ladders should be used only for:

  1. A. Long heavy work
  2. B. Short-duration, low-risk work
  3. C. Carrying loads
  4. D. Painting whole buildings
Answer: B. Short-duration, low-risk work

Q7. Site traffic should be managed by:

  1. A. Letting vehicles reverse freely
  2. B. Separating vehicles and pedestrians with safe routes
  3. C. Using no signs
  4. D. Working at night only
Answer: B. Separating vehicles and pedestrians with safe routes

Q8. CDM regulations mainly place duties on:

  1. A. Only workers
  2. B. Clients, designers and contractors to plan and manage safety
  3. C. The public
  4. D. Suppliers only
Answer: B. Clients, designers and contractors to plan and manage safety

Q9. A permit-to-work is needed for:

  1. A. Office work
  2. B. High-risk work such as confined spaces or hot work
  3. C. Lunch breaks
  4. D. Meetings
Answer: B. High-risk work such as confined spaces or hot work

Q10. Workers in trenches deeper than 1.2 m generally need:

  1. A. Nothing
  2. B. Support/battering of sides or a trench box
  3. C. A ladder only
  4. D. Extra water
Answer: B. Support/battering of sides or a trench box
