Q1. The most common cause of death in construction is:

  1. A. Electricity
  2. B. Falls from height
  3. C. Noise
  4. D. Dust
Answer: B. Falls from height

Q2. Scaffolds should be erected and altered by:

  1. A. Anyone
  2. B. Trained, competent scaffolders
  3. C. Visitors
  4. D. The client
Answer: B. Trained, competent scaffolders

Q3. Before digging, underground services should be:

  1. A. Ignored
  2. B. Located and marked using plans and detectors
  3. C. Guessed
  4. D. Cut quickly
Answer: B. Located and marked using plans and detectors

Q4. A hard hat protects mainly against:

  1. A. Noise
  2. B. Falling objects
  3. C. Dust
  4. D. Cold
Answer: B. Falling objects

Q5. Excavations can kill workers by:

  1. A. Collapse of the sides burying them
  2. B. Sunlight
  3. C. Rain
  4. D. Wind
Answer: A. Collapse of the sides burying them

Q6. Ladders should be used only for:

  1. A. Long heavy work
  2. B. Short-duration, low-risk work
  3. C. Carrying loads
  4. D. Painting whole buildings
Answer: B. Short-duration, low-risk work

Q7. Site traffic should be managed by:

  1. A. Letting vehicles reverse freely
  2. B. Separating vehicles and pedestrians with safe routes
  3. C. Using no signs
  4. D. Working at night only
Answer: B. Separating vehicles and pedestrians with safe routes

Q8. CDM regulations mainly place duties on:

  1. A. Only workers
  2. B. Clients, designers and contractors to plan and manage safety
  3. C. The public
  4. D. Suppliers only
Answer: B. Clients, designers and contractors to plan and manage safety

Q9. A permit-to-work is needed for:

  1. A. Office work
  2. B. High-risk work such as confined spaces or hot work
  3. C. Lunch breaks
  4. D. Meetings
Answer: B. High-risk work such as confined spaces or hot work

Q10. Workers in trenches deeper than 1.2 m generally need:

  1. A. Nothing
  2. B. Support/battering of sides or a trench box
  3. C. A ladder only
  4. D. Extra water
Answer: B. Support/battering of sides or a trench box

Q11. During scaffolding operations, what is a crucial safety check required before use?

  1. A. Ensuring the scaffold is painted a bright safety color
  2. B. Verifying that it has been inspected by a competent person at required intervals
  3. C. Checking that it is secured directly to overhead power lines
  4. D. Confirming that timber boards are soaked in water to prevent splitting
Answer: B. Verifying that it has been inspected by a competent person at required intervals

Q12. What is the primary purpose of conducting a workplace risk assessment in construction?

  1. A. To completely eliminate all financial costs associated with projects
  2. B. To identify hazards and implement adequate control measures to prevent harm
  3. C. To satisfy insurance companies without altering site work methods
  4. D. To replace the need for physical safety supervision on site
Answer: B. To identify hazards and implement adequate control measures to prevent harm

Q13. What is the primary danger associated with working in a confined space such as a deep chamber or tank?

  1. A. Excessive exposure to loud background music
  2. B. Atmospheric hazards including oxygen deficiency or toxic gases
  3. C. Interference from public pedestrian traffic
  4. D. Sudden freezing temperatures due to high altitude
Answer: B. Atmospheric hazards including oxygen deficiency or toxic gases

Q14. Which of the following hierarchies is standard for controlling workplace health and safety risks?

  1. A. Elimination, Substitution, Engineering controls, Administrative controls, PPE
  2. B. PPE, Administrative controls, Engineering controls, Substitution, Elimination
  3. C. Substitution, PPE, Elimination, Engineering controls, Administrative controls
  4. D. Administrative controls, Elimination, PPE, Substitution, Engineering controls
Answer: A. Elimination, Substitution, Engineering controls, Administrative controls, PPE

Q15. Which measure is most effective for preventing injuries from moving site vehicles?

  1. A. Implementing strict segregation of pedestrians and vehicles
  2. B. Requiring all drivers to sound their horns continuously
  3. C. Removing all mirrors from heavy machinery to improve focus
  4. D. Allowing workers to walk freely across active haul routes
Answer: A. Implementing strict segregation of pedestrians and vehicles

Q16. When managing hazardous substances on a construction site, what document provides vital chemical safety details?

  1. A. Material Safety Data Sheet or Safety Data Sheet
  2. B. Daily lunch menu and catering schedule
  3. C. Company marketing brochure and client portfolio
  4. D. General architectural blueprint drawings
Answer: A. Material Safety Data Sheet or Safety Data Sheet

Q17. What is the primary function of a toe board installed on a working scaffold platform?

  1. A. To support heavy construction materials
  2. B. To prevent tools and materials from falling off the edge
  3. C. To provide extra structural rigidity to the uprights
  4. D. To act as a step for workers accessing higher levels
Answer: B. To prevent tools and materials from falling off the edge

Q18. Which safety device automatically cuts off electrical power when a current leakage to earth is detected on a construction site?

  1. A. Circuit breaker
  2. B. Step-down transformer
  3. C. Residual current device (RCD)
  4. D. Fuse box
Answer: C. Residual current device (RCD)

Q19. What is the recommended safe working angle for placing a leaning portable ladder against a wall?

  1. A. 45 degrees
  2. B. 75 degrees
  3. C. 60 degrees
  4. D. 90 degrees
Answer: B. 75 degrees

Q20. What is the primary purpose of shoring in deep excavation works?

  1. A. To keep groundwater away from the work zone
  2. B. To provide a permanent foundation support
  3. C. To prevent collapse and cave-ins of trench walls
  4. D. To make excavation faster using heavy machinery
Answer: C. To prevent collapse and cave-ins of trench walls

Q21. Which category of PPE is specifically required to protect workers from falling objects on a construction site?

  1. A. High-visibility vest
  2. B. Safety helmet
  3. C. Steel-toe boots
  4. D. Ear defenders
Answer: B. Safety helmet

Q22. What is the main objective of conducting a 'Toolbox Talk' on a construction site?

  1. A. To inspect and repair heavy machinery faults
  2. B. To discuss daily operational progress with management
  3. C. To brief workers on specific safety topics and hazards
  4. D. To evaluate contractor financial performance
Answer: C. To brief workers on specific safety topics and hazards

Q23. Which of the following is considered the most effective control measure in the hierarchy of risk control for construction hazards?

  1. A. Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)
  2. B. Administrative controls
  3. C. Elimination
  4. D. Engineering controls
Answer: C. Elimination

Q24. Which of the following activities specifically requires a formal Permit-to-Work (PTW) system on a construction site?

  1. A. Manual sweeping of walkways
  2. B. Hot work such as welding and flame cutting
  3. C. Delivering bricks with a forklift
  4. D. Installing indoor ceiling tiles
Answer: B. Hot work such as welding and flame cutting

Q25. What is the main function of shoring systems used in deep construction excavations?

  1. A. To keep the trench dry from rain
  2. B. To prevent collapse and soil cave-ins
  3. C. To make it easier to remove excavated soil
  4. D. To provide lighting inside the trench
Answer: B. To prevent collapse and soil cave-ins
