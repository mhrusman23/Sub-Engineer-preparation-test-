Q1. Factor of safety is the ratio of:

  1. A. Ultimate strength to working stress
  2. B. Working stress to ultimate strength
  3. C. Load to area
  4. D. Length to width
Answer: A. Ultimate strength to working stress

Q2. A key is used to:

  1. A. Hold oil
  2. B. Transmit torque between shaft and hub
  3. C. Cool bearings
  4. D. Reduce friction
Answer: B. Transmit torque between shaft and hub

Q3. Which of these is a rolling-contact bearing?

  1. A. Bush
  2. B. Journal bearing
  3. C. Thrust pad
  4. D. Ball bearing
Answer: D. Ball bearing

Q4. Fatigue failure occurs under:

  1. A. Static loads only
  2. B. Thermal load only
  3. C. No load
  4. D. Repeated or fluctuating loads
Answer: D. Repeated or fluctuating loads

Q5. Stress concentration occurs at:

  1. A. Plain flat areas
  2. B. Sudden change in section
  3. C. Centre of solid shaft
  4. D. Neutral axis only
Answer: B. Sudden change in section

Q6. The module of a gear is the ratio of pitch diameter to:

  1. A. Pressure angle
  2. B. Face width
  3. C. Addendum
  4. D. Number of teeth
Answer: D. Number of teeth

Q7. A flywheel is used to:

  1. A. Store kinetic energy and smooth speed fluctuations
  2. B. Cool the engine
  3. C. Reduce vibration only
  4. D. Increase torque ratio
Answer: A. Store kinetic energy and smooth speed fluctuations

Q8. The endurance limit is the stress below which a material can withstand:

  1. A. No stress
  2. B. One cycle only
  3. C. A single heavy shock
  4. D. Infinite load cycles
Answer: D. Infinite load cycles

Q9. A clutch is used to:

  1. A. Change fluid pressure
  2. B. Reduce shaft diameter
  3. C. Store energy
  4. D. Connect and disconnect two shafts
Answer: D. Connect and disconnect two shafts

Q10. Rivets are commonly used for:

  1. A. Temporary joints
  2. B. Cooling
  3. C. Lubrication
  4. D. Permanent joints
Answer: D. Permanent joints

Q11. In a bearing, the radial clearance is increased to improve which of the following?

  1. A. Load carrying capacity
  2. B. Speed capability
  3. C. Thermal expansion tolerance
  4. D. Lubrication film thickness
Answer: C. Thermal expansion tolerance

Q12. Which of the following is a primary cause of stress concentration in a shaft with a sudden diameter change?

  1. A. Uniform material hardness
  2. B. Smooth fillet radius
  3. C. Sharp corner
  4. D. Low coefficient of friction
Answer: C. Sharp corner

Q13. Which failure mode is most likely when a component is subjected to a single, very high impact load?

  1. A. Fatigue
  2. B. Creep
  3. C. Fracture
  4. D. Wear
Answer: C. Fracture

Q14. In a gear rack, the spacing between adjacent teeth is called:

  1. A. Pitch
  2. B. Tooth width
  3. C. Base circle
  4. D. Module
Answer: A. Pitch

Q15. The endurance limit of a metal is defined as the stress level below which the material can endure:

  1. A. A single overload cycle
  2. B. A finite number of cycles
  3. C. An infinite number of cycles
  4. D. No loading at all
Answer: C. An infinite number of cycles

Q16. Which of the following is a primary design consideration for achieving a low weight in a machine component?

  1. A. Using a high‑strength alloy
  2. B. Increasing the cross‑sectional area
  3. C. Choosing a material with high density
  4. D. Adding a thick protective coating
Answer: A. Using a high‑strength alloy

Q17. Which material property is most critical when selecting a bearing material for high‑temperature applications?

  1. A. Electrical conductivity
  2. B. Creep resistance
  3. C. Low melting point
  4. D. High ductility
Answer: B. Creep resistance

Q18. What is the main reason for using a shoulder key in a shaft‑to‑hub assembly?

  1. A. To reduce axial play between the shaft and hub
  2. B. To provide a sealing surface for lubricants
  3. C. To transmit torque while allowing axial movement
  4. D. To increase the bearing life
Answer: A. To reduce axial play between the shaft and hub
