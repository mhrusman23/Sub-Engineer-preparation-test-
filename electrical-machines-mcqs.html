Q1. Which type of loss in a transformer does not vary with load?

  1. A. Copper loss
  2. B. Core loss
  3. C. Stray loss
  4. D. Windage loss
Answer: B. Core loss

Q2. The device used to step-up or step-down AC voltage is called a:

  1. A. Generator
  2. B. Motor
  3. C. Transformer
  4. D. Rectifier
Answer: C. Transformer

Q3. Which device is used for step-up or step-down of alternating voltage?

  1. A. Generator
  2. B. Transformer
  3. C. Rectifier
  4. D. Inverter
Answer: B. Transformer

Q4. What is the primary function of a transformer in an AC circuit?

  1. A. Convert AC to DC
  2. B. Store electrical energy
  3. C. Change voltage levels
  4. D. Measure frequency
Answer: C. Change voltage levels

Q5. Which material is commonly used as a filament in incandescent electric lamps?

  1. A. Copper
  2. B. Aluminum
  3. C. Tungsten
  4. D. Carbon
Answer: C. Tungsten

Q6. Which type of motor is best suited for traction purposes due to high starting torque?

  1. A. DC series motor
  2. B. Synchronous motor
  3. C. Three-phase induction motor
  4. D. DC shunt motor
Answer: A. DC series motor

Q7. Which of the following is a secondary cell?

  1. A. Daniell cell
  2. B. Lead-acid cell
  3. C. Leclanche cell
  4. D. Voltaic cell
Answer: B. Lead-acid cell

Q8. Which type of motor is most suitable for electric traction?

  1. A. Synchronous motor
  2. B. Three-phase induction motor
  3. C. DC series motor
  4. D. Shunt motor
Answer: C. DC series motor

Q9. Transformer works on the principle of:

  1. A. Self induction
  2. B. Mutual induction
  3. C. Faraday's law of electrostatics
  4. D. Joule's law
Answer: B. Mutual induction

Q10. Which of the following generators has its field winding connected in parallel with the armature?

  1. A. Series generator
  2. B. Shunt generator
  3. C. Compound generator
  4. D. Permanent magnet generator
Answer: B. Shunt generator

Q11. Which material is commonly used as a filament in an incandescent light bulb?

  1. A. Copper
  2. B. Aluminium
  3. C. Tungsten
  4. D. Iron
Answer: C. Tungsten

Q12. A transformer works on the principle of:

  1. A. Capacitance
  2. B. Mutual induction
  3. C. Electrostatic induction
  4. D. Self induction only
Answer: B. Mutual induction

Q13. A transformer transfers power between circuits without a change in:

  1. A. Turns ratio
  2. B. Current
  3. C. Frequency
  4. D. Voltage
Answer: C. Frequency

Q14. The synchronous speed of an AC machine depends on frequency and number of:

  1. A. Brushes
  2. B. Phases only
  3. C. Windings only
  4. D. Poles
Answer: D. Poles

Q15. The commutator in a DC machine converts:

  1. A. Speed to torque
  2. B. DC to AC
  3. C. AC in armature to DC at terminals
  4. D. Voltage to current
Answer: C. AC in armature to DC at terminals

Q16. The rotor of a squirrel cage induction motor has:

  1. A. Slip rings
  2. B. Permanent magnets only
  3. C. A commutator
  4. D. Short-circuited bars
Answer: D. Short-circuited bars

Q17. Slip of an induction motor is the difference between synchronous speed and:

  1. A. Rotor speed
  2. B. Stator voltage
  3. C. Supply current
  4. D. Line frequency
Answer: A. Rotor speed

Q18. A step-up transformer has more turns in the:

  1. A. Tank
  2. B. Core only
  3. C. Secondary winding
  4. D. Primary winding
Answer: C. Secondary winding

Q19. The back EMF in a DC motor:

  1. A. Opposes the applied voltage
  2. B. Aids the applied voltage
  3. C. Is constant always
  4. D. Is zero at all speeds
Answer: A. Opposes the applied voltage

Q20. A generator converts:

  1. A. Heat to light
  2. B. Mechanical energy to electrical energy
  3. C. Light to heat
  4. D. Electrical energy to mechanical energy
Answer: B. Mechanical energy to electrical energy

Q21. Transformer core is laminated to reduce:

  1. A. Winding resistance
  2. B. Copper loss
  3. C. Eddy current loss
  4. D. Flux
Answer: C. Eddy current loss
