Q1. The first law of thermodynamics is a statement of:

  1. A. Conservation of energy
  2. B. Conservation of mass
  3. C. Conservation of momentum
  4. D. Entropy increase
Answer: A. Conservation of energy

Q2. An isothermal process takes place at constant:

  1. A. Entropy
  2. B. Temperature
  3. C. Volume
  4. D. Pressure
Answer: B. Temperature

Q3. In an adiabatic process there is no:

  1. A. Temperature change
  2. B. Heat transfer
  3. C. Work
  4. D. Pressure change
Answer: B. Heat transfer

Q4. The SI unit of entropy is:

  1. A. W
  2. B. K
  3. C. J
  4. D. J/K
Answer: D. J/K

Q5. The efficiency of a Carnot engine depends on the:

  1. A. Source and sink temperatures
  2. B. Working fluid
  3. C. Mass only
  4. D. Pressure only
Answer: A. Source and sink temperatures

Q6. The second law of thermodynamics introduces the concept of:

  1. A. Internal energy only
  2. B. Enthalpy only
  3. C. Entropy
  4. D. Mass flow
Answer: C. Entropy

Q7. An isochoric process occurs at constant:

  1. A. Pressure
  2. B. Volume
  3. C. Entropy
  4. D. Temperature
Answer: B. Volume

Q8. Absolute zero temperature in Celsius is about:

  1. A. -273.15
  2. B. -373
  3. C. -100
  4. D. 0
Answer: A. -273.15

Q9. The unit of specific heat capacity in SI is:

  1. A. N/m
  2. B. W/m.K
  3. C. J/kg
  4. D. J/kg.K
Answer: D. J/kg.K

Q10. The triple point of water is the state where:

  1. A. Only liquid exists
  2. B. Only vapour exists
  3. C. Gas condenses only
  4. D. Solid, liquid and vapour coexist
Answer: D. Solid, liquid and vapour coexist

Q11. Which thermodynamic property remains strictly constant during a throttling process?

  1. A. Entropy
  2. B. Enthalpy
  3. C. Temperature
  4. D. Pressure
Answer: B. Enthalpy

Q12. What is the net work done by a system during a complete thermodynamic cycle?

  1. A. Equal to total heat added
  2. B. Zero
  3. C. Equal to net heat transfer
  4. D. Equal to change in internal energy
Answer: C. Equal to net heat transfer

Q13. For an ideal gas, the change in internal energy depends exclusively on which parameter?

  1. A. Pressure
  2. B. Volume
  3. C. Temperature
  4. D. Entropy
Answer: C. Temperature

Q14. Which cycle serves as the theoretical standard of comparison for all heat engines?

  1. A. Rankine cycle
  2. B. Otto cycle
  3. C. Carnot cycle
  4. D. Diesel cycle
Answer: C. Carnot cycle

Q15. What does the area enclosed by a T-S diagram represent in a thermodynamic cycle?

  1. A. Net work done
  2. B. Total heat added
  3. C. Net heat transferred
  4. D. Change in entropy
Answer: C. Net heat transferred

Q16. Which device is used to measure the dryness fraction of wet steam?

  1. A. Throttling calorimeter
  2. B. Barometer
  3. C. Manometer
  4. D. Venturi meter
Answer: A. Throttling calorimeter

Q17. The ratio of the work done by a Carnot engine to the heat absorbed from the hot reservoir is called what?

  1. A. Coefficient of performance
  2. B. Thermal efficiency
  3. C. Heat rejection ratio
  4. D. Specific work
Answer: B. Thermal efficiency

Q18. Which statement correctly describes the Joule‑Thomson effect for a real gas at low pressure?

  1. A. Temperature rises when the gas is throttled
  2. B. Temperature remains constant during throttling
  3. C. Temperature drops when the gas is throttled
  4. D. Pressure increases during throttling
Answer: C. Temperature drops when the gas is throttled

Q19. The Gibbs free energy change of a spontaneous process at constant temperature and pressure is:

  1. A. Positive
  2. B. Negative
  3. C. Zero
  4. D. Unrelated to spontaneity
Answer: B. Negative

Q20. During a constant‑volume heating of an ideal gas, which property of the gas changes?

  1. A. Pressure remains unchanged
  2. B. Temperature increases
  3. C. Volume decreases
  4. D. Entropy decreases
Answer: B. Temperature increases

Q21. What is the specific value of absolute zero temperature measured on the Celsius scale?

  1. A. 0 °C
  2. B. -273.15 °C
  3. C. -100 °C
  4. D. -460 °C
Answer: B. -273.15 °C

Q22. Which of the following properties is an intensive property of a thermodynamic system?

  1. A. Internal energy
  2. B. Enthalpy
  3. C. Temperature
  4. D. Volume
Answer: C. Temperature

Q23. An isobaric thermodynamic process is characterized by which of the following constant parameters?

  1. A. Volume
  2. B. Pressure
  3. C. Temperature
  4. D. Entropy
Answer: B. Pressure

Q24. During a reversible adiabatic process in a closed system, which thermodynamic property remains constant?

  1. A. Enthalpy
  2. B. Temperature
  3. C. Entropy
  4. D. Pressure
Answer: C. Entropy

Q25. Which thermodynamic law states that it is impossible for any device to operate in a thermodynamic cycle and produce no effect other than the transfer of heat from a cooler body to a hotter body?

  1. A. Zeroth law
  2. B. First law
  3. C. Second law
  4. D. Third law
Answer: C. Second law
