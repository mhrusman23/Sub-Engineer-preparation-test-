Q1. The United Nations headquarters is located in:

  1. A. Geneva
  2. B. New York
  3. C. Paris
  4. D. London
Answer: B. New York

Q2. CPEC is a major project between Pakistan and:

  1. A. India
  2. B. China
  3. C. USA
  4. D. Iran
Answer: B. China

Q3. The UN Secretary-General is the head of:

  1. A. The Security Council
  2. B. The UN Secretariat
  3. C. The World Bank
  4. D. NATO
Answer: B. The UN Secretariat

Q4. BRICS is a grouping that originally included Brazil, Russia, India, China and:

  1. A. Spain
  2. B. South Africa
  3. C. Sweden
  4. D. Sri Lanka
Answer: B. South Africa

Q5. The Paris Agreement deals with:

  1. A. Trade tariffs
  2. B. Climate change
  3. C. Nuclear weapons
  4. D. Migration only
Answer: B. Climate change

Q6. Pakistan is a member of which regional organisation?

  1. A. EU
  2. B. SAARC
  3. C. ASEAN (as full member)
  4. D. AU
Answer: B. SAARC

Q7. The World Trade Organization (WTO) headquarters is in:

  1. A. New York
  2. B. Geneva
  3. C. Vienna
  4. D. Brussels
Answer: B. Geneva

Q8. Climate change summits of the UN are called:

  1. A. G20
  2. B. COP (Conference of the Parties)
  3. C. BRICS Summit
  4. D. OPEC Meeting
Answer: B. COP (Conference of the Parties)

Q9. The IMF mainly provides:

  1. A. Military aid
  2. B. Financial assistance and loans to countries with balance-of-payments problems
  3. C. Food aid only
  4. D. Scholarships
Answer: B. Financial assistance and loans to countries with balance-of-payments problems

Q10. Artificial Intelligence regulation is debated globally mainly because of concerns about:

  1. A. Only cost
  2. B. Jobs, privacy, bias and safety
  3. C. Electricity use only
  4. D. Language
Answer: B. Jobs, privacy, bias and safety
