Q1. Pakistan came into being on:

  1. A. 6 September 1965
  2. B. 23 March 1940
  3. C. 23 March 1956
  4. D. 14 August 1947
Answer: D. 14 August 1947

Q2. The Pakistan Resolution was passed in Lahore in:

  1. A. 1940
  2. B. 1947
  3. C. 1935
  4. D. 1930
Answer: A. 1940

Q3. The first Prime Minister of Pakistan was:

  1. A. Iskander Mirza
  2. B. Muhammad Ali Jinnah
  3. C. Khawaja Nazimuddin
  4. D. Liaquat Ali Khan
Answer: D. Liaquat Ali Khan

Q4. The first Governor-General of Pakistan was:

  1. A. Iskander Mirza
  2. B. Ghulam Muhammad
  3. C. Quaid-e-Azam Muhammad Ali Jinnah
  4. D. Liaquat Ali Khan
Answer: C. Quaid-e-Azam Muhammad Ali Jinnah

Q5. The Allahabad Address of 1930 was delivered by:

  1. A. Quaid-e-Azam
  2. B. Sir Syed Ahmad Khan
  3. C. Liaquat Ali Khan
  4. D. Allama Muhammad Iqbal
Answer: D. Allama Muhammad Iqbal

Q6. The present Constitution of Pakistan was adopted in:

  1. A. 1962
  2. B. 1947
  3. C. 1973
  4. D. 1956
Answer: C. 1973

Q7. The first Constitution of Pakistan was adopted in:

  1. A. 1962
  2. B. 1973
  3. C. 1956
  4. D. 1947
Answer: C. 1956

Q8. The Khyber Pass connects Pakistan with:

  1. A. India
  2. B. Iran
  3. C. China
  4. D. Afghanistan
Answer: D. Afghanistan

Q9. The Indus Valley Civilisation site Mohenjo-daro is in:

  1. A. Balochistan
  2. B. Sindh
  3. C. Khyber Pakhtunkhwa
  4. D. Punjab
Answer: B. Sindh

Q10. The number of provinces in Pakistan is:

  1. A. 6
  2. B. 5
  3. C. 3
  4. D. 4
Answer: D. 4

Q11. Which of these passes is located entirely within Pakistan and does not connect to any neighboring country?

  1. A. Khyber Pass
  2. B. Bolan Pass
  3. C. Tochi Pass
  4. D. Naltar Pass
Answer: D. Naltar Pass

Q12. The first constitution of Pakistan was drafted in which year?

  1. A. 1947
  2. B. 1956
  3. C. 1962
  4. D. 1973
Answer: B. 1956

Q13. The Khunjerab Pass, the highest paved international border crossing, connects Pakistan with which neighboring country?

  1. A. India
  2. B. Afghanistan
  3. C. Iran
  4. D. China
Answer: D. China

Q14. The historic city of Lahore was the capital of which ancient empire?

  1. A. Mughal Empire
  2. B. Sassanian Empire
  3. C. Gandhara Kingdom
  4. D. Maurya Empire
Answer: A. Mughal Empire

Q15. Which of the following rivers is NOT a major tributary of the Indus River?

  1. A. Chenab
  2. B. Sutlej
  3. C. Jhelum
  4. D. Ganges
Answer: D. Ganges
