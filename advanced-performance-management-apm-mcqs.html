Q1. Benchmarking involves comparing performance with:

  1. A. Budget only
  2. B. Best practice
  3. C. Last year only
  4. D. Competitors' secrets only
Answer: B. Best practice

Q2. In activity-based costing, a cost driver is:

  1. A. The cost centre
  2. B. The factor that causes an activity's cost
  3. C. A fixed cost
  4. D. Total overhead
Answer: B. The factor that causes an activity's cost

Q3. The four categories of cost of quality are prevention, appraisal, internal failure and:

  1. A. Marketing
  2. B. Design
  3. C. Training
  4. D. External failure
Answer: D. External failure

Q4. Fitzgerald and Moon's building block model includes dimensions, standards and:

  1. A. Rewards
  2. B. Taxes
  3. C. Budgets
  4. D. Assets
Answer: A. Rewards

Q5. Life cycle costing considers costs over a product's:

  1. A. Production phase only
  2. B. Selling phase only
  3. C. Design phase only
  4. D. Entire life
Answer: D. Entire life

Q6. Controllable profit is a measure of divisional performance that excludes:

  1. A. Sales
  2. B. Variable costs
  3. C. Direct costs
  4. D. Costs the manager cannot control
Answer: D. Costs the manager cannot control

Q7. The minimum transfer price equals marginal cost plus:

  1. A. Tax
  2. B. Selling price
  3. C. Opportunity cost
  4. D. Fixed cost
Answer: C. Opportunity cost

Q8. Beyond budgeting aims to replace traditional budgets with:

  1. A. More detailed budgets
  2. B. Annual fixed budgets
  3. C. Adaptive and flexible targets
  4. D. No performance measures
Answer: C. Adaptive and flexible targets

Q9. Mission statements normally describe an organisation's:

  1. A. Monthly sales
  2. B. Tax position
  3. C. Cash flows
  4. D. Purpose and values
Answer: D. Purpose and values

Q10. Which is a non-financial performance measure?

  1. A. Net profit
  2. B. ROI
  3. C. Customer satisfaction
  4. D. Residual income
Answer: C. Customer satisfaction

Q11. In the context of the Balanced Scorecard, which perspective focuses on continuous improvement, innovation, and employee skills?

  1. A. Financial perspective
  2. B. Customer perspective
  3. C. Internal business process perspective
  4. D. Learning and growth perspective
Answer: D. Learning and growth perspective

Q12. When applying value chain analysis, which activity is classified as a primary activity rather than a support activity?

  1. A. Procurement of raw materials
  2. B. Human resource management
  3. C. Inbound logistics and warehousing
  4. D. Firm infrastructure development
Answer: C. Inbound logistics and warehousing

Q13. What is the primary objective of introducing environmental management accounting (EMA) in an organization?

  1. A. To comply strictly with statutory tax audits
  2. B. To track physical energy flows and monetized environmental costs
  3. C. To improve public relations through green advertising
  4. D. To eliminate all manufacturing waste overnight
Answer: B. To track physical energy flows and monetized environmental costs

Q14. In value-based management, which metric measures the excess return generated above the total cost of capital invested in the business?

  1. A. Earnings before interest and taxes
  2. B. Economic value added
  3. C. Return on capital employed
  4. D. Net operating profit after tax
Answer: B. Economic value added

Q15. What is a major behavioral risk associated with linking managerial bonuses strictly to short-term variance analysis?

  1. A. Increased long-term strategic investment
  2. B. Encouraging dysfunctional budget gaming and slack creation
  3. C. Elimination of all operational inefficiencies
  4. D. Enhanced cross-departmental cooperation
Answer: B. Encouraging dysfunctional budget gaming and slack creation

Q16. When applying Porter’s Five Forces to assess a firm’s strategic performance, which of the following would be an appropriate new performance measure for the ‘threat of new entrants’?

  1. A. Average time to market for new products
  2. B. Number of patents filed per year
  3. C. Market share growth rate
  4. D. Employee training hours per employee
Answer: A. Average time to market for new products

Q17. Which behavioural risk is most likely to arise when performance targets are linked solely to financial outcomes?

  1. A. Increased employee motivation
  2. B. Ethical misconduct such as data manipulation
  3. C. Improved cross‑department collaboration
  4. D. Higher customer satisfaction scores
Answer: B. Ethical misconduct such as data manipulation

Q18. In a scenario analysis for a new product launch, which of the following best represents the ‘expected value’ calculation?

  1. A. Sum of all possible costs divided by the number of scenarios
  2. B. Highest possible profit scenario multiplied by its probability
  3. C. Weighted average of all profit outcomes based on their probabilities
  4. D. Lowest cost scenario divided by the number of scenarios
Answer: C. Weighted average of all profit outcomes based on their probabilities

Q19. A manager notices that the operating margin of Division A is consistently lower than the company target. Which action is most appropriate according to performance management principles?

  1. A. Increase the selling price of all products in Division A
  2. B. Conduct a root‑cause analysis to identify cost drivers and inefficiencies
  3. C. Transfer all high‑margin products to Division B
  4. D. Eliminate all non‑core activities regardless of impact
Answer: B. Conduct a root‑cause analysis to identify cost drivers and inefficiencies

Q20. Which KPI would most directly measure the effectiveness of a company’s customer‑service process?

  1. A. Return on Investment (ROI)
  2. B. Customer Satisfaction Score (CSAT)
  3. C. Gross Profit Margin
  4. D. Employee Turnover Rate
Answer: B. Customer Satisfaction Score (CSAT)

Q21. Which of the following best describes the purpose of a variance analysis in a performance management system?

  1. A. To identify the most profitable product line
  2. B. To compare actual results with budgeted figures and explain deviations
  3. C. To forecast future sales volumes
  4. D. To calculate the company’s tax liability
Answer: B. To compare actual results with budgeted figures and explain deviations
