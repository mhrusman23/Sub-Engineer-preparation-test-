Q1. What is the primary purpose of financial accounting?

  1. A. To manage daily office tasks
  2. B. To provide useful financial information to external users
  3. C. To compute worker wages
  4. D. To audit government taxes
Answer: B. To provide useful financial information to external users

Q2. Which accounting principle states that revenue should be recorded when earned, regardless of cash receipt?

  1. A. Matching principle
  2. B. Cost principle
  3. C. Accrual concept
  4. D. Going concern concept
Answer: C. Accrual concept

Q3. What is the fundamental accounting equation?

  1. A. Assets = Liabilities + Equity
  2. B. Assets = Revenue - Expenses
  3. C. Equity = Assets + Liabilities
  4. D. Income = Assets - Liabilities
Answer: A. Assets = Liabilities + Equity

Q4. Which financial statement shows a company's financial position at a specific point in time?

  1. A. Income Statement
  2. B. Statement of Cash Flows
  3. C. Statement of Financial Position (Balance Sheet)
  4. D. Statement of Changes in Equity
Answer: C. Statement of Financial Position (Balance Sheet)

Q5. Under the matching principle, expenses are recognized in the same period as:

  1. A. Cash is paid
  2. B. The related revenues are earned
  3. C. The owner decides
  4. D. The tax is filed
Answer: B. The related revenues are earned

Q6. Which of the following is classified as a non-current asset?

  1. A. Accounts receivable
  2. B. Inventory
  3. C. Machinery and equipment
  4. D. Cash at bank
Answer: C. Machinery and equipment

Q7. What does the going concern assumption imply?

  1. A. The business will close down soon
  2. B. The business will continue its operations in the foreseeable future
  3. C. All assets must be sold immediately
  4. D. No profit will be made
Answer: B. The business will continue its operations in the foreseeable future

Q8. What is the normal balance of an expense account?

  1. A. Debit
  2. B. Credit
  3. C. Zero
  4. D. Both debit and credit
Answer: A. Debit

Q9. What is the primary focus of management accounting?

  1. A. External reporting
  2. B. Internal decision making
  3. C. Tax compliance
  4. D. Auditing financial statements
Answer: B. Internal decision making

Q10. Which of the following costs changes in direct proportion to the volume of activity?

  1. A. Fixed cost
  2. B. Variable cost
  3. C. Sunk cost
  4. D. Step cost
Answer: B. Variable cost

Q11. What is calculated by subtracting variable costs from total sales revenue?

  1. A. Gross profit
  2. B. Net income
  3. C. Contribution margin
  4. D. Operating income
Answer: C. Contribution margin

Q12. At the break-even point, what is the total profit of the business?

  1. A. Equal to fixed costs
  2. B. Maximum
  3. C. Zero
  4. D. Equal to variable costs
Answer: C. Zero

Q13. Which budgeting method starts from a base of zero and requires every item to be justified?

  1. A. Incremental budgeting
  2. B. Zero-based budgeting
  3. C. Flexible budgeting
  4. D. Static budgeting
Answer: B. Zero-based budgeting

Q14. What type of variance occurs when actual costs are lower than standard costs?

  1. A. Adverse variance
  2. B. Favourable variance
  3. C. Operating variance
  4. D. Expenditure variance
Answer: B. Favourable variance

Q15. Which cost remains constant in total regardless of the volume of production within a relevant range?

  1. A. Variable cost
  2. B. Direct cost
  3. C. Fixed cost
  4. D. Semi-variable cost
Answer: C. Fixed cost

Q16. What is a cost that has already been incurred and cannot be recovered called?

  1. A. Opportunity cost
  2. B. Relevant cost
  3. C. Sunk cost
  4. D. Standard cost
Answer: C. Sunk cost

Q17. What is the term for a tax that takes a larger percentage of income from high-income earners than from low-income earners?

  1. A. Regressive tax
  2. B. Proportional tax
  3. C. Progressive tax
  4. D. Flat tax
Answer: C. Progressive tax

Q18. Which of the following describes a tax levied directly on a person's total income and profits?

  1. A. Indirect tax
  2. B. Direct tax
  3. C. Value Added Tax
  4. D. Customs duty
Answer: B. Direct tax

Q19. What is the primary characteristic of an indirect tax?

  1. A. It is based strictly on net wealth
  2. B. The burden can be shifted to another person
  3. C. It cannot be passed on to consumers
  4. D. It is levied directly on corporate profits
Answer: B. The burden can be shifted to another person

Q20. What is tax avoidance?

  1. A. Illegal concealment of taxable income
  2. B. Failure to file a tax return on time
  3. C. Using legal methods to minimize the amount of income tax owed
  4. D. Refusing to pay taxes based on personal beliefs
Answer: C. Using legal methods to minimize the amount of income tax owed

Q21. Which type of tax is charged at each stage of the supply chain where value is added to a product or service?

  1. A. Income tax
  2. B. Capital gains tax
  3. C. Value Added Tax (VAT)
  4. D. Inheritance tax
Answer: C. Value Added Tax (VAT)

Q22. What is the tax called that is levied on the profits made from the sale of a capital asset such as property or shares?

  1. A. Excise duty
  2. B. Capital gains tax
  3. C. Corporation tax
  4. D. Customs duty
Answer: B. Capital gains tax

Q23. Which term describes the intentional and illegal practice of misrepresenting the true state of affairs to tax authorities to reduce tax liability?

  1. A. Tax avoidance
  2. B. Tax planning
  3. C. Tax evasion
  4. D. Tax mitigation
Answer: C. Tax evasion

Q24. What is fiscal drag?

  1. A. A reduction in government spending during a recession
  2. B. The process where inflation pushes taxpayers into higher tax brackets
  3. C. The delay in collecting corporate taxes
  4. D. A subsidy given to struggling industries
Answer: B. The process where inflation pushes taxpayers into higher tax brackets

Q25. What is the primary objective of an external audit?

  1. A. To detect all instances of fraud
  2. B. To provide an opinion on financial statements
  3. C. To prepare the annual tax returns
  4. D. To manage daily business operations
Answer: B. To provide an opinion on financial statements
