Q1. In a water supply scheme, what is the maximum permissible limit of turbidity in drinking water in NTU?

  1. A. 1 NTU
  2. B. 5 NTU
  3. C. 10 NTU
  4. D. 25 NTU
Answer: B. 5 NTU

Q2. What is the acceptable pH range for drinking water recommended by standard environmental health guidelines?

  1. A. 4.5 to 6.0
  2. B. 6.5 to 8.5
  3. C. 9.0 to 11.0
  4. D. 2.0 to 4.0
Answer: B. 6.5 to 8.5

Q3. Temporary hardness in water is primarily caused by the presence of which of the following compounds?

  1. A. Sulfates of calcium and magnesium
  2. B. Chlorides of sodium and potassium
  3. C. Bicarbonates of calcium and magnesium
  4. D. Nitrates of iron and aluminum
Answer: C. Bicarbonates of calcium and magnesium

Q4. Which instrument is standardly used to measure the turbidity or cloudiness of water?

  1. A. Hydrometer
  2. B. Nephelometer
  3. C. Viscometer
  4. D. Pycnometer
Answer: B. Nephelometer

Q5. What does the 5-day Biochemical Oxygen Demand (BOD) test at 20°C primarily measure in a water sample?

  1. A. Total organic matter content
  2. B. Amount of dissolved oxygen consumed by bacteria to oxidize biodegradable wastes
  3. C. Total amount of suspended solids present
  4. D. Concentration of heavy metal ions
Answer: B. Amount of dissolved oxygen consumed by bacteria to oxidize biodegradable wastes

Q6. What is the primary purpose of adding alum to raw water during the coagulation process?

  1. A. To kill pathogenic bacteria
  2. B. To precipitate suspended colloidal impurities
  3. C. To remove hardness caused by calcium
  4. D. To adjust the pH to a neutral level
Answer: B. To precipitate suspended colloidal impurities

Q7. Which of the following chemical compounds is most commonly used for the disinfection of public water supplies?

  1. A. Sodium chloride
  2. B. Calcium hypochlorite
  3. C. Copper sulfate
  4. D. Aluminum sulfate
Answer: B. Calcium hypochlorite

Q8. Which of the following units is most commonly utilized to express the concentration of trace contaminants in water quality analysis?

  1. A. Milligrams per liter
  2. B. Grams per milliliter
  3. C. Kilograms per cubic meter
  4. D. Pounds per gallon
Answer: A. Milligrams per liter

Q9. What is the primary gas responsible for causing odor and corrosive problems in stagnant wastewater systems?

  1. A. Carbon dioxide
  2. B. Hydrogen sulfide
  3. C. Methane
  4. D. Ammonia
Answer: B. Hydrogen sulfide

Q10. Permanent hardness in natural water supplies is primarily attributed to the presence of which dissolved ions?

  1. A. Calcium and magnesium bicarbonates
  2. B. Calcium and magnesium sulfates and chlorides
  3. C. Sodium and potassium carbonates
  4. D. Iron and manganese oxides
Answer: B. Calcium and magnesium sulfates and chlorides

Q11. What physical water quality parameter is directly measured using a standard silica gel or platinum wire dispersion device?

  1. A. Turbidity
  2. B. Color
  3. C. Taste and Odor
  4. D. Temperature
Answer: B. Color

Q12. Which specific bacterium is universally adopted as the primary indicator organism for assessing microbiological contamination in drinking water?

  1. A. Salmonella typhi
  2. B. Vibrio cholerae
  3. C. Escherichia coli
  4. D. Pseudomonas aeruginosa
Answer: C. Escherichia coli

Q13. What is the standard incubation period and temperature maintained during the conventional Biochemical Oxygen Demand (BOD) test?

  1. A. 3 days at 25 °C
  2. B. 5 days at 20 °C
  3. C. 7 days at 37 °C
  4. D. 10 days at 15 °C
Answer: B. 5 days at 20 °C

Q14. What is the term used to describe the total concentration of calcium and magnesium ions expressed in terms of calcium carbonate equivalent?

  1. A. A) Acidity
  2. B. B) Alkalinity
  3. C. C) Hardness
  4. D. D) Salinity
Answer: C. C) Hardness

Q15. Which disinfection byproduct is most commonly formed when free chlorine reacts with natural organic matter present in raw surface water supplies?

  1. A. A) Chloramines
  2. B. B) Trihalomethanes
  3. C. C) Carbon dioxides
  4. D. D) Nitrosamines
Answer: B. B) Trihalomethanes

Q16. Which laboratory test is universally adopted to ascertain the concentration of biodegradable organic matter in domestic wastewater?

  1. A. Chemical Oxygen Demand
  2. B. Biochemical Oxygen Demand
  3. C. Total Dissolved Solids
  4. D. Electrical Conductivity
Answer: B. Biochemical Oxygen Demand

Q17. Which chemical compound is chiefly responsible for contributing to the carbonate hardness present in natural water sources?

  1. A. Calcium sulfate
  2. B. Magnesium chloride
  3. C. Calcium bicarbonate
  4. D. Sodium nitrate
Answer: C. Calcium bicarbonate

Q18. Which specific chemical species is chiefly responsible for imparting alkalinity to natural water supplies?

  1. A. A) Carbonates and bicarbonates
  2. B. B) Chlorides and sulfates
  3. C. C) Nitrates and phosphates
  4. D. D) Calcium and magnesium
Answer: A. A) Carbonates and bicarbonates

Q19. What is the primary equipment used in water treatment laboratories to measure color intensity by comparing samples with platinum-cobalt standards?

  1. A. A) Nephelometer
  2. B. B) Tintometer
  3. C. C) Hydrometer
  4. D. D) Spectrophotometer
Answer: B. B) Tintometer

Q20. During the laboratory analysis of wastewater, what is the standard temperature maintained inside the incubator for conducting a 5-day BOD test?

  1. A. A) 15 °C
  2. B. B) 20 °C
  3. C. C) 27 °C
  4. D. D) 37 °C
Answer: B. B) 20 °C

Q21. What is the primary operational purpose of installing a Flash Mixer ahead of the sedimentation tank in a water treatment facility?

  1. A. To rapidly disperse coagulant chemicals into raw water
  2. B. To filter out large floating debris
  3. C. To aerate the water and remove dissolved gases
  4. D. To store treated water before distribution
Answer: A. To rapidly disperse coagulant chemicals into raw water

Q22. What is the primary objective of performing the break-point chlorination process in a water treatment plant?

  1. A. To remove suspended impurities
  2. B. To destroy all disease-causing bacteria and oxidize organic compounds
  3. C. To adjust the pH level to neutral
  4. D. To reduce the total hardness of water
Answer: B. To destroy all disease-causing bacteria and oxidize organic compounds

Q23. What chemical compound is typically added to treated drinking water supplies to assist in the prevention of dental caries?

  2. B. B) Aluminum sulfate
  3. C. C) Copper sulfate
