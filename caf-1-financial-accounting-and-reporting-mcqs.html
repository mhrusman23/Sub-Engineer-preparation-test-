Q1. Which standard deals with inventories?

  1. A. IAS 2
  2. B. IAS 16
  3. C. IAS 1
  4. D. IAS 36
Answer: A. IAS 2

Q2. Straight-line depreciation charges an equal amount each year over the asset's:

  1. A. Warranty period
  2. B. Useful life
  3. C. Borrowing period
  4. D. Tax life
Answer: B. Useful life

Q3. A trial balance is prepared to check that total debits equal total:

  1. A. Credits
  2. B. Assets
  3. C. Liabilities
  4. D. Expenses
Answer: A. Credits

Q4. Which financial statement reports performance over a period?

  1. A. Statement of financial position
  2. B. Trial balance
  3. C. Statement of profit or loss
  4. D. Bank reconciliation
Answer: C. Statement of profit or loss

Q5. Revenue expenditure is expenditure that is:

  1. A. Paid to owners
  2. B. Recorded as equity
  3. C. Capitalised as an asset
  4. D. Charged to profit or loss in the period
Answer: D. Charged to profit or loss in the period

Q6. An accrued expense is a:

  1. A. Current liability
  2. B. Equity reserve
  3. C. Current asset
  4. D. Non-current asset
Answer: A. Current liability

Q7. Under IAS 8, a change in accounting estimate is applied:

  1. A. Prospectively
  2. B. Only to prior years
  3. C. Retrospectively with restatement
  4. D. Never
Answer: A. Prospectively

Q8. Bad debts written off are recorded as an:

  1. A. Income
  2. B. Liability
  3. C. Expense
  4. D. Asset
Answer: C. Expense

Q9. Capital expenditure includes the cost of:

  1. A. Paying wages
  2. B. Buying a new machine
  3. C. Buying stationery
  4. D. Paying monthly rent
Answer: B. Buying a new machine

Q10. The Conceptual Framework states that useful information has fundamental qualities of relevance and:

  1. A. Timeliness only
  2. B. Faithful representation
  3. C. Verifiability only
  4. D. Comparability only
Answer: B. Faithful representation

Q11. Which accounting principle requires financial statements to be prepared on the assumption that the entity will continue operations into the foreseeable future?

  1. A. Accrual basis
  2. B. Going concern
  3. C. Business entity
  4. D. Materiality
Answer: B. Going concern

Q12. According to IAS 16, what is the initial measurement basis for an item of property, plant, and equipment?

  1. A. Fair value
  2. B. Net realizable value
  3. C. Cost
  4. D. Recoverable amount
Answer: C. Cost

Q13. How should a contingent liability be treated in the financial statements if the probability of an outflow of economic resources is remote?

  1. A. Recognized as a liability
  2. B. Disclosed in the notes only
  3. C. No disclosure or recognition
  4. D. Recognized as an asset
Answer: C. No disclosure or recognition

Q14. Which of the following errors does a traditional trial balance fail to detect?

  1. A. Incorrect debit and credit amounts
  2. B. Omission of a complete transaction
  3. C. Posting an entry to the wrong side of an account
  4. D. Mathematical addition errors in ledger accounts
Answer: B. Omission of a complete transaction

Q15. Under the revaluation model for property, plant, and equipment in IAS 16, how is a revaluation increase generally recognized?

  1. A. In profit or loss as revenue
  2. B. In other comprehensive income and accumulated in equity
  3. C. As a direct reduction of liabilities
  4. D. As retained earnings directly
Answer: B. In other comprehensive income and accumulated in equity

Q16. What is the primary objective of general purpose financial reporting according to the IASB Conceptual Framework?

  1. A. To calculate exact company valuation
  2. B. To provide financial information useful to primary users for decision making
  3. C. To determine tax liabilities for the government
  4. D. To audit past management performance
Answer: B. To provide financial information useful to primary users for decision making
