Q1. Legal planning to minimise tax without breaking the law is:

  1. A. Tax default
  2. B. Tax evasion
  3. C. Tax fraud
  4. D. Tax avoidance
Answer: D. Tax avoidance

Q2. Transfer pricing rules rely on the:

  1. A. Lowest price principle
  2. B. Highest price principle
  3. C. Book value principle
  4. D. Arm's length principle
Answer: D. Arm's length principle

Q3. Double tax treaties help to prevent:

  1. A. Single taxation
  2. B. Any taxation
  3. C. Double taxation
  4. D. Withholding
Answer: C. Double taxation

Q4. Withholding tax on payments made to non-residents is commonly applied to:

  1. A. Rent of local homes only
  2. B. Dividends, royalties and interest
  3. C. Salaries of residents only
  4. D. Gifts only
Answer: B. Dividends, royalties and interest

Q5. Deferred tax arises from differences between accounting profit and:

  1. A. Taxable profit
  2. B. Retained profit
  3. C. Cash profit
  4. D. Gross profit
Answer: A. Taxable profit

Q6. A tax audit is an examination by the tax authority of a taxpayer's:

  1. A. Share price
  2. B. Customer lists only
  3. C. Returns and records
  4. D. Bank loans only
Answer: C. Returns and records

Q7. A permanent establishment is typically a:

  1. A. Fixed place of business in another country
  2. B. Bank card
  3. C. Temporary trip
  4. D. Online chat
Answer: A. Fixed place of business in another country

Q8. Tax risk management means identifying and managing the risk of:

  1. A. Rising sales
  2. B. Non-compliance and disputes
  3. C. Staff turnover only
  4. D. Lower costs only
Answer: B. Non-compliance and disputes

Q9. The tax return must be supported by:

  1. A. Estimates only
  2. B. Accurate records
  3. C. Guesswork
  4. D. Verbal statements
Answer: B. Accurate records

Q10. Professional ethics require tax advisers to act with:

  1. A. Integrity
  2. B. Pressure on regulators
  3. C. Disregard for law
  4. D. Secrecy from clients
Answer: A. Integrity

Q11. Which specific concept involves shifting taxable profits from high-tax jurisdictions to low-tax jurisdictions using intra-group financing?

  1. A. Thin capitalization
  2. B. Tax evasion
  3. C. Tax amnesty
  4. D. Revenue sharing
Answer: A. Thin capitalization

Q12. Under modern international tax frameworks, corporate tax planning strategies primarily focus on aligning value creation with:

  1. A. Physical office size
  2. B. Economic substance
  3. C. Advertising expenses
  4. D. Shareholder volume
Answer: B. Economic substance

Q13. When computing taxable income, accounting depreciation is replaced by which of the following for tax purposes?

  1. A. Residual value
  2. B. Tax depreciation allowance
  3. C. Accumulated depletion
  4. D. Historical asset cost
Answer: B. Tax depreciation allowance

Q14. Which of the following actions constitutes illegal tax evasion rather than legal tax avoidance?

  1. A. Investing in government tax-free bonds
  2. B. Failing to declare cash sales receipts
  3. C. Claiming all legitimate business deductions
  4. D. Structuring transactions to qualify for exemptions
Answer: B. Failing to declare cash sales receipts

Q15. Advance tax installments paid by a company during the tax year are treated as a:

  1. A. Direct tax liability
  2. B. Prepaid tax asset
  3. C. Tax expense deduction
  4. D. Capital reserve item
Answer: B. Prepaid tax asset

Q16. In the context of corporate taxation, tax shields are primarily created by the deductibility of:

  1. A. Dividend payments
  2. B. Interest expenses
  3. C. Retained earnings
  4. D. Share issue costs
Answer: B. Interest expenses
