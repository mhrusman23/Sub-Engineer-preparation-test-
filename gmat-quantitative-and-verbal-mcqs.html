Q1. If n is an even integer, which of the following must also be even?

  1. A. n + 1
  2. B. 2n + 2
  3. C. n/2 always
  4. D. 3n + 1
Answer: B. 2n + 2

Q2. A store sells a jacket for $90 after a 10% discount. The original price was:

  1. A. $95
  2. B. $99
  3. C. $100
  4. D. $110
Answer: C. $100

Q3. In GMAT Critical Reasoning, to weaken an argument you should:

  1. A. Add a new topic
  2. B. Undermine an assumption the argument relies on
  3. C. Agree with the conclusion
  4. D. Repeat the premises
Answer: B. Undermine an assumption the argument relies on

Q4. If a car travels 150 miles in 3 hours, how far will it travel in 5 hours at the same speed?

  1. A. 200 miles
  2. B. 225 miles
  3. C. 250 miles
  4. D. 300 miles
Answer: C. 250 miles

Q5. Which fraction is largest?

  1. A. 3/5
  2. B. 5/8
  3. C. 2/3
  4. D. 7/11
Answer: C. 2/3

Q6. In Sentence Correction, a modifier placed far from what it modifies usually creates:

  1. A. A correct sentence
  2. B. A dangling/misplaced modifier error
  3. C. A stronger sentence
  4. D. A question
Answer: B. A dangling/misplaced modifier error

Q7. If x + y = 10 and x − y = 4, then x =

  1. A. 5
  2. B. 6
  3. C. 7
  4. D. 8
Answer: C. 7

Q8. Data Sufficiency questions ask whether:

  1. A. The answer is A
  2. B. The given statements are enough to answer the question
  3. C. The question is easy
  4. D. More data can be invented
Answer: B. The given statements are enough to answer the question

Q9. The average of five numbers is 20. Their sum is:

  1. A. 25
  2. B. 50
  3. C. 100
  4. D. 120
Answer: C. 100

Q10. In a Reading Comprehension passage, the primary purpose question asks about:

  1. A. A detail
  2. B. The overall reason the passage was written
  3. C. A word meaning
  4. D. The author's age
Answer: B. The overall reason the passage was written

Q11. What core skill is primarily measured by the GMAT Reading Comprehension subsection?

  1. A. Memorization of historical dates
  2. B. Ability to analyze, interpret, and synthesize information from dense passages
  3. C. Mental arithmetic speed
  4. D. Correct spelling of complex medical terms
Answer: B. Ability to analyze, interpret, and synthesize information from dense passages

Q12. Which of the following describes the formatting of Problem Solving questions in the GMAT Quantitative section?

  1. A. They feature open-ended numeric entry boxes with no choices.
  2. B. They provide five multiple-choice options with exactly one correct answer.
  3. C. They consist of true or false statements only.
  4. D. They require writing short paragraphs.
Answer: B. They provide five multiple-choice options with exactly one correct answer.

Q13. During the GMAT quantitative reasoning section, are candidates permitted to use an electronic calculator?

  1. A. Yes, scientific calculators are provided on screen.
  2. B. Yes, handheld financial calculators are allowed.
  3. C. No calculators are permitted during the quant section.
  4. D. Yes, basic four-function calculators are allowed.
Answer: C. No calculators are permitted during the quant section.

Q14. Which specific verbal question type in the GRE requires selecting two correct choices from a list of options to complete a sentence?

  1. A. Reading Comprehension
  2. B. Critical Reasoning
  3. C. Sentence Equivalence
  4. D. Quantitative Comparison
Answer: C. Sentence Equivalence

Q15. In GMAT Critical Reasoning, a question that asks you to find a statement that destroys the logical link of an author's plan is typically looking to:

  1. A. Weaken the argument
  2. B. Strengthen the conclusion
  3. C. Summarize the paragraph
  4. D. Paraphrase the premise
Answer: A. Weaken the argument

Q16. What is the primary purpose of Quantitative Comparison questions found on the GRE?

  1. A. To test advanced calculus proofs
  2. B. To compare two mathematical quantities based on given information
  3. C. To evaluate typing speed and accuracy
  4. D. To measure vocabulary comprehension
Answer: B. To compare two mathematical quantities based on given information

Q17. In GRE Sentence Equivalence questions, how many correct answer choices must a test-taker select from the given options?

  1. A. A) Exactly one
  2. B. B) Exactly two
  3. C. C) Three or more
  4. D. D) All options that fit
Answer: B. B) Exactly two

Q18. In a GMAT Critical Reasoning question, if a prompt asks you to identify the statement that, if true, most seriously undermines the author's conclusion, you are being asked to:

  1. A. A) Strengthen the argument
  2. B. B) Weaken the argument
  3. C. C) State an assumption
  4. D. D) Summarize the passage
Answer: B. B) Weaken the argument

Q19. Which of the following describes the formatting and rules of the GMAT Quantitative Reasoning section under current test guidelines?

  1. A. A) Calculators are fully permitted for all arithmetic
  2. B. B) No calculator is allowed during the exam
  3. C. C) Scratch paper is strictly prohibited
  4. D. D) Questions are limited exclusively to geometry
Answer: B. B) No calculator is allowed during the exam

Q20. If 8 workers can complete a landscaping project in 6 days, how many days will it take 12 workers to complete the exact same project assuming everyone works at a constant rate?

  1. A. A) 3
  2. B. B) 4
  3. C. C) 5
  4. D. D) 9

Q21. A shirt originally priced at $40 is marked down by 25%. What is the final sale price of the shirt?

  1. A. A) $10
  2. B. B) $25
  3. C. C) $30
  4. D. D) $35
Answer: C. C) $30

Q22. What is the value of x if 3^(x + 2) = 27?

  1. A. A) 0
  2. B. B) 1
  3. C. C) 3
  4. D. D) 5
Answer: B. B) 1

Q23. If a boutique offers a handbag for $120 after a 20% discount, what was the original price of the handbag?

  1. A. $140
  2. B. $150
  3. C. $160
  4. D. $175
Answer: B. $150

Q24. In GMAT Critical Reasoning, to strengthen an argument you should typically:

  1. A. Introduce an unrelated premise
  2. B. Support an assumption upon which the argument depends
  3. C. Repeat the conclusion using different phrasing
  4. D. Weaken the primary evidence provided
Answer: B. Support an assumption upon which the argument depends

Q25. In a Reading Comprehension passage, a question concerning the author's tone usually asks about:

  1. A. The historical timeline of events
  2. B. The underlying attitude or emotional stance of the writer toward the subject
  3. C. The specific definition of a technical term used in paragraph two
  4. D. The exact word count of the conclusion
Answer: B. The underlying attitude or emotional stance of the writer toward the subject
