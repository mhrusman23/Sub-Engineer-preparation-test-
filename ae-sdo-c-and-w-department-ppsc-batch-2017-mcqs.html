Q1. Euler's crippling load for a column with one end fixed and one end free, as compared to Euler's crippling load for a column with both ends hinged, will be:

  1. A. Less
  2. B. More
  3. C. Equal
  4. D. None of these
Answer: A. Less

Q2. After a tension test on two specimens, the specimen having 60% reduction in area has ______ ductility than that of having 40%.

  1. A. Less
  2. B. More
  3. C. Equal
  4. D. No relation
Answer: B. More

Q3. The shear force diagram of a simply supported beam carrying uniformly distributed load consists of:

  1. A. Two right angled triangles
  2. B. One parabola
  3. C. One rectangle
  4. D. Two rectangles
Answer: A. Two right angled triangles

Q4. If a member is subjected to tensile stress of 20 kN/m2 along one axis and compressive stress of 10 kN/m2 perpendicular to the first axis, the maximum shear stress will be:

  1. A. 5 kN/m2
  2. B. 10 kN/m2
  3. C. 15 kN/m2
  4. D. 20 kN/m2
Answer: C. 15 kN/m2

Q5. A rectangular beam of cross-sectional area A, length L, width B and depth D will have ______ elastic strength than a rectangular beam of same area, length and width but depth 2D.

  1. A. Less (1/4)
  2. B. More
  3. C. Same
  4. D. Double
Answer: A. Less (1/4)

Q6. The shear strength of a hollow shaft (outer diameter = D, inner diameter = D/2) will be ______ times the shear strength of a solid shaft of diameter D.

  1. A. 15/16
  2. B. 1/2
  3. C. 3/4
  4. D. 7/8
Answer: A. 15/16

Q7. When a closed helical spring is subjected to an axial compressive load, the deformation in the spring is due to:

  1. A. Crushing
  2. B. Torsion
  3. C. Bending
  4. D. Shear
Answer: B. Torsion

Q8. The ratio of change in volume to original volume is called:

  1. A. Volumetric strain
  2. B. Poisson's ratio
  3. C. Volumetric stress
  4. D. Bulk modulus
Answer: A. Volumetric strain

Q9. In a shear force diagram, where the shear force is zero, the bending moment at that point is:

  1. A. Maximum
  2. B. Minimum
  3. C. Zero
  4. D. Negative
Answer: A. Maximum

Q10. Ductility of a material ______ with increase in percentage reduction in area of the member during a tension test.

  1. A. Decreases
  2. B. Increases
  3. C. Remains same
  4. D. None of these
Answer: B. Increases

Q11. Strain energy stored in a spring under maximum load without sufficient permanent deformation is called:

  1. A. Impact energy
  2. B. Proof resilience
  3. C. Proof stress
  4. D. Modulus of resilience
Answer: B. Proof resilience

Q12. A beam will have uniform strength throughout the length of the member if:

  1. A. Beam has same cross-sectional properties throughout the length
  2. B. Loading throughout the length is same
  3. C. Depth is uniform
  4. D. Both A and B
Answer: A. Beam has same cross-sectional properties throughout the length

Q13. If normal stress $ is acting along the longitudinal axis of a member, the resultant of stresses along a plane at angle @ with respect to the applied normal stress will be:

  1. A. $ sin@
  2. B. $ cos@
  3. C. $ sin2@
  4. D. $ cos2@
Answer: B. $ cos@

Q14. Point of contraflexure occurs in:

  1. A. Simply supported beams
  2. B. Fixed ended beams
  3. C. Cantilever beams
  4. D. Overhanging beams
Answer: B. Fixed ended beams

Q15. Buckling in compression members takes place along:

  1. A. Least radius of gyration
  2. B. Parallel to load axis
  3. C. Perpendicular to load axis
  4. D. Minimum cross-sectional dimension
Answer: A. Least radius of gyration

Q16. Along principal planes, shear stresses are:

  1. A. Zero
  2. B. Minimum
  3. C. Maximum
  4. D. Constant
Answer: A. Zero

Q17. Cubic parabola shear force diagram of cantilever beams occurs in case of:

  1. A. Uniformly distributed load
  2. B. Uniformly varying load
  3. C. Between points where no load acts
  4. D. Point loading
Answer: B. Uniformly varying load

Q18. Slenderness is the ratio between:

  1. A. Least radius of gyration to length
  2. B. Length to least radius of gyration
  3. C. Length to least lateral dimension
  4. D. Area to perimeter
Answer: B. Length to least radius of gyration

Q19. Type of welds usually used in construction now-a-days:

  1. A. Fillet weld
  2. B. Beam weld
  3. C. Rivet weld
  4. D. Structure weld
Answer: A. Fillet weld

Q20. In practical, drawings prepared by practicing drafters for 3-D views are:

  1. A. Isometric
  2. B. Orthographic
  3. C. Auxiliary
  4. D. Perspective
Answer: D. Perspective

Q21. Purpose of curing of concrete is to stop:

  1. A. Increase in temperature of concrete
  2. B. Decrease in moisture of concrete
  3. C. Loss of moisture
  4. D. Dry shrinkage cracking
Answer: C. Loss of moisture

Q22. Shear stress in a rectangular cross-section beam due to torque will be maximum at:

  1. A. Center of longer side of cross-section
  2. B. Center of shorter side of cross-section
  3. C. Corners
  4. D. Maximum distance away from centroid
Answer: D. Maximum distance away from centroid

Q23. Limiting strain in concrete is:

  1. A. 0.001
  2. B. 0.002
  3. C. 0.003
  4. D. 0.004
Answer: C. 0.003

Q24. Split cylinder test is performed to determine:

  1. A. MOR (Modulus of Rupture)
  2. B. Durability
  3. C. Slump
  4. D. Compressive strength
Answer: A. MOR (Modulus of Rupture)

Q25. In an I-section beam subjected to transverse loading, shear stress will be maximum at:

  1. A. Bottom of flange in flange portion
  2. B. Bottom of flange in web portion
  3. C. Extreme fibers
  4. D. Neutral axis
Answer: D. Neutral axis
