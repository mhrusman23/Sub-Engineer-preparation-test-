Q1. The function of theodolite is to measure.

  1. A. Deflection angle
  2. B. Horizontal angle
  3. C. Vertical angle
  4. D. All of these
Answer: D. All of these

Q2. The theodolite whose telescope can rotate through complete revolution in vertical plane is called:

  1. A. Tilting
  2. B. Non-transit
  3. C. Transit
  4. D. All of these
Answer: C. Transit

Q3. The size of theodolite is expressed by

  1. A. Length of telescope
  2. B. Magnifying power
  3. C. Dia of main scale
  4. D. Weight of theodolite
Answer: C. Dia of main scale

Q4. The face left position of theodolite is also called.

  1. A. Telescope inverted
  2. B. Telescope reversed
  3. C. Telescope normal
  4. D. None of these
Answer: C. Telescope normal

Q5. When telescope bubble of theodolite is upward it is called.

  1. A. Telescope normal
  2. B. Face left
  3. C. Face right
  4. D. Both a & b
Answer: D. Both a & b

Q6. The other name of horizontal axis of theodolite is:

  1. A. Z-axis
  2. B. Trunnion axis
  3. C. Zero axis
  4. D. None of these
Answer: B. Trunnion axis

Q7. When the bubble of telescope of the theodolite is downward, it is called.

  1. A. Diaphragm
  2. B. Telescope inverted
  3. C. Telescope normal
  4. D. Face left
Answer: B. Telescope inverted

Q8. The axis of rotation of telescope in the horizontal plane is called:

  1. A. Horizontal axis
  2. B. Vertical axis
  3. C. Trunnion axis
  4. D. None of these
Answer: B. Vertical axis

Q9. Transiting of a theodolite is also called:

  1. A. Inverting
  2. B. Plunging
  3. C. Reversing
  4. D. Swinging
Answer: C. Reversing

Q10. The theodolite which shows readings in digits is called:

  1. A. Vernier
  2. B. Digital
  3. C. Micrometer
  4. D. All of these
Answer: B. Digital

Q11. Reticles of a telescope are cleared with:

  1. A. Foot screws
  2. B. Focusing screw
  3. C. Eye piece
  4. D. Object glass
Answer: C. Eye piece

Q12. Number of horizontal cross hairs in stadia diaphragm is:

  1. A. 2
  2. B. 1
  3. C. 4
  4. D. 3
Answer: D. 3

Q13. Rotating telescope of theodolite through 180° in vertical plane is called:

  1. A. Transiting
  2. B. Centring
  3. C. Rotating
  4. D. Swinging
Answer: A. Transiting

Q14. Function of optical plummet of theodolite:

  1. A. Sighting
  2. B. Set in case
  3. C. Centering
  4. D. None of these
Answer: C. Centering

Q15. At the time of measurement of angle, bubble axis of theodolite should be at such condition with respect to its vertical axis:

  1. A. Any one
  2. B. Coincident
  3. C. Normal
  4. D. Parallel
Answer: C. Normal

Q16. The sum of internal angles of a closed traverse is:

  1. A. (2m − 4)60°
  2. B. (2n − 4)90°
  3. C. (2n ± 4)60°
  4. D. (2n + 4)90°
Answer: B. (2n − 4)90°

Q17. Positive departure is also called.

  1. A. Westing
  2. B. Southing
  3. C. Easting
  4. D. Northing
Answer: C. Easting

Q18. Fore bearing of AB is 110°, its back bearing will be:

  1. A. 290°
  2. B. 240°
  3. C. 280°
  4. D. 315°
Answer: A. 290°

Q19. Latitude is determined by the formula:

  1. A. L × cos θ
  2. B. L / cos θ
  3. C. L × sin θ
  4. D. L / sin θ
Answer: A. L × cos θ

Q20. The formula to calculate the departure of a survey line is:

  1. A. l sin θ
  2. B. l tan θ
  3. C. l sin θ / (cos θ sin θ)
  4. D. l cos θ
Answer: A. l sin θ

Q21. Value of departure towards West/Left-side is considered as:

  1. A. negative
  2. B. positive
  3. C. True
  4. D. any one
Answer: A. negative

Q22. Value of deflection angles ranges as:

  1. A. 0 – 180°
  2. B. 90° – 270°
  3. C. 0 – 360°
  4. D. 0 – 90°
Answer: D. 0 – 90°

Q23. If line of collimation is horizontal, then vertical angle will be:

  1. A. 0°
  2. B. 30°
  3. C. 60°
  4. D. 90°
Answer: D. 90°

Q24. Sum of external angles of any closed traverse is:

  1. A. (2n − 4)90°
  2. B. (4 − 2n)90°
  3. C. (2n + 4)90°
Answer: C. (2n + 4)90°

Q25. In Gale's table, all points of traverse are brought in the quadrant:

  1. A. 2nd
  2. B. Third
  3. C. First
  4. D. 4th
Answer: C. First
