Q1. A flexible budget adjusts for:

  1. A. Inflation only
  2. B. Different levels of activity/volume
  3. C. New products
  4. D. Staff changes
Answer: B. Different levels of activity/volume

Q2. A favourable materials price variance means:

  1. A. More material was used
  2. B. Materials were bought for less than the standard price
  3. C. Production stopped
  4. D. Quality improved
Answer: B. Materials were bought for less than the standard price

Q3. The master budget starts with:

  1. A. The cash budget
  2. B. The sales budget
  3. C. The production budget
  4. D. The capital budget
Answer: B. The sales budget

Q4. Standard costing is used to:

  1. A. Set tax rates
  2. B. Set expected costs and analyse variances from actual
  3. C. Price shares
  4. D. Pay salaries
Answer: B. Set expected costs and analyse variances from actual

Q5. CVP analysis studies how profit changes with:

  1. A. Only price
  2. B. Volume, costs and prices
  3. C. Staff numbers
  4. D. Location
Answer: B. Volume, costs and prices

Q6. The break-even point is where:

  1. A. Revenue is highest
  2. B. Total revenue equals total costs (zero profit)
  3. C. Costs are zero
  4. D. Sales stop
Answer: B. Total revenue equals total costs (zero profit)

Q7. A rolling (continuous) budget is:

  1. A. Prepared once
  2. B. Updated regularly by adding a new period as one ends
  3. C. Never changed
  4. D. Only for cash
Answer: B. Updated regularly by adding a new period as one ends

Q8. Variance analysis helps managers to:

  1. A. Blame workers
  2. B. Understand why actual results differ from plan and act
  3. C. Avoid planning
  4. D. Set prices only
Answer: B. Understand why actual results differ from plan and act

Q9. Big data analytics in finance is mainly used to:

  1. A. Replace accountants
  2. B. Find patterns and improve forecasts and decisions
  3. C. Print reports
  4. D. Store invoices
Answer: B. Find patterns and improve forecasts and decisions

Q10. The balanced scorecard measures performance in financial and:

  1. A. Only customer areas
  2. B. Customer, internal process, and learning & growth perspectives
  3. C. Only staff areas
  4. D. Only market areas
Answer: B. Customer, internal process, and learning & growth perspectives
