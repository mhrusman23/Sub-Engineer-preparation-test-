Q1. A flexible budget adjusts for:

  1. A. Inflation only
  2. B. Different levels of activity/volume
  3. C. New products
  4. D. Staff changes
Answer: B. Different levels of activity/volume

Q2. A favourable materials price variance means:

  1. A. More material was used
  2. B. Materials were bought for less than the standard price
  3. C. Production stopped
  4. D. Quality improved
Answer: B. Materials were bought for less than the standard price

Q3. The master budget starts with:

  1. A. The cash budget
  2. B. The sales budget
  3. C. The production budget
  4. D. The capital budget
Answer: B. The sales budget

Q4. Standard costing is used to:

  1. A. Set tax rates
  2. B. Set expected costs and analyse variances from actual
  3. C. Price shares
  4. D. Pay salaries
Answer: B. Set expected costs and analyse variances from actual

Q5. CVP analysis studies how profit changes with:

  1. A. Only price
  2. B. Volume, costs and prices
  3. C. Staff numbers
  4. D. Location
Answer: B. Volume, costs and prices

Q6. The break-even point is where:

  1. A. Revenue is highest
  2. B. Total revenue equals total costs (zero profit)
  3. C. Costs are zero
  4. D. Sales stop
Answer: B. Total revenue equals total costs (zero profit)

Q7. A rolling (continuous) budget is:

  1. A. Prepared once
  2. B. Updated regularly by adding a new period as one ends
  3. C. Never changed
  4. D. Only for cash
Answer: B. Updated regularly by adding a new period as one ends

Q8. Variance analysis helps managers to:

  1. A. Blame workers
  2. B. Understand why actual results differ from plan and act
  3. C. Avoid planning
  4. D. Set prices only
Answer: B. Understand why actual results differ from plan and act

Q9. Big data analytics in finance is mainly used to:

  1. A. Replace accountants
  2. B. Find patterns and improve forecasts and decisions
  3. C. Print reports
  4. D. Store invoices
Answer: B. Find patterns and improve forecasts and decisions

Q10. The balanced scorecard measures performance in financial and:

  1. A. Only customer areas
  2. B. Customer, internal process, and learning & growth perspectives
  3. C. Only staff areas
  4. D. Only market areas
Answer: B. Customer, internal process, and learning & growth perspectives

Q11. Which variance measures the difference between the actual quantity of direct materials used and the standard quantity allowed, valued at the standard price?

  1. A. Materials price variance
  2. B. Materials quantity usage variance
  3. C. Labor efficiency variance
  4. D. Fixed overhead volume variance
Answer: B. Materials quantity usage variance

Q12. In cost-volume-profit analysis, what term describes the volume level where total revenues exactly equal total costs?

  1. A. Margin of safety
  2. B. Operating leverage point
  3. C. Break-even point
  4. D. Contribution margin threshold
Answer: C. Break-even point

Q13. What is the primary purpose of utilizing a balanced scorecard in performance management?

  1. A. To exclusively monitor short-term stock prices
  2. B. To link financial metrics with non-financial drivers across multiple perspectives
  3. C. To replace traditional financial statements entirely
  4. D. To calculate precise overhead allocation rates
Answer: B. To link financial metrics with non-financial drivers across multiple perspectives

Q14. Which budget category specifically details the expected cash inflows and outflows for a future period to ensure adequate liquidity?

  1. A. Capital budget
  2. B. Financial budget
  3. C. Operational budget
  4. D. Master budget
Answer: B. Financial budget

Q15. Which type of standard represents an efficient level of performance that includes allowances for normal operational downtime and waste?

  1. A. Ideal standard
  2. B. Practical standard
  3. C. Theoretical standard
  4. D. Static standard
Answer: B. Practical standard

Q16. When developing a comprehensive master budget for a manufacturing firm, what is typically the very first component that must be prepared?

  1. A. Cash budget
  2. B. Direct labor budget
  3. C. Sales budget
  4. D. Capital expenditure budget
Answer: C. Sales budget

Q17. In cost‑volume‑profit analysis, the contribution margin ratio is calculated as:

  1. A. (Sales – Variable Costs) ÷ Sales
  2. B. (Sales – Fixed Costs) ÷ Sales
  3. C. (Sales – Variable Costs) ÷ Fixed Costs
  4. D. Sales ÷ (Variable Costs + Fixed Costs)
Answer: A. (Sales – Variable Costs) ÷ Sales

Q18. Which of the following statements best describes the purpose of a pro‑forma financial statement?

  1. A. It reports actual financial results for the current year
  2. B. It provides a forecast of future financial performance based on assumptions
  3. C. It records only cash transactions
  4. D. It replaces audited financial statements for regulatory filing
Answer: B. It provides a forecast of future financial performance based on assumptions

Q19. A company uses a flexible budget to compare actual results with the budgeted amounts for a given level of activity. Which of the following best describes the primary advantage of a flexible budget over a static budget?

  1. A. It eliminates the need for variance analysis
  2. B. It allows the budget to be updated automatically with actual sales
  3. C. It adjusts budgeted figures to the actual activity level
  4. D. It guarantees that actual costs will equal budgeted costs
Answer: C. It adjusts budgeted figures to the actual activity level

Q20. A variance that measures the difference between the actual quantity of a resource used and the standard quantity allowed for the actual output is known as:

  1. A. Price variance
  2. B. Efficiency variance
  3. C. Volume variance
  4. D. Sales variance
Answer: B. Efficiency variance

Q21. Which of the following is a key component of the budgeting process that involves setting financial targets for the next fiscal year?

  1. A. Capital budgeting
  2. B. Operating budgeting
  3. C. Cash budgeting
  4. D. Strategic budgeting
Answer: B. Operating budgeting

Q22. In a rolling forecast, the budget is updated by:

  1. A. Adding a new period and dropping the oldest period
  2. B. Recalculating all figures from scratch each month
  3. C. Keeping the original budget unchanged
  4. D. Only adjusting the revenue forecast
Answer: A. Adding a new period and dropping the oldest period

Q23. Which of the following describes an unfavorable labor efficiency variance?

  1. A. Workers were paid a lower hourly wage than standard
  2. B. Actual direct labor hours exceeded the standard hours allowed for actual production
  3. C. Production output exceeded the initial sales forecast
  4. D. Fewer workers were employed than required by the budget
Answer: B. Actual direct labor hours exceeded the standard hours allowed for actual production

Q24. What is the primary purpose of establishing a zero-based budgeting system?

  1. A. To automatically increase all departmental budgets by a fixed percentage each year
  2. B. To require every manager to justify all requested expenses completely from scratch for each new period
  3. C. To freeze discretionary spending entirely during economic downturns
  4. D. To eliminate the need for any variance analysis at year-end
Answer: B. To require every manager to justify all requested expenses completely from scratch for each new period

Q25. When conducting variance analysis, a favorable direct materials quantity variance indicates that:

  1. A. More material was purchased than needed for current production
  2. B. The actual quantity of materials used was less than the standard allowed for actual output
  3. C. Suppliers increased their raw material prices unexpectedly
  4. D. Defective finished goods had to be completely scrapped
Answer: B. The actual quantity of materials used was less than the standard allowed for actual output
