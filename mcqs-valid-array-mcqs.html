Q1. Maximum limit of percentage of steel in R.C.C structures is ....... by volume.

  1. A. 4%
  2. B. 10%
  3. C. 8%
  4. D. 15%
Answer: C. 8%

Q2. Volume of slopy portion of column base is mostly determined by formula.

  1. A. Pyramidal
  2. B. Any one
  3. C. Ordinate

  1. A. 60°
  2. B. 30°
  3. C. 75°
  4. D. 45°
Answer: D. 45°
