Q1. The GRE Analytical Writing section asks you to write:

  1. A. A poem
  2. B. An essay analysing an issue/argument
  3. C. A story
  4. D. A letter
Answer: B. An essay analysing an issue/argument

Q2. In the 'Analyse an Argument' task, you should:

  1. A. Agree with the argument
  2. B. Examine the argument's logic and assumptions
  3. C. Write your own opinion only
  4. D. Summarise the argument
Answer: B. Examine the argument's logic and assumptions

Q3. A strong essay is organised with:

  1. A. One paragraph
  2. B. An introduction, developed body paragraphs and a conclusion
  3. C. Only examples
  4. D. Bullet points
Answer: B. An introduction, developed body paragraphs and a conclusion

Q4. Time given for each GRE writing task is:

  1. A. 15 minutes
  2. B. 30 minutes
  3. C. 45 minutes
  4. D. 60 minutes
Answer: B. 30 minutes

Q5. To score well, your essay should mainly show:

  1. A. Long words only
  2. B. Clear, well-reasoned analysis with support
  3. C. Perfect handwriting
  4. D. Speed
Answer: B. Clear, well-reasoned analysis with support

Q6. An assumption in an argument is:

  1. A. A stated fact
  2. B. An unstated belief the argument depends on
  3. C. The conclusion
  4. D. An example
Answer: B. An unstated belief the argument depends on

Q7. Which weakens an argument most directly?

  1. A. Praising the author
  2. B. Showing a key assumption may be false
  3. C. Repeating the claim
  4. D. Adding a title
Answer: B. Showing a key assumption may be false

Q8. In the Issue task, a balanced approach means:

  1. A. Taking no position
  2. B. Presenting a clear position while addressing complexity/counterpoints
  3. C. Copying the prompt
  4. D. Writing two essays
Answer: B. Presenting a clear position while addressing complexity/counterpoints

Q9. Good transitions in an essay:

  1. A. Confuse the reader
  2. B. Show how ideas connect
  3. C. Add length only
  4. D. Are unnecessary
Answer: B. Show how ideas connect

Q10. Before time ends, you should leave a few minutes to:

  1. A. Start again
  2. B. Proofread for errors
  3. C. Add new arguments
  4. D. Delete the essay
Answer: B. Proofread for errors

Q11. What is the very first section you will face when starting the GRE test?

  1. A. Analytical Writing
  2. B. Quantitative Reasoning
  3. C. Verbal Reasoning
  4. D. Experimental Section
Answer: A. Analytical Writing

Q12. What level of English convention usage is expected for a top-scoring GRE essay response?

  1. A. Basic grammar with numerous spelling errors
  2. B. Informal slang and conversational idioms
  3. C. Superior facility with standard written English conventions
  4. D. Exclusively archaic and poetic terminology
Answer: C. Superior facility with standard written English conventions

Q13. How much time is allotted to complete the 'Analyze an Issue' writing task on the GRE?

  1. A. 15 minutes
  2. B. 30 minutes
  3. C. 45 minutes
  4. D. 60 minutes
Answer: B. 30 minutes

Q14. When addressing an Issue prompt on the GRE, what approach should you take regarding your stance?

  1. A. You must remain completely neutral
  2. B. You must agree with the premise provided
  3. C. You select a position and develop it using relevant reasons and examples
  4. D. You must avoid taking any clear thesis
Answer: C. You select a position and develop it using relevant reasons and examples

Q15. Which of the following characterizes a high-scoring GRE essay's structural progression?

  1. A. Random flow of unrelated points
  2. B. Smooth transitions and logically developed ideas
  3. C. A heavy reliance on bullet points
  4. D. Repetition of the prompt in every paragraph
Answer: B. Smooth transitions and logically developed ideas

Q16. What does the GRE 'Analyze an Argument' task primarily require you to evaluate?

  1. A. Your personal feelings about the topic
  2. B. The aesthetic style of the author's writing
  3. C. The logic and underlying assumptions of the given text
  4. D. The historical accuracy of the statistics provided
Answer: C. The logic and underlying assumptions of the given text

Q17. What should a writer do after completing the essay draft during the 30‑minute time limit?

  1. A. Immediately submit without review
  2. B. Spend the remaining time adding more examples
  3. C. Proofread for clarity and minor errors
  4. D. Write a new introduction
Answer: C. Proofread for clarity and minor errors

Q18. What is the main purpose of the 30‑minute ‘Analyze an Issue’ task on the GRE?

  1. A. To test specific knowledge about a scientific topic
  2. B. To evaluate the test‑taker’s ability to write a persuasive essay
  3. C. To assess the speed of reading passages
  4. D. To measure vocabulary breadth
Answer: B. To evaluate the test‑taker’s ability to write a persuasive essay

Q19. What does the GRE expect from the writer regarding the use of advanced vocabulary?

  1. A. Only simple words are acceptable
  2. B. Use of complex words is mandatory
  3. C. Appropriate, clear language that supports the argument
  4. D. Avoid all technical terms
Answer: C. Appropriate, clear language that supports the argument

Q20. Which element is NOT required in a high‑scoring GRE analytical essay?

  1. A. A clear thesis statement
  2. B. Concrete examples that support each claim
  3. C. A list of unrelated facts
  4. D. Logical organization of ideas
Answer: C. A list of unrelated facts

Q21. Which strategy best helps maintain coherence throughout the essay?

  1. A. Repeating the thesis in every paragraph
  2. B. Using transition words to link ideas
  3. C. Writing long sentences without punctuation
  4. D. Leaving the conclusion blank
Answer: B. Using transition words to link ideas

Q22. When addressing an Issue task instruction that asks you to consider ways the statement might not hold true, what are you expected to explore?

  1. A. Counterarguments and potential exceptions
  2. B. Typographical errors in the prompt
  3. C. Personal biographical details of the author
  4. D. Alternative formatting options for paragraphs
Answer: A. Counterarguments and potential exceptions

Q23. What is a key characteristic of a coherent discussion in analytical writing?

  1. A. Sustaining a well-focused line of reasoning throughout
  2. B. Shifting topics every single sentence
  3. C. Using as many complex jargon words as possible
  4. D. Writing a narrative story instead of an essay
Answer: A. Sustaining a well-focused line of reasoning throughout

Q24. What primary competency does the GRE Analytical Writing measure evaluate in test takers?

  1. A. Memorization of advanced scientific terminology
  2. B. Ability to articulate complex ideas clearly and effectively
  3. C. Proficiency in creative fiction and poetry writing
  4. D. Speed of typing complex computer code
Answer: B. Ability to articulate complex ideas clearly and effectively

Q25. How much time is officially allotted to complete the Analyze an Issue task on the GRE?

  1. A. 15 minutes
  2. B. 30 minutes
  3. C. 45 minutes
  4. D. 60 minutes
Answer: B. 30 minutes
