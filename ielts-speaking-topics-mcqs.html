IELTS Speaking Topics MCQs (IELTS & English Tests)

Q1. In Part 1, answers should generally be:

  1. A. One word
  2. B. A few sentences — direct answer plus a reason or detail
  3. C. Five minutes long
  4. D. Written
Answer: B. A few sentences — direct answer plus a reason or detail

Q2. A good way to extend a Part 1 answer is to:

  1. A. Repeat the question
  2. B. Give a reason and a short example
  3. C. Change the topic
  4. D. Stay silent
Answer: B. Give a reason and a short example

Q3. Part 2 cue cards usually ask you to describe:

  1. A. A graph
  2. B. A person, place, object or experience, with points to cover
  3. C. An essay
  4. D. A news report
Answer: B. A person, place, object or experience, with points to cover

Q4. During the 1-minute preparation in Part 2, you should:

  1. A. Write the full speech
  2. B. Make brief notes of key points
  3. C. Memorise sentences
  4. D. Close your eyes
Answer: B. Make brief notes of key points

Q5. If you cannot remember a word in Speaking, you should:

  1. A. Stop speaking
  2. B. Describe the word or use a similar word and continue
  3. C. Speak in your own language
  4. D. End the test
Answer: B. Describe the word or use a similar word and continue

Q6. In Part 3, questions are usually about:

  1. A. Your daily routine only
  2. B. Wider issues and opinions linked to the Part 2 topic
  3. C. Spelling
  4. D. Your family only
Answer: B. Wider issues and opinions linked to the Part 2 topic

Q7. Which is a useful phrase for giving opinions?

  1. A. 'In my view...'
  2. B. 'No comment.'
  3. C. 'Whatever.'
  4. D. 'I don't care.'
Answer: A. 'In my view...'

Q8. Fluency is damaged most by:

  1. A. A slight accent
  2. B. Long pauses while searching for words
  3. C. Speaking clearly
  4. D. Using simple words
Answer: B. Long pauses while searching for words

Q9. For the topic 'hometown', a strong answer includes:

  1. A. Only the name
  2. B. Location, what it is known for, and what you like about it
  3. C. A song
  4. D. The weather only
Answer: B. Location, what it is known for, and what you like about it

Q10. Idioms in Speaking should be:

  1. A. Used in every sentence
  2. B. Used naturally and correctly, not forced
  3. C. Avoided completely
  4. D. Translated word for word
Answer: B. Used naturally and correctly, not forced

Q11. When asked 'Do you like...?', the weakest answer is:

  1. A. 'Yes, because...'
  2. B. 'No, not really. I prefer...'
  3. C. 'Yes.' (nothing more)
  4. D. 'Sometimes, it depends...'
Answer: C. 'Yes.' (nothing more)

Q12. Part 3 answers benefit from:

  1. A. One-word replies
  2. B. Giving reasons, examples and comparisons
  3. C. Reading aloud
  4. D. Silence
Answer: B. Giving reasons, examples and comparisons

Q13. A 'hesitation device' like 'well' or 'actually' can:

  1. A. Lower your score always
  2. B. Give you thinking time and sound natural
  3. C. Replace answers
  4. D. Confuse the examiner
Answer: B. Give you thinking time and sound natural

Q14. Describing a memorable trip in Part 2, you should speak about:

  1. A. Only the date
  2. B. Where, when, who with, what happened and why it was memorable
  3. C. The price only
  4. D. Nothing in detail
Answer: B. Where, when, who with, what happened and why it was memorable

Q15. Pronunciation is assessed on:

  1. A. Having a native accent
  2. B. Being easy to understand, with clear sounds and natural stress
  3. C. Speaking loudly
  4. D. Speaking fast
Answer: B. Being easy to understand, with clear sounds and natural stress

Q16. If the examiner interrupts you in Part 2, it usually means:

  1. A. You failed
  2. B. The time for that part is over — this is normal
  3. C. You made a big mistake
  4. D. The test is cancelled
Answer: B. The time for that part is over — this is normal

Q17. Collocations help Speaking because they:

  1. A. Are long
  2. B. Make your English sound natural
  3. C. Are formal
  4. D. Are easy to memorise
Answer: B. Make your English sound natural

Q18. For 'future plans' questions, the best tense use is:

  1. A. Past simple only
  2. B. 'Going to' / 'will' / 'I am planning to...'
  3. C. Present simple only
  4. D. No verbs
Answer: B. 'Going to' / 'will' / 'I am planning to...'

Q19. Comparing past and present (e.g. transport) shows the examiner:

  1. A. Nothing
  2. B. Your ability to use a range of tenses
  3. C. Your memory only
  4. D. Your accent
Answer: B. Your ability to use a range of tenses

Q20. The best attitude in the Speaking test is:

  1. A. Nervous silence
  2. B. Friendly, natural conversation — treat it as a discussion
  3. C. Shouting answers
  4. D. Reading from paper
Answer: B. Friendly, natural conversation — treat it as a discussion
