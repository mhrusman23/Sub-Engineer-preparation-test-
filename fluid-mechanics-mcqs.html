Q1. The SI unit of dynamic viscosity is:

  1. A. N/m
  2. B. Pa.s
  3. C. kg/m3
  4. D. m2/s
Answer: B. Pa.s

Q2. Bernoulli's equation is based on conservation of:

  1. A. Energy
  2. B. Charge
  3. C. Mass only
  4. D. Momentum only
Answer: A. Energy

Q3. Reynolds number is the ratio of inertial forces to:

  1. A. Surface tension
  2. B. Viscous forces
  3. C. Pressure forces
  4. D. Gravity forces
Answer: B. Viscous forces

Q4. Flow in a pipe is generally laminar when Reynolds number is below about:

  1. A. 2000
  2. B. 50000
  3. C. 10000
  4. D. 4000
Answer: A. 2000

Q5. The density of water at 4 C is about:

  1. A. 1000 kg/m3
  2. B. 100 kg/m3
  3. C. 9810 kg/m3
  4. D. 10 kg/m3
Answer: A. 1000 kg/m3

Q6. Pressure in a static fluid increases with:

  1. A. Width
  2. B. Depth
  3. C. Length of container
  4. D. Colour
Answer: B. Depth

Q7. A Newtonian fluid has a shear stress proportional to:

  1. A. Volume
  2. B. Temperature only
  3. C. Velocity gradient
  4. D. Pressure
Answer: C. Velocity gradient

Q8. The continuity equation expresses conservation of:

  1. A. Charge
  2. B. Mass
  3. C. Momentum
  4. D. Energy
Answer: B. Mass

Q9. A venturimeter is used to measure:

  1. A. Temperature
  2. B. Viscosity only
  3. C. Density only
  4. D. Flow rate
Answer: D. Flow rate

Q10. Buoyant force equals the weight of:

  1. A. Fluid displaced
  2. B. The container
  3. C. Air
  4. D. The object
Answer: A. Fluid displaced

Q11. What is the ratio of dynamic viscosity to fluid density known as?

  1. A. Bulk modulus
  2. B. Kinematic viscosity
  3. C. Surface tension
  4. D. Compressibility
Answer: B. Kinematic viscosity

Q12. Which instrument uses a divergent cone to help convert kinetic energy back into pressure energy with minimum loss?

  1. A. Orifice meter
  2. B. Pitot tube
  3. C. Venturi meter
  4. D. Manometer
Answer: C. Venturi meter

Q13. What does a piezometer tube attached to a pipe primarily measure?

  1. A. Dynamic pressure
  2. B. Total pressure head
  3. C. Static pressure head
  4. D. Stagnation pressure
Answer: C. Static pressure head

Q14. What is the primary physical principle behind the operation of a centrifugal pump impeller?

  1. A. Free vortex motion
  2. B. Forced vortex motion
  3. C. Potential flow rotation
  4. D. Solid body translation
Answer: B. Forced vortex motion

Q15. Under what condition does flow separation typically occur in a fluid passing over a curved boundary?

  1. A. Favorable pressure gradient
  2. B. Adverse pressure gradient
  3. C. Constant pressure gradient
  4. D. Zero shear stress zone
Answer: B. Adverse pressure gradient

Q16. What dimensionless parameter represents the ratio of inertia forces to elastic forces in a fluid flow?

  1. A. Froude number
  2. B. Weber number
  3. C. Mach number
  4. D. Euler number
Answer: C. Mach number
