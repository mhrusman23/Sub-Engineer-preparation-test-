TOEFL Writing MCQs (IELTS & English Tests)

Free MCQs with answers for TOEFL Writing. 20 questions in 1 chapter test are in the app; all are shown here.

Q1. The TOEFL integrated writing task asks you to:

  1. A. Give your opinion
  2. B. Summarise how a lecture relates to a reading passage
  3. C. Write a story
  4. D. Describe a chart
Answer: B. Summarise how a lecture relates to a reading passage

Q2. In integrated writing, the lecture usually:

  1. A. Repeats the reading
  2. B. Casts doubt on or challenges points in the reading
  3. C. Is unrelated
  4. D. Is shorter than one line
Answer: B. Casts doubt on or challenges points in the reading

Q3. The independent (Academic Discussion) task requires you to:

  1. A. Summarise only
  2. B. State and support your opinion in a discussion
  3. C. Copy the professor
  4. D. Write a letter
Answer: B. State and support your opinion in a discussion

Q4. Your integrated summary should be mainly:

  1. A. Your opinion
  2. B. The lecture's points linked to the reading's points
  3. C. New examples
  4. D. Questions
Answer: B. The lecture's points linked to the reading's points

Q5. Copying long sentences from the reading:

  1. A. Is the best method
  2. B. Should be avoided — paraphrase instead
  3. C. Is required
  4. D. Gives full marks
Answer: B. Should be avoided — paraphrase instead

Q6. A strong discussion post has:

  1. A. One line
  2. B. A clear position, reasons and examples, about 100+ words
  3. C. Only emojis
  4. D. No position
Answer: B. A clear position, reasons and examples, about 100+ words

Q7. Organisation in writing means:

  1. A. Random sentences
  2. B. Introduction, body paragraphs and clear connections
  3. C. One paragraph only
  4. D. Long words
Answer: B. Introduction, body paragraphs and clear connections

Q8. Grammar variety means using:

  1. A. One tense
  2. B. A mix of sentence structures correctly
  3. C. Only questions
  4. D. Only short sentences
Answer: B. A mix of sentence structures correctly

Q9. In integrated writing, you should spend more words on:

  1. A. The reading
  2. B. The lecture content (it answers the reading)
  3. C. Your opinion
  4. D. The introduction
Answer: B. The lecture content (it answers the reading)

Q10. Time for the writing section is about:

  1. A. 10 minutes
  2. B. 50 minutes total
  3. C. 2 hours
  4. D. Unlimited
Answer: B. 50 minutes total

Q11. Linking words like 'moreover' and 'in contrast':

  1. A. Are errors
  2. B. Improve coherence
  3. C. Are banned
  4. D. Replace content
Answer: B. Improve coherence

Q12. Proofreading in the last minutes should check:

  1. A. The title
  2. B. Verb forms, plurals and punctuation
  3. C. The word count only
  4. D. Nothing
Answer: B. Verb forms, plurals and punctuation

Q13. An example in your discussion post should:

  1. A. Be unrelated
  2. B. Support your reason clearly
  3. C. Be very long
  4. D. Be copied
Answer: B. Support your reason clearly

Q14. Formal academic style avoids:

  1. A. Linking words
  2. B. Slang and contractions
  3. C. Examples
  4. D. Paragraphs
Answer: B. Slang and contractions

Q15. If the reading says 'X is beneficial' and the lecture disagrees, your summary should:

  1. A. Hide the disagreement
  2. B. Clearly show the contrast between them
  3. C. Only summarise the reading
  4. D. Give your view
Answer: B. Clearly show the contrast between them

Q16. Word count matters because:

  1. A. Longer is always better
  2. B. Too short answers cannot develop ideas fully
  3. C. Exactly 300 words is required
  4. D. It is not checked
Answer: B. Too short answers cannot develop ideas fully

Q17. Taking notes during the lecture for integrated writing:

  1. A. Is not allowed
  2. B. Is essential — the lecture plays only once
  3. C. Is useless
  4. D. Is done after writing
Answer: B. Is essential — the lecture plays only once

Q18. A topic sentence should:

  1. A. End the paragraph
  2. B. Announce the paragraph's main point
  3. C. Be a question
  4. D. Be copied
Answer: B. Announce the paragraph's main point

Q19. Your own knowledge/examples in integrated writing:

  1. A. Are the main content
  2. B. Should not replace the source information
  3. C. Are required
  4. D. Double your score
Answer: B. Should not replace the source information

Q20. The discussion task shows posts from two students. You should:

  1. A. Ignore them
  2. B. Engage with the discussion while making your own point clear
  3. C. Copy one of them
  4. D. Disagree with everyone rudely
Answer: B. Engage with the discussion while making your own point clear
