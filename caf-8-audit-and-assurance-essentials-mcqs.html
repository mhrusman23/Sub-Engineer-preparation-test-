Q1. The overall objective of an audit is to enhance the confidence of:

  1. A. The auditor only
  2. B. Staff only
  3. C. The tax authority only
  4. D. Intended users in the financial statements
Answer: D. Intended users in the financial statements

Q2. The auditor should maintain professional:

  1. A. Optimism
  2. B. Loyalty to management only
  3. C. Scepticism
  4. D. Secrecy
Answer: C. Scepticism

Q3. An engagement letter sets out the:

  1. A. Terms of the audit engagement
  2. B. Dividend policy
  3. C. Company's accounting policies
  4. D. Tax liability
Answer: A. Terms of the audit engagement

Q4. A walkthrough test is used to:

  1. A. Understand a system and its controls
  2. B. Count inventory
  3. C. Compute tax
  4. D. Prepare accounts
Answer: A. Understand a system and its controls

Q5. Audit sampling is used when the auditor:

  1. A. Tests all items
  2. B. Signs the accounts for management
  3. C. Prepares the accounts
  4. D. Tests less than 100% of items
Answer: D. Tests less than 100% of items

Q6. Inventory count attendance mainly addresses:

  1. A. Presentation
  2. B. Classification of debt
  3. C. Ownership of land
  4. D. Existence
Answer: D. Existence

Q7. A qualified opinion is issued when misstatements are material but:

  1. A. Not pervasive
  2. B. Absent
  3. C. Pervasive
  4. D. Corrected
Answer: A. Not pervasive

Q8. Auditor independence means being free from:

  1. A. Influences that compromise objectivity
  2. B. Any contact with clients
  3. C. All audit fees
  4. D. Using audit software
Answer: A. Influences that compromise objectivity

Q9. Direct confirmation from a bank provides evidence about:

  1. A. Product pricing
  2. B. Staff morale
  3. C. Inventory quality
  4. D. Bank balances
Answer: D. Bank balances

Q10. Analytical procedures are used to:

  1. A. Compare and evaluate financial relationships
  2. B. Sign accounts
  3. C. Replace all other procedures
  4. D. Count cash
Answer: A. Compare and evaluate financial relationships

Q11. Which of the following professional requirements relates primarily to the auditor's mindset during the execution of an audit?

  1. A. Professional skepticism
  2. B. Professional competence
  3. C. Confidentiality
  4. D. Integrity
Answer: A. Professional skepticism

Q12. What is the primary purpose of obtaining written representations from management near the end of the audit?

  1. A. To replace the need for substantive analytical procedures
  2. B. To confirm management's responsibility for the preparation of financial statements
  3. C. To calculate the final materiality threshold
  4. D. To determine the sample size for substantive testing
Answer: B. To confirm management's responsibility for the preparation of financial statements

Q13. Which of the following describes a test of controls rather than a substantive procedure?

  1. A. Vouching a sample of recorded sales invoices to shipping documents
  2. B. Sending positive balance confirmation requests to major trade debtors
  3. C. Inspecting purchase orders for evidence of proper management authorization
  4. D. Recalculating depreciation expense for the current financial year
Answer: C. Inspecting purchase orders for evidence of proper management authorization

Q14. When an auditor decides to use sampling, what is the primary risk associated with statistical sampling methods?

  1. A. The risk of human error during sample selection
  2. B. The risk that the sample does not reflect the entire population
  3. C. The risk that management will withhold requested documentation
  4. D. The risk that accounting software will generate incorrect trial balances
Answer: B. The risk that the sample does not reflect the entire population

Q15. Under what condition should an auditor express an adverse opinion on the financial statements?

  1. A. When the auditor is unable to obtain sufficient appropriate audit evidence due to a limitation in scope
  2. B. When misstatements, individually or in the aggregate, are both material and pervasive
  3. C. When there is a significant uncertainty regarding the entity's status as a going concern
  4. D. When the financial statements depart from a standard accounting framework without justification
Answer: B. When misstatements, individually or in the aggregate, are both material and pervasive

Q16. Which subsequent event review procedure is most effective for discovering unrecorded liabilities near year-end?

  1. A. Reviewing minutes of board of directors' meetings
  2. B. Examining cash disbursements made after the reporting period
  3. C. Confirming accounts receivable balances with major customers
  4. D. Performing ratio analysis on interim financial statements
Answer: B. Examining cash disbursements made after the reporting period

Q17. What primary type of assurance is provided by an external auditor in an audit of historical financial statements?

  1. A. Absolute assurance
  2. B. Reasonable assurance
  3. C. Negative assurance
  4. D. Limited assurance
Answer: B. Reasonable assurance

Q18. Which of the following procedures provides the most reliable external audit evidence?

  1. A. Oral inquiries made to the company's internal accounting staff
  2. B. Inspection of documents generated entirely within the client's organization
  3. C. A bank confirmation sent directly by the bank to the independent auditor
  4. D. Analytical comparisons of monthly operating expenses
Answer: C. A bank confirmation sent directly by the bank to the independent auditor

Q19. When is an auditor most likely to issue a disclaimer of opinion?

  1. A. When the financial statements contain material and pervasive misstatements
  2. B. When the auditor is unable to obtain sufficient appropriate audit evidence and the possible effects could be both material and pervasive
  3. C. When management refuses to correct a clearly trivial misstatement
  4. D. When there is a minor disagreement regarding the accounting policy for depreciation
Answer: B. When the auditor is unable to obtain sufficient appropriate audit evidence and the possible effects could be both material and pervasive

Q20. Which of the following best describes the concept of professional skepticism during an audit?

  1. A. Assuming management is dishonest in all financial matters
  2. B. An attitude that includes a questioning mind and a critical assessment of audit evidence
  3. C. Accepting all management representations without further verification
  4. D. Focusing exclusively on financial errors rather than potential fraud
Answer: B. An attitude that includes a questioning mind and a critical assessment of audit evidence

Q21. What is the main objective of evaluating control risk as part of assessing risks of material misstatement?

  1. A. To determine whether the financial statements comply with local tax laws
  2. B. To evaluate the effectiveness of the entity's internal control in preventing or detecting material misstatements
  3. C. To calculate the total audit fee for the engagement
  4. D. To replace the requirement for obtaining written representations from management
Answer: B. To evaluate the effectiveness of the entity's internal control in preventing or detecting material misstatements

Q22. What is the primary purpose of performing a walkthrough test during the planning phase of an audit?

  1. A. To calculate the final materiality threshold for the financial statements
  2. B. To trace a transaction through the information system from origin to final disposition to confirm understanding of controls
  3. C. To substitute for substantive analytical procedures on ending balances
  4. D. To physically count 100 percent of the entity's inventory items
Answer: B. To trace a transaction through the information system from origin to final disposition to confirm understanding of controls
