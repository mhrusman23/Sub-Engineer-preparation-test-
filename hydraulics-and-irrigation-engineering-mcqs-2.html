Q1. What is the standard freeboard provided in a canal section?

  1. A. 0.25 m to 0.5 m
  2. B. 0.5 m to 1.5 m
  3. C. 2.0 m to 3.0 m
  4. D. 3.5 m to 5.0 m
Answer: B. 0.5 m to 1.5 m

Q2. Which dimensionless parameter represents the ratio of inertial forces to gravity forces in open channel flow?

  1. A. Reynolds number
  2. B. Froude number
  3. C. Weber number
  4. D. Mach number
Answer: B. Froude number

Q3. What is the term for the sudden rise in water level that occurs when a gate in an open channel is suddenly closed?

  1. A. Water hammer
  2. B. Hydraulic jump
  3. C. Surge wave
  4. D. Critical depth
Answer: C. Surge wave

Q4. According to Bligh's Creep Theory, what does the hydraulic gradient depend on?

  1. A. Total head loss and total creep length
  2. B. Permeability of the foundation soil only
  3. C. Exit gradient at the downstream end
  4. D. Upstream water depth only
Answer: A. Total head loss and total creep length

Q5. Which irrigation method applies water in the form of a spray, similar to natural rainfall?

  1. A. Border strip method
  2. B. Furrow method
  3. C. Sprinkler method
  4. D. Basin method
Answer: C. Sprinkler method

Q6. What does Lacey's regime theory for stable channels primarily incorporate into its silt factor equation?

  1. A. Mean particle size of bed silt
  2. B. Channel slope and roughness
  3. C. Wetted perimeter and hydraulic radius
  4. D. Total seepage discharge
Answer: A. Mean particle size of bed silt

Q7. What is the primary purpose of providing an uplift pressure relief filter underneath the downstream floor of a weir?

  1. A. To increase the exit gradient
  2. B. To reduce the uplift force on the floor
  3. C. To prevent silt deposition in the pocket
  4. D. To increase the effective creep length
Answer: B. To reduce the uplift force on the floor

Q8. Which specific instrument is primarily utilized to measure the velocity of flowing water at a specific point in an open channel?

  1. A. Piezometer
  2. B. Current meter
  3. C. Venturi meter
  4. D. Manometer
Answer: B. Current meter

Q9. What is the primary function of a dividing wall in a diversion headworks structure?

  1. A. To increase the upstream water storage capacity
  2. B. To separate the under-sluices from the main weir or barrage body
  3. C. To prevent silt entry into the main canal completely
  4. D. To reduce the downstream exit gradient
Answer: B. To separate the under-sluices from the main weir or barrage body

Q10. Which non-dimensional number defines the ratio of inertial forces to viscous forces in open channel hydraulics?

  1. A. Froude number
  2. B. Reynolds number
  3. C. Weber number
  4. D. Mach number
Answer: B. Reynolds number

Q11. What does Kennedy's silt theory primarily utilize to determine the critical velocity of a stable channel?

  1. A. Bed slope
  2. B. Depth of flow
  3. C. Wetted perimeter
  4. D. Total discharge
Answer: B. Depth of flow

Q12. Which type of hydraulic jump is characterized by an initial Froude number ranging between 4.5 and 9.0, producing a very effective energy dissipator?

  1. A. Undular jump
  2. B. Weak jump
  3. C. Oscillating jump
  4. D. Steady jump
Answer: D. Steady jump

Q13. What is the term used to describe the total depth of water required by a crop from the time of sowing to the time of harvesting?

  1. A. Duty
  2. B. Delta
  3. C. Base period
  4. D. Intensity of irrigation
Answer: B. Delta

Q14. What is the primary function of a fish ladder provided at a weir or barrage?

  1. A. To regulate flood discharge
  2. B. To facilitate the upstream and downstream movement of fish
  3. C. To trap silt particles before entering the canal
  4. D. To measure the velocity of river flow
Answer: B. To facilitate the upstream and downstream movement of fish

Q15. In open channel flow, what term describes the condition where the specific energy is minimum for a given discharge?

  1. A. Subcritical flow
  2. B. Supercritical flow
  3. C. Critical flow
  4. D. Uniform flow
Answer: C. Critical flow

Q16. Which of the following irrigation methods has the highest overall water application efficiency?

  1. A. Border strip irrigation
  2. B. Furrow irrigation
  3. C. Drip irrigation
  4. D. Flooding method
Answer: C. Drip irrigation

Q17. What is the ratio of the volume of water stored in the root zone of the soil to the volume of water delivered to the field called?

  1. A. Water conveyance efficiency
  2. B. Water storage efficiency
  3. C. Water use efficiency
  4. D. Water distribution efficiency
Answer: B. Water storage efficiency
