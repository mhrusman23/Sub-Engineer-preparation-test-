Q1. The ratio of the ultimate bearing capacity of soil to the factor of safety is called:

  1. A. Safe bearing capacity
  2. B. Net bearing capacity
  3. C. Ultimate load capacity
  4. D. Allowable pressure
Answer: A. Safe bearing capacity

Q2. The property of soil due to which it undergoes a change in volume under applied load is called?

  1. A. Permeability
  2. B. Consolidation
  3. C. Capillarity
  4. D. Compaction
Answer: B. Consolidation

Q3. The minimum depth of foundation for structures in clayey soils is generally kept at:

  1. A. 0.5 meters
  2. B. 0.9 meters
  3. C. 1.5 meters
  4. D. 2.0 meters
Answer: B. 0.9 meters

Q4. The ratio of volume of voids to volume of solids is the:

  1. A. Porosity
  2. B. Degree of saturation
  3. C. Water content
  4. D. Void ratio
Answer: D. Void ratio

Q5. Water content is the ratio of weight of water to weight of:

  1. A. Air
  2. B. Total soil
  3. C. Solids
  4. D. Voids
Answer: C. Solids

Q6. The soil with the smallest particle size is:

  1. A. Gravel
  2. B. Clay
  3. C. Sand
  4. D. Silt
Answer: B. Clay

Q7. The permeability of soil is highest in:

  1. A. Peat
  2. B. Silt
  3. C. Gravel
  4. D. Clay
Answer: C. Gravel

Q8. The Standard Proctor test is used to determine:

  1. A. Permeability
  2. B. Shear strength
  3. C. Specific gravity
  4. D. Maximum dry density and optimum moisture content
Answer: D. Maximum dry density and optimum moisture content

Q9. The angle of internal friction is zero for:

  1. A. Gravel
  2. B. Rock
  3. C. Dry sand
  4. D. Pure clay in undrained condition
Answer: D. Pure clay in undrained condition

Q10. Consolidation of soil is the:

  1. A. Drying of soil
  2. B. Instant rise in volume
  3. C. Gradual reduction in volume under load
  4. D. Freezing of soil
Answer: C. Gradual reduction in volume under load

Q11. The Atterberg limits include liquid limit, plastic limit and:

  1. A. Yield limit
  2. B. Elastic limit
  3. C. Tensile limit
  4. D. Shrinkage limit
Answer: D. Shrinkage limit

Q12. Bearing capacity is the ability of soil to:

  1. A. Conduct heat
  2. B. Support loads without shear failure
  3. C. Hold water
  4. D. Dry quickly
Answer: B. Support loads without shear failure

Q13. The effective stress in soil equals total stress minus:

  1. A. Cohesion
  2. B. Overburden
  3. C. Pore water pressure
  4. D. Friction
Answer: C. Pore water pressure

Q14. The coefficient of curvature for a well-graded gravel soil lies between:

  1. A. 1 to 3
  2. B. 4 to 6
  3. C. 6 to 8
  4. D. 9 to 10
Answer: A. 1 to 3

Q15. Which of the following costs should be capitalized as part of the initial carrying amount of property, plant, and equipment under IAS 16?

  1. A. Initial operating losses incurred before the asset achieves planned performance
  2. B. Costs of conducting business in a new location
  3. C. Costs of site preparation and initial delivery and handling
  4. D. Administration and other general overhead costs
Answer: C. Costs of site preparation and initial delivery and handling

Q16. In capital budgeting, what does a discounted payback period explicitly incorporate that a standard payback period ignores?

  1. A. Initial investment size
  2. B. Project salvage value
  3. C. Time value of money
  4. D. Inflation adjustments
Answer: C. Time value of money

Q17. Which of the following is NOT a characteristic of a normally consolidated clay?

  1. A. High compressibility
  2. B. Low permeability
  3. C. High shear strength
  4. D. Large void ratio
Answer: C. High shear strength

Q18. In the Mohr–Coulomb failure criterion, the parameter that represents the resistance to shear at zero normal stress is called:

  1. A. Cohesion
  2. B. Friction angle
  3. C. Effective stress
  4. D. Void ratio
Answer: A. Cohesion

Q19. The coefficient of permeability for a well‑graded sandy soil is typically:

  1. A. 10⁻⁸ m/s
  2. B. 10⁻⁶ m/s
  3. C. 10⁻⁴ m/s
  4. D. 10⁻² m/s
Answer: D. 10⁻² m/s

Q20. During a standard Proctor compaction test, the maximum dry unit weight is achieved at a moisture content of approximately:

  1. A. 5%
  2. B. 10%
  3. C. 15%
  4. D. 20%
Answer: C. 15%

Q21. The settlement of a shallow foundation on a saturated clay layer is primarily governed by:

  1. A. Immediate elastic compression
  2. B. Time-dependent consolidation
  3. C. Lateral soil movement
  4. D. Thermal expansion
Answer: B. Time-dependent consolidation

Q22. Which of the following is NOT a characteristic of normally consolidated clay?

  1. A. High compressibility
  2. B. Low permeability
  3. C. Low shear strength
  4. D. High swelling potential
Answer: D. High swelling potential

Q23. A saturated sand layer of 2 m thickness has a specific gravity of 2.70 and a void ratio of 0.65. What hydraulic head would create a quick condition in this layer?

  1. A. 1.2 m
  2. B. 1.8 m
  3. C. 2.4 m
  4. D. 3.0 m
Answer: B. 1.8 m

Q24. In the Mohr–Coulomb failure criterion, the parameter that represents the shear strength of a dry soil at zero effective stress is called:

  1. A. Cohesion
  2. B. Angle of internal friction
  3. C. Effective stress
  4. D. Shear modulus
Answer: A. Cohesion

Q25. What is the void ratio of a soil whose volume of voids equals 0.6 times the volume of solids?

  1. A. 0.4
  2. B. 0.6
  3. C. 0.8
  4. D. 1.0
Answer: B. 0.6
