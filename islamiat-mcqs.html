Q1. The first revelation to the Holy Prophet (PBUH) came in the cave of:

  1. A. Uhud
  2. B. Hira
  3. C. Safa
  4. D. Thawr
Answer: B. Hira

Q2. The total number of surahs in the Holy Quran is:

  1. A. 99
  2. B. 114
  3. C. 120
  4. D. 110
Answer: B. 114

Q3. The first surah of the Holy Quran is:

  1. A. Al-Baqarah
  2. B. Al-Ikhlas
  3. C. Al-Nas
  4. D. Al-Fatihah
Answer: D. Al-Fatihah

Q4. The longest surah of the Holy Quran is:

  1. A. An-Nisa
  2. B. Al-Fatihah
  3. C. Al-Baqarah
  4. D. Al-Imran
Answer: C. Al-Baqarah

Q5. The number of pillars of Islam is:

  1. A. 6
  2. B. 5
  3. C. 4
  4. D. 7

Q6. The Hijrah of the Holy Prophet (PBUH) from Makkah was to:

  1. A. Habsha
  2. B. Jerusalem
  3. C. Taif

Q11. Which battle is referred to as 'Yaum al-Furqan' or the Day of Distinction in Islamic history?

  1. A. Battle of Badr
  2. B. Battle of Uhud

Q12. In which Islamic calendar month is the main event of Hajj, including the stay at Arafat, observed?

Q13. Who was appointed as the scribe to write down the Treaty of Hudaybiyyah on behalf of the Prophet (PBUH)?

Q14. What title was given to Hazrat Hamza (R.A) due to his unmatched bravery and martyrdom in battle?
