Q1. Without taxes, the Modigliani-Miller theory says capital structure:

  1. A. Always lowers value
  2. B. Always raises value
  3. C. Fixes dividends
  4. D. Does not affect firm value
Answer: D. Does not affect firm value

Q2. Which input does the Black-Scholes model use?

  1. A. Number of employees
  2. B. Dividend of the buyer
  3. C. Volatility of the underlying share
  4. D. Credit rating of the buyer
Answer: C. Volatility of the underlying share

Q3. In an interest rate swap, parties normally exchange:

  1. A. Fixed and floating interest payments
  2. B. Shares
  3. C. Principal amounts
  4. D. Currencies permanently
Answer: A. Fixed and floating interest payments

Q4. Interest rate parity links forward exchange rates to:

  1. A. Share prices
  2. B. Tax rates
  3. C. Interest rate differentials
  4. D. Inflation differentials only
Answer: C. Interest rate differentials

Q5. Purchasing power parity links exchange rate changes to:

  1. A. GDP only
  2. B. Interest rates only
  3. C. Tax rates
  4. D. Inflation differentials
Answer: D. Inflation differentials

Q6. Adjusted present value (APV) equals base case NPV plus the PV of:

  1. A. Depreciation
  2. B. Financing side effects
  3. C. Dividends
  4. D. Working capital
Answer: B. Financing side effects

Q7. Which risk can be removed by diversification?

  1. A. Unsystematic risk
  2. B. Systematic risk
  3. C. Inflation risk
  4. D. Market risk
Answer: A. Unsystematic risk

Q8. Duration is used to measure a bond's price sensitivity to:

  1. A. Credit ratings
  2. B. Dividend changes
  3. C. Interest rate changes
  4. D. Tax changes
Answer: C. Interest rate changes

Q9. In the Gordon growth model, share value equals D1 divided by:

  1. A. (Ke + g)
  2. B. Ke x g
  3. C. (Ke - g)
  4. D. g only
Answer: C. (Ke - g)

Q10. Value at Risk (VaR) estimates the:

  1. A. Potential loss at a given confidence level and period
  2. B. Average profit
  3. C. Total assets
  4. D. Exact future loss
Answer: A. Potential loss at a given confidence level and period

Q11. Which valuation technique explicitly incorporates the interest tax shield as a separate cash flow component?

  1. A. Dividend Valuation Model
  2. B. Adjusted Present Value Method
  3. C. Free Cash Flow to Equity Model
  4. D. Capital Asset Pricing Model
Answer: B. Adjusted Present Value Method

Q12. What is the primary objective of employing a zero-cost collar hedging strategy using currency options?

  1. A. To completely eliminate downside risk without any premium cost
  2. B. To lock in a fixed exchange rate with zero volatility
  3. C. To fund the purchase of a put option by selling a call option
  4. D. To eliminate all transaction costs in foreign trade
Answer: C. To fund the purchase of a put option by selling a call option

Q13. Which corporate restructuring method involves a parent company distributing shares of its subsidiary directly to its existing shareholders?

  1. A. Equity Carve-out
  2. B. Spin-off
  3. C. Management Buyout
  4. D. Liquidation
Answer: B. Spin-off

Q14. According to the Pecking Order Theory of capital structure, what is a firm's preferred source of financing for new projects?

  1. A. New ordinary equity
  2. B. Convertible bonds
  3. C. Retained earnings
  4. D. Long-term bank loans
Answer: C. Retained earnings

Q15. When applying the Black-Scholes option pricing model, what does an increase in the underlying asset's volatility generally do to the value of a European put option?

  1. A. Increases the value
  2. B. Decreases the value
  3. C. Has no effect on the value
  4. D. Sets the value to zero
Answer: A. Increases the value

Q16. Which type of economic risk refers to the potential impact of unexpected exchange rate fluctuations on the present value of a firm's future operating cash flows?

  1. A. Transaction risk
  2. B. Translation risk
  3. C. Economic risk
  4. D. Accounting risk
Answer: C. Economic risk

Q17. What is the primary objective of a treasury manager employing a currency collar strategy using options?

  1. A. To completely eliminate all forms of economic exposure indefinitely without any cost
  2. B. To lock in a guaranteed exchange rate while bypassing the need for currency options
  3. C. To protect against adverse exchange rate movements while limiting the cost by giving up favorable extreme movements
  4. D. To speculate on future currency appreciation for short-term trading gains
Answer: C. To protect against adverse exchange rate movements while limiting the cost by giving up favorable extreme movements

Q18. According to the pecking order theory of capital structure, managers prefer to finance new investments in which specific order of funding sources?

  1. A. New equity first, followed by debt, and retained earnings last
  2. B. Retained earnings first, followed by debt, and new equity as a last resort
  3. C. Debt first, followed by new equity, and retained earnings last
  4. D. New equity first, followed by retained earnings, and debt last
Answer: B. Retained earnings first, followed by debt, and new equity as a last resort

Q19. When an acquiring company is evaluating a target firm and needs to account for significant issue costs of new debt and tax shields on interest, which advanced appraisal method is most theoretically appropriate?

  1. A. Traditional Net Present Value using WACC
  2. B. Adjusted Present Value (APV)
  3. C. Internal Rate of Return (IRR)
  4. D. Dividend Valuation Model
Answer: B. Adjusted Present Value (APV)

Q20. When estimating a project's cost of capital for a new division in a different industry, what is the primary purpose of ungearing a proxy company's equity beta?

  1. A. To remove the financial risk distortion caused by the proxy company's specific capital structure
  2. B. To calculate the exact dividend growth rate of the target firm
  3. C. To determine the total market capitalization of the acquiring entity
  4. D. To eliminate systematic market risk from the asset beta
Answer: A. To remove the financial risk distortion caused by the proxy company's specific capital structure

Q21. Which of the following corporate restructuring techniques involves creating a completely separate, independent legal entity from a parent company by distributing shares proportionally to existing shareholders?

  1. A. Management Buy-out (MBO)
  2. B. Equity Carve-out
  3. C. Spin-off
  4. D. Liquidation
Answer: C. Spin-off

Q22. In the context of multinational treasury management, which exposure measures the long-term impact of unexpected currency fluctuations on the present value of a firm's future operating cash flows?

  1. A. Transaction exposure
  2. B. Translation exposure
  3. C. Economic exposure
  4. D. Accounting exposure
Answer: C. Economic exposure

Q23. Which of the following is considered a primary core syllabus area tested extensively within the ACCA Advanced Financial Management examination?

  1. A. Advanced Investment Appraisal
  2. B. Basic bookkeeping entries
  3. C. Individual income tax returns
  4. D. Manual ledger reconciliation
Answer: A. Advanced Investment Appraisal

Q24. When evaluating corporate reconstructions and reorganisations, senior financial advisors must assess the wealth transfer implications between equity holders and:

  1. A. Bond holders
  2. B. Auditors
  3. C. Suppliers
  4. D. Regulators
Answer: A. Bond holders

Q25. What is the primary role expected of a candidate passing the Advanced Financial Management exam when operating in a corporate environment?

  1. A. Senior financial advisor
  2. B. Junior bookkeeper
  3. C. Tax filing clerk
  4. D. External payroll officer
Answer: A. Senior financial advisor
