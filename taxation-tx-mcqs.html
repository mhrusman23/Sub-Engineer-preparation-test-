Q1. Deliberately hiding income from the tax authority is:

  1. A. Tax avoidance
  2. B. Tax evasion
  3. C. Tax mitigation
  4. D. Tax planning
Answer: B. Tax evasion

Q2. Which of these is a direct tax?

  1. A. Customs duty
  2. B. VAT
  3. C. Sales tax
  4. D. Income tax
Answer: D. Income tax

Q3. A tax whose rate rises as income rises is called:

  1. A. Proportional
  2. B. Progressive
  3. C. Regressive
  4. D. Flat
Answer: B. Progressive

Q4. Which is one of Adam Smith's canons of taxation?

  1. A. Complexity
  2. B. Secrecy
  3. C. Volatility
  4. D. Certainty
Answer: D. Certainty

Q5. Withholding tax is a tax that is:

  1. A. Charged on assets only
  2. B. Paid only by employers
  3. C. Refunded automatically
  4. D. Deducted at source before payment
Answer: D. Deducted at source before payment

Q6. VAT is charged on the:

  1. A. Property value
  2. B. Value added at each stage of production
  3. C. Wages only
  4. D. Total profit only
Answer: B. Value added at each stage of production

Q7. A capital gain is generally calculated as proceeds less:

  1. A. Cost (allowable)
  2. B. Turnover
  3. C. Market value
  4. D. Depreciation
Answer: A. Cost (allowable)

Q8. Tax residence mainly determines:

  1. A. The colour of tax forms
  2. B. Which country can tax worldwide income
  3. C. The tax year length
  4. D. The audit fee
Answer: B. Which country can tax worldwide income

Q9. Using the tax rules legally to reduce a tax bill is:

  1. A. Smuggling
  2. B. Tax avoidance
  3. C. Tax evasion
  4. D. Fraud
Answer: B. Tax avoidance

Q10. An indirect tax is usually:

  1. A. Paid directly on salary
  2. B. Paid only by companies
  3. C. Collected by an intermediary from the consumer
  4. D. Charged on dividends only
Answer: C. Collected by an intermediary from the consumer

Q11. Which of the following describes the statutory mechanism where a UK resident individual transfers income-generating assets to a non-resident person while retaining the power to enjoy that income?

  1. A. Transfer of assets abroad provisions
  2. B. Controlled foreign company rules
  3. C. Personal portfolio bond legislation
  4. D. Settlements legislation for minor children
Answer: A. Transfer of assets abroad provisions

Q12. What is the primary tax treatment of trading losses incurred in the final tax year of a UK unincorporated sole trader business?

  1. A. They can only be carried forward against future trading profits of the same trade
  2. B. They can be carried back against total taxable income of the final tax year and the preceding three tax years
  3. C. They are permanently lost upon cessation of trade
  4. D. They must be offset exclusively against capital gains of the final year
Answer: B. They can be carried back against total taxable income of the final tax year and the preceding three tax years

Q13. Under UK corporation tax rules, which expenditure qualifies for capital allowances under the Structures and Buildings Allowance (SBA)?

  1. A. Acquisition cost of commercial land
  2. B. Cost of constructing a new commercial office building
  3. C. Expenditure on plant and machinery integral to a building
  4. D. Cost of repairing an existing factory roof
Answer: B. Cost of constructing a new commercial office building

Q14. For Value Added Tax (VAT) purposes, what is the default tax point when goods are removed from a warehouse before a tax invoice is issued?

  1. A. The date the goods are physically delivered
  2. B. The date payment is received by the supplier
  3. C. The basic tax point being the date of removal
  4. D. The date the VAT return is submitted
Answer: C. The basic tax point being the date of removal

Q15. How are qualifying charitable donations (gift aid payments) treated when calculating an individual's adjusted net income for high income child benefit charge purposes?

  1. A. They are ignored entirely
  2. B. They are deducted grossed up at the basic rate of tax
  3. C. They are added directly to total taxable income
  4. D. They are deducted at the exact net amount paid
Answer: B. They are deducted grossed up at the basic rate of tax

Q16. When a company is classified as a close company for UK corporation tax purposes, what specific tax implication arises concerning loans made to participators?

  1. A. The loan is treated as a taxable trading receipt
  2. B. A temporary tax charge under section 455 is levied on the company
  3. C. The participator receives a tax-free distribution
  4. D. Corporation tax rates are automatically doubled
Answer: B. A temporary tax charge under section 455 is levied on the company

Q17. Which of the following items is strictly disallowed when computing a company's tax-adjusted trading profit?

  1. A. Staff salaries and bonuses
  2. B. Depreciation of non-current assets
  3. C. Regular business electricity bills
  4. D. Cost of goods sold for resale
Answer: B. Depreciation of non-current assets
