Q1. The word 'enormous' is closest in meaning to:

  1. A. Tiny
  2. B. Very large
  3. C. Expensive
  4. D. Ancient
Answer: B. Very large

Q2. 'Rapid' means:

  1. A. Slow
  2. B. Fast
  3. C. Careful
  4. D. Weak
Answer: B. Fast

Q3. An 'advantage' is the opposite of:

  1. A. Benefit
  2. B. Disadvantage
  3. C. Profit
  4. D. Chance
Answer: B. Disadvantage

Q4. 'To reduce' means to:

  1. A. Increase
  2. B. Make smaller
  3. C. Repeat
  4. D. Hide
Answer: B. Make smaller

Q5. 'Essential' means:

  1. A. Unnecessary
  2. B. Very important and needed
  3. C. Expensive
  4. D. Rare
Answer: B. Very important and needed

Q6. A 'consequence' is:

  1. A. A cause
  2. B. A result
  3. C. A promise
  4. D. A question
Answer: B. A result

Q7. 'Approximately' means:

  1. A. Exactly
  2. B. About / roughly
  3. C. Never
  4. D. Always
Answer: B. About / roughly

Q8. 'Significant' is closest to:

  1. A. Small
  2. B. Important or large in amount
  3. C. Silent
  4. D. Sudden
Answer: B. Important or large in amount

Q9. To 'achieve' a goal means to:

  1. A. Give it up
  2. B. Reach it successfully
  3. C. Describe it
  4. D. Forget it
Answer: B. Reach it successfully

Q10. 'Available' means:

  1. A. Sold out
  2. B. Ready for use
  3. C. Broken
  4. D. Hidden
Answer: B. Ready for use

Q11. Which of the following best defines the word 'ambiguous'?

  1. A. Very clear and direct
  2. B. Open to multiple interpretations
  3. C. Completely incorrect
  4. D. Highly dangerous
Answer: B. Open to multiple interpretations

Q12. What is the primary meaning of 'mitigate'?

  1. A. To make worse or more severe
  2. B. To lessen the severity or seriousness of something
  3. C. To investigate thoroughly
  4. D. To delay indefinitely
Answer: B. To lessen the severity or seriousness of something

Q13. The term 'ubiquitous' is best used to describe something that is:

  1. A. Rare and hard to find
  2. B. Extremely old
  3. C. Present, appearing, or found everywhere
  4. D. Hidden from public view
Answer: C. Present, appearing, or found everywhere

Q14. What does the word 'feasible' mean in a professional context?

  1. A. Impossible to complete
  2. B. Too expensive to attempt
  3. C. Possible to do easily or conveniently
  4. D. Required by law
Answer: C. Possible to do easily or conveniently

Q15. Choose the correct synonym for 'deteriorate':

  1. A. To improve steadily
  2. B. To become progressively worse
  3. C. To remain constant
  4. D. To expand rapidly
Answer: B. To become progressively worse

Q16. What does the word 'meticulous' imply about a person's work?

  1. A. Done with extreme care and precision
  2. B. Done in a careless or hasty manner
  3. C. Completed without any planning
  4. D. Created using recycled materials
Answer: A. Done with extreme care and precision

Q17. What is the closest synonym for the word 'lucid'?

  1. A. Confusing
  2. B. Clear
  3. C. Bright
  4. D. Dense
Answer: B. Clear

Q18. Which of the following best defines the word 'obsolete'?

  1. A. Brand new
  2. B. Very expensive
  3. C. No longer in use
  4. D. Highly popular
Answer: C. No longer in use

Q19. Choose the most appropriate antonym for the word 'generous':

  1. A. Stingy
  2. B. Kind
  3. C. Wealthy
  4. D. Helpful
Answer: A. Stingy

Q20. What does the word 'ephemeral' mean?

  1. A. Lasting forever
  2. B. Extremely heavy
  3. C. Lasting for a very short time
  4. D. Very bright in color
Answer: C. Lasting for a very short time

Q21. What is the primary meaning of the word 'alleviate'?

  1. A. To increase severity
  2. B. To make suffering less severe
  3. C. To cause a delay
  4. D. To completely destroy
Answer: B. To make suffering less severe

Q22. Which term best describes a person who is reserved and keeps to themselves?

  1. A. Extrovert
  2. B. Introvert
  3. C. Altruist
  4. D. Optimist
Answer: B. Introvert

Q23. A project deadline represents:

  1. A. The starting date of an assignment
  2. B. The final time limit by which work must be completed
  3. C. The budget allocation for a task
  4. D. The meeting agenda for stakeholders
Answer: B. The final time limit by which work must be completed

Q24. An 'itinerary' in travel planning refers to:

  1. A. A detailed travel plan or schedule
  2. B. A request for financial refund
  3. C. A baggage weight receipt
  4. D. A passport renewal form
Answer: A. A detailed travel plan or schedule

Q25. The term 'sustainability' in environmental contexts most closely relates to:

  1. A. Rapid industrial expansion
  2. B. Resource management without depleting future supplies
  3. C. Permanent urban construction
  4. D. Complete economic isolation
Answer: B. Resource management without depleting future supplies
