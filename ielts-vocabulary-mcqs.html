IELTS Vocabulary MCQs (IELTS & English Tests)

Free MCQs with answers for IELTS Vocabulary. 30 questions in 2 chapter tests are in the app; the first 25 are shown here.

Q1. The word 'enormous' is closest in meaning to:

  1. A. Tiny
  2. B. Very large
  3. C. Expensive
  4. D. Ancient
Answer: B. Very large

Q2. 'Rapid' means:

  1. A. Slow
  2. B. Fast
  3. C. Careful
  4. D. Weak
Answer: B. Fast

Q3. An 'advantage' is the opposite of:

  1. A. Benefit
  2. B. Disadvantage
  3. C. Profit
  4. D. Chance
Answer: B. Disadvantage

Q4. 'To reduce' means to:

  1. A. Increase
  2. B. Make smaller
  3. C. Repeat
  4. D. Hide
Answer: B. Make smaller

Q5. 'Essential' means:

  1. A. Unnecessary
  2. B. Very important and needed
  3. C. Expensive
  4. D. Rare
Answer: B. Very important and needed

Q6. A 'consequence' is:

  1. A. A cause
  2. B. A result
  3. C. A promise
  4. D. A question
Answer: B. A result

Q7. 'Approximately' means:

  1. A. Exactly
  2. B. About / roughly
  3. C. Never
  4. D. Always
Answer: B. About / roughly

Q8. 'Significant' is closest to:

  1. A. Small
  2. B. Important or large in amount
  3. C. Silent
  4. D. Sudden
Answer: B. Important or large in amount

Q9. To 'achieve' a goal means to:

  1. A. Give it up
  2. B. Reach it successfully
  3. C. Describe it
  4. D. Forget it
Answer: B. Reach it successfully

Q10. 'Available' means:

  1. A. Sold out
  2. B. Ready for use
  3. C. Broken
  4. D. Hidden
Answer: B. Ready for use

Q11. 'Generate' means:

  1. A. To destroy
  2. B. To produce or create
  3. C. To measure
  4. D. To hide
Answer: B. To produce or create

Q12. A 'trend' is:

  1. A. A sudden stop
  2. B. A general direction of change
  3. C. A mistake
  4. D. A rule
Answer: B. A general direction of change

Q13. 'Domestic' refers to:

  1. A. Foreign things
  2. B. Things inside one's own country or home
  3. C. Wild animals
  4. D. Imports
Answer: B. Things inside one's own country or home

Q14. To 'contribute' means to:

  1. A. Take away
  2. B. Give or add something (help, money, ideas)
  3. C. Argue
  4. D. Compete
Answer: B. Give or add something (help, money, ideas)

Q15. 'Complex' means:

  1. A. Simple
  2. B. Complicated, with many parts
  3. C. Cheap
  4. D. Fast
Answer: B. Complicated, with many parts

Q16. 'Initial' means:

  1. A. Final
  2. B. First / at the beginning
  3. C. Secret
  4. D. Huge
Answer: B. First / at the beginning

Q17. A 'factor' is:

  1. A. A result
  2. B. Something that influences a result
  3. C. A machine
  4. D. A person
Answer: B. Something that influences a result

Q18. 'Majority' means:

  1. A. Less than half
  2. B. More than half
  3. C. Exactly half
  4. D. None
Answer: B. More than half

Q19. To 'occur' means to:

  1. A. Prevent
  2. B. Happen
  3. C. Decide
  4. D. Grow
Answer: B. Happen

Q20. 'Require' means:

  1. A. To offer
  2. B. To need something as necessary
  3. C. To refuse
  4. D. To enjoy
Answer: B. To need something as necessary

Q21. 'Survey' (noun) is:

  1. A. A type of exam
  2. B. A study that asks people questions to gather information
  3. C. A building
  4. D. A journey
Answer: B. A study that asks people questions to gather information

Q22. 'Income' is:

  1. A. Money spent
  2. B. Money earned
  3. C. Tax form
  4. D. A bank
Answer: B. Money earned

Q23. 'Expand' means:

  1. A. To shrink
  2. B. To become larger
  3. C. To freeze
  4. D. To end
Answer: B. To become larger

Q24. A 'benefit' is:

  1. A. A problem
  2. B. An advantage or helpful effect
  3. C. A cost
  4. D. A rule
Answer: B. An advantage or helpful effect

Q25. 'Maintain' means:

  1. A. To destroy
  2. B. To keep something in good condition / continue
  3. C. To begin
  4. D. To sell
Answer: B. To keep something in good condition / continue
