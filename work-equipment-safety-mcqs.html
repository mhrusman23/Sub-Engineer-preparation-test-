Q1. PUWER mainly requires that work equipment is:

  1. A. New
  2. B. Suitable, maintained and inspected
  3. C. Expensive
  4. D. Painted
Answer: B. Suitable, maintained and inspected

Q2. Machine guards should be:

  1. A. Removed for speed
  2. B. Securely fixed and not easily bypassed
  3. C. Made of paper
  4. D. Optional
Answer: B. Securely fixed and not easily bypassed

Q3. An emergency stop control should be:

  1. A. Hidden
  2. B. Easily reached and clearly marked
  3. C. Painted black
  4. D. Locked away
Answer: B. Easily reached and clearly marked

Q4. Before maintenance, equipment should be:

  1. A. Running
  2. B. Isolated and locked off (lockout/tagout)
  3. C. Cleaned only
  4. D. Moved outside
Answer: B. Isolated and locked off (lockout/tagout)

Q5. Only trained and authorised workers should:

  1. A. Clean the office
  2. B. Operate dangerous machinery
  3. C. Answer phones
  4. D. Open windows
Answer: B. Operate dangerous machinery

Q6. A pre-use check of equipment helps to:

  1. A. Waste time
  2. B. Find defects before someone is hurt
  3. C. Increase wear
  4. D. Avoid inspections
Answer: B. Find defects before someone is hurt

Q7. The danger zone of a machine is where:

  1. A. Tools are stored
  2. B. A person could contact moving parts
  3. C. The switch is
  4. D. Guards are kept
Answer: B. A person could contact moving parts

Q8. Defective equipment should be:

  1. A. Used carefully
  2. B. Taken out of service and reported
  3. C. Sold
  4. D. Hidden
Answer: B. Taken out of service and reported

Q9. Guards on a circular saw include:

  1. A. A riving knife and top guard
  2. B. A cloth cover
  3. C. A warning letter
  4. D. Nothing
Answer: A. A riving knife and top guard

Q10. Inspection records for equipment should be:

  1. A. Thrown away
  2. B. Kept and available
  3. C. Kept at home
  4. D. Never written
Answer: B. Kept and available
