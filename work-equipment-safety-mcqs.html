Q1. PUWER mainly requires that work equipment is:

  1. A. New
  2. B. Suitable, maintained and inspected
  3. C. Expensive
  4. D. Painted
Answer: B. Suitable, maintained and inspected

Q2. Machine guards should be:

  1. A. Removed for speed
  2. B. Securely fixed and not easily bypassed
  3. C. Made of paper
  4. D. Optional
Answer: B. Securely fixed and not easily bypassed

Q3. An emergency stop control should be:

  1. A. Hidden
  2. B. Easily reached and clearly marked
  3. C. Painted black
  4. D. Locked away
Answer: B. Easily reached and clearly marked

Q4. Before maintenance, equipment should be:

  1. A. Running
  2. B. Isolated and locked off (lockout/tagout)
  3. C. Cleaned only
  4. D. Moved outside
Answer: B. Isolated and locked off (lockout/tagout)

Q5. Only trained and authorised workers should:

  1. A. Clean the office
  2. B. Operate dangerous machinery
  3. C. Answer phones
  4. D. Open windows
Answer: B. Operate dangerous machinery

Q6. A pre-use check of equipment helps to:

  1. A. Waste time
  2. B. Find defects before someone is hurt
  3. C. Increase wear
  4. D. Avoid inspections
Answer: B. Find defects before someone is hurt

Q7. The danger zone of a machine is where:

  1. A. Tools are stored
  2. B. A person could contact moving parts
  3. C. The switch is
  4. D. Guards are kept
Answer: B. A person could contact moving parts

Q8. Defective equipment should be:

  1. A. Used carefully
  2. B. Taken out of service and reported
  3. C. Sold
  4. D. Hidden
Answer: B. Taken out of service and reported

Q9. Guards on a circular saw include:

  1. A. A riving knife and top guard
  2. B. A cloth cover
  3. C. A warning letter
  4. D. Nothing
Answer: A. A riving knife and top guard

Q10. Inspection records for equipment should be:

  1. A. Thrown away
  2. B. Kept and available
  3. C. Kept at home
  4. D. Never written
Answer: B. Kept and available

Q11. What is the primary purpose of applying Lockout Tagout (LOTO) procedures before servicing machinery?

  1. A. To speed up the cleaning process
  2. B. to prevent accidental release of hazardous energy
  3. C. to reduce electrical power bills
  4. D. to test the backup generator
Answer: B. to prevent accidental release of hazardous energy

Q12. When a crane is operating within its safe working load limit, which environmental factor can still make the lifting operation unsafe?

  1. A. Strong and gusty winds
  2. B. bright morning sunlight
  3. C. dry ambient air
  4. D. low ground humidity
Answer: A. Strong and gusty winds

Q13. Which of the following best describes an unsafe condition in a workplace environment?

  1. A. Not wearing safety glasses while grinding
  2. B. taking shortcuts across a forklift lane
  3. C. a damaged ladder with a cracked rung
  4. D. skipping safety briefings intentionally
Answer: C. a damaged ladder with a cracked rung

Q14. According to machinery safety standards, what is a characteristic requirement for safety control devices?

  1. A. They must be painted completely black
  2. B. they should be clearly visible and easily identifiable
  3. C. they must be hidden inside the control panel
  4. D. they should require two keys to operate
Answer: B. they should be clearly visible and easily identifiable

Q15. What mechanical hazard category is associated with a moving machine part pulling a worker's loose clothing or hair into the mechanism?

  1. A. Entanglement
  2. B. electrocution
  3. C. radiation
  4. D. asphyxiation
Answer: A. Entanglement

Q16. What is the primary role of a hold-to-run control device on industrial machinery?

  1. A. To increase the operating speed automatically
  2. B. To stop the machine immediately when the operator releases the actuator
  3. C. To lock the control panel permanently
  4. D. To bypass all safety interlocks during maintenance
Answer: B. To stop the machine immediately when the operator releases the actuator

Q17. Which safety device prevents a machine from starting while its protective guard is open?

  1. A. Interlock safety switch
  2. B. Emergency pull wire
  3. C. Pressure relief valve
  4. D. Thermal overload relay
Answer: A. Interlock safety switch

Q18. Why are fixed physical guards generally preferred over movable interlocking guards?

  1. A. They allow faster access to internal parts
  2. B. They have fewer mechanical or electrical parts that can fail
  3. C. They are completely transparent
  4. D. They can be easily removed without tools
Answer: B. They have fewer mechanical or electrical parts that can fail

Q19. What hazard is most commonly created by rotating shafts and coupling mechanisms?

  1. A. Entanglement and drawing-in
  2. B. High voltage electric shock
  3. C. Chemical burns
  4. D. Toxic gas inhalation
Answer: A. Entanglement and drawing-in

Q20. What is the main purpose of conducting statutory thorough examinations on lifting equipment?

  1. A. To clean the external surfaces
  2. B. To test paint quality
  3. C. To detect hidden structural defects and wear before failure
  4. D. To calibrate the engine fuel efficiency
Answer: C. To detect hidden structural defects and wear before failure

Q21. When assessing risks related to hand-held power tools, what is an important safe operating practice?

  1. A. Carrying the tool by its electrical cord
  2. B. Disconnecting power sources before changing cutting accessories
  3. C. Disabling safety triggers to speed up work
  4. D. Using damaged bits to save replacement costs
Answer: B. Disconnecting power sources before changing cutting accessories

Q22. Why are trip devices such as pressure-sensitive mats or trip wires utilized around heavy machinery?

  1. A. To measure the exact weight of the manufactured goods
  2. B. To automatically stop the equipment when a person enters a hazardous area
  3. C. To track the daily attendance of floor operators
  4. D. To provide continuous illumination in dark workspaces
Answer: B. To automatically stop the equipment when a person enters a hazardous area

Q23. Who is legally and primarily permitted to operate specialized, high-risk industrial lifting equipment?

  1. A. Any general employee who finishes their shift early
  2. B. Only workers who have received specific, formal training and authorization
  3. C. Visitors touring the manufacturing facility
  4. D. Maintenance workers from an unrelated department
Answer: B. Only workers who have received specific, formal training and authorization

Q24. Which action is most critical before beginning any repair or maintenance work on a powered conveyor system?

  1. A. Isolate the power supply and apply Lockout Tagout (LOTO)
  2. B. Increase the belt speed to clear residual materials
  3. C. Notify the building supervisor via email
  4. D. Clean the exterior casing with a damp cloth
Answer: A. Isolate the power supply and apply Lockout Tagout (LOTO)

Q25. What is the primary danger associated with entanglement hazards around rotating drive shafts?

  1. A. Loud operational noise leading to hearing loss
  2. B. Loose clothing or hair getting caught and pulling the worker into the machine
  3. C. Frequent thermal overheating of the motor
  4. D. Sudden electrical short circuits due to friction
Answer: B. Loose clothing or hair getting caught and pulling the worker into the machine
