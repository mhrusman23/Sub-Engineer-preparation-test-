Q1. The law of contract in Pakistan is mainly contained in the:

  1. A. Partnership Act, 1932
  2. B. Sale of Goods Act, 1930 only
  3. C. Contract Act, 1872
  4. D. Companies Act, 2017
Answer: C. Contract Act, 1872

Q2. The sale of goods in Pakistan is governed by the:

  1. A. Contract Act, 1872 only
  2. B. Sale of Goods Act, 1930
  3. C. Companies Act, 2017
  4. D. Arbitration Act only
Answer: B. Sale of Goods Act, 1930

Q3. A valid contract requires free consent and:

  1. A. Lawful consideration
  2. B. A stamp always
  3. C. Two witnesses always
  4. D. A written deed always
Answer: A. Lawful consideration

Q4. Partnerships in Pakistan are governed by the:

  1. A. Contract Act only
  2. B. Sale of Goods Act
  3. C. Partnership Act, 1932
  4. D. Banking Ordinance
Answer: C. Partnership Act, 1932

Q5. Cheques and promissory notes are governed by the:

  1. A. Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881
  2. B. Companies Act, 2017
  3. C. Income Tax Ordinance, 2001
  4. D. Partnership Act, 1932
Answer: A. Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881

Q6. An agreement enforceable by law is a:

  1. A. Promise only
  2. B. Proposal
  3. C. Invitation
  4. D. Contract
Answer: D. Contract

Q7. A contract with a minor is generally:

  1. A. Voidable at the minor's option only
  2. B. Valid
  3. C. Always enforceable
  4. D. Void
Answer: D. Void

Q8. Coercion, undue influence, fraud and misrepresentation affect the:

  1. A. Language of contract
  2. B. Place of contract
  3. C. Free consent of the parties
  4. D. Stamp duty
Answer: C. Free consent of the parties

Q9. In sale of goods, the 'condition' is a stipulation that is:

  1. A. Optional
  2. B. Essential to the main purpose of the contract
  3. C. Irrelevant
  4. D. Minor and collateral
Answer: B. Essential to the main purpose of the contract

Q10. An offer when accepted becomes a:

  1. A. Warranty
  2. B. Promise
  3. C. Guarantee
  4. D. Condition
Answer: B. Promise

Q11. Which specific legal remedy allows a party to ask the court to compel the exact performance of a promise rather than awarding damages?

  1. A. Quantum Meruit
  2. B. Injunction
  3. C. Specific Performance
  4. D. Rescission
Answer: C. Specific Performance

Q12. Under the Contract Act, what is the legal status of an agreement made without any lawful consideration?

  1. A. Valid
  2. B. Void
  3. C. Voidable
  4. D. Illegal
Answer: B. Void

Q13. When goods are delivered to a buyer on approval or 'on sale or return' basis, when does the property in the goods pass to the buyer?

  1. A. At the time of physical delivery
  2. B. When the buyer signifies approval or does any other act adopting the transaction
  3. C. Only after the full price is paid in cash
  4. D. After exactly thirty days from delivery
Answer: B. When the buyer signifies approval or does any other act adopting the transaction

Q14. What is the primary liability of a surety under a contract of guarantee upon default by the principal debtor?

  1. A. Secondary and contingent only
  2. B. Co-extensive with that of the principal debtor unless provided otherwise
  3. C. Limited strictly to fifty percent of the total debt
  4. D. Exempted until all legal action against the debtor fails
Answer: B. Co-extensive with that of the principal debtor unless provided otherwise

Q15. In the context of negotiable instruments, what term is used when the maker or holder signs their name on the back or face of an instrument for the purpose of negotiation?

  1. A. Acceptance
  2. B. Endorsement
  3. C. Dishonour
  4. D. Cession
Answer: B. Endorsement

Q16. What is the legal effect of the retirement of a partner on their liability for acts done by the firm prior to their retirement?

  1. A. Automatically absolved from all past liabilities
  2. B. Remains liable for past debts unless discharged by a valid agreement with creditors and partners
  3. C. Liable only if the registrar of firms issues a public notice
  4. D. Transfers past liabilities entirely to incoming partners
Answer: B. Remains liable for past debts unless discharged by a valid agreement with creditors and partners

Q17. Which legal term describes obligations resembling those created by contract, imposed by law in the absence of an actual agreement to prevent unjust enrichment?

  1. A. Quasi-contract
  2. B. Implied contract
  3. C. Unilateral contract
  4. D. Contingent agreement
Answer: A. Quasi-contract

Q18. Which specific legal agreement is contingent upon the happening or non-happening of a future uncertain event collateral to such contract?

  1. A. Wagering agreement
  2. B. Contingent contract
  3. C. Voidable agreement
  4. D. Quasi-contract
Answer: B. Contingent contract

Q19. In the context of corporate governance under the Companies Act, what is the minimum frequency required for holding annual general meetings (AGMs) for a listed company?

  1. A. Every quarter
  2. B. Once every six months
  3. C. Once in every calendar year
  4. D. Every three years
Answer: C. Once in every calendar year

Q20. What is the primary effect on a partnership firm when a new partner is introduced with the consent of all existing partners?

  1. A. Dissolution of the entire existing firm automatically
  2. B. Formation of a new legal corporate entity
  3. C. Modification of the existing partnership relation among the continuing partners
  4. D. Conversion into a sole proprietorship
Answer: C. Modification of the existing partnership relation among the continuing partners

Q21. What is the primary legal consequence under the Contract Act if a party fails to perform their promise by the designated time, and time was expressly designated as the essence of the contract?

  1. A. The contract becomes automatically enforceable in perpetuity
  2. B. The contract becomes voidable at the option of the injured party
  3. C. The defaulting party gains additional time by default
  4. D. The contract is converted into a wagering agreement
Answer: B. The contract becomes voidable at the option of the injured party

Q22. Under the Partnership Act, what is the extent of liability of an individual partner for the debts and obligations of the firm incurred during their tenure?

  1. A. Limited strictly to their capital contribution
  2. B. Limited only to personal non-business assets
  3. C. Joint and several liability
  4. D. Exempt from all third-party claims
Answer: C. Joint and several liability

Q23. Which specific document sets out the internal management regulations and rules of a registered company in Pakistan?

  1. A. Prospectus
  2. B. Articles of Association
  3. C. Memorandum of Association
  4. D. Statutory Report
Answer: B. Articles of Association

Q24. Under the Contract Act 1872, what legal classification applies to an agreement that is enforceable by law at the option of one party only?

  1. A. Void agreement
  2. B. Illegal contract
  3. C. Voidable contract
  4. D. Valid contract
Answer: C. Voidable contract

Q25. In the context of the Sale of Goods Act, transferring ownership of specific goods from the seller to the buyer immediately upon contract formation is known as what?

  1. A. Delivery
  2. B. Pledge
  3. C. Sale
  4. D. Agreement to sell
Answer: C. Sale
