Q1. Nearly Liter of water per bag of cement is required.......

  1. A. 30
  2. B. 25
  3. C. 35
  4. D. 28
Answer: D. 28

Q2. The raw material manufacturing of glass is.......

  1. A. potash
  2. B. Silica
  3. C. lime
  4. D. all of these
Answer: D. all of these

Q3. Thickness of D.P.C layer of concrete (1:2:4) ....

  1. A. 1"
  2. B. 2"
  3. C. 3'’
  4. D. 1.5"
Answer: D. 1.5"

Q4. Making the surface rough before finishing the surface is termed as....

  1. A. dobbing
  2. B. rendering
  3. C. hacking
  4. D. Back ground
Answer: C. hacking

Q5. Revolved section is also called....

  1. A. Rolled section
  2. B. Half section
  3. C. partial section
  4. D. full section
Answer: A. Rolled section

Q6. Minimum size of kitchen......

  2. B. front view
  3. C. side view
  4. D. all of these
Answer: D. all of these

Q8. Writing on drawing sheet is called....

  1. A. Writing
  2. B. Lettering
  3. C. Numbering
  4. D. Sketching

Q9. Boundary lines of still water in lake shows....

  1. A. Horizontal surface
  2. B. Level surface
  3. C. vertical surface

Answer: D. Contour line

Q10. Leveling, centering and orientation of plane table is called as......

  1. A. radiation
  2. B. Setting
  3. C. intersection
  4. D. centering

Q11. Bench mark established at the end of days leveling work is called......

  1. A. Permanent B.M
  2. B. GTS B.M
  3. C. Arbitrary
  4. D. Temporary B.M

Q12. The method of surveying in which the field work and plotting is done simultaneously is Called....

  1. A. Plane table survey

  3. C. Tachometry survey

Q13. The specification not included in departmental standard specification is called.........

Q14. More accurate method of calculating earth work is........

  1. A. mean area

Q15. A table showing quantity of materials required for a particular work is called........
