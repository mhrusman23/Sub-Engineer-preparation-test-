Heat Transfer MCQs (Mechanical Engineering)

Free MCQs with answers for Heat Transfer. 16 questions in 2 chapter tests are in the app; all are shown here.

Q1. Heat transfer by direct molecular contact is:

  1. A. Convection
  2. B. Conduction
  3. C. Evaporation
  4. D. Radiation
Answer: B. Conduction

Q2. Heat transfer through a vacuum occurs by:

  1. A. Radiation
  2. B. Diffusion
  3. C. Conduction
  4. D. Convection
Answer: A. Radiation

Q3. Fourier's law relates to:

  1. A. Mass transfer
  2. B. Conduction
  3. C. Convection
  4. D. Radiation
Answer: B. Conduction

Q4. Newton's law of cooling relates to:

  1. A. Radiation
  2. B. Conduction
  3. C. Convection
  4. D. Fusion
Answer: C. Convection

Q5. Stefan-Boltzmann law states that radiation is proportional to:

  1. A. Square of temperature
  2. B. Fourth power of absolute temperature
  3. C. Temperature
  4. D. Cube of temperature
Answer: B. Fourth power of absolute temperature

Q6. The SI unit of thermal conductivity is:

  1. A. W/m.K
  2. B. J/kg.K
  3. C. W/m2
  4. D. J/K
Answer: A. W/m.K

Q7. A good insulator has:

  1. A. High thermal conductivity
  2. B. High density only
  3. C. High melting point only
  4. D. Low thermal conductivity
Answer: D. Low thermal conductivity

Q8. A fin is used to:

  1. A. Reduce heat area
  2. B. Increase mass
  3. C. Increase heat transfer area
  4. D. Store heat
Answer: C. Increase heat transfer area

Q9. The Prandtl number is the ratio of momentum diffusivity to:

  1. A. Thermal diffusivity
  2. B. Viscosity
  3. C. Mass diffusivity
  4. D. Density
Answer: A. Thermal diffusivity

Q10. In a counter-flow heat exchanger, the two fluids flow in:

  1. A. Perpendicular directions
  2. B. Circles
  3. C. The same direction
  4. D. Opposite directions
Answer: D. Opposite directions

Q11. Which dimensionless number represents the ratio of convective heat resistance to conductive heat resistance inside a solid body?

  1. A. Prandtl number
  2. B. Nusselt number
  3. C. Biot number
  4. D. Stanton number
Answer: C. Biot number

Q12. What is the primary mode of heat transfer in a fluid when bulk fluid motion is absent?

  1. A. Radiation
  2. B. Convection
  3. C. Conduction
  4. D. Advection
Answer: C. Conduction

Q13. In radiative heat exchange between two infinitely large parallel gray plates, what factor describes their surface radiation properties?

  1. A. View factor
  2. B. Emissivity
  3. C. Stefan-Boltzmann constant
  4. D. Thermal diffusivity
Answer: B. Emissivity

Q14. Which parameter indicates the time rate of heat transfer through a material per unit thickness and per unit temperature difference?

  1. A. Thermal resistance
  2. B. Thermal conductivity
  3. C. Thermal diffusivity
  4. D. Heat capacity
Answer: B. Thermal conductivity

Q15. What is the logarithmic mean temperature difference (LMTD) used for in thermal engineering design?

  1. A. Calculating heat transfer in insulated pipes
  2. B. Analyzing transient cooling of metals
  3. C. Designing and rating heat exchangers
  4. D. Determining critical insulation radius
Answer: C. Designing and rating heat exchangers

Q16. What happens to the critical radius of insulation for a cylinder if the thermal conductivity of the insulating material is decreased?

  1. A. It increases
  2. B. It decreases
  3. C. It remains unchanged
  4. D. It drops to zero
Answer: A. It increases
