Q1. The first revealed Surah (in order of revelation) is generally considered:

  1. A. Al-Fatiha
  2. B. Al-'Alaq
  3. C. Al-Baqarah
  4. D. Yaseen
Answer: B. Al-'Alaq

Q2. Zakat is payable at the rate of ___ on qualifying wealth:

  1. A. 1%
  2. B. 2.5%
  3. C. 5%
  4. D. 10%
Answer: B. 2.5%

Q3. The event of Isra and Mi'raj is associated with which Prophet?

  1. A. Musa (AS)
  2. B. Muhammad (PBUH)
  3. C. Isa (AS)
  4. D. Ibrahim (AS)
Answer: B. Muhammad (PBUH)

Q4. The first Caliph of Islam was:

  1. A. Umar ibn al-Khattab (RA)
  2. B. Abu Bakr as-Siddiq (RA)
  3. C. Uthman ibn Affan (RA)
  4. D. Ali ibn Abi Talib (RA)
Answer: B. Abu Bakr as-Siddiq (RA)

Q5. Tawhid refers to:

  1. A. Prophethood
  2. B. The oneness of Allah
  3. C. Angels
  4. D. Revelation
Answer: B. The oneness of Allah

Q6. The Quran was compiled into a single standard copy during the caliphate of:

  1. A. Abu Bakr (RA)
  2. B. Uthman ibn Affan (RA)
  3. C. Umar (RA)
  4. D. Ali (RA)
Answer: B. Uthman ibn Affan (RA)

Q7. Hudood punishments in Islamic law are:

  1. A. Decided by judges freely
  2. B. Fixed punishments prescribed in the Quran and Sunnah for specific offences
  3. C. Financial penalties only
  4. D. Abolished
Answer: B. Fixed punishments prescribed in the Quran and Sunnah for specific offences

Q8. The Treaty of Hudaybiyyah was signed in:

  1. A. 2 AH
  2. B. 6 AH
  3. C. 8 AH
  4. D. 10 AH
Answer: B. 6 AH

Q9. Ijtehad means:

  1. A. Blind following
  2. B. Independent legal reasoning by qualified scholars
  3. C. A type of prayer
  4. D. Charity
Answer: B. Independent legal reasoning by qualified scholars

Q10. The last sermon of the Prophet (PBUH) was delivered at:

  1. A. Madinah
  2. B. Arafat during the Farewell Hajj
  3. C. Taif
  4. D. Badr
Answer: B. Arafat during the Farewell Hajj

Q11. The opening chapter (Surah) of the Holy Quran is:

  1. A. Al-Baqarah
  2. B. Al-Fatihah
  3. C. Al-Ikhlas
  4. D. An-Nas
Answer: B. Al-Fatihah

Q12. The longest Surah of the Holy Quran is:

  1. A. Al-Imran
  2. B. Al-Baqarah
  3. C. An-Nisa
  4. D. Al-An'am
Answer: B. Al-Baqarah

Q13. The shortest Surah of the Holy Quran is:

  1. A. Al-Ikhlas
  2. B. Al-Asr
  3. C. An-Nasr
  4. D. Al-Kawthar
Answer: D. Al-Kawthar

Q14. The Holy Quran is divided into how many Paras (Ajza)?

  1. A. Twenty
  2. B. Twenty-five
  3. C. Thirty
  4. D. Forty
Answer: C. Thirty

Q15. Which Surah is known as the 'Heart of the Quran'?

  1. A. Yaseen
  2. B. Al-Mulk
  3. C. Ar-Rahman
  4. D. Al-Kahf
Answer: A. Yaseen

Q16. Ayat al-Kursi (the Verse of the Throne) is found in Surah:

  1. A. Aal-e-Imran
  2. B. Al-Baqarah
  3. C. An-Nisa
  4. D. Al-Maidah
Answer: B. Al-Baqarah

Q17. Which Surah of the Quran does not begin with Bismillah?

  1. A. Al-Anfal
  2. B. Yunus
  3. C. Hud
  4. D. At-Tawbah
Answer: D. At-Tawbah

Q18. Bismillah appears within a verse (verse 30) of Surah:

  1. A. Al-Kahf
  2. B. Al-Isra
  3. C. Al-Qasas
  4. D. An-Naml
Answer: D. An-Naml

Q19. How many times is the name 'Muhammad' mentioned in the Holy Quran?

  1. A. Three times
  2. B. Five times
  3. C. Four times
  4. D. Seven times
Answer: C. Four times

Q20. The only woman mentioned by name in the Holy Quran is:

  1. A. Asiya (RA)
  2. B. Khadijah (RA)
  3. C. Maryam (RA)
  4. D. Fatima (RA)
Answer: C. Maryam (RA)

Q21. The revelation of the Holy Quran was completed over a period of about:

  1. A. 23 years
  2. B. 10 years
  3. C. 13 years
  4. D. 20 years
Answer: A. 23 years

Q22. The first revelation was received by the Prophet (PBUH) in:

  1. A. Cave Thawr
  2. B. Cave Hira
  3. C. Mount Uhud
  4. D. Masjid-e-Nabawi
Answer: B. Cave Hira

Q23. The angel who brought the divine revelation (Wahi) to the prophets is:

  1. A. Jibreel (AS)
  2. B. Mikaeel (AS)
  3. C. Israfil (AS)
  4. D. Izraeel (AS)
Answer: A. Jibreel (AS)

Q24. After the Battle of Yamama, the Quran was first collected into a single book (Mushaf) during the caliphate of:

  1. A. Umar ibn al-Khattab (RA)
  2. B. Ali ibn Abi Talib (RA)
  3. C. Abu Bakr al-Siddiq (RA)
  4. D. Muawiyah (RA)

Q25. The compilation of the Quran under Abu Bakr (RA) was carried out by:

  1. A. Abdullah ibn Masud (RA)

  3. C. Muaz ibn Jabal (RA)
  4. D. Zaid ibn Thabit (RA)
