Q1. The first revealed Surah (in order of revelation) is generally considered:

  1. A. Al-Fatiha
  2. B. Al-'Alaq
  3. C. Al-Baqarah
  4. D. Yaseen
Answer: B. Al-'Alaq

Q2. Zakat is payable at the rate of ___ on qualifying wealth:

  1. A. 1%
  2. B. 2.5%
  3. C. 5%
  4. D. 10%
Answer: B. 2.5%

Q3. The event of Isra and Mi'raj is associated with which Prophet?

  1. A. Musa (AS)
  2. B. Muhammad (PBUH)
  3. C. Isa (AS)
  4. D. Ibrahim (AS)
Answer: B. Muhammad (PBUH)

Q4. Tawhid refers to:

  1. A. Prophethood
  2. B. The oneness of Allah
  3. C. Angels
  4. D. Revelation
Answer: B. The oneness of Allah

Q5. The Quran was compiled into a single standard copy during the caliphate of:

  1. A. Abu Bakr (RA)
  2. B. Uthman ibn Affan (RA)
  3. C. Umar (RA)
  4. D. Ali (RA)
Answer: B. Uthman ibn Affan (RA)

Q6. Hudood punishments in Islamic law are:

  1. A. Decided by judges freely
  2. B. Fixed punishments prescribed in the Quran and Sunnah for specific offences
  3. C. Financial penalties only
  4. D. Abolished
Answer: B. Fixed punishments prescribed in the Quran and Sunnah for specific offences

Q7. The Treaty of Hudaybiyyah was signed in:

  1. A. 2 AH
  2. B. 6 AH
  3. C. 8 AH
  4. D. 10 AH
Answer: B. 6 AH

Q8. Ijtehad means:

  1. A. Blind following
  2. B. Independent legal reasoning by qualified scholars
  3. C. A type of prayer
  4. D. Charity
Answer: B. Independent legal reasoning by qualified scholars

Q9. The last sermon of the Prophet (PBUH) was delivered at:

  1. A. Madinah
  2. B. Arafat during the Farewell Hajj
  3. C. Taif
  4. D. Badr
Answer: B. Arafat during the Farewell Hajj

Q10. Which prominent companion earned the title 'Saifullah' (Sword of Allah) for his brilliant tactical leadership in early Islamic campaigns?

  1. A. Ali ibn Abi Talib (RA)
  2. B. Khalid bin Walid (RA)
  3. C. Hamza bin Abdul-Muttalib (RA)
  4. D. Umar ibn al-Khattab (RA)
Answer: B. Khalid bin Walid (RA)

Q11. During which battle did Muslims experience a severe setback due to archers leaving their designated positions on Mount Uhud?

  1. A. Battle of Badr
  2. B. Battle of Khandaq
  3. C. Battle of Uhud
  4. D. Battle of Tabuk
Answer: C. Battle of Uhud

Q12. What term is used in Islamic jurisprudence to describe the unanimous consensus of Islamic scholars on a legal ruling?

  1. A. Qiyas
  2. B. Ijma
  3. C. Fatwa
  4. D. Tafseer
Answer: B. Ijma

Q13. The historic Battle of Badr took place in which Islamic month?

  1. A. Ramadan
  2. B. Dhul-Hijjah
  3. C. Muharram
  4. D. Rabi al-Awwal
Answer: A. Ramadan

Q14. What is the literal and legal meaning of 'Riba' in Islamic finance and commerce?

  1. A. Commercial profit from lawful trade
  2. B. Usury or excessive interest charged on loans
  3. C. Voluntary charity given to the poor
  4. D. A formal tax collected on agricultural produce
Answer: B. Usury or excessive interest charged on loans

Q15. What is the primary technical definition of 'Sunnah' in Islamic jurisprudence?

  1. A. Compulsory injunctions found exclusively in the Quran
  2. B. The legal verdicts given by the Companions
  3. C. The traditions, sayings, and approvals of the Prophet Muhammad (PBUH)
  4. D. Unanimous consensus of modern scholars
Answer: C. The traditions, sayings, and approvals of the Prophet Muhammad (PBUH)

Q16. What primary objective is highlighted in Islamic jurisprudence regarding the prescription of fasting during the month of Ramadan?

  1. A. Physical weight loss
  2. B. Attaining God-consciousness
  3. C. Experiencing political hardship
  4. D. Testing endurance in warfare
Answer: B. Attaining God-consciousness

Q17. Which angel is specifically entrusted with the duty of bringing down rain according to Islamic belief?

  1. A. Jibril (AS)
  2. B. Mikail (AS)
  3. C. Israfil (AS)
  4. D. Izrail (AS)
