Q1. The first revealed Surah (in order of revelation) is generally considered:

  1. A. Al-Fatiha
  2. B. Al-'Alaq
  3. C. Al-Baqarah
  4. D. Yaseen
Answer: B. Al-'Alaq

Q2. Zakat is payable at the rate of ___ on qualifying wealth:

  1. A. 1%
  2. B. 2.5%
  3. C. 5%
  4. D. 10%
Answer: B. 2.5%

Q3. The event of Isra and Mi'raj is associated with which Prophet?

  1. A. Musa (AS)
  2. B. Muhammad (PBUH)
  3. C. Isa (AS)
  4. D. Ibrahim (AS)
Answer: B. Muhammad (PBUH)

Q4. The first Caliph of Islam was:

  1. A. Umar ibn al-Khattab (RA)
  2. B. Abu Bakr as-Siddiq (RA)
  3. C. Uthman ibn Affan (RA)
  4. D. Ali ibn Abi Talib (RA)
Answer: B. Abu Bakr as-Siddiq (RA)

Q5. Tawhid refers to:

  1. A. Prophethood
  2. B. The oneness of Allah
  3. C. Angels
  4. D. Revelation
Answer: B. The oneness of Allah

Q6. The Quran was compiled into a single standard copy during the caliphate of:

  1. A. Abu Bakr (RA)
  2. B. Uthman ibn Affan (RA)
  3. C. Umar (RA)
  4. D. Ali (RA)
Answer: B. Uthman ibn Affan (RA)

Q7. Hudood punishments in Islamic law are:

  1. A. Decided by judges freely
  2. B. Fixed punishments prescribed in the Quran and Sunnah for specific offences
  3. C. Financial penalties only
  4. D. Abolished
Answer: B. Fixed punishments prescribed in the Quran and Sunnah for specific offences

Q8. The Treaty of Hudaybiyyah was signed in:

  1. A. 2 AH
  2. B. 6 AH
  3. C. 8 AH
  4. D. 10 AH
Answer: B. 6 AH

Q9. Ijtehad means:

  1. A. Blind following
  2. B. Independent legal reasoning by qualified scholars
  3. C. A type of prayer
  4. D. Charity
Answer: B. Independent legal reasoning by qualified scholars

Q10. The last sermon of the Prophet (PBUH) was delivered at:

  1. A. Madinah
  2. B. Arafat during the Farewell Hajj
  3. C. Taif
  4. D. Badr
Answer: B. Arafat during the Farewell Hajj
