Q1. The main objective of an external audit is to:

  1. A. Prepare the accounts
  2. B. Express an opinion on whether the financial statements are fairly presented
  3. C. Detect all fraud
  4. D. Set tax rates
Answer: B. Express an opinion on whether the financial statements are fairly presented

Q2. Audit risk is the risk that the auditor:

  1. A. Loses the client
  2. B. Gives an inappropriate opinion on materially misstated statements
  3. C. Works overtime
  4. D. Charges too little
Answer: B. Gives an inappropriate opinion on materially misstated statements

Q3. Inherent risk is higher when:

  1. A. The business is simple
  2. B. The business is complex and judgement-based
  3. C. Controls are strong
  4. D. Staff are experienced
Answer: B. The business is complex and judgement-based

Q4. An unmodified (clean) audit opinion means:

  1. A. The company is profitable
  2. B. The statements present fairly, in all material respects
  3. C. No errors exist at all
  4. D. The company will succeed
Answer: B. The statements present fairly, in all material respects

Q5. Materiality in auditing refers to:

  1. A. The size of the audit team
  2. B. How large a misstatement would influence users' decisions
  3. C. The fee charged
  4. D. The number of pages
Answer: B. How large a misstatement would influence users' decisions

Q6. Independence is essential for auditors because:

  1. A. It looks good
  2. B. Users must trust the auditor's objectivity
  3. C. It is cheaper
  4. D. It speeds the audit
Answer: B. Users must trust the auditor's objectivity

Q7. Substantive procedures include:

  1. A. Only interviews
  2. B. Tests of details and analytical procedures on transactions and balances
  3. C. Reading the news
  4. D. Marketing
Answer: B. Tests of details and analytical procedures on transactions and balances

Q8. If internal controls are strong, the auditor can generally:

  1. A. Skip the audit
  2. B. Reduce the extent of substantive testing
  3. C. Charge more
  4. D. Avoid documentation
Answer: B. Reduce the extent of substantive testing

Q9. Audit evidence is more reliable when it comes from:

  1. A. Management's memory
  2. B. Independent external sources
  3. C. The sales team
  4. D. Social media
Answer: B. Independent external sources

Q10. A going-concern doubt must be evaluated because:

  1. A. It affects share price
  2. B. The statements assume the business will continue operating
  3. C. Banks require it
  4. D. It is a tax rule
Answer: B. The statements assume the business will continue operating

Q11. Which of the following best describes sampling risk?

  1. A. The risk that the auditor’s conclusion may differ from the conclusion that would be reached if the entire population were tested
  2. B. The risk that the auditor will fail to detect a material misstatement
  3. C. The risk that the client will not cooperate with the audit
  4. D. The risk that the auditor’s evidence is not reliable
Answer: A. The risk that the auditor’s conclusion may differ from the conclusion that would be reached if the entire population were tested

Q12. An auditor performs a substantive analytical procedure on the year‑end inventory balance and finds a significant variance. The next best step is to:

  1. A. Issue a qualified opinion immediately
  2. B. Ask management for a written explanation
  3. C. Adjust the inventory balance in the financial statements
  4. D. Disregard the variance as normal business fluctuation
Answer: B. Ask management for a written explanation

Q13. Which audit procedure is primarily used to evaluate the effectiveness of an internal control over financial reporting?

  1. A. Substantive testing of balances
  2. B. Control testing
  3. C. Analytical review
  4. D. Audit sampling
Answer: B. Control testing

Q14. If the auditor concludes that the financial statements are free of material misstatement, the appropriate audit opinion is:

  1. A. Qualified
  2. B. Adverse
  3. C. Unmodified
  4. D. Disclaimer of opinion
Answer: C. Unmodified

Q15. During a risk assessment, the auditor identifies that the client’s revenue recognition policy is highly complex. Which type of risk is most likely increased?

  1. A. Detection risk
  2. B. Inherent risk
  3. C. Control risk
  4. D. Audit risk
Answer: B. Inherent risk

Q16. An auditor receives a signed letter from management confirming that all accounts receivable balances are accurate. This evidence is considered:

  1. A. External source evidence
  2. B. Management representation
  3. C. Analytical procedure
  4. D. Test of details
Answer: B. Management representation

Q17. Which of the following professional services provides the highest level of assurance to financial statement users?

  1. A. Compilation
  2. B. Review
  3. C. Audit
  4. D. Preparation
Answer: C. Audit

Q18. What type of control deficiency exists if the design or operation of a control does not allow management or employees to prevent or detect misstatements on a timely basis?

  1. A. Significant deficiency
  2. B. Control deficiency
  3. C. Material weakness
  4. D. Operating anomaly
Answer: B. Control deficiency

Q19. Which document is considered the primary permanent record of the auditing procedures performed, evidence obtained, and conclusions reached?

  1. A. Engagement letter
  2. B. Management representation letter
  3. C. Audit working papers
  4. D. Trial balance
Answer: C. Audit working papers

Q20. Which assertion is primarily tested when an auditor physically inspects inventory on hand at year-end?

  1. A. Completeness
  2. B. Existence
  3. C. Valuation
  4. D. Rights and obligations
Answer: B. Existence

Q21. When an auditor decides to restrict substantive testing due to reliance on internal controls, which test must be performed first?

  1. A. Tests of controls
  2. B. Dual-purpose tests
  3. C. Substantive analytical procedures
  4. D. Substantive tests of details
Answer: A. Tests of controls

Q22. Which specific threat to auditor independence arises when an audit firm performs bookkeeping services for the same audit client?

  1. A. Advocacy threat
  2. B. Self-review threat
  3. C. Familiarity threat
  4. D. Intimidation threat
Answer: B. Self-review threat

Q23. When an auditor discovers that management is involved in a material misstatement due to fraud, the auditor should first:

  1. A. Resign from the engagement immediately
  2. B. Report the matter directly to the SEC
  3. C. Bring the matter to the attention of a level of management at least one level above those involved
  4. D. Adjust the financial statements on behalf of management
Answer: C. Bring the matter to the attention of a level of management at least one level above those involved

Q24. Which audit documentation is typically classified as part of the current file rather than the permanent file?

  1. A. Articles of incorporation
  2. B. A copy of the current year's trial balance and adjusting journal entries
  3. C. Bylaws of the corporation
  4. D. Flowcharts of major accounting cycles established five years ago
Answer: B. A copy of the current year's trial balance and adjusting journal entries

Q25. An auditor's principal objective in reviewing the client's legal expense account is to:

  1. A. Determine the legal fees paid to the general counsel
  2. B. Identify pending or threatened litigation that may require disclosure or accrual
  3. C. Verify the accuracy of tax returns filed during the year
  4. D. Assess the efficiency of the legal department
Answer: B. Identify pending or threatened litigation that may require disclosure or accrual
