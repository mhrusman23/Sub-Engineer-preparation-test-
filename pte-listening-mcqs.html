Q1. In PTE 'Summarize Spoken Text', your summary should be:

  1. A. 10-20 words
  2. B. 50-70 words
  3. C. 200 words
  4. D. One word
Answer: B. 50-70 words

Q2. 'Highlight Correct Summary' asks you to choose:

  1. A. The summary that best matches the recording
  2. B. The longest summary
  3. C. The first summary
  4. D. A summary you write
Answer: A. The summary that best matches the recording

Q3. In 'Write from Dictation', marks are given for:

  1. A. Every correct word
  2. B. Handwriting
  3. C. Speaking clearly
  4. D. Speed
Answer: A. Every correct word

Q4. 'Select Missing Word' — the recording ends with:

  1. A. A beep replacing the last word(s)
  2. B. Music
  3. C. A question
  4. D. Silence of one minute
Answer: A. A beep replacing the last word(s)

Q5. For PTE Listening, note-taking should focus on:

  1. A. Every word
  2. B. Key points and signal words
  3. C. Only names
  4. D. Nothing
Answer: B. Key points and signal words

Q6. 'Highlight Incorrect Words' compares:

  1. A. Two speakers
  2. B. A transcript with the recording
  3. C. Two essays
  4. D. Two maps
Answer: B. A transcript with the recording

Q7. If a word is misspelled in Write from Dictation:

  1. A. Full marks anyway
  2. B. That word loses its mark
  3. C. The whole test fails
  4. D. Extra time is given
Answer: B. That word loses its mark

Q8. PTE Listening recordings are played:

  1. A. Twice
  2. B. Once only
  3. C. Three times
  4. D. On request
Answer: B. Once only

Q9. A speaker's tone can help you identify:

  1. A. The date
  2. B. Their attitude or feeling
  3. C. The spelling
  4. D. The word count
Answer: B. Their attitude or feeling

Q10. In multiple-choice listening, options are often:

  1. A. Read before the audio starts — use that time to predict
  2. B. Hidden until the end
  3. C. In another language
  4. D. Always in order
Answer: A. Read before the audio starts — use that time to predict

Q11. During the PTE 'Highlight Incorrect Words' task, your cursor should follow along with the audio to:

  1. A. Click the correct summary
  2. B. Transcribe the entire paragraph
  3. C. Identify words on the screen that differ from what the speaker says
  4. D. Type the missing words at the end
Answer: C. Identify words on the screen that differ from what the speaker says

Q12. When approaching PTE Listening Multiple Choice Single Answer questions, what strategy is recommended before the audio starts?

  1. A. Writing a full essay
  2. B. Quickly reading the prompt and answer options
  3. C. Closing your eyes to rest
  4. D. Adjusting your microphone volume
Answer: B. Quickly reading the prompt and answer options

Q13. In the 'Select Missing Word' task of PTE Listening, the final word or group of words in the audio recording is:

  1. A. Spelled aloud letter by letter
  2. B. Replaced by a beep sound
  3. C. Repeated three times
  4. D. Omitted entirely
Answer: B. Replaced by a beep sound

Q14. What skill contributes the most score points toward your Reading score from the PTE Listening module?

  1. A. Summarize Spoken Text
  2. B. Highlight Incorrect Words
  3. C. Fill in the Blanks
  4. D. Write from Dictation
Answer: B. Highlight Incorrect Words

Q15. In the 'Select Missing Word' listening task, how do you know the final word has been reached?

  1. A. A loud buzzer sounds
  2. B. The audio pauses with a beep
  3. C. The screen turns red
  4. D. The timer stops completely
Answer: B. The audio pauses with a beep

Q16. In the PTE 'Summarize Spoken Text' task, what is the recommended length range for your written summary?

  1. A. 20 to 30 words
  2. B. 50 to 70 words
  3. C. 100 to 120 words
  4. D. 150 to 200 words
Answer: B. 50 to 70 words

Q17. During the PTE 'Highlight Incorrect Words' task, how are penalties applied for incorrect clicks?

  1. A. No penalty for wrong clicks
  2. B. Negative marking for each wrong click
  3. C. Test is immediately terminated
  4. D. Half a mark deducted per mistake
Answer: B. Negative marking for each wrong click

Q18. In PTE 'Fill in the Blanks' (Listening), what is the best strategy while listening to the audio?

  1. A. Write down the missing words directly on your erasable notepad
  2. B. Close your eyes and listen
  3. C. Ignore the text and read the options
  4. D. Type random words to save time
Answer: A. Write down the missing words directly on your erasable notepad

Q19. How many times does the audio recording play in the 'Write from Dictation' task?

  1. A. Once
  2. B. Twice
  3. C. Three times
  4. D. As many times as you want
Answer: A. Once

Q20. In PTE Listening Multiple Choice (Multiple Answers) questions, how is scoring generally handled for incorrect choices?

  1. A. No penalty whatsoever
  2. B. Negative marking applies per incorrect choice
  3. C. Bonus points for leaving it blank
  4. D. Zero score for the entire listening section
Answer: B. Negative marking applies per incorrect choice

Q21. What is the typical duration range for audio prompts in the PTE 'Summarize Spoken Text' task?

  1. A. 10 to 20 seconds
  2. B. 30 to 45 seconds
  3. C. 60 to 90 seconds
  4. D. 2 to 3 minutes
Answer: C. 60 to 90 seconds

Q22. When a question in PTE Listening asks 'Why does the speaker...?', what specific element of the listening input is being targeted?

  1. A. The speaker's purpose
  2. B. The exact date and time
  3. C. The total word count
  4. D. The background noise level
Answer: A. The speaker's purpose

Q23. In the PTE Listening section task 'Highlight Incorrect Words', which two primary language skills are simultaneously assessed?

  1. A. Listening and Reading
  2. B. Speaking and Writing
  3. C. Reading and Writing
  4. D. Listening and Speaking
Answer: A. Listening and Reading

Q24. In the PTE Listening task 'Summarize Spoken Text', what is the required word count range for the candidate's written response?

  2. B. 50 to 70 words
  3. C. 100 to 120 words
  4. D. 150 to 200 words

Q25. How many times will the audio clip play automatically in the PTE Fill in the Blanks listening task?

  1. A. Once

  4. D. As many times as needed
