PTE Listening MCQs (IELTS & English Tests)

Free MCQs with answers for PTE Listening. 20 questions in 2 chapter tests are in the app; all are shown here.

Q1. In PTE 'Summarize Spoken Text', your summary should be:

  1. A. 10-20 words
  2. B. 50-70 words
  3. C. 200 words
  4. D. One word
Answer: B. 50-70 words

Q2. 'Highlight Correct Summary' asks you to choose:

  1. A. The summary that best matches the recording
  2. B. The longest summary
  3. C. The first summary
  4. D. A summary you write
Answer: A. The summary that best matches the recording

Q3. In 'Write from Dictation', marks are given for:

  1. A. Every correct word
  2. B. Handwriting
  3. C. Speaking clearly
  4. D. Speed
Answer: A. Every correct word

Q4. 'Select Missing Word' — the recording ends with:

  1. A. A beep replacing the last word(s)
  2. B. Music
  3. C. A question
  4. D. Silence of one minute
Answer: A. A beep replacing the last word(s)

Q5. For PTE Listening, note-taking should focus on:

  1. A. Every word
  2. B. Key points and signal words
  3. C. Only names
  4. D. Nothing
Answer: B. Key points and signal words

Q6. 'Highlight Incorrect Words' compares:

  1. A. Two speakers
  2. B. A transcript with the recording
  3. C. Two essays
  4. D. Two maps
Answer: B. A transcript with the recording

Q7. If a word is misspelled in Write from Dictation:

  1. A. Full marks anyway
  2. B. That word loses its mark
  3. C. The whole test fails
  4. D. Extra time is given
Answer: B. That word loses its mark

Q8. PTE Listening recordings are played:

  1. A. Twice
  2. B. Once only
  3. C. Three times
  4. D. On request
Answer: B. Once only

Q9. A speaker's tone can help you identify:

  1. A. The date
  2. B. Their attitude or feeling
  3. C. The spelling
  4. D. The word count
Answer: B. Their attitude or feeling

Q10. In multiple-choice listening, options are often:

  1. A. Read before the audio starts — use that time to predict
  2. B. Hidden until the end
  3. C. In another language
  4. D. Always in order
Answer: A. Read before the audio starts — use that time to predict

Q11. In Summarize Spoken Text, your summary should include:

  1. A. Only the first point
  2. B. The main idea and key supporting points
  3. C. Your opinion
  4. D. Every word
Answer: B. The main idea and key supporting points

Q12. Fill in the Blanks (listening): you type the missing words while:

  1. A. Reading
  2. B. Listening — the words are exactly as spoken
  3. C. Speaking
  4. D. After one hour
Answer: B. Listening — the words are exactly as spoken

Q13. Spelling in listening fill-in blanks:

  1. A. Does not matter
  2. B. Must be correct to get the mark
  3. C. Can be phonetic
  4. D. Is auto-corrected
Answer: B. Must be correct to get the mark

Q14. Highlight Incorrect Words: click words that:

  1. A. Are long
  2. B. Differ from what the speaker actually says
  3. C. Are verbs
  4. D. You dislike
Answer: B. Differ from what the speaker actually says

Q15. Wrong clicks in Highlight Incorrect Words:

  1. A. Cost nothing
  2. B. Lose marks (item score not below zero)
  3. C. End the test
  4. D. Are encouraged
Answer: B. Lose marks (item score not below zero)

Q16. Select Missing Word: listen to the end because:

  1. A. The start is a trick
  2. B. The missing word comes at the end and must fit the whole meaning
  3. C. It is louder
  4. D. It repeats
Answer: B. The missing word comes at the end and must fit the whole meaning

Q17. For multiple-choice listening, read the options:

  1. A. After the audio
  2. B. Before the audio plays
  3. C. Never
  4. D. During the next item
Answer: B. Before the audio plays

Q18. Note-taking for lectures should use:

  1. A. Full sentences
  2. B. Abbreviations and key nouns/verbs
  3. C. Drawings only
  4. D. Your own language only
Answer: B. Abbreviations and key nouns/verbs

Q19. If the speaker lists three items, expect:

  1. A. No questions
  2. B. Questions about the list — note all three
  3. C. A break
  4. D. The end
Answer: B. Questions about the list — note all three

Q20. Write from Dictation: capital letters and full stops:

  1. A. Are never needed
  2. B. Help show sentence boundaries; type exactly what you hear
  3. C. Are banned
  4. D. Give bonus marks
Answer: B. Help show sentence boundaries; type exactly what you hear
