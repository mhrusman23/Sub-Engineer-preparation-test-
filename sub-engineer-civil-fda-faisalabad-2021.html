Q1. Jointing method for PPRC fitting......

  1. A. solution
  2. B. heating
  3. C. welding
  4. D. Threading
Answer: B. heating

Q2. Fore bearing of AB is 110°; its back bearing will be...... (Formula BB-FB=180)

  1. A. 280°
  2. B. 315°
  3. C. 290°
  4. D. 240°
Answer: C. 290°

Q3. A point on the earth immediately above the center of earthquake....

  1. A. focus point
  2. B. Quake center
  3. C. seismic
  4. D. epicenter
Answer: D. epicenter

Q4. The specification not included in departmental standard specification is called......

  1. A. special
  2. B. detailed
  3. C. General
  4. D. standard
Answer: A. special

Q5. Outer inclined member in a steel truss....

  1. A. principle rafter
  2. B. Main tie
  3. C. strut
