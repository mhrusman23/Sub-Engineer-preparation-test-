Q1. A triplicate form used to issue some articles from store is called....

  1. A. bin card
  2. B. indent
  3. C. demand slip
  4. D. Stock register
Answer: A. bin card

Q2. The quantity of cement (in bags) will be required for 100 sft, flush pointing with cement mortar (1:3) ....

  1. A. 2 bags
  2. B. 1.53 bags
  3. C. 0.53 bags
  4. D. 2.53 bags
Answer: C. 0.53 bags

Q3. The distance of C.G from vertex of a triangle of height "h"......

  1. A. h/4
  2. B. h/2
  3. C. 2h/3
  4. D. h/3
Answer: C. 2h/3

Q4. Showing end speed of limit is such type of road sign....

  1. A. warning
  2. B. informatory
  3. C. Regulatory
  4. D. mandatory
Answer: B. informatory

Q5. Prepared surface at which sub base or base layer of road is provided is called....

  1. A. under layer
  2. B. gradient
  3. C. formation
  4. D. Sub grade
Answer: D. Sub grade

Q6. The workers who are paid out of funds available for a project are....

  1. A. work charged
  2. B. permanent
  3. C. temporary
  4. D. Regular
Answer: A. work charged

Q7. Wing wall of a culvert usually deflects at........degree.

  1. A. 60
  2. B. 90
  3. C. 45
  4. D. 30
Answer: B. 90

Q8. A bridge in which position of deck is below the supporting parts is called........bridge.

  1. A. through
  2. B. low level
  3. C. Deck
  4. D. skew
Answer: A. through

Q9. Tender is invited for....

  1. A. contract
  2. B. Piece work
  3. C. work order
  4. D. all of these
Answer: A. contract

Q10. Center of earthquake is termed as....

  1. A. focus center
  2. B. seismic
  3. C. epicenter
  4. D. Quack center
Answer: A. focus center

Q11. Paint with white lead base is suitable for painting of....

  1. A. Wood work
  2. B. iron work
  3. C. both a & b
  4. D. plaster
Answer: D. plaster

Q12. The main property of a liquid which controls its rate of flow....

  1. A. Surface tension
  2. B. specific gravity
  3. C. viscosity
  4. D. cohesion
Answer: C. viscosity

Q13. An air pollution control equipment....

  1. A. inertial separator
  2. B. telemeter
  3. C. Oscilloscope
  4. D. all of these
Answer: A. inertial separator

Q14. A small chamber constructed at the street ends to get storm water is called....

  1. A. lamp hole
  2. B. regulator
  3. C. cleanout
  4. D. Inlet
Answer: D. Inlet

Q15. To get dry volume, wet volume of cement for 0.40 water cement ratio, water required will be....

  1. A. 14-liter
  2. B. 28-liter
  3. C. 20-liter
  4. D. 35-liter
Answer: C. 20-liter

Q16. Heat transfer through......

  1. A. convection
  2. B. radiation
  3. C. Conduction
  4. D. all of these
Answer: D. all of these

Q17. A dredger similar to crawler shovels......

  1. A. Bucket
  2. B. grapple/rock
  3. C. dipper
  4. D. hydraulic
Answer: B. grapple/rock

Q18. In water bound macadam roads, binding materials is......

  1. A. cement
  2. B. stone dust
  3. C. Sand
  4. D. none of these
Answer: B. stone dust

Q19. Ratio of concrete used in foundation......

  1. A. 1:2:4
  2. B. 1:1.5:3
  3. C. 1:1:2
  4. D. 1:4:8
Answer: D. 1:4:8

Q20. Minimum height of headroom......

  1. A. 2.5 m
  2. B. 2.0 m
  3. C. 3.5 m
  4. D. 3.0 m
Answer: A. 2.5 m

Q21. Chizzy formula is used to calculate......

  1. A. volume
  2. B. velocity
  3. C. discharge
  4. D. Area
Answer: B. velocity

Q22. Quantity of solids in water measured in milligram per liter is also equivalent to......

  1. A. PPM
  2. B. TDS
  3. C. Threshold no.
  4. D. Reynolds no.
Answer: A. PPM

Q23. Quantity of dry mortar in 100 cft mortar is....

  1. A. 50
  2. B. 20
  3. C. 30
  4. D. 40
Answer: C. 30

Q24. Vertical distance between dip and crown of a trap....

  1. A. Cistern
  2. B. seal trap
  3. C. floor trap
  4. D. pedestral
Answer: B. seal trap

Q25. A time period for which a particle of water detains in settling tank is called....

  1. A. stop period
  2. B. detention period
  3. C. dew time
  4. D. Design period
Answer: B. detention period
