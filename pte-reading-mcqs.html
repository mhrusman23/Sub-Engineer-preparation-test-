Q1. PTE 'Multiple Choice, Multiple Answers' questions have:

  1. A. Negative marking for wrong choices
  2. B. No penalty ever
  3. C. Only one answer
  4. D. Spoken answers
Answer: A. Negative marking for wrong choices

Q2. In 'Re-order Paragraphs', you must:

  1. A. Write new paragraphs
  2. B. Put jumbled paragraphs in the correct order
  3. C. Translate paragraphs
  4. D. Count the words
Answer: B. Put jumbled paragraphs in the correct order

Q3. 'Reading & Writing: Fill in the Blanks' tests:

  1. A. Listening
  2. B. Vocabulary and grammar in context
  3. C. Pronunciation
  4. D. Handwriting
Answer: B. Vocabulary and grammar in context

Q4. A collocation is:

  1. A. A type of question
  2. B. Words that naturally go together
  3. C. A punctuation mark
  4. D. A reading speed
Answer: B. Words that naturally go together

Q5. For PTE Reading time management, it is wise to:

  1. A. Spend equal time on every item
  2. B. Not spend too long on any single item
  3. C. Skip the fill-in blanks
  4. D. Read everything twice
Answer: B. Not spend too long on any single item

Q6. In single-answer multiple choice, the correct option is usually:

  1. A. The longest one
  2. B. A paraphrase of the text
  3. C. The first one
  4. D. Written in capital letters
Answer: B. A paraphrase of the text

Q7. 'Fill in the Blanks' (dropdown) answers depend mostly on:

  1. A. Luck
  2. B. Grammar and meaning in context
  3. C. The passage title
  4. D. Word length
Answer: B. Grammar and meaning in context

Q8. Skimming in PTE Reading helps you:

  1. A. Memorise details
  2. B. Get the gist quickly
  3. C. Improve pronunciation
  4. D. Write essays
Answer: B. Get the gist quickly

Q9. If unsure in multiple-answers questions, it is safer to:

  1. A. Select every option
  2. B. Select only options you are confident about
  3. C. Select none
  4. D. Select the longest options
Answer: B. Select only options you are confident about

Q10. PTE Reading section timing is:

  1. A. Untimed
  2. B. Strictly timed as one section
  3. C. Timed per question by a teacher
  4. D. Optional
Answer: B. Strictly timed as one section

Q11. In the PTE Reading 'Multiple Choice, Single Answer' task, what is a common core objective you are asked to determine?

  1. A. The exact phonetic pronunciation of words
  2. B. The sentence that best summarizes the author's opinion or main text
  3. C. The dictionary definition of every single noun
  4. D. The total word count of the second paragraph
Answer: B. The sentence that best summarizes the author's opinion or main text

Q12. What primary factor causes certain icebergs to take on a dark or opaque appearance?

  1. A. The presence of bits of rock
  2. B. A heavy cloud cover overhead
  3. C. The low angle of the Sun above the horizon
  4. D. The presence of large cracks in their surface
Answer: A. The presence of bits of rock

Q13. Roughly how long is the total allocated time duration for the PTE Reading section?

  1. A. 10 to 15 minutes
  2. B. 29 to 30 minutes
  3. C. 45 to 50 minutes
  4. D. 60 to 75 minutes
Answer: B. 29 to 30 minutes

Q14. In the PTE Reading 'Multiple Choice, Single Answer' task, what is a common core objective you are frequently asked to determine about the passage?

  1. A. The exact date of publication
  2. B. The author's primary purpose or overall summary
  3. C. The phonetic transcription of difficult words
  4. D. The total word count of the text
Answer: B. The author's primary purpose or overall summary

Q15. When tackling the PTE Reading section, under what condition must you process the passages and questions?

  1. A. At your own pace with unlimited breaks
  2. B. Under strict timed conditions for the module
  3. C. With a personal dictionary provided
  4. D. Allowing extra time per paragraph upon request
Answer: B. Under strict timed conditions for the module

Q16. In the context of early reading progress studies, which metric is commonly used to measure oral reading performance?

  1. A. Words per minute
  2. B. Paragraphs per hour
  3. C. Sentences per breath
  4. D. Vowels per line
Answer: A. Words per minute

Q17. Which subskill is directly assessed during the PTE Reading module alongside academic vocabulary comprehension?

  1. A. Identifying the writer's tone, style, or attitude
  2. B. Transcribing spoken audio into text accurately
  3. C. Demonstrating proper microphone positioning
  4. D. Translating paragraphs into native dialects
Answer: A. Identifying the writer's tone, style, or attitude

Q18. To practice effectively for the PTE 'Multiple Choice, Single Answer' format, what simple exercise can you perform with any general reading material?

  1. A. Memorize the first and last sentences verbatim
  2. B. Translate the text into another language
  3. C. Read the text and try to summarize it in a single sentence
  4. D. Count the frequency of specific punctuation marks
Answer: C. Read the text and try to summarize it in a single sentence

Q19. When you encounter a word you do not know in a passage, the best strategy is to:

  1. A. Look it up in a dictionary immediately
  2. B. Guess from context
  3. C. Skip the sentence and return later
  4. D. Write it down and ignore it
Answer: B. Guess from context

Q20. Which skill is NOT directly assessed in the PTE Reading section?

  1. A. Identifying main ideas
  2. B. Recognising text structure
  3. C. Transcribing spoken words
  4. D. Understanding author’s purpose
Answer: C. Transcribing spoken words

Q21. In the PTE Reading ‘Fill in the Blanks’ task, the blanks are most often located:

  1. A. At the end of paragraphs
  2. B. In the middle of sentences
  3. C. At the beginning of the passage
  4. D. In the margin beside the text
Answer: B. In the middle of sentences

Q22. Which study method is frequently recommended to enhance overall vocabulary and grammatical precision for PTE Reading?

  1. A. Memorizing fictional storybooks
  2. B. Reading regular native English academic books and texts
  3. C. Listening exclusively to audio lectures without text
  4. D. Practicing handwritten calligraphy daily
Answer: B. Reading regular native English academic books and texts

Q23. What is the primary objective of the Re-order Paragraphs task in PTE Reading?

  1. A. To summarize long novels into single sentences
  2. B. To restore jumbled academic paragraphs into their proper logical sequence
  3. C. To translate foreign text into standard English
  4. D. To check spelling errors in a provided passage
Answer: B. To restore jumbled academic paragraphs into their proper logical sequence

Q24. Which of the following describes the scoring nature of the PTE Reading section regarding time limits?

  1. A. Each individual question has an independent countdown timer
  2. B. The entire section operates under one single strict overall time limit
  3. C. Candidates can take extra unrecorded time if needed
  4. D. Time is allocated per paragraph by an automated proctor
Answer: B. The entire section operates under one single strict overall time limit

Q25. How many distinct task types make up the entire PTE Academic Reading section?

  1. A. Three
  2. B. Four
  3. C. Five
  4. D. Six
Answer: C. Five
