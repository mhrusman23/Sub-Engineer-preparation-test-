Q1. PPE stands for:

  1. A. Personal Protective Equipment
  2. B. Public Protection Exercise
  3. C. Private Property Entry
  4. D. Planned Preventive Equipment
Answer: A. Personal Protective Equipment

Q2. PPE is considered which level in the hierarchy of control?

  1. A. The first choice
  2. B. The last resort
  3. C. The only control
  4. D. An engineering control
Answer: B. The last resort

Q3. Employers must provide PPE:

  1. A. At a cost to workers
  2. B. Free of charge where risks cannot be controlled otherwise
  3. C. Only to managers
  4. D. Only on request
Answer: B. Free of charge where risks cannot be controlled otherwise

Q4. Hearing protection is needed at sustained noise levels around:

  1. A. 50 dB
  2. B. 85 dB(A) and above
  3. C. 30 dB
  4. D. 120 dB only
Answer: B. 85 dB(A) and above

Q5. The correct glove for handling sharp metal is:

  1. A. Thin latex
  2. B. Cut-resistant gloves
  3. C. Cotton gloves
  4. D. No gloves
Answer: B. Cut-resistant gloves

Q6. Respirators (face masks) must be:

  1. A. Shared between workers
  2. B. Face-fit tested for the wearer
  3. C. Worn loosely
  4. D. Used once a year
Answer: B. Face-fit tested for the wearer

Q7. Safety footwear mainly protects against:

  1. A. Cold
  2. B. Crushing injuries and punctures to feet
  3. C. Noise
  4. D. Dust in eyes
Answer: B. Crushing injuries and punctures to feet

Q8. Eye protection is essential when:

  1. A. Reading
  2. B. Grinding, cutting or handling chemicals
  3. C. Walking outside
  4. D. In meetings
Answer: B. Grinding, cutting or handling chemicals

Q9. PPE must be stored:

  1. A. Anywhere
  2. B. Clean, dry and away from damage
  3. C. On the floor
  4. D. In sunlight
Answer: B. Clean, dry and away from damage

Q10. If PPE is damaged, the worker should:

  1. A. Keep using it
  2. B. Report it and get a replacement
  3. C. Repair it with tape
  4. D. Share a colleague's
Answer: B. Report it and get a replacement

Q11. What is the primary function of a safety harness in construction work at height?

  1. A. To completely prevent a fall from happening
  2. B. To arrest a fall and reduce impact forces on the body
  3. C. To make the worker feel more secure on scaffolding
  4. D. To carry heavy hand tools securely while climbing
Answer: B. To arrest a fall and reduce impact forces on the body

Q12. Which specific safety sign category is characterized by a blue circle with a white pictogram?

  1. A. Prohibition signs
  2. B. Warning signs
  3. C. Mandatory action signs
  4. D. Emergency escape signs
Answer: C. Mandatory action signs

Q13. What is the main purpose of conducting a face-fit test for respiratory protective equipment?

  1. A. To check if the mask filter is expired
  2. B. To ensure an airtight seal between the mask and the wearer's face
  3. C. To measure the chemical concentration in the working atmosphere
  4. D. To clean dust off the interior of the respirator
Answer: B. To ensure an airtight seal between the mask and the wearer's face

Q14. Which color and geometric shape are universally designated for prohibition safety signs?

  1. A. Red circle with a diagonal slash
  2. B. Yellow triangle with a black border
  3. C. Blue circle with a white icon
  4. D. Green rectangle with white text
Answer: A. Red circle with a diagonal slash

Q15. When should high-visibility clothing be worn by personnel on a busy industrial site?

  1. A. Only during heavy rainfall or stormy conditions
  2. B. Whenever moving vehicles or heavy equipment are operating nearby
  3. C. Only when working inside closed administrative offices
  4. D. At night time when normal lights are switched off
Answer: B. Whenever moving vehicles or heavy equipment are operating nearby

Q16. What is the correct regulatory hierarchy principle regarding the use of Personal Protective Equipment?

  1. A. PPE should always be applied before engineering controls
  2. B. PPE is the primary solution for all occupational hazards
  3. C. PPE is the last line of defense when other controls fail
  4. D. PPE is optional if workers feel comfortable without it
Answer: C. PPE is the last line of defense when other controls fail

Q17. A worker insulated with an electrical isolation torch must wear which type of gloves to prevent electric shock if the work is performed above 8 kV?

  1. A. Rubber insulated gloves
  2. B. Cork rubber gloves
  3. C. Leather gloves
  4. D. Aluminium-coated gloves
Answer: A. Rubber insulated gloves

Q18. When a worker is operating a heavy drilling machine, the most appropriate PPE to protect the eyes is:

  1. A. Dual‑lens glasses
  2. B. Standard safety glasses
  3. C. Mini‑spade face shield
  4. D. Band‑type goggles
Answer: C. Mini‑spade face shield

Q19. A construction site uses a red high‑visibility vest with an orange dash panel. What is the primary hazard that this vest is designed to warn others about?

  1. A. The worker’s presence in low‑light conditions
  2. B. The presence of hazardous material
  3. C. An elevated work area
  4. D. An armed security presence
Answer: A. The worker’s presence in low‑light conditions

Q20. An employee reports that their respirator filter has stuck to the face due to improper seating. Which aspect of PPE operation is most likely incorrect?

  1. A. Filter was not replaced within recommended time
  2. B. Facepiece was not fitted correctly in a size‑fit test
  3. C. Filter was not sterilised
  4. D. Reseals were not tightened
Answer: B. Facepiece was not fitted correctly in a size‑fit test

Q21. On a chemical plant, a safety sign shows a green triangle with a drop of liquid inside. Which precaution does this sign typically represent?

  1. A. High temperature
  2. B. Flammable
  3. C. Corrosive
  4. D. Cryogenic
Answer: C. Corrosive

Q22. What is the correct definition of a prohibition safety sign according to standard workplace safety regulations?

  1. A. A sign indicating the location of emergency exit routes
  2. B. A sign specifying an action that must be strictly carried out
  3. C. A sign indicating behavior or an action that is strictly forbidden
  4. D. A sign warning workers about flammable liquid storage areas
Answer: C. A sign indicating behavior or an action that is strictly forbidden

Q23. Before any new item of personal protective equipment is issued to an employee, what is the employer's primary legal duty regarding suitability?

  1. A. Ensure it is the cheapest available brand on the market
  2. B. Assess the PPE to ensure it is suitable for the specific workplace risk
  3. C. Require the employee to pay a security deposit for the item
  4. D. Ensure the equipment has expired manufacturing warranty dates
Answer: B. Assess the PPE to ensure it is suitable for the specific workplace risk

Q24. What is the primary purpose of conducting a face-fit test for a tight-fitting half-mask respirator?

  1. A. To measure the shelf life of the filter cartridges
  2. B. To check the chemical composition of the breathing air
  3. C. To ensure a proper and leak-tight seal against the wearer's face
  4. D. To verify the correct color coding of the respiratory equipment
Answer: C. To ensure a proper and leak-tight seal against the wearer's face

Q25. Which specific geometric shape and color combination is internationally standardized for mandatory safety signs?

  1. A. Red circle with a diagonal slash
  2. B. Blue circle with white pictogram
  3. C. Yellow triangle with black border
  4. D. Green rectangle with white symbol
Answer: B. Blue circle with white pictogram
