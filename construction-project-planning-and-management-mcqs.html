Q1. This is an art of arranging various activities and group of peoples in an organization to achieve a common goal.

  1. A. Management
  2. B. Construction team
  3. C. Engineering
  4. D. All of these
Answer: A. Management

Q2. A form of human association to achieve a common goal is called

  1. A. Organization
  2. B. Staff
  3. C. Project Team
  4. D. Construction team
Answer: A. Organization

Q3. To collect necessary data about the project ---- survey is carried out.

  1. A. Preliminary
  2. B. Detailed survey
  3. C. Interview
  4. D. All of these
Answer: A. Preliminary

Q4. Preparation of programme of construction orderly and systematically is called

  1. A. Execution
  2. B. Tendering
  3. C. Scheduling
  4. D. Planning
Answer: C. Scheduling

Q5. Construction machinery is used due to

  1. A. Economy
  2. B. Accuracy
  3. C. Fast speed
  4. D. All of these
Answer: D. All of these

Q6. A software used for project management in Civil Engineering.

  1. A. Primavera
  2. B. Eagle point
  3. C. Auto Desk
  4. D. Auto CAD
Answer: A. Primavera

Q7. Carrying out constructional work according to specifications is called.......

  1. A. Progress
  2. B. Inspection
  3. C. Execution
  4. D. Quality control
Answer: D. Quality control

Q8. In departmentally execution of work the enrolment of labour is carried out on......

  1. A. Running Bill
  2. B. Muster Roll
  3. C. M.B
  4. D. Pay Bill
Answer: B. Muster Roll

Q9. The formal acceptance of the proposal by the administrative department is known as........

  1. A. Bid
  2. B. Technical sanction
  3. C. Grant
  4. D. Administrative approval
Answer: D. Administrative approval

Q10. Detailed measurements in respect of works and supplies of materials are recorded in.

  1. A. M.B
  2. B. Final Bill
  3. C. Muster Roll
  4. D. Running Bill
Answer: A. M.B

Q11. Material and equipment of any division of works department is called.

  1. A. Store
  2. B. Depot
  3. C. Godown
  4. D. Deposit works
Answer: A. Store

Q12. In project management this should be the main objective.

  1. A. Co-ordination
  2. B. Economy
  3. C. Progress
  4. D. Peace
Answer: B. Economy

Q13. The ratio between high management and middle level management senior staff of a good organization should be

  1. A. 1:3 to 1:5
  2. B. 1:7 to 1:10
  3. C. 1:5 to 1:7
  4. D. 1:1 to 1:3
Answer: A. 1:3 to 1:5

Q14. Feasibility report is based on such estimate.

  1. A. Rough estimate
  2. B. Revised estimate
  3. C. Any one
  4. D. Detailed estimate
Answer: A. Rough estimate

Q15. A schedule representing scheme of execution is called ---- schedule.

  1. A. Construction
  2. B. Finance
  3. C. Material
  4. D. Labour
Answer: A. Construction

Q16. Earth moving machine having blade at front.

  1. A. Scraper
  2. B. Ripper
  3. C. Tractor
  4. D. Bulldozer
Answer: D. Bulldozer

Q17. The smallest unit in a project.

  1. A. activity
  2. B. enterprise
  3. C. node
  4. D. WBS
Answer: A. activity

Q18. Quality control may be achieved by making,

  1. A. Execution
  2. B. Progress
  3. C. Inspection
  4. D. Quality control
Answer: C. Inspection

Q19. Sanction of daily labour is accorded by

  1. A. XEN
  2. B. S.E
  3. C. S.D.O
  4. D. Chief Engineer
Answer: A. XEN

Q20. Approval sanctioned by competent authority for execution of construction project is termed as,

  1. A. Technical sanction
  2. B. Administrative approval
  3. C. Grant
  4. D. Bid
Answer: A. Technical sanction

Q21. Measurement book may be required to produce in court of law as ........ in case of disputes.

  1. A. Record
  2. B. Evidence
  3. C. Pass book
  4. D. Charges
Answer: B. Evidence

Q22. Over all control of stores lies with an officer called.

  1. A. XEN
  2. B. Sub-Engineer
  3. C. S.E
  4. D. SDO
Answer: A. XEN

Q23. Managerial function which decides what to do, when to do and how to do a job.

  1. A. Staffing
  2. B. Scheduling
  3. C. Planning
  4. D. Feed back
Answer: C. Planning

Q24. The ratio between middle level management and subordinates of a good organization should be

  1. A. 1:30 to 1:50
  2. B. 1:10 to 1:15
  3. C. 1:20 to 1:30
  4. D. 1:15 to 1:20
Answer: D. 1:15 to 1:20

Q25. Project report is based on such estimate.

  1. A. Detailed estimate
  2. B. Any one
  3. C. Revised estimate
  4. D. Rough estimate
Answer: A. Detailed estimate
