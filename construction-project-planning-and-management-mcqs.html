Q1. This is an art of arranging various activities and group of peoples in an organization to achieve a common goal.

  1. A. Construction team
  2. B. Management
  3. C. Engineering
  4. D. All of these
Answer: B. Management

Q2. In project management this should be the main objective.

  1. A. Co-ordination
  2. B. Peace
  3. C. Economy
  4. D. Progress
Answer: C. Economy

Q3. Managerial function which decides what to do, when to do and how to do a job.

  1. A. Staffing
  2. B. Feed back
  3. C. Scheduling
  4. D. Planning
Answer: D. Planning

Q4. A function of management which deals with the appointment of the requisite number of persons to fulfill the responsibilities.

  1. A. Feed back
  2. B. Staffing
  3. C. Scheduling
  4. D. Planning
Answer: B. Staffing

  3. C. Organization

Q6. Money, material, machinery and management are the ---- for construction industry.

Q16. The ratio between high management and middle level management senior staff of a good organization should be
