Q1. Under accrual accounting, revenue is recorded when it is:

  1. A. Received in cash
  2. B. Earned, regardless of when cash is received
  3. C. Spent
  4. D. Budgeted
Answer: B. Earned, regardless of when cash is received

Q2. Which financial statement shows a company's position at a single point in time?

  1. A. Income statement
  2. B. Balance sheet
  3. C. Cash flow statement
  4. D. Statement of changes in equity
Answer: B. Balance sheet

Q3. Depreciation is:

  1. A. A cash payment
  2. B. The allocation of an asset's cost over its useful life
  3. C. A tax
  4. D. A loan
Answer: B. The allocation of an asset's cost over its useful life

Q4. FIFO inventory costing assumes:

  1. A. Last items bought are sold first
  2. B. First items bought are sold first
  3. C. Average cost is used
  4. D. Items never expire
Answer: B. First items bought are sold first

Q5. Goodwill arises when:

  1. A. A company is profitable
  2. B. A business is bought for more than the fair value of its net assets
  3. C. Shares are issued
  4. D. A loan is taken
Answer: B. A business is bought for more than the fair value of its net assets

Q6. A contingent liability should be recorded when the loss is:

  1. A. Remote
  2. B. Probable and reasonably estimable
  3. C. Possible only
  4. D. Imaginary
Answer: B. Probable and reasonably estimable

Q7. The statement of cash flows is divided into operating, investing and:

  1. A. Financing activities
  2. B. Tax activities
  3. C. Sales activities
  4. D. Owner activities
Answer: A. Financing activities

Q8. Under US GAAP, research costs are generally:

  1. A. Capitalised
  2. B. Expensed as incurred
  3. C. Deferred forever
  4. D. Ignored
Answer: B. Expensed as incurred

Q9. Working capital equals:

  1. A. Total assets
  2. B. Current assets − Current liabilities
  3. C. Revenue − expenses
  4. D. Cash only
Answer: B. Current assets − Current liabilities

Q10. Which accounting principle requires the matching of incurred expenses with the revenues they helped generate during the same period?

  1. A. Revenue recognition principle
  2. B. Expense recognition principle
  3. C. Full disclosure principle
  4. D. Cost principle
Answer: B. Expense recognition principle

Q11. Under the allowance method, when an account receivable is written off as uncollectible, what is the immediate effect on total net assets?

  1. A. Total net assets decrease
  2. B. Total net assets increase
  3. C. Total net assets remain unchanged
  4. D. Total liabilities increase
Answer: C. Total net assets remain unchanged

Q12. When a bond is issued at a premium, the carrying value of the bond over its remaining life will:

  1. A. Increase each period
  2. B. Decrease toward the face value
  3. C. Remain constant at the issue price
  4. D. Fluctuate with market interest rates
Answer: B. Decrease toward the face value

Q13. In a period of rising prices and stable inventory quantities, which cost flow assumption will result in the highest reported net income?

  1. A. Weighted average
  2. B. FIFO
  3. C. LIFO
  4. D. Specific identification
Answer: B. FIFO

Q14. How should a material gain from the expropriation of foreign property by a foreign government be presented on the income statement under US GAAP?

  1. A. As an extraordinary item
  2. B. As part of discontinued operations
  3. C. Within income from continuing operations as a separate line item
  4. D. Directly in retained earnings
Answer: C. Within income from continuing operations as a separate line item

Q15. Which of the following cash flows is classified as an operating activity on the statement of cash flows under US GAAP?

  1. A. Cash paid for dividends to stockholders
  2. B. Cash received from the issuance of common stock
  3. C. Cash paid for interest expense
  4. D. Cash received from the sale of equipment
Answer: C. Cash paid for interest expense

Q16. Which inventory cost flow assumption yields the highest cost of goods sold during a period of sustained inflation?

  1. A. FIFO
  2. B. LIFO
  3. C. Weighted Average
  4. D. Specific Identification
Answer: B. LIFO

Q17. How are unearned revenues classified on a classified balance sheet?

  1. A. Non-current assets
  2. B. Current assets
  3. C. Current liabilities
  4. D. Stockholders' equity
Answer: C. Current liabilities

Q18. Under US GAAP, how should development costs incurred internally for a new software product be treated after technological feasibility is established?

  1. A. Expensed immediately as incurred
  2. B. Capitalized as an intangible asset
  3. C. Deferred until the product is sold
  4. D. Recorded as research expense
Answer: B. Capitalized as an intangible asset

Q19. Which of the following items is classified as a financing activity on the statement of cash flows?

  1. A. Payment of cash dividends
  2. B. Purchase of equipment
  3. C. Interest paid on bonds
  4. D. Receipt of dividend revenue
Answer: A. Payment of cash dividends

Q20. What is the primary objective of financial reporting according to the FASB conceptual framework?

  1. A. To calculate the exact market value of the firm
  2. B. To provide useful information to present and potential investors and creditors
  3. C. To ensure zero tax liability for the entity
  4. D. To verify management honesty to internal auditors
Answer: B. To provide useful information to present and potential investors and creditors

Q21. When treasury stock is purchased at a price above its original issuance cost, how is the excess amount accounted for?

  1. A. Recognized as a net income loss
  2. B. Debited to additional paid-in capital from treasury stock
  3. C. Debited to retained earnings if APIC is insufficient
  4. D. Credited directly to common stock
Answer: C. Debited to retained earnings if APIC is insufficient

Q22. When should a loss contingency be accrued according to ASC 450?

  1. A. When the loss is possible but not probable
  2. B. When the loss is probable and the amount can be reasonably estimated
  3. C. When the loss is remote and the amount is unknown
  4. D. When the loss is probable but the amount cannot be estimated
Answer: B. When the loss is probable and the amount can be reasonably estimated

Q23. Which of the following is required to record a deferred tax liability under ASC 740?

  1. A. A temporary difference where the book value of an asset exceeds its tax basis
  2. B. A permanent difference that reduces taxable income
  3. C. An expense that has already been recognized for tax purposes
  4. D. A loss that is probable but not reasonably estimable
Answer: A. A temporary difference where the book value of an asset exceeds its tax basis

Q24. Under US GAAP, research and development costs are generally treated as:

  1. A. Capitalized and amortized over the product life
  2. B. Expensed as incurred
  3. C. Deferred until the product is sold
  4. D. Included in inventory costs
Answer: B. Expensed as incurred

Q25. Which of the following best describes a contingent liability?

  1. A. A liability that is certain to occur in the future
  2. B. A liability that may or may not occur depending on a future event
  3. C. A liability that has already been paid
  4. D. A liability that is recorded only when the loss is realized
Answer: B. A liability that may or may not occur depending on a future event
