Q1. A good CSS essay must have:

  1. A. Only an introduction
  2. B. A clear outline, balanced arguments and a strong conclusion
  3. C. Only quotations
  4. D. No structure
Answer: B. A clear outline, balanced arguments and a strong conclusion

Q2. The thesis statement of an essay presents:

  1. A. A story
  2. B. The writer's main stance/central idea
  3. C. A definition only
  4. D. The conclusion
Answer: B. The writer's main stance/central idea

Q3. In CSS essay marking, the most valued quality is:

  1. A. Length only
  2. B. Clarity, relevance and logical argument
  3. C. Difficult words
  4. D. Handwriting
Answer: B. Clarity, relevance and logical argument

Q4. An essay outline should be:

  1. A. Written after the essay
  2. B. Logical, with headings that cover the topic from all angles
  3. C. Copied from a book
  4. D. Skipped
Answer: B. Logical, with headings that cover the topic from all angles

Q5. A balanced essay on a controversial topic should:

  1. A. Ignore the other side
  2. B. Discuss multiple perspectives before concluding
  3. C. Only criticise
  4. D. Only praise
Answer: B. Discuss multiple perspectives before concluding

Q6. Which opening makes an essay stronger?

  1. A. 'Since the dawn of time...' (cliché)
  2. B. A relevant, precise introduction linked to the topic
  3. C. A long poem
  4. D. A personal story only
Answer: B. A relevant, precise introduction linked to the topic

Q7. Coherence in an essay means:

  1. A. Long paragraphs
  2. B. Ideas flow logically from one to the next
  3. C. Many quotations
  4. D. Big vocabulary
Answer: B. Ideas flow logically from one to the next

Q8. The conclusion of an essay should:

  1. A. Introduce a new topic
  2. B. Summarise the argument and give a way forward
  3. C. Repeat the introduction word for word
  4. D. Ask questions only
Answer: B. Summarise the argument and give a way forward

Q9. For CSS Essay, candidates usually write about:

  1. A. Only literature
  2. B. Current social, economic, political and global issues
  3. C. Only history
  4. D. Only science fiction
Answer: B. Current social, economic, political and global issues

Q10. Critical thinking in an essay is shown by:

  1. A. Copying opinions
  2. B. Analysing causes, effects and solutions with evidence
  3. C. Writing more pages
  4. D. Using quotations in every line
Answer: B. Analysing causes, effects and solutions with evidence

Q11. The modern essay as a literary form was pioneered by:

  1. A. Michel de Montaigne
  2. B. Francis Bacon
  3. C. Joseph Addison
  4. D. Samuel Johnson
Answer: A. Michel de Montaigne

Q12. The word 'essay' comes from the French word 'essai', which means:

  1. A. a sermon
  2. B. an attempt or trial
  3. C. a tale
  4. D. a debate
Answer: B. an attempt or trial

Q13. In Bacon's essay 'Of Studies', he writes: 'Reading maketh a full man; conference a ready man; and writing an ___ man.'

  1. A. wise
  2. B. perfect
  3. C. exact
  4. D. complete
Answer: C. exact

Q14. 'Some books are to be tasted, others to be swallowed, and some few to be chewed and digested' is a famous line by:

  1. A. Joseph Addison
  2. B. Charles Lamb
  3. C. William Hazlitt
  4. D. Francis Bacon
Answer: D. Francis Bacon

Q15. The first edition of Bacon's Essays, published in 1597, contained:

  1. A. 10 essays
  2. B. 24 essays
  3. C. 38 essays
  4. D. 58 essays
Answer: A. 10 essays

Q16. The periodical 'The Spectator' (1711) was founded by:

  1. A. Swift and Pope
  2. B. Addison and Steele
  3. C. Johnson and Boswell
  4. D. Lamb and Hunt
Answer: B. Addison and Steele

Q17. Sir Roger de Coverley, the famous fictional country gentleman, is a character created in:

  1. A. Essays of Elia
  2. B. The Rambler
  3. C. The Spectator
  4. D. Gulliver's Travels
Answer: C. The Spectator

Q18. 'Essays of Elia' was written by:

  1. A. William Hazlitt
  2. B. Thomas De Quincey
  3. C. Leigh Hunt
  4. D. Charles Lamb
Answer: D. Charles Lamb

Q19. Charles Lamb wrote his essays under the pen name:

  1. A. Elia
  2. B. Alpha of the Plough
  3. C. Boz
  4. D. George Eliot
Answer: A. Elia

Q20. 'Dream Children: A Reverie' is a well-known personal essay by:

  1. A. William Hazlitt
  2. B. Charles Lamb
  3. C. John Keats
  4. D. S. T. Coleridge
Answer: B. Charles Lamb

Q21. 'A Dissertation upon Roast Pig' is a humorous essay by:

  1. A. Joseph Addison
  2. B. Leigh Hunt
  3. C. Charles Lamb
  4. D. Thomas De Quincey
Answer: C. Charles Lamb

Q22. 'A Modest Proposal' (1729), the famous satirical essay suggesting a shocking solution to poverty in Ireland, was written by:

  1. A. Alexander Pope
  2. B. Samuel Johnson
  3. C. Daniel Defoe
  4. D. Jonathan Swift
Answer: D. Jonathan Swift

Q23. Samuel Johnson wrote most of the essays in the periodical:

  1. A. The Rambler
  2. B. The Guardian
  3. C. The Spectator
  4. D. The Tatler
Answer: A. The Rambler

Q24. Dr Samuel Johnson defined an essay as 'a loose ___ of the mind; an irregular, indigested piece.'

  1. A. system
  2. B. sally
  3. C. sermon
  4. D. structure
Answer: B. sally

Q25. George Orwell's famous essay on the decay of language and political writing is:

  1. A. Why I Write
  2. B. Shooting an Elephant
  3. C. Politics and the English Language
  4. D. Books v. Cigarettes
Answer: C. Politics and the English Language
