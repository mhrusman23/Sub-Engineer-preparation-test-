Q1. A good CSS essay must have:

  1. A. Only an introduction
  2. B. A clear outline, balanced arguments and a strong conclusion
  3. C. Only quotations
  4. D. No structure
Answer: B. A clear outline, balanced arguments and a strong conclusion

Q2. The thesis statement of an essay presents:

  1. A. A story
  2. B. The writer's main stance/central idea
  3. C. A definition only
  4. D. The conclusion
Answer: B. The writer's main stance/central idea

Q3. In CSS essay marking, the most valued quality is:

  1. A. Length only
  2. B. Clarity, relevance and logical argument
  3. C. Difficult words
  4. D. Handwriting
Answer: B. Clarity, relevance and logical argument

Q4. An essay outline should be:

  1. A. Written after the essay
  2. B. Logical, with headings that cover the topic from all angles
  3. C. Copied from a book
  4. D. Skipped
Answer: B. Logical, with headings that cover the topic from all angles

Q5. A balanced essay on a controversial topic should:

  1. A. Ignore the other side
  2. B. Discuss multiple perspectives before concluding
  3. C. Only criticise
  4. D. Only praise
Answer: B. Discuss multiple perspectives before concluding

Q6. Which opening makes an essay stronger?

  1. A. 'Since the dawn of time...' (cliché)
  2. B. A relevant, precise introduction linked to the topic
  3. C. A long poem
  4. D. A personal story only
Answer: B. A relevant, precise introduction linked to the topic

Q7. Coherence in an essay means:

  1. A. Long paragraphs
  2. B. Ideas flow logically from one to the next
  3. C. Many quotations
  4. D. Big vocabulary
Answer: B. Ideas flow logically from one to the next

Q8. The conclusion of an essay should:

  1. A. Introduce a new topic
  2. B. Summarise the argument and give a way forward
  3. C. Repeat the introduction word for word
  4. D. Ask questions only
Answer: B. Summarise the argument and give a way forward

Q9. For CSS Essay, candidates usually write about:

  1. A. Only literature
  2. B. Current social, economic, political and global issues
  3. C. Only history
  4. D. Only science fiction
Answer: B. Current social, economic, political and global issues

Q10. Critical thinking in an essay is shown by:

  1. A. Copying opinions
  2. B. Analysing causes, effects and solutions with evidence
  3. C. Writing more pages
  4. D. Using quotations in every line
Answer: B. Analysing causes, effects and solutions with evidence

Q11. Why is statistical data and empirical evidence crucial when writing essays for examinations like FPSC and PPSC?

  1. A. It allows the writer to fabricate impressive-looking figures without verification
  2. B. It substantiates arguments with objective proof, strengthening credibility
  3. C. It replaces the need for a thesis statement entirely
  4. D. It makes the essay look like a mathematics paper
Answer: B. It substantiates arguments with objective proof, strengthening credibility

Q12. What characterizes a well-structured essay outline in a high-stakes civil services exam?

  1. A. A chaotic list of random thoughts jotted down in haste
  2. B. A logical hierarchy covering introduction, thematic body headings, sub-points, and a forward-looking conclusion
  3. C. A single long paragraph summarizing all headings together
  4. D. Writing the outline only after the entire essay has been completed
Answer: B. A logical hierarchy covering introduction, thematic body headings, sub-points, and a forward-looking conclusion

Q13. What is the recommended approach for concluding a reflective essay on a global crisis?

  1. A. Introducing an entirely new major argument that was never discussed before
  2. B. Ending abruptly without summarizing the main points
  3. C. Synthesizing the core arguments and presenting a constructive, pragmatic way forward
  4. D. Complaining about the severity of the problem with no hope for resolution
Answer: C. Synthesizing the core arguments and presenting a constructive, pragmatic way forward

Q14. What is the primary function of a topic sentence at the beginning of a body paragraph in a competitive English essay?

  1. A. To introduce a completely new sub-topic unrelated to the thesis
  2. B. To summarize the entire conclusion of the essay
  3. C. To guide the reader by stating the main argument or focus of that specific paragraph
  4. D. To fill up word count with decorative phrases
Answer: C. To guide the reader by stating the main argument or focus of that specific paragraph

Q15. Which of the following best exemplifies effective transitional devices within a formal essay?

  1. A. Using slang terms to shift paragraphs
  2. B. Relying solely on numeric bullet points
  3. C. Employing linking words like 'consequently', 'furthermore', and 'conversely' to maintain flow
  4. D. Starting every single paragraph with the exact same phrase
Answer: C. Employing linking words like 'consequently', 'furthermore', and 'conversely' to maintain flow

Q16. How should an aspirant handle counter-arguments in a comprehensive CSS essay addressing a socio-economic debate?

  1. A. Ignore them completely to avoid confusion
  2. B. Acknowledge opposing viewpoints fairly and refute them logically with solid reasoning
  3. C. Mock the opposing stance to show superior conviction
  4. D. Place all counter-arguments strictly in the introductory paragraph
Answer: B. Acknowledge opposing viewpoints fairly and refute them logically with solid reasoning

Q17. What role does empirical evidence play in a competitive essay?

  1. A. It makes the argument objective and credible
  2. B. It lengthens the paragraph count unnecessarily
  3. C. It replaces the need for a thesis statement
  4. D. It serves only as a decorative element
Answer: A. It makes the argument objective and credible

Q18. What is the primary function of a topic sentence in a body paragraph?

  1. A. To introduce a completely new topic
  2. B. To state the main idea of that specific paragraph
  3. C. To summarize the entire essay conclusion
  4. D. To provide a random quotation
Answer: B. To state the main idea of that specific paragraph

Q19. Which of the following best characterizes effective transition words between paragraphs?

  1. A. They create smooth logical bridges between ideas
  2. B. They increase the word count without meaning
  3. C. They signal the end of the essay abruptly
  4. D. They confuse the reader intentionally
Answer: A. They create smooth logical bridges between ideas

Q20. An essay outline for CSS should primarily serve as:

  1. A. A rigid list of unrelated facts
  2. B. A logical roadmap guiding the entire argument
  3. C. A collection of memorized quotations
  4. D. A detailed summary of the conclusion
Answer: B. A logical roadmap guiding the entire argument

Q21. How should a candidate address counter-arguments in a competitive essay?

  1. A. Ignore them completely to avoid confusion
  2. B. Acknowledge and objectively refute them with reasoning
  3. C. Mock them to strengthen personal bias
  4. D. Accept them as the final truth of the topic
Answer: B. Acknowledge and objectively refute them with reasoning

Q22. The conclusion of a successful CSS essay should:

  1. A. Introduce completely new arguments
  2. B. Synthesize main points and offer a forward-looking thought
  3. C. Repeat the introduction word for word
  4. D. End abruptly without a final remark
Answer: B. Synthesize main points and offer a forward-looking thought

Q23. What is the primary purpose of writing a thesis statement in a CSS essay?

  1. A. To list all statistical data to be used
  2. B. To state the central argument that guides the entire paper
  3. C. To thank the examiner for evaluation
  4. D. To introduce an irrelevant personal anecdote
Answer: B. To state the central argument that guides the entire paper

Q24. What is the best way to demonstrate analytical depth rather than mere descriptive narration?

  1. A. Listing random historical events in chronological order
  2. B. Examining causes, socio-economic impacts, and evaluating potential solutions
  3. C. Writing emotional appeals without factual backing
  4. D. Repeating the prompt multiple times
Answer: B. Examining causes, socio-economic impacts, and evaluating potential solutions

Q25. Which tone is most appropriate for a formal competitive examination essay?

  1. A. Sarcastic and aggressive
  2. B. Objective, analytical, and professional
  3. C. Overly casual and colloquial
  4. D. Melodramatic and overly emotional
Answer: B. Objective, analytical, and professional
