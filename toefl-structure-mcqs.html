Q1. 'The results of the experiment ___ surprising.'

  1. A. was
  2. B. were
  3. C. is
  4. D. be
Answer: B. were

Q2. Which sentence is correct?

  1. A. Although it was raining, but we went out.
  2. B. Although it was raining, we went out.
  3. C. Although it was raining we, went out.
  4. D. It was raining although, we went out.
Answer: B. Although it was raining, we went out.

Q3. 'By this time tomorrow, I ___ the exam.'

  1. A. finish
  2. B. will have finished
  3. C. am finish
  4. D. finished yesterday
Answer: B. will have finished

Q4. Choose the correct form: 'The committee ___ its decision.'

  1. A. have made
  2. B. has made
  3. C. make
  4. D. making
Answer: B. has made

Q5. 'No sooner had we arrived ___ it started to rain.'

  1. A. when
  2. B. than
  3. C. then
  4. D. that
Answer: B. than

Q6. Which is correct?

  1. A. He is one of the best student.
  2. B. He is one of the best students.
  3. C. He is one of best student.
  4. D. He is the one of best students.
Answer: B. He is one of the best students.

Q7. 'The data ___ been analysed.'

  1. A. has
  2. B. have
  3. C. is
  4. D. are
Answer: B. have

Q8. 'Hardly ___ the door when the phone rang.'

  1. A. I had closed
  2. B. had I closed
  3. C. I have closed
  4. D. did I closed
Answer: B. had I closed

Q9. In the sentence "She has been studying for three hours," which grammatical structure is used?

  1. A. Past simple
  2. B. Present perfect continuous
  3. C. Future continuous
  4. D. Past continuous
Answer: B. Present perfect continuous

Q10. Which sentence correctly uses a modal verb to express obligation?

  1. A. You must finish the report by tomorrow.
  2. B. You must finish the report until tomorrow.
  3. C. You must finish the report from tomorrow.
  4. D. You must finish the report after tomorrow.
Answer: A. You must finish the report by tomorrow.

Q11. Identify the sentence that contains a subject-verb agreement error.

  1. A. The group of students are preparing for the exam.
  2. B. The team of researchers is publishing their findings.
  3. C. Each of the candidates has submitted a resume.
  4. D. The committee has reached a consensus.
Answer: A. The group of students are preparing for the exam.

Q12. Choose the correct form to complete: "If I ___ you, I would take the job."

  1. A. was
  2. B. were
  3. C. am
  4. D. be
Answer: B. were

Q13. Which option completes the sentence: "The committee will announce its decision ___ Friday."

  1. A. on
  2. B. at
  3. C. by
  4. D. in
Answer: A. on

Q14. Choose the sentence that correctly uses a comparative adjective.

  1. A. This book is more interesting than the movie.
  2. B. This book is more interesting then the movie.
  3. C. This book is more interesting to the movie.
  4. D. This book is more interesting at the movie.
Answer: A. This book is more interesting than the movie.

Q15. Each of the reports submitted by the department ___ thoroughly reviewed before publication.

  1. A. have been
  2. B. has been
  3. C. are being
  4. D. were being
Answer: B. has been

Q16. The more diligently you study for the exam, ___ your final score will be.

  1. A. the highest
  2. B. higher
  3. C. the higher
  4. D. highest
Answer: C. the higher

Q17. Scarcely had the shipment arrived at the warehouse ___ the distribution process started.

  1. A. than
  2. B. when
  3. C. then
  4. D. before
Answer: B. when

Q18. Neither the professor nor the teaching assistants ___ available for consultation this afternoon.

  1. A. is
  2. B. are
  3. C. was
  4. D. has
Answer: B. are

Q19. One of the primary challenges facing urban planners ___ traffic congestion during peak hours.

  1. A. are
  2. B. is
  3. C. were
  4. D. have been
Answer: B. is

Q20. Hardly had the lecture begun ___ a student raised her hand to ask a question.

  1. A. than
  2. B. when
  3. C. then
  4. D. that
Answer: B. when

Q21. Which sentence is grammatically correct?

  1. A. Although the weather was bad, we decided to go hiking.
  2. B. Although the weather was bad but we decided to go hiking.
  3. C. Although the weather was bad we decided to go hiking.
  4. D. Although the weather was bad, we decided to go, hiking.
Answer: A. Although the weather was bad, we decided to go hiking.

Q22. The more you ___ the practice tests, the better your score will be.

  1. A. do
  2. B. does
  3. C. did
  4. D. done
Answer: A. do

Q23. The more you study the advanced grammar rules, ___ you will become at solving structure questions.

  1. A. the proficient
  2. B. the more proficient
  3. C. the most proficient
  4. D. more proficient
Answer: B. the more proficient

Q24. Each of the packages delivered to the office ___ inspected for safety.

  1. A. have been
  2. B. has been
  3. C. were being
  4. D. are being
Answer: B. has been

Q25. Despite heavy traffic on the highway, we ___ to the examination center on time.

  1. A. arrive
  2. B. arrived
  3. C. arriving
  4. D. have arrive
Answer: B. arrived
