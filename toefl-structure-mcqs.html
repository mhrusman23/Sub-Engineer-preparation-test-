Q1. Choose the correct sentence:

  1. A. The teacher explained the lesson clearly.
  2. B. The teacher clearly the lesson explained.
  3. C. Explained the teacher the lesson clearly.
  4. D. The lesson the teacher explained clearly it.
Answer: A. The teacher explained the lesson clearly.

Q2. 'The results of the experiment ___ surprising.'

  1. A. was
  2. B. were
  3. C. is
  4. D. be
Answer: B. were

Q3. Which sentence is correct?

  1. A. Although it was raining, but we went out.
  2. B. Although it was raining, we went out.
  3. C. Although it was raining we, went out.
  4. D. It was raining although, we went out.
Answer: B. Although it was raining, we went out.

Q4. 'By this time tomorrow, I ___ the exam.'

  1. A. finish
  2. B. will have finished
  3. C. am finish
  4. D. finished yesterday
Answer: B. will have finished

Q5. Choose the correct form: 'The committee ___ its decision.'

  1. A. have made
  2. B. has made
  3. C. make
  4. D. making
Answer: B. has made

Q6. 'No sooner had we arrived ___ it started to rain.'

  1. A. when
  2. B. than
  3. C. then
  4. D. that
Answer: B. than

Q7. Which is correct?

  1. A. He is one of the best student.
  2. B. He is one of the best students.
  3. C. He is one of best student.
  4. D. He is the one of best students.
Answer: B. He is one of the best students.

Q8. 'The data ___ been analysed.'

  1. A. has
  2. B. have
  3. C. is
  4. D. are
Answer: B. have

Q9. Choose the correct sentence:

  1. A. I suggest that he goes home.
  2. B. I suggest that he go home.
  3. C. I suggest that he going home.
  4. D. I suggest that he went go home.
Answer: B. I suggest that he go home.

Q10. 'Hardly ___ the door when the phone rang.'

  1. A. I had closed
  2. B. had I closed
  3. C. I have closed
  4. D. did I closed
Answer: B. had I closed

Q11. Choose the correct sentence:

  1. A. The book that I bought it is new.
  2. B. The book that I bought is new.
  3. C. The book what I bought is new.
  4. D. The book I bought it is new.
Answer: B. The book that I bought is new.

Q12. 'The manager, as well as the workers, ___ present.'

  1. A. were
  2. B. was
  3. C. are
  4. D. be
Answer: B. was

Q13. Choose the correct sentence:

  1. A. Scarcely had he entered when the phone rang.
  2. B. Scarcely he had entered when the phone rang.
  3. C. Scarcely had he entered than the phone rang.
  4. D. Scarcely did he entered when the phone rang.
Answer: A. Scarcely had he entered when the phone rang.

Q14. 'It is essential that she ___ on time.'

  1. A. is
  2. B. be
  3. C. will be
  4. D. being
Answer: B. be

Q15. Choose the correct form: 'The report needs ___.'

  1. A. revise
  2. B. revising / to be revised
  3. C. revised it yesterday by
  4. D. revise by
Answer: B. revising / to be revised

Q16. 'Not only ___ late, but he also forgot the file.'

  1. A. he was
  2. B. was he
  3. C. he is being
  4. D. being he
Answer: B. was he

Q17. Choose the correct sentence:

  1. A. Having finished the work, the manager left.
  2. B. Having finished the work, the office was closed by the manager leaving.
  3. C. Having finished, the work the manager left.
  4. D. Finished the work, the manager he left.
Answer: A. Having finished the work, the manager left.

Q18. 'The committee members ___ among themselves.' (each acting separately)

  1. A. disagrees
  2. B. disagree
  3. C. is disagreeing be
  4. D. disagreeing
Answer: B. disagree

Q19. Choose the correct sentence:

  1. A. I suggest that he study harder.
  2. B. I suggest that he studies harder always to.
  3. C. I suggest that he studying harder.
  4. D. I suggest that he studied harder yesterday now.
Answer: A. I suggest that he study harder.

Q20. 'By the time we arrived, the film ___.'

  1. A. already started
  2. B. had already started
  3. C. has start
  4. D. starts
Answer: B. had already started
