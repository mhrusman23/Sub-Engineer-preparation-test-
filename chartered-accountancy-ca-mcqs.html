Q1. Under IFRS 3, how should an acquirer measure a non-controlling interest (NCI) in the acquiree on the acquisition date?

  1. A. Always at fair value
  2. B. Always at proportionate share of net identifiable assets
  3. C. At fair value or proportionate share of net identifiable assets on a transaction-by-transaction basis
  4. D. At the present value of future cash flows
Answer: C. At fair value or proportionate share of net identifiable assets on a transaction-by-transaction basis

Q2. When an entity applies hedge accounting under IFRS 9, how is a cash flow hedge of a forecasted transaction accounted for?

  1. A. The entire change in fair value is recognized immediately in profit or loss
  2. B. The effective portion of the gain or loss is recognized in other comprehensive income and accumulated in equity
  3. C. The entire change in fair value is recognized in other comprehensive income permanently
  4. D. No entries are made until the forecasted transaction actually occurs
Answer: B. The effective portion of the gain or loss is recognized in other comprehensive income and accumulated in equity

Q3. According to IFRS 16, how does a lessee initially measure its lease liability?

  1. A. At the total undiscounted lease payments
  2. B. At the present value of the lease payments not paid at that date
  3. C. At the fair value of the underlying asset only
  4. D. At historical cost
Answer: B. At the present value of the lease payments not paid at that date

Q4. Under IFRS 2, how should a cash-settled share-based payment transaction be initially and subsequently measured?

  1. A. At intrinsic value initially and at grant date fair value subsequently
  2. B. At fair value at each reporting date and at the date of settlement, with changes in fair value recognized in profit or loss
  3. C. At historical cost without any revaluation
  4. D. At the present value of future cash flows discounted using the risk-free rate
Answer: B. At fair value at each reporting date and at the date of settlement, with changes in fair value recognized in profit or loss

Q5. Under IAS 12, how should the tax effects of items recognized outside profit or loss (either in OCI or directly in equity) be recognized?

  1. A. Always in profit or loss to offset operating income
  2. B. Outside profit or loss, in correlation with the items to which they relate
  3. C. Deferred indefinitely until the entity is liquidated
  4. D. Recognized directly in retained earnings without exception
Answer: B. Outside profit or loss, in correlation with the items to which they relate

Q6. Under IFRS 13, what represents the highest and best use of a non-financial asset for fair value measurement purposes?

  1. A. The use that is currently being made by management
  2. B. The use that maximizes the asset's value by market participants considering physical, legal, and financial feasibility
  3. C. The historical cost multiplied by the consumer price index
  4. D. The lowest cost alternative available to the entity
Answer: B. The use that maximizes the asset's value by market participants considering physical, legal, and financial feasibility

Q7. How should a parent account for the loss of control of a subsidiary while retaining a non-controlling financial interest under IFRS 10?

  1. A. Recognize the retained interest at historical cost without any gain or loss
  2. B. Derecognize all assets, liabilities, and NCI, and recognize any investment retained at fair value with the resulting gain or loss in profit or loss
  3. C. Keep all subsidiary assets on the balance sheet until the remaining shares are sold
  4. D. Recognize the gain or loss entirely in other comprehensive income
Answer: B. Derecognize all assets, liabilities, and NCI, and recognize any investment retained at fair value with the resulting gain or loss in profit or loss

Q8. Under IAS 36, what is the recoverable amount of an asset or cash-generating unit?

  1. A. The higher of fair value less costs of disposal and value in use
  2. B. The lower of fair value and historical cost
  3. C. The book value plus accumulated depreciation
  4. D. The market value determined by management
Answer: A. The higher of fair value less costs of disposal and value in use

Q9. Under IAS 24, which of the following is considered a related party of a reporting entity?

  1. A. A regular customer with whom the entity transacts a large volume of business
  2. B. A close family member of a person who has joint control over the reporting entity
  3. C. A provider of finance solely in the course of their normal business with the entity
  4. D. A trade union with which the entity negotiates wages
Answer: B. A close family member of a person who has joint control over the reporting entity

Q10. How should an entity account for a change in valuation technique used to measure fair value under IFRS 13?

  1. A. As a correction of a prior period error retrospectively
  2. B. As a change in accounting estimate applied prospectively
  3. C. By adjusting opening retained earnings directly without disclosure
  4. D. As a change in accounting policy applied retrospectively
Answer: B. As a change in accounting estimate applied prospectively

Q11. Under IAS 27 (Separate Financial Statements), how must an entity account for investments in subsidiaries, joint ventures, and associates in its separate financial statements?

  1. A. At cost, or in accordance with IFRS 9, or using the equity method
  2. B. Always using the equity method
  3. C. Always at fair value through profit or loss
  4. D. Using proportionate consolidation only
Answer: A. At cost, or in accordance with IFRS 9, or using the equity method

Q12. Under IAS 21, how should a foreign operation's financial statements be translated into the presentation currency when its functional currency is not hyperinflationary?

  1. A. Assets and liabilities at historical rates, income and expenses at transaction rates
  2. B. Assets and liabilities at closing rates, income and expenses at exchange rates at the dates of the transactions
  3. C. All items at the average rate for the period
  4. D. All items at the opening rate of the period
Answer: B. Assets and liabilities at closing rates, income and expenses at exchange rates at the dates of the transactions

Q13. Under IAS 12, how are deferred tax assets and liabilities measured?

  1. A. Using the tax rates that have been enacted or substantively enacted by the end of the reporting period
  2. B. Using future estimated tax rates
  3. C. At historical tax rates
  4. D. At discounted present value
Answer: A. Using the tax rates that have been enacted or substantively enacted by the end of the reporting period

Q14. Which of the following standards governs the independent auditor's report on financial statements?

  1. A. ISA 200
  2. B. ISA 500
  3. C. ISA 700
  4. D. ISA 240
Answer: C. ISA 700

Q15. What type of test is performed to obtain audit evidence about the operating effectiveness of controls?

  1. A. Substantive procedure
  2. B. Test of controls
  3. C. Analytical procedure
  4. D. Walkthrough test
Answer: B. Test of controls

Q16. Which component constitutes the audit risk model alongside control risk and detection risk?

  1. A. Business risk
  2. B. Inherent risk
  3. C. Sampling risk
  4. D. Financial risk
Answer: B. Inherent risk

Q17. What is the primary objective of an external audit of financial statements?

  1. A. To detect all frauds and errors
  2. B. To express an opinion on the financial statements
  3. C. To prepare the annual accounts
  4. D. To provide tax advisory services
Answer: B. To express an opinion on the financial statements

Q18. Which of the following is considered a negative confirmation request?

  1. A. A request where the responding party responds only if they disagree
  2. B. A request where the responding party always responds
  3. C. A request sent only to bank debtors
  4. D. A request used exclusively for inventory counts
Answer: A. A request where the responding party responds only if they disagree

Q19. Who is primarily responsible for the preparation and fair presentation of the financial statements?

  1. A. The external auditor
  2. B. Those charged with governance and management
  3. C. The internal audit department
  4. D. The shareholders
Answer: B. Those charged with governance and management

Q20. What term describes the risk that the auditor expresses an inappropriate audit opinion when the financial statements are materially misstated?

  1. A. Business risk
  2. B. Audit risk
  3. C. Detection risk
  4. D. Inherent risk
Answer: B. Audit risk

Q21. Which of the following refers to checking a sample of transactions from the ledger back to the original source documents?

  1. A. Tracing
  2. B. Vouching
  3. C. Counting
  4. D. Inquiry
Answer: B. Vouching

Q22. Which professional ethical principle requires an auditor to be straightforward and honest in all professional and business relationships?

  1. A. Objectivity
  2. B. Integrity
  3. C. Professional competence
  4. D. Confidentiality
Answer: B. Integrity

Q23. Which of the following describes the auditor's fundamental responsibility regarding the prevention and detection of fraud?

  1. A. To guarantee the complete absence of fraud in financial statements
  2. B. To design an internal control system for the client
  3. C. To maintain professional skepticism throughout the audit
  4. D. To perform a detailed forensic investigation of all transactions
Answer: C. To maintain professional skepticism throughout the audit

Q24. What type of audit procedure is specifically designed to detect material misstatements at the assertion level?

  1. A. Risk assessment procedure
  2. B. Substantive procedure
  3. C. Analytical review of governance
  4. D. Preliminary engagement activity
Answer: B. Substantive procedure

Q25. When an auditor decides to set control risk at a higher level, how does it affect the extent of substantive testing?

  1. A. It reduces the need for substantive testing
  2. B. It has no impact on substantive procedures
  3. C. It requires an increase in the extent of substantive testing
  4. D. It eliminates the need for analytical procedures
Answer: C. It requires an increase in the extent of substantive testing
