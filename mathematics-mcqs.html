Q1. 15% of 200 is:

  1. A. 15
  2. B. 25
  3. C. 30
  4. D. 20
Answer: C. 30

Q2. The LCM of 12 and 18 is:

  1. A. 72
  2. B. 6
  3. C. 36
  4. D. 54
Answer: C. 36

Q3. The HCF of 24 and 36 is:

  1. A. 12
  2. B. 18
  3. C. 6
  4. D. 24
Answer: A. 12

Q4. The square root of 144 is:

  1. A. 14
  2. B. 11
  3. C. 12
  4. D. 13
Answer: C. 12

Q5. The sum of angles of a triangle is:

  1. A. 90 degrees
  2. B. 180 degrees
  3. C. 360 degrees
  4. D. 270 degrees

Q6. The area of a circle with radius r is:

  1. A. pi r squared

  3. C. pi d
  4. D. pi r
