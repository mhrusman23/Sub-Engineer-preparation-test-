Q1. The normal fetal heart rate range is:

  1. A. 80-100 bpm
  2. B. 110-160 bpm
  3. C. 170-200 bpm
  4. D. 60-90 bpm
Answer: B. 110-160 bpm

Q2. Folic acid before and during early pregnancy prevents:

  1. A. Diabetes
  2. B. Neural tube defects in the baby
  3. C. High blood pressure
  4. D. Anaemia only
Answer: B. Neural tube defects in the baby

Q3. The first stage of labour ends when:

  1. A. The baby is born
  2. B. The cervix is fully dilated (10 cm)
  3. C. Contractions start
  4. D. The placenta is delivered
Answer: B. The cervix is fully dilated (10 cm)

Q4. A newborn's Apgar score is assessed at:

  1. A. Birth only
  2. B. 1 and 5 minutes after birth
  3. C. 24 hours
  4. D. One week
Answer: B. 1 and 5 minutes after birth

Q5. Preeclampsia is characterised by:

  1. A. Low blood pressure
  2. B. High blood pressure and protein in the urine
  3. C. Fever
  4. D. Low blood sugar
Answer: B. High blood pressure and protein in the urine

Q6. Breastfeeding should ideally begin:

  1. A. After one week
  2. B. Within the first hour after birth
  3. C. After the first bath only
  4. D. At one month
Answer: B. Within the first hour after birth

Q7. The anterior fontanelle of a baby normally closes by about:

  1. A. 1 month
  2. B. 18 months
  3. C. 5 years
  4. D. At birth
Answer: B. 18 months

Q8. Kangaroo mother care is especially used for:

  1. A. Older children
  2. B. Preterm and low-birth-weight babies
  3. C. Teenagers
  4. D. Twins only
Answer: B. Preterm and low-birth-weight babies

Q9. A child with dehydration will show:

  1. A. Excess tears
  2. B. Dry mouth, sunken eyes and low urine output
  3. C. Weight gain
  4. D. Fast growth
Answer: B. Dry mouth, sunken eyes and low urine output

Q10. During pregnancy, the drug most known to harm the fetus is:

  1. A. Paracetamol in normal doses
  2. B. Alcohol
  3. C. Iron tablets
  4. D. Folic acid
Answer: B. Alcohol

Q11. A postpartum client who soaked two perineal pads within 15 minutes is most likely experiencing:

  1. A. A) Normal lochia rubra
  2. B. B) Postpartum hemorrhage
  3. C. C) Endometritis
  4. D. D) Normal diuresis
Answer: B. B) Postpartum hemorrhage

Q12. When assessing a newborn, the nurse notes bluish discoloration of the hands and feet while the central body is pink. This finding is documented as:

  1. A. A) Jaundice
  2. B. B) Acrocyanosis
  3. C. C) Vernix caseosa
  4. D. D) Milia
Answer: B. B) Acrocyanosis

Q13. Which sign is considered a presumptive (subjective) indicator of pregnancy?

  1. A. A) Fetal heart tones
  2. B. B) Amenorrhea
  3. C. C) Ultrasound visualization
  4. D. D) Palpation of fetal movement by examiner
Answer: B. B) Amenorrhea

Q14. During which stage of labor does full cervical dilation and 100% effacement occur?

  1. A. A) First stage
  2. B. B) Second stage
  3. C. C) Third stage
  4. D. D) Fourth stage
Answer: A. A) First stage

Q15. The primary purpose of administering vitamin K intramuscularly to a newborn shortly after birth is to:

  1. A. A) Prevent hemorrhagic disease of the newborn
  2. B. B) Stimulate surfactant production in the lungs
  3. C. C) Boost long-term immunity against hepatitis
  4. D. D) Prevent ophthalmia neonatorum
Answer: A. A) Prevent hemorrhagic disease of the newborn

Q16. The hormone responsible for the positive result on a standard home pregnancy test is:

  1. A. A) Progesterone
  2. B. B) Human chorionic gonadotropin
  3. C. C) Oxytocin
  4. D. D) Estriol
Answer: B. B) Human chorionic gonadotropin

Q17. A mother with gestational diabetes requires which monitoring test to evaluate the baby’s glucose status after delivery?

  1. A. Neonatal heel-prick glucose test
  2. B. Maternal fasting glucose
  3. C. Cord blood ketones
  4. D. Fetal heart rate tracing
Answer: A. Neonatal heel-prick glucose test

Q18. Which vitamin is essential for the newborn’s skeletal development and should be supplemented in the immediate post‑delivery period?

  1. A. Vitamin D
  2. B. Vitamin C
  3. C. Vitamin B12
  4. D. Vitamin K
Answer: A. Vitamin D

Q19. At what age should an infant typically be able to sit unsupported without assistance?

  1. A. A) 2 to 3 months
  2. B. B) 6 to 8 months
  3. C. C) 10 to 12 months
  4. D. D) 15 to 18 months
Answer: B. B) 6 to 8 months

Q20. What is the primary rationale for administering oxytocin (Pitocin) immediately following the third stage of labor?

  1. A. A) To relieve postpartum perineal pain
  2. B. B) To stimulate milk letdown for breastfeeding
  3. C. C) To contract the uterine musculature and prevent postpartum hemorrhage
  4. D. D) To expedite the expulsion of the fetal membranes
Answer: C. C) To contract the uterine musculature and prevent postpartum hemorrhage

Q21. Which physiological finding is a definitive diagnostic sign of pregnancy rather than a presumptive or probable sign?

  1. A. A) Amenorrhea
  2. B. B) Uterine enlargement
  3. C. C) Fetal heartbeat visualization via ultrasound
  4. D. D) Positive home pregnancy urine test
Answer: C. C) Fetal heartbeat visualization via ultrasound

Q22. A postpartum mother on her second day postpartum reports painful uterine cramps while breastfeeding. How should the nurse interpret this finding?

  1. A. A) An abnormal indication of retained placental fragments
  2. B. B) A normal physiological response caused by oxytocin release during nursing
  3. C. C) A symptom of severe postpartum urinary tract infection
  4. D. D) An early sign of impending deep vein thrombosis
Answer: B. B) A normal physiological response caused by oxytocin release during nursing

Q23. When is Rh immunoglobulin (RhoGAM) routinely administered to an Rh-negative pregnant woman to prevent isoimmunization?

  1. A. A) At 12 weeks and immediately after the first trimester
  2. B. B) At 28 weeks gestation and within 72 hours post-delivery if the infant is Rh-positive
  3. C. C) Only after delivery if the baby is Rh-negative
  4. D. D) Every month throughout the entire gestation period
Answer: B. B) At 28 weeks gestation and within 72 hours post-delivery if the infant is Rh-positive

Q24. Which clinical manifestation is most characteristic of respiratory distress syndrome (RDS) in a preterm newborn?

  1. A. A) Bradypnea and lethargy
  2. B. B) Expiratory grunting, nasal flaring, and intercostal retractions
  3. C. C) Acrocyanosis and irregular heart rate
  4. D. D) Persistent hyperactive startle reflex
Answer: B. B) Expiratory grunting, nasal flaring, and intercostal retractions

Q25. Which sign is classified as a probable (objective) indicator of pregnancy?

  1. A. Nausea and vomiting in the morning
  2. B. Positive urine pregnancy test detecting hCG
  3. C. Fetal heart tones heard by ultrasound
  4. D. Quickening felt by the mother
Answer: B. Positive urine pregnancy test detecting hCG
