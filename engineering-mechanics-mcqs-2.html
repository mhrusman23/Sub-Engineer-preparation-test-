Q1. The SI unit of force is the:

  1. A. Pascal
  2. B. Watt
  3. C. Joule
  4. D. Newton
Answer: D. Newton

Q2. Newton's second law states that force equals mass times:

  1. A. Time
  2. B. Acceleration
  3. C. Velocity
  4. D. Displacement
Answer: B. Acceleration

Q3. The moment of a force is the product of force and:

  1. A. Velocity
  2. B. Time
  3. C. Mass
  4. D. Perpendicular distance
Answer: D. Perpendicular distance

Q4. A body is in equilibrium when the resultant force is:

  1. A. Equal to weight
  2. B. Zero
  3. C. Maximum
  4. D. Infinite
Answer: B. Zero

Q5. The centroid of a rectangle is at its:

  1. A. Corner
  2. B. Edge
  3. C. Geometric centre
  4. D. Base
Answer: C. Geometric centre

Q6. The unit of work is the:

  1. A. Pascal
  2. B. Newton
  3. C. Joule
  4. D. Watt
Answer: C. Joule

Q7. Power is the rate of doing:

  1. A. Force
  2. B. Work
  3. C. Distance
  4. D. Mass
Answer: B. Work

Q8. The coefficient of friction is the ratio of limiting friction to:

  1. A. Normal reaction
  2. B. Weight times velocity
  3. C. Area
  4. D. Mass
Answer: A. Normal reaction

Q9. Momentum is the product of mass and:

  1. A. Acceleration
  2. B. Time
  3. C. Velocity
  4. D. Force
Answer: C. Velocity

Q10. A couple consists of two equal and opposite forces that are:

  1. A. Collinear
  2. B. Equal and in the same line
  3. C. Perpendicular
  4. D. Parallel and non-collinear
Answer: D. Parallel and non-collinear

Q11. Which term describes the capacity of a strained body to do work on being released?

  1. A. Kinetic energy
  2. B. Strain energy
  3. C. Potential energy
  4. D. Thermal energy
Answer: B. Strain energy

Q12. What is the ratio of lateral strain to longitudinal strain within the elastic limit called?

  1. A. Bulk modulus
  2. B. Modulus of rigidity
  3. C. Poisson's ratio
  4. D. Young's modulus
Answer: C. Poisson's ratio

Q13. Which quantity remains conserved in an elastic impact between two moving bodies?

  1. A. Only kinetic energy
  2. B. Only momentum
  3. C. Both momentum and kinetic energy
  4. D. Neither momentum nor energy
Answer: C. Both momentum and kinetic energy

Q14. What is the polar moment of inertia of a circular cross-section with diameter d about its centroidal axis?

  1. A. pi * d^4 / 32
  2. B. pi * d^4 / 64
  3. C. pi * d^3 / 16
  4. D. pi * d^4 / 12
Answer: A. pi * d^4 / 32

Q15. Which theorem states that if three coplanar forces acting at a point are in equilibrium, each force is proportional to the sine of the angle between the other two?

  1. A. Varignon's theorem
  2. B. Lami's theorem
  3. C. Parallel axis theorem
  4. D. Superposition theorem
Answer: B. Lami's theorem
