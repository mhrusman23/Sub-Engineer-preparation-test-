Q1. Paint with white lead base is suitable for painting of......

  1. A. wood work
  2. B. plaster
  3. C. Steel work
  4. D. all of these
Answer: B. plaster

Q2. A type of roof suitable in plain areas where rainfall is less....

  1. A. Shell
  2. B. dome
  3. C. flat
  4. D. sloping
Answer: C. flat

Q3. The lower edge of sloping roof is termed....

  1. A. Ridge
  2. B. valley
  3. C. eave
  4. D. node
Answer: C. eave

Q4. Thickness of single layer of plaster is kept.........mm thick.

  4. D. 12
