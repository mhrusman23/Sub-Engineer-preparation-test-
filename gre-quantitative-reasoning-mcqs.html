Q1. If 3x + 5 = 20, then x =

  1. A. 3
  2. B. 5
  3. C. 7
  4. D. 15
Answer: B. 5

Q2. What is 15% of 200?

  1. A. 15
  2. B. 30
  3. C. 45
  4. D. 20
Answer: B. 30

Q3. The average of 4, 8, 12 and 16 is:

  1. A. 9
  2. B. 10
  3. C. 11
  4. D. 12
Answer: B. 10

Q4. If a train travels 120 km in 2 hours, its average speed is:

  1. A. 40 km/h
  2. B. 50 km/h
  3. C. 60 km/h
  4. D. 80 km/h
Answer: C. 60 km/h

Q5. The area of a circle with radius 7 (π = 22/7) is:

  1. A. 44
  2. B. 154
  3. C. 308
  4. D. 49
Answer: B. 154

Q6. If x² = 49, the possible values of x are:

  1. A. 7 only
  2. B. −7 only
  3. C. 7 and −7
  4. D. 49 and −49
Answer: C. 7 and −7

Q7. A shirt priced at $80 is sold at a 25% discount. The sale price is:

  1. A. $55
  2. B. $60
  3. C. $65
  4. D. $75
Answer: B. $60

Q8. The probability of getting heads in one fair coin toss is:

  1. A. 1/4
  2. B. 1/3
  3. C. 1/2
  4. D. 1
Answer: C. 1/2

Q9. If 5 workers finish a job in 10 days, 10 workers (same rate) finish it in:

  1. A. 20 days
  2. B. 10 days
  3. C. 5 days
  4. D. 2 days
Answer: C. 5 days

Q10. The next number in the sequence 2, 6, 12, 20, ... is:

  1. A. 24
  2. B. 28
  3. C. 30
  4. D. 36
Answer: C. 30
